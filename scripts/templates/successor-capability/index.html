What should become possible?

Unsaved proposal

The construction

Describe the new thing itself, the useful outcome it preserves, and what would count as a real test.

A family, not a one-off

Explain what another person or project could reuse, and where the idea may stop working.

Economic assumptions, not commitments

These are hypotheses to investigate, not customers, financing, revenue, savings or adoption established by this page. Write “unknown” where there is no evidence.

KEEP THE BOUNDARY VISIBLE

What this does not do

  • Beneficiaries, market demand, realized value and adoption remain unverified hypotheses.
  • Hashes check consistency, not authorship, truth or independent approval.
  • A successor starts without active evidence. A prior version’s pass does not transfer.
