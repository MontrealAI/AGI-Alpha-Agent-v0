EXPLORATION WORKSPACE · BLUE SKY ADMISSION NOT ESTABLISHED

Ambition, candidate admission, demonstrated construction and commercial qualification are separate states. Preparing a mission does not prove a breakthrough.

Before substantial flagship construction: compare three possibilities
  1. What consequential activity becomes possible, and for whom?
  2. What limitation remains with the strongest accessible tools and workflows?
  3. What recent frontier change makes that limitation worth challenging now?
  4. What missing instrument, method, representation or productive arrangement will you construct?
  5. Which independent before-and-after experiment, removal test and practical replacement could disprove the change?
  6. How will the first commercial delivery directly exercise the construction?
  7. Which further family of valuable activities could it enable?

Keep these answers and uncertainty in the existing project history: outcome, value hypothesis, context, acceptance and competing strategies below. An affordability claim needs the economic threshold crossed and the activity it opens.

Blue Sky HOLD permits a separately bounded commercial probe with a hypothesis, deliverables, allowance and stop. Profit does not lift HOLD or release the main flagship construction or publication budget.

        A few things worth knowing.
        What can I do for free?
        Develop a valuable mission, compare its proposed strategies and save your workspace for free. The configured native executor performs Computer Use work through its model provider. A maintained catalog example is also available as a local engineering tool.
        What counts as Alpha here?
        Alpha is the useful advantage you can demonstrate for a concrete opportunity. An attractive proposal, generated code or mathematical argument is a candidate: test the actual result, review the evidence and account for full costs before claiming value.
        What do I keep?
        You control the files you download: a workspace, reusable code, inputs, output and project history. Included code is Apache 2.0; bundled AGI Jobs components retain their MIT license. Input rights and any rights in commissioned or third-party work remain yours to establish.
        Does this certify Specialist ASI or authorize deployment?
        No. This is a working, bounded local demonstration. Independent evaluation on protected fresh work and explicit accountable authorization are separate steps. The Gym develops capability; it does not certify it.
