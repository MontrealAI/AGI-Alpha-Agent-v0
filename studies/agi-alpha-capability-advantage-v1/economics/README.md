# Numerical capability market adapter

This is a new, explicitly scoped research adapter. It retains the old canonical financial functions and their exact source bytes, but supplies the new numerical task families and measured reconstruction alternatives. It does not relabel dependency-metadata jobs as engineering work. It has no transaction, deployment, credential, token-issuance or execution-authorization API.

## Before execution

`market-protocol.json` is **DRAFT_NOT_EXECUTED** until the root research lead approves and freezes it. The final calibration must come from the frozen production cohort. `calibration-template.json` gives the required v2 schema; unresolved fields are intentionally null and cannot run. A raw prior capability test that did not occur uses `{accepted:null,cpu_seconds:null,basis:"NOT_RUN"}`. It is never converted into failure. Independently reconstructed and conventional alternatives remain available to all arms.

The model resamples finite executed cases and uses their stratum-level observed acceptance for decisions. V2 preserves A/B/C/D outcomes and complete-stage CPU separately. Complete CPU includes retrieval, admission and verification exactly once; no additional per-job retrieval CPU charge is accepted. Connected chain identities and difficulty strata must agree across all three stages. It never reads a sampled case's future acceptance to choose a route. Known acquisition and staleness risks also reduce the quoted delivered quality and raise expected cost per accepted outcome. The eventual execution can still fail, and every failed transfer cost remains a negative contribution to direct asset payback. The model is conditional on this small research cohort; those fractions are not estimated field reliability.

Measured construction intervals spanning all three families must be allocated once. For example, a 303.680-second complete conventional construction interval may be allocated evenly across G1/G2/G3, with that basis disclosed; it must not be charged three times. `resource_allocation` can bind the allocated field to its measured total. Verification inside that interval cannot be added again as an independent resource. Role elapsed time is a resource measurement, not active human labor time or measured model billing.

## Economic comparisons

A keeps each recipient's own accumulated reconstruction. B shares through the validated registry. C uses the same capability/evidence through the graph. D is a conventional implementation. All four can choose competent ordinary tools, measured independent reconstruction and the strongest conventional reconstruction; capability reuse does not remove these alternatives. A reconstructed method is retained for future requests. A shadow alternative also retains reconstruction after the first shared use, preventing repeated credit for avoiding the same build.

Every construction route uses the same complete category schedule, including discovery, unsuccessful candidates, validation, integration, model/tools and audit attention. Separate arm resource facts can state measured elapsed allocation, a scenario resource proxy, or an interval already included in construction. Unknown root research time can be scenario-priced only as an explicitly estimated proxy, never labeled measured. A and D retain their own 225.039-second and 303.680-second intervals. Shared construction is charged once per generation. Registry/graph initial index construction is charged once, in G1. Recipient acquisition is charged once per successful acquisition. Failed acquisition, stale use, validation, execution, maintenance and scheduled revalidation remain costs. More nodes confer no value by themselves.

One market purchase denotes **one separately requested stage service**: a forward response pack, inverse calibration, or robust design. Its complete source-bound execution must include the dependency work. This is a market hypothesis; it does not turn each 3-stage experimental chain into three customers or three actual sales.

The 48 structural sensitivity cells have balanced factor margins, not a full factorial or a probability distribution over real markets. They vary population 4/16/64, zero/low/reference/high recurrence, copying at 0/4/12 weeks, prior overlap, recipient diversity, hard-case mix, tenfold cheaper reconstruction, integration costs, acquisition failure, finite useful life, stale skills, deterministic/logit buyer choice, equal-across-arm price scenarios, collection delay, and contributor reward share. The actual independent-agent sample is not enlarged by these simulated populations.

Each cell is crossed with three complete-category cost schedules and 16 preselected seeds: **2,304 paired worlds and 9,216 primary enterprise histories**. The same-world finance controls are separately counted. No new seed is selected from favorable results.

All prices and tariffs are explicit assumptions. Low/reference/high research-resource rates are USD1/10/100 per elapsed research-resource hour; model/tool allowances are USD1/10/100 per build; assumed audit attention is 5/20/60 minutes at USD30/60/120 per hour. Actual authoring/model invoices and active attention remain unknown. Common setup/weekly overhead and USD361.52 old historical allocation retain the prior study's assumption for comparability, without claiming it is the new study's actual complete cost. Report both this declared scenario history and the actual-cost unidentified boundary.

The retained baseline is USD1,000 seed and USD200 reserve over 52 service weeks. The old `createFirm`, `expense`, `payBills`, `collect`, `liquidity`, `canonicalProjection` and `generatedResources` functions are reused. `PRIMITIVES_TRANSFORMATION.json` proves the only change to expose private helpers is appending exports to an exact copy; every original byte remains intact. The original module remains untouched.

## Buyer, competitor and contributor choices

Buyers choose enterprise purchase, conventional service, independent construction/retention or abstention using the inherited `chooseBuyer` function. Expected quality comes only from the finite execution cohort. No Merkle/proof, graph, trust or token premium is credited. Buyer value USD50 and the USD5/20/80 price sensitivity are explicitly unvalidated willingness-to-pay hypotheses. Conventional posted prices start at 1.2 times the common offering price and fall to 0.8 after modeled copying; this is a strategy scenario, not a competitive equilibrium estimate.

A finite competitor cash balance pays measured-resource/scenario-priced reconstruction before copying. Contributor willingness requires forecast internal reward to cover the incremental publication/validation reservation cost. Copying reduces expected fee lifetime. The reward share 0/25/50 percent is a sensitivity assumption. Actual modeled internal allocations are reported separately with exactly zero consolidated enterprise revenue/value. These choices are a bounded behavioral game, not a Nash-equilibrium solution or empirical contributor study.

## Financeable succession

G1 can use seed liquidity. G2/G3 require cash already generated by operations after unpaid obligations and one protected reserve; outstanding invoices, customer savings and token prices cannot fund them. Admission needs tested source, positive complete-cost forecast and current affordability. The forecast uses only prior same-family opportunities, finite remaining useful life and recipients who still lack their own reconstructed asset.

Every actual completed research prototype remains in project-inclusive accounting even if the model declines adoption: its unfinanced cost is an outside research contribution, never new enterprise cash. Actual total cost and actual historical return remain null.

For B/C, only the complete **G2+G3 later-investment bundle** can pass this model's succession check. Every completed later prototype cost must be financed from generated funds, the bundle must be used, its aggregate direct value must cover full construction/upkeep/failed-transfer costs, and its cash and unrounded project-inclusive profit must both exceed the same-seed no-later-shared-capability control. This control does not qualify G2 or G3 individually; no individual-generation success labels are returned. If no later asset is built, operations are identical and the incremental project value is the unfinanced later research cost. These are counterfactual finance calculations over prototypes already built in research, not a claim that real customers funded their actual construction.

## Reproduction and safeguards

Development controls use synthetic fixtures and are not market-confirmation results:

```sh
node test_network_market.mjs
```

The source review identified eight additional material issues, all corrected before any market-confirmation outcome; `review-response.json` maps each correction to the current source. The suite now contains 21 passing synthetic controls, including post-finish failure finalization, unknown-prior handling, cost symmetry, distinct per-arm timing, bundle-only qualification and fractional computation.

The first observed synthetic control failure was a fixture that unintentionally inherited the new USD80 price, above its assumed USD50 buyer value. The no-sale outcome was correct. The fixture now explicitly selects its intended USD20 price. Original test source and failure receipt are retained in `development-history/`.

Once root supplies actual `calibration.json`:

```sh
node run_market.mjs --validate-only --protocol=market-protocol.json
```

This performs no economic outcomes. Before the one registered campaign, the protocol must be marked `FROZEN_BEFORE_MARKET_OUTCOMES`, with time and coordination URL. A source freeze must bind protocol, calibration, runner, adapter and inherited functions by SHA256 and byte count.

```sh
node run_market.mjs --protocol=market-protocol.json --freeze=market-freeze.json --output=results-primary
```

The draft research execution envelope was changed from 180 to 900 wall/CPU seconds before any confirmation outcome, following the root lead's explicit instruction and independent source review. The 48×3×16 panel and seeds were unchanged. The output must not already exist. The runner retains complete compressed worlds, canonical projections, cash/invoice/bill/decision histories, same-world finance controls, compact records, all cell distributions, a preselected example, a CSV and completion/source identities. It stops and retains partial evidence on the frozen wall/CPU bound or any invariant failure. There is no automatic restart. The conditional Monte Carlo intervals summarize simulated seed variation only, are not simultaneous intervals across all cells, and do not measure confidence in commercial profitability.

Compute costs accumulate as fractional cents and the canonical cash ledger pays the ceiling of the cumulative amount; final excess is less than one cent. Exact unrounded paired profit sensitivity is also reported. A cent-denominated tie is not a scientific claim of zero computational advantage.

Zero actual customer collections, unknown all-in research cost and **no Level6 claim** remain explicit. Publication/source qualification remains the root owner's separate responsibility.
