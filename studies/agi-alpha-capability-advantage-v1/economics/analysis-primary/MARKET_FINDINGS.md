# Capability Advantage: completed economic findings

## Finding

The registered economic campaign found **no qualifying self-financing G2+G3 bundle** in either sharing arm. First-generation sharing sometimes repaid its allocated costs. The later capabilities did not produce the repeated, financeable advantage sought by the study. The results support continued investigation of ordinary reconstruction and integration savings, not a claim that these numerical methods are scarce, unsupported by other agents, or a proven commercial moat.

This is a **conditional simulation calibrated to executed engineering cases**. Actual customer collections are **$0**. Actual complete authoring, model usage, human attention, and other research costs are **unknown**. None of the model's dollar values are actual sales, invoices, paid resource bills, or earned surplus.

## What was executed and preserved

The completed production cohort supplied 144 accepted arm-stage observations: 12 connected chains, three stages, four arms. Raw pre-acquisition capability was not tested. Independent A and conventional D reconstructed the methods and completed every stage. All four algorithms' acceptance outcomes and complete stage CPU times were retained separately in calibration; a missing skill filename was never treated as an unsupported capability.

The registered market experiment executed 48 structural conditions × three cost schedules × 16 fixed seeds: **144 cells, 2,304 paired worlds, and 9,216 primary enterprise histories**. There were also **46 same-world financing controls**, not additional sampled markets. The campaign completed in 61.995436 seconds wall time and 68.412901 seconds CPU, inside its frozen 900-second envelope. The analyzer verified every frozen source identity and reconciled compressed histories with compact results. It did not rerun the simulator, change prices, or select replacement worlds. [1–4]

The cells are a balanced-marginal sensitivity panel, not an estimated distribution of real markets. Counts below are descriptive counts across that panel. They are not probabilities of commercial success. Each cell mean averages only its 16 registered seeds.

### Graph value and currency rounding

All C−B cash comparisons tied. This does **not** establish an exact computational null. Fractional compute differences were carried through the histories and reported separately. Without cash rounding, C−B had **one negative, 134 zero, and nine positive cell means**; there were **five negative, 2,250 zero, and 49 positive worlds**. Individual world differences ranged from **−0.0046608056 to +0.0663056944 cents**, or approximately −$0.0000466 to +$0.0006631. The fixed $1/CPU-hour tariff makes these sub-cent timing effects economically immaterial here. They do not identify a useful graph premium. [3,4]

Graph control authoring costs were not separately metered. Equal scenario authoring allocations for B and C are assumptions, not observed equality of total costs. Including unknown extra graph engineering costs cannot be inferred from these sub-cent timing differences.

### Value per recipient and reuse

For each used asset, the analyzer takes recorded direct savings against the recipient's retaining independent alternative, deducts every execution and acquisition cost, then allocates that asset's construction plus upkeep **once**, equally across attempted uses, including failures. This asset-level view excludes unrelated completed prototypes and common enterprise overhead. The whole-project measure above accounts for those costs; the two views must not be added together. Failed assets without users retain their unallocated full costs. [6,7]

## Business interpretation and limits

The useful engineering capabilities are real executed prototypes. The economics show that a reusable method can have positive value for particular recipients while still failing to repay network-wide overhead or support the next generation. Repeated demand, complement availability, recipient alternatives, contributors' willingness to share, cash timing, and useful lifetime all constrain the result. The balanced panel changes these jointly; it cannot identify separate causal effect sizes for each factor.

Buyers could purchase conventionally, construct and retain their own solution, or abstain. They exercised all of those options. Contributor rewards were internal redistribution. Competitor imitation was finite-budget and reduced outside prices and contribution rents. Tenfold cheaper reconstruction was a sensitivity, not an actually tested stronger foundation model. The numerical algorithms were independently reconstructable in the executed comparator population; profitable ordinary reuse would not establish a scarce-capability moat.

Actual Level 3 monetary network value remains unidentified because complete costs and paid demand are not observed. This registered model did not achieve Level 5 even within its declared assumptions. Graph routing did not add a meaningful economic benefit here. Level 6 acceleration was not tested. These findings justify preserving the library and evidence while declining an affirmative financing claim on this experiment.
