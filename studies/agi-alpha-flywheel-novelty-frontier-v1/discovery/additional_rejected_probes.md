## Primary references actually opened

- LLM-IDEA, Surya Shetty and Ulisses Braga-Neto. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11253 . Its Oct 2026 scope is particularly relevant: an automatic identifiability verdict and protocol intervention are already described.
- Verifiable and Provably Secure Machine Unlearning, CISPA primary publication summary, 11 April 2025. https://cispa.de/en/research/publications/84771-verifiable-and-provably-secure-machine-unlearning . The framework binds proof of initial training, correct unlearning and absence from the retained dataset.
- Protecting the Undeleted in Machine Unlearning, Cohen et al., FORC 2026, published 1 June 2026. https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.FORC.2026.17 . Perfect-retraining equivalence can expose remaining records through adversarial deletion requests.
- Enhancing Controlled Query Evaluation through Epistemic Policies, Cima et al., IJCAI 2024, pages 3307–3314. https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2024/366 . Rich logical knowledge policies and query rewriting already exist.
- PlanTwin: Privacy-Preserving Planning Abstractions for Cloud-Assisted LLM Agents, Yu et al., arXiv 2603.18377v2, revised 20 March 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18377 . Sanitized planning twins, local gatekeepers and cumulative disclosure controls already exist.
- Compositional abstraction error and a category of causal models, Rischel and Weichwald, UAI 2021. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v161/rischel21a.html . Error bounds for composed causal abstractions are existing theory.
- On the Equivalence of Causal Models: A Category-Theoretic Approach, Otsuka and Saigo, CLeaR 2022. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v177/otsuka22a.html . Equivalence and consistent intervention translation are existing theory.

## Strongest conclusion from this bounded probe

A strong negative novelty result is available: generic mechanisms for certifying privacy-preserving action, identifying useful interventions, verifiable unlearning, and compositional causal transfer are already covered. A flagship needs a materially new *particular construction*, theorem, experimental result, or offering, with the closest of these mechanisms included as a control. A policy compiler plus an LLM interface would not satisfy the supplied discovery gate.

No human-expert discovery trial, model-vs-model benchmark, historic replay, prospective foresight result or actual commercial collection is created by this search.
