### Keep the measurement. Change the frame.

This edition constructs and executes a reusable instrument for a consequential scientific task:
carry measurements of an existing segmentation into a new physical coordinate frame while preserving
the meaning of the requested quantities. The instrument binds actual source bytes, distinguishes native
specimen support from mapped geometric support, and refuses unsupported missions.

The exact retained executable was recovered through the existing research lifecycle and used by a
separate process on a different public microscopy study. It passed **48/48 compatible affine missions**
and refused **12/12 unsupported or malformed missions**. The competent analytical baseline also passed
48/48 and was faster in the observed numerical workload. An ordinary directory containing the identical
artifact produced identical scientific output. These results establish useful construction and actual
reuse; they do not establish new algorithmic novelty, an incremental quality or speed advantage, a
graph-specific benefit, or profitable compounding.

The fresh suite contains **two supplied label groups from one image/timepoint**, with 48 authored maps.
It is not a sample of 48 independent biological specimens. Acceptance means satisfaction of the frozen
geometric research contract, not customer acceptance or validation of segmentation biology.

## The transformation witness

**Original purpose:** report identities, support counts, native physical measure, mapped geometric
measure and centroids of supplied labels under an explicit affine physical-coordinate map.

**Restriction examined:** every measurement must first be made from a common-grid target raster.
That representation can change a quantitative answer through sampling even when the requested
geometric quantity has an exact analytical transport rule.

**Replacement:** compute source moments once per invocation, then use the affine determinant for mapped measure
and the affine map for the centroid. Preserve the native measure separately. This is established
mathematics and physical-space image-analysis practice, assembled into a source-bound delivery tool.

**Falsifiers:** any within-scope measurement failure defeats the technical claim; accepting a changed
scientific purpose defeats the semantic claim; a competent equal-cost baseline defeats a claim of
new feasibility or incremental Alpha. The competent baseline ties accepted quality here, so the
stronger opportunity/Alpha claim is not admitted. See the [machine-readable witness](evidence/transformation_witness.json).

A named nearest-neighbor raster diagnostic produced maximum per-region volume error of 49.1% at
3 micrometers, compared with the original continuous voxel-support measure. This is a development
diagnostic of that pipeline, not a statement about the limits of competent users, frontier models,
all resampling algorithms, or biological segmentation accuracy.

## Practical operating blueprint

1. **Qualify the useful outcome.** Obtain source rights, actual bytes, calibration, label meaning,
   requested measures, deadline, customer-effort ceiling and acceptance tolerances. Distinguish a
   source-support measurement from segmentation, registration or biological validation.
2. **Offer the competent route first.** Give conventional tools the same formulation and caching
   permission. Quote the complete service against the human outside option and the buyer's own
   alternative; allow decline or deferral. Do not assume several laboratories want one shared adapter.
3. **Execute and account.** Bind source and contract, run the scoped instrument, compare reference
   quantities, retain corrections/refusals, and count all actual tools, model usage, staff/customer
   work, support and refund obligations in A. Research beyond the paid outcome belongs in C.
4. **Evaluate extra retention.** Assess B only for costs additional to A. Estimate realized reusable
   benefit relative to the recipient's competent existing tools. Reject or defer when evidence does
   not cover additional costs and risk. Profitable delivery can continue after that decision.
5. **Reuse under fresh authority.** Verify artifact identity, provenance, rights, version and current
   dependencies. Recheck each mission's meaning and acceptance. Existing protected-network access,
   revocation, Jobs-validator roles and settlement boundaries remain separate from execution authority.
6. **Retire or revise prospectively.** Preserve old evidence, monitor changes in format/tool versions,
   test negative transfer and capability obsolescence, then freeze a new version before new evaluation.

accepted-quality costs and actual eligible demand. A failure, a tie, insufficient realized work or a
changed purpose is a legitimate stop. Four briefs are a small discriminating pilot, not a population
