\sectiontitle{Reading the evidence correctly}
``Accepted'' refers to the declared numerical or semantic test unless explicitly described otherwise. It is not customer sign-off. The common scorer is outside the three hosted workers but inside the same AI-assisted team; it is not independent external validation. The 48 missions are authored maps over two partitions, not 48 independent biological specimens.

The instrument measures supplied label support. A label can contain disconnected components and can touch a source boundary. It does not validate biological cell counts, segmentation quality, physical registration, nonlinear maps, intensity measurements or clinical meaning. Numerical error bounds are software agreement, not microscope precision.

\sectiontitle{Evidence vocabulary}
\textbf{Observed}: executed outputs, bytes, timings and refusals. \textbf{Sourced}: official specifications, public data provenance and a human-service rate card. \textbf{Assumed/calibrated}: workload, labor, buyer values, probabilities and service scope. \textbf{Simulated}: every customer decision, payment and commercial outcome. A simulation interval describes variation within those assumptions.

\sectiontitle{The transformation witness}
\textbf{Original purpose.} Preserve supplied label identities, native physical measure and centroids, and report an explicitly requested affine geometric pushforward.\par
\textbf{Binding restriction under examination.} Require all quantitative measurement to pass through a common target raster.\par
\textbf{Why removable.} Affine maps act directly on voxel moments; the useful quantities do not require re-rasterization. This is an established analytical alternative, not an invention of new affine mathematics.\par
\textbf{Replacement and realization.} Own one bounded NPY byte snapshot, bind its SHA-256 to the contract, validate units/axes/meaning, summarize native support and transform its moments.\par
\textbf{Falsifier.} A valid, supported mission fails fixed numerical tolerances; changed or unsupported meaning is accepted; or the claimed advantage disappears against an equally competent baseline. The last falsifier applies to superiority: conventional analysis matches accepted quality.

Refundable deposits remain liabilities; delayed invoices are not available cash. Default, partial delivery, withdrawal, common failure, refunds and support are modeled separately. \sectiontitle{Reuse without inherited authority}
The integration exercises unchanged Journal, TrustRegistry and CostLedger components plus the existing research lifecycle. Actual signatures and authenticated encryption are tested with labeled fixture identities. Unknown members, revoked rights, changed bytes, altered mission meaning and duplicate A/B/C activity charges are refused. The native protected-capsule interface refuses this unsupported scientific scope. Production admission has not been obtained.

A successor inherits useful executable structure. It must still check its source, dependencies, input meaning, requested outputs and acceptance. Authority is a separate grant. Revocation prevents new mediated access; it cannot erase copies already retrieved. Rights-cleared data and code can be shared; secrets and protected evaluation material cannot.
