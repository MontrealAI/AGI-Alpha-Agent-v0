# A measurement worth carrying forward

A laboratory has already paid to acquire an image and delineate its objects. A collaborator needs those objects in another coordinate frame. The useful result is an answer whose meaning survives the move: which supplied regions were measured, how much native physical space they occupy, and where their centers lie under a declared geometric map. A converted file or attractive rendering cannot establish that result by itself.

This study constructs and executes a small instrument for that purpose. It binds a measurement contract to exact source bytes, computes the requested quantities, rejects unsupported meanings, and packages executable behavior for another recipient. After source freeze, the recovered instrument accepts **48 of 48 compatible fresh missions** on a separate public microscopy study and correctly refuses **12 of 12 incompatible or malformed missions**. The artifact is actually executed in new Python processes; transfer is more than retrieval of a document. [E1–E4]

The strongest ordinary alternative matters. A competent analytical workflow accepts the same 48 missions, and an ordinary directory with identical artifact contents produces identical scientific outputs. A separate, small hosted-model trial yields **12/12 accepted outcomes for U, E and E+**. Thus the study establishes a usable instrument and fresh executable transfer; it establishes no accepted-quality advantage over the competent recipient, no graph-specific benefit, and no measured complete-cost advantage. [E3, E5]

The commercial study asks what would make such a service worth operating and such a capability worth retaining. Under explicit buyer, effort and failure assumptions, a frozen expert operating policy produces mean simulated economic surplus of **CAD596.86**, comprising a fictional originating-job surplus of CAD220 and CAD376.86 from subsequent work. Actual customers and collections are zero; complete actual research cost and operating profit are unknown. The primary retention policy rejects an unproven increment after a CAD5 screen. With four realized eligible uses, a CAD75 additional retention burden would require more than **17.31 minutes of avoidable CAD65/hour expense per use**. No such saving was observed. [E6]

Three semantic distinctions preserve the original purpose. First, the number of label identities is not the number of connected components or independently verified biological cells. The instrument accepts only missions that make these distinctions explicit. It can refuse an unresolved axis, unsupported meaning or conflicting source identity. It cannot infer that a mathematically valid matrix secretly represents the caller's unintended inverse map. That remaining responsibility belongs to mission preparation and acceptance, and it must be costed in any service trial.

# 3. Carry the quantity, not an unnecessary raster

Let label $k$ occupy index centers $I_k$ in a $d$-dimensional source array. Let $D=\operatorname{diag}(s_1,\ldots,s_d)$ contain positive physical spacings, and $o$ the source origin. The contract treats integer index $i$ as a voxel center and its support as $i+[-1/2,1/2)^d$. For $n_k=|I_k|$,

$$
V_k=n_k\prod_{j=1}^{d}s_j,\qquad
c_k=o+D\left(\frac{1}{n_k}\sum_{i\in I_k}i\right).
$$

For an explicit physical-coordinate affine map $T(x)=Ax+b$, with nonsingular $A$,

$$
V_k^{\mathrm{mapped}}=|\det A|\,V_k,\qquad
c_k^{\mathrm{mapped}}=Ac_k+b.
$$

These identities follow from affine change of variables and linearity of the first moment. Reflection uses the absolute determinant. Native volume $V_k$ stays in the source metric; the second volume describes the mapped geometric support. In two dimensions, the same formulation gives area. This is established mathematics, implemented here for a precisely bounded handoff. [3–5, E1]

![A published source partition and its centroids carried into a new frame. Left: actual IDR0062 label slice. Right: native and mapped centroids from the recorded development result. The transformation is authored; biological validity is not inferred. Source imagery: Blin et al., CC BY 4.0.](../figures/measurement_story.png){width=95%}

The implementation takes a single owned, bounded NPY byte snapshot, hashes those exact bytes, checks the header and payload before loading, and then measures the trusted array. The public CLI performs this binding; the internal array helper assumes trusted input. The bound is one GiB per source. Integer labels may be signed and nonzero, with magnitude at most $2^{53}-1$ for exact JSON interchange; zero is background. [E1, E8]

Source calibration is axis-aligned. Affine columns follow `source_axes`, rows follow `target_axes`; maps are expressed in physical micrometers. The contract preserves the requested label set and numerical tolerance. It does not silently substitute display resampling, nonlinear mapping, topology, intensity, segmentation correction or clinical interpretation. This limited scope is what makes the instrument auditable and the experiment decisive.

# 4. A real source, a revealing diagnostic

Development used IDR0062A image 6001240 from the published NesSys study. Its native label array has shape 236 × 275 × 271, spacing 0.5002025531914894 / 0.3603981534640209 / 0.3603981534640209 micrometers, and 61 nonzero label identities. Four labels touch a source boundary; they are not asserted to be complete cells. All 32 compressed chunks and metadata were preserved. The exported array has SHA-256 beginning `c557c80743037400`; its full digest is in the provenance record. [6, E2]

The constructed instrument and conventional analytical reference returned the same label set and measures. Maximum centroid disagreement was $1.42\times10^{-14}$ micrometers. The instrument's recorded numerical work took 3.156 seconds; conventional computation took 0.784 seconds. This is successful numerical realization, with no speed advantage claimed. [E2]

A separate named diagnostic applied SciPy nearest-neighbor resampling to common grids before measurement. It kept all 61 IDs but changed their support measures. At 0.75 micrometers, six regions exceeded 1% discrepancy and the maximum was 13.262%. At 1.5 micrometers, 26 exceeded 1%; at 3 micrometers, 51 did. The analytical method avoids that particular discretization step. [E2]

## The cheapest next test of expertise

For a simple accepted-invoice model, let price be $P$, acceptance probability $q$, accepted-invoice default probability $d$, complete delivery expense $a$, and extra method expense $\Delta a$. A method with setup/retention cost $B$ over $N$ realized uses needs

$$
\Delta q>\frac{\Delta a+B/N}{P(1-d)}
$$

to improve expected contribution, holding price, quality meaning and settlement terms fixed. At assumed CAD425 and 4% default, CAD1 extra per job with no fixed cost requires about **0.245 percentage points** of acceptance improvement. The threshold must fit the available headroom $1-q$. This simple calculation is a design aid; the fuller simulator includes partial delivery and refunds. A low-cost method should be evaluated only enough to resolve this economically meaningful difference. [E6]

# 10. Retain selectively, then revalidate

The economic question is not whether an artifact is reusable in principle. It is whether this recipient gains enough eligible future benefit, beyond what it already possesses, to justify additional cost and risk. Here the competent recipient already has the mathematics. Numerical transfer succeeds, but extra quality and labor savings are not established.

With total incremental B cost $B=75$ CAD, avoidable labor expense $w=65$ CAD/hour and $N$ realized eligible uses, the simple matched-quality threshold is

$$
\Delta t>\frac{B}{wN}.
$$

Four uses require 17.31 minutes each; two require 34.62 minutes; one requires 69.23 minutes. With no eligible use there is no finite saving threshold. The word **avoidable** matters: reduced salaried idle time is not automatically cash, and the same saved capacity cannot be counted again as wage savings and speculative future revenue. [E6]

A feasible admission screen compares expected benefit with achievable headroom before investing in a large assessment campaign. It includes the cheap rejected screen, false admissions, upkeep, scope drift and obsolescence. Forecast reuse is an uncertain random variable, including zero. The half-hour loss is retained as evidence that a favorable point estimate can still misallocate funds.

The capability record carries scope, prerequisites, version, source digest, dependencies, license/provenance, measurement evidence and limitations. The current module is a fixed analytical instrument, not a fine-tuned model or autonomous learning loop. No optional Gym gain was observed; the modeled Gym is a separate C decision. Future practice must retain its original artifact, environment, feedback, update rule and protected evaluation before claiming an improved version.

For a new mission, stable source qualification may be reused only when identity and scope remain valid. Input bytes, labels, physical units, map direction, requested meaning, acceptance and current authority must still be checked. An unfamiliar biological purpose, nonlinear map or changed dependency is a reason for a new qualification path, not permission to reuse an old ACCEPT.

| 5. Assess incremental retention | Count additional packaging, rights, admission, upkeep and rejected-screen cost once | Retain only when the recipient's expected extra benefit exceeds those costs and risk |
| 6. Equip a fresh mission | Retrieve exact authorized bytes through the existing lifecycle, or ordinary authorized library | New mission meaning and execution authority checked separately |
| 7. Reconcile and decide | Compare actual collections, obligations, cash and complete A/B/C costs | Continue, revise prospectively or stop; no speculative earnings fund commitments |

For a research deployment, the minimum prerequisites are a supported integer label array, declared physical calibration, an explicit invertible affine map, the frozen Python dependencies and a person or authorized process able to resolve scientific meaning. A full NGFF reader is not required for the bounded NPY interface. A production integration would need its own qualified scope and release review.

The English/French demonstration lets a reader inspect actual source-region moments and vary a mathematical rotation/scale. Native volume remains fixed; mapped geometric volume changes under the displayed map. It also exposes the conditional retention threshold. Those browser controls recompute a mathematical scenario from archived moments; they do not rerun Python, authenticate a network member, settle a job or record a new experiment.

The operating order matters. A useful delivery may justify continued service even when retention is rejected. An artifact may deserve research preservation even when commercial admission fails. Conversely, a technically successful tool cannot borrow profitability from a favorable scenario, and a profitable scenario cannot grant production authority. The connected example therefore ends at a justified decision point rather than pretending every flywheel stage has succeeded.

# 14. The next productive possibility

The most informative next step is **four real, unfamiliar imaging briefs** from willing laboratories or an imaging core, chosen to span different source layouts and genuinely unresolved measurement questions. The technical question is already sufficiently clear to avoid building more infrastructure first. The unresolved variable is the work required to establish meaning and complete an accepted handoff.

Before assignment, a beneficiary and evaluator should define useful quantities, tolerances, report content, deadline, maximum customer effort and acceptable price. Use a competent existing workflow E and E+ with the frozen artifact, balanced or randomized across briefs where feasible. Let both use standard libraries, caching and the same scientific clarification. Record preparation, provenance checking, adaptation, retries, correction, communication, verification, packaging, latency, model/tool usage and actual invoices or avoidable labor expense. Do not insert a fixed human-review allowance in place of observation.

The primary decision is complete incremental cost at matched accepted quality. If four eligible uses actually occur under the present CAD75/CAD65 assumptions, E+ must save more than 69.23 total avoidable minutes before an additional margin exists. A pilot may update that threshold if measured costs differ. Customer effort, late or partial delivery, unsupported tasks and refusals remain part of acceptance. A four-brief pilot estimates feasibility; it does not establish sustained specialist superiority.

A cheaper parallel check can test the frozen E rubric against U on fresh mission specifications with hidden semantic traps and an expert-quality oracle. Its purpose is to identify an acceptance or correction-cost difference large enough to pay for the method. Stop if both remain competent and the remaining uncertainty is commercially immaterial. No new paid API campaign is required merely to make the evaluation look ambitious.

## Boundaries that could change the conclusion

This study covers affine transformations of supplied finite label partitions, not registration estimation, nonlinear deformation, topology, intensity conservation, biological validity or complete cells. It uses one development image and two held groups in one new image/timepoint. Hosted trials have one worker per arm, no randomization and incomplete cost telemetry. Customer decisions, prices, labor savings and collections are modeled; the conservative buyer screen and separately provisioned fixed-cost capacity are assumptions. Protected production microscopy scope remains unqualified. [E1–E6]

The productive opportunity is therefore a disciplined service test: determine whether a portable, source-bound measurement contract reduces real intake, correction or verification effort for recipients who do not already possess the same capability. If it does, retain selectively and test further transfer. If it does not, use the competent existing method and preserve the instrument as a reproducible research result. Both outcomes advance the mission without converting ambition into invented evidence.
