## Scope of the positive result

Primary E has CAD596.86 mean simulated economic surplus: CAD220 from the explicitly conditional accepted originating job, and CAD376.86 from fresh work after subsequent operating costs. Mean net collections are CAD1,160.94, A cash cost CAD534.14 and opportunity cost CAD29.94. The originating price/cost and all workload/customer assumptions are supplied scenarios. Mean ending available funds CAD1,976.80 includes CAD1,500 seed capital; capital is excluded from revenue. Four of 96 primary worlds lose economic value even with the fixed positive origin. The Monte Carlo interval for the mean is [520.92, 672.80] within assumptions.

The primary retained benefit is zero. E+ rejects retention after a CAD5 screening cost. Its profitable operation does not establish additional Alpha. Conditional half-hour saving has negative incremental value because the supplied four-reuse forecast exceeds the realized workload. A one-hour saving can support positive incremental value under the same frozen operating policy, but no such human effort saving was measured. Identical-content ordinary-library histories exactly match E+ in every world.

## Accounting and validation

Eight targeted accounting tests pass, including refundable-deposit availability, delayed collection, partial refunds, default separation, origin affordability, disjoint incremental costs, library parity and 240 adverse-route histories. Ruff formatting/lint pass. All 13,824 confirmation histories verify the cent-exact cash identity; all liabilities/receivables settle within the declared horizon. These checks validate the simulator implementation, not the reality of its assumptions.

Actual collections and paid API calls are zero. Complete actual research cost and actual operating profit remain unknown. No live identity, contractual buyer acceptance or production execution is established.

## Review qualifications retained beside interpretation

The buyer-choice equation is a conservative maximum-price screening assumption: it
subtracts the full quote even when failed/partial delivery would lead to a smaller
payment. It is not exact risk-neutral expected utility under the modeled settlement
terms. No revised choice rule or improved retrospective result is substituted.

The 24-hour capacity bound applies only to fresh delivery/correction/support labor.
Fixed construction, coordination, retention and practice charges require outsourced
or separately provisioned capacity. Their staffing/lead times are not modeled. The
results do not establish affordability in a system where the same constrained staff
must perform those activities within those 24 hours.

The retention threshold of 17.31 minutes per use requires four realized eligible
reuses at the assumed avoidable CAD65/hour cash cost. Four forecast opportunities
are insufficient; actual modeled demand is frequently lower. The half-hour and
negative-transfer admission losses are preserved. No real human-effort saving is
measured. The README supplies separate H/U/E/E+ definitions and a no-gain-inclusive
low-cost Operational Alpha threshold for a future matched comparison; the economics
does not create hosted-model trial results.
