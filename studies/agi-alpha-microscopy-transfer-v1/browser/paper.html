A measurement worth carrying forward

A laboratory has already paid to acquire an image and delineate its objects. A collaborator needs those objects in another coordinate frame. The useful result is an answer whose meaning survives the move: which supplied regions were measured, how much native physical space they occupy, and where their centers lie under a declared geometric map. A converted file or attractive rendering cannot establish that result by itself.

This study constructs and executes a small instrument for that purpose. It binds a measurement contract to exact source bytes, computes the requested quantities, rejects unsupported meanings, and packages executable behavior for another recipient. After source freeze, the recovered instrument accepts 48 of 48 compatible fresh missions on a separate public microscopy study and correctly refuses 12 of 12 incompatible or malformed missions. The artifact is actually executed in new Python processes; transfer is more than retrieval of a document. [E1–E4]

The strongest ordinary alternative matters. A competent analytical workflow accepts the same 48 missions, and an ordinary directory with identical artifact contents produces identical scientific outputs. A separate, small hosted-model trial yields 12/12 accepted outcomes for U, E and E+. Thus the study establishes a usable instrument and fresh executable transfer; it establishes no accepted-quality advantage over the competent recipient, no graph-specific benefit, and no measured complete-cost advantage. [E3, E5]

The commercial study asks what would make such a service worth operating and such a capability worth retaining. Under explicit buyer, effort and failure assumptions, a frozen expert operating policy produces mean simulated economic surplus of CAD596.86, comprising a fictional originating-job surplus of CAD220 and CAD376.86 from subsequent work. Actual customers and collections are zero; complete actual research cost and operating profit are unknown. The primary retention policy rejects an unproven increment after a CAD5 screen. With four realized eligible uses, a CAD75 additional retention burden would require more than 17.31 minutes of avoidable CAD65/hour expense per use. No such saving was observed. [E6]

1. Explore the need before choosing the test

The beneficiary is a research laboratory or imaging core transferring quantitative results between pipelines, instruments or collaborators. Its desired outcome is an accepted measurement report with reproducible source identity and explicit scope. The costly dependency under investigation is a workflow convention: rebuilding a target raster before measuring an object whose requested aggregate quantities can instead be derived directly from source support.

A bounded search considered four materially different candidates. The dossier records 20 search queries and 14 opened primary pages, plus later contrary references; the final source inventory contains 22 entries with retrieval status preserved. The initial discovery limit was extended for three decisive existing-method references, to prevent a false novelty claim. This was enough to select a tractable, consequential test, not a global novelty search. [E7]

The microscopy choice has accessible real inputs, a meaningful failure mode and an equivalent human-service reference. It also has strong prior art. NGFF describes coordinate systems and transformations; its specification excludes exact transformed/resampled pixel reproducibility. SimpleITK already exposes per-label physical size and centroid, scikit-image exposes spacing-aware region properties, and ITK separates spatial objects from their presentation. These are building blocks and serious comparators. [1–5]

The search therefore did not discover affine moment transport as a new algorithm, nor establish that imaging specialists lack it. It identified a purpose-specific packaging and verification question: can a small artifact make the intended quantity explicit, survive a recipient handoff, and refuse changes that invalidate that meaning? The falsifier for a claimed E+ advantage is straightforward: if the competent existing workflow meets the same contract at equal or lower complete cost, the new capability adds no demonstrated advantage to that recipient.

The declared working budgets were a short discovery phase, 35 minutes for initial implementation, 35 for evaluation and 40 for writing, with extensions only for concrete unresolved issues. Source and task commitments bound the actual experiment. These are planning budgets, not measured labor or a claim that every phase finished within its allowance. Failed and revised development attempts remain in the archive. [E1, E7, E8]

2. The transformation witness

The useful purpose is narrower than “preserve the image.” It is to report supplied label identities, occupied native physical measure and centroids, and the geometric pushforward of those same regions under an explicit affine map. Display pixels, object topology, fluorescence intensity and biological correctness are separate purposes.

Witness element Inspectable content
Original purpose Recover the stated geometric quantities for the supplied non-background label support, with exact source identity, declared units and meaning
Binding restriction A named common-grid pipeline requires raster resampling before measurement; discretization can change occupied support
Why removable Counts, first moments and support measure under an affine map have an analytical relation to the source partition; a target raster is not required for these quantities
Replacement Keep the native partition and metric; transport geometric moments through the declared map; keep native and mapped measures distinct
Executable realization Frozen microscopy_transfer.py, a validated contract, immutable byte binding, machine-readable output and refusal reasons
Falsifying observation Wrong IDs/counts, measure or centroid outside tolerance; unsupported meaning accepted; source mismatch accepted; or an advantage claim defeated by the competent comparator

This replaces a restriction of the named pipeline, not a proven ceiling on all existing methods. A source-space analytical baseline receives the same formulation and permissions. Its success separates the value of the formulation from any claim of better execution or learned discovery. [E1–E3]

Three semantic distinctions preserve the original purpose. First, the number of label identities is not the number of connected components or independently verified biological cells. A single label can have disconnected support. Second, native physical measure remains tied to the source metric. Mapped geometric measure may change under a deformation. A coordinate re-expression alone does not physically enlarge the specimen. Third, an explicit map is supplied input; this study neither estimates nor validates biological registration.

The instrument accepts only missions that make these distinctions explicit. It can refuse an unresolved axis, unsupported meaning or conflicting source identity. It cannot infer that a mathematically valid matrix secretly represents the caller’s unintended inverse map. That remaining responsibility belongs to mission preparation and acceptance, and it must be costed in any service trial.

This is role separation within one AI-assisted team, not external validation. 3. Carry the quantity, not an unnecessary raster

Let label kk occupy index centers IkI_k in a dd-dimensional source array. Let D=diag(s1,…,sd)D=\operatorname{diag}(s_1,\ldots,s_d) contain positive physical spacings, and oo the source origin. The contract treats integer index ii as a voxel center and its support as i+[−1/2,1/2)di+[-1/2,1/2)^d. For nk=|Ik|n_k=|I_k|,

Vk=nk∏j=1dsj,ck=o+D(1nk∑i∈Iki). V_k=n_k\prod_{j=1}^{d}s_j,\qquad c_k=o+D\left(\frac{1}{n_k}\sum_{i\in I_k}i\right).

For an explicit physical-coordinate affine map T(x)=Ax+bT(x)=Ax+b, with nonsingular AA,

Vkmapped=|detA|Vk,ckmapped=Ack+b. V_k^{\mathrm{mapped}}=|\det A|\,V_k,\qquad c_k^{\mathrm{mapped}}=Ac_k+b.

These identities follow from affine change of variables and linearity of the first moment. Reflection uses the absolute determinant. Native volume VkV_k stays in the source metric; the second volume describes the mapped geometric support. In two dimensions, the same formulation gives area. This is established mathematics, implemented here for a precisely bounded handoff. [3–5, E1]

The implementation takes a single owned, bounded NPY byte snapshot, hashes those exact bytes, checks the header and payload before loading, and then measures the trusted array. The public CLI performs this binding; the internal array helper assumes trusted input. The bound is one GiB per source. Integer labels may be signed and nonzero, with magnitude at most 253−12^{53}-1 for exact JSON interchange; zero is background. [E1, E8]

Source calibration is axis-aligned. Affine columns follow source_axes, rows follow target_axes; maps are expressed in physical micrometers. The contract preserves the requested label set and numerical tolerance. It does not silently substitute display resampling, nonlinear mapping, topology, intensity, segmentation correction or clinical interpretation. This limited scope is what makes the instrument auditable and the experiment decisive.

4. A real source, a revealing diagnostic

Development used IDR0062A image 6001240 from the published NesSys study. Its native label array has shape 236 × 275 × 271, spacing 0.5002025531914894 / 0.3603981534640209 / 0.3603981534640209 micrometers, and 61 nonzero label identities. Four labels touch a source boundary; they are not asserted to be complete cells. All 32 compressed chunks and metadata were preserved. The exported array has SHA-256 beginning c557c80743037400; its full digest is in the provenance record. [6, E2]

The constructed instrument and conventional analytical reference returned the same label set and measures. Maximum centroid disagreement was 1.42×10−141.42\times10^{-14} micrometers. The instrument’s recorded numerical work took 3.156 seconds; conventional computation took 0.784 seconds. This is successful numerical realization, with no speed advantage claimed. [E2]

A separate named diagnostic applied SciPy nearest-neighbor resampling to common grids before measurement. It kept all 61 IDs but changed their support measures. At 0.75 micrometers, six regions exceeded 1% discrepancy and the maximum was 13.262%. At 1.5 micrometers, 26 exceeded 1%; at 3 micrometers, 51 did. The analytical method avoids that particular discretization step. [E2]

These results do not show that resampling is always inappropriate. It is useful for display and for tasks that genuinely require a target raster. They show that an unnecessary representation change can alter a requested quantity. They also do not show that a competent analyst would choose the deficient route: the strong analytical baseline already avoids it. The transformation is useful at the workflow level; uniqueness, a new market and Operational Alpha remain unproved.

The cheapest next test of expertise

to improve expected contribution, holding price, quality meaning and settlement terms fixed. 7. Construct the offering and its arrangement together

The proposed offering is a source-bound quantitative transfer report: declared measurements, native and mapped results, exact inputs, validation checks, limitations, and machine-readable evidence. The narrow prototype does not deliver a full interoperable NGFF conversion, segmentation or validated registration. A prospective buyer must agree that this deliverable satisfies its actual scientific purpose.

CHUM’s cellular-imaging service lists formatted image-analysis rates of $129.75 per hour for external academic/hospital clients and $143.50 per hour for businesses. Its stated scope includes staff analysis and quantitative extraction with tools such as FIJI, CellProfiler and Imaris. This is a relevant existing alternative, not a quote for the proposed report. CAD is an explicit inference from the Montreal provider; the retrieved rate block uses a dollar sign without spelling out currency. [8, 9]

At the business rate, assumed human durations of 1.5, 2.5 and 4 hours correspond to CAD215.25, CAD358.75 and CAD574.00. The range exposes the decisive uncertainty: the human reference can be cheaper than the model-selected CAD425 quote. Buyers may select that alternative or decline. Hours, buyer values, residual obligations and demand are assumptions. The study does not price the service as a fixed discount to human work or a markup on token cost. [E6]

The simulation gives matched enterprises the same eight prospective buyers, resources and choices. Buyers face service value, quote, effort, outside options, withdrawal, common qualification failure and default. The participation expression subtracts the full quoted price from expected delivered value. Because conditional settlement can later reduce payment, this is a conservative maximum-price screening rule, not exact risk-neutral expected utility. That behavioral assumption remains explicit; it was not corrected retrospectively to obtain a better result. [E6]

10. Retain selectively, then revalidate

The economic question is not whether an artifact is reusable in principle. It is whether this recipient gains enough eligible future benefit, beyond what it already possesses, to justify additional cost and risk. Here the competent recipient already has the mathematics. Numerical transfer succeeds, but extra quality and labor savings are not established.

With total incremental B cost B=75B=75 CAD, avoidable labor expense w=65w=65 CAD/hour and NN realized eligible uses, the simple matched-quality threshold is

Δt>BwN. \Delta t>\frac{B}{wN}.

Four uses require 17.31 minutes each; two require 34.62 minutes; one requires 69.23 minutes. With no eligible use there is no finite saving threshold. The word avoidable matters: reduced salaried idle time is not automatically cash, and the same saved capacity cannot be counted again as wage savings and speculative future revenue. [E6]

A feasible admission screen compares expected benefit with achievable headroom before investing in a large assessment campaign. It includes the cheap rejected screen, false admissions, upkeep, scope drift and obsolescence. Forecast reuse is an uncertain random variable, including zero. The half-hour loss is retained as evidence that a favorable point estimate can still misallocate funds.

The capability record carries scope, prerequisites, version, source digest, dependencies, license/provenance, measurement evidence and limitations. The current module is a fixed analytical instrument, not a fine-tuned model or autonomous learning loop. No optional Gym gain was observed; the modeled Gym is a separate C decision. Future practice must retain its original artifact, environment, feedback, update rule and protected evaluation before claiming an improved version.

For a new mission, stable source qualification may be reused only when identity and scope remain valid. Input bytes, labels, physical units, map direction, requested meaning, acceptance and current authority must still be checked. An unfamiliar biological purpose, nonlinear map or changed dependency is a reason for a new qualification path, not permission to reuse an old ACCEPT.

The English/French demonstration lets a reader inspect actual source-region moments and vary a mathematical rotation/scale. Those browser controls recompute a mathematical scenario from archived moments; they do not rerun Python, authenticate a network member, settle a job or record a new experiment.

The operating order matters. A useful delivery may justify continued service even when retention is rejected. An artifact may deserve research preservation even when commercial admission fails. Conversely, a technically successful tool cannot borrow profitability from a favorable scenario, and a profitable scenario cannot grant production authority. The connected example therefore ends at a justified decision point rather than pretending every flywheel stage has succeeded.

14. The next productive possibility

The most informative next step is four real, unfamiliar imaging briefs from willing laboratories or an imaging core, chosen to span different source layouts and genuinely unresolved measurement questions. The technical question is already sufficiently clear to avoid building more infrastructure first. The unresolved variable is the work required to establish meaning and complete an accepted handoff.

Before assignment, a beneficiary and evaluator should define useful quantities, tolerances, report content, deadline, maximum customer effort and acceptable price. Use a competent existing workflow E and E+ with the frozen artifact, balanced or randomized across briefs where feasible. Let both use standard libraries, caching and the same scientific clarification. Record preparation, provenance checking, adaptation, retries, correction, communication, verification, packaging, latency, model/tool usage and actual invoices or avoidable labor expense. Do not insert a fixed human-review allowance in place of observation.

The primary decision is complete incremental cost at matched accepted quality. If four eligible uses actually occur under the present CAD75/CAD65 assumptions, E+ must save more than 69.23 total avoidable minutes before an additional margin exists. A pilot may update that threshold if measured costs differ. Customer effort, late or partial delivery, unsupported tasks and refusals remain part of acceptance. A four-brief pilot estimates feasibility; it does not establish sustained specialist superiority.

A cheaper parallel check can test the frozen E rubric against U on fresh mission specifications with hidden semantic traps and an expert-quality oracle. Its purpose is to identify an acceptance or correction-cost difference large enough to pay for the method. Stop if both remain competent and the remaining uncertainty is commercially immaterial. No new paid API campaign is required merely to make the evaluation look ambitious.

Boundaries that could change the conclusion

This study covers affine transformations of supplied finite label partitions, not registration estimation, nonlinear deformation, topology, intensity conservation, biological validity or complete cells. It uses one development image and two held groups in one new image/timepoint. Hosted trials have one worker per arm, no randomization and incomplete cost telemetry. Customer decisions, prices, labor savings and collections are modeled; the conservative buyer screen and separately provisioned fixed-cost capacity are assumptions. Protected production microscopy scope remains unqualified. [E1–E6]

The productive opportunity is therefore a disciplined service test: determine whether a portable, source-bound measurement contract reduces real intake, correction or verification effort for recipients who do not already possess the same capability. If it does, retain selectively and test further transfer. If it does not, use the competent existing method and preserve the instrument as a reproducible research result. Both outcomes advance the mission without converting ambition into invented evidence.
