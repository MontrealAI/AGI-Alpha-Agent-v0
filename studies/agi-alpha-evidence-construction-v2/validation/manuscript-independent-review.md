## Findings requiring attention

### 1. Complete the additive source-to-contract linkage

**Material to the connected-example claim.** The executed economic protocol and path receipts still identify the development and two fresh sources as `PENDING_FINAL_ARTIFACT_BINDING`. The draft says the evaluator's acceptance is linked to the corresponding deliverable, and its closing provenance section promises source-qualified receipts.

Keep the original protocol and paths unchanged. For three initial modeled buyers receiving a common development packet, state that their compatible requirements are supplied assumptions. Binding an actual technical artifact to a simulated order still does not create customer acceptance, a paid job or a collection receipt.

### 2. Distinguish the four-use forecast from the two realized fresh jobs

**Material to admission and the next-test threshold.** B is USD60. The reported 7.2-minute threshold is correct for **four** forecast compatible future uses at USD125/hour. The connected success path actually realizes **two** fresh jobs after retention. For two uses, the threshold is **14.4 minutes per use**, before extra nonlabor, failure or requalification expense.

The policy's assumed 10-minute lower saving bound gives USD83.32 benefit over four uses and therefore admits against USD60. The same bound over only two uses gives USD41.66 and does not justify admission. This is an unverified reuse-forecast assumption, not evidence available from two recipient reports. The modeled reference uses a larger 20-minute actual saving and does clear the two-use threshold; its USD23.30 campaign increment remains correct. Display both horizons and make the real-world test compare against the horizon actually supported.

The whole-build threshold 17.76 minutes over five total deliveries is also arithmetically correct: `60*(125+60)/(125*5)`. Distinguish this ex-ante whole-construction comparison from the post-construction B-only decision. Necessary construction already charged in A is not charged again during retention.

### 3. Reconcile the reference cash package with the 2×2 coordination surcharge

**Material to claims of complete costs for one arrangement.** `coordination_cents=2000` is charged by the factorial diagnostic only. The main cash paths never charge this amount. As a result, the reference E+ first cohort contributes USD317.01 after C, whereas the joint 2×2 package contributes USD322.01 before C, which would be USD297.01 after the same USD25 C. The USD20 difference is not cent rounding.

Either clearly identify the main reference as three separately acquired orders whose ordinary intake is already in A, with the 2×2 adding an optional distinct group-arrangement expense, or add a separate prospective surcharge diagnostic for the coordinated package. Do not describe the two packages as the same fully costed arrangement. A common USD20 surcharge to both E and E+ would reduce their absolute profits by USD20 while preserving the USD23.30 difference; an asymmetric surcharge would change that difference. The evidence must specify which responsibilities incur it.

### 4. Qualify capacity completeness in stress paths

**Material limitation of the resource model, not the reference cash arithmetic.** The reference correctly counts routine support and the 60-minute core in capacity. However, the additional 30-minute support overrun and 15-minute failed-correction charges add money but no `work` capacity event. Thus the stored support-overrun capacity remains 288 minutes despite 90 extra modeled support minutes, and the common-failure path remains 288 despite 45 extra correction minutes. B and C have monetary prices but no separately specified capacity consumption.

Do not claim complete provider-capacity accounting for those stress paths. Cash losses remain valid under their assumptions. Explicitly limit the stored capacity result to initial construction and routine delivery/support, or preserve a prospective capacity correction separately. No additional broad testing is needed to identify this limitation; it follows directly from the charged activities and omitted work events.

### 5. Label the price expectation as the frozen decision approximation it is

**Useful qualification.** The price grid uses `acceptance_probability * collection_probability * full_price - complete_cost`. The event ledger instead retains the accepted USD50 deposit when a buyer defaults on its remaining balance. These are slightly different expected-collection models. With the reference probabilities, ignoring the retained accepted deposit is conservative by USD0.95 per initially served order: `0.95 * 0.02 * 50`.

The current text already says the policy is not a global equilibrium. Add that the grid is a frozen simplified pricing expectation, while the detailed paths separately execute deposits/default. Do not describe its selected price as an exact expected-profit optimum for every detailed cash-event process. This qualification does not require changing the selected grid or retrofitting confirmation.

## Checked conclusions that are supported

- **Reference increment:** `5*(210.99-169.33)-125-60 = 23.30`. This includes the assumed per-delivery savings five times, necessary core once, and B once. The common USD25 C cancels in the comparison. The first cohort is essentially tied before retention: E USD317.03 versus E+ USD317.01 after C. E+ is not already superior at that stage.
- **Funding:** After B, E+ has USD257.01 of collected contribution, exceeding its USD169.33 fresh-packet cost under the stated seed-preservation policy. U's inability to fund its next packet from USD192.02 is policy-conditional, not inability to use remaining seed. The draft states this distinction.
- **Source arithmetic:** The intervals 7.6485684–7.6678788 and 7.5005852–7.5205852 match the independent rational diagnosis. The 0.760 minimum convexity gap, inconsistent 95/91 versus 100/86 count margins, and conditional SD discrepancy are correctly reported. The strict corrected point remains held. No branch becomes an authoritative correction or ANCOVA replication.
- **Purpose preservation:** An accepted audit/conditional-reconstruction contract is explicitly different from a request for an identified corrected point. Keeping the latter held is necessary; the draft does so. The paper does not claim that refusing an exact-point request satisfies that original request.
- **Residual obligations:** The 25-minute verification reference is expressly an assumption. The 0/10/25/60-minute diagnostic holds the semantic requirement fixed and labels zero-time verification as hypothetical qualified automation. That adequately avoids treating 25 human minutes as a universal mandate. The urgent threshold 36 is conditional on that reference; changing residual work changes the threshold.
- **Market and quality assumptions:** The 72 worlds are appropriately described as stress worlds, not an empirical distribution or confidence interval. The fresh random worlds vary payment and economic conditions, while common/partial quality failures are separate named paths. No general empirical quality probability is established.
- **Rights:** The generic capsule excludes primary reports, customer material and evaluation keys; new code carries Apache-2.0. The draft appropriately distinguishes public article attribution from author-linked raw data lacking a verified redistribution license. Preserve that distinction in the final archive and source manifests.
- **Causal boundary:** Conventional algebra given the same formulation ties the instrument. The ordinary library ties E+. Current evidence therefore supports a usable constructed interface and a discovered source conflict, not a new mathematical method, graph premium or measured generalized invention skill.
