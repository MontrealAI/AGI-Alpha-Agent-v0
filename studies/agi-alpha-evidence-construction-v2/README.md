## What the conditional economy says

H is an explicitly estimated equivalent human service, not a market quote. Buyer values, participation, labor, quality probabilities, collections and support are assumptions. An estimated USD 332 outside option and a frozen finite price screen select a USD 325 quote with three hypothetical initial buyers.

At an assumed 20-minute E+ saving, five modeled deliveries produce:

| Configuration | Collections | A: delivery | B: additional retention | C: additional discovery | Profit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| E | 1,625.00 | 1,054.95 | 0.00 | 25.00 | 545.05 |
| E+ | 1,625.00 | 971.65 | 60.00 | 25.00 | 568.35 |
| Identical ordinary library | 1,625.00 | 971.65 | 60.00 | 25.00 | 568.35 |

All values are simulated USD. The first three E/E+ orders contribute 317.03/317.01 after A/C before retention; the incremental benefit occurs only under assumed later savings. The reference E+ cohort requires 757.99 opening cash including a 100 reserve. Refundable deposits remain liabilities. The model's two fresh orders can be funded from collected surplus, without treating seed as earnings.

**Retention thresholds:** more than 14.4 minutes saved per use for only two compatible uses; 7.2 minutes for a credible four-use forecast. Repaying the 125 construction plus 60 retention over five deliveries requires 17.76 average minutes. A 60-total-provider-minute urgent contract needs at least 36 minutes saved relative to E. The ordinary expert base offering is already feasible; a frontier expansion is conditional on meeting the stricter contract in actual operation.

Across 72 fresh assumed worlds, E+ wins 3, ties 57 and loses 12 relative to E. Those scenario counts are not market probabilities. Common failure loses 751.74; late collection can halt expansion. Optional extra Gym is rejected; no investment count or reinvestment fraction is compulsory.
