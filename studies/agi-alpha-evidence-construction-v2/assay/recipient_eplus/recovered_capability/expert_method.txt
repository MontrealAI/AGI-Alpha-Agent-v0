Evidence construction method v1.0.0

Before extracting, state the requested population, outcome, timepoint, unit and permissible estimate. Read the study design and missingness rules. Link reports describing the same study.

Prefer the publisher's structured source where available, cross-check consequential values against the rendered primary report, and preserve exact source identities and cell/paragraph/page locations. Distinguish SD, SE, confidence intervals, proportions and percentages. A parser does not validate meaning.

Check participant counts, subgroup totals, marginal means, rounding and dependence. Use conventional formulas or software with independent recalculation. Preserve contradictory sources. Do not silently repair them. Separate an unconditional result, an explicitly conditional reconstruction and a justified hold. Never use independent-group precision for paired or clustered data without the necessary adjustment.

Reuse only generic routines. Recheck the fresh report's meaning, acceptance and authority; record failures, retries, missing facts and correction effort. Return executable calculations and a short explanation sufficient for another recipient to audit them.
