## What a complete small connected example should report

Freeze acceptance conditions for a useful delivered artifact, including source semantics, required outputs, deadline, customer effort and price ceiling. Give U and E the same ordinary tool access and information; E+ adds only the new retained artifact. A strong conventional solver may tie. A useful transfer result does not require algorithmic novelty.

Use actual output checks and actual recipient artifact execution. If complete billing or human-time measurement is missing, do not impute zero. Keep actual accounting unknown and run a separately labeled full-cost model. A useful conditional result states a measurable residual-cost ceiling or required gain, rather than selecting a favorable gain and calling it evidence.

For a fixed accepted quality target, define complete cost per attempt `a_j`, acceptance probability `s_j`, and additional correction cost already included in `a_j`. Under a declared independent repeated-attempt approximation, expected delivery cost is `a_j/s_j`. A bounded-attempt version must price failure, support and refunds explicitly. At acceptance price `q` and collection probability `p`, service contribution before optional retention is `p*q - a_j/s_j - support - default handling`. This isolates E–U operation from E+–E retention.

For a retained capability with expected same-quality per-use saving `d`, forecast compatible uses `R`, additional B assessment/admission/upkeep cost `B`, and optional extra development `C`, retention needs `R*d > B+C` when C is part of that investment decision. If construction was already necessary to deliver the paid job and charged in A, do not charge it again in B/C. If it was unpaid research, do not call it customer-funded A. Report paid-job contribution and all-in campaign profit separately.

Missing complete-cost categories can be collected into residual `u`. A case with known-or-assumed modeled contribution `m` works only when `u < m`; give this threshold and stress it. Changes in success, market price, demand, transfer eligibility and model defaults must be allowed to erase the margin.

Every stochastic market outcome remains simulated. Earned funds can finance a next simulated task only after acceptance and collection, after reserving known liabilities and support. Show a first-job contribution, total campaign profit, cash before the next job, and an explicit subsequent use or stop. Do not claim commercial expansion if the competent baseline also meets the declared feasibility conditions.

## Admission feasibility

The unchanged research Alpha gate requires6–24 independent paired mission clusters, fresh evidence, a positive one-sided95% Student-t lower per-use bound, and positive lifetime net benefit after evaluation and lifecycle costs. A small same-recipient timing exercise does not supply independent clusters merely by repeating code many times. The bound is conditional on the model and cannot validate assumptions.

Use prior `gate_reachability.assess_bound`: if per-use delta is bounded by M, reserve by S, expected uses R, remaining lifecycle L and assessment A, lifetime lower net benefit cannot exceed `(M-S)R-L-A`. A nonpositive or insufficient upper bound rejects assessment before spending; reaching the bound is not admission. Preserve rejected-candidate costs and never relabel prospective prescreen savings as recovered past profit.

If real E+–E evidence is null or incomplete, the research artifact can be retained for reproducibility while **economic network admission remains unsupported**. A separate modeled scenario may demonstrate exactly the threshold for conditional admission. This is more informative than either inventing positive gains or refusing to run the scenario.
