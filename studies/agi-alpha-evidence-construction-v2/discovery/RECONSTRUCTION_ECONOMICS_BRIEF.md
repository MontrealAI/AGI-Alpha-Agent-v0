# Complete economic model for upstream report-to-evidence reconstruction

Proposed small model; parameters remain unfrozen until the experimental service scope and calibration source are verified.

## Offering

One accepted report-to-evidence packet reconstructs a specified source table, binds each extracted quantity to location and meaning, records transformations, exposes unresolved ambiguity, and returns a reproducible calculation. It is not a full systematic review, medical recommendation or verified replication of every source claim. A narrow human analyst providing the same packet is H. Price and H effort must match this exact scope.

Observed software acceptance and observed recipient use can populate a delivery receipt. External buyer acceptance, human labor, provider billing, collection and service liability remain separate fields. A research evaluator's accepted packet must never become a commercial receipt by relabeling.

## Four configurations

| Configuration | Supplied capability | Main contrast |
|---|---|---|
| H | Equivalent human analyst, ordinary tools, identical scope | Primary commercial reference; estimated hours/rate if no sourced quote |
| U | Competent ordinary frontier-model use, identical sources and tools | Same-model starting point, no deliberately weak prompt |
| E | Frozen expert extraction/verification workflow | Operational Alpha relative to U |
| E+ | E plus exact retained executable compiler/checker | Incremental transfer benefit relative to E |

The strong conventional expert may use the same common libraries, statistical formulas and existing products. Only the retained artifact is withheld for the E/E+ treatment. If an equivalent ready-made artifact is already available to E, mark `already_available` and reject its incremental-Alpha claim.

## One connected event chain

1. Initial buyer receives a priced specification with explicit acceptance, deadline and input obligations, then accepts, defers or declines under a declared value/outside-option model.
2. The enterprise reserves complete expected delivery cost, refund liability and support obligations before beginning. It executes source reconstruction and bounded correction. Failed attempts cost money in modeled A even when not accepted.
3. A delivery is accepted only if the predeclared quality and timing conditions hold. Invoice and collection are separate; default and delay do not change the accepted artifact.
4. The enterprise inspects candidate retention. A necessary feasibility bound runs before B assessment. B contains only additional assessment, packaging, admission, security/rights, transfer and upkeep; construction needed for the first paid outcome is already A.
5. Optional C is extra Gym/R&D beyond the paid task. Initial unpaid research is C, not relabeled as customer-funded A. Complete campaign profit subtracts C even if a narrower service-contribution line does not.
6. A different recipient actually uses the recovered archive on an unfamiliar source. Its competent E control gets the same evidence and ordinary tools. Current mission semantics, authority and output acceptance are rechecked.
7. Only collected surplus above obligations/reserve can fund the next simulated order. The final decision may be ordinary E production with no retention, retained E+ service, an additional bounded experiment, or STOP.

## Cost completeness without invented telemetry

Each unique activity has a stable ID, A/B/C bucket and one of the native nine categories: model, tools, compute, storage, network, human, acceptance, correction, overhead. Zero is allowed only as an explicit scenario assumption or a measured absence; actual unknown remains null. Cash expenditure and operator opportunity cost are separate subtotals.

For each configuration j:

`A_j = model_j + tools_j + compute_j + storage_j + network_j + residual_human_j + acceptance_j + correction_j + overhead_j`.

Corrections and retries are events, not a hidden multiplicative markup. Latency and customer effort are constraints as well as optional monetary costs. Do not charge customer effort as enterprise cash; show it separately or use it explicitly in the buyer's participation utility.

Do not impose human review for a fixed arbitrary number of minutes. Model the actual unresolved obligations identified by the extraction experiment. If a fully automated checker can discharge an obligation in the supported scope, cost its execution. If source semantics remain uncertain, report required human inspection and a range calibrated to comparable work.

The external reported41-minute median saving, if verified, is a calibration observation about its original workflow and evaluation population. It is not this artifact's saving. The modeled grid must include zero and negative incremental improvement. Do not infer model-token consumption from output length or internal agent elapsed time.

## Useful break-even quantities

At a matched accepted quality, let `t_E` and `t_E+` be residual paid expert minutes, `r` the labor rate/hour and `c_other` all nonlabor complete costs. For a compatible fresh order:

`d = r*(t_E - t_E+)/60 - (c_other,E+ - c_other,E)`.

If B assessment plus additional lifecycle/upkeep costs total `B`, and optional extra development is `C`, conditional retention over R compatible uses requires:

`d > (B + C)/R`.

Equivalently, the required time saving is:

`t_E - t_E+ > 60*((B+C)/R + c_other,E+ - c_other,E)/r`.

For a buyer accepted price q, collection probability p and support/default handling costs h, complete service contribution is `p*q - A_j - h`. This is a simple one-attempt expression; the implemented bounded-retry tree should sum all paid branches explicitly. If unknown complete costs sum to u, report the admissible residual bound `u < p*q - known_or_assumed_costs`. This is a decisive, measurable next-test target.

Pricing can enumerate a small predeclared grid up to the H-equivalent outside-option and explicit buyer-value cap. Buyers may decline and the enterprise may choose zero production. Optimize using only development assumptions, freeze the selected policy, then evaluate fresh assumed worlds. A favorable post-hoc price is not confirmation.

## Minimal factorial and stress design

Compare independent per-order reconstruction, shared reusable compiler alone, coordinated orders alone and their combination. Allow the unrestricted expert to use all four, so a shared arrangement does not receive an artificial monopoly on ordinary business practice. State whether the empirical artifact actually removes repeated work; demand compatibility is an assumption unless observed.

Use a small table of decisive paths instead of a giant simulator:

- All accepted, collected on time.
- Buyer declines before work.
- Withdrawal after shared construction and one partial delivery.
- Common semantic defect causes correction or refund for all affected orders.
- One buyer defaults after accepted delivery.
- Final payment arrives after the proposed next job must start.
- Transfer does not help or adds adaptation cost.
- Model defaults absorb the retained method, making its advantage zero.

For every path show accepted outcomes, A/B/C, gross collections, earned collections, profit, refundable liability, receivables, reserve, minimum available cash, and next action. A positive expected contribution with a negative required-time cash balance is not financeable.

## Evidence interpretation

Actual extraction/compilation and recipient execution support a technical research-delivery claim. A fully priced scenario can support conditional feasibility. Neither grants live economic admission. The existing modeled lifecycle may admit the actual archive under explicit synthetic assumptions, while actual evidence remains insufficient and production scope remains held. An ordinary library with identical archive and permissions should produce identical outputs; no graph-specific performance advantage is needed.

If H, U, E and E+ all meet the declared requirements, report comparative economics or a tie. Newly feasible work requires a predeclared condition the competent baseline fails and the enhanced configuration meets, with the failed condition traceable to a demonstrated change or an explicit scenario assumption.

The next real-world test should target the dominant uncertainty: a metered analyst reconstructs one unfamiliar eligible paper with and without the retained artifact; record all correction, semantic review, customer clarification and provider billing, then request a scoped paid pilot only after this complete packet and price are reviewable.
