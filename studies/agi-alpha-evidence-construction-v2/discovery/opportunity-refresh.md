# Bounded opportunity refresh: a connected next experiment

Research date: 27 October 2026. This is an independent, bounded candidate screen for the new additive edition, not a claim of customer demand or a replacement for earlier evidence. It proposes a focused extension beyond the previous study's supplied secondary tables. It excludes the active PEtab identifiability/model-reuse lane and the economic-construction lane.

## Recommendation

Select **claim-linked primary-source reconstruction** for a small, explicitly scoped family of reports. Deliver an inspectable extraction and executable quantitative claim package from original reports; use its actual generic evidence-schema, source-locator, consistency-checking and conversion routines on an unfamiliar report. This tests the prior study's unexecuted boundary: source meaning and reconstruction, rather than millisecond recomputation of already supplied effect sizes. Price and retention remain conditional until complete effort is measured. A quality tie is acceptable and commercially informative; improvement must not be presumed.

The narrow unresolved gap is that an executable analysis may repeat mechanically without agreeing with its article, while a collection of extracted numbers may omit the population, outcome, timepoint, unit or dependence structure needed for valid reuse. The proposed offering bridges those boundaries. It is not a new data-extraction algorithm, new reproducibility platform, first evidence card, autonomous peer review, or a substitute for clinical judgment.

## Evidence and calibration boundaries

**S1 — Code Ocean, verification process.** The official documentation describes staff checking of capsules and mechanical repeatability; it explicitly says that checking agreement with article results is outside that verification. This establishes a product-scope distinction, not proof that no competitor verifies claims. [Official source](https://docs.codeocean.com/osl-guide/publishing-on-code-ocean/the-verification-process/code-oceans-verification-process-for-computational-reproducibility-and-quality). Retrieved as `turn34view1`.

**S2 — Cochrane Handbook chapter 5.** The chapter distinguishes studies from reports, requires linking multiple reports, and describes preserving sufficiently unambiguous data and conversion decisions for later synthesis. It is an established expert baseline, not proposed invention. [Official source](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-05). Retrieved as `turn35view1`; chapter updated October 2019, Handbook version 6.5 (2024).

**S3 — Gartlehner et al., Annals of Internal Medicine 2025, author institution record.** The prospective comparison covered six reviews, 63 studies and 9,341 data elements. LLM extraction followed by human verification achieved 91.0% accuracy, versus 89.0% for the human-only workflow; major errors remained 2.5% and 2.7%, respectively. Median extraction time was 41 minutes lower per study with assistance. The authors note subjective adjudication and difficulty consistently tracking time. This supports sensitivity ranges for assisted work, not a guarantee for current models, our domain, unattended delivery, or E+ over E. [Author institution](https://www.rti.org/publication/artificial-intelligence-assisted-data-extraction-with-a-large-lan), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7326/ANNALS-25-00739). Retrieved as `turn35view0`. An attempted PMC full-text retrieval returned a browser challenge; that full text was not inspected.

**S4 — BuildingSync official project.** A common schema and accompanying validators already support exchange and reuse of building-energy audit data. Consequently an independent invention claim for standardized audit exchange would be untenable; any opportunity must concern a specific unresolved conversion or evidence-quality need. [Official source](https://buildingsync.net/). Retrieved as `turn35view2`.

**S5 — NREL/DOE simuwatt Audit, search-level evidence only.** The primary publication describes an existing integrated energy-audit workflow and model/component reuse. Its PDF was identified but not fully inspected; it supports deferral pending a specific gap, not a quantitative performance claim. [Primary PDF](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy13osti/57469.pdf), search result `turn34search0`.

## Smallest connected study

1. Freeze the beneficiary, output contract, time limit, source access and acceptance criteria before the first task. Choose an original research report whose key numerical table can be independently checked. Preserve authoritative source bytes and rights.
2. U receives a competent direct request. E receives a concise established protocol: source locator, outcome/timepoint/units, independent calculation and ambiguity handling. E+ later receives the same protocol plus the frozen actual retained artifact. All configurations receive normal available tools; the expert is permitted to construct alternatives.
3. Deliver original-source values and their meanings, a machine-readable record, an executable conversion/check routine, and a result linked to its inputs. Correct errors before calling the task accepted and count correction effort.
4. Transfer the actual artifact to an independent recipient for unfamiliar source work. Protect acceptance answers; do not reuse the development paper as a fresh test. If standard tools already supply the generic routines, give those to the baseline and claim only additional tested functionality.
5. Model sale and retention decisions using complete delivery cost A, incremental retention/admission/upkeep B and optional extra development C. Current model bill telemetry may be unavailable; model token ranges explicitly and treat observed wall time as a separate measure.
6. Stress one failure, partial delivery, no participating buyer, withdrawal, correction/support cost, delayed collection and complete shared-capability failure. No favorable learning multiplier is necessary. Conditional viability is a useful result if its assumptions and break-even thresholds are exposed.

## Transformation witness

- **Original purpose:** deliver a quantitatively faithful, reusable answer to a defined research question from original sources.
- **Binding restriction:** prose/PDF values and separate analysis code do not preserve enough meaning for a later operator to confidently reuse them without repeating source inspection.
- **Removability hypothesis:** a scoped, source-linked intermediate representation can preserve the stable extraction/conversion work and expose what must be checked afresh.
- **Replacement:** a versioned evidence contract with executable validation, explicit unknowns and per-mission selection of accepted values.
- **Executable realization:** original-source reconstruction followed by actual artifact transfer and recomputation on an unfamiliar report.
- **Falsifier:** wrong source linkage or interpretation, hidden requirement relaxation, no complete-cost reduction over an already competent recipient, or transfer that saves work only by bypassing necessary review.

## Useful conditional thresholds without invented success

Let `h_E` and `h_E+` be complete delivery labor-equivalent hours at matched accepted quality, `r` a disclosed replacement-service cost rate, `t_E` and `t_E+` complete model/tool costs, `B` incremental assessment/retention/upkeep, and `n` realized compatible future uses. Reuse is economically justified only when `n * ((h_E-h_E+) * r + t_E-t_E+) > B`, after expected requalification, failures and negative transfer. If the observed effort is indistinguishable, show the minimum required savings `B/n`; do not substitute the published 41-minute AI-versus-human median for an E+-versus-E effect.

Paid delivery has its own price and acceptance threshold. A profitable expert service can exist even if this inequality fails. The model must preserve that possibility. A newly feasible offering requires a preregistered quality/deadline/effort/price condition that the competent baseline actually fails; otherwise report improved economics or no advantage.
