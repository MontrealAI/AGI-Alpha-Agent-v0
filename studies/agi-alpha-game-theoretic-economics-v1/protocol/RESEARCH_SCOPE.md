# Game-Theoretic Economics of Compounding Capability

This is an additive research contribution on the actual AGI ALPHA component-upgrade decision offering. The buyer role is the maintainer of the native agent's FastAPI request boundary and mission-input model. The useful deliverable is a source-bound compatibility decision, exact proposed component-lock patch, before/after observations, independent verification, reproduction instructions and unresolved-scope statement. Component preservation permits carrying evidence into full qualification; it does not authorize replacing the native application lock.

## Bounded discovery and recurring need

Generic version lists and API-difference reports were rejected as the offering: Dependabot, Renovate and Griffe are strong existing alternatives. Source-inventory speedups and baseline-probe caching were also rejected as the proposed economic investment. The two probes execute in parallel and include the unchanged fifteen-second timeout; removing one probe cannot be represented as halving turnaround. Subsecond CPU savings do not identify substantial economic value.

The retained complete-evidence producer is a real asset with a prior source freeze and fresh single-dependency results. The additional production study evaluates four new grouped version tuples under a prospective protocol. The package bodies were seen before; the combined targets and results were not. These are research-proposed maintainer options, not procurement demand, actual upgrades or independent defect families. Replays, assertions, repeated exports and unchanged requests count as zero new jobs.

Public operating requirements support the existence of recurring dependency maintenance, while grouping and automation mean that every upstream release is not a billable job. The GOV.UK example is calibration evidence only; GOV.UK is not an AGI ALPHA customer or study participant.

## Distinct contribution

The market experiment adds a repeated behavioral procurement game, paired investment counterfactuals, finite working capital, endogenous rejection/switching, responding competitors and explicit sensitivity to unidentified inputs. It does not claim a solved equilibrium. Its cash-event history is a simulation audit record projected into the unchanged canonical shadow ledger; it is not a replacement operating ledger.

Three questions remain separate: the usefulness and price attractiveness of the service, the net value of the exact capability investment, and any advantage of AGI ALPHA's improvement process over an equally equipped conventional builder. Strong conventional parity is retained. A profitable modeled service can coexist with a losing investment, and a worthwhile investment can coexist with a tie between builders.

## Scope of the monetary model

USD is a scenario numeraire. Manager's USDC route and Jobs' AGIALPHA route remain distinct source-inspected protocol capabilities, without live invocation here. Token issuance, transfer volume, price appreciation and customer savings are never counted as enterprise revenue. The canonical observed account retains zero study customer collections. Simulated invoices, collections, refunds, obligations and liquidity are explicitly typed as modeled events.

The next construction cycle requires another actually constructed and tested asset with a positive, affordable incremental case. A scheduler, a growing modeled balance or an untested project envelope does not satisfy that condition.
