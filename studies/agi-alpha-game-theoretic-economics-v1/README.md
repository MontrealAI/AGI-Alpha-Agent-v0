### Funding is a separate question

The original strict rule uses:

~~~text
min(cash − unpaid obligations − reserve,
    collected receipts − all accrued costs − reserve)
~~~

Reserve is encumbered, not expensed twice. This rule requires a reserve out of cumulative earnings even when seed capital supplies liquidity. With $200 reserve and $32.69 adoption it generally needs $232.69 prior net earnings. No initial adoption met that rule; this does not prove that no smaller positive profit could finance an adoption under another funding policy.

The preselected seed 2001 path pays $32.69 in week 8 from $745.37 currently spendable after reserve and obligations. It pauses one week and uses the asset on 17 subsequent simulated jobs. It collects $560, incurs $437.35 prospective costs including a $20 refund, and ends with $122.65 profit and $1,122.65 cash including seed. Paired gain is $136.56. Historical cost changes profit to −$238.87. Strict earned availability is −$77.35.

Initial seed funding does not disqualify a future Flywheel. The continuation separately asks whether later operating activity leaves resources to finance a **distinct tested worthwhile asset** without another injection. Sixteen original diagnostic paths have positive strict earned availability at the end, but none contains a qualified second asset.
