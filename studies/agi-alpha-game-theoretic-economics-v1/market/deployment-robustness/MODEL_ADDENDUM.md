# Bounded USD20 deployment robustness and resource provenance

This is a new user-authorized extension after the preserved primary, exploratory and fixed-target diagnostic stages. It asks whether the previously identified prospective deployment region persists across bounded changes in buyer friction, recurrence, delivery conditions and competitors. Its fresh stochastic seeds are held out from the earlier campaigns. The design remains conditional on the declared ten-minute finalization advantage, costs and buyer value model; fresh random draws cannot identify those unknown economic parameters.

The price policy is fixed **target** USD20 with the original unit-cost floor. The finite enterprise starts with the same USD1000 cash and USD200 reserve, and may use seed-inclusive available cash for the first actual-asset deployment. There is no replenishment, extra external capital or presumed reinvestment percentage. The original expected-benefit, affordability, deployment-delay and failure rules remain in force. Full-history accounting charges the assumed historical R&D at opening; prospective adoption-only accounting discloses that earlier R&D as sunk. Neither convention proves that historical R&D was originally financed from customer work.

## A disclosed funding-provenance view

The previous strict funding metric deducted the reserve in both its cash and generated-resource bounds and required recovery of the opening historical R&D. Preserve that field exactly. The new question is whether an enterprise with explicitly seed-financed historical research can make a subsequent investment from cash generated by delivery after recovering all prospective expenses.

At week t, write C for cash on hand, L for unpaid accrued obligations, R for the one reserve, Y for collected simulated receipts, E for all accrued expenses, H for historical opening R&D actually paid from seed, and U for outstanding generated-resource earmarks whose bills have not yet accrued. The reporting view is:

\[
G_t=\max\{0,\min(C_t-L_t-R,\;Y_t-E_t+H_t)-U_t\}.
\]

This equation creates no cash. The first bound forbids spending reserves, obligations or uncollected receivables. The second excludes the initial seed and conservatively requires recovery of setup, first adoption, delivery, failed work, upkeep, refunds and other prospective accrued costs. Only the expressly seed-paid opening historical R&D bill receives H credit. That bill remains in full-history expenses and in actual modeled cash depletion. An unpaid historical bill receives no credit. No prospective cost is relabeled historical to increase availability.

The reserve is imposed once in the cash bound under this convention. It is not also subtracted from already generated profit. This is a disclosed allocation choice, not evidence that a prior conclusion under a different funding definition was wrong. A sensitivity keeps historical R&D in the recovery requirement by setting H=0. The unchanged original strict metric remains separately visible, including negative values.

An earmark U is only an unaccrued commitment of generated resources. It reduces both available cash and available generated resources. For example, a cash bound100, generated bound500 and earmark80 leave20 available, not100. When the associated cost becomes a bill in E, release its earmark; otherwise the same obligation would be charged twice. Unless an actual second candidate is qualified and an allocation is explicitly authorized in the frozen research protocol, U=0. No illustrative token allocation, anticipated invoice, projected appreciation or buyer saving enters Y.

Both weekly and terminal G are reported. Terminal receipts collected after the52-week service horizon do not retroactively finance an earlier investment. A funding-availability claim identifies the actual week, cash, liabilities, reserve, recovered expenses and applicable construction horizon. It does not convert an eventual balance into prior affordability.

The bounded second-asset discovery ended NO-GO, and its actual incremental monetary cost D remains unknown. It is not present in the original core bills and is not part of the historical opening-R&D allocation. Consequently every G here is explicitly **BEFORE_UNPRICED_SECOND_ASSET_DISCOVERY**: an upper funding ceiling conditional on a missing cost, not an identified all-in spendable amount. For any attributed D>=0, post-search funding is at most max(0,G-D), and fixed-path enterprise profit is reduced by that D. There is no default assumption that D=0, no invented upper cost bound, and no addback of failed prospective discovery as historical research. This closed-form qualification requires no additional scenario grid or new random draws.

## Qualification is separate from affordability

A positive G alone does not authorize or demonstrate a new capability. The bounded production investigation inspected three candidate burdens and ended NO-GO; its source-identified record is bound to the freeze. No second asset is supplied, no generated resources are earmarked, and no second investment is made. Qualification would have required an observed material burden, frozen acceptance criteria, fresh tests, a strong conventional comparator and complete cost uncertainty. The missing evidence is recorded. The unchanged simulator's next-asset policy retains reserves; no automatic purchase or hypothetical improvement is inserted into these market trajectories.

No claim of repeated self-financing, autonomous invention or self-acceleration follows from the resource-provenance view. These require an actual worthwhile second construction and fresh measured use. Human or assistant engineering contributions remain separately labeled.
