# Second-capability discovery gate: no construction justified in this extension

**Decision: NO-GO for a second financed productive asset.** A bounded review of three candidate burdens found no candidate that both addresses a material remaining burden in the chosen component-assessment offering and has a defensible fresh-input, measurement and complete-cost plan within the current authorized execution closure. This is a decision to preserve resources after discovery, not a claim that every possible future improvement is worthless.

The continuation authorized at most fifteen minutes of discovery before any new construction or outcome inspection. This gate uses only already recorded source, resource, failure and custody evidence. It creates no production mission, new customer job, investment, inferred labor saving or simulated revenue. The original four joint missions, twelve assessments and all freezes remain unchanged. `second-capability-gate.json` records the evidence identities and decision boundary.

## What remains after the first actual asset

The existing retained producer and strong conventional producer each completed all four new grouped-change assessments with zero explicit finalizations. The ordinary arm required four explicit AI research-agent dispositions; those are recorded actions, not measured human minutes. All twelve assessments passed their finite component contract. The result identifies an already-addressed coordination burden; it does not identify a further remaining manual review step that a second asset has demonstrably removed. Active human attention, customer effort, authoring inference, complete construction and maintenance costs remain unknown. See [the actual summary](../production/joint-summary.json) and [offering/asset boundary](../production/OFFERING_AND_CAPABILITY.md).

The original real body-timeout witness is fifteen seconds. Both before/after probes run in the same paired execution, so deleting one baseline does not imply halved delivery turnaround. The previously rejected baseline-observation cache remains rejected. A duplicate importer/custody runtime remains outside the active owner boundary for `contrib/upgrade_decision`; neither is reopened as a disguised second asset.

## Reopening criterion and investment consequence

Reopen the gate when an actual new maintained-source or requirement change exposes a recurring burden that survives the existing retained and conventional tools, and a useful acceptance contract can be frozen before candidate confirmation. Record the strongest practical adoption alternative, a fresh source-bound cohort, unchanged safety/authority boundaries, local resource receipts, and explicit bounds or unknowns for implementation, failed attempts, verification, integration, upkeep, model authoring and residual attention. Measure a service effect that buyers can value—coverage, reliability, remaining effort or actual turnaround—before assigning conditional market benefits.

Any later investment must name that exact constructed and tested asset, fit cash actually available at the decision point in the model, and repay its complete modeled cost from its own subsequent incremental effects. This discovery admits no second asset and therefore supplies no second investment event. Keeping reserves is the supported action at this gate. The current study can investigate conditional financeability of its existing asset; it does not demonstrate two successful construction cycles or self-acceleration.
