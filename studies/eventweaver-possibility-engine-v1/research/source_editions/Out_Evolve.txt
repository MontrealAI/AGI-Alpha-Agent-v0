                                                        Abstract
        The most consequential output of intelligence may be a capability that makes further discoveries
    possible. We propose Out-Evolve: a research and construction architecture that directs transforma-
    tional intelligence toward new means of experimentation, production and subsequent invention. Its
    central engineering target is to construct a system for which a simpler model is true, while preserving
    the useful purpose that motivated the work. A conditional identification result makes the potential
    leverage precise. An arbitrary scalar response over twenty binary settings requires 1,048,576 exact
    point queries to determine completely. A constructed platform with a qualified interaction-order
    bound of two can be identified from 211 exact scalar measurements. A compatible additional setting
    can double the configuration family with 21 further calibrations. These results concern declared func-
    tion classes; the physical structure, precision and inheritance guarantees must be earned through
    construction and qualification. Two executed exact studies examine complementary mechanisms:
    reopening a retained operation for a successor, and constructing a measurement architecture whose
    users can support its cost. We then specify a commercial program for SUCCESSOR Ω: develop
    a family of accepted manufacturing capabilities, retain the means that make them useful, and use
    those assets and available surplus to help construct a different successor capability. The paper
    contributes a construction objective, conditional theory, inspectable mechanism studies and a three-
    stage evaluation protocol. Its ambition is a company whose discoveries repeatedly expand what
    available intelligence can know, build and economically deploy.

1 Build what makes the next breakthrough possible
The next breakthrough may be a new way to make breakthroughs.
   An intelligent system can discover a material, design a machine, or improve a process. Its more
consequential output may be an instrument that makes a class of materials experimentally accessible,
a production architecture that makes a family of machines economical, or a method that helps construct
the next instrument. Each achievement changes the starting conditions for subsequent intelligence. The
valuable inheritance is a new capacity to act, learn and build.

   Transformational intelligence discovers a fundamentally different game to play by
   constructing better conditions for achieving the original purpose. Out-Evolve directs
   that intelligence toward capabilities from which entire families of discoveries and
   businesses can grow.

    The central engineering move is unusually demanding: construct a system for which a simpler model
is true. A process may be diﬀicult to understand because useful state is hidden, experiments confound
several causes, or components interact in uncontrolled ways. An invention can change those conditions.
A new reference path can make a quantity identifiable. A stabilized interface can make an interaction
repeatable. A different physical decomposition can make the behavior of a whole apparatus follow from
a manageable number of measurements. Intelligence has then changed the system it must understand.
    The possible difference is large. An arbitrary response over twenty binary settings has 1,048,576
independently variable values. A platform whose useful response is guaranteed to contain only single-
setting and pairwise interactions has 211 coeﬀicients. Appropriate exact measurements determine its
entire response family. The mathematical contrast is conditional; the diﬀicult and valuable invention
is the useful architecture that earns the guarantee. Its construction, precision and original-purpose
requirements are explicit in section 2.
    Now give the capability a successor. A production cell that serves external customers can also manu-
facture some of the fixtures, calibrated structures and test apparatus needed by a different production
cell. Its retained implementation becomes construction material. Accepted output provides receipts;
resources remaining after obligations can finance further construction. Knowledge, instruments, pro-
ductive hardware and capital begin to reinforce one another, provided each transition is technically
valid and economically supported.
    This is the proposed business of SUCCESSOR Ω: the Intelligence Leverage Company. Its product
is a qualified means of producing valuable outcomes across a family. Its strategic question is: which
capability would make the next valuable capabilities possible, and what would make constructing it
worthwhile now?

1.1 The progression beyond the foundations
Transformational Intelligence makes the formulation of a problem an object of discovery, with an explicit
bridge back to the user’s purpose [5]. Invent the Economy makes participation, technical construction
and cash timing part of the same design problem [4]. Out-Evolve applies both ideas to the creation of
discovery and production capacity: construct a useful regime, identify what becomes possible within it,
and use the resulting capability to help construct its successor.
   The progression has three linked achievements. First, a new capability changes what can be
observed, tested, controlled or produced. Second, a capability family supports fresh purposes through
an identified common mechanism. Third, a successor capability uses retained technical means and
economically available resources to acquire a different accepted function. Naming a family does not
establish transfer; each link needs its own evidence.

2 Construct a system for which a simpler model is true
A powerful way to improve discovery is to change the system being investigated. Instead of asking
an ever larger model to understand an opaque process, construct interfaces, operating conditions and
component boundaries that make its behavior identifiable. The retained invention is then more than
a prediction: it is a working arrangement under which a whole family of predictions becomes justified.

                          Construct a system for which a simpler model is true.

  This is a physical and computational design objective. Isolation, controlled preparation, stabilized
operating conditions, calibrated reference paths and explicitly limited couplings can remove interactions
that would otherwise make every new configuration a separate experiment. The mathematical case
below shows what such a construction could buy. It does not assume that these conditions can be
obtained cheaply, or that a sparse fit establishes them.

Exact-query counts can conceal demanding precision. For the calibration design in equation (3), read

changing how it is constructed. The experimentally economical family is earned through physical
structure, qualified access and retained evidence. The resulting advantage depends on the working
construction and its qualified scope.

3 A discovery system that constructs its next advantages
Out-Evolve is an outer research and construction architecture. Its candidate is an executable change
to the means of achieving a purpose: a representation, an experiment, an instrument, a product
architecture, a process or an adoption arrangement. Search engines, coding agents, scientists and
engineers can implement its construction steps. The architecture directs their work toward a capability
with consequences beyond its first use.

3.1 From an obstacle to an invention requirement
A binding obstruction can tell the constructor what must change. Two observationally indistinguishable
worlds call for a discriminating experiment. Uncontrolled interactions call for a different interface or
a qualified model of their joint effects. A failure to fund a shared instrument calls for a change in the
relationship between its full cost and beneficiaries’ qualified alternatives. An opaque retained operation
can call for reopening its implementation.

from an unresolved search. The architecture accommodates both. Its requirement is that the status of
the evidence determines what may be inferred from it.

3.2 The construction cycle
1. Fix the valued purpose. Record the useful output, quality, permissions, resource limits and time
   horizon. Identify which inherited means may change.
2. Find the consequential restriction. Use capable existing methods. Return an accepted solution,
   a qualified obstruction, or a testable hypothesis about what blocks progress.
3. Design the missing capability. Search representations, experiments, interfaces, hardware and
   operating arrangements. Admit complementary bundles when no component works alone.
4. Construct and qualify it. Build the realization, establish its assumptions, and show how its
   acceptance implies the original useful outcome. Evaluate full discovery and deployment cost.

5. Recompute the world it changes. Recheck alternative methods, customer alternatives, un-
   certainty, liabilities and maintenance. An invention can improve competing routes as well as its
   own.
6. Freeze and transfer. Give a different investigator fresh purposes after freezing the artifact. Re-
   tain the implementation, calibration, evidence, costs and legitimate adaptation rights that actually
   support transfer.
7. Commission a successor. Use the capability to construct a different accepted capability. Compare
   the complete campaign, including unsuccessful construction and external inputs.

   These steps need not be serial in implementation. Alternative designs and experiments can proceed
in parallel, while evidence and resource commitments remain accountable. A prototype may disprove
the original diagnosis. A customer may require a different quality envelope. A qualified alternative
may remove the business case. Those discoveries change the next construction decision.

   Useful capabilities can also be complements. A programmable instrument without valid measure-

milestones. A claim of future complementarity still needs a concrete joint realization and an affordable
path to it.

3.4 A productive organization of agents
Computer use connects intelligence to existing design, simulation, laboratory and business software.
Persistent agents can maintain hypotheses, designs and experimental records across long campaigns.
Recent work explores both long-horizon computer-use context and persistent multi-agent scientific
environments [7, 19]. Those capabilities make the architecture more implementable; they do not
establish that an entire research or industrial occupation has been automated.
   The proposed organization gives agent teams distinct constructive roles: identify restrictions, pro-
pose mechanisms, design discriminating experiments, compile implementations, challenge evidence and
assemble commercially acceptable work packages. Shared instruments and capability contracts make
their work composable. A discovery earns continued resources through reproducible usefulness to an-
other project. Agent population becomes valuable when it produces interoperable means of progress.
   The long-run objective is an organization whose accumulated instruments and productive assets let
new participants begin beyond the previous generation’s limits. Its inheritance includes machines that
work and agreements that can be performed, alongside code and ideas.

4.2 Construct the conditions under which the model is useful
The cell must expose consequential state: references for locating the part, clamping conditions, tool

8 The civilization-scale ambition
The distant ambition is an expanding capacity to construct useful capacity. Scientific instruments
improve access to phenomena. Qualified processes make more exact or eﬀicient components. Those
components support better instruments, factories and energy systems. Productive assets create the
means to extend that cycle. The return of an invention can include a new set of inventions that can
now be attempted economically.
   That objective reaches beyond the first manufacturing program. A new characterization method
could make a materials family searchable. A more observable process could reduce the intelligence
needed for reliable control. A new machine architecture could lower the cost of constructing scientific
equipment. An improved production method for energy hardware could increase the resources available
to further industry. Each is a candidate capability chain, with an engineering and adoption problem
at every link.

   The compounding frontier is the set of valuable capabilities we can afford to con-
   struct next. Intelligence becomes transformational when it changes that set.

   At the largest horizon, the program could extend from terrestrial instruments and factories to space-
based industrial infrastructure and, ultimately, stellar-scale useful energy capture. Reaching that
horizon would require advances in energy conversion, materials, precision production, autonomous
construction, transport and coordinated investment. A line of software cannot supply those assets. A
sustained succession of accepted productive capabilities could contribute to building them. The present
paper gives that ambition a unit of progress rather than a forecast or a growth law.
   Physical capacity and financial capacity must both remain real. Energy, materials, land or orbital
access, manufacturing lead times, reliability, maintenance and demand constrain each expansion. The
next binding bottleneck can change. A successful system allocates its intelligence to discovering which
construction now matters most, including cases where maintaining a reliable existing capability creates
more value than pursuing a new one.
   A future civilization of agents could inherit instruments, experimentally grounded knowledge, pro-
ductive hardware and legitimate institutions through which new participants can act. Its scale would
be measured in accepted science and useful production. Its enduring achievement would be to leave
the next generation more capable of making what it needs.

9 Conclusion
Out-Evolve proposes intelligence that constructs the conditions of further discovery. Its strongest move
is to make a useful system more knowable by changing how it is built. The resulting capability can
support a family of purposes and, with a valid inheritance bridge, help construct a successor. The
commercial counterpart is to design the production and purchase arrangement together, so accepted
value supplies the resources for further construction.
   The conditional theory identifies an information advantage and the structural evidence required to
earn it. The exact studies expose two mechanisms of the proposed architecture: inspectable inheri-
tance and economically viable coordination. The industrial challenge joins them through construction,
independent use and full accounting.
Build what makes the next breakthrough possible. Then use it to build what comes
after.

C Constructing a capability that can support its own adoption
A technical improvement can make a service cheaper without making its delivery supportable by its ben-

their information content, and uses the resulting coalition alternatives to decide which arrangements

this is immediate when 𝑇 ≤ 3𝑞 and otherwise follows from 𝑇 ≤ 9𝑞/2. As 𝑇 ≤ ∑𝑖 𝑚𝑖 , one can choose
𝑝𝑖 ∈ [𝐿, 𝑚𝑖 ] summing to 𝑇 . Every pair then satisfies 𝑝𝑖 + 𝑝𝑗 = 𝑇 − 𝑝𝑘 ≤ 𝑇 − 𝐿 ≤ 3𝑞. Scaling this vector
downward attains min(𝑇 , 𝑐𝑁 ) after the additional bound is imposed.

   The independent checker verifies the constructive formula on 648 rational cap cases. The proof gives
the full continuous domain; the finite checks guard against implementation mistakes. None of the caps
in those checks is a measured buyer valuation.
