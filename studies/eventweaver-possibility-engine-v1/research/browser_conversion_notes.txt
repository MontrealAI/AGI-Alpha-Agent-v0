Browser-reader integration record, 28 October 2026

Initial direct Pandoc conversion of the complete LaTeX manuscript preserved most prose but did not interpret its custom L paragraph-column type. It flattened all tables into tabular text, emitted seven PDF figure embeds rather than self-contained images, and retained the manual bibliography as an unnumbered block with empty citation spans. These defects were detected before final browser acceptance; that first conversion was not released.

The browser builder now expands local LaTeX inputs, replaces only the equivalent L column notation with standard paragraph columns, retains the manual bibliography's exact entry order while adding numbered stable anchors, resolves each citation against that map, and embeds figures rasterized directly from their source PDFs. It checks the source and HTML table, figure and bibliography counts for equality and fails on an unresolved citation. Mathematical expressions use native MathML and no online renderer. These are browser-transcription changes, not changes to the LaTeX manuscript, technical contracts, frozen product sources, commercial inputs or results.

The final browser acceptance additionally checks complete-paper sections, citations, figures, keyboard operations, responsive navigation, zero external asset requests, and 1,536 separate browser-oracle parity ticks. The browser parity check is not added to the technical confirmation sample. The final report and exact HTML SHA256 are in results/browser_build.json and results/browser_qa.json. Internal screenshots are kept separately from the public deliverables.
