SCALING DECISION — NO SUPPORTED BILLION-PLUS CLAIM

The selected USD1200 supported-library offering has not earned expansion. Primary trade sources establish a large photonics industry, but do not establish EventWeaver's eligible buyer count, compatible installed base, willingness to pay or incremental productive benefit.

SPIE's own February 2026 release reports approximately 1600 Photonics West exhibitors and USD381 billion of 2024 core-component revenue. Those are a broad prospect-discovery pool and an industry statistic. They are not this product's addressable buyers or revenue. Photonics21's actual 2024 report values instrumentation and optical measurement systems at USD16 billion in 2022, a broad category containing many products outside the frozen grammar. Its company definitions differ from SPIE's; the counts must not be added. EPIC's official indexed site reports over 800 members, also a mixed and overlapping pool rather than qualified OEM demand.

A precise ambition can be stated: USD1 billion of annual net incremental productive value in calendar 2031, measured in 2026 dollars and relative to the strongest feasible conventional/current-AI alternative. The identity 10000 active instruments times USD100000 net annual benefit reaches that number; neither factor has been evidenced. It is a falsifiable hurdle, not a forecast. At the frozen USD1200 ticket, USD1 billion annual revenue instead requires 833334 accepted packs. With 18 weekly delivery hours and 3 delivery plus 8 reserved support hours per order, one modeled delivery resource has a ceiling of about 85 packs or USD102109 gross revenue a year, before failures, renewals and acquisition limits. Multiplying agents cannot remove these obligations.

An illustrative narrower funnel of 1600 broad directory firms, 10% eligible, 25% adopting and two packs a year gives 80 packs and USD96000 revenue. Every funnel fraction is unverified. This arithmetic makes the missing evidence visible; it does not validate a small business either.

The fresh commercial model is adverse even before any billion-scale extrapolation: base mean full-project loss is USD29628, high-demand loss USD33810, and the identical-free-library scenario makes no deliveries. Current-AI improvements help competitors and leave a modeled loss of USD30721. These are conditional simulated findings, not measured field probabilities. The selected price is the least loss within a bounded 15-policy comparison; it is not a global optimum and the study does not prove every instrument business impossible.

The strongest newly justified opening is a named OEM-backed integration or licensing problem where the buyer can demonstrate that a new instrument capability has value beyond installed filters and competent free tooling. First spend a capped six analyst hours (USD810 modeled cost) to classify 30 named platforms from public documentation. Only after separate outreach authorization, use a bounded buyer-evidence exercise (USD1620 planning cap) to establish actual recent tasks, acceptance authority and outside costs. A subsequent customer-backed trial should have a capped budget, unchanged acceptance and observed integration/support costs. No outreach, contract or spending was performed by this review.

No monetary expected value of information is invented. The default is already no expansion, so these gates must earn the right to reopen that decision. Their purpose is to measure the missing commercial bridge, not to justify spending with a small assumed probability of an enormous future payoff.
