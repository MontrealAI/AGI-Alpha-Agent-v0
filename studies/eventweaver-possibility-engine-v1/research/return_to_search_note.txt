BOUNDED RETURN TO SEARCH — 28 OCTOBER 2026

DECISION: NO FLAGSHIP EARNED.
The completed EventWeaver design-pack experiment remains useful evidence, but its failed commercial policy cannot remain the transformational flagship. The replacement search produced three consequential hypotheses and three explicit rejections. None is promoted on the strength of an announcement, generated enthusiasm or synthetic substitute.

1. COUNTERFACTUAL EXPERIENCE FOUNDRY. Radical possibility: robots learn rare productive recovery actions without waiting for costly accidents. Missing means: experimentally calibrated action-conditioned experience generation, with a recovery controller that transfers to withheld real interactions. First offer must deliver that skill on one real integrator-owned fixture; a video pack is insufficient. NVIDIA Cosmos (October 21), Genie 4 (October 27), Gemini Robotics ER 2 public preview (October 27) and Argon demonstrations (October 13) make this worth revisiting. But open NVIDIA blueprints already generate/check synthetic data; Genie remains restricted preview with acknowledged physical/spatial drift. We accessed no model deployment, robot or real transfer trace. No measured customer saving or complete delivery cost exists. Rejected as this campaign's flagship; strongest conditional next opening.

2. LOCAL-FEEDSTOCK MATERIAL RECIPE COMMONS. Radical possibility: previously unusable local by-products become dependable productive inputs through adaptive characterization and qualification. The first offer must physically make and qualify a batch-specific recipe. Microsoft's October 20 Saint-Gobain case reports accelerated hypotheses worth testing; it does not demonstrate our proposed qualified product. Earlier MatterGen is crystal-generation prior art, not a ready concrete-mixture predictor. No samples, assays, lab quote or accepting producer were available. A literature/recipe advisory service removes the essential invention. Rejected.

3. PRIVATE CROSS-OWNER EXPERIMENTAL INSTRUMENT. Radical possibility: participants obtain an experimental conclusion that none can produce alone, without surrendering raw records. First offer must execute an authorized joint experiment whose estimand and disclosure constraints make the instrument indispensable. This is locally programmable in part, but mature DataSHIELD and SecretFlow are strong free alternatives; contracts, measurement compatibility and participant incentives remain the binding means. No authorized consortium/data or evidence of a cheaper unsupported operation exists. Synthetic parties would erase the critical barrier. Quine's September 29 research announcement does not supply accessible production capability: its access is phased and its evidence includes wet-lab validation. Rejected.
