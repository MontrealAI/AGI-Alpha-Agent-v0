INDEPENDENT ECONOMIC FINAL REVIEW — EVENTWEAVER V1
Date: 2026-10-28 14:06:17 UTC
Reviewer: separate same-session ecosystem/economics agent; not an independent institution.

DECISION
STOP standalone commercial expansion under the selected commercial-v1 policy. Retain the functioning instrument and its evidence. A bounded OEM co-development investigation may be a justified next research opening only under a new, prospectively frozen scope, cost ceiling, acceptance contract and stopping rule. No buyer commitment, market price, purchase or financing is established by these modeled results.

Independent reconstruction PASS: 10,240 complete commercial histories, 1,265,250 journal entries, zero arithmetic or checked decision errors. Seven histories end with unpaid lender claims and are explicitly marked terminal insolvent. A passing reconstruction verifies the implementation of the declared model; it does not turn that model into evidence of market demand or commercial viability.

2. RECONSTRUCTION METHOD AND COVERAGE
The independent standard-library auditor consumes recorded journal, lifecycle, buyer and account data. It neither imports business.py nor calls the simulator. Accounting uses integer cents and debit-positive balances. It reconstructs every account and reported monetary output, cash/equity/debt reconciliation, restricted deposit custody, collections, losses and scenario summaries. It also independently recomputes buyer outside-option utility formulas and checked negotiation, participation, seller cost and prospective funding decisions from recorded inputs.
Key totals: 201,298 outside-option choices; 1,006,490 independently recomputed utility values; 58,184 seller cost and prospective funding gates; 49,296 contracts; 47,442 initial accepted deliveries; 3,696 renewals; 122,880 reported monetary checks; 283,964 account reconstructions. All ten scenario profit means and loss frequencies match.
Nine synthetic auditor controls passed: one valid case plus deliberately corrupted profit, deposit recognition, restricted spending, collection closure, invoice price, execution chronology, buyer participation and outside-option selection. Controls test sensitivity to those known mistakes; they do not establish exhaustive bug absence.

6. SEPARATE STRATEGIC GAME
A separate finite price/entry sensitivity was constructed after the commercial freeze without changing the lifecycle policy. The independent standard-library reconstruction regenerated its 4,000 synthetic buyer types, all 324 payoff cells, best-response equilibrium sets and logit fixed points. Zero errors. Both individual and shared-core arrangements give no entry as the entrant action in the declared pure entry equilibrium; active-only equilibria leave the entrant with a loss. Shared-core cost savings are available to both firms on equal rights, not reserved to the proposed enterprise.
This verifies only the declared finite game. Price grids, demand, buyer beliefs and costs are assumed; the incumbent has an explicit sunk-fixed-cost advantage. It is neither a fitted market model nor a unique general equilibrium result. Supplier bargaining is represented by participation and reservation-rate/capacity rules and scenarios, not an endogenous multi-market bargaining solution.

7. MATERIAL LIMITATIONS THAT MUST ACCOMPANY CLAIMS
Buyer values, demand, conversion, acceptance, collection and cost parameters are estimates. Model-generated participation is not independent willingness-to-pay evidence. Primary wage references provide a human-cost anchor, not a market quote for this pack. No actual customer acquisition, contracts, sales, payments, external financing or paid commitments occurred.
The product is a bounded functional digital kernel and acceptance pack. Its modeled pricing cannot be justified by the value of a complete instrument, photodetector, timing interface or production-qualified FPGA. Those physical capabilities remain outside the current technical contract.
The eight-hour support capacity reservation per accepted order is an aggregate capacity proxy. It is not a calendar-level staffing simulation or verified weekly service-level guarantee. Acquisition and delivery capacity limits and common-mode stresses are useful declared checks but do not validate real staffing or procurement lead times.
Commercial simulation acceptance represents scope/fit and delivery outcomes for an already demonstrated technical class. The full RTL toolchain is not rerun for each synthetic buyer. Variable human interventions are modeled counts, not observational autonomy measurements.
Modeled realized buyer benefit compares one modeled realized service path with expected modeled outside utility. It is not a paired observed customer counterfactual. Free tools, skilled current-AI use, competitors, internal teams, waiting and doing nothing constrain participation in the model; their assumed economics have not been validated in customer interviews or paid trials.
Technical ordinary-library parity and the identical-free-library commercial stress do not constitute a separately measured real enterprise cost study. They defeat exclusive advantage claims where they tie, while leaving the narrow useful construction intact.
Conditional AI improvement applies to both sides and remains a scenario. It does not establish present viability or justify a speculative billion-dollar valuation. No scenario supplies profitable surplus for reinvestment.
External validity is the main unresolved risk, not numerical Monte Carlo precision. The audit is conducted by a separate agent in the same session, not by an outside accounting firm or an external market participant.

8. NEXT OPENING AND STOP CONDITIONS
Retain EventWeaver as a reproducible instrument, with technical scope and parity results intact. Investigate a bounded OEM co-development opening only if a concrete integration bottleneck and acceptance condition can be documented, a beneficiary has a credible participation reason against its strongest alternative, and incremental delivery and ongoing obligations can earn positive contribution at a finite price. The next protocol must charge new integration, physical verification where claimed, acquisition, support, validation and retention costs; any shared-core reuse must remain available to a competent conventional comparison.
Actual customer outreach, purchases, commitments and financing are outside this mandate. The completed review package can specify the proposed gate, required evidence and capped experiment without taking those actions. No positive hypothetical option value should offset this campaign loss unless an independently justified, non-double-counted next investment is explicitly priced and tested.
