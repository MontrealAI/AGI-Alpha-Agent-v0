DECISIVE SCOPE
The accepted product is a functional firmware design pack for a narrowly defined
clocked digital kernel: at most four predicates, eight event channels and twenty
ticks of history. It is not a time-to-digital converter, timestamp-loss solution,
physical FPGA implementation, calibrated scientific instrument or low-latency
system integration. Compare the same acceptance condition on both sides.
The useful construction can be real even when the standalone offering loses money.

COMPETENT ALTERNATIVES
Each buyer can choose a current-AI-assisted engineer using ordinary HDL and free
verification tools; an in-house team; existing vendor SDK/custom-measurement routes;
waiting; or doing nothing. For offline processing the existing software route may
satisfy the entire need. Digital trace equivalence cannot establish that our kernel
outperforms it in physical timing. Use the 6–16hour aggressive design/verification
case and a10–24hour total scoped internal case including review; both are estimates.
Current-AI competitors can copy the same useful formulation and use a library.
They need not continue billing yesterday's labor hours as frontier tools improve.
Incumbents can bundle support, reduce price, specialize or lower perceived risk.
Buyer procurement/review/trust costs may favor the incumbent even with equal quality.

REVIEWED DESIGN DIRECTION
Root's proposed price grid USD1200–3600 is appropriate to stress the actual scope.
USD6000 can remain an explicitly unevidenced dominated-price control; it cannot be
used as a rescue case without independent evidence. A12month acquisition horizon
with up to12additional months of runoff is preferable to stopping at an attractive
intermediate cash balance. The proposed120–240 scoped prospects/~24 qualified
opportunities is a demand assumption, not a reachable-market measurement. Log
unqualified leads, failures, refusals, competitors selected and doing-nothing choices.
The proposed USD24–35k construction plus USD5–10k acquisition program should be
charged once per fresh enterprise, not amortized only across hypothetical future
orders. Initial discovery and failed attempts belong in that amount or a clearly
separate cost line. Conventional comparisons pay equivalent arrangement costs.

FULL LIFECYCLE THAT MUST APPEAR IN JOURNAL
Acquisition: cost incurred for reached prospects even when no quote or sale follows.
Qualification: unsupported specifications refused; no engineering sale outside scope.
Quotation/negotiation: price/capacity/rights/deadline clear; buyer selects outside option.
Contracting: exact requirements fixed before fulfillment, cancellation and refund terms.
Execution: actual technical capability linked to delivered artifact class; human reviews
and exceptions remain costs. Simulator must not fill an unbuilt physical integration.
Acceptance/refusal: separate first-pass acceptance, correction, final refusal and dispute.
Collection: refundable deposits, invoicing, late payment, collection costs and default.
Support: warranty costs/queue/corrections even for an order whose cash was collected.
Renewal: optional buyer participation after covered warranty; no automatic free annuity.
Investment/exit: retention only after protected obligations, or stop new orders while
finishing all existing support, refunds, receivables, payables and financing runoff.

THREE-ORDER NONEXCLUSIVE CORE
Potential gain is avoided duplicate construction and generic qualification. It must
be computed from an explicit common component, not from counting the same three
orders twice. Each buyer receives its own accepted deliverable and enough rights
to use/modify it; participants knowingly accept a reusable nonexclusive generic core.
Contingent formation should have a stated expiry. Before enough participants join,
deposits remain refundable/restricted and cannot finance construction. If the group
fails, return deposits and charge acquisition/coordination work to the enterprise.
Withdrawal does not force the remaining participants to accept a higher price.
Common-source defects can create correlated refund/support obligations for all three.
Pool coordination, delay and private specification handling are real costs.
An identical arrangement must be available to a capable conventional provider.
Generic public/shared code creates free-riding and future imitation; support/acceptance
may remain valuable, but stored code alone does not create a monopoly or royalty.

RECURRING ARRANGEMENT
A USD450 renewal can cover only a stated new useful service (for example a bounded
annual compatibility/verification review). Existing promised warranty is already owed.
Renew only when the buyer's expected incremental benefit exceeds price plus effort,
and the seller's incremental expected contribution is positive after support risk.
Do not treat eventual renewal receipts as certain funding for original losses.

STRATEGIC MODEL
Specify information and actions: buyer knows its own urgency and internal alternative;
seller observes submitted requirements and its capacity, not true private value;
competitor chooses a quoted price/service against its own costs; specialist supplier
sets rate/capacity and can decline overload. Buyer utility should include acceptance
probability, time, procurement, integration and price. Compare maximizing outside
option and bounded-rational choice. Tie-breaking is material and must be disclosed.
Supplier bargaining must retain its participation constraint. A price cut below
competitor cost should be labeled temporary bundling/loss-leading with its own budget.
Payment-stage defection is possible after delivery: model collection compliance as
an assumption, an enforceable deposit/severable-delivery mechanism, or a stress case.
A simulated rational buyer does not prove demand. Do not call a sampled sequential
policy an equilibrium unless its stated deviations were actually checked.

COMPLETE COST DENOMINATOR
Discovery, unsuccessful candidates, product construction, independent verification,
inference, tools, compute, data, legal/licensing/admin, sales/procurement, customer
onboarding, human exceptions, correction, maintenance, warranty, refunds/fees,
collection, financing and asset extraction/validation/upkeep. License/open-source
tools may have zero license price while labor and hosting remain nonzero.
Shared development is expensed once, then allocated descriptively without subtracting
it twice. Marketing/acquisition fixed and per-prospect costs must not duplicate.
Engineering hourly rates must consistently mean either fully loaded cost or contract
price. If fully loaded, do not add its same salary/benefits again as overhead.

CASH AND ACCOUNTING INVARIANTS FOR INDEPENDENT AUDIT
1. Customer refundable deposit increases restricted cash and deposit liability, not
   service revenue or available operating funds.
2. Preacceptance refund reverses the deposit/custody balance; principal is not a loss.
   Bank/processing/correction costs are losses. Postacceptance commercial refunds
   reverse revenue (or create an equivalent explicit refund expense), not both.
3. Invoice issuance recognizes receivable/revenue only at the specified accepted
   milestone. Collection converts receivable to cash, without a second sale.
4. Default writes off the uncollected receivable. Delayed payment remains outstanding
   until collection or final writeoff; do not stop the horizon while it is favorable.
5. Equity/loan draw is financing cash, not revenue. Loan principal repayment is not
   expense; interest and fees are expenses. Do not expense capital twice.
6. Restricted cash never funds payroll or reinvestment. Capital requirement is based
   on minimum unrestricted cash with obligations respected, not total bank balance.
7. A warranty provision is a liability/expense only if later spend reverses it; otherwise
   use cash-based expected warranty costs without separately charging a provision.
8. Terminal deposit, receivable, refund and debt balances must either be zero or be
   explicit unresolved liabilities/assets in the result. Full runoff requires closure.
9. At fully closed terminal state with no assets/liabilities, end unrestricted cash
   minus outside equity injections equals full-project profit, after distributions.
10. Human service hours must include failed/refused work, acquisition and support;
    capacity cannot be spent twice or silently expanded after an overload.

UNCERTAINTY AND DECISION
Use correlated commercial worlds: low demand with price compression, supplier rates
with capacity shortages, and shared technical defects with clustered warranty demand.
Parameter/model uncertainty is larger than Monte Carlo sampling error. Report scenario
means and Monte Carlo SE separately, not a narrow confidence interval as confidence in
real-world profitability. Technical verification on authored traces cannot set real
commercial acceptance to100%. Hidden customer assumptions remain possible.
Include current-feasible primary, aggressive competition, thin demand, common-mode
defect, supplier squeeze, delayed/default collections and future-AI conditional cases.
Future improvements must benefit rivals too and cannot rescue today's viability claim.
Report operating contribution, full-project profit, loss probability/quantiles, peak
capital, cash shortfalls at fixed capital, all relevant break-even thresholds and
buyer benefit versus the actual chosen best outside option. Negative cases stay visible.
If all realistic frozen policies lose after full cost, STOP expansion of the standalone
offering. A lower-cost next experiment is justified by attainable information about
buyer demand/integration burden, not a tiny probability times an invented billion-dollar
payoff. The useful compiler may still be retained selectively if its incremental use
exceeds extraction, validation, integration and upkeep costs; ordinary library may tie.
