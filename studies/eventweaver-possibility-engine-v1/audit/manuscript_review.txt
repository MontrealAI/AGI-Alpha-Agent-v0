CORRECTION 1 — RENEWAL ACCEPTANCE/COLLECTION/WARRANTY SCOPE
Location: paper/sections_5_8.tex, 'Scope that can be sold honestly', after the paragraph ending 'chosen only when buyer utility and seller contribution justify it'; or beside the lifecycle's complete-runoff claim. Also summarize in paper/appendices.tex beside the renewal assumption.
Evidence: src/business.py renewal block. Once the renewal utility/seller gate and 12% procurement-refusal draw permit the renewal, the model schedules acceptance at finish+187 and full collection at finish+247. It does not draw a renewal technical failure, correction, default or second warranty. Initial deliveries have those branches. The generic statement that the service has a 45-day warranty, followed by the complete-lifecycle narrative, can imply equivalent renewal coverage unless this distinction is explicit.
Suggested sentence:
'Renewal is a narrower modeled revalidation service: after its utility and procurement gates, acceptance and full collection are assumed, with no additional failure, default or warranty cycle. Full-project closure covers these declared obligations; it does not validate a repeated full-service warranty business.'
Impact: bounds the completeness claim without changing any recorded cost or outcome. Adding realistic renewal risks would require a new frozen model; do not alter the existing campaign or retroactively infer those outcomes.

CORRECTION 2 — UNPAID TERMINAL DEBT IS A TERMINAL CLAIM, NOT FULL FINANCING RESOLUTION
Location: paper/sections_5_8.tex, 'Accounting and financing', after the paragraph ending 'universal financing closure is not claimed'; or paper/appendices.tex, 'Accounting identities, audits and terminal exceptions'.
Evidence: src/business.py accrues interest to operational_end, calls ledger.settle(operational_end), and retains the unpaid balance. results/terminal_insolvencies.json records terminal dates and balances. No later interest, lender recovery, restructuring or insolvency proceeding is simulated after those dates. The current manuscript correctly preserves seven balances but should state this final scope boundary explicitly.
Suggested sentence:
'Unpaid debt is reported at each history's terminal operating date. The simulation does not model later interest, creditor recovery or insolvency resolution; those seven histories are financing failures with unresolved lender claims, rather than completed debt runoff.'
Impact: does not change the seven-case count, USD2,524.62 no-recourse balance, USD6,285.11 aggregate high-demand balances or the reported terminal profit. It prevents those terminal figures from being mistaken for final resolved loss after an insolvency process.

CORRECTION 3 — SUCCESSOR 'CALIBRATION' IS A LOGICAL EVENT SEQUENCE
Location: paper/sections_9_12.tex, 'A fresh mission that changes the grammar', sentence 'The new mission is a heralded detection followed by conditioned calibration on other channels.'
Evidence: src/successor.py and the frozen successor grammar implement Boolean event predicates and one-tick virtual connections. No physical calibration parameter, uncertainty, calibrated clock or measurement accuracy is estimated. Other manuscript sections correctly exclude physical calibration.
Suggested replacement:
'The new mission is a heralded detection followed by a conditionally activated calibration-trigger event sequence on other channels.'
An even narrower replacement is '...followed by a conditionally activated second event predicate on other channels.'
Impact: preserves the useful compositional extension while preventing the mission label from implying that calibration itself was executed.
