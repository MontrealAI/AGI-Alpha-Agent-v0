INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL FINAL REVIEW
EventWeaver / AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL / 28 October 2026

REVIEW DECISION
The bounded functional firmware design pack passes the frozen acceptance contract. The successor extension also passes its separately frozen contract and permits an acyclic composition that the original compiler rejects. These findings justify a reviewable technical offering and a bounded customer-backed qualification experiment. They do not, by themselves, justify commercial expansion, a physical-instrument claim, an exclusive technical advantage, or a learned-successor claim.

STRONGEST SUPPORTED CLAIMS
1. Main construction: all 768 fresh traces across 24 specifications and 196608 ticks agree among five execution paths: independent offline oracle, conventional Python, EventWeaver Python and both actual RTL designs compiled and executed through Yosys CXXRTL. This comprises 491520 predicate-tick decisions and 630 positive outputs. Fifty-two directed actual-RTL cases and fourteen invalid-specification rejections also pass. Twenty-eight bounded RTL equivalence proofs pass at recorded depths from 8 to 22 steps. Source: results/technical_confirmation/summary.json, traces.jsonl.gz, directed.json, invalid.json and per-design equivalence.log files.
2. Successor: all 48 fresh traces across six networks and 6144 ticks agree among the mathematical network oracle, conventional network, successor network, both actual RTL designs and an ordinary library holding the identical compiler and wrapper. Sixty-two positive stage outputs occur. Ten directed actual-RTL cases exercise virtual A, B, C and veto inputs, exact one-tick composition, reset, late veto, non-topological ordering and a three-stage chain. Nine invalid networks are rejected. Twelve bounded equivalence proofs pass at depths 16–34 steps. The original compiler rejects the virtual-channel grammar. Source: results/successor_confirmation/.
3. The conventional method ties on functional acceptance in both studies. The identical-library control ties exactly, as expected from identical code. This identity control is not an independent implementation or evidence of learned specialization.

MATERIAL NEGATIVE RESULT
Across the fresh main cases, the EventWeaver Python model took 12.482 seconds, versus 0.276 seconds for conventional history evaluation and 0.181 seconds for the offline oracle. The conventional model was about 45 times faster in this particular sparse-trace workload. Paired CXXRTL replay took 0.677 seconds; no isolated physical throughput or FPGA timing comparison was conducted. Code-generation and verification costs remain chargeable even when the generated RTL functions correctly.

PRESERVED FAILURE AND CORRECTION
The first development harness sampled CXXRTL ports before a same-clock settling evaluation, creating apparent one-tick delays in 18 of 32 traces. Both RTL designs agreed, and their bounded proofs passed. The pre-fix verifier, failed observations and diagnosis are preserved in audit/failures/cxxrtl_sampling_v1/. The driver correction occurred before freeze, did not introduce an extra rising edge and did not change the acceptance semantics or either implementation. No fresh-confirmation failure was observed or removed.

The study executed one fresh confirmation campaign per freeze; it does not claim a separate external laboratory rerun.

BOUNDS ON INTERPRETATION
The contract uses lossless eight-bit event masks on a logical clock, with no sub-bin multiplicity. No TDC, timestamp parser, clock-domain crossing, physical I/O, board deployment, place-and-route timing, optical accuracy or calibrated measurement was tested. Yosys bounded equivalence starts from an explicit reset and is not an all-time or physical proof. Simulation and formal checking share Yosys as a frontend, so their agreement does not provide independent EDA-toolchain validation; the separate mathematical oracle contributes a different semantic check over the executed cases.

Prior NIST/BrightEyes time-tagging, commercial filtering tools and temporal-logic-to-FPGA research defeat a broad novelty claim. The defensible contribution is the narrow executable contract, delivered construction, acceptance evidence and tested business arrangement. The successor adds a new composition grammar using a retained artifact; it does not learn new weights, and the same-artifact library tie limits any superiority claim. Its planned USD4100 extension/retention cost is an estimate and must not be presented as funded by predecessor surplus without a matching ledger entry.

OBJECTIVITY
This review was produced by a separate AI-assisted workstream. The conventional implementation and offline oracle were written from the frozen semantics without reading the candidate implementation; generated RTL was inspected afterward to diagnose the harness failure. There was no human specialist review, customer acceptance, laboratory experiment or independent physical validation. Shared model-family reasoning can introduce correlated errors. Commercial willingness to pay, procurement behavior and financial returns require the separate explicit simulation assumptions and cannot be inferred from these technical pass counts.
