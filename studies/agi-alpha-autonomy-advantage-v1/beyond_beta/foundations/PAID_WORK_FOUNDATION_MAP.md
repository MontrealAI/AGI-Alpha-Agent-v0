# Paid Work. Retained Alpha. Expanding Possibility.

## Latest accounting boundary

The latest user steering makes paid-job learning the primary mechanism. It supersedes any earlier reading that would require a separately funded invention project before useful learning can occur. The study must keep three disjoint cost accounts:

| Account | What it pays for | Treatment in a retention decision |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Paid job** | Everything needed to deliver the contracted outcome: intake, investigation, learning, specification, implementation, proof, checking, correction, packaging, and support. Unsuccessful attempts remain here. | Already incurred delivery cost stays in the job and enterprise ledger. It is not charged again to the retained capability. Job revenue does not by itself prove Alpha. |
| **B — Incremental retention** | The additional work to extract a reusable method, sanitize it, package it, test fresh reuse, assess net Alpha, admit it, distribute it, adapt it, and keep it current. Failed retention attempts remain here. | Compare future incremental benefit against these additional costs and the best current available method. Do not recharge the original job’s learning or acceptance verification. |
| **C — Optional separate investment** | Additional research or construction outside the paid job and outside the incremental retention work already recorded. | A separate expected-value and cash decision. It may be worthwhile, deferred, abandoned, or unnecessary. No investment quota or mandatory number of successors. |

Every cost event needs one accounting owner and one source event ID. Shared work may be allocated, but allocation fractions must sum to one. Enterprise value includes A+B+C; a retention decision uses incremental B, plus any actually additional C it requires. A cost already assigned to A must never reappear as B/C construction or qualification merely because its artifact is later admitted.

The independent graph review’s first-qualification cost-floor controls concern unpaid **B/C** costs previously incurred for the same qualification program. They do not authorize charging A again. Conversely, labeling a new failed qualification as part of a previously paid job must not erase an actually additional cost. Historical total economics and prospective retention economics are different, simultaneously reportable quantities.

## The three objects and their useful constraints

**Mission Gym.** The Gym manuscript defines a controlled, versioned, rights-cleared experimental representation of a mission: hidden state, role-specific observations, bounded actions, transitions, vector outcomes, hard constraints, scenario distribution, independent verifier, and proof receipts (§3, physical pp18–21). The candidate’s internal beliefs are separate from the Gym’s hidden truth (§4.3). This directly supports a simulation whose actors cannot inspect latent correctness, future demand, or protected evaluation labels. A realistic-looking interface does not establish realistic transitions; Gym-to-reality basis risk remains explicit.

**Specialist ASI.** The manuscript defines this as an earned, scoped comparative state of a complete system, including ordinary tools, integration, verification, correction, reliability and costs, not a model label (§9, physical pp39–41). The source’s requirement to outperform the best credible alternative set is broader than a human-price comparison. BeyondBeta therefore reports Beta-versus-human economics and additional Alpha separately, and does not claim actual Specialist ASI from a simulation. A benchmark score, graph count or identity attestation cannot supply the missing evidence.

**Successor Ω.** Empirical superiority and authority are separate. The source requires protected fresh proof followed by accountable, scoped, versioned, expiring and revocable admission; descendants do not inherit authority (§§10–11, physical pp41–47). Navigator §5.2 (physical p20, printed p10, Eqs26–30) explicitly separates producer, verifier and admitter. The new local adapter’s fixture identities can exercise this role separation, but do not constitute live ENS or production authorization. The user’s automated-provider instruction controls this study; the drafts’ wider institutional discussion of human principals does not silently add routine paid human labor to its primary model.

## Quantities the manuscripts do not supply

The sources do not provide this study’s paid demand, current model acceptance probability, job-learning yield, retention cost, useful lifetime, transfer probability, or future Alpha erosion rate. Those require independent sourcing or explicit assumptions. They do not justify a fixed reinvestment fraction, permanent exclusivity, mandatory separate R&D, or automatic positive value from copying a transcript.

The Gym paper’s learning-curve definition normalizes later formation burden for comparable mission difficulty and proof quality (§11.5). Its recursive-improvement definition requires a retained capability to improve the ability to detect, construct, prove or govern a superior successor on fresh work (§11.1). More descendant jobs or more graph nodes do not establish that stronger claim. Beta can remain commercially valuable while additional Alpha is zero; Alpha can persist in one scope and erode in another (§7).

## Minimal ledger and causal checks for the new engine

1. Every modeled payment/cost resolves to A, B or C exactly once; account totals reconcile to enterprise cost and cash.
2. Paid-job learning happens before its job acceptance and remains charged when the job fails. Retention is optional and follows actual motivating evidence.
3. The future-work contrast gives both arms equivalent current Beta, accepted task scope, source information, budgets and ordinary tools; only the declared retention/sharing treatment differs.
4. Qualification data are fresh and hidden from the construction/selection decision. The independent modeled truth process does not share the producer’s confidence as ground truth.
5. Net retained Alpha includes extraction, fresh validation, admission, distribution, adaptation and upkeep, while account A is preserved separately without another charge.
6. Sharing checks actual modeled functional overlap and adaptation; availability does not force uptake. Existing inventory is included before any incremental Alpha claim.
7. Optional C investment uses presently available funds and estimated incremental value, never a required asset count or future receipts.
8. Moving-Beta revalidation uses exact releases and current authority. Success, failure, impairment and retirement all remain visible.

These are prospective invariants. They do not report economic outcomes or establish any real commercial or Specialist ASI state.
