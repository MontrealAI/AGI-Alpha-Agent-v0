# Early independent review of the paid-work economic engine

Scope: read-only prospective review of `beyond_beta/src/economy.py` before calibrated worlds. Findings were sent directly to its owner and root as they were identified. No economic worlds were run by this reviewer. The code is still being assembled; this is a repair checklist, not a claim about final results.

| ID | Concrete issue in the inspected draft | Required resolution or explicit scope limit |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | `evidence()` called `bind_cost_ownership()` without assigning its returned deep copy, leaving fictional zero retention cost in the graph evidence. | Bind the returned evidence to actual B/C ledger entries and immutable A provenance; reconcile graph fixed cost to modeled paid expenses. |
| E02 | `forecast()`, motivating-job thresholds and `emerge()` used latent `usefulAccepted` instead of observable acceptance/feedback. | Policies use information available to the enterprise. Preserve false accepted lessons and undetected defects in the simulated truth process. |

Count actual stage occurrences and keep direct attribution distinct from paired enterprise value. |
| E07 | Maintenance was charged and marked complete before its scheduled work completed. | Model a pending upkeep state; affected authority cannot be restored by uncompleted work. |
| E08 | `seen_candidates(family,Beta)` suppressed every subsequent proposal after even an initial decline or cash deferral. | Avoid an undeclared one-proposal quota. Use a justified duplicate/context cache and allow material new paid-work evidence to change the decision. |

| E10 | Optional C research was not an active decision path; only opening research was charged C and `forecast(deliberate=True)` was unused. | Implement if part of the declared experiment, or report it as unexercised scope. Absence of an action is not evidence that the optimizer considered and rejected it. |

The principal causal check remains A+B+C reconciliation: original paid learning is never recharged as retention, and additional extraction/qualification/transfer costs are never erased merely because their source method originated in a paid job. Whole-company economics include every account once. The retention contrast uses new fresh-work gains and costs relative to competent current Beta plus already available methods.

Independent graph enforcement tests remain separate from economic validation. Signed fictional evidence can establish that a local gate enforces its arithmetic and rights rules; it cannot establish real correctness, empirical demand, or a true current-frontier capability gain.
