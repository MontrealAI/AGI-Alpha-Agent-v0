# Source and evidence companion

The companion is a reviewable record of the completed BeyondBeta study, its executed local mechanisms, conditional economic experiment and preserved negative findings. It is not a claim of actual model production, actual customer acceptance, live identity enforcement or collected commercial revenue.

## Contents and layout

The ZIP preserves workspace-relative sibling paths. Keep this layout intact after extraction:

- `beyond_beta/`: implementation, controls, protocol and calibration, foundation reviews, complete bounded summaries, analysis, native and replay diagnostics, LaTeX, final figures, English/French demo and its source.
- `venture_creation/src/`: unchanged generic cash/capacity, authority and native integration dependencies; no previous study's prices or economic conclusions are imported.
- `human_market/validation/skill_admission.mjs`: the unchanged authority foundation used by the native adapter. Historical admission rules are not invoked by BeyondBeta.
- `native_snapshot/`: the exact required native `planMission` source and provenance manifests.
- `PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json`: every included path, byte length, SHA-256, frozen membership and separately delivered raw artifact identity.
- `PUBLICATION_FILELIST.json`: the compact proposed public subset and its explicit omissions; no publication is performed by packaging.

All 52 files listed by `beyond_beta/protocol/source-freeze.json` are included unchanged. The public subset omits one non-runtime provenance document containing private locators, while retaining all executable dependencies. Use the full companion for the complete 52-file verification; the public subset's 51/52 coverage is explicitly declared in `publication/PUBLIC_SOURCE_COVERAGE.json`.

## What the ZIP excludes

No raw `.gz` streams are duplicated. Private supplied manuscript PDFs, reference images and full external source-document copies are not redistributed; source identifiers and review records explain the references. Cache directories, bytecode, TeX auxiliary files, heavyweight QA renders and temporary layout-check outputs are excluded. Final scientific figures, final PDF and QA metadata are included.

The companion contains private source-locator metadata and is not the public-repository upload list. The public list screens text files for private locators, excludes the affected non-runtime evidence file, replaces generated demo payload duplication with a compact `demo.zip`, and keeps bulky bound summaries available in the companion. A compact mechanically derived `publication/public-results.json` exposes all reported cells and paired comparisons with source hashes; it is not a new analysis or a replacement for the raw evidence.

## Reproduction and accounting limits

See `RUNBOOK.md`. Work in a fresh extracted copy if running controls or audits that emit result files; preserve the supplied evidence. A clean full campaign uses a new output directory and a documented 7,200-second operational allowance. That allowance is not a retrospective claim that the original 1,800-second run was uninterrupted.

Every exact quality, demand, resource, transfer and future-Beta parameter remains a declared scenario assumption unless its calibration record says otherwise. Recorded simulation costs are complete within the declared model; actual complete study cost is unknown. Hashes establish integrity, and fixture signatures establish local enrolled-key binding. Neither establishes scientific truth, actual commercial value or live authority.
