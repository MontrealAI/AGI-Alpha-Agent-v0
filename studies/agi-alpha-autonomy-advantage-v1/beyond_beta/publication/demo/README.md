The demonstration leads with the commercial service: useful delivery, human-service reference, offered price, complete cost, profit, spendable earned funds and throughput. Optional retained Alpha, sharing and investment follow. The original reference /1001 /network-alpha detailed example is preserved, with the separately trained Beta service shown alongside it.

`index.html` is a self-contained file. It uses no remote executable, font or visual assets. External links identify primary research and official documentation. The overview is pre-rendered in English for non-script previews; the interactive selectors and full French translation use the embedded JavaScript.

## Evidence and binding

`build_demo.py` never runs an economic world. Until a valid `registered-results.json` exists, every economic panel stays empty and the file is explicitly marked pending. `bind_registered_results.py --bind` requires a completed registered campaign, exact frozen source identities, the untouched training-selection commitment, all confirmation identities and the complete original independent audits plus their separately identified post-freeze diagnostic replay. The original 51 training epoch flags remain preserved; the diagnostic adds the Beta/policy epoch increments omitted by the frozen auditor, without changing any economic record. It derives a publication summary from the preserved records; it never selects a different example, price or policy.

All confirmation summaries and all declared conditions are represented. Detailed cash curves are embedded for reference /1001 in all six arms; other complete cash traces remain in the bound research archive. The connected candidate trace selects the first chronologically recorded paid-job candidate, including a decline or failure. This rule and the reporting-priority change are documented in separate prospective records.

The independent selection of the Beta commercial policy is distinct from the matched-controller retention contrast. Origin delivery A is never charged a second time as B or C. The company total also includes operations and financial costs. Candidate-direct B charges are explicitly distinguished from per-use B billed to later jobs. Eventual receipts are not represented as available before collection.

## Checks and limits

Run `python qa_static.py` and `node qa_dom.mjs` from this directory after building. Their records identify structural and translation/control checks; they do not claim browser rendering verification. A normal Chromium sandbox launch was blocked by the execution environment, and that failure remains recorded. No sandbox bypass or replacement browser download was used.

The economic study is conditional simulation. Actual commercial jobs, model-production calls and collected sales remain zero, and actual complete research cost is unknown. Fixture identity, encryption, signatures and canonical graph controls are executed local mechanisms. They do not establish live ENS authority, real task correctness, actual commercial Alpha, Specialist ASI, graph superiority or self-acceleration.
