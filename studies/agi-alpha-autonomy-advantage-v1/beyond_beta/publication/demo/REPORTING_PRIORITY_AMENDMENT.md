# Reporting priority amendment — profitable work first

Written in response to the latest user steering, before the demo author inspected calibrated economic outcomes.

The frozen study already selects competent Beta, optional Alpha and the overall preferred policy separately. This publication-only change places the commercial Beta service first: useful outcome, matched human-service reference, offered price, complete cost, complete profit, spendable earned funds and useful throughput. Optional retention, sharing and further investment follow this commercial result. No hypothesis, scenario, price grid, acceptance rule, policy, seed, sampling order or recorded outcome is changed.

The main comparison prominently shows the independently trained Beta service beside the optional-Alpha policy for the same scenario and seed. Their full-policy difference is kept separate from the matched-controller retention contrast and the sharing contrast. All declared conditions remain visible. The original reference /1001 /network-alpha detailed example remains unchanged. Its paired trained-Beta service is displayed alongside it, without choosing a more favorable default.

The first chronologically recorded paid-job candidate remains the traced candidate, whether declined, rejected or admitted. Retention is neither required for useful profitable work nor automatically justified by it. There is no reinvestment quota and no assumption that a larger graph creates commercial value.
