\begin{abstract}
We investigate whether competent frontier intelligence can profitably deliver complete proof-carrying software components at buyer-chosen prices referenced to equivalent human services. A finite-cash simulation separates paid delivery, additional capability retention and optional research. Buyers can choose a human service, wait or decline. The tested graph lifecycle guards scoped admission and network reuse without changing existing settlement authority. We execute 1,728 training histories and 4,560 fresh confirmation histories. Model/API prices and labor sources anchor costs; task capability, human effort, demand and skill effects remain explicit assumptions.

\section{Question, mechanism and independently testable claims}

The primary question is whether an automated network can sustainably earn complete-cost operating profit and available surplus by delivering demanding, useful accepted work. We answer it before asking whether retained mission intelligence or further investment improves the enterprise. Profitable Beta-only operation is a positive commercial result; it needs no graph advantage or prescribed investment count. We distinguish two sources of advantage. \textbf{Beta} is competent use of current frontier models and ordinary tools, including decomposition, adequate context, source grounding, implementation, proof software, critique, correction and recovery. \textbf{Alpha} is the additional net mission advantage of a retained capability over that Beta and the useful capabilities already available to a recipient.

The commercial comparison asks whether complete accepted Beta delivery is economical relative to equivalent human service. The incremental comparison asks whether retaining a method improves the same enterprise. An attractive human--AI price gap cannot establish Alpha. Conversely, a failed retention project does not invalidate profitable ordinary service. Our experiment permits both outcomes and does not require a fixed investment sequence.

The primary pathway is paid useful work, possible learning during delivery, optional incremental extraction and qualification, admission if net Alpha is supported, optional reuse by genuine beneficiaries, then fresh work under the current frontier. The customer buys an outcome, not an experiment designed solely to generate graph nodes. A demanding job may leave no reusable method; an apparently reusable method may be redundant, fail fresh verification or become obsolete before repayment.

\section{A demanding venture chosen before outcomes}

We independently screened four complete R\&D offerings: proof-carrying bespoke software, simulation-native inverse design, hardware-aware algorithm discovery and coordination-mechanism design. The prospective criteria were useful executable value (25\%), autonomous falsifiability (25\%), distinguishable incremental Alpha (25\%), a clean moving-Beta comparison (15\%) and bounded implementation feasibility (10\%). These are declared research judgments, not observed commercial rankings. Scores were respectively 4.25, 4.15, 3.90 and 3.60 on a five-point scale. The calibration register preserves unsuccessful alternatives and the reasons for selection.

\subsection{What the customer receives}

The primary customer is a software platform, scientific-computing or planning-product team commissioning one bounded component, with up to two external interfaces. The complete outcome includes a mathematical or state-based specification faithful to the requested behavior; the implemented component; checked proof or an explicitly bounded verification record; environmental and callee assumptions; an integration adapter; reproducible regression cases; and bounded correction/support. A proof of a simplified function does not establish the integrated product. Unsupported or high-impact ambiguity triggers clarification, abstention or refusal rather than hidden human completion.

The recurring trigger is a new numerical rule, constraint family, dependency version or interface behavior. The model assumes potential buyers face such recurring needs. Public software and optimization research establishes that these burdens exist; it does not establish willingness to purchase this particular package. Demand and value distributions therefore remain scenario assumptions.

The binding restriction is the repeated effort needed to construct a faithful formal interface between user intent, code, proof assumptions and deployment context. Beta already has formal methods, property testing, compiler tools and standard algorithms. Potential Alpha consists of a reusable scoped contract representation, a separately validated proof/adapter composition or a method that reduces dependency revalidation. Those assets must save work or improve feasible scope after acquisition and upkeep. A new filename or a technically correct proof is insufficient.

\section{Frozen experiment and results}

The protocol is finalized before calibrated histories. Training compares 36 pricing/retention/funding rules across three profiles, two demand conditions and eight seeds: 1,728 histories. Best ordinary Beta, best optional-Alpha policy and the overall winner are selected separately. If Beta wins, it remains the recommended policy; the optional-Alpha contrast is still reported as a prespecified mechanism test.

Confirmation uses 40 fresh seeds per condition, cycling 14 reference, 13 burdensome and 13 efficient profiles. Six arms across 19 conditions produce 4,560 histories, representing 760 paired exogenous worlds. Five arms hold the selected Alpha pricing controller fixed: moving Beta alone, creator-private Alpha availability, mandatory network Alpha, network Alpha with frozen Beta, and frozen Beta alone. The sixth uses the independently trained Beta pricing policy. Adaptive controllers can produce different realized prices from their own histories; no arm receives another arm's future prices.

Conditions include zero/harmful Alpha, high overlap, failed adaptation, delayed collections, low/no/high repeat demand, higher resource prices, common specification errors, fast/slow Beta improvement, explicit Beta absorption, diverse hard work, tight quotas and short asset life. All cells and failures are retained. The demonstration defaults to reference/seed 1001/network Alpha before results are inspected. Selecting a different displayed world never changes the primary estimates.

Stage budgets are explicit aggregate quantities with bounded per-request envelopes. A 4 GB hosted-container allowance of \usd{0.12} per 20 minutes normalizes to \usd{0.36} per container-hour; it is not a CPU-core rental quote. Search calls and storage are separately recorded. \section{Three accounts, one enterprise}

\textbf{A: Job economics.} Charge every resource used to deliver the paid outcome, including learning, experimentation, implementation, failed attempts and corrections undertaken for that job. Useful work can produce a reusable insight while still being fully charged. Acceptance creates an invoice under the modeled contract; cash arrives only according to collection timing and default. Payment is never treated as proof that the work was correct.

\textbf{B: Retention and reuse.} Charge only additional extraction, generalization, sanitization, protected fresh qualification, assessment, packaging, admission, distribution, recipient probes/adaptation, revalidation and upkeep. Origin-job commitments retain A's provenance but contribute zero to the prospective retention hurdle. A cheap retention option is possible only when the paid delivery already did the underlying development and further qualification is sufficient for the declared reuse scope.

\textbf{C: Optional separate investment.} Deliberate research beyond the paid job is a distinct activity. Its construction and failed research remain in C, with additional qualification/reuse work in B. It is not automatically imposed on each learned candidate. Whole-enterprise economics always include A+B+C and operating/financial costs, whether or not an individual asset later looks attractive.

For a fresh case $i$, comparator $b$ and candidate $s$, the policy evaluates a paired net difference
\begin{equation}
d_{i,b}=\bigl[V_i a_{i,s}-c_{i,s}\bigr]-\bigl[V_i a_{i,b}-c_{i,b}\bigr]
-\frac{K_{B,s}+K_{C,s}}{N_s},
\end{equation}
where $a$ indicates a useful accepted outcome, $c$ includes attributable reuse costs and $N_s$ is a bounded forecast of remaining qualified reuse. Origin delivery $K_A$ is excluded from this incremental comparison but retained in enterprise totals. The graph's supplied evidence uses observed simulated acceptance, which can be wrong; the hidden truth process separately scores false admissions and downstream material failures.

The declared admission rule requires the quality contract and positive $\bar d_b-2\operatorname{SE}(d_b)$ for every required comparator. This is a policy arithmetic rule under the modeled paired cases, not a distribution-free confidence guarantee or protection against common misinterpretation. The candidate cannot choose its uncertainty allowance, omit a relevant comparator or change the policy after seeing results. Failed additional qualification costs remain recorded and cannot be erased by relabeling a never-admitted record as revalidation.

Prospective revalidation treats genuinely sunk retained-asset costs as sunk while preserving historical recovery. Its horizon cannot extend beyond remaining useful life. These are distinct questions: whether to keep an asset now; whether its whole retention program repaid; and whether the enterprise recovered all historical research. We report them separately.

\subsection{Cash, obligations and allocation}

The finite ledger separates seed capital, offered price, customer savings, invoices, collected receipts, operating costs, taxes/reserves, warranty obligations, public spending holds and available funds. Before dispatch, a maximum public cash and capacity reservation covers bounded retries and possible adverse capability effects. An unknown future success or receipt cannot fund current work. Prices, refunds, defaults, fees and settlement runoff remain in the ledger. Reported complete operating profit is collected receipts minus all accrued modeled costs, before the tax reserve is paid. The tax, warranty and base reserves reduce available operating funds; a reserve is not counted again as an expense. No token issuance, internal transfer or assumed appreciation enters operating income; any applicable \$AGIALPHA utility is a separate, unpriced integration boundary.

Accepted useful outcomes are the cost denominator. We report marginal delivered work, recurring operations and fully allocated enterprise cost separately. A rejected job consumes resources without increasing that denominator. Individual direct savings are diagnostic; whole-program cost includes every rejected retention and research attempt, and paired enterprise profit includes foregone sales caused by capacity use. Customer attention is reported as customer burden, not hidden provider staffing.

\section{Buyers, optional retention and bounded scale}

Buyers compare the offered network service with the matched human service, postponement and declining. Utility depends on private project value, quality, delivery time, trust, switching effort, budget and customer attention. Prices are payable conditional on accepted delivery for both service alternatives. Declining has zero utility and wins a zero-utility tie. Purchase is not automatic when the network price is lower.

The price policy searches an absolute grid of \usd{500}, \usd{1,500}, \usd{4,000}, \usd{10,000} and \usd{20,000}, plus a declared adaptive controller. The controller uses its own past offer/conversion history, not private buyer values or future outcomes. No fixed discount or cost-plus margin is imposed. The objective is complete terminal operating value subject to finite cash and a declared false-pass constraint. It is a repeated buyer best-response model with a bounded provider policy search, not a claim of universal equilibrium or optimality against unmodeled competitors.

Retention decisions use public completed-job feedback, current costs, observed qualification and explicit priors. Candidates can emerge from paid work without value or automatic admission. A costed assessment may reject, defer or support retention. A separate research option is available but never compulsory. Construction, integration and failure risk can make retaining cash the correct decision. Every shareable admitted capability is available throughout the eligible network; optional use requires a genuine functional gap and expected reuse value.

The same six agent workers and aggregate resources apply across causal arms. Enterprise expansion itself is not globally optimized: fixed resources deliberately isolate the value of optional capabilities and availability. The selected policy is best only within the declared grid and controller family. Producer and independent verifier work consume finite capacity. Quotas, model latency, proof-container capacity, queues, bounded retries and maintenance are represented. Weekly fluid quota constraints are planning bounds; they do not certify minute-by-minute burst compliance or arbitrary parallelism. Conservatively reserved unused retry capacity is not resold. Thus lower consumed resources do not automatically imply demonstrated higher sellable throughput.

Correlated mistakes are explicit. A common specification error can defeat correction and similar-model validation; independently informative checks have imperfect sensitivity and specificity. A latent invalid asset can falsely qualify and harm later work. Protected cases and scorer internals remain outside capsules. Automated clarification is bounded, and unsupported work exits the offering. Routine provider automation does not authorize deployments, policy changes or settlement actions outside existing governance.

\section{Interpretation, limits and empirical next steps}

The campaign supports profitable Beta-only service under its reference and efficient profiles. The best tested policy retains cash and continues ordinary delivery; it does not require capability compounding. Optional retention fails the incremental economic comparison in the primary conditions. These conclusions are conditional on scope, demand, capability, pricing and complete-cost assumptions. They establish neither realized sales nor production reliability, actual Specialist ASI or autonomous scientific invention. An admission count is not a value metric, and a technically successful capsule does not establish that the corresponding software method exists outside the simulation.

The strongest uncertainty concerns end-to-end faithful specification and complete acceptance without routine provider review. Published task results do not remove that uncertainty. Buyer values, repeat rates, trust, useful learning yield and transfer are also unobserved. The report therefore emphasizes thresholds, adverse scenarios and the conditions that erase the apparent advantage. Human-reference effort is estimated rather than observed; the result is not a scoped proof about the entire services market. The frozen buyer model also applies its hard precision gate only to the network, while human quality enters utility without the same eligibility test. This is an asymmetric choice rule, not complete buyer-contract symmetry. Human turnaround is fixed rather than sampled, and postponement does not create a deferred-job queue. These restrictions constrain any claim of commercial optimality.

The execution evidence supports the simulator and local governance controls. A later empirical campaign should first deliver a small, bounded, rights-cleared component portfolio using the exact declared frontier routes and complete usage metering. Independently protected cases should test requirements fidelity, source/proof correspondence, assumptions and corrections. The candidate should then be extracted from a motivating job, assigned A/B/C cost ownership, and compared on fresh missions against competent same-version Beta with known equivalent public methods allowed. Human-team comparables and actual customer choice can separately validate commercial assumptions.

Only after those tests should operational identity, encrypted execution boundaries or deployment authority be integrated through existing review procedures. No production governance changes are made here. Replication must also measure whether a later worthwhile capability campaign becomes cheaper or more reliable at matched difficulty before describing the process as self-acceleration.

\section{Break-even interpretation}

Let $g$ be additional expected saving or accepted-outcome value per applicable reuse after variable distribution, adaptation, risk and upkeep, $K$ the remaining retention plus separate-research cost, and $N$ the feasible qualified uses before expiry. The basic prospective threshold is
\begin{equation} N g > K,\qquad g>0.\end{equation}
This condition is necessary for a simple additive asset and not sufficient when qualification capacity displaces more valuable sales, dependencies are missing or cash is unavailable. With applicable arrival rate $\lambda$, uptake $u$, transfer success $t$ and useful life $L$, an illustrative upper bound is $N\leq\lambda u t L$; correlated failures and frontier erosion can reduce it. The simulator uses observed history and explicit priors rather than the future realized $N$ when deciding.

For service viability, a usable threshold is accepted collections per period exceeding complete recurring cost plus allocated setup/research burden. Human replacement cost, price, customer savings, revenue, profit and spendable operating funds are distinct. A favorable production cost does not set the price and a large invoice does not supply cash before collection.
