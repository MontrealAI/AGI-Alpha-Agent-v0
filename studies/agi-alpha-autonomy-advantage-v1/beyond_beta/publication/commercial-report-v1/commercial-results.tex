\section{Commercial core before additional Alpha}\label{sec:commercial-core-results}

This section evaluates the independently price-trained Beta policy, with no retained Alpha investment. The buyer can select the network, a human service, postponement or decline. Every amount below is a conditional simulation in USD: actual customer jobs and collections remain zero, and complete actual study cost remains unknown. Human work hours are assumed task breakdowns; their cost-derived service reference is not an observed consulting quotation.

The reference condition contains 40 independent enterprises over 72 operating weeks each. Its observed profile mixture is retained without reweighting. All declared delivery, research, shared operations, failed attempts, fees and refunds enter company cost, including costs in worlds that deliver no useful accepted output.

\par\smallskip The full unit-cost denominator is the sum of useful accepted outputs; its numerator includes every world, including 13 with zero useful output. On matched useful jobs, 793/793 (100.0\%) were priced below their own human reference. The pooled signed reference-minus-price difference is \$29,696,253, or \$37,448 per useful outcome (78.9\% of the matched reference total). The price grid is absolute: a provider can have low delivery cost while quoting above the human reference on some jobs. These are hypothetical price differences, not measured customer savings or willingness to pay.

\textbf{Quality remains a separate condition.} Training used a 2.5\% false-pass eligibility bound; it does not certify held-out reliability. The reference held-out pooled rate is 0.9\%. The condition appendix reports the same measure for every stress, including correlated specification errors.
