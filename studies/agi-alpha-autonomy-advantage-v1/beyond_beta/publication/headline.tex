\par\textbf{Primary finding.} The best tested ordinary-service policy charged \usd{10,000} and made no retained-capability investment. In the reference profile, 14 fresh histories produced a pooled full cost of \usd{1,058} per useful outcome against a \usd{23,714} cost-derived human reference; mean complete profit was \usd{151,024} and available operating funds \usd{122,040} over 72 business weeks plus runoff. All 14 were profitable. The same price produced no orders and cash closure in all 13 burdensome-profile histories. Optional retention reduced reference-condition mean profit by \usd{65}. Three network-admitted assets in the high-repeat/slow-Beta condition did not recover their direct retention costs. The supported positive result is conditional profitable Beta service; capability compounding and realized commercial success are not established.
