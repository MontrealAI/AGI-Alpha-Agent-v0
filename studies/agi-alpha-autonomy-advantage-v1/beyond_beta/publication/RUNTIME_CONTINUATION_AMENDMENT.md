# Operational envelope amendment — declared before outcome inspection

Prepared 27 October 2026 while the original frozen campaign was still running.
At this point the root had inspected only progress counts, elapsed time and file
sizes, not calibrated economic outcomes or policy scores.

The original execution enforces a 1,800-second wall-time cap. Progress suggested
that completing its 1,728 training plus 4,560 confirmation histories might require
longer. The scientific protocol, sources, scenarios, seeds, policy grid, objective,
selection, outcome definitions and analysis remain frozen and unchanged.

If and only if the original process stops at that declared cap with complete
valid gzip/JSON records, a deliberate additional execution may complete the exact
remaining prefix under a further 5,400-second envelope. It must not repeat,
replace or selectively remove any completed history. The original failure record,
elapsed work and original compressed-byte prefix commitments are preserved.
Gzip members may append to the recorded stream without altering prior bytes.
Incomplete or corrupt records cause a hard failure; no truncation is permitted.

The original runner writes a world before its time check, and appends its summary
to an in-memory list afterward. Consequently complete recorded worlds, not the
possibly one-short in-memory failure counters, establish the completed prefix.
Training selection must still be committed after all training and before any
confirmation. An existing selection commitment is checked and never replaced.

The continuation calls the original frozen simulation and selection functions.
It changes no scientific parameter, does not inspect outcomes to choose what to
run, and does not authorize an automatic second continuation. This is an
operational extension to finish a predetermined experiment, not a favorable-case
rerun. If unused, this amendment and utility remain labeled not executed.

The independent remaining histories may execute in a bounded pool of four local
processes. Output is committed in the original prescribed order. No world shares
state or future information with another; this changes wall time, not agents or
resources inside the economic model. Pending computation, if interrupted, is not
silently counted as completed. The simulator's actual compute cost remains
unknown. Any storage relocation preserves identical byte commitments and is
reported separately as an environment operation.
