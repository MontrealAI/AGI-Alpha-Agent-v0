# Prospective claims and analysis

Written before the first calibrated BeyondBeta history. Fictional control values are not economic outcomes. The frozen protocol and source closure define all histories and controller selection; this file defines how those results will be interpreted.

## Primary economic questions

1. **Beta service opportunity:** report complete modeled operating profit, useful acceptance, material false passes, cost per useful accepted outcome, customer effort, cash shortfall and buyer choices for the independently trained Beta policy. The human comparator is the fresh matched task/rate estimate, not measured worker effort or a quote. Price, human reference, customer saving, collections and spendable funds remain distinct.
2. **Paid-job retention increment:** in the reference condition, compare network-alpha minus moving-beta-only using identical exogenous seeds, the same controller, six workers and resource constraints. All A/B/C/operations/financial costs and displaced business enter complete enterprise profit. Additional retention may be rejected; the contrast is a policy effect, not an effect conditional on a lucky successful skill.

## Secondary mechanism questions

- Compare mandatory shared availability with creator-private availability, using the same agent count and budgets. Availability alone and reuse counts are not network value.
- Compare the Alpha-minus-Beta difference under moving and frozen frontiers. Absolute Beta gains are not Alpha gains.
- Compare network-alpha with the independently trained Beta policy, separately from matched-controller comparisons. Report the overall training winner; do not select the deployment recommendation by inspecting confirmation.
- Report candidates from paid jobs and optional separate research separately, additional B/C expense, all failures, fresh admission, recipient gaps/adaptation, direct resource recovery and whole-program net. A sunk origin job is never charged again to prospective admission, but remains in full enterprise cost.
- Report the number of distinct qualified invention/retention campaigns separately from descendant jobs. No inference of self-acceleration from volume, graph growth or repeated use.

## Uncertainty and reporting

Every one of the 19 declared conditions and all six arms is reported. Each condition has40 paired seeds cycling14 reference/13 burdensome/13 efficient profiles. Those profile weights are the declared design mix, not a population estimate. Show means, medians, 5th/95th scenario quantiles, loss frequency, cash-shortfall frequency and conditional95% bootstrap intervals for paired mean contrasts, using2,000 seed-resampling draws with analysis seed601775. Intervals quantify Monte Carlo variation within assumptions, not uncertainty about real demand or actual model competence. Cells with no accepted jobs report an undefined per-accepted cost rather than zero. Do not pool allstressconditions into a representative market probability.

The reference paid-job retention contrast is primary; other conditions diagnose boundaries. Report all signs and raw distributions. No post-hoc switch in workload, resource allocation, discount or acceptance requirement is allowed. An exploratory redesign after outcomes needs a separately named protocol and fresh seeds; it cannot replace the initial result.

## Break-even and capacity diagnostics

Derive accepted-volume and collected-price thresholds from observed simulated variable spending, failures and common fixed expenses; state when the calculation assumes stationary composition. For retained assets, report remaining incremental cost divided by attributable net saving per applicable reuse, forecast uses/lifetime, realized repayment and all failed program expense. A direct asset saving omits displaced sales; paired whole-enterprise profit is authoritative for the resource-allocation policy.

Inspect consumed resources separately from public worst-case reservations. Weekly token/request accounting is a fluid planning model, not exact intra-minute rate-limit execution. Producer and verifier budgets, queue delay, bounded retries and maintenance compete for the same finite resources. No claim of improved sellable throughput follows automatically from lower consumed resources while unused reservations remain blocked.

## Scope of positive conclusions

Positive service economics support conditional modeled viability; positive retained net value supports additional modeled Alpha; positive sharing contrasts support a modeled network increment. None establishes real customer sales, a deployed skill graph, actual Astra/Terra task competence, professional certification, Specialist ASI, graph superiority over a comparable registry, permanent exclusivity, or realized autonomous improvement. The typed research capsule and native cash/authority bridge are executed software; their modeled mission content is not a completed customer software component.
