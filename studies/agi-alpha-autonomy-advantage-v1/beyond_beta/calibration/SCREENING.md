# BeyondBeta: bounded independent venture screen

Prepared 26 October 2026 before BeyondBeta outcomes. This is a new choice of offering and hypotheses. Earlier studies, failed findings, frozen protocols and manuscripts remain unchanged. Only external factual sources and their dates are reused; no old GPU hours, human112-hour reference, price, acceptance rate, construction budget or compulsory asset sequence is carried forward.

## Recommendation

Select a **proof-carrying bespoke software foundry** as the primary offering, with a **simulation-native inverse-design foundry** as the strongest contrast. The primary product is a commissioned bounded numerical or planning component, accompanied by a faithful executable specification, implementation, checked proof or explicitly bounded verification record, environmental-assumption manifest, integration adapter, regression package and bounded support. A proof about a simplified function is not accepted as proof of the integrated product.

The primary scientific question is incremental: can admitted reusable contract and proof-composition assets improve complete delivery economics over a Beta system that already has current frontier reasoning, formal-verification software, competent implementation and independent checking? Beta is permitted to reconstruct the same contracts and methods. Alpha has no unexplained expert multiplier. A gain must arise from avoiding measured repeated work, improving demonstrable coverage or making a formerly uneconomic complete job feasible under the same acceptance contract.

## Four complete offerings and falsifiers

### 1. Proof-carrying bespoke software

**Buyer/trigger.** Software platform, scientific-computing or planning-product teams needing a new bounded component, changed numerical rule, interface behavior or constraint family. Real recurring repository issues support the existence of change demand; they do not establish demand for a paid proof-carrying package.

**Competent Beta.** Current frontier reasoning plus ordinary libraries, SMT/proof tools, property-based testing, known algorithms, independent specification checking and all permitted reconstruction. Beta may discover an equivalent method on any job. It must not be compared with a draft, an old weak model or a test-only baseline when Alpha receives a stronger acceptance instrument.

**Alpha candidates.** (i) Versioned interface contracts and environmental summaries with explicit assumptions and falsification evidence; (ii) a specification-preserving proof/adapter composition procedure that discharges compatibility obligations when composing those contracts. Each asset and their joint use need separate qualification. These are opportunities, not a mandatory two-step sequence.

**Held-out test.** New functions, callers and dependency versions, with untouched behavioral clauses and adversarial counterexamples. Compare Beta, Alpha without retained assets, Alpha with admitted assets, and Beta receiving an independently equivalent public method. Include end-to-end source/proof correspondence, missing assumptions, stale contracts and solver timeout. Reject a proof whose specification omits customer requirements.

**Falsifier.** Beta reconstructs as cheaply, formalization cost exceeds saved proof work, composition weakens the specification, or a newer Beta version supplies the equivalent asset. A successful proof checker cannot validate a false axiom or establish customer usefulness by itself.

### 2. Simulation-native inverse design

**Buyer/trigger.** A non-safety-critical numerical R&D team repeats inverse design or parameter inference as objectives, boundary conditions or uncertain inputs change.

**Product.** Executable design solver, trusted reference generator, derivative/error diagnostics, applicability detector, reproducible package and fallback. The first bounded scope could use parametric diffusion/thermal fields rather than certify real equipment.

**Beta/Alpha distinction.** Beta already knows reduced bases, adjoints, operator inference and ordinary surrogate fitting. Alpha may retain a qualified problem-family representation and a residual/error-control composition that reduces high-fidelity reference solves across genuinely new tasks. Training/reference generation, domain-shift rejection and fallback are charged.

**Falsifier.** Standard reduced-order software matches the asset, held-out gradients are inaccurate, optimizer exploits surrogate error, or new boundary conditions invalidate the representation. Numerical agreement is not physical validation. No speedup ratio from a paper is assigned to current Astra/Terra.

### 3. Hardware-aware algorithm foundry

**Buyer/trigger.** Infrastructure team changes operators, shapes, devices or compilers. **Product:** integrated optimized component, independent numerical/performance record and fallback. **Beta:** tuned libraries, compiler fusion/autotuning, profiling and current frontier code search. **Alpha:** an admitted workload-specific transformation and portability guard with measurable reuse savings. **Falsifier:** mature compiler already performs it, requalification consumes the gain, or new Beta/compiler release makes it redundant. This remains a strong candidate, but it is not selected merely because a previous study used it. No prior resource or human-hour envelope is reused.

### 4. Coordination/mechanism design

**Buyer/trigger.** Resource-allocation platform changes capacity, participant types or welfare constraints. **Product:** executable allocation/payment rule, scoped incentive/feasibility guarantees, adversarial strategic simulation, integration and rollback. **Beta:** known VCG/auction families, optimization and ordinary mechanism search. **Alpha:** reusable constraint-preserving transformations plus validated composition for a new bounded institutional rule. **Falsifier:** the fixing process sacrifices the claimed objective, incentive conditions fail off-distribution, or realistic behavior differs from the simulated type model. No real payments, deployments or legal compliance claims are required for the research witness.

## Moving Beta and network admission

External progress evidence justifies versioning Beta; it does not identify a future rate of progress or an Alpha asset's half-life. Fix the same Beta release/tool versions for each paired Alpha/Beta comparison, then repeat at declared release epochs. Measure an overlap probe for each active asset after a Beta/tool update. If the new baseline offers an equivalent function at comparable complete cost, its incremental Alpha value is zero or negative, even if the file remains in the registry.

Every admitted Alpha asset becomes available network-wide. Use is optional and requires scope, version, applicability and incremental-value checks. Creator-local ownership cannot delay mandatory availability. Discovery, retrieval, adaptation, independent validation, revocation and upkeep are charged. Registry and graph get the same admitted inventory and evidence; graph guidance earns no automatic benefit. Beta may use its native/public equivalents and may independently reconstruct, but does not receive Alpha's experimental admitted assets in the treatment-control contrast. This isolates the requested Alpha layer rather than pretending that an equally capable baseline cannot invent.

## Prospective economic hypotheses

1. **Initial service economics:** complete autonomous delivery may be viable even when Alpha contributes zero. Compare competent human service and strong current Beta separately; human replacement cost is neither price nor revenue.
2. **Incremental retained value:** after construction, failed attempts, qualification and upkeep, Alpha's fresh-work cost or acceptance frontier improves over the same-version Beta. Include a zero-gain and harmful-composition case.
3. **Complementarity:** joint contract/adapter qualification can enable an outcome that neither asset alone supplies. Test a factorial rather than impose a synergy multiplier.
4. **Network value:** mandatory availability and optional adoption can recover construction over multiple genuine recipients. An equivalent-capability recipient produces no transfer value. Internal transfers and token issuance are not outside revenue.
5. **Erosion:** newer Beta/tool releases reduce unique applicability or reconstruction cost. Positive static-Beta value need not survive moving Beta.
6. **Research efficiency:** a better proposal/experiment method must improve new complete research campaigns with the same starting Beta, information and budget. More reused jobs are not self-acceleration.

Price is an optional policy choice derived from buyer response, scope and alternatives, including declining to operate. Do not fix a discount from human labor estimates. A practical prospective simulator can train a bounded absolute-price or value-based policy grid, then freeze it before held-out buyers/jobs/version epochs. Actual willingness to pay, sales, collections and human-team measurements are absent. Full actual research cost is unknown. Simulated revenue must remain explicitly simulated.
