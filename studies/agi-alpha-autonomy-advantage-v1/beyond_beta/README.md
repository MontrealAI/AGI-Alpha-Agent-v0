# Paid Work and Retained Alpha — BeyondBeta

This package studies a proof-carrying numerical and planning software service: a buyer receives a bounded component, an explicit specification, implementation and checking evidence, packaging, and scoped support. It asks whether useful paid work can also yield reusable capabilities whose **additional** future value exceeds the complete cost of retaining, qualifying, sharing and maintaining them against a competent, improving baseline.

**The economic results are conditional simulation.** Actual customer jobs, production model calls and collected sales remain zero; actual complete research cost is unknown. The local cash, graph lifecycle, cryptographic fixtures, native integration, analysis and verification procedures were executed. Their execution does not establish real customer demand, live identity control, task competence or commercial Alpha.

The optional-retention comparison uses `policy-23` at the same quote, with a risk-adjusted investment rule and operating-only funding. Confirmation contains 4,560 histories across 19 conditions and six arms. Selecting an optional-retention policy never forces a capability investment or an Alpha success.

The commercial service, the human-service reference, the strongest ordinary policy, retained-capability value, sharing, moving-Beta erosion and whole-enterprise policy value are distinct questions. Read the paper and demonstration for the complete conditional results rather than inferring a flywheel from one positive asset or a growing graph.

## The three accounts

- **A — delivery:** all work and learning necessary to complete the original customer job, including unsuccessful attempts and correction.
- **B — incremental retention and reuse:** additional extraction, qualification, publication, discovery, adaptation, revalidation and upkeep.
- **C — optional separate research:** separately chosen research work and its failures.

Origin A remains immutable provenance and enterprise cost. It is not charged a second time to prospective Alpha. The enterprise ledger also retains shared operations, payment adjustments, obligations, reserves and failures. Seed cash is financing, never revenue.

## Executed checks and preserved limitations

The graph controls pass 60/60 fictional adversarial cases. The original confirmation auditor passes all 4,560 histories. Its original training report preserves 51 temporal-epoch flags; a separate post-freeze diagnostic identifies the missing Beta/policy increments in the auditor and passes all 6,288 unchanged histories. The frozen auditor and its original FAIL remain included.

Native confirmation reconciles 24 preselected histories through 48 existing `planMission` calls and 264 modeled direct-agent authority checks. Two separately labeled verification replays reproduce exact economic outcomes despite fresh ephemeral public keys. These replays add no primary samples. Existing club settlement roles are unchanged; fixture eligibility is not live ENS authentication.

The original storage interruption is preserved: 1,289 complete records, their exact record hashes, the original incomplete gzip bytes, an unfinished tail, and explicit continuation metadata. Complete original CPU time and monetary research cost remain unknown. No failure is erased to make the final campaign appear uninterrupted.

## Full companion versus public source subset

The source/evidence companion preserves **all 52 frozen files byte for byte**, including exact sibling runtime dependencies. The public publication candidate contains **51 of those 52 files** and all runtime dependencies. It omits the non-runtime `foundations/source-provenance.json`, which contains private source locators. The public coverage manifest declares that omission. Full source-freeze verification therefore requires the companion; the public subset must not be described as the complete frozen evidence closure.

Full private supplied manuscripts and reference images are not redistributed. Their identities, hashes and inspection findings remain in the companion's provenance records. Three raw gzip streams and the historical evidence archive are supplied separately rather than duplicated inside the companion ZIP.

Nothing in the packaging tool publishes to GitHub, Library, a chain or another service. Publication is a separate action by the research lead.
