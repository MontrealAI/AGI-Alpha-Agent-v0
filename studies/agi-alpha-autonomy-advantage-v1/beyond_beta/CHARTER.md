## Paid-job learning and cost ownership

The primary mechanism is customer-paid useful work -> possible reusable capability -> additional retention and fresh Alpha qualification -> network-wide availability -> better fresh work. A candidate need not originate in a separate research project. Not every demanding delivery produces a useful or rights-cleared candidate.

Maintain three disjoint activity accounts. A: Job economics includes all delivery resources, including learning, experimentation and development undertaken to produce that outcome. B: Retention economics includes only additional extraction, generalization, fresh qualification, packaging, integration, distribution, revalidation and upkeep. C: Optional separate investment includes deliberate research beyond the paid job. Charge each activity once. Origin-job costs remain in complete enterprise economics and provenance but are not charged again to the prospective retention admission test. Revenue is recorded only when the modeled payment arrives. A profitable job can leave a skill with low additional retention cost; that possibility and its failures must be tested, not assumed.

Read the supplied Gym, Specialist ASI and Successor Omega manuscripts alongside Transformational Intelligence. Connect Identify -> Out-Learn -> Out-Think -> Out-Design -> Out-Strategise -> Out-Execute -> Out-Evolve to concrete task and decision events. These manuscripts organize the research; their concepts do not certify that the modeled enterprise exists.

Keep unsuccessful approaches in a separate failure-memory structure. They may inform research decisions but never acquire positive Alpha or execution authority merely because they are remembered. Protected evaluation cases and scorer internals stay outside shareable capsules. Requalification must respond to relevant models, tools, data, routing, verifiers and environment changes, not merely a model name.

## Scientific question

Beta means competent current frontier intelligence plus ordinary tools, used expertly by producers, validators, correction agents, capability builders and investment evaluators. Alpha means additional **net mission advantage** over Beta and relevant already available capabilities. Test Beta's economics against equivalent human services separately from Alpha's incremental mission/economic value. Do not double count them. A model writing a heuristic is not evidence that it performed the substantive job.

Start from first principles with bounded independent venture screening, new hypotheses/calibration/protocol/experiments. The organizing manuscript is Transformational Intelligence: Learning to Change the Game. Preserve authorized purpose while changing representations, information acquisition, dependencies, executable protocols and intermediate objectives. Choose ambitious executable venture/R&D work, not clerical automation or business plans. Define binding restriction, mechanism, construction, failure and falsifier. Complementary bundles may create value jointly.

## Economics and decisions

Human-service reference must match scope/period/quality/responsibility and use documented fees or transparent multidisciplinary labor-and-rate estimates. Record geography/currency/date/uncertainty. Advertised fees do not prove human-only work; novel-offer reference and unobserved willingness to pay must be disclosed. Commercial competitor benchmark is human service, not rival AI architectures.

Price is a decision variable. Buyers may choose the network, human service, postpone or decline. Include quality/trust, switching/integration, finite demand/budgets, capacity/time, repeat needs and collections. Define objective/constraints before searching; train/select then test fresh conditions. Report best within model, not universal equilibrium. No fixed discount, cost-plus rule, mandatory investment count or reinvestment percentage.

Allow retain cash, ordinary delivery, invest, defer, bundle, abandon, or no investment. Spend only if expected complete incremental value and current cash justify it. Complete costs include every producer/validator/tool/compute/storage/retry/recovery stage, invention/search/failures, confirmation, migration/distribution/access control/upkeep/composition/revalidation, setup and subscriptions. Separate seed, prices, savings, collections, costs, obligations/reserves and available earned funds. Future receipts cannot pay current spending. Token issuance/appreciation/internal transfers are not profit.

Test conservative/central/favorable capability/demand/cost profiles, moving Beta, Alpha erosion and sharing under finite concurrency/quotas/queues/validator capacity. Report cost per useful accepted outcome, break-even demand, affordable investment, useful lifetime/repayment, cash shortfall and bottlenecks. Preserve unsuccessful conditions. Freeze final protocol before confirmation; later redesigns are exploratory with fresh tests.
