## Confirmed finding

The primary experiment supports **conditional modeled Beta service profitability**. It does **not** establish a profitable retained-Alpha flywheel. The fair, bounded development search selected Beta-only operation in all three regimes; fresh confirmation executed 128 independent worlds per regime and 12 paired policy arms.

| Selected enterprise, two modeled years | Mean complete pre-tax profit | Median profit | Profitable worlds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | −$28,572.69 | −$28,244.12 | 0 / 128 |
| Central | $497,058.08 | $454,511.24 | 127 / 128 |
| Favorable | $2,423,230.89 | $2,446,298.60 | 128 / 128 |

For the central useful-delivery cohort, the pooled human-team reference is **$54,729.08**, the modeled bid **$25,599.20**, and the fully allocated enterprise cost **$4,672.21 per useful outcome**. The matching favorable figures are **$88,702.85**, **$55,896.30**, and **$4,587.98**. Costs include unsuccessful attempts and worlds. Reference rates are reported evidence; scope hours, demand, buyer value and task performance are explicit assumptions. These are not observed quotes, sales or production costs.

All **4,608 primary policy histories contain zero capability campaigns**. Paid jobs leave candidates, but the controller declines retention after accounting for additional proof, upkeep and displacement of profitable work. A separately identified development-world illustration completes paid learning, simulated admission and four useful peer jobs; its whole-policy profit is **$281,949.96 below matched Beta**. It is a selected negative economic illustration, not a successful confirmation. The optional invention-screen experiment also fails complete-cost recovery. The paper preserves these limitations and the full registered sensitivity results.

The conservative configuration should not be launched under its assumptions. The search compares specified operating policies; it does not establish global optimality, separately optimize a paid-retention-only policy, or optimize a prelaunch no-entry option. Profit is before tax; tax reserves constrain available cash without becoming fictional expenses. The declared $5,000 historical-research allowance is an assumption, not a measurement of earlier spending.

## The accounting distinction

| Record | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **A — paid job** | All formulation, learning, experimental search, development, verification, correction and delivery undertaken for the customer. |
| **B — retention and reuse** | Additional rights-cleared extraction, generalization, fresh Alpha tests, packaging, distribution, adaptation, revalidation and upkeep. |
| **C — optional research** | Deliberately chosen development beyond the paid scope. Its full project cost includes associated B assessment and lifecycle expenses. |

Every resource bill has exactly one cost lane. Common business and the declared historical-research allowance are separate. Source-job A remains in the job and consolidated accounts; it is not billed again to retention or used a second time as its admission hurdle. Candidate emergence is uncertain, and it confers no validation status. A currently collected accepted job may still harbor an undetected error.

## Reproduce offline

The simulator needs **Node.js 20 or later** and no third-party Node packages. The convenience copier uses Python 3. It makes a new directory and refuses to overwrite registered outputs. No API access, credentials, paid service or live transaction is required.

```bash
python3 reproduce.py --mode tests --output /tmp/paid-alpha-tests
python3 reproduce.py --mode confirmation --output /tmp/paid-alpha-confirmation
python3 reproduce.py --mode full --output /tmp/paid-alpha-full
```

`full` redoes development policy search, the strengthened Beta policy search, fresh confirmation, sensitivities, capacity selection/confirmation and the separately registered independent-campaign mechanism test. `confirmation` uses the exact saved final policies. `FLYWHEEL_WORKERS` sets local parallelism from 1 through 8; arithmetic, seeds and ordered output do not change. Dates in reproduced metadata may differ; numerical results and redacted economic graph records are reproducible. Cryptographic tests deliberately use fresh random ephemeral encryption material.

Direct local software qualification:

```bash
node --test model/*.test.mjs graph/lifecycle.test.mjs
```

Paper rebuild requires the standard LaTeX/Python plotting environment described by `paper/build_paper.py` and `paper/bind_results.py`. The executable simulator itself has no plotting or TeX dependency.
