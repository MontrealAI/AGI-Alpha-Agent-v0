# Paid work. Retained Alpha. Expanding possibility.

## Prospective scope

This is a modeled study of a three-agent optimization foundry. A customer buys a scoped executable scheduling, batching or rolling-planning policy, original-constraint checks, held-out comparison with a competent known solver, and reproducible handoff. An unchanged known solver alone is not sold as an invented improvement. Formal inputs and non-safety-critical prototype scope are required. Unresolved interpretation may be rejected or deferred. Physical deployment and professional certification are outside the contract.

The study uses current Astra Pro/max and Terra/max with adequate context, tools, ordinary solvers, critique, correction and generic cheap checks in every arm. All job success, learning, workload, demand and skill-effect parameters are explicit uncertain assumptions; none is converted from an unrelated benchmark score. Tariffs and human-service rates have dated primary sources. The graph and simulator are executed research code; the simulated frontier jobs are not live API experiments.

## Separate questions and ledgers

1. Can competent **Beta** profitably deliver the matched useful outcome below the modeled human-team service reference?
2. Does retained **Alpha** add net value over Beta plus capabilities already available to each recipient?
3. Does network availability add value beyond local use at the same aggregate agents, compute and quotas?
4. Can the advantage survive hypothetical changes in Beta, tools and available methods?

**A** contains every paid-job activity, including formulation, search, learning, development, verification and correction. A candidate opportunity may emerge from those same resources; it does not create another research bill. **B** contains additional rights-cleared extraction, generalization, tests, packaging, distribution, adaptation, revalidation and upkeep. **C** is optional separate research beyond the job. Each bill has exactly one lane. Common business and historical-allowance costs are separately identified. Consolidated profit includes them all once. Source-job A is a reference in a retention record, not a second expense or Alpha admission hurdle. New optional C is included in its complete incremental investment and Alpha hurdle.

Paid-learning retention selection uses observable acceptance, completed modeled collection and absence of an already-observed refund. It never filters on hidden simulated correctness. A latent erroneous accepted output can therefore create a candidate, fail fresh checks, produce a false admission, or be retired. Rights-restricted and failed candidates remain outside positive admitted Alpha.

## Buyer game and policy objective

For buyer value V, conditional quality q, completion belief s, offered price P, trust/switching burden T, incremental failure loss D, and delay burden L, the AI utility is `s*(q*V-P)-T-(1-s*q)*D-L`. The human alternative has its own stated completion, quality, price and delay. Postponement and no purchase remain options. D excludes the already-lost value V. Both offerings use a contingent accepted-outcome pricing convention solely as a stated modeling assumption; the public rate schedule does not establish those contract terms. Human effort is estimated per accepted package including an unsuccessful-search allocation.

Price is a human-reference ratio selected from a prespecified grid, with an optional observed-capacity adjustment. Retention, optional research, bundles, reserves, deferral and abandonment are economic choices. A policy scores `mean discounted accrued profit + 0.25 * lower-tail CVaR10`, subject to observed training insolvency at most5%. If no policy qualifies, choose the least-risk available policy and disclose the constraint failure. No mandatory investment, reinvestment percentage or combined scientific success score is imposed.

Twenty-four training worlds per declared scenario select enterprise, investment-enabled network and Beta-only policies. The exact selections are saved before 128 fresh confirmation worlds per scenario. The independent unit is the world/network seed, not every descendant job. The twelve paired arms and all parameter sweeps are listed in `protocol.json`. Capacity choices use a separate training grid and a separate fresh confirmation set. Selection never reads its confirmation outcomes.

## Alpha evidence and lifecycle

Candidate-producing customer work is separate from fresh paired evidence. Each candidate is compared with current competent Beta plus relevant already-available capabilities. Both arms receive the same held-out mechanical endpoint, and both consume billed/reserved resources. Twelve fresh paired probes, with six on revalidation, are the default; a separate 96-case capability check is charged. A one-sided Student-t lower bound is a conditional decision heuristic, not a guarantee against systematic bias. Correlated semantic mistakes persist, and the simulations report false admissions and escaped errors separately.

The complete incremental retention/research hurdle, including assessment, adaptation allowances and future upkeep, is compared with conservative estimated future reuse from prior observed inquiries. Evaluation replaces its blind cash reservation with actual cost once observed. Correctness alone cannot admit a record. Bundles retain their joint conditions; individual components need not be independently profitable. No graph-size term, rarity premium or token appreciation enters value.

The executed research implementation separates creator, writer, validator, admitting root publisher and execution authorization. Direct-label authenticated fixture proofs support the additive agent-attestation layer; existing Jobs club contracts and governance are unchanged. Current Beta/policy/scope/environment/comparison universe and dependency pins are checked. Admission enables network-wide discovery; adaptation and use are optional. Stale, retired, superseded or revoked history cannot regain authority. Encrypted capsules contain sanitized capability descriptions, not protected evaluation cases/scorers. Encryption is real local AES-GCM; identity/enforcement are declared research fixtures, not deployed ENS or zero-knowledge systems.

## Cash, automation and capacity

No routine provider human review or manual onboarding is included. All inferred roles and automated tools have explicit budgets. Complete API input/output/reasoning/cache use, failed candidates, correction, tools, compute, storage, intake, support, bounded recovery, acquisition, subscriptions, setup and historical allowance are charged. Subscription access never supplies unlimited free API calls. The current nominal tariff is held fixed for the modeled horizon; future tariff/market changes are explored as sensitivities.

Cash starts from finite external seed. Work reserves its blind maximum before execution. Actual bills, delayed collections, bad debt, refunds, unpaid obligations, maintenance commitments and protected reserves are separate. Reserves are not expenses; customer savings and internal capability valuations are not revenue. Available cash deducts obligations and reserves; conservative earned-available funds additionally require collected operating profit. No future receipt finances a current commitment. Reported profit is before payment of the held tax provision; reinvestible cash excludes that provision.

Token, CPU, validator, occupancy, concurrency and queue constraints apply to delivery and capability work. Reserved resource flows are distributed over actual modeled service weeks. Unused worst-case reservations are not released early, a conservative scheduling assumption. Stale queued reuse is canceled; revoked or stale in-flight reuse may be quarantined, with incurred costs retained. Material errors trigger paid bounded automated recovery or abstention, not free human intervention. Throughput is a target conditional on the stated quotas, not a claim of account entitlement or production scale.

## Falsifiers and reporting

Preserve the zero-demand, zero-invention, zero-productive-skill, no-Alpha-effect and full-Beta-absorption conditions. Compare Alpha with no new capability, network availability with local use, bundles with components, paid learning with no paid learning, and optional separate research with none. Report full-cost profit, cash risk, accepted/useful outcomes, errors, candidate/admission/retirement, fresh recipient work and capacity separately. Report the five/95th outcome percentiles, mean Monte Carlo intervals and Wilson frequency intervals, with epistemic ranges distinguished from sampling noise.

The optional independent-campaign experiment has its own registered fresh seeds. It does not infer recursive improvement from repeated customer jobs, and it does not count a locally cheaper experiment protocol as a profitable network-admitted asset. The paper claims neither realized Specialist ASI nor operational SUCCESSOR Ω.

All code, parameter sources and this protocol are hashed before training. Earlier unrun designs, prototype failures and previous studies are preserved. Any substantive later redesign requires a new labeled protocol and fresh seeds; software corrections retain the failed attempt and a clear amendment. No results have been selected to manufacture success.
