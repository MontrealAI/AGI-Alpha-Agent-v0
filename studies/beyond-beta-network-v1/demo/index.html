01 / The new possibility

Customers pay for outcomes. Learning may remain.

Job development is charged once. Retention pays only the work still needed to make a candidate reusable and valuable. Competent Beta, local Alpha and network Alpha use the same agents and aggregate compute.

01

Deliver with competent Beta

02

Identify what the job leaves

A useful paid job may leave a representation, policy, verifier or experiment protocol. Candidate emergence and reusable rights are uncertain.

03

Pay only the additional work

04

Admit and make Alpha available

Price ceiling relative to declining—

08 / Scientific boundaries

Four evidence categories. One honest ledger.

Observed in our work

Simulator execution, source inspection and audit checks. No observed customer sale, frontier job or autonomous invention.

Reported externally

Official pricing, public service rates and algorithm-discovery research. Vendor reports are distinct from independent studies.

Calibrated from sources

Role-to-service mapping and tariff arithmetic. Team effort and commercial behavior remain assumptions; a benchmark score is never a customer acceptance rate.

Assumed or explored

Demand, buyer value, effort, failures, search success, transfer and lifetime. Their uncertainty is tested and preserved.
