## What the source contributes

### A mission environment, not merely a prompt

The Gym is a controlled, versioned mission environment with state, observations, bounded actions, transitions, reward dimensions, hard constraints, scenario distributions, verification and receipts (PDF pp.18–21 / body pp.6–9). Training or formation environments are distinct from adversarial, transfer, protected fresh-proof and requalification environments. Teaching material does not certify a candidate; refusal and truncation are distinct from successful completion.

**Use here:** model the customer's supplied formal problem and executable constraint bridge, distinguish candidate-producing work from fresh admission probes, and keep protected cases and scorer internals outside the reusable capsule. A complete pipeline must account for intake, tools, verifiers, failures, packaging and lifecycle work. Formal correctness applies to the specified problem; physical deployment and omitted intent remain outside this scope.

### Competent Beta, gross opportunity and net Alpha

The manuscript treats commonly rentable frontier capability plus public methods and tooling as valuable Beta. Gross opportunity is not yet Alpha. Alpha is residual mission advantage after the complete cost and relevant risk burden, and it may erode through diffusion, imitation, model improvement, drift and upkeep (PDF pp.33–35 / body pp.21–23; definitions PDF pp.78–80).

**Use here:** the counterfactual keeps competent frontier reasoning, ordinary algorithms, tools and reconstructable available methods. The human-service price difference is a commercial comparison and is not inserted into the Alpha counterfactual. A correct or novel skill is not automatically valuable. Future Beta improvement can retire a formerly admitted asset.

The manuscript's discussion of private complements or strategic advantage is a theoretical proposition. It is not evidence that a specific capability is rare, exclusive, durable, profitable or demanded. We do not assume those properties.

### Formation, protected proof and authority are separate

SEIZE proposes bounded evidence acquisition and allows retaining, renting, repairing, building, partnering, holding or retiring. The source distinguishes formation evidence from protected promotion evidence and prohibits the candidate from certifying itself or expanding its authority (PDF pp.36–38 / body pp.24–26). Acceptance, validation, admission and action authority are separate decisions (PDF pp.42–44 / body pp.30–32; state machine and API, PDF pp.72–77).

**Use here:** an accepted paid job may create a candidate opportunity; that candidate still needs fresh net-Alpha assessment, rights and scope checks, eligible attestations and lifecycle admission before shared use. The graph test implementation does not establish the economic truth of the simulated probe results. Access to a capability never grants production action authority.

### Specialist ASI is a bounded empirical designation

The source requires a complete mission-specific institution to outperform the strongest credible alternatives on fresh representative work, at a preset positive margin and subject to capability, economics, reliability, sovereignty, governance and transfer constraints (PDF pp.39–41 / body pp.27–29). A material change needs new evidence. The source's operational admission includes an accountable human authority; its example implementation is not a production security boundary.

**Use here:** the paper does not designate the simulated foundry as realized Specialist ASI or an admitted operational Successor Ω. The user-authorized primary is a fully automated bounded research simulation with no routine provider human review. That scope is not equivalent to satisfying the manuscript's production institution and authority requirements. The source's real-world completion gate belongs to later empirical validation and does not block a clearly labeled modeled edition.

### Reuse, failure memory and recursive improvement

Repeated use, prompt editing and job volume do not alone establish recursion. A stronger claim requires retained knowledge to improve the formation, proof or governance of a fresh successor, without weakening proof or governance. Failure memory can reduce repeated mistakes, but rejected architectures and prohibitions are separate from positive admitted capabilities (PDF pp.46–48 / body pp.34–36). The source also requires fresh proof after material changes (PDF p.69 / body p.57).

**Use here:** distinguish customer outcomes, admitted reusable capability, recipient work, and independent later invention campaigns. The optional meta experiment tests a limited family-screen hypothesis. It does not demonstrate arbitrary algorithm discovery, realized Specialist ASI, open-ended self-improvement or profitable graph admission by itself. Failure records remain auditable without becoming active positive Alpha.

### Asset lifecycle and reality gap

The source discusses cost basis, carrying cost, migration, rights, useful life and requalification. Its managerial asset ledger is not an accounting-standard conclusion. A model or Gym can have a reality gap, and simulated verification does not eliminate that gap (PDF pp.49–52 / body pp.37–40).

**Use here:** charge every activity once across the paid-work, incremental-retention and optional-research ledgers; include upkeep, transfers, revalidation and retirement. Do not present internal asset valuations as cash or operating earnings. Fresh economic advantage is assessed against current Beta, with cash available before spending.

## Latest governing accounting: A, B and C

The user's latest charter, rather than a numerical empirical result in this manuscript, supplies the explicit three-part study accounting:

- **A — paid-job work:** formulation, learning, candidate search, construction, tests, correction and delivery already undertaken to satisfy the customer. These expenses remain in the job and consolidated ledger.
- **B — additional retention and reuse:** only extraction, sanitization and rights clearance, generalization, fresh assessment, packaging, integration, distribution, transfer, upkeep and revalidation not already paid in A.
- **C — optional separate research:** work beyond the paid scope, chosen only when its prospective benefit justifies its complete additional cost.

The retention decision does not demand repayment of A a second time. It compares remaining B/C costs with future incremental mission advantage over competent Beta. Enterprise profit still includes A+B+C and common expenses exactly once. A job's accepted outcome does not guarantee collection, a usable candidate, rights clearance, admission, transfer or profitable reuse. Cash from a future collection cannot pay a current retention expense.

The learning sequence is connected operationally: identify the buyer's restriction; out-learn through paid experiments; out-think and out-design a candidate formulation; out-strategise by pricing and selecting optional retention; out-execute the supported outcome; out-evolve only if fresh evidence establishes a useful retained advantage. These names are a conceptual map, not new measured performance multipliers.

## Quantities explicitly excluded from calibration

1. The Aurelia invoice-integrity cycle is explicitly synthetic and unaudited (PDF pp.56–60 / body pp.44–48). Its accuracy, human burden, savings, transfer gains, rejected earlier system and investment returns are not empirical AI performance or market evidence. No number from that example enters the current model.
2. The commercial price bands, including large enterprise pilot bands, are strategic illustrations rather than observed fees or willingness to pay (PDF pp.65–66 / body pp.53–54). They do not replace the independently sourced hourly service-rate benchmark and explicitly assumed scope hours.
3. Mission-physics and geometry language is an institutional design analogy, not a proved natural law of organizations (claim boundary in front matter).
4. The code sketches describe an architecture, not a complete secure production system (PDF p.76 / body p.64). Our tested local graph controls have a separately declared implementation and threat boundary.
5. The source's empirical completion criterion is a future validation standard. This simulation does not claim that paid mandates, fresh physical-world value, real authority admission or a superior real successor occurred.

## Decisive limitations retained in the study

The venture fails to establish a productive flywheel if paid demand does not materialize; useful accepted outcomes are too rare or expensive; candidates do not retain rights-cleared reusable content; fresh Alpha assessment rejects them; transfer and integration consume their gains; upkeep or advancing Beta erodes value; or cash and capacity cannot fund the worthwhile remainder. Profitable Beta service, inexpensive retention, positive network contribution and faster independent invention are distinct findings and can differ in sign.

The manuscript's falsification section (PDF pp.67–69 / body pp.55–57) supports narrowing, holding, rejecting and retiring when those conditions fail. No favorable conclusion or mandatory succession count is inherited from the source.
