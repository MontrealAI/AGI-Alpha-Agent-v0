## Organizing principles for this study

**Competent Beta and incremental Alpha are different economic objects (PDF33–35).** Beta includes widely available frontier models, standard agents, ordinary tools, public data, and purchasable implementation. It may deliver valuable and profitable work. Alpha is the remaining mission advantage over the strongest applicable reference after complete lifecycle burden, risk, dependencies, and simulation-to-reality uncertainty. Model access, an unfamiliar representation, or an extra graph node is not sufficient. The new study can find profitable customer work with no retained Alpha; that is a valid outcome. Conversely, future reusable value must not be credited twice as both customer job savings and enterprise income.

The latest user charter refines the accounting for paid work. Resources already used to complete the customer outcome remain in job ledger A. Retention ledger B contains only new extraction, sanitization, rights work, generalization, fresh assessment, distribution, and upkeep. Optional separate research is ledger C. Prospective admission tests the remaining incremental burden; consolidated profitability retains the complete A+B+C denominator. This is a current decision/accounting interpretation, not a claim that the manuscript explicitly uses those ledger labels.

**Proof, admission, action, and capital allocation remain separate (PDF41–44).** The manuscript distinguishes underwriting a bounded evidence program, authorizing its execution, accepting an output, admitting future influence, and allocating capital. Capability dominance does not itself permit external effect. Its full Ω definition requires fresh proof, accountable admission, issued scope, rollback readiness, and Chronicle binding. It also allows restriction, rejection, continued proof, incumbent retention, and rollback. Our in-process `ADMITTED` record implements a much narrower modeled capability gate. It is not an achieved Specialist ASI or production SUCCESSOR Ω designation, and it does not issue financial, legal, physical, or settlement authority.

**Reuse is not sufficient evidence of recursive improvement (PDF46–48).** The manuscript requires a retained capability to improve the ability to identify, build, prove, or govern a later successor on fresh work. More descendant jobs, more sharing, higher traffic, or repeated use of an old workflow do not establish that result. Promotion is conditional on a preregistered positive lower bound and no deterioration in proof, rights, or governance. The stated monotonicity proposition is conditional on those gates; it does not guarantee a superior descendant exists. In this study, improved delivery economics, valuable recipient transfer, a new feasible job family, and improved invention/validation efficacy should be reported separately.

**The current system cannot certify or install its own successor (PDF46–48,75–77).** Formation can propose candidates and expose failure clusters. Protected cases, scorer internals, release credentials, the mission constitution, and admission control remain outside candidate control. Each material descendant must receive a new claim-bound assessment and scoped authority. The research module therefore binds Beta/policy versions, rejects stale probes, keeps terminal records terminal, and requires separate action authorization. The simulated independent agent attestation is an explicit research fixture; it is not a substitute for independent real-world custody or production review.

**Negative memory and positive retained capability have different roles (PDF47).** Rejected architectures, failed transfers, reward exploits, rights failures, and verifier weaknesses may reduce repeated mistakes. Their existence does not give them positive economic value automatically. The graph keeps failure memory in a separate record class with no positive-capability or action authority. A later use of that memory must still show fresh incremental benefit if it is claimed as Alpha.

**The evaluator and environment are assets that can also be wrong (PDF49–54).** The manuscript treats the Gym as a portable, governed asset with rights, partitions, versioning, custody, upkeep, expiry, and an economic ledger. It explicitly identifies the possibility that a candidate wins in the Gym and loses in reality. Hidden-state simplifications, omitted tails, unrealistic buyers/adversaries, semantic verifier correlation, and drift are not eliminated by a large number of simulation runs. The paper should describe its modeled lower bounds as conditional on the declared process, with separate sensitivity and later empirical validation. Cryptographic commitments can protect bindings; they cannot prove that the underlying mission or outcome measure is correct.

## The synthetic example is not calibration for this venture

The Aurelia example reports simulated formation, protected comparisons, a canary, failure, rollback, and reproof (PDF57–60). It explicitly identifies its economic quantities as generated from synthetic ground truth and states that customer mandates, rights-cleared data, independently calculated production value, and real operational admission are still needed. Its prices, costs, acceptance, or impressive gains must therefore not be copied into the current simulator as observed market or frontier-model evidence. Its reusable contribution is the separation of stages and the preservation of failed conditions.

## Implementation witness and limits

The current graph implements candidate/evidence/assessment/validation/admission transitions, current-scope discovery, explicit optional reuse, Beta and policy changes, fresh revalidation, prerequisite checks, canonical promotion restrictions, revocation, supersession, retirement, and separate failure memory. Local AES-256-GCM capsules and typed commitments are actual software operations. Identity and validator authentication use the pinned upstream fixture, with no live ENS or cryptographic identity claim. No ZK circuit, production key management, on-chain publication, or runtime security boundary was deployed.

The controls test known protected values and reserved scorer/case fields. They do not provide semantic secrecy against arbitrary undeclared encodings; independent evaluation custody is still required. Plaintext disclosed to an authorized recipient can be copied. The module can block future mediated access, not erase an existing copy.
