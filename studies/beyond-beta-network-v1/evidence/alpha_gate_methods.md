# Modeled Alpha evidence contract

The graph accepts a candidate only after the economic assessment and its separate authentication, version, dependency and authorization checks pass. Neither a model's self-description nor a correct output is by itself evidence of Alpha. The comparison is with the same current competent Beta configuration and the relevant already available capabilities. Human-service prices enter the commercial model separately.

`model/alpha_probe.mjs` budgets both sides of each fresh paired mission probe, a common held-out mechanical evaluation of each proposed output, and a final bounded capability check. The common mission evaluator uses12 counterexample cases per arm by default, with identical coverage and keyed test draws. It prevents a baseline's own false pass from being treated as superior to a safer candidate that detects a defect. Skipped checks after no proposal save actual expense, but both arms reserve the full test budget in advance. The final capability check has a96-case budget. The gate's effective independent sample is the number of fresh mission clusters, not the count of within-mission test cases.

The paired workflows use the same keyed task and quality uncertainty. Ordinary solvers, context, tools, critique, correction and baseline cheap checks remain available. A probe records common observed constraint-check acceptance, resource expense and contracted mission-value proxy. Mechanical defect truth generates the simulated test observations; semantic purpose is not proved by an executable check. A semantically wrong artifact can pass these observations, and a shared blindspot can affect both test arms. The simulation's latent truth is retained to measure false-pass risk; the gate does not consume it. Mission-check costs are one-time assessment expenses, not a claim that these additional held-out research tests run on every later operating job.

`model/alpha_gate.mjs` computes a one-sided95% Student-t lower bound over paired mission-cluster deltas. The probe budget is at most24, with at least6 independent clusters. If several recipients evaluate one mission cluster, their mean is one statistical observation. Duplicate probe identities, stale/nonfresh evidence, missing bindings or fewer clusters produce insufficient evidence. The bound is a small-sample approximation conditional on the model; it is not a distribution-free guarantee and cannot eliminate semantic bias shared by the producer and evaluator. A separate configured systematic-risk deduction may reduce the per-use bound, but any such deduction remains an explicit assumption.

The lifetime lower bound is forecast eligible uses times the lower per-use advantage, minus remaining incremental non-evaluation lifecycle costs, minus actual paired-evaluation cost. For an artifact learned while completing paid customer work, the original customer search and development cost (A) is already in the service ledger. It must not be charged a second time to retention. The incremental gate includes remaining extraction/generalization, integration, confirmation, distribution and upkeep (B), plus any separately chosen extra research (C). Consolidated enterprise profit still includes A, failed customer work and all B/C attempts. If there is no prior paid source job, a separate research campaign retains its complete C construction cost.

The caller's hurdle initially reserves the entire worst-case evaluation workload. The probe wrapper replaces that reservation by the actual expense in the final gate, deducting evaluation once. Rejection still pays all executed retention, optional research and test costs. A positive mean alone is insufficient. An exact-null capability, a redundant equivalent, a failed independent check, or a nonpositive complete lower bound cannot receive an admit verdict.

The report binds candidate ID/version/digest, Beta version, policy version, scope, environment, current comparison-universe digest, evaluation and expiration weeks, and prerequisite version pins. It includes the full paired-evidence digest and probe-identity digest. The graph separately checks current pins and eligible direct-label agent attestations. A report carries no retrieval, composition, admission or execution authority. Current graph growth invalidates a pending candidate's stale comparison when relevant; previously admitted records use explicit revalidation/supersession rules rather than a claim of permanent Alpha.

The main experiment must report both apparent admissions and latent false Alpha, plus later revalidation, copying, absorption and retirement. Gate outcomes are modeled evidence, not operationally validated frontier inventions. The fresh independent construction-campaign mechanism experiment remains separate from descendant service-job reuse and does not assert that network admission or distribution expenses have been paid by its local intervention.
