\textbf{AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL}

\textbf{TRANSFORMATIONAL INTELLIGENCE ∞ · SUCCESSOR Ω Harness}

Evidence date: \textbf{26 October 2026}. This companion explains what
the sources support, how assumptions enter the competent-Beta /
validated-Alpha network model, and which claims require future empirical
work. The executable simulator, frozen protocol, source-linked registers
and result ledgers are the authoritative quantitative artifacts.
Financial events, customer choices, capability discoveries and
attestations in those ledgers are simulated.

\hypertarget{1-the-fresh-charter}{%
\section{1. The fresh charter}\label{1-the-fresh-charter}}

The study asks whether customers can fund demanding useful work that
leaves capabilities worth retaining; whether those capabilities produce
additional net mission advantage beyond competent frontier intelligence
and ordinary tools; whether Alpha benefits other authenticated agents;
and how it changes as Beta advances. Profitable Beta delivery,
inexpensive retention, incremental Alpha, network benefit and improved
invention efficacy are separate outcomes. Shareable admission makes a
capability discoverable throughout the eligible network, while adoption
remains optional. No previous workload, fixed discount, staffed review,
required investment count or result is inherited.

The latest charter partitions costs into \textbf{A: paid-job work},
including the learning and development already needed to deliver;
\textbf{B: additional retention and reuse}; and \textbf{C: optional
separate research}. The retained capability is not charged again for
work already paid in A. Every activity still appears once in
consolidated enterprise economics. A useful paid outcome may leave no
rights-cleared transferable skill, or the skill\textquotesingle s
additional validation and upkeep may cost more than its future
advantage.

The primary offering is a bounded non-safety-critical scheduling,
batching or planning policy module. It includes a formal input contract,
a competent baseline, an original-constraint checker, held-out
performance evidence, a runtime manifest and a narrow adapter. The
customer-facing outcome is the supported executable asset. A business
plan, fluent proposal or model agreement is insufficient.

The buyer supplies a structured specification or authorized formal
simulator. Automated intake and clarification are charged; unresolved
semantics cause abstention or deferral. No manual onboarding or routine
human provider review is hidden in the primary cost model. Full
operational discovery, physical deployment and certification are outside
scope. A human reference covers the same bounded handoff, independent
testing and correction allowance.

The Gym defines a controlled versioned mission environment, including
original constraints, observations, actions, transitions, scenarios,
verification and receipts (PDF pp.18--21). Teaching and candidate
formation do not constitute protected fresh proof. Acceptance, admission
and authority are distinct decisions (pp.36--44, 72--77). The retained
institution includes purpose, tools, verifiers, evidence, memory,
operations and permissions, rather than only a particular acting model.
This supports preserving mission intelligence when a model, solver,
routing method or environment changes.

The source separates competent broadly available Beta, gross opportunity
and residual net Alpha after the complete relevant burden (pp.33--35).
It requires continuing requalification because imitation, drift and
advancing Beta can erase advantage. Repeated execution and prompt
changes are not sufficient evidence of recursion: later independent
successor formation or validation must improve, without weakening proof
requirements (pp.46--48). Failure memory is useful but distinct from
positive admitted capability.

The paper uses these distinctions to connect \textbf{Identify →
Out-Learn → Out-Think → Out-Design → Out-Strategise → Out-Execute →
Out-Evolve}. The sequence is a conceptual map from a buyer restriction
through paid experiments and formulation to a supported outcome,
optional retention and fresh evidence. It is not a calibrated multiplier
and does not guarantee that every job produces Alpha.

The source\textquotesingle s operational Specialist ASI and Successor Ω
designations require real fresh mission evidence and separate
accountable admission. Our fully automated research simulation does not
satisfy or claim those realized designations. The synthetic Aurelia
example (pp.56--60), its earlier rejection and transfer gains, and
illustrative enterprise price bands (pp.65--66) are expressly excluded
from capability, human-price and willingness-to-pay calibration. The
institutional physics language is a design analogy. The
source\textquotesingle s empirical completion gate describes later
real-world validation; it does not prevent a clearly labeled modeled
edition.

\hypertarget{5-buyer-value-recurrence-and-pricing}{%
\section{5. Buyer value, recurrence and
pricing}\label{5-buyer-value-recurrence-and-pricing}}

Buyer value is not the human provider\textquotesingle s expense and is
not the AI producer\textquotesingle s cost. The customer can choose the
matched human team, continue with existing software, postpone or
decline. Integration, trust, delay, residual error, repeat use and
finite capacity can outweigh a lower offered price. Price ratios and
scarcity responses are policy choices evaluated within a declared
catalogue, not a fixed discount inherited from another study.

The buyer separates completion probability \texttt{s} from conditional
correctness \texttt{q}. The expected useful value is
\texttt{s\ ×\ q\ ×\ V}; expected contingent payment is \texttt{s\ ×\ P}.
Integration, trust and delay are added separately. Additional failure
harm is \texttt{(1\ −\ s\ ×\ q)\ ×\ D}, where \texttt{D} excludes the
lost useful value already absent from the first term. Initial completion
beliefs, conditional correctness beliefs and their update rules are
assumed. Completion updates include all finished attempts, including
abstention and no improvement; conditional correctness updates use
observable checks and later failure reports rather than hidden simulator
truth.

The
\href{https://www.hexaly.com/customers/how-bluefaces-solves-complex-project-scheduling-problems-in-minutes-with-hexaly}{Hexaly
Blue Faces case} is vendor-authored qualitative evidence of recurring
scheduling needs, sophisticated constraints and support burden. It does
not report an applicable price or willingness-to-pay estimate. The
\href{https://www.informs.org/Recognizing-Excellence/Award-Recipients/UPS-2016-Edelman}{INFORMS
ORION citation} shows a major integrated optimization deployment, whose
value and implementation burden should not be scaled to a small module
handoff.

The \href{https://aws.amazon.com/fargate/pricing/}{AWS Fargate price
example} supplies a resource-price unit, not customer demand. Its
Linux/x86 US East CPU rate is \$0.0404784/vCPU-hour. Ten million
\emph{assumed reducible billed} hours and a five-percent \emph{assumed}
reduction imply \$20,239.20 gross potential annual CPU value before
integration, operation and realization costs. A reduced makespan that
leaves resource billing unchanged produces none of that saving. Memory,
commitments, discounts, deadlines and unused capacity are separate
factors.

The AI booking window is its offered service/resource limit. The human
alternative has a separately assumed four-week lead time, accounted for
in buyer utility; it is not disqualified by the AI provider's tighter
window. Newly serviceable work means feasible for this AI enterprise
under its declared budget, not impossible for humans.

A new need can arise from new supported constraints, workload drift, a
new resource budget or an adaptation campaign. The frequency and buyer
pool remain hypotheses. Thousands of descendant schedule instances are
not thousands of independent invention campaigns or consultancy sales.
An invention that removes a recurring task can reduce demand for the old
task while creating a different asset-maintenance opportunity; the model
must account for both effects rather than assume permanent revenue.

\hypertarget{8-competent-beta-net-alpha-and-recipient-evidence}{%
\section{8. Competent Beta, net Alpha and recipient
evidence}\label{8-competent-beta-net-alpha-and-recipient-evidence}}

Beta includes appropriate current frontier models, ordinary tools,
established optimization solvers, adequate context and decomposition,
critique, correction, and each agent\textquotesingle s already-available
methods. All substantive producer, critic, evaluator, builder and
investment roles receive the same expert model configuration. A
saved-file difference does not establish a capability difference. A
recipient can reconstruct an available method; that work is timed and
costed.

The finite candidate catalogue contains a typed constraint-lifting
representation, an original-domain certificate carrier, and a
family-specific counterexample experiment protocol. Representation and
certificate can be complementary. A competent baseline may ignore an
unhelpful isolated component, so it is not artificially penalized for
lacking a bundle. The resource, validation and transfer effects are
assumptions, not reported Astra performance. This supplied catalogue is
not evidence of discovering arbitrary new algorithm classes or
open-ended self-improvement.

A demanding supported job may supply a candidate\textquotesingle s
modeled origin and bounded evidence. Emergence and sharing rights are
uncertain. Retention selection uses observable accepted and collected
status rather than hidden truth, so an undetected false pass can enter
the candidate pool and face fresh checks. The lifecycle distinguishes
candidate, assessed Alpha, admitted shareable Alpha, rejected,
superseded, revoked and retired states. Net Alpha means additional
mission advantage after remaining incremental retention, paid paired
evaluation, integration, operation, risk and upkeep, relative to
versioned Beta and the recipient\textquotesingle s available
capabilities. Development already charged to the paid job is not charged
twice. Validity, novelty, identity, a graph node or an encrypted file is
insufficient.

The Alpha gate evaluates fresh paired modeled missions and deducts
remaining incremental lifecycle cost, including the paid assessment
itself. Already charged A development stays in the job ledger and is not
a second admission hurdle. Its one-sided Student-t lower bound is a
decision heuristic conditional on the supplied stochastic model. A small
cluster count, skewed payoffs or shared semantic bias can defeat an
apparent confidence claim. It is not an empirical 95\% guarantee about
frontier intelligence or actual buyer value. False admission, false
rejection, costly revalidation and retirement remain possible.

After shareable admission, all eligible authenticated network members
can discover the capability, scope, version, prerequisites and status.
Availability is mandatory in the primary network. Adoption is optional:
an equivalent method, irrelevant workload, failed adaptation or high
integration cost can make abstention the correct decision. A local-only
arm is a counterfactual for causal isolation, not a
selective-distribution policy for the primary network. Agent count and
aggregate compute are held fixed.

Recipient evidence uses fresh disjoint work and explicitly compares
competent reconstruction or already-available capabilities. Shared
retention or optional research is charged once to the network;
retrieval/adaptation and revalidation add cost when incurred. Internal
payments redistribute resources but cannot create consolidated revenue.
Human-service price savings establish neither Alpha nor network gain.
Protected cases and scorer internals are excluded from capsules; failure
memory is not promoted as positive Alpha.

Beta is versioned. A hypothetical future improvement in ordinary models,
solvers or general tooling can absorb existing Alpha. Baseline-change
revalidation may preserve an integration or reliability advantage,
require fresh evidence, narrow the scope, supersede or retire the
capability. Stale history cannot reactivate retired influence. The
future path is a sensitivity assumption, not an announced model release
or a claim of permanent scarcity.

A cheap experiment screen can reject expensive bad candidates and also
miss useful ones. Competent Beta already has a substantial screen. The
additional protocol never removes the independent final check. Common
semantic errors can survive model critique, retries and apparently
separate roles. Simulated independent-check fields describe the model;
no newly invented algorithm is actually benchmarked here.

Independent invention campaigns include failed candidates, final
confirmation, integration and the retained experiment
protocol\textquotesingle s cost. More descendant jobs measure reuse.
Improved construction/validation efficacy requires a fresh campaign
endpoint, equal budgets, full amortization, capacity limits and an exact
zero-increment control. It is reported separately from profit, graph
growth and network dissemination.

The completed optional campaign experiment has a preserved post-freeze
endpoint limitation: six non-inventive fallback outputs per arm received
an unnecessary final review, and five passed that local review. The
reported 21 local false passes per arm therefore comprise 16 invalid
actual method proposals and five fallback outputs; none is a graph
admission. The matched unnecessary review charge was \$81.35 per arm
across 256 campaigns. Removing it diagnostically leaves every paired
saving, the 131/132 useful-outcome counts and the 1,056-campaign
resource break-even unchanged. The frozen totals remain the registered
results. See \nolinkurl{results/analysis/meta_endpoint_caveat.md} and
\texttt{meta\_readout.json}; this is an optional-runner classification
defect, not a frontier-model limitation.

\hypertarget{9-finite-cash-and-complete-costs}{%
\section{9. Finite cash and complete
costs}\label{9-finite-cash-and-complete-costs}}

The ledger separates initial seed, offered prices, customer savings,
earned charges, net collections, payment timing, refunds, resource
costs, ongoing operation, historical research allowance, protected
obligations and available cash. The \$5,000 historical-development
allowance is assumed, not a measurement of actual past research
expenditure. Profit is fully allocated but pre-tax; tax reserves
constrain available cash and are not a final tax-expense calculation.
Recovery and refunds are charged within the bounded prototype/handoff
terms; consequential-damage indemnity is not estimated. Token utility,
internal transfers and assumed appreciation cannot manufacture operating
earnings.

Consolidated expense is A+B+C+common business costs, with each activity
billed once. A paid job can leave a low-cost reusable opportunity, but
the retention workload, candidate rate, rights clearance and future
reuse remain uncertain assumptions. A profitable A does not guarantee
profitable B; a valuable B does not erase an unprofitable A. Historical
development allowances remain in the fully allocated account.

Work, retention and optional research must be reserved before execution.
Uncollected future revenue cannot fund current B or C. Full allocation
includes all paid search, failed construction, fresh confirmation,
migration, integration, upkeep and recovery in the appropriate ledger.
Refund principal is treated consistently with net collections rather
than duplicated as a second resource expense. A positive expected margin
does not guarantee that cash survives a long collection delay.

Prospective retention or research considers future incremental benefit
against only remaining B/C costs; historical and A costs remain in their
original fully allocated accounts. Operate, hold, wait, retain a bundle,
research separately, adapt and abandon are legitimate actions. There is
no prescribed count of productive investments and no reinvestment
percentage. The controller receives declared priors and observable
history, not hidden future quality draws.

Policy optimality is bounded by the declared catalogue. All searched
enterprise policies enter and incur setup and the historical allowance;
a prelaunch no-entry choice was not optimized. A loss-making selected
entry policy does not justify launch. The no-separate-research
diagnostic is not an independently optimized retention-only controller.

Break-even expressions hold case mix and conversion/cost ratios fixed.
They are conditional thresholds, not substitutes for rerunning demand,
congestion and failure dynamics. Buyer price ceilings can be empty once
full cost, quality, integration and delay are considered.

\section{Français --- portée des

Les tarifs publiés et les résultats de recherche externes ancrent les
coûts et les mécanismes possibles. Ils ne mesurent ni la demande de
cette offre, ni le taux de réussite d'Astra sur ce périmètre. Les heures

transfert et la durée utile des capacités restent des hypothèses
explicites.

Bêta désigne les modèles actuels utilisés avec compétence, les outils
ordinaires, les solveurs solides et les méthodes déjà disponibles. Le
travail client A paie déjà la formulation, l'apprentissage, la recherche
et les tests nécessaires à la livraison. B couvre seulement la
conservation et la réutilisation supplémentaires ; C désigne une
recherche distincte et facultative. Aucun coût n'est facturé deux fois.
L'Alpha exige un avantage futur net au-delà de Bêta après les dépenses
supplémentaires encore nécessaires, tandis que la rentabilité de
l'entreprise conserve l'ensemble A+B+C.

Une admission partageable rend la capacité découvrable par tous les
membres authentifiés admissibles ; son utilisation reste facultative.
Quand Bêta progresse, l'avantage peut disparaître et la capacité être
retirée. Un candidat, un graphe ou une preuve cryptographique ne
démontre pas une valeur économique. Les exemples synthétiques du
manuscrit Gym et ses fourchettes commerciales illustratives ne servent
pas à calibrer cette étude.

La simulation exécute un réseau à trésorerie et capacité finies. Ses
achats, encaissements, inventions, attestations et investissements sont
modélisés. Aucune vente réelle, invention autonome effective ou
amélioration générale de l'intelligence n'est revendiquée. Réutiliser un
solveur et mieux réussir de nouvelles campagnes d'invention sont deux
résultats distincts. Attendre, conserver les fonds ou abandonner une
construction peut être la bonne décision économique.
