## 1. The fresh charter

The study asks whether customers can fund demanding useful work that leaves capabilities worth retaining; whether those capabilities produce additional net mission advantage beyond competent frontier intelligence and ordinary tools; whether Alpha benefits other authenticated agents; and how it changes as Beta advances. Profitable Beta delivery, inexpensive retention, incremental Alpha, network benefit and improved invention efficacy are separate outcomes. Shareable admission makes a capability discoverable throughout the eligible network, while adoption remains optional. No previous workload, fixed discount, staffed review, required investment count or result is inherited.

The latest charter partitions costs into **A: paid-job work**, including the learning and development already needed to deliver; **B: additional retention and reuse**; and **C: optional separate research**. The retained capability is not charged again for work already paid in A. Every activity still appears once in consolidated enterprise economics. A useful paid outcome may leave no rights-cleared transferable skill, or the skill's additional validation and upkeep may cost more than its future advantage.

The primary offering is a bounded non-safety-critical scheduling, batching or planning policy module. It includes a formal input contract, a competent baseline, an original-constraint checker, held-out performance evidence, a runtime manifest and a narrow adapter. The customer-facing outcome is the supported executable asset. A business plan, fluent proposal or model agreement is insufficient.

The buyer supplies a structured specification or authorized formal simulator. Automated intake and clarification are charged; unresolved semantics cause abstention or deferral. No manual onboarding or routine human provider review is hidden in the primary cost model. Full operational discovery, physical deployment and certification are outside scope. A human reference covers the same bounded handoff, independent testing and correction allowance.

## 5. Buyer value, recurrence and pricing

Buyer value is not the human provider's expense and is not the AI producer's cost. The customer can choose the matched human team, continue with existing software, postpone or decline. Integration, trust, delay, residual error, repeat use and finite capacity can outweigh a lower offered price. Price ratios and scarcity responses are policy choices evaluated within a declared catalogue, not a fixed discount inherited from another study.

The buyer separates completion probability `s` from conditional correctness `q`. The expected useful value is `s × q × V`; expected contingent payment is `s × P`. Integration, trust and delay are added separately. Additional failure harm is `(1 − s × q) × D`, where `D` excludes the lost useful value already absent from the first term. Initial completion beliefs, conditional correctness beliefs and their update rules are assumed. Completion updates include all finished attempts, including abstention and no improvement; conditional correctness updates use observable checks and later failure reports rather than hidden simulator truth.

The [Hexaly Blue Faces case](https://www.hexaly.com/customers/how-bluefaces-solves-complex-project-scheduling-problems-in-minutes-with-hexaly) is vendor-authored qualitative evidence of recurring scheduling needs, sophisticated constraints and support burden. It does not report an applicable price or willingness-to-pay estimate. The [INFORMS ORION citation](https://www.informs.org/Recognizing-Excellence/Award-Recipients/UPS-2016-Edelman) shows a major integrated optimization deployment, whose value and implementation burden should not be scaled to a small module handoff.

The [AWS Fargate price example](https://aws.amazon.com/fargate/pricing/) supplies a resource-price unit, not customer demand. Its Linux/x86 US East CPU rate is $0.0404784/vCPU-hour. Ten million *assumed reducible billed* hours and a five-percent *assumed* reduction imply $20,239.20 gross potential annual CPU value before integration, operation and realization costs. A reduced makespan that leaves resource billing unchanged produces none of that saving. Memory, commitments, discounts, deadlines and unused capacity are separate factors.

The AI booking window is its offered service/resource limit. The human alternative has a separately assumed four-week lead time, accounted for in buyer utility; it is not disqualified by the AI provider’s tighter window. Newly serviceable work means feasible for this AI enterprise under its declared budget, not impossible for humans.

A new need can arise from new supported constraints, workload drift, a new resource budget or an adaptation campaign. The frequency and buyer pool remain hypotheses. Thousands of descendant schedule instances are not thousands of independent invention campaigns or consultancy sales. An invention that removes a recurring task can reduce demand for the old task while creating a different asset-maintenance opportunity; the model must account for both effects rather than assume permanent revenue.

## 8. Competent Beta, net Alpha and recipient evidence

Beta includes appropriate current frontier models, ordinary tools, established optimization solvers, adequate context and decomposition, critique, correction, and each agent's already-available methods. All substantive producer, critic, evaluator, builder and investment roles receive the same expert model configuration. A saved-file difference does not establish a capability difference. A recipient can reconstruct an available method; that work is timed and costed.

The finite candidate catalogue contains a typed constraint-lifting representation, an original-domain certificate carrier, and a family-specific counterexample experiment protocol. Representation and certificate can be complementary. A competent baseline may ignore an unhelpful isolated component, so it is not artificially penalized for lacking a bundle. The resource, validation and transfer effects are assumptions, not reported Astra performance. This supplied catalogue is not evidence of discovering arbitrary new algorithm classes or open-ended self-improvement.

A demanding supported job may supply a candidate's modeled origin and bounded evidence. Emergence and sharing rights are uncertain. Retention selection uses observable accepted and collected status rather than hidden truth, so an undetected false pass can enter the candidate pool and face fresh checks. The lifecycle distinguishes candidate, assessed Alpha, admitted shareable Alpha, rejected, superseded, revoked and retired states. Net Alpha means additional mission advantage after remaining incremental retention, paid paired evaluation, integration, operation, risk and upkeep, relative to versioned Beta and the recipient's available capabilities. Development already charged to the paid job is not charged twice. Validity, novelty, identity, a graph node or an encrypted file is insufficient.

The Alpha gate evaluates fresh paired modeled missions and deducts remaining incremental lifecycle cost, including the paid assessment itself. Already charged A development stays in the job ledger and is not a second admission hurdle. Its one-sided Student-t lower bound is a decision heuristic conditional on the supplied stochastic model. A small cluster count, skewed payoffs or shared semantic bias can defeat an apparent confidence claim. It is not an empirical 95% guarantee about frontier intelligence or actual buyer value. False admission, false rejection, costly revalidation and retirement remain possible.

After shareable admission, all eligible authenticated network members can discover the capability, scope, version, prerequisites and status. Availability is mandatory in the primary network. Adoption is optional: an equivalent method, irrelevant workload, failed adaptation or high integration cost can make abstention the correct decision. A local-only arm is a counterfactual for causal isolation, not a selective-distribution policy for the primary network. Agent count and aggregate compute are held fixed.

Recipient evidence uses fresh disjoint work and explicitly compares competent reconstruction or already-available capabilities. Shared retention or optional research is charged once to the network; retrieval/adaptation and revalidation add cost when incurred. Internal payments redistribute resources but cannot create consolidated revenue. Human-service price savings establish neither Alpha nor network gain. Protected cases and scorer internals are excluded from capsules; failure memory is not promoted as positive Alpha.

Beta is versioned. A hypothetical future improvement in ordinary models, solvers or general tooling can absorb existing Alpha. Baseline-change revalidation may preserve an integration or reliability advantage, require fresh evidence, narrow the scope, supersede or retire the capability. Stale history cannot reactivate retired influence. The future path is a sensitivity assumption, not an announced model release or a claim of permanent scarcity.

A cheap experiment screen can reject expensive bad candidates and also miss useful ones. Competent Beta already has a substantial screen. The additional protocol never removes the independent final check. Common semantic errors can survive model critique, retries and apparently separate roles. Simulated independent-check fields describe the model; no newly invented algorithm is actually benchmarked here.

Independent invention campaigns include failed candidates, final confirmation, integration and the retained experiment protocol's cost. More descendant jobs measure reuse. Improved construction/validation efficacy requires a fresh campaign endpoint, equal budgets, full amortization, capacity limits and an exact zero-increment control. It is reported separately from profit, graph growth and network dissemination.

The completed optional campaign experiment has a preserved post-freeze endpoint limitation: six non-inventive fallback outputs per arm received an unnecessary final review, and five passed that local review. The reported 21 local false passes per arm therefore comprise 16 invalid actual method proposals and five fallback outputs; none is a graph admission. The matched unnecessary review charge was $81.35 per arm across 256 campaigns. Removing it diagnostically leaves every paired saving, the 131/132 useful-outcome counts and the 1,056-campaign resource break-even unchanged. The frozen totals remain the registered results. ## 9. Finite cash and complete costs

The ledger separates initial seed, offered prices, customer savings, earned charges, net collections, payment timing, refunds, resource costs, ongoing operation, historical research allowance, protected obligations and available cash. The $5,000 historical-development allowance is assumed, not a measurement of actual past research expenditure. Profit is fully allocated but pre-tax; tax reserves constrain available cash and are not a final tax-expense calculation. Recovery and refunds are charged within the bounded prototype/handoff terms; consequential-damage indemnity is not estimated. Token utility, internal transfers and assumed appreciation cannot manufacture operating earnings.

| Ledger | What is charged | What is not charged again |
|---|---|---|
| **A — paid-job work** | Formulation, information acquisition, learning, candidate construction, failed search, tests, correction, compute, delivery, support and recovery for the customer outcome | The cost classifier partitions existing job charges and adds zero expense |
| **B — incremental retention and reuse** | Additional extraction, sanitization and rights work, generalization, fresh net-Alpha assessment, packaging, integration, distribution, transfer, upkeep and revalidation | Development and testing already charged in A |
| **C — optional separate research** | Separately chosen beyond-scope search and construction, including unsuccessful attempts; downstream admission and reuse use B, so a complete separate project justifies B+C | No compulsory C charge is imposed on every retained paid-work candidate |

Consolidated expense is A+B+C+common business costs, with each activity billed once. A paid job can leave a low-cost reusable opportunity, but the retention workload, candidate rate, rights clearance and future reuse remain uncertain assumptions. A profitable A does not guarantee profitable B; a valuable B does not erase an unprofitable A. Historical development allowances remain in the fully allocated account.

Work, retention and optional research must be reserved before execution. Uncollected future revenue cannot fund current B or C. Full allocation includes all paid search, failed construction, fresh confirmation, migration, integration, upkeep and recovery in the appropriate ledger. Refund principal is treated consistently with net collections rather than duplicated as a second resource expense. A positive expected margin does not guarantee that cash survives a long collection delay.

Prospective retention or research considers future incremental benefit against only remaining B/C costs; historical and A costs remain in their original fully allocated accounts. Operate, hold, wait, retain a bundle, research separately, adapt and abandon are legitimate actions. There is no prescribed count of productive investments and no reinvestment percentage. The controller receives declared priors and observable history, not hidden future quality draws.

Policy optimality is bounded by the declared catalogue. All searched enterprise policies enter and incur setup and the historical allowance; a prelaunch no-entry choice was not optimized. A loss-making selected entry policy does not justify launch. The no-separate-research diagnostic is not an independently optimized retention-only controller.

Break-even expressions hold case mix and conversion/cost ratios fixed. They are conditional thresholds, not substitutes for rerunning demand, congestion and failure dynamics. Buyer price ceilings can be empty once full cost, quality, integration and delay are considered.

Bêta désigne les modèles actuels utilisés avec compétence, les outils ordinaires, les solveurs solides et les méthodes déjà disponibles. Le travail client A paie déjà la formulation, l’apprentissage, la recherche et les tests nécessaires à la livraison. B couvre seulement la conservation et la réutilisation supplémentaires ; C désigne une recherche distincte et facultative. Aucun coût n’est facturé deux fois. L’Alpha exige un avantage futur net au-delà de Bêta après les dépenses supplémentaires encore nécessaires, tandis que la rentabilité de l’entreprise conserve l’ensemble A+B+C.

Une admission partageable rend la capacité découvrable par tous les membres authentifiés admissibles ; son utilisation reste facultative. Quand Bêta progresse, l’avantage peut disparaître et la capacité être retirée. Un candidat, un graphe ou une preuve cryptographique ne démontre pas une valeur économique. Les exemples synthétiques du manuscrit Gym et ses fourchettes commerciales illustratives ne servent pas à calibrer cette étude.

La simulation exécute un réseau à trésorerie et capacité finies. Ses achats, encaissements, inventions, attestations et investissements sont modélisés. Aucune vente réelle, invention autonome effective ou amélioration générale de l’intelligence n’est revendiquée. Réutiliser un solveur et mieux réussir de nouvelles campagnes d’invention sont deux résultats distincts. Attendre, conserver les fonds ou abandonner une construction peut être la bonne décision économique.
