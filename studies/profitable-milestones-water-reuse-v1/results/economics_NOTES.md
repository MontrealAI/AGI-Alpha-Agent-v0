## The offering and price comparison

The modeled customer buys one bounded source/cost reconciliation, evidence-gap register, executable decision instrument, sampling-plan specification, independent checking, packaged handoff and one correction round. The price does not buy a treatment plant, verified process-water suitability or a promised breakthrough. An informative negative gate is payable only if it satisfies the agreed digital scope; an unaccepted/undelivered report earns no fee and its deposit is refunded.

A same-scope professional estimate is 32 hours × USD125/hour × 1.30 overhead = USD5,200. This is a transparent resource estimate, not a retrieved quotation, recruited-human measurement, buyer reservation price or scientific proof that AI is cheaper. The cost range from 24–48 hours and USD100–175/hour is explicitly uncertain. Price selection compares USD1,500, USD2,500 and USD3,500 without imposing a fixed discount. Conditional buyer reservation distributions, a trust/booking factor and the human outside option constrain uptake. Switching cost is USD300.

The development risk-neutral weights are .25/.50/.25 for conservative/central/favorable scenarios, not observed scenario frequencies. Weighted analytical full-project estimates are −3,968.29, +18,933.32 and +25,202.44 for prices 1,500, 2,500 and 3,500 respectively. These calculations use expected capacity and the full planned acquisition load. Confirmation executes individual arrivals, occupied slots, delayed settlement and affordability stops, so realized simulation means differ from the development approximation.

Important failure: USD3,500 exceeds the conservative reservation maximum of USD3,400. That scenario produces no bookings. The frozen launch continues acquisition until its risk headroom is consumed, while honoring committed overhead. It does not adapt price or infer a Bayesian demand posterior. This is a preserved limitation, not a robust launch recommendation. An earlier stop or revised pricing strategy would need a separate prospective test. Do not remove the loss or retrofit a successful price to confirmation.

## Probabilities, dependence and capacity

First-pass artifact delivery, correction rescue, acceptance conditional on delivered artifact, collection conditional on acceptance and buyer adoption conditional on acceptance are distinct variables. A common campaign shock reduces demand and quality and raises cost for every job in the affected world. Conditional draws are independent given that shared state; unconditional jobs are correlated. These are transparent stress hypotheses rather than calibrated frequencies.

Delivery probability includes successful correction. Negative process feasibility is not an artifact failure. The future physical-viability prior is a separate variable in the customer account.

Two in-flight slots are available. A first-pass report occupies its slot for one week; a first-pass failure occupies it for two while correction is attempted. Intake refuses excess work; corrections cannot disappear from occupancy. The optional module would consume one slot during its two-week build. Lost capacity is charged in its expected incremental value, and financial costs are charged once. Forty staff hours is not asserted as a measured operational capacity: slots are the explicit modeled capacity envelope.
