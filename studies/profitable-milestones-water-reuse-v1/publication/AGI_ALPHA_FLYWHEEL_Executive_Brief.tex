\hypertarget{a-useful-milestone-has-been-delivered-profitability-remains-conditional}{%
\subsection{A useful milestone has been delivered; profitability remains
conditional}\label{a-useful-milestone-has-been-delivered-profitability-remains-conditional}}

The \textbf{Reuse Investment Gate Pack} is an executable digital
offering for an industrial plant owner, reuse developer or adviser
deciding whether to fund the next water-reuse study. It supplies
source-linked inputs, corrected cash economics, conditional quality
checks, explicit stop/defer reasons and a next-measurement plan. A
negative opportunity can be a useful accepted deliverable if that
question and scope were agreed in advance.

\hypertarget{the-delivered-result}{%
\subsubsection{The delivered result}\label{the-delivered-result}}

A published process-water case states less than one year's payback. Its
own cost table instead implies \textbf{3.37 years}, before omitted
costs: EUR82,000 stated annual savings less EUR28,630 cash operating
expense leaves EUR53,370 against EUR180,000 capital. At an assumed 12\%
discount rate over ten years, the partial-input NPV is EUR121,552.

The pack returns \textbf{DEFER}. Discharge limits are not a buyer's
process-water specification, and long-run performance, complete costs
and six other evidence domains are unresolved. This is an actionable
digital screening result, not a new treatment technology, physical
validation or plant authorization. Source: Marjana Simonič,
\emph{Membranes} 11(12), 976 (2021), DOI 10.3390/membranes11120976.

\hypertarget{commercial-scope-and-modeled-economics}{%
\subsubsection{Commercial scope and modeled
economics}\label{commercial-scope-and-modeled-economics}}

The selected offer is \textbf{US\$3,500}, against a transparent
equivalent-scope human estimate of US\$5,200. The modeled contract
includes a reserved 30\% deposit, acceptance of the agreed work, one
bounded correction and refund on failed final acceptance. No customer
was contacted and no contract or payment occurred.

The model covers 26 selling weeks plus seven settlement weeks and
requires \textbf{US\$10,000 external seed}. It includes unsuccessful
work, correction, acquisition, support, bad debt, setup and reserved
obligations. These primary figures are an explicit accounting-correction
replay: initial frozen runs omitted settlement overhead. Their original
central result was US\$6,477; reserving the added expense also restricts
intake, reducing the corrected mean to US\$2,962. The price and original
seeds were not retuned.

\textbf{The campaign is not robust across demand assumptions.} The
conservative price attracts zero orders and acquisition consumes the
risk budget. The central case loses money in 40.6\% of modeled
histories. Buyer-price interpretation, acquisition, acceptance and
residual effort can reverse the result. Actual collections are
\textbf{US\$0}; complete actual cost and profit are \textbf{unknown}.

\subsection{What comes next---and what the evidence does not
justify}\label{what-comes-nextand-what-the-evidence-does-not-justify}}

\hypertarget{three-separate-accounts}{%
\subsubsection{Three separate accounts}\label{three-separate-accounts}}

Provider profit comes from the specified service fee less its full
costs. Customer value comes from usable output or an improved decision,
net of price and adoption burden. Future plant or venture upside belongs
to whoever later owns the relevant rights and bears its risks. These are
not additive earnings.

A separate \textbf{hypothetical} empirical test has EUR30,621 gross
information value and EUR22,348 net value under a 0.50 viability prior,
0.80 sensitivity/specificity, US\$8,000 test fee, fixed scenario
conversion of 1.10 USD/EUR and EUR1,000 delay burden. It changes a
modeled stop decision after a positive signal. Those probabilities are
uncalibrated planning assumptions. This value is not attributed to the
delivered desk pack, and the physical project remains deferred.

\hypertarget{finance-and-selective-continuation}{%
\subsubsection{Finance and selective
continuation}\label{finance-and-selective-continuation}}

The corrected central first-job funding threshold is \textbf{US\$8,945},
or \textbf{US\$9,295 under the shared shock}, including committed
overhead, reserves and one acquired lead that books. It does not
guarantee a first sale. Zero-seed operation refuses.

All optional US\$1,200/US\$2,500 reusable-module investments remain
\textbf{STOP} after construction, maintenance, failure, scope fit and
lost delivery capacity. Available money alone is insufficient. The study
demonstrates neither retained Alpha nor compounding.

\hypertarget{technical-evidence-and-recovery}{%
\subsubsection{Technical evidence and
recovery}\label{technical-evidence-and-recovery}}

Ordinary and expert implementations each satisfy 62 of 64 original
synthetic confirmation cases. One discrepancy violates an exact
negative-NPV rule; another reveals decimal/binary interpretation
ambiguity. The conventional evaluator matches the declared cases but
also supplies the numerical oracle; its self-match is not independent
validation. The final wrapper uses it, preserves both failures and
passes 14 post-confirmation diagnostics with separate exact-arithmetic
checks. No expert-model advantage or improved fresh-confirmation rate is
claimed.

The corrected economics ledger contains 185,135 event rows across 768
histories. An independent Decimal implementation checks all cash,
accrual and reserve identities. Local native accounting keeps unknown
actual costs unknown; existing FusionPlan verification is exercised. ENS
is unverified; MARK is not executed; no live token or payment
transaction occurs.

\hypertarget{scale-is-a-defined-scenario}{%
\subsubsection{Scale is a defined
scenario}\label{scale-is-a-defined-scenario}}

6,000 qualified projects at the historical EUR180,000 case capital would
represent \textbf{EUR1.08 billion cumulative plant deployment by 2040}.
Eligibility, current costs and adoption are unverified. This is neither
provider revenue nor valuation. At US\$3,500 per pack, the same 6,000
orders would produce US\$21 million gross service receipts before costs
and competition.

\hypertarget{decision}{%
\subsubsection{Decision}\label{decision}}

The completed package supports a \textbf{separately authorized, bounded
buyer pilot} of the screening service, with measured input effort,
acceptance, corrections, bills and collection. It does not justify
automatically funding the full modeled campaign, buying the hypothetical
field experiment or committing plant capital. Useful progress can
precede the larger answer; continuation must earn its own evidence and
funding.

The research package includes the full paper, source, executable study,
frozen inputs, original and corrected ledgers, readable delivery pack,
failure records and an unposted LinkedIn draft. Distribution is a pinned
review edition; canonical publication follows existing-owner
qualification.
