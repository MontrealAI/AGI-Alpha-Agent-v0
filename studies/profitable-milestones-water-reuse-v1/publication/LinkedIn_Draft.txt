🌌♾️ AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — When Capability Creates Opportunity

What if the first commercial product on the way to a major opportunity were useful progress?

A customer does not need to buy a guaranteed breakthrough. It can buy a specified result that makes the next decision better.

Our new research edition, THE PAID-PROGRESS OPTION, puts that strategy to an executable test.

We built a Reuse Investment Gate Pack for industrial water reuse: source-linked inputs, complete conditional economics, explicit evidence gaps and a decision about the next engineering milestone.

The public case gave a consequential answer. Its tabulated costs imply about 3.37 years' simple payback, before omitted costs, despite a published claim of less than one year. Its discharge limits do not establish the buyer's process-water requirements. The pack therefore returns DEFER and identifies the missing evidence.

That can be useful work—if it is the work the customer agreed to purchase.

The commercial study is deliberately separate. At a hypothetical US$3,500 fee, our corrected model produces mean project outcomes of −US$9,000, +US$2,962 and +US$77,279 across conservative, central and favorable scenarios. It requires US$10,000 seed capital. The central scenario loses money in 40.6% of histories. These are modeled outcomes, not earned revenue or proven demand.

We preserved two numerical-boundary failures and corrected missing settlement overhead. We also rejected every optional retained-capability investment whose complete cost was not justified. No model superiority or automatic compounding is claimed.

The ambition remains large: useful milestones that help people decide which new productive possibilities deserve construction. The discipline is equally important: keep provider profit, customer value and uncertain venture upside separate.

Useful work can earn its own fee. Its evidence can justify the next step—or show why stopping is better.
