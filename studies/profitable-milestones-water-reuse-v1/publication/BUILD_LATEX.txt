AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — The Paid-Progress Option
Standalone LaTeX build

The source archive preserves publication/ and its neighboring figures/ directory.
Requires XeLaTeX, DejaVu Serif/Sans/Sans Mono, and the normal packages referenced
in the preamble (including fontspec, titlesec, fancyhdr, needspace and tocloft).

From the extracted root:
  cd publication
  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error AGI_ALPHA_FLYWHEEL_Paid_Progress.tex
  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error AGI_ALPHA_FLYWHEEL_Paid_Progress.tex
  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error AGI_ALPHA_FLYWHEEL_Executive_Brief.tex
  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error AGI_ALPHA_FLYWHEEL_Executive_Brief.tex

references.bib is included. The generated standalone TeX contains the rendered
bibliography and does not require a separate BibTeX step. Markdown originals,
styles and exact figure sources are also included. The complete study archive
contains the Python figure/publication builder and numerical evidence.

The published output was compiled twice and all 20 paper pages and both brief
pages were rendered and visually checked. Binary PDF hashes can change with
build timestamps, TeX versions and fonts without changing the numerical study.

This is a research review edition, not a claim of canonical Pages deployment.
