## 1. Transformational Intelligence

**Citation:** Vincent Boucher. *Transformational Intelligence: Learning to Change the Game.* MONTREAL.AI, Montréal, Québec, Canada. October 2026. Research manuscript.

Core definition: an authorized purpose, an inherited problem formulation and an evaluation instrument are different objects. A useful structural change alters representations, information, dependencies, production, task boundary, coordination or evaluation while preserving the intended outcome and respecting authority. Transformation does not require scientific novelty or a larger search space. Its useful value must be checked against a competent unrestricted alternative.

The exact example compares passive causal observations (50% ceiling under its supplied model) with active intervention (8191/8192 accuracy in at most 12 queries). An unrestricted optimal planner matches the active strategy. This is a formulation-dependent result, not an LLM performance result or a new causal-discovery algorithm.

The practical discovery object is the pair (construction, discriminating experiment). The system should diagnose a specific restriction and design the cheapest permitted informative test; merely increasing model confidence is insufficient. Complementary changes may warrant bounded exploratory expenditure even if each is unattractive alone. Production commitment remains a separate decision.

Useful lifecycle equation:
C_life = C_search + C_build + C_confirm + C_migrate + sum_r(C_execute,r + C_verify,r + C_maintain,r).
Verification savings alone can be valuable only when they exceed the additional construction and production cost over the relevant reuse horizon. A faster online solve is not a lower full project cost.

Freeze the candidate, model configuration, purpose bridge, sampling unit and comparison protocol before fresh confirmation. A natural test and constructed finite task bank establish different claims. Replays after publication reproduce data; they cannot recreate the original holdout. Negative controls must include truly required restrictions, uninformative experiments, uneconomic setup and omitted outcomes.

Retain scope, prerequisites, cost, failure modes, mechanism evidence and independent checking with each capability. Artifact transfer is not evidence that the recipient improved. Compare with competent baselines and ordinary artifact libraries. Compounding remains a hypothesis requiring repeated measured improvement, not graph growth or one promising descendant.

## 2. Invent the Economy

**Citation:** Vincent Boucher. *Invent the Economy: Transformational Intelligence for Creating Viable Markets.* MONTREAL.AI, Montréal, Québec, Canada. October 2026. Research manuscript.

Economic-possibility package P=(technical capability, actual counterparties, commercial arrangement, financing schedule, rights/authority, evidence). Its five evidence stages are technical possibility, modeled viability, accepted arrangement, realized exchange, repeatable market. No earlier stage proves a later one.

Foundational exact model: four hypothetical buyers, each with success payment 300, benefit 360 and adaptation cost 75; shared construction 600; qualification probability .8. Units are thousands of illustrative USD. Expected provider contribution is +120; successful contribution +300. Required prefunding is 900 because construction and adaptation precede payment. Failure loses 600. All buyers share the same core qualification event; four buyers are not four independent risks. A 40/unit route burden makes expected contribution -8. A competent unrestricted enumerator ties the constructed arrangement. 2,240 supplied parameter cases agree with a separate checker; 540 admitted and 1,700 rejected. Counts do not measure real-business success rates.

Successor illustration: successful origin earns 300 above protected 900 reserve. A successor with setup 300 plus adaptation 150 has expected +60 but requires 450; it cannot use only origin surplus. Setup 150 instead requires 300 and has expected +210. The full two-stage model then has expected contribution +288 with explicitly assumed costs and probabilities. These are alternative supplied cost reductions, not measured technical learning.

The paper already establishes profit-versus-liquidity distinction. The new paid-milestone edition should add actual useful digital work plus decision-contingent information value and staged commercial continuation; merely restating this theorem would duplicate the foundation.

Participant prices must satisfy willingness/payment limits and expected incremental benefit after adoption cost. Aggregate surplus does not confer consent. Fee competition and outside options matter. Claims of pricing optimality require more than choosing maximum assumed values.

Cash at each prefix is unrestricted initial cash plus collected receipts minus paid costs plus authorized financing. Refundable deposits and reserves are restricted under actual terms; conditional invoices are not funding. Cash conservation and complete obligations are separate from expected contribution.

Customer benefit, enterprise receipts/obligations and token operations remain separate. Token burn is not cash. USDC and applicable $AGIALPHA routes have distinct contractual roles; activity is not holder profit. A fixed accepted-outcome fee avoids a purely mechanical incentive to retain useless billable work, but does not guarantee demand or margin.

## 5. Concrete continuity recommendations

1. Make a paid first milestone an accepted usable result whether the larger venture advances or stops, under prospective contract terms.
2. Separate useful negative experimental findings from provider delivery failure: only the former can earn the contracted fee.
3. Define each information experiment's decision set and prior best action. EVSI before experiment cost is E_y[max_a E(U(a,theta)|y)] - max_a E(U(a,theta)). Deduct price, adoption effort and delay at the customer boundary. A label such as “knowledge gained” is not a cashable benefit.
4. Provider expected profit uses success of delivering the contracted information artifact, acceptance and collection; underlying venture technical success is a different random variable. Dependence should be conditioned on shared scenario state.
5. Filter liquidity and obligations before choosing profitable continuation. Include stop/defer and fit next costs within actually collected, unencumbered surplus, not expected future receipts.
6. Use inherited execution/accounting interfaces with exact provenance; do not claim that reuse independently proves Alpha.
7. Do not impose live receipts as a prerequisite to a labeled conditional commercial conclusion; do not relabel that conclusion as realized commercial proof.
