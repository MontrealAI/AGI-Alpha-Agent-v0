# Opportunity research: paid industrial reuse decision gates

Research date: 2026-10-27. Analyst role: independent opportunity/prior-art review. No customer contact, spending, physical experiment, or purchase occurred.

## Recommendation

Select an industrial reclaimed-water **investment gate pack**: a digital instrument that tells a plant owner what operating conditions, product-water specifications, evidence, and economics would justify the next engineering commitment. Its value is a usable, conservative capital-allocation and commissioning decision, including an actionable refusal. The wider opportunity is modular, standardized delivery of industrial reuse projects that currently cannot justify bespoke preparation costs. This is a commercial hypothesis; neither industrial reuse nor project standardization is novel.

The construction should be broader than a published-paper audit: provide an explicit source-to-use schema, provenance-linked input extraction, conditional quality/volume/economic envelopes, witness generation when constraints fail, a value-of-information decision over the next measurement, and a staged resource cap. A customer could purchase the portable instrument and a bounded application before physical viability of a particular plant is established.

Do not certify physical safety, regulatory approval, plant design, or commercial acceptance from published laboratory observations. Make customer process specifications and long-run operating data required inputs for a site-level deployment decision.

## Published case: use and limits

Simonič (2021), DOI 10.3390/membranes11120976, reports laboratory RO treatment of mixed industrial streams. Tables contain source chemistry and estimated costs; its narrative states capital EUR180,000 and gross annual savings EUR82,000. Table 5 annual operating items sum EUR28,630; EUR18,000 depreciation raises reported annual accounting cost to EUR46,630. The tabulated cash figures imply simple payback 3.373 years, before omitted costs, contradicting the narrative claim under one year. Maximum allowed concentrations are discharge criteria, not validated customer process-water acceptance criteria. Fouling/cleaning passages around 200 minutes conflict; sustained uptime is unresolved. Acid addition and treatment alter chemistry, so unreactive mixing alone cannot reconstruct treated-feed composition.

## Conservative construction specification

1. Input: source observations with units, sample context and uncertainty; process-water limits separately supplied by customer; treatment-performance bounds; flow/demand; capex/opex/time model; evidence status.
2. Output: no unconditional viability verdict. Return supported conditional envelope, a counterexample if a constraint fails, or explicit refusal if chemistry/end-use data or legal authority is missing.
3. Separate technical pass, customer acceptance, adoption and collection in the economics. Technical success does not imply a paying client.
4. Use the published case only as an anchored replay. Fresh scenarios should alter product-water limits, uptime, discharge costs, capital, tariffs, concentration intervals, missing inputs and incorrect units.
5. Ordinary competent baseline can use the same sources and formulas. Established process tools remain superior for rigorous treatment simulation; the study should not label deterministic checking as a new RO technology.
6. A next measurement is valuable only if an outcome changes a decision among stop/defer/pilot/commit. Preserve zero-value-information cases.

## Scale: threshold scenario, not a market forecast

World Bank (2025) describes conditional global potential of 430 million cubic metres/day and up to US$340 billion cumulative capital by 2040. Those are broad sector scenarios, not AGI ALPHA revenue. EEA's portal covers more than 60,000 industrial sites, but that count includes many unsuitable sectors and says nothing about water-reuse eligibility.

A bottom-up **illustrative capital-deployment threshold** is 6,000 qualified projects globally × EUR180,000/project = EUR1.08 billion cumulative plant capital by 2040, in constant case-study euros. Using the case's 100,900 cubic metres/year feed and assumed 85% recovery gives 85,765 cubic metres/year output/project; 6,000 projects would represent 1.410 million cubic metres/day. This is a scale condition, not evidence that 6,000 equivalent sites or customers exist. Currency, historical-price basis, adoption horizon, target chemistry, site engineering and finance constraints must stay beside it. The construction should be judged on the first milestone without multiplying this capital by an arbitrary success probability.

An alternative annual-value threshold at case cash surplus EUR53,370/project-year would require about 18,737 simultaneously operating equivalents for EUR1 billion/year. That is materially more demanding and should not be claimed as a forecast. Fees are separate: e.g., 6,000 × a hypothesized EUR5,000 initial pack = EUR30 million total provider gross billings before acceptance, collection, costs and competitive share, not EUR1.08 billion provider revenue.

## Human benchmark and pricing

Use transparent equivalent-scope labor, not a full engineering EPC contract. Example planning range (not a quote): 24–40 analyst/engineer hours, 4–8 verification/packaging hours, all-in bill rates EUR100–180/hour gives EUR2,800–8,640. Rates and hours need sensitivity; no source directly documents willingness to pay this offering. BLS reports US environmental engineers median USD51.50/hour in May 2025; employee wage is not bill rate, and USD must not be silently converted to EUR. Public wage data are only a reasonableness anchor. Actual research, failures, QA, acquisition, corrections and support must still be charged once in provider costs.
