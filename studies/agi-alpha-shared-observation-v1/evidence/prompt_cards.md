# Prospective matched-workflow cards · version 1

Status: authored on 27 October 2026 **after** the recorded software confirmation. These cards are a frozen starting protocol for the next matched model trial. They were not used to generate the 48-case evidence and establish no model performance gain. The retained compiler, its scope contract and the independent reference had their own earlier freezes.

## Common contract for H, U, E and E+

Deliver a decision-support package for the supplied mission. Preserve every operator's original risk criterion, action availability, deadline and fallback obligation. State which observations are useful, all reachable conditional decisions, individual participation, required funding, service price, complete delivery cost and refusal reasons. Success requires both semantic correctness and the declared customer conditions; a correct refusal is a technical result but not an accepted paid order.

Use the same mission, calibration evidence, outside options, model identifier, tool permissions and time/resource limits in U, E and E+. Permit all configurations to discover or propose ordinary available methods. H is a scoped equivalent human service, with a real quote or explicitly estimated hours and rates. Do not restrict U to a no-tool chat, deny it documentation, or prohibit a competent alternative.

Before starting, enter the same maximum token/tool budget and deadline for every AI arm; choose them from the actual provider limits and the buyer's needs. This study's unmeasured 30,000-input/10,000-output token illustration is a cost hypothesis, not an empirically adequate budget. Keep the protected solution and checker implementation out of model context. Reveal only acceptance criteria and legitimate task data. Assign unfamiliar missions and freeze order/randomization before observing outcomes.

## U · representative ordinary use

> Analyze this mission and produce a useful, auditable decision-support package. Use the provided evidence and available tools, check your work, and explain any uncertainty or missing information. Preserve the stated risk limits, action deadlines and fallback requirements. Include the operator-level decisions and economics, shared-order terms if useful, resource needs, and a clear recommendation. Refuse proposals that cannot meet the original requirements. You may use established methods or propose a better arrangement within the same permissions and budget.

This is a competent ordinary request with a complete contract. Its outcomes must be measured; there is no assigned penalty for having a shorter prompt.

## E · established expert operation

Use the U request and this checklist:

1. Extract original purpose, units, state semantics, outside options, rights and action deadlines. Distinguish observations from modeled assumptions.
2. Compute an explicit baseline. Condition on every reachable outcome, including adverse and missing/late results. Pool only outcomes indistinguishable at the operator's own decision time.
3. Check every resulting action against the original risk and timing constraints. An average-safe plan is insufficient. Preserve the declared fallback and full reserve.
4. Evaluate each buyer separately, recompute the roster after declines/withdrawals, and reconcile all payment/default/refund branches. Keep pass-through funds separate from service revenue.
5. Independently verify arithmetic and source meaning using an available conventional implementation or independently derived calculation. Record and charge retries, correction, tool use and customer effort.
6. Deliver a qualified plan or an auditable refusal, with remaining uncertainty and complete A/B/C classification. Stop when the task contract is met or the budget is exhausted.

## E+ · expert operation with retained capability

Use E and provide the exact retained archive, its manifest and permitted scope. Execute the artifact on the new input, inspect its output and perform fresh mission-specific semantic checks. Reuse stable qualification only when identity and scope match; obtain authority separately. Do not pass protected answers, prior recipient solutions or credential material with the artifact. If the recipient already has an equivalent tool, record the parity and do not count repackaging as learning.

## Measurement and falsifiers

Record first-pass and final accepted outcomes, refusal quality, actual tokens, tools, compute, wall time, retries, corrections, customer effort, paid and unpaid human work, fees, refunds and support. Separate semantic acceptance conditional on a functioning package from joint acceptance including common failure. At matched budgets compare accepted outcomes; at matched accepted quality compare complete cost. Use a blinded checker and domain reviewer for physical meaning before any operational use.

Estimate E minus U separately from E+ minus E. Include equality and negative effects. Operational Alpha requires measured task-level improvement after its additional costs. Retention requires incremental recipient benefit beyond its competent baseline, including assessment and upkeep. A nicer prompt, successful byte transfer, extra resources or a positive expected value in a constructed world does not satisfy either test.

Primary falsifiers are an unsafe/late reachable branch, a waived original requirement, a buyer worse off without compensation/consent, an unfunded cash prefix, loss of semantic validity after transfer, no net gain at matched quality, or a better available outside option. Preserve failed trials and revise prospectively before a fresh confirmation set.
