## Accounting call shape

Commands are `{action, record}` dictionaries. `charge` requires `activity_id`, `mission_id`, bucket `A|B|C`, category `model|tools|compute|storage|network|human|acceptance|correction|overhead`, `accrued_micro_usd` (integer or null for unknown), `paid_micro_usd`, basis `measured|attested|unknown`, 64-hex evidence digest and note. `seal`, `seal-retention`, and `seal-investment` require all nine categories for the relevant bucket. Capital, invoice and collection records carry `record_id`, `mission_id`, amount in USD micro-units and evidence digest. `reserve` carries a micro-USD integer. Do not feed real/model receipts into the same evidence label. A compact per-scenario aggregate is preferable to thousands of per-event calls because the ledger has bounded capacity.
