# Bounded independent opportunity dossier

Prepared 2026-10-27 for the AGI Alpha Flywheel research lead. This is a discovery note, not an executed experiment or claim of discovery. Four materially different candidates were challenged using eight opened primary sources. Known mechanisms are building blocks. No live customers, willingness to pay, private satellite data, physical equipment, or new service demand were observed.

## Recommendation

The strongest distinct conditional experiment in this portfolio is **contingency-ready joint observation procurement for small satellite operators**. Its purpose is to avoid wasting a scarce maneuver or accepting an unsafe delay when several encounters depend on the same poorly known object. It is defensible only as a constructed instrument and conditional arrangement: orbital conjunction assessment, data exchange, commercial tracking, coordinated satellite operations and even federated autonomous satellite marketplaces already exist. There is no basis here for a novel-market or novel-algorithm claim.

The key experiment must allow a new observation to increase estimated risk. Buying information cannot be represented as guaranteed safety improvement. An admissible observation contract preserves the original risk limit, the maneuver deadline, the fallback maneuver and its funding for every participant. The technical question is whether common information can be bought once while preserving all these obligations. The economic question is whether the shared benefit covers acquisition, translation, independent acceptance, reserves and overhead under participation and common-failure uncertainty.

This recommendation is conditional on the lead not finding a stronger opportunity. Do not select it merely because a Gaussian encounter model is easy to code.

## Proposed joint-observation experiment

### Transformation witness

Original purpose: meet each operator’s stated collision-risk and decision-deadline requirements while preserving finite fuel and mission availability.

Binding restriction: each operator buys or waits for information separately; no executable statement shows when a shared observation can replace separate acquisition without removing maneuver obligations.

Reason potentially removable: multiple encounters depend on the same uncertain secondary state, so one observation can be informative for several distinct decisions. This is an explicit mechanistic assumption, not a measured market fact.

Replacement: an observation contract that returns contingent action branches, each operator’s acceptance test, fallback deadline, allocated acquisition charge, refusal/withdrawal behavior and protected maneuver reserve.

Executable realization: Gaussian state/measurement update or another clearly scoped known estimator, plus an ordinary numerical risk calculator and time/cash accounting. No new risk model is required or claimed.

Falsifying observation: apparent benefit disappears when uncertainty correlation, realistic measurement failure, mandatory fallback, existing free data, the competent joint planner or all construction/qualification costs are included.

### Smallest complete implementation

1. Generate development encounter fixtures with explicit positions, covariances, measurement operators, deadline and fallback costs. Freeze before confirmation. Prefer real public CDM metadata to calibrate geometry distributions, but do not mislabel final predicted risk as collision ground truth.
2. Baseline E receives the same scientific sources, numerical library, joint-procurement permission, time and cash. E+ receives only the retained compiler as additional capability. An independently implemented exhaustive small-plan planner is the correctness and conventional-parity control.
3. For each potential measurement, enumerate outcome branches or sample a fixed fresh cohort. Recompute posterior risk. If the threshold is exceeded or the measurement fails/arrives late, preserve the fallback action. Do not count the cash reserved for that action as available operating funds.
4. Compare no service, independent purchases, shared construction without participation arrangement, coordination without construction, and joint instrument plus arrangement.
5. Include zero overlap between operator information needs, badly oriented sensors, correlated outages, late delivery, one participant withdrawal, adverse new information and partial service delivery.
6. Export the actual compiler with its frozen specification and provenance. A separate recipient process uses it on different encounter geometry and a changed deadline. Recheck mission meaning and authority. Test that old calibration assumptions are rejected when inappropriate.
7. Retain only if expected future avoided adaptation cost exceeds additional assessment, packaging, serving and upkeep. A parity result can still support useful engineering while rejecting new Alpha.

### Useful conditional thresholds

For operator i, let V_i be expected avoidable outside-option cost from the contingent plan, net of the operator’s own adaptation and remaining fallback burden. Let s_i be its allocated price. Participation requires s_i <= V_i, with withdrawal allowed before commitment. Let K be the shared observation and construction cash requirement, Q the expected refunds/support cost, and R the required reserve. Feasible funding requires sum(s_i) >= K + Q + R at the necessary time; expected profitability alone does not prove liquidity.

If an assessment/retention artifact costs B and reduces future adaptation cost by d on compatible jobs, with compatible-reuse probability p and N opportunities, the simplest conditional admission test is N*p*d > B plus maintenance and failure risk. These variables must remain assumptions unless measured. Use p=0 and d=0 in the sensitivity envelope.

Operational Alpha should be analyzed by required improvement, not assigned. If expert workflow costs C_E versus ordinary C_U and a qualified delivery collects P, then at fixed quality the extra accepted-delivery probability required to justify expert overhead is (C_E-C_U)/P, before other differential refunds/latency effects. A deterministic compiler does not establish frontier-model performance.

## Decision

No candidate in this bounded search warrants a claim of newly discovered market demand or proven frontier-AI superiority. Joint observation procurement offers a distinct, source-motivated conditional mechanism that can be built and falsified quickly. Its strongest result would be an executable, purpose-preserving plan and quantified feasibility region. The existing competent baseline must be allowed to tie. A negative economics result should preserve the artifact and yield a concrete smallest buyer/provider test rather than a blocker list.
