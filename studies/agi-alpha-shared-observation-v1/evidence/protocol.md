# Prospective protocol and budget

Research lane: shared-observation-v1, 2026-10-27. Parent main source: 47c17f6aca6b1bb8779dd3e47d5ba5f8af3329f4.
Existing research and integration predecessor: 231834269599d85e49cfe1ca9917a1a874522d44.

Initial effort budgets are 20 minutes discovery, 35 implementation, 25 evaluation, 25 writing/demo. These are
planning caps rather than metered human labor or customer-billed time. The root records elapsed milestones and
extends only for a concrete unresolved correctness or packaging issue. No paid API or external resources.

Discovery compared satellite observation pooling, emerging-contaminant procurement, cold-chain preservation and
on-board Earth-observation service composition, with eight primary source documents and three foundations.
Broader novelty of all four was rejected/narrowed. Selected question: can a reusable finite contingency compiler
produce original-purpose-qualified observation offers that several modeled operators can jointly afford when
separate purchases are not worthwhile? Prior art includes conventional conjunction assessment, free screening,
commercial observations, joint estimation and federated satellite coordination.

The opportunity and first example are CONSTRUCTED CONDITIONAL HYPOTHESES, not sampled market demand.
The originating three-operator scenario deliberately instantiates the proposed mechanism; it is not a random
representative customer. It uses a two-state synthetic uncertainty model. No ESA CDM dataset was downloaded or
relabeled as truth. Final collision-risk estimates in that dataset would not be actual collision labels.

First diagnostic: a reviewer-supplied state-dependent timeout counterexample challenges the invalid assumption
that missing information is uninformative. Keep its original result. This is neither U nor a competent E baseline.
Build the corrected general compiler; exercise eight development cases. Freeze compiler, profile, schema and
economic assumptions before opening the independently generated 48-case confirmation cohort.

Technical acceptance: schema, probability conservation, independent posterior calculation, every reachable
branch's original risk threshold and action timing, fixed safe timeout fallback, scope/rights prerequisites,
input identity and execution_authorized=false. The finite checker is research acceptance, not buyer acceptance
or independent institutional assurance. Separate ordinary exact enumeration has identical information and
coordination rights. A tie rejects algorithmic/formulation superiority over an equipped expert.

Recipient: transfer the actual two-file executable artifact through the existing source-qualified lifecycle,
execute it in a separate isolated process on unfamiliar input, and apply the independent checker. An ordinary
directory containing identical bytes and permissions is required as a parity control. Protected input/answers
are not distributed with the capability. New mission checks and authority remain separate.

U/E/E+ model performance is not inferred from this deterministic computation. Primary simulated semantic
acceptance is equal. Use unfavorable, equal and favorable gain ranges to derive the required benefit of expert
operation and retention. The same frontier model, tool opportunities and budgets apply to U and E. Human H
is a scoped hours×wage×burden estimate; no consulting quote or observed sale is asserted.

Economics are frozen before the originating result is used: hypotheses about price, acquisition, adaptation,
withdrawal, default, common package failure and paid effort live in economics/assumptions-v1.json. Compare
technical/arrangement ablations, individual procurement, joint procurement and an unrestricted expert. Track
refunds, pass-through funds, earned fees, cost A/B/C, cash prefixes, buyer fallback reserves and available funds.
Admit reusable capability only under a prospective break-even reuse/cost rule. Do not force positive retention.

Stress tests include all-late data, noninformative data, adverse information, unsafe fallback, missing rights,
calibration mismatch, no shared benefit, withdrawal, low cash, common failure and negative transfer. Rejecting
inapplicable work is a correct instrument outcome, not a successful purchased observation.

Claims excluded: measured Astra/Terra task superiority, physical collision prediction or safe satellite control,
new satellite-market invention, real customer acceptance/collections, live ENS, native scientific capsule
admission, graph-specific value, learned sensor parameters, independent weight-trained specialists, universal
autonomy and sustained compounding. Fresh executable reuse and conditional feasibility remain testable.
