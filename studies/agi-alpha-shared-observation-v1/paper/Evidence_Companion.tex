\subsection{Economics and obligations}\label{economics-and-obligations}}

\path{economics/assumptions-v1.json} freezes illustrative USD prices:
\$600 acquisition, \$80 per accepted package and \$20 customer
adaptation. Acceptance is 0.90, common package failure 0.05, precommit
withdrawal 0.10 and unpaid-balance default 0.03. These are explicit
hypotheses. The fixed fee is neither an optimized tariff nor a discount
from a measured human quote.

\path{results/economics/event_tree.json}; \texttt{summary.json} is its
compact reporting companion. Expected service collections are

Delivery-required learning belongs to A. \textbf{B} is additional
reusable packaging: \$180 initially and \$2 per reuse. \textbf{C}, if
chosen, is \$240 of extra research; this is an assumed optional budget,
not an observed bill. Neither B nor C is commercially admitted in the
primary outcome. At an unmeasured \$10 saving per use, 23 compatible
uses repay B after upkeep; zero saving rejects investment. Paid provider
effort is included in A; unpriced additional obligations and actual
research opportunity costs remain unknown. Every additional expected
\$22.83 of provider cost exhausts the origin's margin.

The accounting separates service fees from acquisition custody and buyer
savings. Thirty percent of the service fee is deposited. Common package
failure refunds all deposits, leaving the provider with sunk acquisition
expense. Individual nonacceptance, including postcommit withdrawal,
refunds the fee deposit but retains the acquired-information charge.
This is distinct from scientific timeout, which is already in technical
expected expense. Default affects the unpaid fee balance. Refund
liabilities and unpaid obligations are subtracted before funds become
available.

An illustrative all-commit/all-accept/all-pay branch receives \$840:
\$600 pass-through and \$240 service revenue. Its A cost is \$135 and
contribution \$105. With seed capital \$1,500, terminal cash is \$1,605;
the \$300 reserve leaves \$1,305 available. This branch is not the
\$22.83 expectation. Buyer cash must additionally cover full fees,
adaptation and fallback obligations; required balances are
\$800/\$950/\$1,100, but buyer financing is unverified. Zero-seed
failure is retained in the liquidity analysis. Seed capital, customer
savings and tokens are not revenue.

\subsection{Preserved failures and

\path{development/first_counterexample.json} preserves the initial
timeout error. Prior risk was 0.00004, below a 0.0001 limit, but risk
conditional on timeout was 0.00025778. Using the prior would falsely
accept that branch. The corrected compiler conditions on each operator's
observation and retains a fixed fallback. This reviewer-suggested
counterexample does not measure ordinary model use.

\path{development/economics-v1/} preserves the initial buyer-liquidity
calculation that reserved only the fee deposit. The correction reserves
the full fee; a targeted regression refuses buyers whose cash lies
between the two requirements. Postcommit withdrawal is also explicitly
exercised.

\path{development/economics-v1_1/} preserves the analytical expertise
grid before correcting fee collection, common failure and expected retry
cost. The corrected collectible factor is 0.979; joint acceptance equals
semantic acceptance after retries times 0.95. An extra \$20 per \$240
cohort requires 8.9601 percentage points of one-attempt conditional
semantic improvement. The correction preserves byte-identical primary
summary, event tree, confirmation and campaign outputs; its record
includes hand-calculated regressions. It is not a retrospective change
to successful primary assumptions.

\hypertarget{comparators-calibration-and-scope}{%
\subsection{Comparators, calibration and
scope}\label{comparators-calibration-and-scope}}

H denotes equivalent finite decision-support work, not flight-safety
certification. The BLS \$134,960 annual wage provides a reference;
dividing by assumed 2,080 hours and applying assumed 8--24 hours and
1.5--2.5 burden yields \$778.62--\$3,893.08. This is an estimated labor
scenario, not a market quote. (\protect\hyperlink{ref-bls2026}{US Bureau
of Labor Statistics 2026})

U, E and E+ are not measured frontier-model trial arms. Their primary
acceptance assumptions are equal at 0.90; the sensitivity grid includes
equal and worse performance. The competent expert has the same
mathematical methods and coordination rights and ties the constructed
method before extra B. Component ablations lacking the contingency
instrument are not evidence that ordinary users or experts lack it.
\path{evidence/prompt_cards.md} is a prospective protocol authored after
confirmation.

Official model documentation establishes identifiers and published
prices, not task success or account entitlement. The declared
30,000-input/10,000-output Astra workload costs \$0.80 at published
rates, already inside A. No direct billed API experiment occurred.
\path{evidence/calibration.md} preserves access limitations, pricing
details and source dates. (\protect\hyperlink{ref-astra}{OpenAI 2026b},
\protect\hyperlink{ref-terra}{2026c},
\protect\hyperlink{ref-pricing}{2026a})

Conjunction assessment, covariance-aware reasoning and coordinated
satellite observations have substantial prior art. NASA guidance, the
ESA challenge and federated-network work bound the claim: the
contribution is the finite executable witness and accounting/transfer
audit, not discovery of Bayesian information value or shared
observation. No orbit propagation, fitted sensor likelihoods or external
physical validation was executed. (\protect\hyperlink{ref-nasa2026}{NASA
Office of the Chief Engineer 2026};
\protect\hyperlink{ref-uriot2020}{Uriot et al. 2020};
\protect\hyperlink{ref-esa2021}{European Space Agency 2021})

The retained profile is a \textbf{scope contract}, not a learned sensor
model. Its bounded limits and Apache-2.0 source rights travel with the
two-file archive; protected answers and mission inputs do not. Existing
lifecycle fixtures retain identity, revocation, selective rights and
separate authority checks. The native scientific scope restriction
remains in force. Twenty-nine fixture controls and eight service-ledger
reconciliation checks pass; native completeness closes modeled
categories, not an actual expense bill. There was no live ENS
authentication or production admission. Revocation prevents future
mediated retrieval; it cannot erase copies already received.

\hypertarget{reproduction-budget-and-release}{%
\subsection{Reproduction, budget and
release}\label{reproduction-budget-and-release}}

\path{evidence/protocol.md} records planned caps of 20 minutes for
discovery, 35 implementation, 25 evaluation and 25 writing/demo: 105
minutes total. These are planning caps, not metered effort or a cost
receipt. Four materially different opportunities were considered before
selection. No paid API or external resource purchase is reported.

The study README and \path{integration/README.md} specify reproduction
and fresh output locations. Preserve archived receipts when rerunning;
public cases reproduce published results only. Complete repository
qualification is not claimed. The next market-changing test requires a
rights-and-timing supplier quote plus two or three operators sharing a
permission-cleared retrospective event, with frozen acceptance and
fallback terms.

The browser-readable edition and English/French demonstration are local
deliverables. Public release requires the repository owner's authorized
process and verification of the resulting stable link; no
live-publication receipt is supplied here. Earlier freezes and failed
trials are preserved. This companion supports review and bounded
experimentation, not procurement or spacecraft execution authority.
