# AGI ALPHA — The Next Upgrade

## A useful dependency-upgrade decision packet

This research builds an executable, source-pinned assessment for a maintainer reviewing changed Python dependencies. It combines the unchanged native AGI ALPHA source inventory with an incumbent workflow, a proposed retained capability and an independently constructed conventional comparator. The packet reports supported argument-binding changes, their witnesses, captured consumer relevance, unresolved scope and attributable resources. An exposed HTTPX/Starlette example connects the static evidence to a working predecessor, an actual failed proposed pair and a known upstream remedy.

The research asks whether retaining the tested capability improves subsequent useful work after construction, verification, upkeep and delivery costs. Its contribution includes the executable experiment and its adverse evidence. Admission, rejection and independent-review records are preserved; commercial payback and accelerating capability construction are not inferred from a faster component, a replay or a conventional tie.

In frozen held-out confirmation, the conventional builder produced independently accepted reports for **12 of 12** historical release transitions; the custom retained candidate produced accepted reports for **9 of 12**. These are twelve representative source-history missions across three package clusters, not paid customer orders. Neither method demonstrated an affected call site within the captured Alpha consumer scope. That bounded capture does not establish absence of runtime breakage elsewhere, and upstream changes alone do not establish customer savings. The final engineering disposition is recorded in [final-admission.json](results/final-admission.json); it is separate from production authorization and economic payback.

## Reproduction

Use the **complete offline companion** matching ARTIFACTS.json, then follow [the reproduction guide](support/README-reproduction.md). The verified environment is CPython 3.12.14 on Linux x86-64 with glibc 2.39 and Node 24.22.1. The bundled wheels require CPython 3.12 and glibc 2.34 or newer. Python with venv/ensurepip and Node are external prerequisites.

```sh
python3.12 support/replay_offline.py \
  --out /tmp/agi-alpha-offline-replay-001 \
  --mission httpx-development \
  --arm conventional
```

The command verifies original identities, installs local hash-pinned wheels with `--no-index`, runs the frozen checks and creates a new bilingual Workbench packet. It refuses an existing output destination. The clean-environment replay, including its initial packaging failure, is indexed in [support/verification/manifest.json](support/verification/manifest.json); the complete package retains the full logs and evidence. Test cases and repeated evaluations do not increase the number of customer jobs.
