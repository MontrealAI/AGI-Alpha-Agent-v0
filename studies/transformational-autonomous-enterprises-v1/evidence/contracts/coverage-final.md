## Declared scope

The new two-mission journey executes a finite weighted-DAG parent and Boolean-reachability successor. It joins exact-byte source-level four-repository delivery to two jobs in one disposable local EVM. Precomputed artifacts are checked again before settlement; no JobMarket-triggered installed Fleet dispatcher is claimed.

`independently-checked` means separate implementation/agent under the same controller. `live-read` is not live execution. Contract code/configuration at one finalized Ethereum block is verified; current organizational deployment and participant enrollment remain unverified. No mainnet transaction or real payment was made.
