## Foundation: the organization is the object

**AGI ALPHA: A Scalable Substrate for Intelligence Organizations** (198 pages; 30 April 2026) is expressly a theoretical systems and implementation-architecture paper. Its organizational substrate binds agents, bounded jobs, validators, tools, memory, incentives, settlement, and governance. The intended causal path is verified work → retained capability → productive capacity → stronger future work (pp. 9–11). Repository activity is implementation evidence, not proof of AGI or empirical superiority (pp. 31–38, 73–76).

Its later evidence docket reports local/proxy HELIOS and Cybersecurity Sovereign comparisons. It expressly retains the absence of external attestation, official public-benchmark wins, real physical multi-node scaling, certified cybersecurity, and verified safe autonomous operation (pp. 189–193). Its failure conditions include overhead exceeding baseline advantage, unreplayable evidence, validators accepting unsafe/low-quality work, and scaling whose coordination cost outgrows verified output. Writer #4 should operationalize this substrate rather than infer that its named roles establish a completed enterprise.

## Candidate contribution and fair interpretation

The strongest non-duplicative integration target identified here is **proof-directed replay reduction for reusable capability composition**: preserve immutable mathematical lemmas and evidence as history, reconstruct current mission proof, and separately re-establish authority. Test whether dependency-aware validation reduces complete measured evaluation burden without accepting changed source, changed policy, altered evidence, changed input, or unsupported scope. Compare against full replay, no inheritance, and the supplied expert. Integrating existing components through the actual four native routes is an engineering contribution; a new formal dependency-sensitive test can make it scientifically inspectable.

This should credit incremental build systems and proof-carrying-code precedents rather than rename them. Primary references verified: Mokhov, Mitchell, Peyton Jones, *Build Systems à la Carte*, ICFP 2018, DOI 10.1145/3236774 (Microsoft Research PDF); Necula, *Proof-carrying code*, POPL 1997, DOI 10.1145/263699.263712 (ACM/author page). Their mechanisms are precedents, not evidence for this implementation.

The $100→$10,000 target is unproven across the inspected predecessor studies. No actual paying customer, firm independent executable bid, settled profit, or complete monetary development bill was established. Abstract work units cannot be converted to dollars without measured prices/usage. Neither token transfers nor an NFT's self-price creates incremental economic value.

Use a distinct new title/edition and preserve every earlier URL/PDF byte. Independent code checking by another session agent is useful implementation review, not institutional or externally blinded evaluation.
