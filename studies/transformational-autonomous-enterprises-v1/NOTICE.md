# Provenance and licensing

This research companion was produced for the Writer #4 assignment, coordinated through MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0 issue #4850. It does not represent approval by every repository owner or an external scientific institution.

New companion code is offered under Apache-2.0, matching its source headers and the primary repository contribution context. Each imported dependency and pinned source repository retains its own license. Those repositories and package binaries are fetched separately by the setup script; their complete source trees and installed dependencies are not redistributed in this archive.

Earlier research papers are cited by their actual public URLs and retained SHA-256 identities. Their complete PDFs and selected extracted text are intentionally excluded from this companion. The new paper's source, bibliography and editable figures are included.

All local EVM addresses, mock-token flows and test signatures are disposable test evidence. Read-only public chain observations are labelled separately. The archive contains no wallet secrets or authorization to operate a financial account.
