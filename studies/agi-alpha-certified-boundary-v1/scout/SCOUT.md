## Decision

No technically novel flagship survived this screen with enough evidence to justify a claim. ## Candidate 1: Outcome-directed experimental instruments

**Proposed requirement.** A scientific buyer needs a guaranteed decision about a target property, under a finite menu of costly tests and a hard budget, while nuisance parameters may remain unidentified. The instrument should return either an executable adaptive experiment or two indistinguishable admissible models demonstrating why the requested decision is unsupported.

**Hypothesis tested by literature.** Existing approaches cannot adequately deliver a decision certificate under finite budgets because they target whole-model recovery rather than the useful property; a target-relative experiment compiler changes this requirement while retaining correct decisions and hard resource limits.

**Conventional challenge.** This general premise is false. Decision Region Determination explicitly reduces uncertainty only enough to enter a decision region. Hyperedge Cutting supplies conditions and a near-optimal policy; DiRECt addresses nonmyopic value of information; ECED handles correlated, noisy tests. At the small scale of this sprint, exact dynamic programming over version spaces is also a strong practical comparator. An obstruction pair is easy to construct from the same version space and is not an independent novelty claim.

**Decisive digital experiment if retained for usefulness.** Freeze finite model families, target partitions, test outcomes, costs and budgets. Compare a candidate policy against exact dynamic programming; independently enumerate every model and response path. Measure accepted decisions, worst-case cost, refusals and false acceptance on fresh cases. Do not compare only against entropy-greedy selection or full-parameter identification.

**Decision:** Kill generic technical-breakthrough claim before deep construction. A useful implementation can remain.

## Candidate 2: Decision certificates from incompatible partial evidence

**Proposed requirement.** An analyst needs to know whether a proposed intervention has a positive effect when several studies expose only incomplete, mismeasured or incompatible margins. The offering supplies sharp bounds, admissible extremal worlds, and a refusal if the desired conclusion is not identified.

**Hypothesis tested by literature.** Existing approaches cannot adequately deliver this conclusion because they require a fully observed common table or unjustified point-identification assumptions; a symbolic compiler over latent response functions changes the evidence representation while preserving the original causal question.

**Conventional challenge.** Balke-Pearl bounds and general automated partial identification already address the core mechanism. Autobounds reduces discrete causal questions to polynomial programs, permits incomplete or mismeasured data, reports sharp bounds when solved, and provides valid interim bounds and an epsilon-sharpness guarantee. A small fully discrete special case can often be solved by linear programming and independently checked by enumeration.

**Decisive digital experiment if retained for usefulness.** Use fixed causal graphs, response-function types and observed-margin constraints. Match all assumptions across approaches. Compare a custom certificate representation against a conventional linear or polynomial program with extremal witnesses, not against an unjustified point estimate. Include incompatible data and nonidentification refusals.

**Decision:** Kill generic inference-breakthrough claim. No evidence of new commercial demand or discovery advantage.

Primary sources:

- Duarte et al. (2024 issue; published online 2023), An Automated Approach to Causal Inference in Discrete Settings, JASA 119(547):1778-1793. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11566246/
- Authors' software: https://github.com/duarteguilherme/autobounds
- Finkelstein and Shpitser (2020), Deriving Bounds And Inequality Constraints Using Logical Relations Among Counterfactuals, PMLR 124:1348-1357. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v124/finkelstein20a.html

## Candidate 3: Exact noise-tailored quantum-code construction

**Proposed requirement.** A quantum-computing team needs a compact code meeting a specified protection level under a documented biased/correlated channel and hardware constraints. A digital construction can supply stabilizer generators and an exact logical-error certificate without claiming physical hardware validation.

**Hypothesis tested by literature.** Existing approaches cannot adequately deliver the target because code distance alone does not determine useful channel-specific protection; a construction directly searching operational logical error changes the objective while preserving encoding, correction and resource requirements.

**Conventional challenge.** The general objective is already established. Su et al. use Quantum Lego and reinforcement learning to optimize arbitrary code properties, including biased-noise logical error, with optimal 13-qubit CSS-code distance and a 17-qubit code whose lower adversarial distance coexists with better channel-specific protection. Evolutionary code search also directly optimizes stabilizer codes for a specified error model. Choosing an ad hoc new noise/constraint combination does not establish a substantive advance.

**Decisive digital experiment.** A genuinely new existence witness would need a frozen previously published bound or unmet device requirement; conventional search and available code families receive the identical channel, constraints and compute. Verify all stabilizer commutations, logical dimensions, code distances and exact channel-weighted decoder loss independently. Fresh channel perturbations test robustness. Hardware performance remains conditional on the channel model.

**Decision:** Do not select in this sprint without a concrete published bound that can plausibly be exceeded. No witness found. Physical manufacture is not needed for a digital claim, but it cannot validate actual hardware economics.

Primary sources:

- Su et al. (2025), Discovery of optimal quantum codes via reinforcement learning, Physical Review Applied 23, 034048. https://journals.aps.org/prapplied/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.23.034048 ; open preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06378
- Webster and Browne (2025), Engineering Quantum Error Correction Codes Using Evolutionary Algorithms. https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10206673/

## Commercial accounting boundary

For all three candidates, a human-led engineering-resource estimate can be modeled from explicit scope, hours, rates, verification, maintenance and acquisition costs. It is not willingness to pay. A novel mathematical construction has no observed selling price here. Revenue, paid acceptance and customer demand are unmeasured; no receipts occurred in this scout. A conventional tie can still permit an engineering service, but it does not establish incremental Operational Alpha or profitable skill compounding.

## Follow-on search rejected on scope/feasibility

Published small-design records in covering arrays, cubature and quantum codes offer exact verification, but no improved witness was found. Covering arrays chiefly improve ordinary testing efficiency, conflicting with the flagship criterion. Cubature record improvement needs numerical search plus exact or interval certification beyond the scouting window. This is a screening judgment, not a proof that these fields lack opportunities.
