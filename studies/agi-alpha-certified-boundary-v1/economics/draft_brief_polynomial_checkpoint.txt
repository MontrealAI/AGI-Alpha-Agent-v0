THE USEFUL RESULT

Partition the integers from 1 to n into complete residue classes, each containing at least two integers of the interval. The “heft” is the sum of the reciprocals of their moduli. Myerson asks whether any such partition, with distinct moduli, can have heft below 0.98. Our certificate establishes

h > 1891871140656049 / 1929687500000000 ≈ 0.9804028583.

It therefore answers the stated question negatively under the original definitions. The proof even permits repeated moduli. It preserves the requirements of complete classes, exact coverage and at least two hits. The known general lower bound √8−2 is approximately 0.8284; this certificate addresses the sharper requested threshold. We do not claim the best possible constant or universal novelty from a bounded literature search.

WHAT WAS CONSTRUCTED

A degree-20 rational polynomial assigns weights to interval positions. Every permitted residue class receives total weight no greater than its reciprocal modulus, while the sum of all position weights exceeds the stated constant. This converts an unbounded family of finite search problems into reusable proof obligations: 78 continuous polynomial patches, a bound covering all larger classes, and an exact identity covering every interval length. Established optimization and polynomial-verification methods support the construction. The scientific contribution is the particular certificate and its consequence, not an exclusive new solver.
