A question that now has a checkable answer

An unbounded search has become a finite, reusable proof. This study constructs an exact mathematical certificate that rules out a proposed family of low-heft congruence partitions for every interval length. A reader can check the result with ordinary Python, without a language model, a numerical optimizer, a wallet or a trusted prediction about future capability. The artifact is the useful outcome: a precise answer that subsequent researchers can inspect, challenge and build upon.

The problem is simple to state. Partition the integers from 11 to nn into complete residue classes, with each class containing at least two integers of that interval. A class with modulus mm contributes 1/m1/m to the partition’s heft. Gerry Myerson asks whether, with distinct moduli, such a partition can have heft below 0.980.98 (Myerson 2026; Myerson, Poon, and Simpson 2009). We establish the following computer-assisted theorem:

h=∑i1mi≥J:=1790256018258529=0.9805039606421744…>0.98. \boxed{\displaystyle h=\sum_i\frac{1}{m_i}\ge J:=\frac{17902560}{18258529} =0.9805039606421744\ldots>0.98.}

The strongest certificate was discovered by the fresh conventional challenge and strengthened by exact normalization in independent review. The proof covers every positive integer nn admitting a partition and even permits repeated moduli. It preserves complete residue classes, exact coverage and at least two hits per class. It does not apply to arbitrary selected arithmetic progressions, singleton classes or two-dimensional coverings. The inspected prior general lower bound is 8−2≈0.828427\sqrt8-2\approx0.828427, from Emanuel’s dissertation, Appendix C, Theorem C.1 (Emanuel 2011). The constant above is certified, not claimed optimal. A bounded literature audit did not locate this result; external mathematical review and a fuller priority assessment remain pending.

The enterprise conclusion is narrower than the scientific result. No customer bought the work; actual revenue and receipts are zero, and complete actual authoring cost is unknown. An explicit planning model for review and integration of this existing artifact is conditionally profitable with four compatible sponsors, but demand is unvalidated and financing is required. A competent conventional shared arrangement has the same economics. No measured Operational Alpha, superhuman specialist, profitable skill compounding or general discovery engine is established.

Abstract. We investigate whether a bounded, independently challenged discovery process can produce a new useful result rather than an architectural proposal or an attractive scenario. A diverse opportunity screen rejects generic novelty claims already addressed by established methods. A first exact-code construction lane fails to improve the strongest located comparators. A subsequent finite-cover investigation first constructs an exact degree-20 dual weight. A fresh conventional challenge independently discovers a simpler piecewise-linear weight; normalization by its exactly checked maximum gives the stronger final bound. Two separately implemented line-arrangement checkers and an analytic tail certify every allowed class, while cell integrals transfer the result to every finite interval length. Fresh tests examine additional classes, scope decisions and malformed or altered inputs. The earlier polynomial proof remains independently verified and reproducible. Failed numerical evidence is retained. Existing AGI ALPHA provenance and execution components are exercised locally without minting, live identity claims or financial activity. Complete conditional accounting separates operating surplus, full-project profit, funding needs, restricted deposits and service obligations. The result demonstrates a portable research capability and a concrete mathematical advance relative to inspected prior bounds. It does not demonstrate an exclusive algorithmic advantage or a commercially validated enterprise.

Discovering the opportunity

Purpose before application

The motivating opportunity was to make a consequential unanswered question yield an inspectable answer under available digital resources. The study did not begin with a prescribed customer workflow. It searched for targets with an external specification, an identifiable limitation, a strong conventional challenge, a plausible beneficiary and a decisive experiment. Mathematical construction became a viable lane because its first useful output can be checked digitally and its acceptance need not depend on a physical prototype or an unavailable archive.

For the selected problem, the limitation statement is:

Existing finite enumeration and finite integer-programming searches that find no qualifying cover cannot rule out one at a larger interval length: a cutoff leaves infinitely many lengths unresolved. An explicit universal weight certificate resolves that remaining possibility while preserving complete classes, exact partitioning and minimum class size. A finite search could, of course, settle existence by finding a valid witness.

This statement concerns the insufficiency of a finite search as a universal proof. It does not claim that conventional mathematics, an existing university or a research organization lacks the ability to discover such a proof. Duality, polynomial approximation and Bernstein verification are established tools. The scientific contribution is the explicit witness and resulting theorem.

The immediate beneficiary is the research community studying covering systems. A separate possible buyer could commission reproducibility, qualified review or integration of a fixed certificate. The former is a demonstrated intellectual use; the latter is a commercial hypothesis. We did not establish that the theorem has a near-term industrial application or that a particular organization is unable to perform this work.

A small, diverse screen and its conventional challenges

Six directions formed the initial screen. The detailed limitation statements and primary-source comparisons are in the evidence package.

Decision-directed experimental instruments. The proposed instrument would choose tests that settle a useful property without identifying an entire model. Decision Region Determination and Hyperedge Cutting already target that distinction, while exact dynamic programming is practical on small finite cases (Javdani et al. 2014). The generic mechanism did not survive the prior-art challenge.

Combining incomplete causal evidence. The proposed representation would return sharp intervention bounds and extremal compatible worlds from partial observations. Automated partial identification already supplies this core mechanism (Duarte et al. 2024). A convenient compiler alone would not establish a new productive possibility.

Combining specialists without training data. The proposal sought transferable capability by aligning and merging networks. Data-free, cycle-consistent model merging is direct prior art (Crisostomi et al. 2024). A new label or a successful merge would not establish economic Alpha or a stronger accepted outcome.

Runtime enforcement. The proposed component would preserve useful action while blocking violations of a formal specification. Shield synthesis is an established construction (Bloem et al. 2015). A new implementation was insufficient for the flagship criterion.

Exact zero-error communication codes. This lane had a concrete improvement target and independent digital verification. Strong published witnesses, MILP, local search and straightforward compositions were admitted. The executed searches reproduced known constructions but did not improve them. We stopped the record-improvement claim rather than changing the channel or weakening the comparator (Itty et al. 2026; Buys, Polak, and Zuiddam 2026; Tandon 2026).

Finite residue partitions. The public question supplied a fixed threshold and clear original requirements. Finite exact-cover search was attempted first. Its failure to find a witness suggested, without proving, a lower-bound direction. A certificate that applies uniformly to all lengths became the surviving research target (Myerson 2026).

Additional scouting considered quantum-code and small-design records. Those screens did not produce a new accepted witness and are preserved as exploratory notes. Screening conclusions are literature judgments, not executed capability benchmarks.

Why this became feasible in this study

The changed arrangement combines an accessible, recently refreshed public question; explicit computational definitions and prior examples; interactive frontier-model reasoning; inexpensive standard optimization; and exact independent verification. The 2026 public updates make the outstanding threshold easy to identify without inventing a benchmark. They do not make the mathematical question new (Myerson 2026). No unique instrument unavailable to other organizations is claimed.

The useful representational move is from “find a cover at larger nn” to “find one weight function that prices every permissible class.” A finite search can miss a distant witness. A valid universal weight converts the same original question into finitely checkable polynomial obligations plus a mathematical tail argument. This is a change in what the particular completed artifact establishes, not a claim that dual certificates were invented here.
