# Bounded portfolio screen: Discover the Work / Build the Advantage

Prepared 2026-10-26 by a same-session independent research agent. This is discovery input, not a selected-outcome report, customer interview, purchase order, or economic confirmation. No public writes, commercial outreach, paid commitments, credentials, or financial actions were performed. Active dependency-upgrade/metadata, graph, and thermal forward/inverse/design implementation lanes were excluded from proposed implementation ownership.

## Decision

The strongest two candidates for a bounded local production attempt are **API-version impact plus an executable migration packet** and **GTFS release qualification plus route/date impact**. Both have observable recurring technical triggers, public inputs, practical acceptance paths, and strong free incumbents. Neither has established paid demand for an AGI ALPHA offering. The actual useful output must go beyond reproducing an existing tool report: a proposed executable change, a precisely supported release decision, or a consumer-specific impact artifact is needed.

Do not build an original generic OpenAPI checker or GTFS validator merely to create a retained skill. Established tools already provide those capabilities. Retention is worth evaluating only for a specialized, tested adapter or composition whose reconstruction and integration burden is measured against those tools.

High human effort is a hypothesis until a competent human-only workflow is timed. A family can have high social/commercial usefulness while failing this study's local feasibility or differentiation gate.

## Proposed preregistered selection/stopping rules

These are proposed before execution and must be frozen by the lead before any confirmatory outcomes. They do not retroactively qualify results already seen.

1. Bound discovery to six families, one primary-source review per family and at most two development prototypes. Record all researcher/agent intervals and queries, including failed retrievals and source corrections. No assumed dollar invoice replaces unavailable actual inference cost.
2. Before construction, require named beneficiary class, a concrete decision enabled, pinned public inputs with permitted reuse, an acceptance specification and an executable strong incumbent. Demand evidence can be a public technical need; label actual demand, internal requirements and market hypotheses separately.
3. Select the top two feasible families above, subject to locally available data/tooling and active-lane ownership. If an input or incumbent cannot be acquired through authorized access in the discovery window, preserve that failure and move to the next family. Do not substitute a toy output and retain the original commercial label.
4. For each finalist, complete one useful development delivery. Reject a generic report that neither supports a precise release decision nor repairs a concrete consumer task. A negative/hold decision is useful only when backed by exact evidence and acceptance requirements.
5. For each retained asset, state the starting tools and gap. Independent recipient gets the same public tools, documentation/search and a declared reconstruction budget. At least one expert recipient should already know the domain; a tie is an expected negative control, not an excluded case.
6. Freeze source, scope, acceptance and costing before fresh cases. Prefer different releases/consumers/agencies rather than random copies of one construction example. Predeclare total case count and all exclusions. Preserve failures; no source repairs, case replacement or efficacy retries after seeing confirmatory outcomes.
7. Compare independent construction, shared registry and graph with exactly the same source/evidence/dependency rows. A competent indexed registry should support traversing dependencies. Any graph benefit must survive its full construction and maintenance cost. Zero graph gain is valid.
8. Cost the complete service: source acquisition, execution, retries, manual exceptions, verification, delivery packaging, support, setup, maintenance and discovery. Count construction once; distinguish elapsed agent authoring time from human labor. Unidentified actual monetary cost remains null/unknown.
9. Stop the scientific confirmation after the predeclared case budget. A failed candidate can motivate a separately registered successor, never a repaired primary result. Current task scope allows an honest useful-service result even when transfer or investment fails.

## Economic calibration inputs and limits

### Labor prices

Primary BLS Pay section for May 2025 reports **software developer median annual wages $135,980** and **software QA/tester median annual wages $104,300**. An annual/2,080 conversion yields approximately $65.375 and $50.144 per nominal hour; 2,080 is a modeling convention, not observed productive hours. Use these only as US wage anchors. The user is in Quebec, and US rates are not Quebec labor quotations.

The same BLS page has a combined quick-facts value of $134,040/$64.44 per hour. An early research message incorrectly treated that combined value as developer-specific; this was corrected before candidate selection. Preserve this correction in the discovery record.

June 2026 BLS employer compensation table reports all private workers at **$46.89 total compensation / $32.82 wages** and the combined management/professional group at **$78.88 total / $54.06 wages**. A multiplier derived from these is a cross-group modeled loading, not an observed software developer-specific cost. Avoid combining medians and means and presenting the result as empirically measured full cost. No rent, management or sales overhead is included unless modeled separately.

### Human time

No credible source found here measures complete human-only time for these exact prospective jobs. Do not assign “hours saved” from record counts, lines of code or agent wall time. Construct the matched manual workflow and either time competent delivery or report transparent scenarios. Scenarios such as 0.25/1/4 hours per accepted job are sensitivity assumptions, not confidence intervals or empirical bounds; they should not automatically become the favorable primary case.

### Demand, alternatives and price

Observed publisher update cadence is a job trigger, not a count of purchases. A weekly GTFS feed does not mean 52 sales. API releases do not establish buyer willingness to pay. Existing free tools cap what raw checking alone can command. Buyers must be able to decline or self-serve with those tools; service value must include integration, verified repair or interpretation that the buyer actually needs. Price and acquisition rate remain assumptions without actual quotations or transactions.

Recommended market stresses: zero/low recurring work; one-off onboarding; priced buyer review/integration; strong free incumbent; competent AI alternative; delay/no collection; copied retained skill; increased upkeep; scope drift forcing rejection. No skill premium or additional throughput should be introduced without measured evidence. CPU savings may be sub-cent and should remain so rather than be enlarged to make investment worthwhile.

### Investment identities

Customer avoided labor is not revenue. Count only chosen simulated purchases as modeled receivables/collections. Available investment funds must exclude seed principal, reserve, unpaid obligations and future collections. Keep project-inclusive historical discovery separate from deployment marginal economics. A capability is worthwhile only when its measured scope-adjusted benefit repays incremental acquisition/integration/validation/upkeep within its useful lifetime. Token utility cannot fund earnings through assumed appreciation.
