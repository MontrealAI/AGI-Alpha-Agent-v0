Their exact bytes were included in the published 61-file primary source freeze before outcomes. They lacked an in-file SPDX comment at that point.

No alternate unexecuted entry points were created, and no source hash or original qualification is relabeled. `licensing-qualification.json` binds their exact retained bytes and the published primary freeze.

Use the original qualified paths in `README.md`, `pretransfer-freeze.json` and `transfer-freeze.json` for reproduction.
