\section*{What was learned}
The study examined six serious opportunities before constructing one instrument. Native-seed purchases and substitutions can obscure original ecological requirements. NASEM documents that concern; established mix design, forward contracts and seed qualification provide strong alternatives. The research rejected those methods as flagship inventions. [1]

The instrument preserves the charter, distinguishes source coverage from qualified lot coverage, and reports unresolved identity, provenance, quantity, timing and seasonal/host obligations. Identical purchases with two different charters---flowering versus nectar evidence---produce different unmet requirements. This witnesses information loss, not autonomous intent discovery. Lots and commercial terms are fictional; the 21-taxon catalog contains attributed source facts. [2]

The development report correctly retains two blocked lot rows even when seasonal counts are sufficient. The actual frozen compiler then produces correct reports on \textbf{24 fresh cases}: eight positive documentary screens, thirteen valid negative reports and three invalid-input rejections. A distinct recipient agent uses its unchanged source on dry-context work and then wet-context work with a monarch-host requirement. [3]

\begin{boundary}[title=Finite success; broader defects]
Five selected post-trial diagnostics expose incomplete validation: omitted price, unsupported host, omitted source, empty lot identifier and a missing nectar field. The core remains frozen. The 24/24 result does not establish general contract compliance or production readiness. [4]
\end{boundary}

\section*{Economics before expansion}
\textbf{Actual sales and collections are zero. Complete actual research cost and profit are unknown.} Unpaid research cannot be described as customer-funded learning. The executable accounting model keeps A, complete paid delivery; B, additional retention; and C, optional development disjoint. It distinguishes deposits, liabilities, earned fees, receivables, collections, refunds, reserves and available funds. [6]

H remains the primary commercial reference, with an estimated three hours at USD 100/hour rather than a quote. The equal-three-hour allocation is an \emph{uncalibrated null control}, not automated delivery cost or required human review. Its USD 400 offer illustrates accounting. E+ spending gives expected contribution USD $-73.14$ and needs USD 457 against assumed USD 400 funds. Hypothetical joint construction gives USD $-742$ and needs USD 7,320 against USD 4,000. Actual evidence and participants are missing, so that project stops earlier. [6]

A transparent retention boundary illustrates the decision without inventing gain. At assumed fixed incremental spending of USD 100 and USD 2 extra serving cost per reuse, twenty-five qualifying reuses require \textbf{more than USD 6 gross benefit per reuse}. Neither that benefit nor the demand is measured. A profitable service could still be offered without investing in retention. [6]

\section*{Operating blueprint}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Offer a precise audit when a beneficiary values the decision.} Preserve the charter; compare with the recipient's competent process and existing tools. Use available solutions when they suffice.
\item \textbf{Repair and qualify before general live service.} Freeze a corrected version and test the five failure classes prospectively. Automate intake, checking and packaging; measure genuine residual effort.
\item \textbf{Fix quality, deadline, effort, price and payment conditions.} Seek a matched human quote or disclose estimated scope/rates. Let the buyer decline, defer or choose alternatives.
\item \textbf{Retain selectively; authorize separately.} Preserve version, dependencies, rights, provenance and scope. Recheck each mission. Exclude protected answers. Identity does not prove fitness or permission.
\item \textbf{Build technical evidence, participation and financing together.} Physical work needs source rights, viable material, actual participants, delivery evidence, failure allocation and a funded cash schedule.
\end{enumerate}

A separately frozen corrected investment selector passes six fresh hypothetical decision sets, choosing feasible alternatives before ranking contribution; this is policy validation, not actual profit.
