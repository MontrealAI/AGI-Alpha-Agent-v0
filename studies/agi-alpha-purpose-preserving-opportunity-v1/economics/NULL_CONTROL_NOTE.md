# Interpretation correction: an uncalibrated null-cost control

The frozen experiment assigns the same three estimated labor hours to H, U, E and E+ solely as a
mechanical null control that supplies no workflow advantage. **For U/E/E+, these hours are not a measured
or realistic delivery-cost estimate, a required human-review allowance, or a recommendation to insert
human labor into the automated workflow.** The human comparator is also an estimate, not a quote.

Consequently the selected USD 400 offer, modeled expected contributions and prefunding numbers illustrate
the accounting and stop policy under stated assumptions. They do not establish an operating price,
commercial feasibility, human-service competitiveness, Operational Alpha, or learned Alpha. In particular,
the default acceptance flag is an assumed conditional input, not independent beneficiary acceptance.

The original inputs, protocol and numerical results are preserved. Prospective cost analysis using measured
workflow traces, actual or explicitly assumed token use and genuine residual obligations belongs in a new
input/version. It cannot retroactively calibrate this null control. Zero actual sales and collections and
unknown complete actual research cost remain the observed commercial boundary.

The equal-information conventional-expert construction result is intentionally a logical tie by construction,
not an independently measured performance result. Its purpose is to prevent assigning the proposal credit
for established forward contracts or for evidence withheld from its comparator.
