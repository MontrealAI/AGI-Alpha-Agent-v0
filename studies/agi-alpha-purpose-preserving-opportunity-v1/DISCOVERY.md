# Opportunity search and selection history

Date: 27 October 2026. Author direction: Vincent Boucher, President of MONTREAL.AI.

This is a fresh same-session research team, not an externally independent institution. The initial
budget was six serious candidates and sixteen primary documents, followed by a three-document
targeted seed-procurement challenge. The three supplied foundations, exact data-access attempts,
task-definition references and official model documentation were additional declared inputs.
No paid API calls, field trials, customer contacts or external-resource purchases occurred.

## Selection result

The search **did not qualify a newly feasible ecological production business**. It rejected generic
forward contracting, seed mixing, demand aggregation, traceability and agricultural data brokering
as flagship novelty. Their existing methods are capable and belong in the expert baseline.

A narrower, falsifiable instrument hypothesis was retained for an executed decision-support study:
**preserve the original functional requirements that can disappear when a purchase record retains
only the accepted substitute, and return an inspectable report of remaining obligations**.

Native-seed procurement is the concrete case because NASEM identifies a purchase/substitution
feedback loop that fails to expand the species/ecotype range. The new artifact does not invent
that diagnosis. It turns an explicit original charter, documented taxon evidence and lot evidence
into a reusable source-screen procedure. Its contribution is assessed against competent workflows,
an independently written conventional checker and an ordinary library holding identical bytes.

The physical development proposal remains blocked where source identity, propagation yield,
rights or participants are unknown. Passing a software audit is not equivalent to restoring habitat.
The study therefore provides a complete inspectable lineage for an **audit artifact**, and a
justified stop for the stronger ecological and commercial claim. It must not be presented as a
successful native-seed invention after the broader opportunity was rejected.

## Transformation witness and falsifier

**Purpose:** deliver a correct, source-linked audit of the recipient's unchanged seasonal and host
requirements, proposed material and unresolved evidence. **Restriction:** a purchase-only record
omits the original purpose and can merge satisfied and unsatisfied needs into the same observed sale.
**Reason it is removable:** retain the explicit charter alongside the purchase and admissibility evidence.
**Replacement:** an executable obligation report with unique taxon counts, documentary resource scope,
lot identity, provenance, timing, units, aggregate capacity and fresh mission binding.
**Realization:** the actual `capability/obligation_compiler.py` and its versioned fact catalogue.
**Falsifier:** an erroneous audit, a lost obligation, false promotion of unknown evidence, no useful
benefit against a competent workflow, or no viable demand for the scoped audit service.

The compiler is researcher-constructed. The catalogue, original charter, normative evidence rules
and fictional lot facts are supplied inputs. It discovers no new biological fact, infers no customer's
private intent from sales and changes no conservation objective. The structural repair enumerator
only proposes combinations from the declared catalogue and labels their unresolved supply evidence.
