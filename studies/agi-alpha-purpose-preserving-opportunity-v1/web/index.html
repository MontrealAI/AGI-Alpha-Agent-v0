04 / Fair comparison

The baseline
is allowed to be strong.

Three separate same-session agents used the same model environment, tools, contract and catalog. U received a reasonable ordinary prompt; E used an explicit expert workflow; E+ also received the retained compiler. No score feedback was returned between packets.

No observed quality advantage. All three conditions produce correct reports. U is fastest across both packets in this small trial; E+ is faster than E, but a single run does not establish a reliable learned advantage or its complete cost.

Timing scope: first recorded command to verification, excluding unobserved dispatch latency. One actor per condition; different clock precision. No token or billing telemetry, human trial, matched human quote or externally validated customer-effort measurement.

H remains the primary commercial reference. The accounting control uses an explicit estimated service-hours scenario. It is not a sourced market quotation and cannot establish a competitive price or profit.

05 / Retain only what can earn its upkeep

Transfer is observed.
Its value is conditional.

A useful artifact is not automatically an economical investment. Explore the break-even boundary under explicit assumptions. Do not convert observed wall times into labor savings or assume ordinary recipients must rebuild their own tools for every job.

07 / The operating blueprint

A concrete next offer.
A strict admission boundary.

A scoped documentary audit is the next offering to validate. The recipient must decide whether its report is useful and worth paying for. Ecological construction remains a separate, unqualified proposal.

Offer

Audit the original requirement

Obtain the original charter, source references and supplier evidence. Agree on the useful decision, accuracy, deadline, effort burden and price beforehand. Seek a matched human-service quote.

Deliver

Automate checks; resolve real unknowns

Run the scoped compiler, inspect exceptions and verify evidence needed for the actual decision. Charge complete delivery once, including necessary adaptation and corrections. Do not insert arbitrary review time.

Retain

A versioned executable, not a promise

Retain rights-cleared code and annotations with hashes, scope, provenance, dependencies and limits. Exclude protected evaluation material from the shared capability. Compare against the recipient's competent existing workflow.

Admit or stop

Require independent usefulness and cash evidence

Confirm buyer acceptance and willingness to pay, actual lot quality and provenance, participation, refund duties and financing. Grant mission-specific authority only after the existing protected network's admission process accepts the capability.
