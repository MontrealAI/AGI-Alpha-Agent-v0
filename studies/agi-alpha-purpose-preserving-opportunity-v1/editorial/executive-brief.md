### What this edition establishes

This edition delivers an executable instrument that preserves a procurement mission's original requirements, identifies unresolved evidence, and transfers unchanged to materially different fresh work. It also reports a necessary stop: the investigated ecological production opportunity has not been shown newly feasible or commercially viable. The bounded workflow experiment finds correct performance from ordinary use, expert operation and expert operation with the retained instrument. It does not establish compounding, an additional capability advantage, or earned profit. [1–4]

The distinction matters. A system can produce a useful, correct negative audit while the physical project remains blocked. An accepted research report is a completed software task; it is not beneficiary acceptance, habitat restoration, a sale, or permission to act.

### The connected example

Restoration procurement supplied a consequential need: preserve seasonal resources, appropriate source identity and other original requirements when available purchases or substitutions fail to meet them. The opportunity search examined six materially different candidates. It rejected generic mix optimization, forward contracting, demand aggregation and traceability as flagship inventions because established methods already address those functions. NASEM's seed-supply assessment provided the narrower concern: purchasing and substitution records can conceal the species and ecotypes originally wanted. [1, 7]

The constructed witness holds the purchase record constant while changing the explicit original charter from documented flowering coverage to documented nectar coverage. The resulting obligations differ. A purchase-only representation therefore cannot determine whether either original requirement was satisfied. This is an information-loss demonstration on constructed examples, not a discovery that procurement professionals lack their clients' charters. [2]

The retained compiler joins that charter with a small, attributed source catalogue and fictional lot evidence. It counts unique qualifying taxa, preserves each seasonal and host requirement, aggregates comparable demand, and reports missing identity, evidence, provenance, capacity or timing. Unknown evidence stays unknown. A source-screen pass never becomes field validation or production authority. [2, 5]

The founder's report correctly retains two blocked lot rows even though its seasonal counts are sufficient. The frozen compiler then produces correct reports on all **24 fresh cases** authored by a separate same-session evaluator after the method freeze: eight positive source screens, thirteen valid negative audits and three invalid inputs. Fresh work changes the documented habitat context, provenance and host requirement. This demonstrates executable transfer within the declared grammar and catalogue. It does not establish effectiveness across ecological settings. [2, 3]

### Competent baselines change the conclusion

All model workflow arms received the same evidence, task contract, tools and **480-second limit per packet**. Ordinary use could write and retain code; expert operation used explicit normalization and verification; E+ received the actual frozen compiler. No external scores or corrections from the first packet were supplied before the second. [4]

E+ is faster than E in these executions; U is faster overall. Both U and E also reuse scripts they constructed during the first packet. No configuration creates a new feasible task under the common quality and deadline conditions. Complete model bills and genuine residual customer effort remain unmeasured. H, the equivalent human service, is an explicitly estimated commercial reference rather than an executed trial or market quote. [4, 6]

### Economics: account completely before investing

Actual customer sales and collections are **zero**. Complete actual research cost is unknown. The study therefore cannot report actual profit, available cash from earned surplus, or customer-funded learning. [6]

The executable economic model keeps A, complete paid delivery; B, additional retention and serving; and C, optional development, disjoint. Its 224 conditional evaluations include declining, withdrawing, correction, shared failure, partial delivery, lateness and default. Refundable deposits remain liabilities and reserves until accepted delivery. A positive expected contribution does not remove a cash shortfall. [6]

Its frozen equal-labor assumption is an **uncalibrated null control**, not an estimate of automated delivery or a requirement for human review. The resulting USD 400 offer is not a validated price. Under those inputs, added capability spending makes E+ lose USD 73.14 in expected complete contribution and require USD 457 against USD 400 available. Even a hypothetical evidence-sufficient pooled construction case has negative expected contribution and insufficient prefunding. These calculations test accounting and stopping rules, not demand or commercial performance. [6]

A separate transparent boundary illustrates the next decision. With assumed fixed incremental spending of USD 100 and serving cost of USD 2 per reuse, 25 reuses require **more than USD 6 gross value per reuse** to create positive incremental value. The experiment does not measure that dollar benefit. Retain the artifact as research evidence; defer further capability investment until a prospective measurement supports it. [6]

### Operating blueprint and next gate

Offer a precisely scoped documentary audit only if a prospective buyer values it. Capture the original charter and acceptance terms before processing. Automate structured intake, checks and packaging; measure only genuine unresolved obligations and corrections. Compare against the buyer's competent current process, including its existing reusable tools. Determine quality, deadline, customer effort and price conditions in advance. Obtain a matched human-service quote or disclose the estimated scope and rate. Record the complete bill, collections, liabilities, support and reserves separately.

Retain versioned source, catalogue provenance, dependencies, rights and scope. Recheck each new mission's meaning. The existing native journal, trust and cost interfaces pass **18 integration checks**, including fresh mission authorization and revocation; these use local fixture identities and do not authenticate live `*.agent.agi.eth` agents. Unsupported production admission remains refused. [5]

The next decisive evidence is independently accepted work for an actual beneficiary, measured against that beneficiary's baseline with complete costs and genuine willingness to pay. Physical seed construction additionally needs verified biological evidence, rights, participants and financing. Until then, this edition supplies a reusable research instrument and a defensible stopping decision, preserving the stronger ambition for a future test.
