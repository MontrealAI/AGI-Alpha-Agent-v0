## Evidence classes

| Label | Meaning in this edition |
|---|---|
| Observed | An execution, output, file identity, timing or test result actually recorded in this session. Constructed inputs do not become observed real-world facts. |

| Simulated | A conditional result generated from those assumptions and deterministic rules. |

## Workflow comparison wording

Use: “All three observed workflow conditions produced 24 correct reports within the common 480-second-per-packet limit. E+ reused unchanged source; U and E also retained their newly constructed scripts. E+ was faster than E on these executions; U was fastest overall. Complete costs and customer effort were unavailable.”

Do not use: “The specialist created a new feasible class of work,” “expert prompting beat ordinary users,” “the graph caused transfer,” or “E+ produced a measured return on investment.”

E minus U accepted-count difference is zero. E+ minus E accepted-count difference is zero. E+ total measured interval is 27.814989 seconds shorter than E, and 8.343284 seconds longer than U. These descriptive differences have no replication-based uncertainty estimate. The three actors share a model family and execution environment; published API pricing is not their measured bill.

## Contract and data limits to disclose

The catalogue contains 21 selected source-derived taxa. It is not a survey of current supply, ecological suitability or nectar yield. Documented mix context is narrower than a universal habitat classification. Most nectar entries are unknown, and the pollen-only exception has an explicit source. Lots, provenance regions, quantities, dates and prices are researcher-constructed. Public workbook download attempts returned HTTP403, so no workbook reanalysis or original 196-mix reanalysis is claimed.

All proposed rows are evaluated under the single declared resource mode. The scope addendum was supplied before fresh evaluation; mixed nectar-support and host-only roles need a new grammar. The frozen compiler also has broader-contract validation gaps beyond the evaluated packets: missing quoted price defaults to zero, host strings have broader acceptance than the protocol, source presence is not fully validated, some numeric/list limits differ, and empty lot identifiers are not normalized in emitted blockers. Preserve those limits and do not call the frozen artifact a general production validator.

The source inspection integration and the actual compiler transfer are separate observations. They must not be combined into a claim that live Jobs validators admitted ecological work. Cryptographic validity is neither semantic truth nor authority.

## Productive frontier and next evidence

The observed feasible audit set is the same for U, E and E+. No physical ecological opportunity enters the established feasible set. No new profitable enterprise has been admitted. The versioned instrument and reproducibility package remain reusable research outputs; discretionary further investment is stopped until measured benefit and demand justify it.
