# Independent review of the single confirmation campaign

## Finding

The recorded experiment is internally consistent and its primary finding is negative for incremental flywheel superiority. All 1,344 recorded outputs satisfy their independent source-byte or archive-drift oracle. The proposed flywheel nevertheless used **5.74% more complete pipeline CPU** than the strongest direct specialist on the paired geometric-mean estimand; the 95% bootstrap interval corresponds to **2.46% to 8.99% more CPU**. The preregistered requirement was an upper ratio bound below 0.95, so `HOLD_FLYWHEEL_SUPERIORITY` is the correct decision.

This review did not rerun the benchmark, modify protected data, or tune frozen source. It inspected every recorded output and independently reconstructed the primary analysis.

## Lineage and the distinct child

The four arms that instantiate bound assets produced 96 child instances in total. Each child executable matches the frozen constructor template with its actual accepted prior-read receipt and actual parent source hash. Each recorded acceptance binds the complete checked read outputs, exact input commit and oracle identity. The retained parent bytes, child bytes and recorded dependencies match their identities.

All **384 bound parent-use records** match the accepted prior read, requested paths, exact source commit and returned source inventory. The flywheel arm accounts for **24 child instances and 96 parent invocations**. These are repeated instances of one predeclared child design, not 24 or 96 independently discovered algorithms.

The child provides a distinct useful interface: complete archive-versus-commit drift diagnosis. Its construction composes prewritten mechanisms and binds accepted executable content. This experiment demonstrates that this composition can execute correctly on fresh instances; it does not establish that autonomous construction discovered a new algorithm or reduced the effort of improving software relative to the strongest practical method.

Zero bound-parent traces in the direct or ordinary-tooling arms does not mean those arms perform no software reuse. Their independently available reader functions are legitimate tools. The causal question concerns the incremental proposed asset/lineage process, not whether function calls or saved software are useful in general.

## Secondary comparisons

Some secondary paired ratios against reconstruction and equivalent content fall below one. They must remain reported, but they do not establish a unique ancestry benefit:

- Exact reconstruction and proposed retention ultimately execute the same reader bytes; template access was legitimately available. Differences can include materialization paths and ordinary timing variation.
- The independent equivalent uses separately authored code with equivalent content, so implementation details can affect cost without establishing a lineage effect.
- The conventional reusable-tool arm follows the same proposed construction mechanism, and its interval relative to the flywheel crosses one.
- The primary comparison favors the strong direct specialist. The ordinary history arm is also cheaper than the proposed flywheel on the recorded paired estimate.
- These secondary intervals are not corrected for simultaneous multiple comparisons and should be interpreted as secondary conditional observations.

A favorable secondary contrast cannot replace the failed primary gate or prove that ancestry itself supplies useful information.

## Limits and claim boundaries

The audit is internal role separation in a shared workspace. Source-bound in-process traces are inspectable evidence, not hardware attestation, an external laboratory replication, or OS-isolated evaluation. The frozen candidate code visibly calls its bound parent and uses the returned bytes, but the review does not claim independently captured system-call provenance.

There is one host, one public source ecosystem and one controlled generator. The 24 units are fresh generated repository instances, not 24 naturally sampled repositories. Four calls within each phase are repetitions within an instance; they do not raise the statistical sample size to 96. The shared OS page cache was not flushed. Fresh-process observations are not physical disk-cold results.

The trial compares bounded deterministic construction and execution. It does not compare autonomous language-model agents. It does not prove general capability growth, an increasing rate of improvement, self-financing research, monetary profit, or a continuously operating enterprise.

Observed operational CPU and elapsed cost are distinct from complete research investment. Human, model, integration and maintenance costs remain incompletely measured. The useful original call-site improvement can be considered independently of the negative incremental flywheel result, subject to its own code review and release process.

The storage-only prospective amendment preserved every scientific file hash and its earlier boundary. It does not imply durable-saving success or authorize bypassing a blocked upload. Local completion, durable preservation, owner review, merge, deployment and verified public access remain separate statuses.
