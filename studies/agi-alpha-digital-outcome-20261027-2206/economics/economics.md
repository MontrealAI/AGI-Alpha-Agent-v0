# Economics: a useful public result, a conditional service case

**Edition DIGITAL-20261027-R1. All amounts are nominal USD. No commercial revenue, provider bill or financing commitment was observed.**

The experiment accepts one inspectable public source/CLI/test/evidence package. It does not establish a paid customer delivery. Its three arms each pass the 64 fresh numerical/status cases in this one mission. Those cases are not 64 customers. The commercially relevant question is whether a buyer will pay for a bounded qualification and handoff of this same package in its own environment, including support and explicit limits. That additional buyer-specific work has not been performed or accepted by a customer; its economics below are conditional. The unmodified package remains available as a free outside option. No license, exclusivity, numerical discovery or lock-in fee is assumed.

A recipient can already use the delivered package to obtain the corrected bounded scalar quantiles, inspect the evidence, and decide whether to integrate or reject it. A scientific-software group with enough numerical expertise can reuse this package, current Boost and ordinary arbitrary precision itself. A buyer with a valuable blocked decision but little available qualification time may instead value a scoped service. The difference is a testable demand hypothesis, not evidence that established organizations cannot do the work.

## What is included, and what is not sold

One proposed engagement delivers the existing bounded scalar API, compiler/dependency recipe, acceptance replay, explicit unsupported/invalid handling, evidence and a documented handoff for one declared buyer environment. The fee also covers bounded correction/support. It promises neither arbitrary SciPy compatibility nor vectorization, global mathematical certification, upstream acceptance, physical performance or a hosted numerical service. Customer-specific environment changes beyond the bounded contract require a new scope; they cannot silently weaken the original acceptance.

The source and numerical tests run automatically; no recurring human review per scalar solve is invented. Human time below estimates additional contract scoping, environment integration, recipient verification/sign-off, handoff and support that would exist only for a future paid engagement. None is described as observed labor in the present automated experiment. The current experiment's actual interventions and failures remain in the three run manifests, including root evaluator correction and expert packaging recovery. Their complete cost is unknown, not zero.

## Cost model and accounting boundaries

Let **M** be the unknown additional model/tool bill per future accepted buyer engagement, inclusive of all its failed attempts and retries. M may be zero if integration needs no model. It is the same sensitivity variable for ordinary, expert and conventional operation because there is no observed bill or general delivery-cost advantage. The construction wall times and tool counts are not token or dollar meters.

Let **D** be the complete historical cost of this whole research/development/publication project, including all three arms, failed approaches, selection, independent evaluation, artifact production and coordination. D is unknown. Charge it once when assessing full-project return; do not charge it again per buyer or create a second bill for learning already paid for. The earlier kinetics sprint is a separately preserved project, not a new incremental cash expense of this offering.

For each scenario, expected failed attempts per accepted engagement are p/(1−p). Their scoped human cost appears once in delivery; M already includes their model charges. Failed acceptance earns no final fee. Additional post-acceptance refunds reduce expected revenue; payment fees are conservatively charged on the original price. Support includes routine correction; the cash reserve covers one additional full refund and a stated extra correction beyond expectation. A reserve remains cash, not an operating expense. It is not enough to guarantee survival of multiple correlated failures.

Operating profit deducts successful and unsuccessful delivery, verification, correction/support, acquisition, infrastructure, payment expenses, maintenance and fixed business costs. Additional commercial setup is separate once-only work on service contracting/onboarding, not recreation of the delivered solver, CLI, tests or documentation. Full-project return additionally subtracts this setup and D. No tax, tax relief, loan proceeds or investment proceeds are counted as operating income. Results are before income tax and financing charges; no debt is assumed. If borrowing is required, subtract its actual fees/interest before declaring viability.

**M=$100 is a sensitivity coordinate, not a measured or inferred model bill.** In the central case, operating profit is positive only when M < $679.075. At M=$100, the operating break-even price is $890.45, and $1,022.03 also recovers additional commercial setup before historical D. Two accepted buyers cover the stated fixed operating cost at the central price and M=$100; this arithmetic does not establish that two buyers exist. Adverse value assumptions support no purchase, so the model does not force an economically inconsistent sale. The adverse loss assumes commercial setup and fixed costs were committed before discovering zero demand. A disciplined conditional-commitment gate could avoid those avoidable new costs, preserving the existing useful free artifact. If a low-price adverse sale nevertheless occurred, its unit contribution before M would be negative $1,347.80; volume would worsen the result.

## Value, participation and willingness to pay

Prices are independent hypotheses. They are not a fixed discount from a fictional market price or an optimization result presented as demand. The recipient's strongest outside option is free package reuse plus its own qualification, or a competent conventional implementation using current libraries.

Central modeled willingness to pay is **8 h × $150 + $600 avoided delay − 1 h × $150 buyer participation = $1,650**. A $1,500 fee leaves only $150 modeled buyer surplus. The $600 delay value and eight hours are unmeasured and consequential. At just two hours of competent free reuse with no valued delay, paid service may have no attractive value. The favorable hypothesis uses 24 self-service hours at $175, $2,000 avoided delay and one participation hour, giving a $6,025 ceiling; the adverse hypothesis uses two self-service and two participation hours with zero delay value, giving a zero ceiling. None measures actual willingness to pay. There are no buyer interviews, signed commitments or receipts in this study.

The executable price grid holds central costs and assumed four buyers fixed solely for sensitivity: $800 is below operating break-even, $1,000 is above it, $1,500 is near the hypothesized buyer ceiling, and $1,800 exceeds that ceiling. It does not keep four real buyers magically present above their valuation. An empirical price test would need conditional commitments or customer procurement evidence. Those are the missing evidence, not an instruction to infer demand from solver accuracy.

Extra buyer participation is useful only if it preserves acceptance and lowers total resource cost. If a buyer supplies one additional accepted setup hour, replacing a $125 provider hour but costing the buyer $150, no discount can both compensate the buyer and preserve provider cost neutrality before fee effects. The central arrangement therefore does not assume this discount creates value. More favorable division of work requires evidence about the buyer's actual comparative advantage. Verification requirements remain unchanged.

## Cash and financing

The cash model pays setup/fixed business cash at day 0, acquisition and pre-acceptance work at each job start, support at day 7, and net collections at day 35. Maintenance is paid 20 days after the last collection. Thus profitability does not imply enough early cash. Historical D is excluded from new working-capital needs only to the extent it was already paid; any unpaid development payable must be added separately.

At central M=$100, peak expected cash deficit is **$2,091.25**. A separately held **$1,875** reserve covers one additional $1,500 full refund plus three extra correction hours at $125. Required unrestricted funding is therefore **$3,966.25**. Available unrestricted funds, actual reserve balances, credit and financing commitments are unknown. No capital limit is raised to force feasibility.

A conditional staged contract can lower that threshold to **$3,476.55**: 30% becomes earned and paid after a separately useful, independently accepted qualification milestone (reproduced defect, supported scope and executable acceptance harness); 70% follows final accepted delivery. All numerical and handoff requirements, refund exposure and reserve remain. Both payment stages incur the proportional processing assumption plus a fixed $0.30; this adds $1.20 to the four-buyer cohort, reducing its return by $1.20. This is a potential contract arrangement, not observed buyer agreement, legal approval or financing. A payment merely described as a refundable deposit stays restricted and contributes **zero** operating capital. A conditional commitment can reduce demand uncertainty but provides no cash by itself.

The reserve protects the specified stress, not every imaginable failure. Two additional central full refunds require $3,000, exceeding the one-refund component by $1,500. Correlated failures or delayed collections remain material risks. Apart from its explicit $1.20 additional transaction expense in the central cohort, staging changes timing, not the underlying delivery margin. It does not erase obligations or convert an expected cash profile into certainty. A finance provider's costs, if used, must fit inside remaining margin and be added once.

## Matched conventional sharing and complementarity

All three arms delivered the same accepted fresh-case counts. The experiment gives no basis for charging an expert-operation premium, assigning it lower model bills, or denying a conventional team the same reusable public code. Its smaller observed solve CPU is a tiny-batch engineering observation, not a monetized economic advantage. Construction wall time was longer in the expert trial, and provider billing is unknown.

As a transparent accounting counterfactual at central M=$100, four isolated engagements each bearing the full cohort business/setup cost produce **−$2,083.70 before D**. Ordinary reuse/sharing produces **+$1,816.30 before D**. An equally capable conventional shared arrangement obtains the same result. The four matched values are conventional isolated −$2,083.70; expert operation alone isolated −$2,083.70; conventional shared +$1,816.30; expert plus shared +$1,816.30. Interaction attributable to expert operation is zero under the explicitly equal costs. Sharing known fixed costs is not a technical breakthrough, novel institution, observed market experiment or exclusive Alpha. Every buyer receives the same public rights; none must finance an exclusive asset, and participant incentives remain contingent on positive private surplus.

No retained capability, gym training or reinvestment expense is placed on the critical path. Retention beyond ordinary library reuse has no demonstrated incremental benefit here. The delivered code and test assets can be retained as ordinary engineering without creating a second charge for their already incurred learning.

## Human development cost, separately from buyer price

For attempting a new bounded repair of comparable scope from the same public recipe, an experienced human service team might spend 2–4 h reproducing and reviewing the source, 4–12 h adapting established solvers, 6–16 h independently checking precision/edge cases, and 2–6 h on build/CLI/documentation: **14–38 hours**, or **$1,400–$6,650** at the scenario rate endpoints. A 24 h × $125 = $3,000 central attempt is a planning estimate, not an observed human benchmark, a market quote, a guaranteed success, or the price of the already free artifact. Difficult undiscovered defects could require more. This hypothetical first-development bill is never added to future per-buyer service costs or to actual D. A conventional team that reuses the released artifact should receive the same reuse advantage.

## Decision and evidence replacement

**Engineering delivery is supported; commercial delivery at a paid price is not yet observed.** The complete conditional service model is plausible under its central demand, effort and billing thresholds, but the free outside option, narrow buyer surplus and missing cost metering prevent an actual-profit claim. Preserve the useful package. Do not purchase extra training or speculative infrastructure on these figures. A paid qualified engagement can be pursued only if its scope, willingness to pay, cost meter, acceptance, reserve and funding are concretely satisfied.

Replace M with metered provider/host charges including retries; replace D with the complete historical ledger; replace hours/failure/refund assumptions with buyer-delivery logs; replace buyer valuations with commitments and procurement behavior; replace funds with unrestricted bank/finance evidence and restricted balances; replace contract timing with accepted obligations. The exact runnable scenarios, cash events and sensitivity are in `economics.py` and `economics_results.json`. Actual observations and unknowns are separately recorded in `historical_cost_record.json`.
