The manuscript already identifies the evaluation as retrospective unfamiliar-task
work on public historical data, with procedural protection on a shared host.
It does not claim an independent industrial sample, physical replication or
protection against model pretraining contamination. The prospective protocol
explicitly identifies its four cases as new requests on previously used sources.

retrospective inspection and reproducibility. Replaying them cannot supply fresh
confirmation for future claims. Future improvement claims require new protected
evidence or requests, frozen before evaluation, with any reuse of the same public
