## The result

We constructed a reusable manufacturing inspection-evidence instrument and
transferred it to a fresh recipient with new mission checks and current local
research authority. It leaves the enterprise better equipped to trace claims,
find counterexamples and specify missing evidence. **This edition does not
establish a profitable new market, measured Operational Alpha, or commercial
compounding.** Its strongest commercial result is a prospective decision to
preserve cash and use the existing expert alternative.

The selected opportunity is consequential and bounded: a receiving-quality
engineer needs to know whether another provider's inspection evidence supports
its obligations, and a successor needs an exact evidence-acquisition plan for
investigation. Existing digital-thread tools are strong alternatives. The new
instrument addresses an inspectable cross-provider semantic problem, not the
invention of STEP, QIF, geometry matching or inspection automation.

## Exploration changed the direction

Ten candidate directions and challenges are preserved. The team investigated
software failures, scientific dependency/evaluation problems, causal evidence,
robotic rheology, manufacturing qualification, thermal metrology and laboratory
portability before selecting the flagship. The familiar microgrid work remains
a historical fixture.

A particularly useful negative pilot reproduced an old Dask/Zarr ZIP corruption
report but found that current software passed. That proposed repair opportunity
was rejected rather than promoted into a large simulator. Thermal examples also
lost their proposed gap when known symmetry and manufacturing information were
included. The selected manufacturing direction survived because actual NIST/MTC
files supplied both a complete geometry/result trace and concrete mismatches
that an audit must resolve.

## What was built

The instrument reads exact QIF 2.1 sources, preserves their literal units,
tolerances and statuses, constructs geometric witnesses from actual AP242 CAD,
checks interval implications and recorded values, and binds repair requests to
specific source obligations. It distinguishes numerical records, physical
families, individual features and part release.

Plate development imported a valid solid with **261 faces** and constructed
**17 requirement–feature–geometry–result traces**. A recorded diameter of
**3.017 mm** passes its local interval while the containing inspection remains
**FAIL**. That distinction survives the report and transfer.

Exact QIF 2.1 rules make the FAIR files' undeclared linear units formally meters;
incoming inspection explicitly uses millimeters. A plausible millimeter repair
is therefore a separately labeled hypothesis, never an automatic correction.
On fresh Box data, **76 values** include an observed violation of the receiving
interval even under that hypothesis. On Cover, **400 selected values** satisfy
the numerical interval, but the source records do not establish coverage of the
required physical family. A permutation-invariant record result does not locate
a hole for rework or release a part.

## Economics: positive modeled delivery is not incremental Alpha

No customer sale or matched engineering-service quote was obtained. Human-service
prices are explicitly estimated from team hours and rates. Market needs, values,
quality, costs and payment events are assumptions. **Actual collections and earned
revenue are zero; complete actual research cost is unknown.**

The frozen model searches price, buyer subset and development terms against
outside options, rights, quality, capacity and cash. It does not impose a fixed
discount, investment count, coalition or training budget. Its central 80 assumed
horizons yield:

| Configuration | Feasible / 80 | Mean, USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Expert, standard | 74 | 2,324.09 |
| Expert, arranged | 75 | 2,476.92 |
| Retained, standard | 27 | 492.72 |
| Retained, arranged | 38 | 771.24 |

Across eleven declared stresses, the combined package creates **zero new
feasibility crossings against the expert standard baseline**. All central
combined-feasible cases already qualify under that alternative. A prospective
six-horizon retention campaign estimates USD2,220.94 benefit against USD2,950
additional construction, retention and transfer cost, and is rejected. Forced
counterfactual construction ends at mean incremental value **−USD1,049.79**;
it is not recovered profit.

The financing model assumes acceptance of its development/failure-record
milestone. An independent later audit stresses rejection of that milestone and
full advance refund after committed work. It identifies **114 of 1,889** original
selected commitments with insufficient opening cash under that additional
contingency. These are conditional exposures, not observed insolvencies. The
original numbers remain unchanged, with their narrower assumption explicit.

## An operating blueprint

**Offer:** a precisely scoped source-comparability and evidence-acquisition
assessment. Price new measurements, authoritative corrections and release
support separately. Determine the recipient's real configured outside option.

**Deliver:** automate inventory, literal checks, geometry traces and reproducible
witnesses; independently verify material claims; ask for the smallest missing
item; measure actual corrections, customer interaction and support.

**Retain:** generic executable readers, proof policies and adapters only when
rights, scope, fresh acceptance and expected reuse justify additional costs.
Keep customer secrets, restricted IP and protected answers outside the package.

**Finance:** state milestone outcomes, private adaptations, risk, refunds,
responsibilities and collection dates. An advance is not earned surplus.
Participation and liquidity are separate tests. Preserve cash when a proposal
fails either one.

**Share:** use the existing protected network, eligible membership and current
mission-specific authority. Access does not confer unrestricted execution or
independent possession of a hosted specialist. No production scope was changed.

**Next decisive evidence:** unrelated industrial packets; actual human/integrator
quotes or trials; buyer-specific rights and source corrections; complete costs;
accepted contracts; and authorized production qualification. No Gym campaign or
specialist ASI claim is warranted by the current evidence.
