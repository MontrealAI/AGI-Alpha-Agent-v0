## 1. How to read the evidence

**Observed** means a source-processing, program, test, model-recipient or interface
execution actually occurred in this workspace and has a receipt. It does not
mean physical validation, customer acceptance or live identity authentication.
**Sourced** means an attributable external statement or supplied artifact.
**Assumed** means a researcher-selected premise. **Simulated** means a result of
executing a model with those premises. No commercial quantity in this edition is
calibrated to a customer cohort. Unknown cost or telemetry is explicitly null.

The headline result is intentionally split: an actual reusable instrument and
fresh transfer exist; initial failures are retained; the operating-method trial
shows no positive acceptance advantage; and modeled retention is rejected.
There is no earned revenue, live part release or evidence of commercial compounding.

## 5. Supplied ingredients versus constructed work

**Supplied:** the public manufacturing experiment, schemas, CAD engine, existing
integrated graphs, known interval/permutation mathematics, ordinary tools,
researcher-defined mission requests, evaluator criteria, model priors and finite
commercial search menu.

**Actually constructed:** exact-source readers and reports; actual CAD candidates
and traces; explicit unit/sign/identity findings; interval and assignment
witnesses; a source-bound acquisition plan; a versioned operating method; an
independent checker; an actual received-code invocation through existing
interfaces; and complete hypothetical cash/accrual accounting.

**Not established:** autonomous buyer discovery, novel geometric algorithms,
physical metrology, production admission, current ENS identity, learning to
invent more efficiently, population reliability, a new paying market or earned
commercial Alpha. An inherited stronger artifact is not proof of an improved
discovery policy.

## 6. Transformation witness and unit rule

The exact QIF 2.1 schema supplies the formal default: absent per-value and
applicable primary linear units mean meters. Incoming files declare millimeters.
The apparent plausibility of small dimensions cannot silently repair a source.
A hypothetical millimeter interpretation is evaluated in a separate result.

For a common interval predicate, reordering the same selected records leaves
its universal truth unchanged. The executable witness either checks every
record or emits a concrete violating value. Interval inclusion separately checks
whether the source requirement entails the destination requirement. Complete
physical-family coverage, specimen relations, equivalent standards, measurement
adequacy and current authority remain separate guards.

The five supported purposes are evidence audit, all selected recorded values,
complete family, feature localization and physical release. Only the first two
can sometimes be resolved without a physical bijection. A family certificate
does not identify a hole; a local PASS does not release a part. The actual Plate
trace contains a 3.017 mm diameter within its local interval and an overall FAIL
in the parent result.

The record-only purpose was distinguished during development, before core
freeze. The stronger already-curated requests retained their original purposes.
No successful weaker statement was substituted for a failed stronger contract.
