\hypertarget{how-to-read-the-evidence}{%
\subsection{1. How to read the
evidence}\label{how-to-read-the-evidence}}

\textbf{Observed} means a source-processing, program, test,
model-recipient or interface execution actually occurred in this
workspace and has a receipt. It does not mean physical validation,
customer acceptance or live identity authentication. \textbf{Sourced}
means an attributable external statement or supplied artifact.
\textbf{Assumed} means a researcher-selected premise. \textbf{Simulated}
means a result of executing a model with those premises. No commercial
quantity in this edition is calibrated to a customer cohort. Unknown
cost or telemetry is explicitly null.

The headline result is intentionally split: an actual reusable
instrument and fresh transfer exist; initial failures are retained; the
operating-method trial shows no positive acceptance advantage; and
modeled retention is rejected. There is no earned revenue, live part
release or evidence of commercial compounding.

The dossier covers ten serious directions/challenges. Each records
beneficiary, useful outcome, observed need versus conjecture, prior art,
surviving gap, construction, decisive experiment, falsifier and
commercial uncertainty. Ordinal scoring is researcher judgment, not
statistical evidence. The actual research budget excludes paid API
procurement, purchases, customer contact and hardware access;
hosted-model cost and human effort are not observable.

Two small viability checks mattered. Thermal examples often became
identifiable using established symmetry or construction information, so
they did not support the proposed flagship. The runtime pilot reproduced
a reported older software failure but passed the same path on the
authoritative current versions. Those negative findings were retained
before the manufacturing direction was selected.

\texttt{dossier/\allowbreak{}runtime\_\allowbreak{}pilot/\allowbreak{}} preserves the original bounded
attempts, known-version controls and unresponsive initial attempts
without inventing a causal explanation. No gain is credited to the team
for an upstream software fix. Unused weather and reanalysis snapshots
are marked unused; no scientific outcomes are fabricated.

The selected manufacturing gate required one complete
source-to-geometry/result trace and a useful assertion beyond querying
the supplied incoming graph. The Plate pilot met that construction gate.
It did not establish demand, willingness to pay or superiority over
existing commercial integrators.

\hypertarget{supplied-ingredients-versus-constructed-work}{%
\subsection{5. Supplied ingredients versus constructed

\textbf{Supplied:} the public manufacturing experiment, schemas, CAD
engine, existing integrated graphs, known interval/permutation
mathematics, ordinary tools, researcher-defined mission requests,
evaluator criteria, model priors and finite commercial search menu.

\textbf{Actually constructed:} exact-source readers and reports; actual
CAD candidates and traces; explicit unit/sign/identity findings;
interval and assignment witnesses; a source-bound acquisition plan; a
versioned operating method; an independent checker; an actual
received-code invocation through existing interfaces; and complete
hypothetical cash/accrual accounting.

\textbf{Not established:} autonomous buyer discovery, novel geometric
algorithms, physical metrology, production admission, current ENS
identity, learning to invent more efficiently, population reliability, a
new paying market or earned commercial Alpha. An inherited stronger
artifact is not proof of an improved discovery policy.

\hypertarget{transformation-witness-and-unit-rule}{%
\subsection{6. Transformation witness and unit
rule}\label{transformation-witness-and-unit-rule}}

The exact QIF 2.1 schema supplies the formal default: absent per-value
and applicable primary linear units mean meters. Incoming files declare
millimeters. The apparent plausibility of small dimensions cannot
silently repair a source. A hypothetical millimeter interpretation is
evaluated in a separate result.

For a common interval predicate, reordering the same selected records
leaves its universal truth unchanged. The executable witness either
checks every record or emits a concrete violating value. Interval
inclusion separately checks whether the source requirement entails the
destination requirement. Complete physical-family coverage, specimen
relations, equivalent standards, measurement adequacy and current
authority remain separate guards.

The five supported purposes are evidence audit, all selected recorded
values, complete family, feature localization and physical release. Only
the first two can sometimes be resolved without a physical bijection. A
family certificate does not identify a hole; a local PASS does not
release a part. The actual Plate trace contains a 3.017 mm diameter
within its local interval and an overall FAIL in the parent result.

The record-only purpose was distinguished during development, before
core freeze. The stronger already-curated requests retained their
original purposes. No successful weaker statement was substituted for a
failed stronger contract.
