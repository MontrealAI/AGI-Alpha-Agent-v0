\textbf{FIND YOUR ALPHA. BUILD YOUR SUCCESSOR Ω.}

\hypertarget{the-result}{%
\subsection{The result}\label{the-result}}

We constructed a reusable manufacturing inspection-evidence instrument
and transferred it to a fresh recipient with new mission checks and
current local research authority. It leaves the enterprise better
equipped to trace claims, find counterexamples and specify missing
evidence. \textbf{This edition does not establish a profitable new
market, measured Operational Alpha, or commercial compounding.} Its
strongest commercial result is a prospective decision to preserve cash
and use the existing expert alternative.

The selected opportunity is consequential and bounded: a
receiving-quality engineer needs to know whether another provider's
inspection evidence supports its obligations, and a successor needs an
exact evidence-acquisition plan for investigation. Existing
digital-thread tools are strong alternatives. The new instrument
addresses an inspectable cross-provider semantic problem, not the
invention of STEP, QIF, geometry matching or inspection automation.

\hypertarget{exploration-changed-the-direction}{%
\subsection{Exploration changed the
direction}\label{exploration-changed-the-direction}}

Ten candidate directions and challenges are preserved. The team
investigated software failures, scientific dependency/evaluation
problems, causal evidence, robotic rheology, manufacturing
qualification, thermal metrology and laboratory portability before
selecting the flagship. The familiar microgrid work remains a historical
fixture.

A particularly useful negative pilot reproduced an old Dask/Zarr ZIP
corruption report but found that current software passed. That proposed
repair opportunity was rejected rather than promoted into a large
simulator. Thermal examples also lost their proposed gap when known
symmetry and manufacturing information were included. The selected
manufacturing direction survived because actual NIST/MTC files supplied
both a complete geometry/result trace and concrete mismatches that an
audit must resolve.

\hypertarget{what-was-built}{%
\subsection{What was built}\label{what-was-built}}

The instrument reads exact QIF 2.1 sources, preserves their literal
units, tolerances and statuses, constructs geometric witnesses from
actual AP242 CAD, checks interval implications and recorded values, and
binds repair requests to specific source obligations. It distinguishes
numerical records, physical families, individual features and part
release.

Plate development imported a valid solid with \textbf{261 faces} and
constructed \textbf{17 requirement--feature--geometry--result traces}. A
recorded diameter of \textbf{3.017 mm} passes its local interval while
the containing inspection remains \textbf{FAIL}. That distinction
survives the report and transfer.

Exact QIF 2.1 rules make the FAIR files' undeclared linear units
formally meters; incoming inspection explicitly uses millimeters. A
plausible millimeter repair is therefore a separately labeled
hypothesis, never an automatic correction. On fresh Box data, \textbf{76
values} include an observed violation of the receiving interval even
under that hypothesis. On Cover, \textbf{400 selected values} satisfy
the numerical interval, but the source records do not establish coverage
of the required physical family. A permutation-invariant record result
does not locate a hole for rework or release a part.

The first instrument failed three actionability requirements because two
repair requests had no affected-obligation bindings. A new version
corrected this on Plate after new requests had already been frozen.
Original failures and bytes remain intact. The new tests use fresh
missions on the same historical data.

7/6/8, with equal eight-call and 1,600-word ceilings. Complete tokens,

refused. The production capsule correctly retained its

The frozen model searches price, buyer subset and development terms
against outside options, rights, quality, capacity and cash. It does not
impose a fixed discount, investment count, coalition or training budget.

exposures, not observed insolvencies. The original numbers remain
unchanged, with their narrower assumption explicit.

\hypertarget{an-operating-blueprint}{%
\subsection{An operating blueprint}\label{an-operating-blueprint}}

\textbf{Offer:} a precisely scoped source-comparability and
evidence-acquisition assessment. Price new measurements, authoritative
corrections and release support separately. Determine the recipient's
real configured outside option.

\textbf{Deliver:} automate inventory, literal checks, geometry traces
and reproducible witnesses; independently verify material claims; ask
for the smallest missing item; measure actual corrections, customer
interaction and support.

\textbf{Retain:} generic executable readers, proof policies and adapters
only when rights, scope, fresh acceptance and expected reuse justify
additional costs. Keep customer secrets, restricted IP and protected
answers outside the package.

\textbf{Finance:} state milestone outcomes, private adaptations, risk,
refunds, responsibilities and collection dates. An advance is not earned
surplus. Participation and liquidity are separate tests. Preserve cash
when a proposal fails either one.

\textbf{Share:} use the existing protected network, eligible membership
and current mission-specific authority. Access does not confer
unrestricted execution or independent possession of a hosted specialist.
No production scope was changed.

\textbf{Next decisive evidence:} unrelated industrial packets; actual
human/integrator quotes or trials; buyer-specific rights and source
corrections; complete costs; accepted contracts; and authorized
production qualification. No Gym campaign or specialist ASI claim is
warranted by the current evidence.

\hypertarget{what-this-edition-leaves-behind}{%
\subsection{What this edition leaves
behind}\label{what-this-edition-leaves-behind}}

The enterprise gains an executable evidence instrument, a useful fresh
recipient handoff, an honest search history, a repaired actionability
defect and a tested stop decision. It gains a sharper account of what
must be true before capability becomes opportunity. It does not gain
invented customers, retrospective profit or automatic permission.

The full paper, code, source notices, frozen results, evidence companion
and English/French demonstration accompany this brief. Research and
writing used AI assistance; role-separated AI review is not independent
human peer review.
