# Prospective commercial experiment: bounded audit offerings

Version 1.0; authored before confirmation, 27 October 2026. All monetary
quantities in this experiment are **assumed USD**, not observed transactions.
Actual customer collections and earned revenue for this study are zero. Complete
actual research cost is unknown because hosted-model billing and human effort
are unavailable. No simulated profit offsets that unknown expense.

## Purpose, comparator and independently generated needs

The sold outcome is a checked, source-traceable inspection-evidence audit with
specified obligations and precise repair requests. A justified insufficiency
finding can satisfy this contract. It cannot satisfy a contract requiring full
report substitution, feature localization, or physical part release. Those
different contracts retain their original acceptance criteria.

H is a scope-matched engineering service, estimated at 12–31 team hours and
USD125–225/hour for unfamiliar packets; an already configured recipient is
estimated at 2–8 hours. These are researcher estimates, not quotations, wages,
timed human trials or a calibrated market distribution. Existing vendor
capabilities and the absence of a matched public quote are documented in
`../dossier/CADofferinggrounding.md`.

Each simulated horizon draws 1–7 separate potential needs before a package is
selected. Buyer values, outside-option costs, configuration status, rights,
adapter compatibility and effort are explicit random assumptions. A common
file format does not guarantee participation or technical compatibility.
An existing configured service is a cheap outside option. Incompatible jobs
may still receive bespoke work, but not assumed retained-tool savings.
Neither public NIST artifacts nor these draws are evidence of actual demand.

## Four mechanism counterfactuals and unrestricted choice

The same needs, source-independent random events, 80-hour capacity and opening
cash are supplied to four arms:

1. expert bespoke work, payment on acceptance;
2. retained generic instrument, payment on acceptance;
3. expert bespoke work, optional development-commission arrangement;
4. retained generic instrument plus that arrangement.

These are controlled mechanism ablations, not claims that an expert is
incapable of proposing an arrangement. The unrestricted enterprise may choose
any of the four, a subset of buyers, or stop. H is the commercial outside option;
U/E/E+ quality comparisons are measured separately, not manufactured here.
No numerical prompting advantage is assigned to U versus E.

Prices are selected from USD500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,500, 4,000, 6,000. A shared
posted price is a deliberate bounded search menu, not a proof of globally
optimal discriminatory pricing. Every subset of the at-most-seven buyers is
considered. Arrangement terms search advance fractions 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and
failure refund fractions 0.5 or 1. Payment-on-acceptance arms use no advance.
There is no prescribed discount, buyer count, training budget or reinvestment
fraction. Equal-price terms are disclosed; future negotiation is outside scope.
The finite search maximizes expected contribution conditional on the stated
commitments, then replans after an observed prelaunch withdrawal. It is not a
solution of the entire stochastic bargaining game. Withdrawal stress tests
the resulting policy prospectively. Assessment C is charged even when the
post-assessment decision is to stop. An enterprise can also decline this whole
research campaign beforehand; the simulation does not count that as a free
completed opportunity assessment.

## Participation and the commissioned-development contract

Successful audit value is V, outside service price H, quality q and customer
effort cost e. The outside utility is max(V-H, 0). An advance aP is a commissioned
development payment. If the audit fails, r of the advance is refunded and the
remaining amount is earned only after the contracted development record and
failure artifacts are delivered and accepted. This allocates real downside to
the buyer; it is not a guaranteed audit sale. If the audit succeeds, aP counts
toward total price P. Buyers anticipate that downside, and participate only if
qV - [qP+(1-q)(1-r)aP] - e >= max(V-H,0). They may also decline for lack of rights.

This contract is a hypothetical commercial design, not a verified legal form.
Its generic research milestone has a distinct acceptance requirement. If a
buyer will not accept that risk or milestone, choose r=1 or payment on acceptance.
An unaccepted development record requires a full refund. No optimistic reuse
value is assigned to a failed artifact. A model stress sets full refunds.

## Costs, risk and liquidity

A contains common construction required for the commissioned work, private
preparation, conditional delivery, serving, support and failed attempts.
B contains additional retained-asset qualification, admission and upkeep.
C contains the additional prospect/research assessment. Actual unpaid work in
this edition remains research, not paid-job learning. No activity appears twice.
The optional Gym is not funded by default and contributes no assumed gain.
All labor is an assumed cash contractor cost; no displaced-production
opportunity cost is charged a second time. Actual employee/capital structures
would require separate estimates.

Instrument cost multipliers include worsening cases, and its common technical
failure affects all related jobs. Bespoke work has independent technical
failures. Conditional private delivery is incurred only when its technical
prerequisite passes. Payment default affects the final balance, withdrawal
occurs before launch and refunds the advance in full, and partial/private
failure can require the contractual refund. Support and maintenance are charged.

Opening cash must cover a conservative precollection envelope: common work,
all committed preparation/delivery/support, extra retention and assessment,
less spendable advances after the full promised refund reserve. This deliberately
does not borrow against uncollected invoices. Report the excess cash requirement
separately from positive expected profit. A fully refundable advance supplies
no spendable development financing under this rule. A sale on credit may be
profitable but rejected for liquidity. Capacity, quality and a ten-business-day
parallel-delivery window are independent gates.

## Feasibility and prospective testing

A package crosses the modeled feasibility boundary only if all participating
buyers prefer it, all required rights are present, expected net contribution
after A/B/C is nonnegative, predicted acceptance probability is at least 0.85,
required opening cash is within the same seed, estimated work fits 80 hours,
and estimated turnaround fits ten business days. These are model assumptions,
not physical acceptance or an achieved SLA. Both arms qualifying means better
modeled economics, not newly feasible work.

Development uses seeds 110–121 only to test accounting and inspect reachable
admission bounds. The complete program, menu and policy are hashed before
confirmation seeds 21000–21079. Confirmation evaluates low/high common build,
negative transfer, efficient incumbent, low demand, all refunds, payment stress,
model-default progress and seed-capital stresses. Results are conditional
scenario counts and distributions, not empirical confidence intervals.
The selected menu and assumptions are not retuned after confirmation.

### Fixed-resource lineage diagnostic

An additional six-horizon retention decision uses only development seeds 110–121.
It compares modeled expected incremental contribution from a preexisting generic
instrument (zero new construction, USD100 additional upkeep per horizon) with
the expert's already arranged service. The proposed initial research build costs
USD2,400 in C, initial retention USD400 in B, and fresh-recipient transfer USD150
in B at the fourth horizon. Initial research is charged once, not again in A.
The six-horizon count is fixed before fresh confirmation; no guaranteed future
demand or increase in compute is assumed. Admission requires expected incremental
benefit exceeding all those additional costs. If rejected, the actual modeled
policy does not build, and its incremental gain and expenditure are zero.

For causal diagnosis only, twenty forced-construction paths use seeds 22000–22119.
They expose the full initial cost, a fresh recipient at stage four, and adverse
realized outcomes. Such paths do not retrospectively admit a rejected campaign
or recover profit. The expert and successor see the same current needs and have
the same 80-hour limit; spendable opening cash is capped at USD6,000 per horizon.
Surplus is held outside that cap, so larger resources do not manufacture the
comparison. An identical-content ordinary library is assigned exactly the same
economic path: no graph-specific advantage is assumed. Scenario quantiles show
the distribution of this artificial world, not confidence about real customers.

Retention is considered only when a finite upper bound on compatible reuse
margin can exceed additional B costs. This structural check prevents an
impossible admission hurdle from being interpreted as evidence that useful
skills cannot exist. It does not grant admission; the full package still pays
all costs and passes current acceptance. No graph-specific economic advantage
is assigned over an ordinary library with identical content and permission.

## What would reject the commercial hypothesis

No attractive participants, efficient incumbents, expensive adaptation, missing
rights, weak transfer, common failure, payment delays/defaults, refund promises,
capacity or lack of capital can all stop production or retention. An unserved
physical-release contract stays infeasible when the required evidence is absent,
irrespective of modeled audit profitability. More nodes or jobs do not establish
compounding, and no surplus-funded resource expansion is assumed in this test.
