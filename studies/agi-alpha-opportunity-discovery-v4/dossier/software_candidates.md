# Opportunity dossier: two software and instrument candidates

**Research cut: 27 October 2026. Status: hypotheses for selection, not selected enterprises or admitted Alpha.**

This dossier responds to the instruction to reconsider the opportunity before constructing another simulator. The microgrid and tank study remains a reproducible fixture with its original rejection, costs unknown where unmeasured, and inheritance controls intact. It supplies no evidence of demand for either candidate below.

The two candidates address different purposes and beneficiaries. Candidate A concerns agent actions in changing business systems; Candidate B concerns the scientific meaning of computations across independently maintained software. Both would need an executable instrument and a commercial arrangement. Neither is justified by renaming existing debugging, regression testing, consulting, or pooled funding.

## Selection finding

| Question | A. Stateful agent counterexamples | B. Scientific meaning and collective repair |

| Valuable purpose | Let an organization delegate useful multi-system work without silently violating the user's intended outcome | Let scientific users change dependencies, backends, or workflows while preserving defined scientific conclusions |

**No flagship selection follows from this desk research.** A is closer to agent-enterprise architecture, but directly overlaps a substantial current product. B has a more explicit technical/public-good complementarity, but slower acceptance and uncertain buying authority. A truthful outcome of the next step may be to reject both.

## A. Executable counterexamples for stateful agent work

### Need and independently motivated evidence

An agent's fluent explanation can disagree with the resulting state of a booking, entitlement, inventory, billing, or service system. The proposed buyer is the team accountable for a particular useful workflow, not an abstract buyer of “better reasoning.” Its desired outcome is a verified service operation with the correct purpose, state changes, responsibilities, and authority.

Three primary sources bound the opportunity:

1. LangChain's May 2026 launch describes customer difficulty identifying recurring failures and creating evaluation coverage. **Its existing LangSmith Engine already clusters production failures, uses code and traces to diagnose causes, proposes PRs, and creates online/offline evaluations.** Its customer statements are vendor-reported evidence, not independently measured savings. [A1]
2. Anthropic distinguishes an agent trace from the actual outcome left in the environment and explains how multi-step actions complicate evaluation. Its example of a valid alternative rejected by a rigid evaluator cautions against treating one prescribed path as the purpose itself. These are established evaluation lessons, not the proposed invention. [A2]
3. The September 2026 Hyper-tau benchmark tests construction of agents from client records, requirements, APIs, inherited code, and resource limits. Its authors report 23.9% success for their strongest tested configuration versus 82.2% for their expert reference. The clients and evaluations are benchmark constructions. This is evidence of a demanding research task, not observed customer losses, a current universal model ceiling, or willingness to pay. [A3]

### Restriction, possible transformation, and uncertainty

**Binding restriction to investigate:** evidence available to a debugging agent may show a sequence of calls but not a reconstructible state, a precise original-purpose constraint, or which intervention would actually prevent failure. Two systems can each report success while their combined state violates the service obligation. If the relevant state cannot be read or safely reproduced, a causal claim may be unidentifiable.

**Replacement hypothesis:** compile authorized traces, API contracts, repository code, state snapshots, and a beneficiary-approved purpose into a small executable witness. The witness reproduces a failure, varies a suspected cause while holding the purpose fixed, and verifies the resulting state. It must explicitly represent events such as withdrawal, retries, delayed consistency, revoked authority, partial completion, and corrective action when they matter to the actual workflow. Those are proposed test dimensions, not a supplied promise that every buyer needs them.

The new value would have to lie in constructing and reducing these witnesses economically across unfamiliar integrations. A trace summary, manually written test, generic simulator, or stronger evaluator prompt does not by itself establish that value. It is not established by the sources that current products lack the narrower capability. The first diligence question is a demonstration against existing products and an expert's existing tools.

### What would need to be constructed

The candidate asset is an inspectable **reproduction and intervention instrument**: state adapters, a versioned purpose specification, an event representation, a counterexample reducer, an intervention runner, and an independent outcome checker. A separate hypothesis is that a learned operating method can choose which evidence or intervention to request more efficiently. Keeping an improved adapter is different from learning to construct better adapters.

The system must discover any reusable structure from separately collected incidents. The research team may supply safe execution infrastructure, a rights-cleared corpus, API documentation, and budget limits; these must be disclosed. It must not supply a common failure taxonomy, all compatible buyers, and a ready-made repair and then claim autonomous discovery.

Current tool-using frontier systems make reading repositories, proposing adapters, running counterfactual tests, and revising hypotheses practically testable. The Hyper-tau result motivates a difficulty, not guaranteed readiness. Model identity, exact settings, context, tool access, pricing, and model-upgrade controls remain to be frozen for an actual experiment. A deterministic simulation cannot establish the frontier's reasoning ceiling.

### Outside options and fair counterfactuals

The strongest available option is not ordinary chat. Include LangSmith Engine when access and scope fit, the organization's existing observability and regression suite, and an experienced engineer using the same frontier model and tools. Buying the product, fixing a tool description, adding an ordinary integration test, narrowing permissions, or temporarily keeping the task with a human may all dominate a new instrument. [A1]

Use H for a scoped human debugging and remediation service, U for representative ordinary operation of the same model with normal tools, E for the established expert workflow, and E+ for that workflow with the candidate instrument. Establish a genuine H scope and quote before claiming a service price. Report E−U and E+−E separately. If an established product already gives the recipient the same result at comparable complete cost, the proposed asset adds no recipient advantage.

### Participation and financing hypothesis

A plausible offering is a bounded diagnosis-and-repair engagement for a named workflow, followed by optional verification when an integration changes. The buyer pays for a reproducible, correctly scoped failure explanation and an accepted corrective result, not a promise of complete autonomous reliability. A maintainer or platform vendor may participate as a supplier or accept a proposed fix; neither is automatically a customer.

Shared construction is justified only if independently recruited buyers have compatible technical needs and rights. A conditional development commitment could fund a common adapter or state abstraction, with customer-specific evidence isolated. Agreements would distinguish payment for accepted investigation from payment for accepted remediation, allocate reproduction and review effort, cap unsupported changes, and specify withdrawal, failed diagnosis, refunds, support, and collection timing. A profitable expected engagement can still be impossible with insufficient prefunding. No deposits, sales, or buyer commitments have been observed here.

### Smallest falsifying test and next possibility

First obtain two independently originated, rights-cleared failure reports from distinct integrations, with owner-defined purpose and acceptance. Before building a generalized platform, ask an expert using existing tooling and a current incumbent product to attempt a reproduction. Reject the candidate if these routinely supply the proposed witness within the buyer's acceptable effort and cost, or if necessary state cannot be obtained without violating rights or disrupting service.

If that gate survives, predeclare a small construction test: two development incidents, then a frozen instrument on at least four fresh incidents from at least two unfamiliar integrations. Allocate the same maximum model resources and elapsed execution budget to U, E, and E+; record preparation and evaluator effort separately. Preserve unresolved cases. An independent owner or qualified evaluator must check the original purpose, reproduced failure, actual effect of the proposed intervention, unchanged unrelated obligations, and absence of unauthorized actions. A blind before/after intervention discriminates causality from persuasive diagnosis. Give the discovered formulation to E as a diagnostic to separate formulation value from superior execution.

This small screen can falsify a reusable-instrument hypothesis; it cannot establish broad superiority. A new recipient must receive executable adapters and verification logic, not merely a report. Define a possible feasibility crossing prospectively using that recipient's maximum release delay, complete verification cost, and accepted failure rate. A later multi-system service becomes newly feasible only if the baseline fails that same gate and the enhanced recipient passes. Otherwise report better economics or no improvement.

### Rights, authority, and stopping conditions

Traces and state may contain customer secrets. Prefer owner-authorized snapshots and reversible test environments; exclude protected answers and credentials from shared assets. Separate permission to inspect, reproduce, propose, and deploy. The recipient receives current authorization for its own system; donor authorization never transfers. Require source/dependency/schema/evaluator identity and fresh purpose checking. Stop if the useful proof depends on inaccessible state, the checker can be gamed, redaction destroys the mechanism, recipient adaptation erases the saving, or incumbent capabilities make the instrument redundant.

## B. Scientific meaning witnesses and collective repair

### Need and independently motivated evidence

Scientific users need the same measurement, inference, or numerical quantity to retain its defined meaning as software changes. Passing a package's existing tests or installing a newer dependency is not sufficient evidence for that purpose. The candidate beneficiary is a laboratory, instrument-software team, research infrastructure operator, or commercial scientific platform with a concrete computation at risk. No such party has been recruited or treated as a buyer in this dossier.

The inspected primary evidence is specific and also limits the claim:

1. NumPy documents that 2.0 scalar-promotion changes can alter precision or produce integer errors/overflows. It already offers migration guidance, warnings, explicit casting remedies, and the Ruff NPY201 automated rule; it also warns that the diagnostic can flag changes irrelevant to a particular application. **A migration wrapper would add little.** This historical transition illustrates semantic sensitivity, not an unresolved October 2026 incident. [B1]
2. Astropy issue #17418 records an interaction among NumPy scalar types, Astropy quantity/table behavior, Photutils, and FITS serialization. Maintainers corrected the initial interpretation: the mixed name types predated NumPy 2.0; the changed representation exposed them. A repair discussion also identified a follow-on issue. This is a real cross-project diagnostic history, not proof that the proposed service is needed today or that NumPy created the underlying bug. [B2]
3. Quansight offers maintainer expertise, upstream engagement, project support, and consortium design. These are meaningful human-service outside options, not missing ingredients to claim as inventions. [B3]
4. The Open Source for Science Fund already pools philanthropic and industry support. Its current FAQ asks for specific technical outcomes and maintainer buy-in, permits related-tool collaborations, and reports its predecessor's $58 million across over 230 projects. That is documented funding history, **not a market-size estimate or revenue available to this enterprise**. [B4]

### Restriction, possible transformation, and uncertainty

**Binding restriction to investigate:** each project has local tests and ownership, while the useful scientific purpose crosses package boundaries. An apparently correct local change may alter units, missing-value semantics, numerical tolerance, ordering, randomness, or the meaning of an output. Downstream groups may each spend effort demonstrating the same incompatibility without a shared, accepted scientific requirement or an affordable route to repair.

**Replacement hypothesis:** construct a portable scientific validity witness linking a real scientific claim to executable invariants, a dependency/environment closure, minimized reproducing data, and a repair obligation across the affected interfaces. The invariant must come from an authorized scientific requirement or defensible mathematical property; the AI cannot invent a convenient tolerance or substitute an easier problem. It may conclude that two intended semantics are incompatible and that a versioned boundary is necessary.

A separate commercial hypothesis is that several beneficiaries can finance a shared witness and upstream-compatible repair when a single beneficiary would decline its complete cost. Collective funding itself is established. The new contribution would need to show that a constructed instrument changes the cost or acceptability of a particular shared scientific undertaking, while consent and funding make construction possible. Neither half alone must be artificially penalized.

### What would need to be constructed

The candidate artifact is an executable **scientific requirement and counterexample instrument**, with methods for extracting candidate invariants from approved documentation and equations, generating discriminating input cases, shrinking failures, comparing dependency/backend variants, and producing a reproducible repair and qualification package. It should preserve assumptions, precision regimes, units, uncertainty, and counterexamples. Maintainer review of scientific and API meaning is part of the outcome when it is actually necessary; arbitrary fixed review minutes are not.

The promising role of frontier AI is integrating unfamiliar documentation, code, test behavior, and scientific requirements, then testing proposed explanations. It remains a hypothesis that this can outperform strong experts with standard property-based testing, differential testing, numerical analysis, and current coding agents. The most difficult part may be eliciting a valid scientific oracle rather than writing a patch. A faster patch generator that increases reviewer burden can have negative complete value.

Start with independently submitted scientific needs and let the procedure discover shared requirements or dependencies. Supplying a known shared bug and compatible sponsors is useful for a bounded mechanism test only if labeled as researcher-supplied; it is not opportunity discovery.

### Outside options and fair counterfactuals

Include maintaining a stable environment, upstream issue reporting, a targeted regression test, existing migration diagnostics, and expert support. NumPy recommends development-version CI with nightly wheels to reveal downstream regressions; this already supplies part of an ecosystem early-warning system. [B5] Quansight and existing funders are potential collaborators and competitors, not an empty market. [B3–B4]

H should be the matched research-software-engineering engagement including diagnosis, tests, review, and deployment assistance. No public price for that exact scope was verified. Obtain a quote, or explicitly estimate disclosed hours and rates. U and E must receive the same scientific documentation, tools, rights, and starting test suite. E includes the strong established methods above. E+ adds the candidate witness instrument. Keep any improvement in domain requirements, instrumentation, AI operation, and learned search policy separable.

### Participation and financing hypothesis

Potential payers are organizations whose actual scientific workflow is blocked or costly to validate. Upstream maintainers supply authority and scarce review capacity; they need compensation or an accepted community benefit and retain release authority. A philanthropist may finance a public capability, but a grant is a distinct funding source with its own eligibility and purpose. The Open Source for Science Fund's published process does not accept arbitrary unsolicited proposals and requires qualified organizational applicants and open licensing. Do not put hypothetical grant money into enterprise revenue. [B4]

A conditional jointly commissioned repair could pool beneficiary commitments around a shared, predeclared scientific acceptance package. Price customer-specific adaptation separately from common construction; allocate review and support costs; expose the option to wait for an open upstream fix. A nonparticipant may benefit later, so exclusive ownership of public code is not a credible default incentive. Possible paid benefits to test include a deadline, supported integration, documented reproducibility, and funded maintainer capacity. None has established willingness to pay here.

Use stage acceptance and limited commitments: accepted reproducibility work, accepted common repair, then each beneficiary's accepted integration. Specify what happens if maintainers reject the change, one buyer withdraws, one application reveals incompatible semantics, or a newer dependency makes the effort obsolete. A development advance remains a liability until its contracted service is earned. Available cash and expected contribution require separate tests.

### Smallest falsifying test and next possibility

The first gate is three independent need submissions from at least two organizations and a willing maintainer or scientific evaluator. Reject cases resolved by a routine documented migration, and reject a commercial claim if beneficiaries cannot identify a meaningful blocked outcome or a plausible budget owner. This is information acquisition, not a request to build a broad repair platform.

For a small technical screen, choose two development requirements and four protected fresh requirements spanning different downstream packages or backends. Include numerical, unit/metadata, and valid negative-transfer cases only when they occur in the recruited needs. A historical case whose public fix the model may know is useful for procedure development, not protected confirmation. Freeze scope and exclude future fixes and evaluator material. Record all attempts, including failure to construct an adequate oracle.

Acceptance requires that the witness distinguishes a scientifically consequential failure from an irrelevant change, that the proposed repair preserves the original scientific requirement, and that independent tests run in the intended environments. Reviewer time, failed experiments, environment setup, support, and computation all count. Run technical contribution alone, coordinated funding alone, and their combination where the real dependency structure warrants that comparison.

A fresh recipient should use the retained method on a different scientific task—for example, qualifying a previously unaffordable backend migration—while rederiving its actual scientific tolerances and rights. Predeclare its available budget, deadline, and validity requirement. If baseline and enhanced workflows both qualify, claim a cost or turnaround improvement; if neither qualifies, retain the failure. A shared repaired library is inherited structure, while a measured improvement in discovering new witnesses is a separate learning claim.

### Rights, authority, and stopping conditions

Preserve upstream licenses and contribution rules. Share only open or expressly authorized code, synthetic/minimized data, and rights-cleared invariants. Scientific data may be private or embargoed; membership in the agent network does not authorize its disclosure. Owners approve scientific requirements; maintainers decide merges/releases; organizations authorize execution. A grant, a benchmark pass, or possession of a dependency graph grants none of those powers.

Stop if no stable requirement can be specified, valid witnesses cost more than the expert alternative, contributor burden exceeds funded capacity, beneficiary needs have no consequential overlap, free-riding prevents commitments, or publicly available upgrades remove the need. The appropriate answer may be to fund established maintainers and use their existing workflow.

## Bounded next decision and evidence discipline

Neither candidate warrants an economic forecast, generalized simulator, or claimed autonomous invention yet. The next investment is a short opportunity-qualification exercise: obtain independent needs, establish outside options, request an incumbent demonstration or expert estimate, and decide whether a residual gap remains. Set a research spending ceiling before that exercise. Do not invent compatibility, demand, prices, participation, or accepted outcomes to make a joint offering viable.

If a candidate survives, record its transformation witness before construction: original useful purpose, binding restriction, reason it may be removable, proposed replacement, executable realization, and a falsifier. Freeze acceptance and source versions before fresh testing. Current models can be evaluated as U/E/E+ with actual tools; modeled frontier behavior must be separately labeled. Actual scientific or engineering results, sourced facts, assumptions, simulated markets, and unknown complete costs belong in separate evidence fields.

The retained repository integration can supply immutable-byte transport, identity-bound generic qualification, fresh semantics, independent current authority, and signed accounting. Its production scope does not already authorize these new instruments. Reuse the native lifecycle and protected access interfaces through the existing owner process, without a competing registry or automatic production admission. No new code was constructed for either candidate during this dossier.
