# Scientific-instrument opportunity dossier

Research screen dated 27 October 2026. Two candidates for comparison with the
other four candidates, not a selected flagship. No new instrument was coded,
trial conducted, buyer contacted or revenue forecast earned. Numbers proposed
below are prospective decision gates, not observations. The completed microgrid
study remains a historical fixture with its negative results preserved.

validation before any scientific or commercial admission. Neither is an automatic
winner. A corpus search cannot establish that a gap is globally unsolved.

Both remain worthwhile future research opportunities. A hybrid implementation of
S1 could demonstrate conditional formal reasoning on supplied graphs; S2 could
demonstrate a new procedure in an assumed fluid simulator. Those are narrower
engineering results, and currently risk repeating the solver-fixture problem the
user explicitly rejected. Neither should be selected merely because those
demonstrations are convenient to execute. A go decision requires an original
purpose with a decisive external or independently validated evaluator and a
specific residual gap that the established expert cannot already resolve.

## S1 — Causal evidence repair for cross-context perturbation studies

### Need and source-backed restriction

The useful decision is deliberately narrow: for a stated research context,
perturbation, dose/time and predeclared cellular-response score, determine whether
the sign, order or threshold of an effect is sufficiently supported to justify a
follow-up experiment. This is basic research support, not a patient-treatment
recommendation.

The Open Problems perturbation benchmark documents costly experiments, limited
cell-type coverage and the difficulty of moving from cell lines to other cellular
contexts. Its public task includes 144 compounds and three donors [S1-1]. The
scPerturBench authors benchmarked 27 methods across 29 datasets and explicitly
study unseen contexts and perturbations [S1-2]. These establish a consequential
research problem, not willingness to pay for our proposed product.

The restriction is that a good prediction score or a pooled collection of papers
does not itself establish the target causal conclusion. Population, intervention,
sampling and measurement differences can invalidate aggregation. Causal data
fusion already has a substantial formal theory that makes those assumptions
explicit [S1-3]. We would investigate whether frontier reasoning can construct
the missing bridge from real assay records and scientific purposes to a usable
formal evidence model, while preserving competing explanations and uncertainty.

### Prior art that the experiment must defeat or use

This is not a proposal to invent do-calculus. The open y0 package implements
identification, surrogate-outcome and counterfactual-transportability algorithms
[S1-4]. Akbari, Etesami and Kiyavash already study minimum-cost interventions for a
specified causal effect and provide algorithms and code [S1-5]. An expert with
these tools is the E comparator.

Nor is it permissible to claim neural perturbation models are uniformly useless.
A 2025 Nature Methods study found that its evaluated deep models did not beat
simple baselines [S1-6]. An October 2026 Nature Biotechnology paper showed that
metric calibration can change this conclusion and reported advantages under
well-calibrated metrics [S1-7]. Both codebases, strong linear/additive controls and
relevant current models belong in the comparator set. The research objective
must be fixed before choosing its metric; an easier evaluator is not an advance.

### Proposed reusable construction

The candidate instrument would accept study records, data dictionaries,
intervention metadata and an original scientific question. It would produce:

1. A typed evidence model with exact source locations for measurement,
   intervention, population and sampling claims; disputed assumptions remain
   alternatives rather than being silently resolved.
2. An executable causal query and either an identified expression, a bounded
   conclusion under stated sensitivity assumptions, or a constructive witness
   that available evidence permits incompatible answers.
3. A proposed additional contrast or measurement, a laboratory-ready question
   specifying what information it must provide, and the effect of acquiring it on
   the **original** decision.
4. A reproducible estimate and calibration/refutation package tied to complete
   source, method, dataset, task and authority identities.

The possible invention is an evidence-to-experiment representation and a
validated discovery procedure for missing scientific structure. If a researcher
supplies the correct causal graph, transport assumptions and missing experiment,
the resulting execution must be labeled a supplied-structure diagnostic. It is
not the flagship discovery result. Proof is always conditional on explicit
assumptions and the formal model; an LLM cannot certify biological truth by
asserting a graph.

Frontier reasoning would reconcile assay semantics, search for conflicting
mechanisms, generate testable alternative representations and construct code.
Independent agents would challenge source grounding, identification and
experimental feasibility. Established symbolic and statistical tools would
check the result. The proposed retained specialist is an executable evidence
adapter and experiment-design policy, with failure cases and uncertainty
calibration—not merely a summary of the biology.

### Offering and complementary commercial mechanism

Prospective counterparties are research teams, bioinformatics cores and screening
facilities. The initial product is an explicitly scoped research decision package,
not a guaranteed biological finding. Acceptance would be specified in advance:
valid conclusion **or** valid non-identification and a useful discriminating
experiment, if that is what the buyer ordered. An accepted-plan-only contract must
not be changed to a report contract after a failure.

Independently elicited questions may reveal a shared missing contrast, reference
condition or assay calibration. If so, an optional joint order could fund that
experiment and the common evidence adapter. Each participant would receive its
own decision artifact and agreed data rights. No fixed coalition size is required.
Deposits would be conditional on a funding threshold, laboratory capacity and
predeclared technical qualification; withdrawal, failed assay, unusable data,
refunds and support would be priced explicitly. No participants are assumed.

Compare technical construction alone, coordinated purchase alone and their
combination. Coordination without a sound inferential instrument can merely
purchase more unusable data. The instrument without affordable access to the
missing contrast can still leave the original decision unresolved.

Human-service anchors exist but are not matched quotes: Notre Dame lists external
bioinformatics consulting at USD117/hour for July 2026–June 2027 [S1-8]; Gladstone
lists external service at USD330/hour and says its median project took about 12
billed hours [S1-9]. These providers differ in scope. A matched quote or disclosed
team-hour estimate is needed before computing H. These fees are neither salaries
nor the market price of the proposed new capability. Laboratory collections,
customer research value and our enterprise profit remain separate quantities.

### Smallest decisive test, prospective gates and stopping

First conduct a bounded evidence-access test before implementation: inspect a
small set of independent, purpose-defined queries and determine whether source
records identify interventions, timing, measurement units, populations and
available target-context outcomes. Stop if a credible evaluator cannot distinguish
a grounded assumption from a plausible invented one. No expensive model training
is justified at this stage.

If that passes, use public data for development, then freeze the adapter/policy
before protected target-context evaluation. An independent custodian should
withhold numerical outcomes and adjudicate source-grounding errors. Public
historical outcomes may have entered model training, so replay alone cannot
establish unfamiliar-problem superiority; truly fresh confirmation requires new
or independently sequestered outcomes. The deterministic proof checker can still
be tested separately on fully specified causal models with known answers.

Predeclare an original-purpose feasibility crossing, such as a target decision
requiring a supported response sign and a fixed precision requirement within a
specified experimental budget. A crossing occurs only if the baseline cannot
meet that requirement and the constructed experiment/evidence combination can.
If E already meets it, report economics or reliability improvement instead.
Compare H, ordinary same-model U, established expert E and retained E+ with the
same evidence and measurement menu. Supplying E the newly discovered formulation
is a diagnostic that locates the mechanism.

Falsifiers include unsupported transport assumptions, false confident decisions,
incorrect conditional proofs, no value from the proposed additional contrast,
poor transfer to a new cell context, or complete costs exceeding the outside
option. The stopping action may be to retain a useful negative certificate and
decline the paid offering. No positive biological effect is required or assumed.

## S2 — Turn liquid-handling transients into a qualified measurement instrument

### Need and precise residual gap

Formulation development requires measurements of flow and stability across many
ingredient combinations. A public Scientific Data study reports 812 formulations,
including 294 stable samples, with rheology and sample-specific uncertainty, and
explains the difficulty of generalizing from a limited ingredient space [S2-1].
The need is useful high-throughput measurement for formulation decisions, not
better answers to a supplied scalar optimization problem.

A strong incumbent already exists. Soh and colleagues built an Opentrons-based
proxy viscometer using dispensed mass over time; the 2025 study covers Newtonian
liquids and proof-of-concept power-law behavior in two non-Newtonian fluids
[S2-2]. Its authors identify broader fluid behavior and operating conditions as
future work. The repository contains raw Newtonian mass profiles, comparison data
for molasses and a surface cleaner, a notebook and an operating protocol [S2-3].
The proposal must not relabel this instrument as our invention.

The **hypothesized residual opportunity** is to discover an acquisition protocol
and inverse model that can distinguish decision-relevant behavior outside that
verified scope: for example, yield behavior or history dependence versus
instrument compliance, wetting and slip. Those examples are candidate explanations
to investigate, not established gaps solved by our method. The core question is
whether active control of dispense history and additional inexpensive signals
contains enough information to provide a qualified measurement on unfamiliar
formulations and apparatus.

### Construction and frontier role

Starting from original measurement needs, the system would have to invent a
physically realizable interrogation sequence, construct competing fluid/apparatus
models, identify where their outputs differ, and implement a measurement adapter
with calibration and refusal conditions. A software regression on the old
Newtonian dataset alone would not satisfy this ambition.

The retained asset would include an executable acquisition policy, instrument
adapter, uncertainty model, calibration standards, operating envelope and
independent verification procedure. A successor could reuse the method but must
requalify its own pipette, tips, balance, fluid class, temperature and intended
property. Access to a remote qualified measurement service would not give every
agent an independently possessed physical instrument.

Frontier agents would combine fluid mechanics, experimental design, sensor
selection and code construction; a separate critic would search for indistinguishable
fluid/apparatus explanations. Conventional rheometry and the published proxy are
the ground-truth/comparator methods. Vendor liquid-class functionality must also
be credited: Opentrons currently provides verified aqueous, viscous and volatile
classes for Flex, with editable transfer settings [S2-4]. Basic pipetting
parameter tuning is already available.

### Offering and participation mechanism

Prospective users include small formulation laboratories, characterization cores
and equipment providers. An offering might combine a calibrated instrument
adapter, installation/qualification and a bounded measurement service. A
consortium could share development of common calibration/reference panels while
keeping proprietary formulations private. Whether users actually share a
calibration bottleneck must be discovered from their requests; do not supply
compatible buyers and count their bundling as invention.

Funding would use milestone commitments for a working adapter, blind accuracy
qualification and fresh-site acceptance. Failure handling includes invalid
readings, consumables, contamination, downtime, calibration drift, retraining,
refunds and replacement/reference testing. A viable price must cover those
obligations and remain attractive against outsourced rheometry. Case Western
lists external-industry Ares rheometer use at USD60/hour self-service,
USD120/hour assisted and USD210/hour full service [S2-5]. That accessible outside
option prevents an unsupported claim that every customer must buy expensive
equipment. A matched testing schedule and current quote are still necessary.

The capability and arrangement could be complementary if reference calibration is
costly but useful to several independent users. Test each alone and the
combination. Neither a positive prediction score nor deposits are earned profit.

### Minimal decisive experiment and feasibility gate

Stage zero is a rights/data/observability audit. The public raw traces can test
whether a proposed representation contains plausible information beyond the
published baseline. They cannot validate unmeasured fluid classes or a new
apparatus. The repository's visible root has no verified code license; the
Cambridge paper record lists CC BY-NC 3.0 for its item [S2-6]. Commercial reuse
rights must be established separately. Public availability is not rights
clearance. A clean implementation of unprotected scientific ideas and properly
licensed new data remains an alternative, subject to actual rights review.

If the target quantity is not identifiable from the available signals, the useful
construction may be a new sensor/sequence, or the candidate should stop. A future
physical test would blind fresh fluids from at least one held-out behavior class
and a fresh apparatus; compare the new instrument, the published proxy and a
qualified rotational-rheometer reference at the same property/accuracy standard.
Freeze tolerances and the throughput/cost requirement before unblinding. Count
reference measurements, repeats, sample preparation, cleaning, failed runs,
specialist time, maintenance and site transfer.

A valid crossing would be a specified measurement batch meeting its original
accuracy, coverage, turnaround and complete-cost ceiling only with the new
instrument/arrangement. If the baseline rheometer service already meets it,
claim only measured economics or workflow improvement. Falsifiers include
indistinguishable mechanisms with different requested properties, calibration
that does not transfer, weak held-out uncertainty coverage, or no advantage after
full calibration and serving cost. Without physical confirmation this edition
could report only a computational hypothesis and executable simulator, not an
admitted new scientific instrument.

## Opportunity search outcomes that should remain in the record

Generic literature-to-laboratory translation was considered and screened down.
Pagel, Jirasek and Cronin's April 2026 primary paper already executes a workflow
that extracts procedures, translates to XDL, checks hardware-specific execution
and validates on robotic platforms [X1]. A new translator would need a different,
demonstrated gap. A 2026 representation paper likewise proposes a shared
orchestration vocabulary across physical and computational operations [X2].

ARPA-H's IGoR program independently articulates the need for mechanistic models,
knowledge-gap identification, reproducible protocols and a qualified-laboratory
marketplace [X3]. This is evidence of a serious research agenda and potential
competition, not an available customer, an award, validation of our approach or a
guaranteed grant. The page currently says submissions are under review and new
submissions are closed. Its tenfold acceleration objective is a program target,
not an achieved effect to copy into our accounting.
