# Flagship selection: inspection evidence that survives a change of recipient and purpose

**Edition:** opportunity-discovery v4. **Decision date:** 27 October 2026.
**Decision status:** select a bounded computational construction experiment. No commercial admission, customer commitment, part release, or production permission follows.

This decision follows the six-candidate first portfolio, a laboratory-protocol prior-art challenge, additional thermal grounding, three second-round candidates, and two small viability pilots. It precedes construction of the retained flagship instrument. The earlier microgrid study remains a preserved fixture. The search history, failed directions, and contrary evidence are part of this edition.

## Selected useful purpose

A receiving-quality engineer needs to know which obligations in one inspection package are supported by another provider's evidence, which apparent matches are false, and exactly what clarification or additional evidence is required. A subsequent process engineer needs a different answer: which physical feature and recorded operations can be associated with a discrepancy. These are different purposes. A family-wide conformity statement does not identify a particular hole for rework and does not release an entire part.

The selected opportunity is an **executable cross-provider inspection-evidence audit and requalification instrument**. Its economic hypothesis is that reusable readers, semantic comparisons, geometric witnesses, and proof checking can reduce repeated custom engineering sufficiently to support a viable scoped service. This is not a claim that existing experts or products cannot perform the work. Human service H and expert operation E include the existing NIST STEP/QIF reference graphs, STEP File Analyzer, established geometry matching, the 2024 digital-thread method, and conventional quality-engineering practice.

## Why this candidate survived

The source is an actual NIST/MTC manufacturing experiment with CAD, first-article reports, incoming inspection, NC code and machine records. The independent incoming graph is an existing asset, not something this study discovered. The public artifacts preserve a genuinely separate FAIR-to-incoming comparability question that the supplied incoming graph does not answer automatically.

The Plate-only viability session imported one valid AP242 solid with 261 faces, 722 edges and 34 cylindrical surfaces. It traced an incoming diameter requirement through a QIF feature to a CAD surface and a recorded actual value. It also exposed concrete reasons to reject naive reuse: matching-looking labels can denote different nominal diameters; material-condition fields and formal-standard declarations differ; FAIR omits a file-units declaration; an incoming tolerance has a negative literal value; and a passing individual diameter is contained within a result set whose overall status is FAIL. These are observations of supplied files, not proof that a manufactured part is defective or that a provider acted incorrectly.

A consequential formulation hypothesis emerged from those needs: **some family-wide decisions can be invariant under an unknown feature permutation**. A verified universal statement over all admissible correspondences can sometimes answer the original family question without recovering each individual label. Other purposes, including locating a particular feature, still require that identity. The three nominal-3 FAIR diameter candidates provide a development example with 60 recorded values. Any physical reuse remains conditional on independently justified units, feature-set coverage, specimen identity, standard interpretation and measurement adequacy. The unit issue will be resolved against the exact QIF 2.1 source; no convenient millimeter default is assumed.

The instrument must construct and check these relationships from actual artifacts. It cannot merely count XML rows, rename a graph, accept matching strings, or replace a requested feature-specific result with a weaker family summary.

## Smallest decisive flagship experiment

1. Develop on Plate only. Construct a source-bound QIF evidence reader, CAD/requirement witnesses, a semantic comparator, family-permutation implication/counterexample checks, and an evidence-repair plan.
2. Freeze executable sources, supported predicates, unit/default policy, schemas, evaluation criteria and dependencies before opening the held-out Box/Cover job contents. Their public nature and common assembly origin are disclosed; this is retrospective contextual withholding, not a new physical trial.
3. Test actual held-out artifact audits and separately labeled adversarial transformations with an independent reader/checker. Preserve raw values, scopes, individual and overall statuses. A false positive proof is a failed candidate; a corrected policy requires fresh tests.
4. Transfer the actual executable capability through the existing source-verified research lifecycle to a separate recipient with a different original purpose. Recheck source revisions, mission semantics and current authority. Compare an ordinary library with identical bytes and permissions.
5. Run a small actual hosted-model operating-method comparison with fair U/E/E+ briefs and tools. Report unavailable token/billing telemetry rather than asserting complete dollar savings.
6. Evaluate offering and financing policies using separately labeled assumptions, targeted technical/arrangement counterfactuals and adverse scenarios. Keep actual collections at zero, actual complete research cost unknown, and simulated contribution distinct from liquidity.

## Commercial package hypothesis

Offer a fixed-scope evidence audit: source inventory, concrete incompatible or conditional correspondences, independently checkable witnesses, a list of missing assertions/evidence, and a receiving-engineer decision packet. A repair or part-release service is a different contract and is not automatically fulfilled by an audit. A customer may use its own tools, commission an established integrator, obtain corrected source exports, repeat inspection, defer or decline.

Potential joint construction concerns generic adapters and verification machinery, not automatic pooling of proprietary designs or inspection records. Counterparties, cost sharing, data rights, milestones and support require explicit agreement. Conditional commitments may finance a common component only when each participant prefers its package to an outside option. No coalition, price, laboratory saving or willingness to pay has been observed.

## Falsifiers and stopping conditions

Reject any advantage if the instrument silently repairs units or tolerance signs, invents feature/specimen identity, confuses a local criterion with part acceptance, broadens a retained permission, produces an unsound universal certificate, or requires more adaptation/assessment than E. Report parity when E already succeeds. Stop expansion when demand, paid quality, reuse, upkeep or liquidity does not support it. A correct computational audit remains useful evidence even if learning, sharing or the proposed enterprise is not economically admitted.

## Addendum: exact QIF 2.1 unit ruling before retained core construction

The Plate pilot subsequently retrieved the standards owner's QIF 2.1 archive at
https://qifstandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/QIF-2.1.zip, linked from
https://qifstandards.org/qif-2-1/. The complete archive SHA256 is
`d9e8b5f1333f2c8827a1c5ff7fa08dc2839be8f9d84dcc3607145dc1f816df14`.
Its nested `QIF_2.1_XMLSchemaFiles.zip/QIFLibrary/Units.xsd` SHA256 is
`2d46993e49300841e66fc05c35dafbcb63ac8085e9c9c8cbc8670e97c13a5fbd`.
Both `LinearValueType` and `ActualLinearValueType` specify meters when neither a per-value linear unit
nor a primary linear unit is declared. Plate FAIR has neither; incoming explicitly declares millimeters.
Thus the raw numeric candidate 3.026–3.065 in FAIR formally represents meters, not millimeters.
The apparent positive family reuse is only a counterfactual after an independently authorized unit correction,
plus feature-set, specimen, requirement, measurement and authority evidence. It is not admitted physical reuse.
The instrument must preserve this obstruction and generate a precise requalification request rather than repair it silently.
This addendum strengthens the selected audit purpose; it does not rewrite the earlier pilot or recover profit.
