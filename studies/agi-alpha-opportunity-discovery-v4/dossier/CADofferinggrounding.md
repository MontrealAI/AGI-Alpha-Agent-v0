# CAD inspection-evidence offering: operating and commercial grounding

**27 October 2026 — pre-selection source research. Recommendation: conditional GO for a bounded, independently checked construction experiment; no finding of customer demand, market price, commercial superiority, or authority to release a part.**

This note supplements `round2_industry.md` and the Plate evidence-viability pilot. It does not replace their provenance or turn that pilot into confirmation. Three focused search queries were used, followed by direct reads of primary official sources. No customer outreach, purchase, account creation, or new flagship implementation was performed. Protected Box/Cover evaluation material was not inspected for this note.

The preceding runtime acquisition is closed as unused exploration in `runtime_workloads/acquisition_notes.md`. Its actual source snapshots remain preserved. The current-release runtime pilot passed the previously reported storage path; that negative opportunity result remains part of the selection history.

## 1. A consequential service with a limited, testable promise

**Proposed offer:** a source-traceable assessment of whether specified inspection evidence supports specified receiving-quality obligations, followed, when requested, by a separately authorized requalification or investigation handoff for a changed mission.

For one defined part/revision and a bounded list of characteristics, the service returns an obligation-by-obligation dossier: supported correspondence, conflicting semantics, unresolved alternative correspondences, or missing evidence; original source references and hashes; relevant geometry and datum witnesses; and a precise acquisition request when a decision cannot be supported. Acceptance is defined before work. A customer buying a complete substitution decision does not receive an accepted delivery merely because the tool abstains. A customer explicitly buying an evidence-sufficiency assessment can accept a justified negative finding if it resolves the agreed question.

The independently useful purposes are receiving-quality assessment and an engineering-change/investigation handoff. They are different responsibilities, not proof of two independently sampled customer populations. The public NIST assembly packet is one historical experiment. Its files do not establish actual buyer willingness to pay, current production urgency, or avoided inspection expense.

The Plate pilot's geometry import receipt records a valid solid with 261 faces and 34 cylindrical faces. That supports undertaking a nontrivial geometry-aware experiment; it is not a certificate that two providers measured the same feature, revision, or physical specimen. The pilot's observed label/nominal disagreements are reasons to test semantic reconciliation, not prices or demand forecasts. Source: `cad_evidence_pilot/geometry_import_receipt.json`; detailed findings remain owned by the pilot record.

### Practical roles and responsibilities

These are **proposed contractual allocations**, informed by the documented OEM/supplier workflows; actual customer procedures and contracts govern live responsibilities.

| Counterparty | What the counterparty supplies or decides | What this enterprise may deliver |
|---|---|---|
| OEM/design authority or receiving-quality buyer | Authorized design revision, inspection obligations, relevant datum/tolerance interpretation, permissible evidence substitution, acceptance and release decisions | A checked correspondence/insufficiency dossier and explicit alternatives against the stated obligations |
| Supplier quality organization | Authorized FAIR/QIF outputs, producing revision, specimen/lot identity, inspection-program context, permission to process/disclose its records | A privately scoped reconciliation of its evidence and an actionable request for missing metadata; no assumption that supplier and buyer data are freely interchangeable |
| Measurement laboratory or internal metrology team | Available equipment/procedure, fixture and coordinate registration, uncertainty/calibration evidence, sample identity, capacity and quote | A narrowly specified evidence request or remeasurement work package; the laboratory supplies and stands behind the actual measurements |
| Digital-engineering integrator / our enterprise | Adapter construction, verification, reproducible source links, exception handling and support | An auditable digital engineering deliverable within the contracted scope; separate optional integration work |

Capvidia's current OEM guidance describes persistent identifiers connecting design requirements, plans and results, together with derivative validation and PLM/QIF exchange. Its CompareVidia product page explicitly assigns derivative-model validation uses to OEMs and incoming revised/translated CAD checks to suppliers. These are established roles and capabilities, not our inventions. [C1–C2]

The offer cannot infer missing specimen identity, reconstruct an unavailable authority revision, certify metrology uncertainty from a file's nominal values, or authorize machining/part release. A geometric match supports one link in the evidence chain; it does not establish every other link. A change in material-condition modifier, datum frame, tolerance zone, sampling plan or intended decision requires its own check.

The 2024 NIST/CCAM integration report and the earlier NIST–MTC study already supply important integration methods. The prior dossier records their findings and the source identities. No new search here established an absence of current commercial coverage. Vendor feature statements establish available advertised capabilities; they are not an independent benchmark of reliability or achievable savings in this packet. Vendor percentage ROI claims are not parameters for this edition.

**Negative control:** a recipient with a complete, correctly configured existing QIF/PLM inspection chain and sufficient persistent identifiers should use it. The instrument should recognize that situation. An unfavorable result there does not invalidate a narrower heterogeneous-legacy service, but neither can that service claim to improve an already solved task.

## 3. Evidence acquisition is often the next useful action

The smallest additional evidence depends on the unresolved obligation. Requests should name the source object and decision affected, rather than ask a customer to upload an entire unrestricted engineering archive.

| Missing support | Potential request | What receipt of it would still not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Authority model or revision equivalence | An authorized native/derivative comparison report, revision identity, or the applicable released derivative | Same physical specimen or successful manufacture |
| Provider-local feature name | The relevant inspection-program feature definition, persistent identifier mapping, geometry reference, or coordinate registration | Equivalent tolerance semantics unless separately checked |
| Datum/material-condition interpretation | Applicable datum-system and characteristic definitions, including modifiers and units | Measurement uncertainty or repeatability |
| Specimen/lot correspondence | Permitted serial/lot record and sampling relation | That every specimen in a lot conforms |
| Measurement adequacy | Applicable procedure, uncertainty statement, calibration/fixture evidence, or a scoped laboratory quote | Authority to substitute that result under the buyer's acceptance policy |

A fresh physical measurement is a separately priced and authorized service, with lead time and sample availability. None was procured here. No public quote matching this packet, these characteristics, and the required uncertainty was obtained. Remeasurement costs and avoided costs must therefore remain unknown or explicitly assumed, not treated as observed savings.

## 4. Human-service comparator: disclose estimates instead of inventing a market quote

**No scope-matched public human-service price was established within this three-query search.** The inspected commercial pages offer contact/configuration or consultation rather than a directly comparable fixed price. Their existing presence makes a human/integrator outside option credible, but does not price it. [C2, C4–C5]

For a hybrid edition, a transparent team-hour scenario can be used until actual quotations or timed human trials exist. The scope below is one unfamiliar, authorized part/revision, two provider files, an available CAD derivative, the public reference material, and approximately 15–40 requested characteristics. It excludes generating new physical measurements and excludes a guarantee that every obligation can be resolved. These are **researcher estimates**, not measured labor or a compensation claim.

| Work component | Illustrative hours for an unfamiliar heterogeneous packet |
|---|---:|
| Intake, rights/scope and source/revision inventory | 2–4 |
| Characteristic and evidence mapping using existing tools | 4–10 |
| Geometry, datum and tolerance interpretation | 3–8 |
| Independent reproduction/check of material assertions | 2–6 |
| Report, exceptions and customer handoff | 1–3 |
| **Total estimated team hours** | **12–31** |

An assumed blended billed rate of **USD 125–225/hour** produces an illustrative human-service labor envelope of **USD 1,500–6,975**. These rates are scenario inputs, not a sourced market rate, salary, employee wage or quote. Tool licenses, data preparation, external metrology, taxes and unusual customer security/onboarding costs must be added where actually applicable. Do not silently set unknown license or integration costs to zero.

For an already configured, semantically complete recipient, a separate **2–8 hour** illustrative scenario gives **USD 250–1,800** at the same assumed rates. This is also unmeasured. It exposes an important failure region: the new instrument may lose its economic case against a recipient's inexpensive established workflow. The experiment must not reserve the efficient case for E+ while pricing H as universally manual.

No routine human-review duration should be imposed merely to generate cost. Record actual residual obligations and measured interaction where a trial is performed; label modeled review effort otherwise. Customer clarification, retries, rejected mappings and correction are costs. A report that is fast because it omits the hard requested obligations is not equivalent service.

For a genuinely new changed-purpose offering with no credible human equivalent, specify a buyer-value hypothesis and participation threshold instead of borrowing the above price. Customer savings, an avoided hypothetical accident, and estimated enterprise revenue are different quantities.

## 5. Commercial complementarity without assumed compatible demand

### What can be common across independent private projects

The reusable asset could contain QIF/STEP schema readers, typed characteristic checks, geometry/coordinate adapters, deterministic provenance reports, and independent verification. Each customer retains private models, specimen records, inspection outputs, decision thresholds and mission acceptance. A generic adapter can be commissioned without contributors pooling those private datasets. Its claimed reusable scope must be demonstrated on fresh projects, not inferred from a common filename extension.

Potential participants are independently sourced OEM quality teams, suppliers, laboratories and integrators. This note supplies no such actual coalition. A buyer may use its current system, hire an incumbent integrator, request missing records, defer, or decline. A supplier may decline disclosure; that can make the proposed service infeasible even when the software works.

### A conditional construction-and-delivery arrangement

An operating sequence worth simulating is:

1. A paid, bounded intake assessment establishes the customer's useful decision, authorized inputs, outside option and unresolved adaptation work. This earns revenue only when the agreed intake deliverable is accepted.
2. The enterprise offers either an individual project price or a conditional generic-adapter development milestone plus private project delivery. Each buyer sees its own outcome, rights, dependencies, total payments, expected timing, failure handling and alternative.
3. Construction begins only if committed financing and enterprise reserves cover the stated development and failure obligations. No fixed buyer count, equal split, compulsory coalition or fixed discount is assumed.
4. A generic milestone is evaluated on rights-cleared qualification inputs. Private acceptance is evaluated afresh on each customer's current mission. A generic pass does not certify any customer's parts.
5. Collections, partial delivery, refunds, withdrawal, support and maintenance are accounted for separately. A later customer can license or commission compatible adaptation under the stated rights; earlier customers do not automatically receive its private data.

Let F denote additional common construction/admission cost, a_i private adaptation and accepted-delivery cost, and s_i customer-specific support/failure exposure. A bundle needs sufficient expected net receipts to cover F + sum(a_i + s_i), but that accounting condition alone does not establish buyer participation or liquidity. Each buyer must prefer the offered expected outcome, customer effort and downside to its actual outside option. A researcher-specified value distribution is a simulation assumption; selecting buyers from that distribution is not autonomous market discovery.

The technical contribution alone, commercial coordination alone, and their combination should be tested separately. Coordination may help finance F; the instrument may reduce a_i. Neither effect is guaranteed. If each buyer already has an efficient complete tool, there may be no useful F to construct. If the common adapter does not generalize, pooling only subsidizes unrelated bespoke work.

### Timing, default and common failure

Upfront deposits create cash and obligations together. Refundable development deposits are not earned revenue, and restricted/refund-exposed balances are not freely available surplus. A positive expected margin can coexist with an unaffordable prefunding requirement. The cash model needs dated outflows for construction, private delivery, serving and support; dated milestone acceptance/collections; and reserves for the applicable refund or remediation promises.

Model correlated generic-parser/geometry errors across projects, customer-specific missing provenance, participant withdrawal, payment default, partial acceptance and maintenance/version costs. Do not assume failures are independent merely because buyers are different. A financing arrangement cannot make absent specimen identity true. When evidence is insufficient, the agreed assessment may still be useful, but a substitution contract must fail or follow its pre-agreed scope-change process.

No numerical fee, demand forecast, collection or profit is supplied here. The arrangement is a hypothesis ready for economic testing once the technical acceptance rows and construction/delivery work are available.

## 6. Retention rights and authority

For the public research artifacts, preserve the downloaded dataset notices, attribution and modification records. NIST's licensing policy distinguishes employee-created non-SRD material, software, SRD and extramural works. Applying one blanket label to all downloaded engineering material would lose those distinctions. [C6] The exact source notices already retained in `round2_industry_sources/` remain controlling provenance.

For private operation, the contract should distinguish processing permission from redistribution, training and sublicensing. Keep customer CAD, measurements, programs and confidential clarification outside the shared capability. Retain only rights-cleared generic code, allowed schema adapters, test material and evidence. Commercial SDK/tool access is not an unrestricted right to redistribute that tool or every derivative asset.

The protected network can offer an admitted generic capability to currently eligible agents, with capability identity, dependencies, limitations, cost evidence and scope. Customer access permission and mission execution authority are separate. A successor must recheck the new design revision, provider records, mission semantics and acceptance requirements. Expired credentials or acceptance of an earlier part cannot be revived by a cached qualification result.

## 7. Selection and experiment gate

**Conditional GO** requires a complete, independently inspectable Plate requirement-to-geometry/result trace and at least one useful assertion or contradiction outside the supplied mappings. The next experiment should compare ordinary use, a strong existing expert workflow, and the retained instrument under the same files, scope and budgets. The human-service envelope above is a modeled comparator until observed.

Before fresh confirmation, freeze the recipient mission, outputs, tool access, allowed sources, acceptance conditions and cost ledger. Give all expert arms existing NIST graphs and conventional CAD/QIF methods. A same-content ordinary library is required for any graph-specific claim. Same-label matching can be a diagnostic failure case, but cannot be the sole ordinary-use or expert baseline.

Stop or narrow the offering if only extraction of supplied answers is achieved, if missing source identity makes all useful requested work indeterminate, if the retained asset silently overclaims equivalence, or if an existing complete method already serves the recipient at lower accepted cost. No protected tasks should be resampled to obtain a positive result. Training or Gym work is optional and needs an identifiable failure, explicit update and prospective test; it is not a mandatory loop.

The credible contribution, if confirmed, is an enterprise-built instrument that makes a difficult evidence assessment reusable across source conventions and newly checked purposes. It is not invention of STEP, QIF, persistent identifiers, geometry comparison, inspection planning, or a digital thread. The economic hypothesis remains open until complete costs, outside options, participation and cash timing are evaluated.
