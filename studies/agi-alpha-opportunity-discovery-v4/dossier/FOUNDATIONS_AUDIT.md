## 1. Transformational Intelligence: what the paper actually establishes

The paper distinguishes the authorized intent charter, the operational formulation, and the instrument used to evaluate an outcome (printed pp. 2–3). A change to one is not automatically a change to another. A new representation or workflow may legitimately serve the same purpose, while a changed material purpose requires a separately authorized comparison. An evaluator can itself be defective; changing it requires independent validation, preserving the earlier measurement epoch and rescoring the competing methods on a common basis (p. 12).

The transformation package is `T = (F', C_T, L_T, W_T)` (p. 7): a replacement formulation, executable construction, realization in the original setting, and a witness. The witness must explain the binding restriction, why it is instrumental rather than mandatory, what replaces it, and an observation that could disconfirm the claimed advantage. An internally valid transformed output must still map to the original outcome obligations. Construction actions, costs and side effects remain part of this bridge; an output decoder cannot undo an unauthorized action.

The paper does not reserve value for novel algorithms. Novelty to an agent, an organization, a task family and science are separate claims (p. 4). A known method can be valuable in a recipient who could not previously apply it. Conversely, if the same effective policy was already available within the original formulation and budget, the result may be better strategy discovery or execution rather than a changed attainable frontier.

Its executed causal illustration is exact under a supplied two-hypothesis finite model. Passive observations have identical laws in both worlds, so every passive procedure has at most 50% correct identification under the uniform prior. An already authorized intervention separates the worlds and gives accuracy `1 − 2^(−m−1)`, reaching `8191/8192` with at most 12 queries. An ordinary unrestricted Bellman planner matches the transformed policy. The researcher supplied the hypotheses, action API, loss, and distinction between candidate frames. No LLM invented the causal model or intervention. These facts occur in the abstract, pp. 4–5, and Appendix A (pp. 19–20).

The architecture permits complementary exploratory changes that fail alone. The elementary pair example (p. 9) demonstrates that greedy retention can prevent a beneficial bundle from being reached; it does not justify unlimited exploration or automatic admission. The campaign must freeze finalists, bridges, comparators, model settings, budgets and sampling rules before fresh confirmation. The old solver supplied with the discovered formulation is an explicit diagnostic (pp. 10, 14). The correct unit for a claim about learning to discover is an independent complete campaign, not hundreds of tasks descended from one invention (pp. 14–15).

**Required preservation in the new edition:** use the framework as a construction-and-falsification requirement. Do not relabel ordinary caching, graph assembly or a stronger prompt as new science; measure the effect on the recipient. Do not state that the prior paper demonstrated general transformational intelligence. Preserve negative controls in which reframing should stop.

## 2. Invent the Economy: the technical construction and arrangement are one object

The economic possibility package is `P = (T, C, A, F, R, W)` (p. 3): technical capability and realizations; counterparties; transfers and responsibilities; financing and delivery schedule; rights and authority; and evidence. The package couples these quantities, rather than assuming they can be optimized independently. A coalition cannot waive an individual participant's purpose or turn aggregate surplus into consent.

The five evidence stages are technical possibility, modeled viability, accepted arrangement, realized exchange and repeatable market (p. 3). Each requires additional evidence. A useful executable program and an internally consistent ledger cannot imply real buyers, contracts or receipts. Simulated or researcher-supplied participant preferences remain assumptions.

### Exact numerical reconciliation

All original model amounts are in thousands of a common numeraire, interpreted illustratively as USD. The main case has four hypothetical buyers, success payment `q=300`, value `v=360`, delivery cost `d=75`, adoption cost zero, shared-core cost `K=600`, no added route/coordination costs, common qualification probability `p=0.8`, and initial cash `B0=900` (pp. 4–8).

| Quantity | Correct calculation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Successful receipts | `4 × 300 = 1,200` | Conditional on common technical qualification and all represented adaptations succeeding |
| Successful complete cost in this model | `600 + 4 × 75 = 900` | Development first, then delivery cost, then collection |
| Successful contribution | `1,200 − 900 = 300` | $300,000 conditional realization, not expectation |
| Expected contribution | `0.8 × (1,200 − 300) − 600 = 120` | $120,000 under the supplied common probability |
| Required prefunding | `600 + 300 = 900` | $900,000 before collection; conditional invoices are not cash |
| Failure loss | `−600` | All buyers share the common failure, so four buyers do not diversify it into independent failures |
| Failure terminal cash | `900 − 600 = 300` | Before any second project |
| Expected terminal cash | `0.2 × 300 + 0.8 × 1,200 = 1,020` | Includes $900,000 initial capital |
| Expected surplus per buyer | `0.8 × (360 − 300) = 48` | Supplied risk-neutral participation calculation |
| Route burden of 40 per delivered buyer | `120 − 0.8 × 4 × 40 = −8` | Added burden can eliminate modeled viability |

With three buyers, the success contribution is `75` but expected contribution is `−60`. This is a particularly useful falsifier against reporting only favorable paths. The original two-state model assumes that after common qualification all specified adaptations succeed and pay. Partial delivery, default, withdrawal, refunds and support failures are explicitly outside the main example and named as necessary extensions for natural proposals (p. 4). The new edition should add them where relevant without calling them corrections to the original declared assumptions.

The factorial comparison changes shared versus separate construction and single versus coordinated binding commitments (pp. 5, 7). Only their combination is viable in this restricted reference case. An ordinary unrestricted enumerator supplied with the same options matches the result. The theorem is not a claim that bilateral entrepreneurship is universally impossible: an entrepreneur able to coordinate, forecast later sales or invest speculatively can reach the same package.

The software screens 60 supplied subset/mode records and verifies a 2,240-case parameter grid (pp. 7–9, Appendix A). It does not construct the core, find actual customers or execute a sale. All 50 tests and 540 admitted/1,700 rejected cases concern the selected finite parameter model, not business prevalence.

### Inheritance, finance and token boundaries

The staged example preserves a 900 reserve after the first successful project, leaving 300 earned contribution available (p. 9). A second project with two buyers and setup 300 has positive expected contribution 60 but requires 450 prefunding; it is unfunded under the reserve rule. Setup 150 requires 300 and yields expected contribution 210. Those reductions are supplied conditions, not observed learning gains. The staged expectation is `120 + 0.8 × 210 = 288`, with branches retaining first-stage failure. The new study must measure or explicitly model any changed construction requirement rather than inherit these favorable values.

The paper separates customer outcomes, venture receipts/obligations and native-token operations (pp. 12–14). Native routes can provide coordination value and impose costs. Issuance or burning is not operating revenue; service success does not identify token price. A utility description does not determine legal classification. Manager's USDC6 route and Jobs/Alpha AGIALPHA18 routes remain different. No shareholder/equity/dividend right is created by this paper. Its intended utility hypothesis is commissioning, settlement and appropriately implemented provider/capacity commitments, tested against a matched nonnative alternative.

**Required preservation in the new edition:** develop the technical artifact and commercial package together. Keep simulated choice, modeled cash, actual tool execution and real counterparties in distinct fields. Permit stop. A structurally positive expected margin still needs a cash-prefix test and capacity/rights checks. Price and participation feasibility do not establish incentive compatibility, truthful revelation or a real market equilibrium.

## 3. Transformational Autonomous Enterprises: useful inheritance without inherited acceptance

This research edition is dated 23 October 2026 and identifies itself as MONTREAL.AI / Writer #4. Preserve that source attribution. The new user-requested edition must use its own exact Vincent Boucher author line rather than silently editing the historical original.

The retained capability is `K = (id, program, schema, dependencies, scope, provenance, cost)` (printed p. 4). Generic module qualification may be reused only under complete unchanged identity and evaluator-owned policy. Current mission inputs and outputs are always freshly checked. A descendant begins with `currentProof:null` and `authority:[]`; history is retained as history. The conditional substitution proposition on p. 5 depends on complete dependency keys, deterministic checks, sound identity comparisons and fresh mission obligations. Hashes do not prove semantics, rights or hostile-host security.

The experiment supplies weighted bill-of-materials and Boolean remediation-impact semantics on directed acyclic graphs (pp. 2–4). The normalized graph machinery transfers; the algebra changes. Multiplicity of paths must contribute repeatedly to material demand but only once to unique affected services. A diamond graph is therefore a meaningful semantic control. Both algorithms are established and supplied; the compiler assembles supported modules. The paper does not claim unrestricted invention of graph algorithms or discovered market demand.

Candidate/source hashing remains. Setup, model/assistant authoring, engineering, maintenance and external review are not captured as a complete monetary cost. The paper explicitly leaves its US$100-to-US$10,000 commercial target unproven (pp. 12, 19–21).

The initial timing order reversed: reuse median 60.08 ms, direct 72.64 ms. The final 59.87/54.92 medians are not grounds for a universal speed assertion. The original lineage metadata could name a capability as its own parent when content stayed unchanged. Correct outputs did not prove correct provenance.

### Connected execution and its boundaries

The paper actually invokes source APIs in AGI ALPHA, AGIJobManager, AGIJobsv0 and AGI-Fleet-Linux, using exact transferred outputs and preserved mission identity (pp. 6–7). Its new artifacts also pass through five real contract implementations on one disposable local EVM (pp. 14–17). This is source execution, not production host qualification or customer trading. The graphs and four-source deliveries were computed first, then local contracts posted/assigned/settled against precomputed bytes after fresh checking. The temporal order must not be rewritten into a production job award triggering a Fleet worker.

The concrete existing token mechanisms include validator stake, worker collateral, MARK reserves, job escrow, worker and validator rewards, burns, slashing and refunds. Under the retained local fixture, an 80-token accepted price yields worker net 75.24, validator net 3.96, burn 0.8, with 78 unused tokens returned from 158 funded. These are mock token units, not dollars, customer revenue or profit. Historical observations and getters are time-bound and require fresh verification before any current-state assertion.

The application separates proposer, evaluator and authority enforcement but all run under one controller and host. It is algorithmic separation, not institutional independence or a hostile operating-system sandbox. The local load result (672 valid tasks, one/two Node workers) does not qualify geographically distributed Fleet execution. Any current protected sharing or settlement claim needs current-source evidence and the authorized owner process.

## 4. What a real synthesis adds

The new contribution should make one exact asset cross three boundaries:

1. **Transformation:** a need leads to a binding restriction, an executable alternative and an original-purpose witness. Its construction and failed alternatives are recorded.
2. **Economic possibility:** that same artifact identity is the common capability in a package with distinct participant requirements, outside options, timing, rights, risks and cash. Technical success alone and arrangement alone remain counterfactuals.
3. **Successor:** a fresh recipient obtains the stable parts under eligible access, then changes or supplies new mission semantics and earns fresh acceptance and authority. Reuse cost, transfer cost and negative transfer are observed or individually labeled assumptions.

This is more than three literature summaries if the program actually joins the artifact, acceptance, cash and inheritance records. A graph node name or copied title is insufficient. Conversely, an ordinary library with the same bytes and rights may match retrieval; that result should be retained without discarding a genuine transferable capability.

## 5. Proposed concrete synthesis test (prospective recommendation, not executed evidence)

A demanding bounded domain could be **executable compatibility and reproducibility assessments for scientific Python software upgrades**, motivated independently by actual upstream changes, migration notes and documented user requirements. The outcome is a reproducible migration/compatibility report plus a passing adapted artifact, not a list of warnings. Distinct beneficiaries can need a numerical workflow, a data-processing workflow and a service wrapper. Their requirements should be gathered before identifying any common instrument. Do not preselect only tasks known to share a winning repair.

Permit an expert agent to discover that several needs share a hidden obstruction—for example, test environments collapse distinct dependency/runtime contexts and thereby make reported regressions ambiguous. It may construct an environment-and-contract instrument with executable differential probes, source-bound acceptance and workload-specific adapters. This is only an example hypothesis; the agent must be free to find a different mechanism or stop. Compare an ordinary well-specified frontier run U, an established expert workflow E with currently available tools, and E+ with the frozen new instrument. Provide all configurations the same source evidence, permissions and budgets. Existing tools such as standard test runners or dependency solvers remain strong conventional controls, not renamed inventions.

The technical acceptance includes original numerical/behavioral contracts and reproducible relevant failures. A diagnostic supplies the newly found formulation to E. A fresh recipient starts without donor history or protected answers, receives the admitted executable artifact only, and confronts unfamiliar software and a differently framed mission. Recheck schema, source, dependencies and checker identity. Include a deliberately incompatible semantic change where reusing the prior result would be wrong, a revoked grant, a changed dependency, and a recipient already equipped with an equivalent instrument.

Commercial exploration can jointly select customer-specific scope, consortium/sponsor versus individual development, payment milestones, deposits or credit, and failure responsibility. Buyer preferences and any rates remain sourced or explicit simulations; participation is not compelled. A coupled technical/arrangement ablation tests whether constructing the instrument alone or coordination alone is sufficient. Pricing should optimize against explicit outside options and preserve strict participant surplus where required. Simulate nonparticipation, correlated technical failure, partial delivery, withdrawal, default/refund, support and congestion. Calculate every cash prefix and keep refundable obligations separate from spendable funds.

Declare before confirmation whether a successor offering qualifies at a useful quality, maximum complete cost, turnaround and funding ceiling. If E already qualifies, report better economics rather than a newly feasible offering. If all routes lose after complete development and evaluation, preserve the executable construction while reject commercial admission. If a technical/arrangement combination qualifies but assumptions are synthetic, report a modeled feasibility crossing, not market creation. A later real pilot can validate willingness to pay and complete operating cost without revising the history.

The cheap operational-expertise experiment should remain separately proportionate: a small versioned preparation/decomposition/verification method, limited development trials and fresh useful jobs under matched model/tools. The main construction campaign must not be used as an inflated mandatory evaluation cost for every inexpensive workflow improvement. Neither experiment demonstrates better discovery policy merely by transferring a stronger artifact; that requires independently initialized campaigns.

## 6. Claims to avoid

- “The three papers establish profitable autonomous enterprise.” They establish frameworks and bounded executed/synthetic results with explicit missing market evidence.
- “Qualification reuse reduces total work by 96%.” The denominator is generic qualification cases.
- “Four buyers diversify the 20% core failure.” They share the failure event.
- “The original venture earns $300,000 expected profit.” Expected contribution is $120,000; $300,000 is conditional success.
- “A positive expected contribution funds development.” Funding depends on the worst authorized cash prefix and liabilities.
- “Inherited proof or membership permits the next action.” Mission acceptance and authority are separate current obligations.
- “A registry/graph creates the saving.” Identical-content ordinary-library controls are required for a graph-specific claim.
- “The system learned to invent better” from a stronger inherited instrument alone. Artifact inheritance and discovery-policy improvement are separate experiments.
