# Thermal opportunity: independent pre-selection experiment challenge

**27 October 2026. Design review only; no thermal implementation or new experiment executed.**

## Decision and minimum useful scope

**Conditional go for a computational instrument study; no-go for a flagship based on the six-component example alone.** Wang–Ban already supplies the equivalence and three-face remedy. Goal-oriented experimental design is established and executable. The potential contribution is constructing a reliable bridge from evidence to a consequential finite-device decision and a physically admissible information-acquisition plan, then transferring it to different work. It need not be a new theorem, but it must outperform an adequately equipped recipient or deliver a demonstrably new instrument. [1–3]

Before construction, obtain two independently motivated mission briefs from published engineering cases or authorized owners. Preserve geometry, heat loads, allowed modifications, thermal acceptance, evidence provenance, deadline and budget. Distinguish source-specified requirements from researcher-supplied ones. If adequate missions or measurement records cannot be obtained, retain a clearly labeled computational methods study; do not call its synthetic traces measured evidence or its beneficiaries customers.

A demanding bounded scope is one anisotropic substrate in a finite three-dimensional layered assembly, nonuniform heat input, interface resistance, and physically specified edge/back boundary conditions. Include uncertain nuisance quantities where the evidence warrants them. Start with steady operation; add a transient recipient only after time-dependent validation. More unknowns alone do not make the problem meaningful.

## Required executable construction

**1. Evidence-to-model adapter.** Ingest traces or explicitly supplied resolved invariants, units, noise/calibration bounds, coating/interface assumptions, registered signed axes, specimen identity and orientation information. Preserve both the raw evidence and transformations. A digitized published curve is not an original calibrated trace. If fitting traces, include correlations and nuisance quantities in the compatible set; fixing them conveniently can manufacture certainty.

**2. Exact global-equivalence component.** For one registered face, implement the complete family

\[
K(\mu)=\begin{pmatrix}S+\mu\mu^{T}/c&\mu\\\mu^{T}&c\end{pmatrix},\qquad c>0,\ S\succ0.
\]

Intersect it with independently justified material constraints. Check positive definiteness and equality of invariants symbolically or with exact arithmetic for rational qualification cases. Include both two-face branches and signed three-face reconstruction. These are prior-art components. Noise replaces exact equalities with justified compatibility regions. Extra same-face frequencies or a uniform rear Robin boundary must not be credited with resolving the missing directions. [1]

**3. Application forward solver.** Solve the original finite-geometry heat problem with complete anisotropic flux. Compute the declared mission quantity. The preferred bounded certification target is heater-weighted mean temperature **only if that is independently the useful acceptance quantity**. Do not substitute it for a peak limit. The planar observation theorem does not certify this application solver. Equivalent surface models can have different application consequences; calculate them.

**4. Separate verification.** Use a genuinely distinct discretization or independently written reference implementation; compare manufactured anisotropic solutions, energy balance, boundary flux, and mesh/time refinement. Include imperfect interfaces and rotated tensors. Shared code paths are not independent checks. Finite-grid agreement supports numerical verification; it is not physical validation. Reserve a discretization allowance in acceptance and refuse marginal decisions lacking a defensible error bound.

**5. Three honest output types.** Return (i) two admissible countermodels with verified opposite mission verdicts, (ii) a globally bounded original-purpose verdict, or (iii) unresolved. A sampled search can establish a counterexample; failure to find one cannot certify its absence. For small bounded ambiguity coordinates, attempt interval branch-and-bound or justified analytical bounds that include solver error. If this is unaffordable, return unresolved. Keep exact observational equivalence, numerical mission consequence, and global certification as separate evidence fields.

**Preferred certificate path.** Complete exact two-face invariants have at most two branches under the theorem's conditions; evaluate both. Independently established symmetry, including an applicable zero coupling for a registered (010) specimen, can remove the ambiguity and must be honored. Noisy point estimates do not inherit the finite-branch claim. Derive outer enclosures covering the entire compatible set, including calibration and registration uncertainty; reject intervals crossing uncontrolled denominators.

For a fixed mesh, fixed load vector \(f\), and positive-definite thermal operator, verified bounds \(0\prec A_-\preceq A\preceq A_+\) imply

\[
f^TA_+^{-1}f\le f^TA^{-1}f\le f^TA_-^{-1}f.
\]

Divide by total heater power for the corresponding heater-weighted temperature rise. Use a common reference ambient or otherwise justify load independence from uncertain coefficients. Entrywise conductivity intervals are not matrix-order bounds; construct and verify the latter. This is a **discrete** certificate unless a separate continuum error bound exists. Peak-temperature results remain checked numerical consequences/counterexamples, never globally certified from samples. Overconservative bounds may correctly return unresolved.

**6. Measurement planner.** Evaluate goal-oriented/decision-focused designs using the same priors and mission loss as E. Add global ambiguity checking and explicit specimen/measurement constraints. Each proposed acquisition needs an accessible face or justified alternative, orientation registration, dimensions, preparation, coating, temperature compatibility, required apparatus, destructive effects, timing and cost. Three-face algebra is not evidence of three accessible faces. A laboratory-reviewed plan is operational evidence; a public-specification-compatible plan is only catalogue admissibility. Simulated additional responses test within-model planning, not real measurement performance.

**7. Evidence-identity grouping.** Discover possible common acquisitions from separately supplied missions. Group only compatible lot/specimen state, treatment, temperature regime, orientation uncertainty, measurement model and rights. Material name alone is insufficient. Preserve declined and incompatible groups. A new calibration or treatment invalidates reuse where relevant.

## Bounded comparison and protection

Use two development jobs, six protected confirmation jobs from the independently motivated mission families, and two fresh recipient jobs with a different purpose. These counts are a proposed screening budget, not a statistical guarantee. Do not sample until a desired mix of outcomes appears. Separate deliberately constructed theorem/safety controls from useful-job outcomes.

H is a scoped specialist engineering/metrology engagement; obtain a quote or label hours/rates as estimates. U gets a competent brief, the same frontier model, tools, evidence and budget. **E receives the published equivalence family, both two-face branches, three-face solution, global-search methods, goal-oriented OED and ordinary engineering verification.** It can abstain, use conservative designs, commission available measurements or propose alternatives. E+ adds the frozen retained instrument. A local least-squares fitter is a diagnostic, not E. [1–3]

Use identical source evidence and allowed acquisitions. Freeze resource caps after an infrastructure pilot and before protected tasks; include preparation, retries, correction, verification, tools, tokens, latency and customer effort. At matched accepted quality, count all construction/assessment costs separately from per-job operation. First compare fixed resources; expansion funded by surplus is a later experiment. E−U measures operation; E+−E measures incremental capability. Without actual frontier-agent trials, report instrument results rather than measured Operational Alpha.

An independent evaluator creates and hashes fresh mission packets, acquisition outcomes, acceptance rules and scoring code before construction. Keep them inaccessible to development agents. Freeze source/dependencies, uncertainty policy, planner, budgets and stopping conditions before release. Search history and failed candidates stay preserved. Historical published examples are qualification material, not leakage-resistant confirmation.

Use adversarial controls for wrong face sign, unregistered orientation, correlated calibration error, a falsely fixed nuisance parameter, a nonplanar observation outside theorem scope, unavailable face preparation, incompatible specimen identities, and an existing conservative design that already passes. Controls must test refusal as well as success. A certificate accepted despite an admissible reversing countermodel is a fatal soundness failure for that certificate policy; correction requires fresh confirmation.

Give E the E+ formulation on a separate diagnostic run. This separates formulation value from implementation efficiency. Give the transferred instrument and an ordinary library with identical contents/permissions to matched recipients; do not attribute content reuse to graph structure.

## Original purpose, fresh recipient and feasibility

An illustrative donor purpose is selecting a mounting orientation under a justified thermal-resistance or mean-temperature criterion. A fresh recipient could assess non-collocated thermal cross-talk on a different geometry and load pattern. Rescaling the same solution to another power limit is a weak transfer test. Cross-response bounds require separate validation; quadratic-form polarization is one established option, possibly too conservative. These are proposed examples, not established needs. Protect real mission thresholds from result-driven revision.

The recipient receives executable source, schema, validators and dependency identities, not just the donor's tensor or narrative. It must establish its own geometry, loads, acceptance, uncertainty and authority. Reusing a better artifact tests inheritance. Demonstrating better invention requires a separate fresh agent to construct a new adapter or acquisition formulation using a frozen method, compared with E; do not conflate them.

Predeclare a feasibility crossing as the recipient's accepted outcome within its actual analysis/acquisition budget and deadline. If both arms qualify, report better economics. If neither qualifies, preserve the refusal. Correctly preventing an unsupported design commitment is useful but does not establish profit or a newly feasible offering.

## Commercial and measurement boundaries

Compare private expert delivery, instrument-assisted private delivery, coordinated acquisition with E, and their combination only where independently observed needs overlap. Buyers may use an existing lab, accept a conservative design, wait or decline. Do not create compatible customers or tune hypothetical prices after seeing scientific outcomes.

Without physical trials, observed quantities are code construction, numerical outputs, certificate/counterexample checks, execution resources and transfer behavior. Sources support theorem scope, published evidence and vendor capabilities. Acquisition outcomes, demand, prices, queues, default and cash flows are modeled unless actually observed. An executable procurement plan is not a booked experiment. Restrict claims accordingly; complete actual costs can remain unknown.

The minimum scientifically useful outcome is a verified original-purpose counterexample or defensible bound plus an admissible next-information plan on consequential finite-device missions. If E already supplies that at comparable complete cost, the new instrument has not established incremental Alpha. Stop if the study remains supplied invariants plus textbook algebra, evidence provenance is inadequate, no original decision changes, the plan requires inaccessible measurements, or certification costs overwhelm its value.
