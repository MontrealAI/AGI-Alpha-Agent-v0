# Four-job hosted operating-method experiment

## Accepted outcomes at the common resource ceiling

| Configuration | Accepted jobs | Mandatory criteria met | Self-reported tool calls | Answer words |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| U: competent ordinary use | 4/4 | 24/24 | 7 | 1,239 |
| E: established expert workflow | 4/4 | 24/24 | 6 | 1,185 |
| E+: E plus retained prompting method | 3/4 | 23/24 | 8 | 1,207 |

All three received the same source packet, ordinary tools, eight-call ceiling and 1,600-word ceiling.
The stronger manufacturing instrument and its completed reports were not supplied to this cheap prompting
comparison. E+ received the versioned method learned during Plate development. No answer was corrected
or retried after grading.

**This packet does not demonstrate added accepted-quality Alpha from E over U, or from the retained method
over E.** E's one fewer reported call than U is an observed resource tally, not a complete cost saving or
causal estimate of expertise. E+ reported one failed calculation execution that it corrected within budget.
All arms produced substantial correct source work.

## The enhanced answer's identifier inconsistency

The one failed criterion is H4.3, exact specimen identification. E+'s answer correctly gives component ID
528 and the full specimen serial in its trace. Its later instruction asks the operator to verify
“serial 528’s specimen.” The file-local component ID is not that specimen serial. Under strict acceptance,
the actionable answer must resolve that contradiction. No new fatal-error category was added; the other
five H4 criteria pass, and all other jobs pass.

The precise source evidence and both answer locations are recorded in Eplus.json. A lenient reading that
accepted the correct trace while ignoring the conflicting instruction would score E+ at 4/4. That is
reported as a sensitivity, not a corrected primary score. Either reading establishes no additional
accepted-quality advantage for the retained method.

## Evidence and limits

U.json, E.json and Eplus.json preserve every mandatory criterion, its result, answer-line evidence,
reason, answer hash, resource record and unexecuted checks. The mandatory criteria and fatal-error list
were frozen before dispatch. The same independent AI-assisted evaluator adjudicated each unchanged answer
once. This is not an independent human metrology review.

Whitespace word counts were recomputed from the exact files. Tool counts, corrections and execution
failures are preserved as recipient self-reports; the grader did not reconstruct them from an independent
execution trace. Active model latency, inference tokens, billing, complete delivery costs and actual
customer effort remain unknown. No revenue or customer savings are inferred.

There is one run per arm over four related jobs from the same historical assembly. The trial establishes
these particular outcomes; it does not establish population superiority, method-caused harm, or an absence
of value from expertise on more demanding work. The ordinary arm was permitted competent evidence work,
tools and corrections.
