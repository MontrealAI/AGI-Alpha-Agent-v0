# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL: useful work and reusable methods

## Prospective boundary

This is a new additive study coordinated through issue #4850. It does not replace
or broaden the Economics of Autonomous Work edition (#4901), its 69-file freeze,
36 primary jobs, historical negative findings or conditional human comparison.
The new protocol, implementation, input/source generators, independent acceptance
and analysis rules are hashed before confirmation input generation. Development
outputs and unsuccessful attempts are retained separately. Confirmation runs once.
No failed primary case is replaced; any retry would remain a charged extra attempt.

Primary question: what useful digital work does the available Agent configuration
deliver, and what reusable methods can contribute to a later mission? The economic
comparator remains competent traditional workers using ordinary non-AI software,
including their own templates, skills and learning. Published price/performance
evidence and explicit sensitivities are used; no new human trial is claimed.

## Actual implementation and missing operational boundary

Execute original pinned AGI ALPHA workbench, Jobs work-order compiler and Strategy
Succession worker, plus a separately authored source-evidence adapter installed
through the original workbench adapter API. Strongest existing local methods,
including SciPy/HiGHS planning specialists, remain available. The study does not
create keys, credentials, an operator journal, a background service, a contract
transaction or a new deployed security boundary. Original held actions stay held.

The authenticated browser-to-approved-executor path requires an existing approved
operator home/token; none is supplied in this study environment. It is NOT RUN
unless those already authorized resources become available without creating new
credentials. The pure local work pipeline is executable. Browser-local computation
and recorded replay are different modes. Neither is relabeled as approved remote
execution or a complete deployed Agent installation. Whole-system commercial
cost remains unidentified while material authoring, attention and deployment
costs are unknown.

## Primary portfolio: 42 jobs, seven bounded families

| Work family | Fresh primary instances | Actual tested portion | Acceptance |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Software and testing | 6 | Three closed-interval repairs, three sorted-unique implementations and comparisons | Correct patch/retained function, exact regression outputs, original failures and algorithm observations |
| Structured analysis | 6 | Exact grouped two-column integer CSV reports | Counts, sums, extrema, input reconciliation and formula-safe export |

| Documents/presentations | 6 | Source-backed briefing and printable HTML slides | Exact content, correct citations, complete required coverage and readable structure |
| Planning/operations | 6 | Six-, nine- and twelve-job resource-constrained schedules | Independent precedence/capacity/deadline/lock/objective checks |
| Scientific computation | 6 | Exact polynomial integration and honest quadrature assessment | Independently computed rationals, errors and tolerance verdicts |
| Web/documentation | 6 | Executable URI and UTF-8 examples in supplied Markdown | Real standard-library results, corrected examples, retained originals/errors |

Each family has two low, two medium and two high cases within its finite grammar.
Instances vary inputs, not occupational identity. There are eight principal input
templates (two for software, one for each other family), not 42 unrelated types of
work. Context names do not expand the tested grammar. All jobs have a shared
employer delivery allowance of one workday; system resource caps are separate.
There is no semantic understanding test of arbitrary books, websites, scientific
claims or unrestricted repositories. Human responsibilities include choosing the
right sources/objectives, authorizing actions, judging business/physical validity
and handling untested exceptions. Geography enters prices and context assumptions,
not a fictional distribution of observed workers.

Primary generator seeds: portfolio 2026102507, planning 2026102517. Produce all
inputs only after the freeze. Order is randomized within each execution track,
deterministically under the seeds; the two tracks are not a globally interleaved
42-job randomized trial.
No later cohort is easier by design; fixed complexity strata remain visible.

## Connected construction, transfer and discovery

Planning follows PLANNING_PROTOCOL.md: one fresh connected three-phase journey,
actual receipt-derived repair, three constructed strategy ASTs if qualified,
matched inherited/rebuilt/equivalent-parent diagnostics and 12 fresh repair cases
in two complete-worker arms. Twenty-four transfer attempts are not 24 new jobs.
The strongest specialist may win; only the candidate's actual contribution counts
as evidence for it. Correctness qualification does not imply economic admission.

The source-evidence mechanism uses seed 2026102527. Twelve fresh parent briefs
receive changed evidence requirements. Compare full installed compilation with
verified inherited-module invocation and source-aware repair, randomized within
each pair. The inherited arm actually imports the exact retained module after
comparing its bytes with installed reviewed source, reads the parent brief, and
checks reusable versus recompiled claims independently. The module was authored
during study development, not invented inside a task. It is one reusable method,
not twelve distinct capabilities. Each changed brief feeds a presentation mission;
this is cross-artifact transfer within an evidence grammar, not broad intelligence.

Three successive cycles use the identical low/medium/high/medium complexity pattern.
Each cycle explores four full/incremental pairs and freezes a selection policy
before one fresh medium validation task is generated. Promote incremental operation
only if all four total delivery-CPU ratios are below .95 and both arms are accepted.
Otherwise retain the strong full compiler. This finite gate is conservative but
is not a population confidence statement. It tests bounded method selection and
when to stop searching; it does not generate an unrestricted new discovery engine.
Each cycle's exploration cost includes both candidates and all failures. Fifteen
parent constructions, 24 paired calls, 12 presentation transfers and three policy
validations yield **54 source-mechanism attempts**. These remain separate from the
42 primary jobs. The frozen finite search cap prevents endless debate/exploration.

Self-acceleration is declared prospectively as three successive positive increments
of independently verified useful capability per complete resource cost, at equal
task difficulty and available resources, with a positive change in that rate
supported by uncertainty analysis. A one-off extension, accumulated files, extra
agents, more compute, transfers or one faster method do not meet it. Missing
material costs blocks a positive full-cost acceleration conclusion. Report
observed method/selection metrics separately even if acceleration is not established.

## Evidence-supported review

REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md specifies 39 randomized review attempts (seed 2026102537):
six clean, 27 seeded defects across nine classes, six fabricated criticisms.
Creator, challenger and adjudicator are bounded deterministic roles; they are not
independent frontier-model votes. The adjudicator recomputes the cited contract
violation before any change. An independently authored Python acceptance layer
remains outside construction. Record misses, false alarms, rejected false
assertions, cost and the complete regression corpus. This is not a natural
prevalence estimate for professional document errors.

## Controls, refusal and finite load

Seed 2026102547 runs 16 control cases: cooperative pre-start STOP, injected
checkpoint interruption, simulated-clock time limit, explicit fresh restart,
eight unsupported/restricted/over-bound input cases, corrupted retained-code
refusal, source-identity refusal, immutable-output refusal and local pointer rollback.
The eight refused categories are arbitrary software, unstructured summary,
financial execution, physical action, private login, clinical prediction,
restricted personal data and excess input size. Refusal is successful containment,
not a delivered job. No live credentials, real financial route or physical system
is tested. Local pointer rollback is not a transaction reversal. No dollars can
be spent by the worker, so monetary-spend enforcement is NOT RUN.

Load uses the same 12 low/high cases (two from each non-planning family), seed
2026102507, in three batches each at concurrency 1, 2 and 4, randomized batch order:
108 attempts on 12 inputs. Each task executes in a separate process to avoid
overlapping cumulative child-CPU attribution. Measure accepted throughput,
submission-to-delivery latency, queue time, CPU, initial backlog and failures.
These are finite local batches, not an admitted fleet or continuous enterprise.
No load result is generalized to the planning/native-solver family or a remote API.

## Accounting, acceptance and analysis

Every operation retains its brief, inputs, outputs, source identity, independent
verdict, failures, events and timing. Complete local delivery includes worker
launch-to-exit plus independent acceptance and export. Worker-only telemetry is
secondary. The final receipt serialization/exit tail is disclosed. Planning and
non-planning boundaries differ slightly and are explicitly named. Per-process CPU
includes waited descendants; threaded solver CPU can exceed wall time. Host CPU
allocation and price are not inferred from logical-core count.

Separate delivery from improvement: original method construction, unsuccessful
candidates, parent reconstruction, qualification and retention are improvement;
useful mission execution and required checking are delivery. Both contribute to
overall adoption economics. Fixed task-time model/API calls are zero on the chosen
deterministic path; authoring-agent tokens/billing, full setup, employer attention,
deployment/support and maintenance are unknown. Null is never converted to zero.
The observed study clock is not an observed human timesheet.

Report acceptance counts with descriptive Wilson intervals, per-family median,
range and full distributions; no population-wide inference from purposive cases.
For paired mechanisms report every quality WIN/TIE/LOSS and paired timing/CPU
ratios with 2,000 task-level bootstrap resamples; do not treat internal iterations
as independent observations. Keep direct-specialist ties and losses prominent.
CPU savings, code reuse, newly supported task grammar, economic payback and
acceleration are distinct claims. Human time ratios are unidentifiable without
matched duration evidence; use predeclared break-even/sensitivity instead.

Economic inputs and assumptions are frozen in economics/PROSPECTIVE_ECONOMICS.md.
Maintain official observations, scenario assumptions and future hypotheses as
separate fields. No accepted fixture share is multiplied across the U.S. or global
labor pool. Actual revenue, cash margin and spendable reinvestment are null.
Bounded economic reinvestment is an explicitly labeled eight-cycle simulation;
actual local resource reuse is reported in seconds and work accounts, not cash.
