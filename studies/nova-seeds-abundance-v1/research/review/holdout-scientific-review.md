## Why the unflattering drift result occurs

Across the full twelve-epoch drift horizon, mean useful output is:

| Policy | Mean useful output |
|---|---:|
| Conservative fresh recalibration | 23.812500 |
| Adaptive expert | 22.755208 |
| Greedy | 13.125000 |
| Verified native / clone / disclosed / stationary exact expert | 11.125000 |

The adaptive expert earns 11.125 in the first epoch, when no prior diagnostic report exists. It then earns 23.8125 in each of epochs two through twelve, tying conservative recalibration. It cannot retrospectively recover the first epoch's output. Saying that it “wins under drift” without naming the static native comparator is too broad. It beats that policy; the conservative comparator wins the complete tested horizon.

The native-minus-adaptive difference is **−11.630208** units with a descriptive interval **[−12.346354, −10.914062]**. The native-minus-greedy difference is **−2.000000**, interval **[−2.687500, −1.343750]**. These are secondary descriptive intervals, not additional preregistered primary tests.

An actual preselected illustrative world, seed 101 at epoch 0, has job weights `[7, 11, 13, 13]` and bias `[-1.76, -1.76, -0.96, -0.96]`. The native and stationary expert plan calibrates at tick 0 and schedules the weight-11 job at tick 1 and the two weight-13 jobs at ticks 2 and 3. The latter measurements use a stale calibration; only 11 useful units survive. The greedy plan has the same nominal optimum but schedules a weight-13 job first, leaving 13 useful units after the same shift. It has no advance drift warning. The difference comes from the ordering of jobs among schedules that tie under the stationary model.

The conservative plan calibrates at ticks 0 and 2 and measures the two weight-13 jobs at ticks 1 and 3, earning 26. In epoch 1, after the shared delayed QC arrives, the adaptive expert selects that same plan and also earns 26. These actual plans, observations, and outcome records are retained in `comparator-diagnostic-review.json`.

This is precisely the distinction the paper should preserve: an exact solution of the supplied model is not a guarantee that the model describes the world. More complete internal proof checking cannot make stale physical assumptions correct.

## Stochastic failures do not establish greedy reliability superiority

Across all conditions, greedy has the same nominal objective as the native policy, but **2,658 different plans** and **344 different useful-output values**. Completion counts always match. Thus objective equality cannot be substituted for plan or weighted-outcome equality.

In the slot-failure condition, verified-minus-greedy mean output is **−0.041667** units with descriptive 95% interval **[−0.239583, +0.148437]**. The sign of the small sample mean is not evidence of a durable reliability advantage. The model provides no known per-slot survival advantage for the greedy policy to exploit. Different value-to-slot assignments interact with the same realized failures.
