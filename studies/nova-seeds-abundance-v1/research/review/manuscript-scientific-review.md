## Checks against actual sources

### Constructed capability and competitors

The geometry in the manuscript matches the frozen source: four ticks, four jobs, binary calibrations at ticks 0–2, rejection or sampling at ticks 1–3, 2,048 complete assignments, one-tick calibration duration ordinarily, inclusive TTL, and a lexicographically weighted objective. The conservative policy recalibrates before each occupied measurement tick. Calling it a capability-removal ablation is necessary: it is not the strongest expert and does not represent all operators who know the idea. That qualification is present.

The native compiler, independently authored exhaustive enumerator, direct outcome evaluator, and value-first greedy comparator are different executable paths. The exact expert is optimal for the supplied stationary model, not for hidden future realized outcomes. The adaptive expert uses the previous epoch's bias profile and a noise margin. All policies receive that same profile in the actual public mission. The manuscript correctly describes the static policies' decision to ignore it as an algorithm choice rather than denied information access.

The complete replica copies the native/evidence code, runs its native optimizer in another Node process, and creates its own fixture signer and ledger. Its copied evidence module executes in the parent process. The manuscript states the latter and does not claim organizational independence or independently acquired infrastructure. Equal cloning outcomes would be an existence witness for complete replication, not a measured account of human invention or market entry.

The direct expert and common evaluator reside in the same independent module and share its feasibility semantics. Their independence is from the native compiler/optimizer, not from every line of one another. Analytic fixture checks and native/direct comparisons reduce common errors but are not a proof of general correctness. The manuscript's scoped wording is appropriate.

### Diagnostics, drift, and demand

The four auxiliary reference acquisitions are extra synthetic resources outside the four contested instrument ticks. They reveal an ideal exact retrospective relative-bias profile only after all current methods finish. The manuscript counts them separately and explicitly limits adaptive recovery to repeatable drift and correct retrospective observations. They must remain in the resource table even though their equal allocation cancels from paired resource differences.

The demand condition reverses persistent within-seed job weights after epoch six. It tests response to publicly supplied priority changes. It does not estimate customer demand, willingness to pay, market evolution, or private information. The manuscript calls the weights synthetic priorities and commercial demand unknown; those statements should remain in the final results discussion.

The doubled-capacity, short-validity, and zero-duration-calibration conditions directly attack the proposed bottleneck. They can remove or weaken its advantage. Any such ties should receive equal prominence with the ordinary-case improvement. Model-valid signatures accompanying truth-invalid synthetic outputs are negative findings, not discardable observations.

### Contract semantics and local counterfactual

Direct source checks agree with the manuscript for NovaSeed's nonzero capsule/plan/evidence commitments, ERC-721 metadata, parent ownership requirement, and 1–128 job bound. Hashes do not provide encryption, physical truth, productive merit, availability, or intellectual-property ownership.

AscensionMark requires Funding phase, sufficient reserves, current business/owner/risk conditions, and a timestamp strictly before `fundingEnd` to bloom. Bloom changes the phase and execution end; it neither burns the NFT nor creates an agent or profit. Buy/transfer timing differs from the unused-funding withdrawal path in `sell`. Reclaim concerns closed-campaign pro-rata remaining reserves, not guaranteed principal or dividends. These distinctions are accurately stated.

Routing checks the indexed Merkle leaf, range, and unused index before posting. The JobMarket stored specification hash uses index zero whereas the Mark route event retains the indexed plan leaf. The manuscript correctly preserves this distinction for later indices. Review uses three selected validators, artifact-bound votes, staked roles and per-bid collateral. Settlement uses a 5% gross review pool and an integer-floor 1% burn on each payout. The USDC route is kept separate.

The source manifest records all five requested contract files as identical between observed revision `197147ffd22c5ae13113162afffaf3ce9c10aa2e` and historical `c0fc5217e364a39a4cd123d17a8317c06b80f820`. This review checked the current local source semantics and manifest, not live chain bytecode. The paper makes only the source-equality claim.

The local contract evidence records 113 transactions, 20,701,896 total gas including fixtures/negative cases, and 4,546,309 tracked lifecycle gas. The off-chain record is 3,619 bytes. These match the manuscript. Gas and off-chain CPU/bytes are different quantities, and the paper does not subtract them as a price comparison. The accepted truncated-prefix negative control and central-key trust boundary are explicit. The test does not establish public-network consensus or full contract equivalence.

### Integration and authority

The two currency route records contain nine receiver observations each: six accepted native stages, a missing-reserve inconclusive case, a rehashed-invalid-measurement rejection, and a stronger accepted successor. The stated 18 observations and four negative/inconclusive cases are correct. Both routes retain the same workspace digest, zero caps, no authority and unprovisioned external execution. They are not 18 independent experiments and do not admit the new scheduling domain.

The published JobDispatcher evidence records two graphs, 16 callbacks, two direct enumerations, 24 rejected negative cases, a valid 121-event journal, and zero production grants/live transactions. Its seven actual families are distinguished from the seven TI journey labels. The held Harness branch and new-domain native commissioning remain explicitly unavailable or unqualified. This is an honest integration boundary.

### Limited disclosure and receipt trust

The disclosure control uses established Node/OpenSSL AES-256-GCM, a fresh random 32-byte key and 12-byte nonce, and mission-associated data. Both authorized operators receive the same plaintext. Wrong key, changed ciphertext and changed associated data are rejected. Keys are not written by that module. Because the generator and synthetic data are public, the manuscript appropriately claims a functional access-control test rather than secrecy of these workloads. The small-domain unsalted-hash attack demonstrates the relevant limit. No productive advantage is attributed to denied access.

The signature/ledger descriptions separate attribution, mission binding, and local fixture permission from physical truth and outside trust. A deliberately false signed statement and a valid truncated history can pass the relevant narrow verification predicates. These are important negative controls and should stay visible.

## Economic and statistical boundaries to preserve

The primary contrast, 5% practical margin, 32 seed clusters, 12 epochs per seed, 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples, and descriptive status of secondary intervals agree with the preregistration and analysis source. Repeated epochs and jobs must not be treated as thousands of independent scientific units. The replication equality check is exact and does not require a significance test.

CPU, sample acquisitions, calibration acquisitions, diagnostic acquisitions, instrument ticks, bytes and gas remain separate resources. Copy/startup costs are warm-host operations, not full rebuilding costs. Human research, AI-assisted authoring, physical capital, opportunity cost, actual energy and customer demand are unknown. The paper correctly refuses to call its results full-cost profitability.

The strongest economic conclusion available is conditional: useful output can survive disclosure while the incumbent has no residual output advantage against an equipped complete copy and may face a cheaper sufficient implementation. Synthetic useful output is not measured social welfare; zero observed customer revenue is not proof that no customer could pay. Homogeneous-service price competition is an interpretation under explicit assumptions, not an experimentally observed market equilibrium.
