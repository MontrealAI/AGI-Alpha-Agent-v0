## Question and constructive intervention

When algorithms, implementation and inputs can be copied, does a calibration-aware resource-use capability retain productive advantage, private rents, or only shared benefit? Construct a bounded simulated instrument allocator that reuses valid calibration across compatible jobs, with scoped, independently checked receipts. Compare to per-job recalibration, the disclosed implementation without receipts, the complete verified artifact, a complete fresh clone, and an independently implemented exact enumerator with the same information, construction rights and resource limits.

The operational resource is simulated. CPU time and bytes are actual measurements. Calibration time, instrument capacity, job weights, demand and failures are synthetic. No physical calibration certification, real customer demand, revenue, financial action, full-cost profitability or permanent moat is asserted. A signature authenticates a recorder; it does not establish physical truth. Knowledge reuse does not confer new mission authority.
