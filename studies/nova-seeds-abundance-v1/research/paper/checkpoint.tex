\noindent\textbf{Initial pre-experiment checkpoint.} This is an openly labelled protocol and preservation artifact. It is not the final paper and contains no empirical finding. The versioned protocol, verified source audit and final source freeze will control the subsequent experiment.
\section*{The question}
When intelligence and its outputs become cheaply copyable, what productive value survives, who can capture it, for how long, and at what cost? This study constructs a bounded simulated instrument allocator and then gives competitors its algorithms, source, relevant inputs and equivalent rights to reproduce its verification machinery.
\section*{The constructive test}
A valid calibration can support more than one compatible observation. A resource-use artifact will turn that physical-model permission into a checkable allocation, compared against per-job recalibration, a disclosed implementation, the complete verified system, a full clone and an independent exact expert. Workload and calibration claims remain simulation. Signatures identify a recorder; they do not prove physical truth.
\section*{What can survive}
Useful output and private capture are different variables. An improvement can become common infrastructure while its inventor's relative advantage vanishes. Ties, a cheaper expert, a sufficient off-chain registry and zero residual alpha are valid outcomes. They will receive the same prominence as positive productive findings.
\section*{Before confirmation}
Development uses only seeds 1--8. Planned confirmation uses seeds 101--132 across twelve epochs and eight declared conditions. Exact geometry, budgets and analysis will be frozen before any confirmatory workload is inspected. Measured computing and bytes, simulated resource ticks, assumed prices and unknown human costs remain separate. No live financial action or commercial-profit claim is included.
\section*{Source and delivery}
The study reads current Alpha, Manager, Jobs and Fleet source, and reuses compatible public modules. Native-route tests do not automatically admit the new workload. Existing review holds and the sole publication coordinator remain authoritative. The complete final manuscript, figures, source, results and canonical compiled PDF will be retained in a hash-verified archive before public publication.
