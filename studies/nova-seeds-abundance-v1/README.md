# Nova-Seeds: The Value That Survives Abundance

**From Copyable Intelligence to Verified Productive Advantage**  
MONTREAL.AI · Writer #10 · Research edition `nova-seeds-abundance-v1`

> “Nova-Seeds encode cosmic foresight, quietly locking away and igniting profound scarcity; invisible intelligence greater than markets reshapes economic destiny.”

This is the opening poetic provocation. The tested question is what productive value survives when a capability's algorithms, source, current inputs, calibration rules and rebuilding rights are available to equally equipped competitors.

The capability is a calibration-aware **simulated** instrument allocator using the existing Alpha finite optimizer/inspector, Jobs signatures and Fleet evidence ledger. It removes a redundant recalibration rule, then faces a complete fresh replica, independent exact and adaptive enumerators, a simple greedy scheduler, drift, failures and substitutes. Every physical outcome is synthetic. No token price, private market, physical sensor or commercial profitability is inferred.

## What survived the copying test

The predeclared confirmation completed **3,072 worlds and 21,504 method records**, with zero execution-method failures. Ordinary useful output rose from **23.8125 to 32.6875 units**, a **37.2703%** increase (paired 95% cluster-bootstrap interval **34.9269%–39.4403%**) against redundant immediate recalibration. This is simulated useful output, not revenue or measured welfare.

The complete clone, disclosed planner and independently written stationary exact expert matched the verified native artifact's plans and outcomes in **every world**. Greedy achieved the same ordinary output at **2.731 ms** attributed CPU per mission, versus **70.738 ms** for the verified native service. Complete copying preserved usefulness and removed any exclusive output advantage in this test.

An unflattering result is central: under hidden measurement drift, conservative recalibration earned **23.8125** units, adaptive planning **22.7552**, greedy **13.125**, and verified native/clone/stationary expert **11.125**. Adaptive planning recovered from epoch two using the same delayed diagnostics offered to everyone, but conservative calibration remained best over the full horizon. Added capacity, short validity and zero-duration calibration eliminated the ordinary ablation gap.

Independent replay checked all records, **15,360 signatures and 15,360 ledgers**, with zero errors. A separate closed-form calculation verified **18,432 nominal objectives**, and the unchanged native inspector accepted all **48 retained full completions**. The campaign used **733.416 measured CPU seconds**, **755.168 elapsed seconds**, and **15,001,143 bytes** of evidence including terminal metrics but excluding analysis. Verification/analysis and mechanism-control costs are separately retained.

**Conclusion:** a transferable productive capability survives full disclosure here; exclusive productive superiority, full-cost profit and a permanent moat are not established. The signed off-chain registry preserves the studied evidence-binding service with no instrument-output loss; its missing escrow/public-consensus functions remain explicit.

The core demonstration needs **Node 24** and its built-in modules; no npm install, wallet, paid service or external endpoint is required. The recorded runtime was Node 24.19.0. From the extracted package root:

```sh
cd research
node src/run_experiment.mjs --mode pilot --out results/my-pilot
```

Use a new output directory. The pilot runs six worlds and seven methods with fresh source copying, keys and ledgers. The frozen source inventory must match. A source modification is a new experiment and requires a new protocol/version; do not silently rewrite the retained freeze.

Actual measured CPU, elapsed time, bytes and local EVM gas are separate quantities. Calibration, sample, auxiliary diagnostic and occupied-tick counts are simulated resource quantities. Each policy is a counterfactual operator of the same instrument; summing their outputs or acquisitions would double count a shared opportunity. Actual compute across all evaluation runs can be summed.

Four ideal auxiliary diagnostic reference acquisitions per epoch reveal the completed relative-bias profile for the next epoch, equally to every method. They are outside the contested four-tick instrument horizon and are reported separately, never free inferred physical information. The adaptive expert uses this delayed profile; fixed policies intentionally do not adapt.

Human research and reconstruction labor, attributable model assistance, real equipment/energy, opportunity cost, liability and commercial demand are unknown. Customer revenue and live financial actions are zero. The 240-row sensitivity table uses invented, clearly labelled accounting assumptions, not market quotes or measured profit. A positive operational benefit and zero exclusive output premium can coexist.
