# A decision a maintainer can use

Posthoc publication draft, 26 October 2026. Read with `primary-product-review.md` and the final economic ledger. This text does not change the frozen methods or accepted scope.

## The offering and its immediate value

The useful deliverable is a **component-compatibility decision packet for one exact dependency proposal**. A maintainer receives the tested requirement, the original consumer source, the before and proposed component locks, executable observations, an independent finite-contract verdict, an inspectable patch and a justified next action. The packet answers a real maintenance question: do this application's request-boundary and mission-input safeguards still behave as required with the specified dependency versions?

For AGI ALPHA, the first completed development packet exercised the original `RequestBoundary` definitions and the `Mission`, `Review` and `Control` input contracts through their original FastAPI import bridge. The native component profile used FastAPI 0.141.1, Starlette 1.7.0, Pydantic 2.13.5 and pydantic-core 2.46.5; the development proposal changed Starlette to 1.10.0, Pydantic to 2.14.1 and pydantic-core to 2.50.1. Both exact ten-package environments met all 61 predeclared cases. The remaining packages and wheel hashes are retained in the locks and preparation receipts. [E1]

That result enables a concrete, limited action: **accept the component evidence and advance the proposal to full application qualification while keeping the current native lock**. The maintainer does not have to infer this component result from an upstream change log or reconstruct its source, environment and observations. The packet does not resolve the full dependency lock, exercise authentication or journal persistence, execute a real mission, or authorize a release. Those are explicit remaining tasks. Invalid-input rejections demonstrate specified guard behavior; they are not newly discovered production defects. [E1–E2]

The project supplies an observed need for these guards and a real source requirement. Published dependency changes supply recurrence triggers. The historical confirmation transitions are representative maintenance assessments selected before their package bodies were acquired; no maintainer adoption, customer acceptance, paid order or prospective recurrence frequency was observed. An unchanged request is a no-op. [E2–E3]

## What the enterprise retained

The completed primary comparison produced six accepted scoped packets in each arm, eighteen evaluations of six distinct historical transitions across two dependency clusters. Every packet recorded 61 conforming cases before and after. No fresh behavioral regression occurred in this primary set. A required six explicit researcher finalizations; B and C required none. This is recorded in `publication/product-review-inputs.json` and reviewed in `publication/primary-product-review.md`; it is not an estimate of human time saved.

The first delivery required a researcher to assemble a complete result from source, exact environments, observations and verifier output. The retained asset packages these objects together and binds the final decision to the evidence that supports it. Subsequent registered proposals invoke the same component contract, bounded paired execution, independent verification, receipt checks and English/French delivery through the existing local SUCCESSOR Ω Workbench extension. The reusable ordinary `unittest` builder has equivalent rights and also automates complete delivery. Both retain their legitimate source, environments, wheels and reusable code. [E3–E4]

The primary operational claim is therefore specific: automatic generation of the accepted scoped packet can remove the study incumbent's explicit researcher disposition/finalization step. It does not establish that the retained design is uniquely capable, that a different algorithm is superior, or that a person's active time equals a process interval. The conventional builder contributed the stronger receipt validator adopted by the retained asset. That construction, failed initial integration, criticism and correction belong to the actual investment. [E4–E5]

The comparison contains three honest alternatives: the study-created researcher-assisted incumbent, the retained producer and a strong conventional reusable builder. The incumbent is not a recruited professional performing the work without AI. A competent human could use existing tests, ordinary Python tooling, dependency proposals and caches; their equivalent accepted work must be measured or modeled separately. Free tools such as Dependabot, Renovate and Griffe are legitimate inputs and alternatives, not zero-effort completed assessments. [E3, E6]

## Cost, responsibility and the economic gate

The records expose preparation, worker and verifier execution, packaging, failed development, corrections, construction and remaining uncertainty. Primary service spans include the entire invocation-to-package interval; the manual arm also includes the root researcher's response and scheduling waits. Nested receipts are diagnostic measurements and must not be added to their enclosing measurements. These spans are neither human attention nor an inference invoice. [E3, E7]

Complete monetary provider cost, price, active customer effort, billed construction inference, maintenance demand and utilization remain unknown. Actual customer collections are zero. The evidence can support an engineering admission within the finite contract; it cannot establish a commercially superior price, positive operating surplus, recovered investment or a financed successor. A hypothetical allocation, token value or customer saving is not a collection. [E6–E7]

For equivalent human work, a defensible comparison retains duration as a variable: wage-only human cost is `w × t / 60`. If complete automated provider cost is `A`, the provider-cost crossing occurs at `t = 60A/w`; customer savings instead depend on actual customer price and customer effort. None of those unknown terms is filled by an attractive assumption. The separately sourced wage context and its occupational/geographic limits are in `analysis/human-threshold-and-internal-test.md`. [E6]

## The next justified allocation

The next useful evidence is one actual pending dependency decision whose maintainer needs this bounded result. Within existing authorized resources, record the maintainer's question, the full accepted scope, input preparation, residual exceptions, active work and delivery costs. Use the retained packet and the strongest ordinary approach with equal permissions. If a real scope-matched purchasing decision later supplies a quote and payment, record price, acceptance and collection separately. No outreach, payment, deployment, new credential or paid commitment is implied here. [E6]

Do not finance an unrelated hypothetical capability from these results. Continue or retire the actual component-assessment asset according to observed use, supported scope, maintenance burden and complete incremental value. A new construction advantage or sustained acceleration requires a new controlled construction problem and its costs; this study does not infer either from reuse.

## Version française : le résultat utile et sa limite

Le livrable est un **dossier de compatibilité des composants pour une proposition précise de mise à jour**. Il réunit les exigences vérifiées, le code source d'origine, les dépendances exactes avant et après, les observations exécutables, la décision du vérificateur indépendant, un correctif de profil et la prochaine action justifiée. Pour le premier cas AGI ALPHA, les contrôles portent sur `RequestBoundary` et sur les entrées `Mission`, `Review` et `Control`, avec le chemin d'importation FastAPI d'origine.

Lorsque les observations respectent le contrat déclaré, le responsable peut accepter ces preuves limitées aux composants et poursuivre la qualification complète de l'application. Il conserve le verrou natif actuel jusqu'aux contrôles restants. Ce dossier n'autorise ni publication, ni exploitation, ni paiement. Les rejets attendus d'entrées invalides ne sont pas des défauts de production découverts.

La capacité conservée automatise la production d'un dossier lié à ses preuves. Une solution conventionnelle réutilisable dispose des mêmes droits et peut accomplir le même travail. La comparaison manuelle de l'étude reste assistée par le chercheur : son temps écoulé ne mesure pas le travail d'un professionnel sans IA.

Les ressources observées sont conservées, mais le coût monétaire complet, le prix, le temps d'attention et la demande restent inconnus. Les encaissements clients réels sont nuls. Le résultat peut justifier une amélioration d'ingénierie dans le périmètre testé ; il ne démontre ni remboursement de l'investissement, ni surplus disponible, ni accélération durable. La prochaine étape utile est un besoin réel de maintenance, avec acceptation et coûts complets observés.
