# Publication and product audit: what the evidence can support

**Role and timing.** This is postfreeze reporting guidance, not a new evaluator or amendment to the primary criterion. The reviewer authored the conventional worker/package and manual parameterization; independent source/economics reviewers must therefore assess the conventional arm. No primary result was available when this guide was drafted. No confirmation worker, test or frozen implementation was run or changed for this document.

The inspected [source freeze](../protocol/source-freeze.json) has SHA256 `f417768b69a6729000d5ce9d10393bfac7eec45b7ce448a0d61c711a6662530c`, binds 83 files, and records six unique missions in two package clusters, 18 arm evaluations and one timing execution per arm/mission. The operative [primary protocol](../protocol/primary-protocol.json), not this guide, controls acceptance and inference. Publication should link the verified public freeze comment and preserve earlier editions, without describing a hash as semantic correctness or external customer approval.

## 1. Lead with the useful result

The supported offering is a **source-pinned component compatibility assessment for a specific proposed dependency refresh**. The maintainer receives the exact before/after component environments, executed request-boundary and mission-input evidence, independent verification, a reviewable component-lock patch, a scoped decision and the remaining application qualification steps. See the frozen [intent charter](../protocol/intent-charter.json) and [component contract](../protocol/component-contract.json).

Do not lead the demo with test counts, a framework diagram or AGI vocabulary. Lead with the maintainer's question and resulting action. For a preserved result: “These component requirements remained satisfied in the proposed environment. Accept this evidence for the next qualification stage; retain the current native lock until the remaining application and release checks are complete.” For a verified regression: “Hold this proposed refresh; this requirement failed after the change, with a reproducible counterexample.” An incomplete result must identify the missing prerequisite and remain unaccepted under the frozen outcome definition.

The work is complete at its declared component scope. It does not answer whether the entire application is safe to upgrade. In particular, `create_app`, authentication, host middleware, Journal persistence, mission execution, supported-platform qualification and deployment are excluded. A component patch is not a qualified replacement for `requirements-agent.lock`. “Current native lock” is accurate; “current production lock” asserts deployment status not established here.

The observed need is internal project maintenance. The proposed research refresh and six historical transitions are not paid bump tickets, actual customer adoption or proof of commercial recurrence. Changed dependency tuples warrant substantive assessments; unchanged reruns and assertion counts do not create more work.

## 2. Show the actual retained capability and its mechanism

The earlier representation supplied a changed-declaration report, while the user needed a consumer requirement decision. In the preserved v1 FastAPI example, 38 direct consumer references reached unchanged captured modules omitted from signature-report construction, and 19 reached missing Starlette reexports. That is the documented obstruction, not a claim that static analysis can never work. The v2 outcome bridge executes actual bounded consumer definitions and joins their evidence to a decision. The substantiated source criticism and the separately adjudicated response should remain inspectable. See [critic discovery](../discovery/critic-opportunities.md).

Show the actual retained files and transitions: common source-grounded requirements, original import projection, reusable probes, exact environment handling, measured execution, independent verification, custody checks and complete package assembly. Distinguish researcher-authored construction from the deterministic operating path. Runtime workers have no model/provider calls; this does not make model-assisted discovery/construction free or show autonomous invention.

Three corrections are especially useful publication examples when linked to their preserved evidence:

- The original eight-package projection bypassed the FastAPI import route. Independent scrutiny led to a corrected original-import environment before freeze; the provisional record remains historical.
- Complete negative verification can exit with code 2. Receipt custody and behavioral acceptance must remain distinct, otherwise a useful rejection assessment is mislabeled incomplete.
- B adopted the conventional receipt checker after a substantiated custody issue. Credit that adoption and charge the constructed dependency. Do not subsequently describe all B/C admission logic as independently invented.

These are concrete creator–critic–verifier contributions. Agreement, critique volume, vocabulary stages and self-reported confidence are not endpoints. The evidence must show the source location, consequential discrepancy, independent adjudication and changed artifact. The pre-freeze synthetic negative control is a control, not a newly discovered upstream regression.

## 3. Keep the three comparisons distinct

| Comparison | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Observed manual completion A versus retained B | Preservation of the same accepted outcome; automation of observed orchestration/finalization; measured resource and failure differences | Human-without-AI speed, priced attention savings or a market price |
| Retained B versus capable conventional C | Whether the proposed production method adds supported benefit over a reusable ordinary implementation | A breakthrough when outcomes tie, or independence after explicitly shared validation adoption |
| Preserved source-only v1 versus complete v2 outcomes | A practical coverage gap and the additional work needed to close it | A cheap equivalent-output baseline or a financial saving from omitting the completion work |

A was constructed and operated by researchers/assistants during this study. Its scripting, model assistance, retained assembler, tools, verifier and environments remain legitimate assets. The new parameterization is charged once, then reused. Do not invent human minutes for its explicit finalization step or assign a wage to assistant elapsed time. The human-without-AI comparison is a separate modeled counterfactual using independently sourced compensation/procurement context and a matched task-duration assumption or break-even threshold; it is not A's timing record.

Cache permission is equal. An exactly bound unchanged baseline may be reused with a complete custody/reference chain; the current implementations elect fresh parallel probes. No arm was denied permission to build reuse. Do not present that implementation choice as a cache prohibition, and do not claim that two concurrent 15-second waits sum to 30 seconds of turnaround. See [cache clarification](../protocol/baseline-cache-permission.md).

## 4. What the economic case must retain

The complete business case has three separate questions: does the bounded output help a maintainer act; does reusable production improve delivery relative to equivalent alternatives; and does the actual investment repay its full cost at an evidenced volume? A positive first answer does not settle the other two.

Report the measured resource vector before any dollar conversion: source/environment preparation and failed attempts; worker, verifier and package execution; tool/model construction; storage/output differences; integration and required scrutiny; maintenance and residual customer tasks. Separate marginal execution, recurring delivery at declared utilization and fully loaded cost including discovery/construction. Use one coherent nested receipt accounting so inclusive parent and child measurements are not added twice. The economic specialist's [evidence contract](../protocol/economic-evidence-contract.md) controls the ledger treatment.

The canonical unknown fields must stay unknown. No explicit paid commitment does not imply zero compute, subscription, storage or inference cost. Actual customer collections are zero in the frozen protocol; unknown starting capital, obligations and reserves are not available surplus. Source snapshots, tokens, simulated allocations and customer savings are not cash receipts. No transaction is necessary for this local work package, and none should be introduced as an ecosystem demonstration.

Use independently sourced wage context only with the correct occupation, geography, currency, reference date and responsibility scope. A wage benchmark is not a contractor price or task duration. Provider cost, customer price and customer savings require different variables. If matched duration or offered price is unknown, give a symbolic break-even condition rather than a precise savings ratio. Do not monetize the number of clicks, commands or unresolved steps without an observed duration/value basis.

Investment analysis must name the asset actually built, including failed B01, common infrastructure, conventional construction/adoption, verification, integration and upkeep. A finite same-asset ledger can correctly remain HOLD. With unknown complete investment, incremental benefit, demand and maintenance, repayment is **not established**; that is different from a measured negative return. An allocation to another hypothetical capability cannot rescue the result. Better construction and sustained acceleration need their own causal evidence beyond successful task reuse.

## 6. Primary packet reporting audit

Review actual outputs as they arrive without changing their evaluator. Record defects separately; preserve the original packet and any publication correction. A reporting correction cannot turn a failed primary execution into an accepted one.

- Bind mission, arm, source, environment, contract, execution, verifier and package identities to the frozen record and registered transition. Check that before/after names and version prose are drawn from the current packet, not the successful development template.
- Reconcile accepted-assessment status with all required before/after results and custody. Distinguish complete regression from incomplete provenance. Do not infer acceptance from a process return code, a Workbench integrity result or the presence of a JSON file alone.
- Ensure counts mean what the prose says: six distinct transitions, two clusters, three methods and one timing observation per method/transition. A missing acquisition remains in the denominator. Invalid-input rejection cases are expected tests, not defect discoveries.
- Match the action and component-lock patch to the exact transition. Keep the native lock and full application qualification boundary visible in EN/FR. Avoid claims of novel repairs, actual adoptions or customer orders.
- Verify that the downloadable package contains the referenced full observations, receipts and source identities; large records referenced from Workbench must remain available by exact hash. A small inline summary must not masquerade as the complete evidence file. Verify final public links before describing publication as live.
- Reconcile resource totals and null monetary fields with primitive records. State whether a figure includes setup, inherited assets, failed attempts, manual finalization, verification and package assembly. Expose additional trace-storage costs rather than attributing output-size differences solely to an algorithm.
- Preserve the authority boundary: local research execution, unchanged native integrity/inspection semantics, HOLD and absent active authority. Local package acceptance is not a release or operator authorization.

The reviewer must disclose C authorship in any primary review. Independent reviewers' verification of C is the relevant additional scrutiny; this guide cannot supply it by assertion.

## 7. Plot-ready data suggestions, without fabricated observations

Use a new analysis table with one row per registered `mission_id × arm`, linked to original receipt/decision hashes. Suggested columns are `cluster`, before/after versions, decision, complete-assessment acceptance, source/custody completeness, case counts, preparation status, pair wall/CPU, verifier/package cost, end-to-end span, output bytes, observed researcher actions, and monetary/attention unknown flags. Keep missing data null, not zero. Preserve the independent input acquisition table so shared profile preparation is allocated once under the declared policy.

1. **Outcome matrix:** six rows and three method columns, showing complete preserved assessment, complete regression assessment or incomplete attempt. Caption the scope and distinguish verdict from release approval. This is the primary practical result.
2. **Paired resource points:** per-transition mechanical wall and CPU in separate panels, with the single-observation limitation. Show A's overall service span separately if captured; do not mix it with B/C subprocess timing or label it human labor. Do not add concurrent side durations as latency.
3. **Cost completeness table:** known physical quantities beside explicit unknown price, inference, maintenance and attention components. Avoid a dollar cost bar assembled from an incomplete subtotal or a pie chart that allocates unknowns to zero.
4. **Evidence-to-action example:** select a clearly labeled development or primary packet after results exist. Show the exact proposed change, outcome, action and download links. Use the observed eight-to-ten-package correction only as a development critique example, not a fresh production incident.

A deep-purple visual identity can distinguish the packet, retained asset and subsequent work. Use clear status words and sufficient contrast; do not make every internally accepted packet look like an approved deployment. Figures should export as ordinary scientific PDF/SVG/PNG artifacts with their exact data table. With six historical transitions and one run per arm, descriptive values are appropriate; generalized reliability rates, p-values, market-size extrapolation and timing precision claims are not supported.
