# Equivalent human work, price evidence, and the smallest useful next test

Prepared 26 October 2026. This note reuses the same-day primary-source research preserved in v1 `discovery/sources.json` and `discovery/commercial-screen.md`; it introduces no new customer, quotation, task duration, payment or market-size assumption.

## What the customer would receive

The bounded offering is one reproducible **component-compatibility decision packet** for an exact consumer revision and one substantive proposed dependency change. It gives the maintainer the tested requirements, source and environment identities, before/after observations, independently verified decision, exact replay instructions and a justified next action: advance this component result to broader integration qualification, or hold the proposed tuple because a specified required behavior failed. This is narrower than a completed migration, whole-application release assessment, security audit or production approval.

A competent professional can perform this work without AI using the codebase's tests, ordinary Python test tooling, lockfiles, package metadata, source search and existing caches. Dependabot and Renovate can provide the upgrade proposal; Griffe can contribute declaration-change evidence. These useful free tools remain legitimate alternatives and inputs. Their free availability does not make staff time or infrastructure free, and a reusable unittest builder is a serious comparator.

The actual manual incumbent constructed during this study is an observed automated probe/package workflow assembled with researcher assistance. It is **not** a measured no-AI human professional. Its new construction costs belong to the study, while the human comparison remains a separately labeled counterfactual until someone performs the accepted scope without AI and records their active work.

## Thresholds that do not invent a duration

For an equivalent accepted deliverable, let `w` be the wage per hour, `t` the actual or explicitly modeled human work minutes, and `A` the complete attributable automated provider cost in the same currency. The wage-only human counterfactual is `H = w × t / 60`; the provider-cost crossing is `t = 60A/w`.

- At the U.S. QA median, the coefficient is **1.1966493817 minutes per USD** of complete automated cost.
- At the Montréal median, the coefficient is **1.2970168612 minutes per CAD**.

These coefficients are algebra, not durations. Because `A` is unknown, the actual crossing time remains unknown. A zero marginal model-call count or an existing funded runtime does not identify construction inference, maintenance or complete provider cost. Without billing access, there is no finite justified upper bound on the complete monetary bill from CPU seconds alone. A nonnegative lower bound is not a commercially useful cost estimate.

For customer savings use the actual quoted price `P` and customer effort, not provider cost `A`: compare equivalent human/free-tool cost against `P + customer effort cost`. If the work's responsibility, useful coverage, customer review or acceptance differs, report those differences before calculating a saving. Unmeasured attention is never priced into the result to make the inequality favorable.

## Smallest practical timed internal test

Use one real pending, permitted component dependency decision, with a substantive changed tuple and a maintainer who actually needs the decision. Freeze the exact question, consumer revision, input permissions and acceptance requirements before the test. Repeating an unchanged request is not another job.

1. **Record the decision need.** The maintainer names the action that depends on the packet and identifies the existing free-tool workflow. This separates a real internal need from a representative historical fixture or assumed customer use case.
2. **Keep the ordinary human method competent.** A maintainer completes the same bounded packet without AI using existing tests, source, Git, normal dependency tooling and caches. Record active work, waiting, errors, corrections and packaging separately, plus the actual responsibility they accept. Existing native work can be timed when it is genuinely being performed; a fabricated human panel or a guessed hour count is unnecessary.
3. **Measure the complete automated delivery.** Begin before mission-specific input preparation and end after independent verification, corrections, downloadable packaging and the maintainer-facing decision. Keep one-time construction, environment preparation and maintenance as separately attributable resources. Record customer input preparation and review separately from provider execution. Charge failed and refused attempts.
4. **Compare accepted outcomes first.** The same independent reviewer applies the frozen requirements to both packets. An incomplete output receives no accepted-delivery credit while retaining its costs. Neither a larger test count nor earlier completion is an additional eventual output.
5. **Produce the actual cost comparison.** Use measured human minutes with either the employer's known compensation basis or the explicitly modeled wage benchmark above. Use observed automated billing if available; otherwise retain unknown monetary fields and compare only supported physical-resource and quality measures.

This is the smallest plausible internal labor test. It can show an operational advantage or a modeled compensation threshold. It cannot, by itself, establish a customer price or recurring paid demand.

## What would make that test priced

A priced test requires an actual scope-matched quote or an authorized internal purchasing decision. Before delivery, the existing authorized buyer would specify a real approved ceiling or accept a concrete quoted price for the exact packet, while seeing the ordinary free-tool alternative and the scope limits. The quoted amount, customer effort, acceptance, payment and later reuse must be recorded separately. The price must come from that real decision or a cost-backed offer, not from the adjacent $1,500 service, a selected savings ratio, token appreciation or an arbitrary reinvestment share.

No outreach, quotation request, commitment or transaction has occurred in this lane. Preparing this test does not supply a numeric price. An internal approved allocation, if later supplied, remains funding authority rather than earned collections. Without such a real choice, price, customer saving and financeable expansion remain unknown even if the completed packet is useful.
