# Completed primary economics — analysis edition 01

## Decision supported by the evidence

The retained capability automatically produced six accepted, source-bound component-compatibility decision packages on six fresh, frozen representative dependency transitions. The actual researcher-assisted incumbent and the strong conventional reusable builder also produced six accepted packages. Both automated arms required zero explicit per-mission finalizations; the incumbent required six. This establishes a bounded workflow improvement over the constructed incumbent. It does not establish superiority over the equally capable conventional builder, a paid customer outcome, lower complete monetary cost, repayment, or financeable expansion.

The useful decision is whether the exact native RequestBoundary and Mission/Review/Control contract survived an identified dependency transition. All six primary transitions preserved that bounded contract. The packets identify untested integration, authentication, Host, journal, credentials and deployment responsibilities. They do not authorize a production upgrade. No naturally occurring primary component regression was observed; synthetic negative controls remain separate from the six missions.

A is the actual researcher-assisted first-completion workflow constructed in this study, with its legitimate helpers and caches. It is not a recruited human benchmark and is not evidence of non-AI human task duration. Its service span includes root response, scheduling and waiting. Its mechanical wall is the disjoint pair-execution and finalization-command intervals. B/C mechanical wall covers their full local Workbench command. All CPU entries use each run’s enclosing child-plus-driver resource receipt exactly once.

The full real 15-second timeout remains in the contract; it dominates the short mechanical durations. Earlier completion did not create more eventual outputs: all arms completed six. Removing an explicit finalization is observable; the amount and economic value of avoided active attention were not measured.

Logical output bytes count the completed run directory and any sibling external execution receipt/stdout/stderr once. They exclude shared environments, publication and archive copies and are not allocated disk blocks, compressed size or a storage bill. C deliberately retains additional raw ASGI input data as well as normalized observations, including large boundary payloads; pair and package copies are counted. Its larger evidence footprint therefore cannot support an unqualified efficiency claim.

## Human comparison and the smallest next commercial test

The source-backed comparison is a threshold, not a saving ratio. For a matched accepted deliverable priced at P and competent non-AI labor valued at hourly wage w, the wage-only break-even duration is t = 60P/w minutes. Provider full cost, customer price and customer savings are distinct. US software QA median wage USD50.14/hour implies 1.19665 minutes per USD; the broad Montréal QA occupation median CAD46.26/hour implies 1.29702 minutes per CAD. Currency, occupation scope, benefits, responsibility, customer effort and quality must remain explicit. No matched duration or P was measured here.

Sources reused from same-day authoritative discovery: [BLS May 2025 occupational wage table](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ocwage.t01.htm); [Canada Job Bank Montréal wage report](https://on.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/22511/geo25154). The earlier source note preserves retrieval dates and occupational limitations in `analysis/human-threshold-and-internal-test.md`.

The smallest practical next test is one real pending internal component-upgrade decision with the same acceptance contract: time a competent reviewer’s active work separately from waiting, preserve access to ordinary tools and caches, collect actual attributable execution/billing records, and record acceptance and remaining responsibilities. That tests internal usefulness and cost. An actual service price additionally requires a purchaser to accept or reject a concrete approved quote; no outreach, quote acceptance or transaction was performed.
