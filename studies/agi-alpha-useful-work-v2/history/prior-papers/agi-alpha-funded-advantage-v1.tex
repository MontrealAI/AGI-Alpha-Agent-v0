\section*{Abstract}
We construct a bounded release-inventory intake capability for the actual AGI
ALPHA Workbench and its existing exact catalogue-comparison engine. Four public
development inventories motivate the preparation bottleneck. Two same-session,
model-assisted builders receive identical information and resource limits; both
implement conventional parsers, and both freeze before independent confirmation.
A strong ordinary interpreter retains standard recipes and the same input cache.
The confirmation uses 128 pinned public source paths, partitioned into 16 distinct
comparison requests across Alpha, Fleet, Jobs and Manager. Four representations
per request are robustness repetitions, not additional jobs or customers.

All three methods produce correct complete results on all 16 requests and all
64 representations per method, correctly refuse 24 prohibited inputs, and pass
eight secondary valid-boundary probes. The data contain two real source-identity
changes. Median end-to-end pack times are approximately 23 milliseconds for every
method. The experiment therefore delivers a useful preparation and discrepancy
tool but does not establish a material incremental benefit over ordinary reusable
software. Full attributable authoring, inference, operating and customer-attention
costs remain unmetered.

The economic contribution binds each proposed investment to the exact executable
asset tested, reuses Alpha's canonical cost and shadow-ledger contracts, and
separates observed results from finite financing scenarios. Positive hypothetical
attention savings are explicitly additional assumptions. They are not evidence
that this candidate earned its development cost. The observed economic decision
is HOLD. No real sale, transaction, token-derived operating surplus, distinctive
builder superiority or sustained acceleration is claimed.

\note{\textbf{Principal finding.} The retained asset works on fresh inputs. The
strong conventional alternatives work equally well. Its full-cost payback has
not been demonstrated; the financing mechanism must preserve that decision.}

\section{The question and the three claims}
An economically useful Flywheel requires more than successful software execution.
Production must yield a usable outcome; collected proceeds must exceed the complete
costs and obligations; an affordable investment must improve later production
enough to repay its construction and upkeep. These conditions concern different
quantities and cannot be collapsed into a favorable unit-cost headline.

This study separates three claims. First, an automated service may have an economic
advantage over equivalent competent human work without AI. Second, investing in
and retaining a capability may improve the enterprise relative to continuing
ordinary delivery. Third, Alpha's improvement method may outperform equally
capable alternatives. A conventional component can support the first two claims.
A tie against a conventional builder does not itself disprove investment value.
However, value over a baseline that already retains the same practical capability
cannot be inferred merely because the new package has a different name.

The user-facing objective is concrete: supply two permitted inventory documents,
receive every declared source-identity discrepancy and a portable evidence pack,
and understand whether further investment is warranted. The experiment makes
this tool available while keeping its economic claims bounded by the evidence.

\section{The productive asset}
\subsection{A narrow, executable contract}
Each candidate exports \code{normalize(text, format='auto')}. It adopts standard
parsing and explicit validation. It accepts one of four JSON inventory shapes,
strict two-column CSV or SHA256SUMS. It returns exact, sorted path/SHA-256 pairs.
An explicit relative \code{pathBase} is applied. Hexadecimal digest case is
normalized; path case, whitespace, Unicode and percent-encoded characters are
preserved exactly. It performs no filesystem or network access and executes no
inspected source code.

Ambiguous roots, duplicate JSON keys, duplicate final paths, invalid hashes,
unsupported hash types, traversal, absolute paths, malformed quoting and exhausted
bounds must refuse. A Git SHA-1 object identifier is never substituted for a
SHA-256 digest. These checks qualify the stated finite interpretation contract,
not the safety or behavior of any program whose identity appears in the inventory.

\subsection{Actual Alpha integration}
The new research adapter calls the unchanged \code{guide.compute} implementation
and runs through \code{createWorkbench(adapters)}. The Workbench supplies mission
and source identities, bounded checkpoints, complete artifact digests, an
inspectable pack and the existing inert Jobs lifecycle compilation. It continues
to report local execution, economic admission HOLD, no authority and disconnected
settlement. The inherited Jobs template language is generic rehearsal metadata,
not a newly qualified source-assurance commission.

Delivered artifacts include a readable report, exact normalized inventories,
complete comparison JSON, full differences CSV, proposed actions CSV and the
retained asset identity. A saved pack can be inspected with the same Workbench.
CSV downloads retain the guide's formula protection; exact original values remain
in JSON and in the mission input. No discrepancy is automatically corrected.

The intake parser and the downstream service have different limits. The parser
permits up to 1 MB of input, 128 records, depth 16 and 64 kB scalars. The unchanged
TI guide imposes smaller normalized-CSV and cell bounds; the Workbench separately
limits individual artifacts and canonical envelopes. A parser-valid input may
therefore be refused by the complete service. The study does not broaden those
limits or promise that every parser-valid document yields a Workbench pack.

\subsection{Construction provenance}
Two model-assisted construction agents receive the same public contract,
development inputs, model capabilities, two-attempt limit and 20-minute elapsed
allowance. Their output is reviewed local research code, not code automatically
installed from a model reply. Construction happens in the current research
session outside the installed Alpha operator runtime. We do not describe this
as autonomous invention by a deployed enterprise.

The Alpha-side candidate uses one attempt and passes 62 development checks.
Its recorded construction interval is 246.661 seconds. The conventional builder
uses two attempts in 170 seconds. Its first version rejects a legal U+2028 path
in SHA256SUMS; a development probe finds this and the second version corrects it,
passing 39 checks. The initial source, defect and repair remain in its construction
record. These are elapsed session intervals, not measured human labor or model
bills. Tokens, inference cost and complete authoring cost remain unknown.

Both final sources freeze before confirmation generation. No confirmation-driven
repair is applied. The prior learned methods remain inactive.

\section{Confirmation design and controls}
\subsection{What constitutes one job}
The collector takes the first 32 lexicographic eligible ordinary text paths from
the union of each pinned current repository tree and its first-parent tree,
then compares their bytes. NUL-containing, non-UTF-8 and individual files above 100 kB are excluded
under a recorded prospective clarification. Four disjoint eight-file subsets per
repository form 16 source-comparison jobs.

Each job is rendered in four representations: an observed native JSON shape,
a whitespace/key-order JSON variation, CSV and SHA256SUMS. These are 64 evaluations
per method and 192 method evaluations overall, but still 16 underlying comparison
requests. The archived inputs are evaluator-derived inventories of real source
bytes. They are not pre-existing release attestations or 16 independent release
events. All raw bytes, Git identities and SHA-256 values are included for offline
reproduction.

\subsection{Three competent methods}
The ordinary arm uses an independently assembled Python standard-library
interpreter with saved format recipes. The Alpha and conventional retained arms
use their separately constructed JavaScript modules. All three receive the same
bounded exact-input memoization policy. Successful prior preparations can be
reused; unchanged old/new inventories need not be parsed twice.

The ordinary interpreter is new evaluation infrastructure. Its engineering and
adoption are not claimed to be free or already installed in historical Alpha.
It is a strong practical capability control, not a measured historical account
of the enterprise's no-investment behavior. This limits the causal economic claim:
the study cannot infer avoided historical interventions without observing them.

All methods use the same downstream Alpha comparison, packaging, acceptance and
source evidence. Method order rotates across cases. The Python arm uses one
persistent ephemeral child process; startup is reported separately, while IPC
remains in end-to-end execution. Cross-language overhead is not evidence of a
superior improvement algorithm.

\subsection{Withholding and independent checking}
Candidate hashes and the public protocol precede independent data generation.
An evaluator that has not read either candidate implementation creates the
confirmation sources and an independent Python oracle. Source-byte SHA-256 and
length checks establish the expected normalized rows. A separate audit recomputes
every produced pack, artifact and semantic result. Same-session role separation
does not establish external institutional independence or a secret third-party
holdout.

Twenty-four malformed or prohibited inputs test refusal. Eight secondary valid
boundary cases test Unicode ordering, quoting and other exact accepted semantics.
They are not counted as additional public-source jobs. The original protocol,
two prospective clarifications, candidate freezes and generation receipts are
preserved. Later accounting scenario details are labeled exploratory where they
were chosen after technical outcomes were available.

\section{Full-cost accounting without fabricated precision}
\subsection{Measured quantities and missing quantities}
The execution ledger records each unique job, representation evaluation, method,
output, source identity, failure, refusal and timing. Construction records retain
the candidate versions, development checks, failed attempt and measured elapsed
intervals. These observations do not provide a complete monetary invoice.

We reuse \code{alpha-automated-costs/v1} and \code{alpha-shadow-ledger/v1}
unchanged. All eight operational stages are represented: preparation, planning,
execution, verification, correction, packaging, reporting and recovery. Marginal,
recurring and fully loaded costs remain distinct. Unknown quantities propagate
into unknown totals. Zero task-time model calls do not make the authoring model,
researcher, integration, maintenance or customer attention free.

For method $a$, complete horizon cost is
\[
C_a=D_a+K_a+I_a+V_a+M_a+
\sum_{j=1}^{N}\left(c^{\rm run}_{aj}+c^{\rm retry}_{aj}
+c^{\rm residual}_{aj}+c^{\rm business}_{aj}\right).
\]
Discovery $D$ includes rejected opportunities. Construction $K$ includes
unsuccessful candidates. Integration $I$, qualification $V$, maintenance $M$,
exceptions and failed delivery all remain costs. Common infrastructure must be
charged or allocated symmetrically; its existence is not a zero-cost assumption.

The scenario investment envelope $U$ is an explicitly assumed complete incremental
budget for discovery, successful and failed construction, integration,
qualification and unknown authoring/inference. It is not an observed charge.
Maintenance and recurring delivery are charged separately. The grid spans
0, 5, 20, 50, 100, 250 and 500 USD; the zero value is only a boundary control.
It cannot establish actual affordability.

\subsection{Investment value and the price-cancellation test}
At a common price $P$ and accepted quantities $Q_0,Q_1$,
\[
\Delta V_N=P(Q_1-Q_0)-(C_1-C_0).
\]
When quality and accepted quantity are equal, the price term cancels. Raising an
assumed selling price may make financing easier, but cannot turn a cost-dominated
investment into an economic improvement. This is a central protection against
manufactured economics.

For net recurring saving $s-m$ and complete fixed incremental cost $F$,
\[
N^*=\left\lceil\frac{F}{s-m}\right\rceil,\qquad s>m.
\]
Equality recovers cost; admission additionally requires strictly positive value.
If $s\leq m$, there is no finite payback. Alpha's canonical investment calculator
reports recovery at nonnegative net value; this study adds the strict-positive
predicate required by the assignment. Affordability and technical acceptance are
separate necessary conditions.

No recurring provider-attention saving was measured. Primary economic paths
therefore assign no such benefit. A separate inverse frontier asks how many
seconds of genuinely avoided work would be required. If $w$ is an appropriate
hourly cost and $\delta c$ the per-job compute advantage,
\[
t^*=\frac{3600}{w}\left(\frac{F}{N}+m-\delta c\right).
\]
This is a threshold, not a claim that the asset actually avoids $t^*$ seconds.
The strongest ordinary recipes already deliver the same results automatically.
Assigning them invented recurring manual labor would invalidate the comparison.

\section{Financing the exact tested asset}
The chronological model is a derived view over the existing canonical events,
not another ledger. Each investment references the exact SHA-256 of the candidate
that was constructed and tested. It never finances an unrelated hypothetical
future capability while describing the tested candidate as successful.

The source-constrained sequence is: complete a mission, check acceptance, record
delivery cost, wait for modeled collection, pay obligations, protect the next-job
and maintenance reserve, inspect available earned contribution, and consider the
identified investment. Only an affordable and positive-value candidate may enter
the modeled deployment branch. Subsequent missions retain that same identity.

Initial cash is explicitly separate from earned operating contribution. A fixed
100 USD seed and 20 USD common setup expense are assumptions, not funds spent.
Price, delay, lower demand, paid failures and maintenance shocks are varied.
Scenarios stop at their finite horizon or resource exhaustion. No exhausted
balance is replenished silently. Collections are simulated and never inserted as
observed receipts in the canonical ledger.

\section{Immediate human-work economics}
Competent human work without AI may use ordinary spreadsheets, Git, scripts and
verification tools. A cell-by-cell manual comparator would be a poor default.
No recruited human panel or credible observed task duration is available here,
so no measured percentage saving is asserted.

BLS May 2025 occupation data [8] report median wages of 65.38 USD/hour for software
developers and 50.14 USD/hour for software quality-assurance analysts/testers.
BLS June 2026 private-industry data [9] report 36.73 USD/hour in total compensation
for office and administrative support occupations. These are sourced labor
benchmarks, not billing rates, customer prices or task durations. An occupation's
median wage is not silently converted into a measured loaded rate.

Provider and buyer thresholds answer different questions:
\[
h^*_{\rm provider}=\frac{60C_{\rm fully\ loaded}}{w},\qquad
h^*_{\rm buyer}=\frac{60(P+C_{\rm attention}+C_{\rm switching})}{w}.
\]
At 36.73 USD/hour, each dollar of buyer total cost requires 1.634 minutes of
equivalent displaced human work. Thus a 5 USD price with zero customer attention
requires more than 8.168 minutes; a 10 USD price requires more than 16.335 minutes.
These are arithmetic thresholds. If the competent worker could complete the
task more quickly with ordinary tools, that comparison would not support the
purchase at those prices.

Customer savings are not enterprise revenue. Provider cost, price, collected
revenue, reserves and investable surplus are reported separately. Unknown
customer attention or switching cost makes the complete buyer comparison unknown.

\section{What the evidence establishes}
The actual constructed asset accepts fresh public-source inventories, preserves
exact declared identities and produces useful complete discrepancy packs through
Alpha. Independent checking finds no incorrect accepted result in this finite
confirmation. Conventional reusable methods provide the same outcomes.

The experiment has not demonstrated the immediate human-work cost advantage,
positive complete-cost incremental investment value, or a distinct Alpha builder
advantage. It does not improve the builder itself, so improvement-of-improvement
and sustained acceleration are not tested. The financing mechanism is concrete
and source-bound; realized self-financing is not established.

The economic admission boundary is now inspectable. A further positive claim
requires measured full costs and either genuine additional accepted work or
genuine avoided production effort beyond strong ordinary reuse, at sufficient
utilization. A higher assumed price, repeated formats or an unmetered authoring
bill cannot supply that missing evidence. The useful tool can be retained as a
research capability while its economic promotion remains held.

\section*{Résumé français}
Nous avons construit une capacité exécutable de préparation d'inventaires de
livraison et l'avons reliée au véritable Workbench AGI ALPHA. Les trois méthodes
testées produisent les mêmes résultats corrects sur 16 demandes distinctes et
leurs 64 représentations par méthode. Elles refusent correctement les 24 entrées
interdites et réussissent les huit cas limites valides. La démonstration révèle
deux changements réels d'identité de fichiers, avec des rapports téléchargeables.

Cette réussite technique ne démontre pas encore un rendement économique positif.
Les méthodes conventionnelles réutilisables obtiennent les mêmes résultats, le
temps d'attention économisé n'a pas été mesuré et les coûts complets de création
restent inconnus. Les scénarios de financement portent sur la capacité réellement
construite et testée; leurs encaissements demeurent simulés. L'admission économique
réelle reste en attente. Aucun revenu réalisé, aucune transaction, aucune
supériorité propre de la méthode d'amélioration ni accélération soutenue n'est
établi.
