# A useful upgrade decision with its complete cost boundary

Discovery note, 26 October 2026. Economics metering began at **14:23:49 UTC**, before inspection. Scope: design the accepted outcome, comparison and accounting boundary for the parent's bounded discovery. No new candidate implementation, experiment, paid commitment, customer contact or source-history selection was performed in this lane. The previous edition is preserved under `research/value-compounds`.

## 1. The question this iteration must answer

Can a reusable change reduce the complete burden of delivering an **actionable, correctly scoped dependency-upgrade decision on fresh work**, while satisfying the same acceptance requirement as a competent alternative?

The economic denominator must be that delivered decision. A faster source report, a correctly formatted refusal, or a large call census cannot substitute for it. Root should choose a genuine project decision and acceptance check before selecting the improvement or viewing confirmation outcomes. This note does not select a candidate.

## 2. What the prior evidence leaves unresolved

The v1 primary campaign produced 12 accepted conventional scoped research packets and 9 accepted custom packets over 12 historical transitions. It established **no affected consumer call** on any primary transition. Every primary consumer call remained unresolved under the strict mapper. Those are valid bounded research outputs, but their acceptance does not establish that a maintainer's complete upgrade question was answered. [L1, L2]

The strongest useful demonstration was one exposed historical Starlette/HTTPX incident. The old environment passed a minimal in-process request, changing HTTPX alone produced the known `app` argument failure, and the known upstream Starlette remedy restored the same request. This establishes that particular behavior, not fresh prediction, a production upgrade or a newly invented repair. [L3]

Its successful three-environment replay took **6.039078 seconds** including local setup. The existing resource account also records **33.488590 seconds of known wall time** across acquisition, metadata checking, diagnostics and successful replay, excluding an initial probe timeout and unmetered diagnostic preparation. The timeout's configured 60-second bound is not an exact measured duration to add mechanically. Construction inference, research, review, customer acceptance and monetary resource rates were unknown. A v2 comparison must start before the preparation that produces its successful result, and must retain failed work. [L4]

All v1 marginal, recurring and fully loaded monetary costs remained null. That was honest, but neither a numeric complete-cost estimate nor a repayment result. V2 can improve measurement completeness without pretending that unavailable billing has become observable. [L5]

## 3. Freeze the delivered outcome before improving it

The smallest credible primary unit is **one real consumer revision, one substantive proposed dependency transition and one explicit consumer requirement**. Prefer the project's own relevant test or documented public requirement. A minimal diagnostic may supplement it; a researcher-created known-failure fixture alone does not establish a complete customer job.

An accepted packet should contain all of the following:

1. Exact consumer, dependency, test, environment and requirement identities, including what changed and why a new mission was warranted.
2. A reproducible baseline that actually reaches the relevant consumer behavior; a target-version execution of the same frozen check; installed-version and environment validation; and bounded, preserved outputs.
3. A decision that answers the agreed question: the proposed change meets that check, violates it at a verified consumer path, or requires a specific identified prerequisite that the agreed contract treats as a useful terminal result. A general “unresolved” label does not qualify when the buyer's question remains unanswered.
4. For a requested repair outcome, the concrete proposed change and a successful check of that change. For an assessment-only outcome, the exact failure and next required code/test action. Freeze which of these is being sold; do not give one arm the cheaper scope.
5. Independent scrutiny of the evidence, correction of report errors, a readable summary and a downloadable replay package. A source-level candidate and an executed behavior remain distinct evidence classes.

“Accepted” here is an internally verified bounded outcome unless an actual customer accepts it. It is not production approval or a guarantee outside the frozen requirement. An inaccessible or held execution route, a failed environment, incomplete input, an unsupported dispatch or an unanswered decision is a charged incomplete attempt unless the predeclared contract explicitly accepts that exact terminal result. Report useful partial evidence, but do not quietly change the denominator.

No-op checks, timing repeats, validation assertions, environment variants and mutation controls are separate accounting activities. They do not create new customer missions. A failed attempt followed by a successful recovery is one mission with all attempts charged.

## 4. The minimum complete delivery meter

Use one mission-level wrapper with subordinate stage records. Start before input validation and source/dependency preparation; stop after independent scrutiny, corrections, packaging and final integrity verification. Retain a separate request-to-delivery clock for external waits. Do not end the primary measurement when the parser or successful test exits.

| Activity | Operating classification | Minimum observation and attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer scope, proposed versions and requirement supplied | Customer setup | Record actual inputs, clarification rounds and action count; minutes remain unknown unless observed. No fixed “one minute review” charge. |
| Intake, hashes, permissions and source retrieval | Automated delivery where implemented | Wall/CPU, requests, response/request bytes, retries, rejected inputs and exact provenance. |
| Environment resolution, wheel acquisition and installation | Initial setup or per-mission preparation, according to actual reuse | Separate first creation from later identity-checked reuse; charge failed resolution/downloads and cache verification. |
| Reusable implementation and evaluator construction | One-time investment | Begin records before coding; preserve rejected candidates, dependencies, author/model intervention and verification. Model tokens and bill remain null if unavailable. |
| Analysis and consumer test execution | Automated delivery | Entire authorized process tree, installed-version checks, subprocess outcomes, test coverage/scope and failures. Zero runtime model calls must be demonstrated for the actual delivery path. |
| Independent scrutiny | Recurring delivery unless it is a genuinely one-time construction check | Record the validator or reviewer used, resources and exceptions. An AI reviewer is not free; a person's duration is not inferred from an assistant timestamp. |
| Correction and recovery | Recurring delivery or investment according to what changed | Charge every attempt to its originating mission or asset; distinguish fixing a report from constructing a new reusable solution. |
| Summary, replay bundle and integrity check | Automated delivery where implemented; otherwise a recorded researcher/operator action | Charge serialization, file creation, rendering, inspection and corrections. Research-only paper design is investment/publication overhead, not silently part of every future job. |
| Customer interpretation, application testing and final approval | Customer effort or explicitly included professional responsibility | State what remains. Do not label it automated because the researcher supplied the missing reasoning once. |
| Source/schema/tool updates, retained artifacts and audits | Shared maintenance | Record actual events and storage duration; future frequency/cost remains unknown until supported. |

For each event retain `event_id`, `mission_id`, `attempt_id`, `arm`, `capability_id`, actor, stage, start/end, status, resource quantities and units, evidence reference, cost basis and unknown reason. These are measurement records feeding the canonical ledger, not a new financial system.

Resource observations should include elapsed time, user/system CPU, memory peak with its platform unit, logical and actual artifact sizes where available, network/request counts and bytes, source/test work, cache reads/writes and verified hits, retries, model calls/tokens when exposed, and residual actions. Python's resource API distinguishes the current process from terminated, waited-for children; user/system CPU are seconds and `ru_maxrss` is a maximum. Do not add memory peaks or double-count a parent total and its child records. Use a dedicated wrapper or other available process-scoped accounting, not a shared interpreter's unrelated cumulative usage. [T1]

Use a monotonic elapsed counter for durations; process CPU excludes waits and is not turnaround. Record clock/runtime/platform identity and retain shared-host timing limitations. A timeout record needs actual elapsed/resource observations in a `finally` path; the configured limit alone is not a complete bill. [T2]

## 5. Three cost views, one accepted denominator

Let `D_a` be all execution, verification, correction, recovery and delivery resources for attempt `a`; let `S` be attributable shared operating and maintenance cost over the declared observation horizon; let `I_new` be this iteration's discovery, successful/failed construction and integration investment; and let `I_legacy` be the attributable cost of already available assets. Let `N_acc` be accepted useful missions, not repeats.

- **Marginal delivery:** the complete attributable resources/charges for the next mission, including all attempts needed to deliver it.
- **Recurring delivery at observed utilization:** `(sum D_a + S) / N_acc`, with failures included. If no mission is accepted, cost per accepted outcome is undefined; report resources spent and zero accepted outcomes.
- **Fully loaded:** recurring delivery plus justified attribution of `I_new` and `I_legacy`. If the monetary investment, allocation or utilization is unknown, the monetary result remains unknown.

Pre-existing v1 tools are legitimate starting assets. Do not recharge their historical cash construction as a new v2 outlay, remove them from a competent baseline, or give their reuse to only one arm. Fully loaded service cost still needs their attributable historical cost rather than an assumption of free ownership. Charge any **new** construction on both sides. Keep shared components common and record differential construction separately, so an investment comparison cannot be improved by a bookkeeping allocation.

Use the existing canonical stages exactly: `preparation`, `planning`, `execution`, `verification`, `correction`, `packaging`, `reporting`, `recovery`. Every stage needs a sourced/measured cost or explicit unknown; zero is justified only when that operation is actually absent in the declared boundary. Keep the canonical modules and established null-preserving adapter rather than replacing them. Monthly capacity, utilization, maintenance, acceptance and attempts parameters must not be inferred from an accelerated research replay. [L6]

## 6. Defensible bounds without billing access

| Quantity | What can be supported here | What cannot be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker CPU, elapsed time, requests, bytes and artifacts | Observed values for a closed, metered execution; declared enforced limits can bound the included resource, with failures/cleanup accounted for | Money, human time, production capacity or complete research inference |
| Model usage in an automated no-model path | Zero calls for that particular verified path | Zero construction/reviewer inference or zero hosting cost |
| Newly executed payments/commitments | Observed absence of new transactions or purchases | Zero economic resource cost, zero subscription allocation or zero outstanding obligation |
| Research inference cost without tokens/invoice/model tariff | Explicit unknown; no defensible finite upper bound is presently available | A bill derived from elapsed assistant time, output words, tool count or an unrelated API price |
| Hosting/storage/network cost without the applicable tariff/allocation | Measured physical quantities and a symbolic unit-cost expression | A particular cloud tariff as the price of this shared authorized environment |
| Customer effort without actual observation | Input/actions/clarifications, and an explicit unknown duration or valuation | Attention savings or a favorable equal-effort assertion presented as measured |
| Full monetary cost | A known charged subtotal, if any, plus named unknowns; otherwise no informative monetary interval | A finite upper bound or positive margin from an incomplete subtotal |

The mathematical nonnegative lower bound of zero is uninformative and must not be presented as an estimate. A public price list becomes a conditional scenario only if the billing unit, hardware/service, allocation and consumed quantity match; fetching a cheap unrelated cloud price would not close the present gap.

A complete **metered delivery-resource vector** can establish a narrower engineering gain. If one method uses less CPU but more requests/storage/review, report that tradeoff rather than declaring it cheaper without weights. Even a dominance result on all metered delivery resources does not repay unknown construction or prove spendable cash savings. Any resource-only crossing must name the resource and excluded costs; it is not full investment repayment.

## 7. Strong comparison with the smallest necessary scope

The primary practical alternative is a competent existing workflow using exact source/release information, Git/search, Griffe where relevant, the project's tests, pinned environments and legitimate caches. Official Griffe documentation already describes API comparisons and reports; official pip guidance provides pinned dependencies, hash checking and wheel bundles. Those capabilities belong in the alternative and may also be used inside AGI ALPHA. [C1, T3]

Compare the actual incumbent completing the agreed outcome, the same workflow with the retained change, and an equally permitted conventional builder. If the original native inventory cannot finish the outcome, show the missing completion work; do not compare its 0.164-second inventory with a complete packet as if outputs were equivalent. Give both builders the same inputs, permission to reuse assets and independent quality gate. Charge construction in both arms.

Keep the real cold-start investment and a realistic continuing sequence separate. Allow legitimate warm caches and identity-checked reuse to every arm. A no-cache arm may be a disclosed ablation; it is not a competent primary comparator. Freeze the protocol after bounded development, then use substantive new consumer/dependency inputs. Reject or qualify an improvement whose apparent advantage comes from omitting environment preparation, scrutiny, correction or customer work.

The minimum human comparator is explicitly modeled **matched competent work without AI**, with ordinary software tools retained. Same-day primary evidence supports USD 50.14/hour for U.S. software QA wage and CAD 46.26/hour for the broader Montréal information-systems occupation. These are wage benchmarks, not contractor bills or durations. A recruited panel is unnecessary for a symbolic crossing, but no precise savings ratio is available without an equivalent duration model. [H1, H2]

For customer price `P`, additional customer effort valued at `E` and wage `w`, the wage-only threshold is `h > 60(P + E) / w`. This is **1.196649 minutes per USD** or **1.297017 minutes per CAD**, kept separate. With price or effort unknown, the result remains symbolic. Provider cost uses its own complete-cost variable and must not be substituted for price. The broader advertised Django assessment remains non-comparable context; it supplies no v2 price. [L7]

## 8. Investment decision and bounded-discovery exit

Keep one finite ledger for the same capability root actually selects. Starting capital, receipts, paid delivery, construction, obligations, reserves and available cash remain separate. Actual collections are zero unless a real receipt is observed. No price, demand, reserve or reinvestment percentage is selected to obtain a positive result.

For genuinely triggered later missions, compare complete accepted-outcome delivery costs. Incremental benefit is `sum(C_baseline_i - C_retained_i) - I_new - incremental_maintenance`, retaining failed candidates and both builders' costs. Legacy costs enter consistently in fully loaded comparisons. A different hypothetical future capability does not repay this investment. Customer savings do not become provider collections.

Advance from discovery only if a real decision/requirement, a measurable complete baseline, a plausible meaningful improvement and untouched confirmation inputs exist. The acceptance check must be capable of recognizing both success and a consequential failure. The selected change should improve complete delivery burden, reliability or useful coverage; a negligible parser optimization alone does not satisfy that gate.

Unknown monetary charges need not prevent a useful, fully metered engineering experiment. They do prevent a claim of complete-dollar superiority, self-financing, available surplus or monetary repayment. Record the stronger result if evidence supports it; otherwise keep the result at the resource/engineering level and identify exactly which missing observation would change the economic decision.
