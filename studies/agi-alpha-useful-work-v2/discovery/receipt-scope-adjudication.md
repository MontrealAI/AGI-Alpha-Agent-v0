# Independent R2 receipt-scope adjudication

**Confirmed as a provenance-completeness qualification, not a behavioral failure.** The original `results/development/baseline-execution.json` contains times, argv, exit status, stdout/stderr and CPU/wall measurements. `baseline-observations.json` records source, slices, contract and package versions. Neither records contemporaneous worker-source, component-lock and interpreter byte identities. Current-file custody hashes cannot retroactively supply those facts. The original evidence is preserved.

The reviewed `src/measured_run.py` (SHA256 `a7a631404a01af13b07ccf9ad2012d2e404ce1aff677155885d3a894b004bdcf`) binds declared inputs before launch, checks them after execution, and hashes declared outputs. It records platform, meter Python, child CPU, meter CPU, child peak RSS, wall time, timeout and exit status. Its prelaunch hashing now sits inside the exception-capture block, with `command_started` distinguishing a launch failure from consumer execution. Earlier failed launches remain chargeable failures whose exact subprocess resources are unknown; the new meter does not retroactively repair them.

The command receipt must be supplied a complete binding inventory: observer, independent verifier, contract and projection records, exact consumer files, component lock and preparation record, worker interpreter, and other outcome-affecting inputs. Every required output must be listed. These command-line inventories are optional in the generic meter, so `complete_success` alone is not package acceptance. The paired-package validator must enforce its actual requirements, source/contract/lock identities and independent useful-outcome verdict.

The meter's own zero model calls do not establish that an arbitrary child command made zero model calls. Generic runtime activity remains unknown. A separately inspected, executed worker can provide its own scoped zero-activity evidence. CPU quantities from parent and child are distinct and may be summed for this command; peak RSS is a maximum and must not be summed across receipts. Sum of sequential receipt wall times is measured command occupancy, not necessarily end-to-end customer turnaround. Do not add a nested worker's self-reported CPU/wall values to the encompassing command receipt.

Residual qualifications remain explicit: preexisting output files are not by themselves proof of newly produced output; caller/worker guards and source identities must provide freshness. A final identity read failure could still prevent a final receipt. Final receipt serialization and writing occur after the stored wall measurement and must be included by a complete outer delivery measurement or identified as unmeasured work. No hypothetical failure is reported as an observed incident.

This independent review read the original provisional receipt's top-level fields, observation identity fields, and meter source. It did not execute consumers or inspect primary confirmation outcomes. Structured findings and exact artifact hashes are in `receipt-scope-adjudication.json`. Scrutiny and integration correction are recorded in the economics meter; monetary amounts remain unknown.
