What you receive

A reusable exact instrument.

A complete count graph, fixed constraints, integer ranks and a SHA-256 integrity digest. Download the instrument and the generated tables. Seeded draws use a deterministic generator with rejection of out-of-range ranks.

The meaning of exact

A law you can name.

The target is uniform over feasible integer tables; each table corresponds to one rank. Uniform ranks give this law; seeded draws are pseudorandom. This is not the Fisher conditional-independence law. Exact constraints do not establish population realism, privacy or scientific suitability.
