Vincent Boucher, President of MONTREAL.AI · Montréal, Québec, Canada  
Executed research edition · 28 October 2026 · Prepared for author review

**Disposition: retain the useful research instrument; do not qualify a profitable flagship or paid launch.**
This study built and exercised an exact constrained-table generator, challenged it with independent conventional
implementations, executed a commercial simulation and a revised policy, and tested a successor using inherited
instruments. The outputs work within their measured scope. Consequential algorithmic novelty, a superior successor
frontier, sustainable enterprise profit and an AI-specific advantage remain unestablished.

All commercial organizations, preferences, commitments and transactions are explicitly simulated. Actual customer
receipts are zero. Complete historical research costs and actual project profit are unknown. The free local product
remains usable without a purchase, account, API key or model call.

## What was executed

### An exact productive instrument

The requested family consists of labeled nonnegative integer tables with fixed row totals, fixed column totals,
individual cell upper bounds and structural zeros. A zero cap forbids a cell. The kernel builds a count graph using
only columns with identical remaining cap signatures and residual totals as interchangeable groups. Integer counts
then support rank/unrank and generation. The output is an executable, reusable description of a finite family.

The target law is **uniform over feasible integer tables**. It is generally different from the fixed-margin
conditional-independence law implemented by SciPy's `random_table`. Uniform integer ranks induce the stated uniform
law; seeded software draws are reproducible pseudorandom draws. These guarantees do not establish population
realism, privacy, physical fidelity, causal validity or suitability for a particular scientific question.

The Python input boundary is 1–16 rows, 1–96 columns, integer caps 0–16, equal marginal totals and total mass at most
10,000. Default construction budgets are 100,000 states, 1.5 million attempted transitions and 30 seconds. A valid
input shape can still exceed the construction budget. Resource refusal, mathematical infeasibility and malformed
input have distinct meanings.

### A successor that works, without a qualified succession advantage

The fresh query asks for the exact distribution of concentration
`K = sum(x * (x - 1) / 2)` and for tables conditioned on a specified level. It evaluates a generating polynomial on
the inherited graph. The browser also supports an attainable upper tail, `K >= threshold`.

The campaign delivered 100 checked tables for each of four size/target combinations. Warm graph evaluation was
fast, but the complete comparison also charged local reading, decompression, parsing, digest checks and structural
validation. Cold inheritance took about **0.283 seconds at 14 columns** and **2.257 seconds at 18 columns**; a fresh
conventional full-distribution calculation took about **0.247** and **1.519 seconds**, respectively. Conventional
target-specific solvers were stronger still for these requests. At `K = 0`, replacing each cap with its minimum
with one is an exact conventional shortcut. A conventional system given the same inherited asset matched its
outputs. [Source: completed successor experiment](results/confirmation_v2/successor.json).

Thus retained assets enable additional outputs, but superior complete-cost transfer, economic compounding and an
AI-specific successor advantage were not established. The asset digest is an integrity binding, not a proof of
authenticity or completeness. Without a separately trusted issuance digest, the Python loader reconstructs the
instrument before accepting its completeness.

### The commercial result is no launch

The revised commercial campaign evaluated four policies over seven scenarios and 256 fresh worlds per cell:
**7,168 simulated worlds**, with a 24-month operating window and six additional settlement months. Policies can
close earlier. Buyers may prefer a competitor, DIY/free software or abstention; suppliers have finite capacity;
competition, acquisition, refunds, custody, invoice delays, recurring labor and construction costs are modeled.

| Base-scenario policy | Mean after-tax full-project result | Decision-relevant observation |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualified instrument at assumed USD 449 | **−USD 26,397.71** | Positive unit contribution did not cover acquisition, recurring costs and startup |
| Subscription at assumed USD 349 | **−USD 26,521.09** | Accepted outcomes did not sustain the enterprise |
| Revised commitment gate at assumed USD 449 | **−USD 3,050.00** | Paid for search and closeout; never launched in the base confirmation worlds |
| Stop | USD 0 simulated profit | Preserved opening capital; this is not a successful business |

The gate required 57 simultaneous qualifying conditional commitments; the largest observed cohort in the 256 base
worlds was 11. Conditional-on-launch profit is **null**, because no launch occurred. The frozen selection rule chose
stop. The USD 449 price, buyer budgets and demand assumptions are not real quotes or willingness-to-pay observations.
The original experiment, revised policy, complete ledgers, adverse scenarios and corrected audit counters are
preserved in [ECONOMICS.md](ECONOMICS.md) and [economics/](economics/).

## Use the local product

Open **`product/index.html` directly in a modern browser**. No HTTP server, external font, asset request or installed
Python package is needed. The delivered page contains its Worker and interface. JavaScript, BigInt, Workers and
Web Crypto SHA-256 must be available. The executed browser acceptance used Chromium 139; other browser engines
were not independently qualified here.

1. Choose a preset or edit `rows`, `cols` and `caps` in the JSON editor.
2. Set the reproducible seed and requested batch size, then choose **Build instrument**.
3. Inspect generated worlds and choose **Verify constraints**. Verification checks every delivered table directly.
4. Choose **Accept evaluation** to create an explicitly simulated, zero-payment local receipt.
5. Download the actual instrument, table JSON/CSV and receipt. Optionally generate a concentration tail from the
   existing instrument, then verify and accept the new batch.

The browser publishes a smaller budget: at most 8 × 24 cells, caps at most 8, total mass at most 256, 25,000 states,
250,000 attempted transitions and 10 seconds per construction/query. Batch sizes are 1–128. Oversized, empty and
interrupted constructions do not become accepted deliveries. The same seed repeats the same batch. All processing
is local; there is no checkout or payment mechanism.

[Browser acceptance](results/browser_acceptance.json) records full-suite checks, exact artifact identity, exhaustive
small families and tails, refusals, cancellation, all four exports, and English/French layouts down to 360 pixels.
It explicitly separates the earlier full-suite timestamp from the final wording and price-metadata check. Temporary
inspection screenshots are not deliverables.

## Reproduce a computational delivery

Choose a fresh output directory. The example below builds the default capacity family and writes `instrument.json`,
`tables.json` and `receipt.json`. A receipt means the computational checks passed; it records no actual revenue.

```bash
python "$study_dir/kernel.py" "$study_dir/examples/capacity_4x8.json" \
    --output /tmp/agi-alpha-worlds-delivery \
    --samples 1000 --seed 20261028 --seconds 30
```

The family count is 20,322. The retained [delivery example](results/delivery_example/) contains the corresponding
instrument, 1,000 generated tables and receipt. Its separate

This is an illustrative executed delivery, not an additional held-out scientific result.

Invalid requests or exceeded budgets produce explicit failure/refusal outcomes. Use a new output path for each
attempt so stale files cannot be mistaken for the output of a refused request. The CLI's `--help` lists the actual
interface; there are no hidden service credentials or model dependencies.

## Replay the frozen scientific campaign

The delivered `campaign.py` checks the four executable source hashes against the frozen v2 protocol before running.
Replay it without altering that protocol or writing into the preserved `results/confirmation_v2` directory:

```bash
python "$study_dir/campaign.py" run \
    --protocol "$study_dir/configs/scientific_protocol_v2.json" \
    --output /tmp/agi-alpha-science-reproduction-v2
```

Use an unused output directory; the scientific harness can overwrite files in an existing output directory.
Its `run` action executes tiny-case correctness, the published comparator, 12 larger requests and successor tests.
It writes full JSON records and a compact `performance.csv`. Deterministic counts and accepted tables can be checked;
wall times and which methods meet a time limit can differ on another machine. A replay of the same seeds is a
reproduction, not fresh confirmation.

If the executable methods change, the existing protocol should reject the run. A deliberately revised study can
create a **new** protocol path with `campaign.py freeze --protocol NEW_PATH`, but must preserve the old protocol,
sources and results, identify the changed methods, and choose fresh evaluation seeds before making a new
confirmation claim. The current `freeze` action repeats its built-in seed values; a different file path alone does
not create a fresh holdout. The `worker` action is an internal isolated-comparator interface, not the full campaign.

## Chronology, source continuity and evidence boundaries

The research date is **2026-10-28**. Frontier observations emphasize the preceding 30 days, especially the final
week. [Research findings](research/findings.md) and the [dated URL catalogue](research/sources.json) distinguish
announced capabilities, actual access and usable dependencies. The exact-table kernel is a separate finite dynamic
program; it is not an implementation of the new OpenAI asymptotic sampler or its cell-bounded approximate counter.

The study continues the pinned AGI ALPHA foundations:

- [Transformational Intelligence][foundation-ti]: changing the means and formulation of discovery and production.
- [Out-Evolve][foundation-evolve]: constructing and retaining productive capabilities.
- [Invent the Economy][foundation-economy]: technical capability and mutually acceptable economic arrangements
  must both be established.
- [Autonomous Enterprises][foundation-enterprise]: reconstruction, capital and enterprise costs remain part of the claim.

The evidence sequence is preserved explicitly:

1. Development and independent review produced the bounded kernel, conventional comparators and observed failure
   cases. Earlier review counts are not pooled with the later confirmation as one independent test set.
2. Scientific v1 completed its tiny-case stage but aborted at the published compiler's resource refusal. An
   annotation-only reference edit also arrived after its freeze. Its original bytes and incomplete disposition
   remain in [archive/science_v1](archive/science_v1/) and
   [science_v1_disposition.json](results/science_v1_disposition.json).
3. Scientific v2 froze at **14:43:55 UTC** and completed at **14:45:29 UTC** on 28 October. Fresh seeds and refusal
   handling are recorded in its protocol; the compiler algorithm was unchanged between these protocols.
4. The row-order challenge and matrix-action alternative are later diagnostic investigations. Their results do
   not retroactively become frozen confirmation evidence.
5. The original commercial confirmation used seeds 2,000,000–2,000,255. The commitment revision used new training
   seeds 141,000–141,063 and new confirmation seeds **3,000,000–3,000,255**. Both select no launch. Development
   exposures, the audit-counter correction and a wage-source freshness correction remain documented.
6. Full browser acceptance completed at **14:49:05.902 UTC**; final copy/receipt-price checks completed at
   **14:51:32.360 UTC**. The acceptance record binds the final product hash and distinguishes these scopes.

“Independent” here means separately implemented agent review and a different computational oracle. It does not
mean an external institution, prospective customer, independently sampled industrial workload or controlled
old-model/new-model experiment. A digest binds bytes; it does not confer scientific, financial or release approval.
