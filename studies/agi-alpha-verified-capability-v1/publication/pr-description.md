## Summary

This additive study asks whether authenticated, evidence-backed graph selection improves useful fresh
work enough to repay construction, validation and maintenance. It delivers an executable sidecar for the
existing SUCCESSOR Workbench, frozen production and market protocols, independent reconciliation,
a publication-ready English/French LaTeX/PDF paper and a self-contained bilingual demonstration.

**The added graph layer does not earn economic adoption in this experiment.** All four methods agree on
the accepted decisions. The strong cached registry and graph use equivalent skills/evidence and inspect
the same four relationship records. The graph selector itself is slightly slower, and the direct
conventional workflow is the fastest measured alternative.

| Method | Primary accepted packets | Inclusive mean CPU, seconds |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A — strong unchanged native workflow | 15/15 | 1.532887 |
| B — cached validated registry recipe | 15/15 | 1.622554 |
| C — scoped graph traversal | 15/15 | 1.603660 |
| D — direct conventional workflow | 15/15 | 1.298856 |

The cohort contains 15 newly composed questions using archived exact wheels and three separate designed
root stresses: 72 method/question evaluations, not 72 independent customer problems. Four unchanged
requests yield cache confirmations and zero new jobs. Application release and customer acceptance are unobserved.

The unchanged inherited strategic simulator and canonical ledger execute 48 predeclared cells × 24 seeds:
1,152 paired worlds / 3,456 enterprise histories. B and C each have 24 operating-funded research attempts
and 417 modeled asset uses, but zero financial admissions or qualifying second investments. All 36 priced
C−A cell means and all 864 priced C−B individual differences are negative. One favorable C−A path is
preserved: +$10.01, with C−B −$1.50 and no qualification. Unknown costs remain unknown in 288 worlds.
All monetary outcomes are modeled USD; actual customer collections are zero.

club-name requirements, contracts, settlement, scheduler, runtime authority and accounting are unchanged.
The original papers, earlier primary negatives, conditional first-asset deployment gains, conventional
ties and unsuccessful second-investment work are retained and cited.

Attestation issuance and graph-root commitment are different permissions. Exact signer/controller,
wrapped-holder/operator and operation-scoped delegation rules are exercised using clearly labeled
noncryptographic fixtures. **Live identity enforcement: NOT RUN.** No credentials, transactions, security
settings or deployed contracts are changed. The custom SHA-256 research commitment profile is not
claimed byte-compatible with the foundational paper's on-chain profile.

The complete companion retains all 47 exact wheel inputs, all 72 packet ZIPs, full raw market histories,
earlier failures and the resource ledger. The bounded public review edition enumerates every omission
and its hash in `publication/review-edition.json`. The exact browser-checked HTML is publicly distributed
in `demo/AGI_ALPHA_Verified_Capability_Demo.zip`; extract and open it locally. It is not a hosted service.

## Testing

Completed bounded checks: 54 authority controls; 26 typed-commitment controls and independent
cross-language proof vectors; 42 reuse/ablation controls; 23 production controls; 20 market-adapter
controls; 21 separate graph probes; and 10 unchanged-development measurement checks. These have
different units and are not added to the scientific sample.

Independent, same-session reviewers reconciled all 72 archived production packets (5,788 assertions)
and all 1,152 raw paired market worlds (1,358,542 assertions), including 341,236 cash transitions and
196,992 weekly balances. The audits do not rerun the simulator or manufacture new production samples.
Initial reviewer schema mistakes and substantive pre-freeze candidate defects remain preserved.

The exact final demo passes 178 browser checks with zero page errors/external requests; all 21 final
PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected. Source and freeze integrity checks pass. The new isolated
reproduction helper was source-reviewed and exercised in plan-only mode; a full repetition was not run.

Ruff, mypy, pre-commit, the full installed project environment and full-repository pytest/coverage were
not available/run for this focused study. The recorded experiment uses Node 24.22.1 and Python 3.12.14;
the repository's Node 22 operating profile is not qualified by these results. No merge, release,
installed operator, public-hosting or live-identity approval is inferred. This remains a **draft source
research contribution** for the existing owners' normal review and publication process.
