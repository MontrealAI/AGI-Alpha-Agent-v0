# AGI ALPHA — The Economy of Verified Capability

**An additive, frozen research contribution. The graph has not earned economic adoption in this experiment.**

This study extends the existing AGI ALPHA Flywheel and SUCCESSOR Workbench with executable,
scoped skill records, separate attestation and root-writing permissions, typed commitments,
refusal controls and graph-guided reuse. It preserves Jobs roles, contracts, validation,
settlement, existing identity rules, the native scheduler and the canonical economic ledger.

Coordination: [AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0 issue #4850](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/issues/4850).
Production and market protocols were registered before their corresponding outcomes.
The original Merkle Roots and PROOF LOOPS papers and inherited research artifacts are preserved.

## What was delivered

A maintainer receives an English/French decision packet for a substantive dependency-lock
proposal: exact source and wheel identities, project-root checks, independent closure evidence,
known limits and an actionable next step. CONSISTENT advances only to application/release
qualification; a substantiated CONFLICT says to hold the proposal; missing evidence refuses a decision.
No wheel is installed and no application or smart contract is executed by this workflow.

The observed project need is dependency review and evidence packaging. Paying buyers, arrival
rates, prices and willingness to pay remain modeled commercial assumptions.

| Method | Primary accepted packets | Mean inclusive CPU, s | Mean measured wall, s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A: unchanged strong native workflow | 15/15 | 1.532887 | 1.597612 |
| B: searchable registry and retained recipe | 15/15 | 1.622554 | 1.678983 |
| C: validated graph traversal | 15/15 | 1.603660 | 1.665872 |
| D: direct conventional workflow | 15/15 | 1.298856 | 1.384554 |

All four methods returned the same six CONSISTENT and nine substantiated CONFLICT primary
decisions. Three designed root stresses are reported separately. The resulting 72 evaluations
are 18 questions, not 72 independent transfer problems. Four unchanged requests yielded cache
confirmations and zero new completed jobs.

B and C use the same four executable skills, evidence, proofs and policy. Both inspect four
relationship rows and read/hash the same evidence bytes. B reads a retained four-member recipe;
C traverses three dependencies. C's small observed total-CPU advantage over B is descriptive:
its selector alone is slightly slower, and neither has an acceptance advantage. D is the faster
measured alternative; its missing native history must be weighed explicitly rather than ignored.
One timing per method/question does not establish a stable general performance effect.

The frozen market campaign contains 48 cells × 24 seeds  = 1,152 paired worlds  / 3,456 enterprise histories.
B and C each have 24 operating-funded, **unqualified** research attempts and zero financially
qualified or directly repaid second investments. All 36 priced C−A scenario means are negative;
863 of 864 priced individual C−A differences are negative and one is positive. All 864 priced C−B
differences are negative. The 288 actual-unknown-cost worlds remain unidentified.

The favorable individual path is retained: reference / completed-research-replay / low /
seed 9110 yields C−A +$10.01 and C−B −$1.50. B and C share the same buyer-choice path after the
declared construction pause. This does not identify a graph-specific benefit or pass the
prospective investment gate. These are **simulated USD**, never actual receipts.

Reference assumptions allocate one-off B $88.50 / C $90.50 and recurring B $0.25 / C $0.40 per week.
They are scenarios, not invoices or empirically supported bounds. Actual complete construction,
authoring, inference, human attention, storage and maintenance cost is unknown. Actual customer
collections are zero. This experiment does not establish realized commercial success or
self-acceleration.

## Authority and proof boundaries

**Live identity enforcement: NOT RUN.** No keys, credentials, transactions or live ENS assertions
were created. Fixtures are explicitly `FIXTURE_NOT_CRYPTOGRAPHIC` and fail closed in live mode.
The tests distinguish direct agent ownership, wrapped ownership, full operator authority,
operation-scoped delegation and renewal-only permissions. Deeper names and club identities do
not acquire graph attestation authority by spelling. Existing Jobs club-validator rules are unchanged.

`ISSUE_SKILL_ATTESTATION` and `COMMIT_GRAPH_ROOT` are separate grants. Attestations bind exact
implementation, dependencies, evidence, composition, scope, environment, rights, policy, graph,
chain, registry, state, nonce and time. Historical validity remains distinct from current reuse.

The custom `SHA256_RESEARCH_V1` profile is explicitly not byte-compatible with the Merkle paper's
Keccak/JCS profile. Inclusion proves membership in a committed typed record set. It does not prove
truth, signer competence, live authority, technical sufficiency or economic usefulness. The growing
three-to-four-skill history is a scoped experiment, not evidence of open-ended autonomous learning.

## Reproduce and review

Use Python 3.12.14, pip 26.2.2, packaging 26.3 and Node 24.22.1 for the recorded environment.
The source repository's supported Node 22 profile has **not** been qualified by this study.
No new network/model calls, credentials or paid service are needed to inspect the complete companion.
Exact production admission also binds all platform marker fields in the frozen jobs; another
environment must be revalidated under a separate protocol, not silently substituted.

From this directory:

```bash
python3 tools/verify_frozen.py
node --test tests/authority.test.mjs tests/commitments.test.mjs tests/market.test.mjs
node tests/reuse.test.mjs
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_production.py
node market/run-campaign.mjs --validate-only
python3 review/reconcile_production.py --help
node review/reconcile_market.mjs --root . --output review/market-reconciliation-NEW.json
```

The executable review scripts are delivered with their exact CLI documentation. They check stored
evidence without rerunning the primary experiment. Software controls are not additional scientific
confirmations. The original outcome/source freezes must remain unchanged.

For a new timing/market reproduction, `python3 tools/reproduce.py --destination /tmp/verified-capability-replay`
prints a plan; add `--run` to create a fresh isolated copy and execute. It refuses an existing
destination, source drift, a different frozen environment or any overwrite of the original evidence.
Fresh timing and its newly bound market freeze are labeled reproduction, not a second confirmation
of the original preregistration. No parameter, seed, price or workload search is performed.

```bash
python3 paper/build.py
python3 demo/build_demo.py
```

Publication/presentation rebuilding changes only derived artifacts; it does not rerun the frozen
production or market experiment. Consult `paper/README.md` and `demo/README.md` for dependencies
and the browser acceptance command.
