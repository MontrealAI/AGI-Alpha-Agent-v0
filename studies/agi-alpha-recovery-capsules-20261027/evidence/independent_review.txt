6. Important limits
These cases are historical educational notebooks with a free modern edition. Their recovery demonstrates bounded technical delivery; their authors/readers are not customers and the study does not establish demand for repairing those exact examples.
The result supports the specified Python/numerical stack and accepted examples. It does not validate biological, medical or other scientific claims, guarantee arbitrary notebook repair, or reproduce every historical floating-point or stochastic output.
GMM modernization and seeded randomness are disclosed adaptations. No equality to unseeded historical cluster labels is asserted. The linear-regression adapter preserves the tested dense fitting semantics and grid dimension; this is not a replacement for the complete sklearn estimator API.
Isomap's finite shape and execution were checked, not exact equivalence to every historical embedding coordinate. Native plot cell completeness, structure, PNG repeatability and visual handoff quality were checked after the preserved correction; this is not pixel equality to historical output. High-degree ill-conditioned polynomial results may vary by linear-algebra configuration, and no equality beyond the recorded comparisons is asserted.
The checks measure computational acceptance. Actual customer collections, observed willingness to pay, complete provider billing and production operating cost remain outside this review.
