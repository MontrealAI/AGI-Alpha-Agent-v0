COMMERCIAL RESULT

A bounded analytical-recovery service is commercially positive under the central assumptions tested: USD900 per finally accepted capsule, eight attempted orders per month, 85% final acceptance, and USD4,000 additional setup. Over twelve months, modeled accepted revenue is USD73,440, operating profit is USD40,309.01, and full project return after setup is USD36,309.01.

The study also produced useful working artifacts: four fresh archived notebooks executed 133 code cells and passed 38 independently specified numerical or semantic checks in confirmation. These are four engineering cases, not 38 jobs. There were no customers, sales or actual collections. Complete actual study cost remains unknown because provider token metering and allocated billing were unavailable.

THE OPPORTUNITY AND OFFER

Research and R&D teams can inherit valuable custom analyses whose original author or environment has disappeared. A Recovery Capsule would restore one authorized analytical Python notebook, provide a runnable notebook and CLI, preserve inputs and source attribution, record changes, and supply independent checks and reproduction instructions.

Supported scope is bounded to 100 code cells, 5 MB of data and 120 CPU seconds per run. The proposed contract offers two-business-day delivery after complete eligible inputs, one correction cycle within 14 days, and a full refund if agreed acceptance remains unmet. Turnaround is proposed, not measured.

The first productive possibility is dependable reuse of a customer-specific analysis without commissioning a larger engineering engagement. Notebook repair is established work. The commercial hypothesis concerns affordable, bounded delivery with verification and responsibility.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS

The executed cases use MIT-licensed code from the first edition of the Python Data Science Handbook. A free newer edition exists. These examples establish technical engineering feasibility; they do not establish willingness to pay for those examples or representative performance on customer research.

The paid target is therefore conditional: custom legacy analyses lacking an equivalent free substitute. Broader studies document reproducibility problems, while MLEModernizer already demonstrates agentic notebook modernization. No technical-novelty, Operational Alpha, retained-skill or Gym advantage is needed to report the conditional commercial result.

CUSTOMER AND PROVIDER ECONOMICS

The same deliverable is estimated to require four, eight or sixteen competent human hours. Published rate anchors and explicit scope estimates give a low reference of USD336 and central reference of USD1,192; sixteen hours at ParaTools’ loaded rate gives USD6,607.52. These are resource estimates, not matched quotations or measured demand. At USD900, the proposed price is 24.5% below the central reference; the low-price and free alternatives can defeat the offer.

Central costs include unsuccessful attempts, corrections, verification, packaging, support, genuine exceptions, acquisition, payment fees, maintenance and attributable operations. Development is charged once. Complete modeled project cost is USD455.04 per finally accepted outcome.

CONDITIONS THAT CHANGE THE ANSWER

At central volume and costs, the full-project break-even price is USD441.26 and the final-acceptance threshold is 44.99%. Broader MLEModernizer recovery rates of 40.1% and 44.9% concern different inputs and mechanical recovery, not matched customer acceptance. Used only as stress points, they produce full project returns of −USD4,434.29 and −USD78.66.

The combined downside loses USD28,943.02. Forty monthly paid inquiries producing no sales lose USD26,800. The central result depends on demand, acceptance, resource consumption and eventual collection assumptions that remain unmeasured.

Expected opening equity is USD6,760.92 with all open-warranty receipts reserved. A first cohort in which all eight payments are refunded requires USD10,298.12, assuming subsequent recovery. Refundable funds do not finance delivery.

EXPANSION

The credible next stage is recurring customer-specific recovery, followed by permissioned shared adapters only when their incremental benefits exceed maintenance and coordination costs. The present contribution is an executed digital-delivery study joined to complete, reproducible conditional economics.
