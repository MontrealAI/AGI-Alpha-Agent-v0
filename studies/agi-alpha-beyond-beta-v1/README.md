## Commercial core first

A profitable Beta-only operating policy is a valid positive commercial result. Useful accepted work, customer payment, complete-cost profit and available operating surplus are evaluated before optional skills, sharing or reinvestment. A rejected enhancement does not invalidate profitable delivery; profitable delivery does not establish recursive improvement. In 500 worlds per condition, central Beta mean complete pre-tax profit is $522,787 with $489,833 above the original seed, reserves and obligations at month 24; favorable profit is $3,421,787 and conservative mean profit is −$3,976. These are conditional simulated outcomes, not observed demand or sales.

## The two questions

1. Can **Beta**—competent current frontier intelligence, ordinary tools and existing available capabilities—deliver a complete useful offering economically against a matched human-service reference?
2. Does **Alpha**—an additional net mission advantage after complete costs—improve this network beyond Beta, and does sharing add value beyond private retention at the same aggregate resources, while each source agent keeps its ordinary local artifacts?

The selected venture is a **Verified Optimization Foundry**. It builds complete configurable decision engines for bounded, non-safety-critical digital operations. A commercial unit is a new buyer/deployment or genuinely new integration/schema contract, including formulation, solver/API, adapter, independent checks, stress suite, evidence, documentation and up to two bounded automated revision/revalidation rounds. Ordinary changes to costs, eligibility and capacities within a sold engine are included, not billed as another full project.

Candidates arise from customer-job delivery. Domain-specific R retains a portable semantic representation/compiler and solver interface. Domain-specific V retains an independently grounded certificate/counterexample workflow. Their source-job construction remains in job costs; additional generalization and validation are charged to retention. The public solver and competent reconstruction remain available to Beta. The research team supplied this opportunity space; autonomous discovery of a real market is not measured.

## Evidence classes

The central accounting has three disjoint records. **A** contains customer delivery, within-job learning and its declared share of operating overhead. **B** contains only extra skill extraction, generalization, fresh validation, portable packaging, sharing and upkeep. **C** separately identifies initial bounded venture discovery and any optional later research. A skill points to its source-job activities without spending those amounts again. A+B+C already includes all charged expense activities; A's overhead subtotal is not an extra charge. Refunds reverse revenue separately. Source approval, payment authorization, cleared collection and refund are distinct events. Only cleared cash can finance B or C.

**Observed locally:** source inspection, deterministic solver/checker cases, lifecycle/adversarial software tests, actual synthetic capsule cryptography, accounting assertions and executed simulation runs.

**External reported evidence:** the supplied Transformational Intelligence manuscript, GoalOS drafts, task-relevant optimization research, official model/tool documentation and published service rates.

**Derived calibration:** arithmetic over source tariffs/rates and declared quantities. The central human-team reference is $22,856.28: published U.S. GSA role rates multiplied by 196 **assumed** hours. It is not a received quote or verified human-only engagement.

**Assumptions:** scope-matched hours, activity quantities, frontier semantic correctness, shared errors, adoption, collection authorization/lag, construction, transfer, reuse, future Beta epochs and obsolescence. A benchmark result is never directly substituted for customer acceptance.

The cost register's paid component/version assessment is a bounded component-test batch. Whole-project reliability and dollar value inferred from it are **explicit model extrapolations**, not a set of free end-to-end frontier project executions. See `evidence/paid_work_cost_allocation.md`.

## What the policies do

- `optimized_beta`: endogenous posted pricing with no new shared-skill retention; expert models, tools, existing equivalents and useful ordinary source-job artifacts remain available.
- `optimized_private`: creator-only use as a causal ablation, with optional separate research. In the primary one-domain case, the creator already keeps the source artifact under Beta; paid portability does not repair or extend it, so the private policy does not buy that redundant packaging.
- `optimized_network`: optional retention of job-born skills and network-wide availability of admitted shareable Alpha; recipients can decline, be incompatible, already have an equivalent or fail transfer.
- `fixed_network`: a fixed 65% reference-price diagnostic with the same optional research. It is not the prescribed strategy.

The decision grid includes discounts, parity and premiums. Buyers can select the network, human service, postponement or no purchase. The controller learns from observable purchases and arrivals, not latent correctness or future outcomes. It is a bounded receding-horizon policy, not a claim of globally optimal game-theoretic behavior.

Investment is optional. Full costs, protected obligations, reserves and shared capacity apply. A positive service margin does not establish Alpha. A positive expected admission does not guarantee realized value. A positive future-use value does not erase delivery losses or historical optional research costs. Job construction is not charged a second time to skill admission. The complete ledger keeps these statements distinct.

## One exploratory policy diagnostic

The frozen recipient prior made favorable uptake structurally impossible and was inconsistent with admission forecasts. The nominal cost-only ablation also retains a recipient-valuation defect. Both are disclosed in `qa/recipient_posterior_review.md`; all frozen outputs and hashes remain unchanged.

A separate protocol was frozen before one 100-world run (seed 951557; 300 matched histories). It changes only a declared decision prior; actual Beta, quality, costs, tests, demand and capacity remain fixed. Useful reuse occurs in 36% of worlds, but the revised network remains $1,172.65 below Beta on average and below it in all 100 worlds. It improves $127.54 versus the frozen network on average. Distinct useful accepted output is unchanged. This supports a modeled useful-transfer path, not profitable capability compounding. No further tuning followed.

## Graph and privacy boundaries

The tested lifecycle is candidate → evidence → Alpha assessment → validation → admission → discovery → authorized reuse → reassessment, retirement, supersession or revocation. Admission evaluates remaining incremental retention/reuse value. Originating-job activities already charged in ledger A are never charged again. The earlier pre-spend decision includes all new B costs; historical B and optional C spending remain in complete enterprise profit.

Creator, writer, validator, admission authority and execution permission are separate. Exact direct-label `*.agent.agi.eth` checks use a simulated registry, not a real ENS ownership proof. Existing AGI Jobs `*.club.agi.eth` roles, contracts, governance and settlement are unchanged. Every reuse checks current canonical state, policy/Beta versions, scope, evidence, prerequisites and dependencies. An old history entry cannot restore retired or revoked influence.

Capsule retrieval, trusted execution, export and composition have distinct semantics. No plaintext confidential content is included. Encryption and authentication cannot prevent copying after an authorized export; revocation cannot erase that copy. The local process and trusted executor implementations are not a production sandbox. Identity, encryption, Merkle commitments and any future policy proof do not establish truth or economic value.
