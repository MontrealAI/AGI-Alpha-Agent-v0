## Independent question

Under which explicit conditions can a finite-cash network of competent frontier agents earn a sustainable surplus from complete, useful work relative to human-service alternatives, and when does retaining and sharing a capability add further net mission value after its complete lifecycle cost and the advance of the underlying frontier?

This is two questions. Human-market advantage is **Beta economics**. The incremental effect of a retained capability against competent Beta and the recipient's existing alternatives is **Alpha**. Neither implies the other. No price, discount, compulsory investment count or conclusion is inherited from a previous study.

The earlier development simulation had not entered confirmation when this question was expanded. Its code, protocol, pilots and audit corrections are preserved. Final hypotheses and policies are versioned anew. Prior frozen studies remain historical evidence rather than admission criteria for this edition.

## What counts as Beta

Beta includes expert use of the currently documented Astra-powered Pro reasoning configuration, Terra execution when appropriate, adequate context, original-source grounding, decomposition, software/solver tools, critique, correction, independent tests, and the agent's existing equivalent capabilities. Every policy can reconstruct the same representation and checker. There is no ban on an ordinary library, no arbitrary expert-use bonus, and no claim that absence of an artifact implies inability.

Model versions and settings are named, costs are metered rather than attributed to a free subscription, and future changes are separate simulated Beta epochs rather than actual announced model releases. The public algorithm in the local assignment witness belongs to Beta; a novel wrapper around it does not automatically qualify for the graph.

## Candidate Alpha and its falsifiers

Two candidate assets are considered: **R**, a retained semantic representation/compiler and solver interface; and **V**, an independently grounded certificate, counterexample and validation protocol. The joint bundle may avoid duplicate construction and make a supported offering economical even when one component is not independently worthwhile. A bundle's evidence and dependencies must remain joint.

The binding restriction is costly repeated engineering and evidence reconstruction across heterogeneous new projects. The proposed transformation preserves the buyer's authorized purpose while changing the reusable representation and executable evidence protocol. Fresh formulation, client-specific integration, semantic checking and stress execution are still required and charged. The candidate can fail because the recipient already has an equivalent, supported scope is too narrow, semantic faults are shared, construction or transfer fails, reuse is inadequate, or Beta absorbs the advantage before repayment.

Admission requires a recorded Alpha hypothesis and a paid, declared comparison against competent Beta plus available alternatives. The record states the test scope, Beta epoch, complete construction/evaluation/integration/distribution/acquisition/use/maintenance cost, evidence uncertainty, projected compatible reuse and expected net benefit. Merely correct, novel, encrypted, authenticated or included-in-a-Merkle-root candidates are insufficient. Simulated expected value is labeled as such; it is not empirical operational validation. A noisy assessment can still be wrong, so realized paired value is reported independently.

Historical construction cost remains in full-cycle economics. A later decision to keep using an already built asset compares future avoidable costs and benefits; sunk cost does not force wasteful continuation or retirement. These two valuations must not be conflated.

## Hypotheses and causal comparisons

1. **H-Beta:** Within a declared scope, expert Beta can produce useful accepted outcomes with positive complete-cost economics at a price chosen in response to buyers' alternatives.
2. **H-Alpha:** Optional candidate construction and admission can improve future complete net value over matched Beta without those candidates, after failed research and lifecycle costs.
3. **H-Share:** Making admitted Alpha available network-wide can add value beyond creator-only retention at the same total compute, agent count, job primitives and cash. Availability is not mandatory use.
4. **H-Erosion:** Advancing Beta and copying can remove Alpha. Paid re-evaluation and retirement can be rational; graph size and access restriction do not establish a durable moat.
5. **H-Authority:** A version-bound lifecycle can prevent bypassed admission and stale or revoked history from influencing canonical reuse in the tested research implementation. This is a software property, not proof of ENS authentication, on-chain enforcement or semantic truth.

Price is endogenous over a frozen grid that includes parity and premiums. Buyers may buy the network offering, use a human provider, postpone or decline. A receding-horizon policy can retain cash, invest, wait, evaluate a complement, or abandon. No fixed discount, reinvestment percentage, investment count, graph growth target or universal acceptance threshold defines success. Fixed pricing is only a diagnostic comparison.

## Evidence and confirmation

Observed evidence consists of executed code, invariant tests, local mathematical mechanism checks, cryptographic operations actually performed, and reproducible simulation output. External reports support workflow plausibility and rate anchors. Derived calibration is arithmetic over documented tariffs and declared activity quantities. Effort, demand, frontier semantic accuracy, construction success, transfer and Beta erosion remain assumptions with ranges and stresses.

The local assignment prototype is an explicitly limited synthetic witness in the appendix. Its 300 successful cases do not prove general frontier competence, autonomous invention, a completed human-team engagement, or net Alpha. The new substantive research implementation is the finite-cash network simulator coupled to the tested lifecycle and access-policy engine. No visual slider or template-generated mission is used as capability evidence.

Freeze code, behavior, parameters, tests, protocol and stress design before new confirmation seeds. Preserve all failed pilots and negative comparisons. Report distributions and paired effects; separate independent invention campaigns from descendant jobs. Later redesigns are exploratory and must receive fresh tests. Growing volume alone does not establish self-acceleration.
