## Material source findings addressed before confirmation

The review identified the following implementation issues while the new simulator was still being completed. The engineer corrected them before freeze; subsequent full-history reconciliation must verify the final bytes and outputs.

| Finding | Required correction |
|---|---|
| New scan, retention and public-equivalent acquisition payments were initially absent from complete-cost totals. | Include every new charge in profit and disjoint A/B/C reconciliation. |
| Activity and source-job fields existed in the result type but were not initially exported. | Export all activity receipts and source-job records in every complete history. |
| The old positive-network metric required a separate research bill. | Include paid-job retention and distinguish its origin and funding source. |
| Future composition costs were missing from the pre-spend retention forecast. | Include prospective recipient composition and the complete bundle reassessment cost. |
| A stale-Beta artifact could enter another candidate's comparison before its own reassessment. | Require the current Beta version in the available-asset matching function. |
| Assessment receipt labels did not resolve to exported cash activities. | Bind each initial assessment to its paid B/C envelope and each reassessment to its actual expense receipt. |
| A cash-blocked recovery could become a dollar arrear while its unexecuted resource work disappeared. | Keep the recovery task queued; execute later only with cash and capacity, or preserve its terminal expense obligation and unperformed resources. |
| A scheduled refund could be labeled actually refunded despite unpaid aggregate refund debt. | Separate refund due from paid status, and label individual allocation unknown when the aggregate record cannot identify it. |
| Reuse forecasts originally credited repeated visits after a recipient would already have retained an ordinary local artifact. | Cap forecast benefits by distinct recipient/domain first encounters; apply the same saturation to capital decisions, admission and the extra-capacity value projection. |
| The operating-only funding metric did not initially include seed-funded B retention. | Require both B retention and optional C research seed funding to be zero before applying that label. |

## Interpretation limits that remain

The price and capital controllers are bounded posterior heuristics. Their expected opportunity cost averages occupied capacity and can miss a peak scheduling bottleneck; they are not globally optimal policies. Acquisition forecasts use expected on-demand distinct adopters, while actual acquisition charges occur only when attempted. Component test outcomes inform an explicitly assumed mapping into whole-project economics; they are not complete customer-project trials.

Every originating agent retains its ordinary local artifact in Beta, including possible flaws. Shared retention must therefore earn value at compatible recipients who lack an equivalent. One domain is one declared schema family in this finite simulation, not evidence of broad transfer to arbitrary adapters. New complete deployment contracts, recurring inner-engine answers, local learning, graph sharing and additional research remain distinct units.

The primary paid-job portability action creates no additional same-domain benefit for its creator: that agent already holds the ordinary artifact in every policy. Accordingly, `optimized_private` does not buy that redundant packaging action; optional separate research remains available. This control does not force private agents to forget prior work. The primary model makes no claim that portability itself repairs a flawed source artifact or generalizes it to a new creator domain.

Failure-memory behavior is implemented and tested in the standalone lifecycle module. The economic simulator does not claim to populate or benefit from that memory. Protected-content declarations in simulated graph records do not establish real sanitization; local known-marker rejection tests are bounded, and authorized plaintext cannot be recalled after export.

A positive forecast, an admitted node, a used capability and a realized matched profit difference are different results. Historical research recovery and any improved ability to construct or validate later capabilities require separate measurements. No sales, model calls, live identities, production commissioning, token earnings or general self-acceleration are established by this review.
