## Economic simulator

Source-level material review completed before confirmation. The implementation was revised before freeze to remove a latent future-transfer forecast, charge copying and revalidation, give initial discovery an explicit setup period, preserve up to two correction rounds, protect known refunds in investment attribution and delivery, defer recovery when capacity is exhausted, and record every monthly resource dimension. The payment mechanism now assumes observable authorization at delivery followed by deterministic settlement lag; this is an explicit assumption, not advance knowledge of an unobserved future default. Early development outputs remain unvalidated historical pilot material.

A generator-consumption error discovered during development had counted only the first resource dimension. It was fixed before confirmation. The independent executable check in `qa/audit_information.py` verifies both iterable resource summation and invariance of the price plan to hidden asset goodness and future transfer bits when observable state is unchanged. It passed. This does not claim all possible information leaks are impossible.

The new canonical bridge in `baseline/reconcile.mjs` invokes unchanged source modules and audits all complete histories, including cash, obligations, reserve arithmetic, complete costs, true-accepted denominators, resource ceilings, funding partitions and pre-investment pairing. Final confirmation receipts are required separately. The first bridge-development receipt is preserved; it reflects missing resource fields in an earlier pilot plus a bridge category-mapping omission, not final evidence.

The price/investment policy is a bounded receding-horizon heuristic. Its use of average construction occupancy can understate peak foregone delivery capacity. Expected quality, transfer, lifetime and customer preference models are uncertain assumptions; the decision procedure is not a global optimum or a general chance-constrained policy. Its reserve test is deterministic; Monte Carlo insolvency is a separate conditional simulation statistic.

Research repayment is a total paired policy-profit comparison including investment and all other costs. It is not a per-component proof of useful transfer, an empirical commercial return, or a self-acceleration measurement. Graph records gate declared scope and version; checks on a combined delivered project remain the ordinary semantic/certificate validation process, with shared errors explicitly possible.

Critical review checks: the controller cannot inspect future or latent truth; price selection must use only observable state and registered candidate rules; held-out confirmation cannot tune its own policies; complete failed intake and failed construction costs count; contributions and reserves must reconcile; commitments cannot exceed available cash; human-comparable scope and agent revision caps must match; correlated errors and endogenous capacity limits remain explicit; transfer checks for equivalent recipient assets; repeated investment and faster capability construction are separate outcomes.
