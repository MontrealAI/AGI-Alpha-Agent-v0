# Fresh frontier workflow cost register

The primary product is a **Verified Optimization Foundry** package: a bounded constraint-language compiler, executable solver interface, independent residual-certificate checker, fresh stress suite, reproducible package and up to two bounded automated revisions for non-safety-critical resource assignment and replanning. The buyer controls deployment. This is a complete software/analysis deliverable; the live toy demonstration is not the priced package. A commercial arrival is a distinct buyer, deployment or new integration contract. Routine assignment instances and parameter changes inside an installed engine are included; they are not new full-price projects.

Official model tariffs were opened again on 26 October 2026. `model_register.json` records exact source URLs and limits. `workflow_costs.json` supplies machine-readable stages for the simulator. Its dollar totals are arithmetic calibration from official tariffs and declared usage quantities. The quantities, effort settings, latency, candidate counts and probabilities of successful construction are assumptions, not observed production performance. No arbitrary expert-use quality uplift is assigned.

## What competent frontier work means here

Astra Pro performs new-domain formulation, objective and constraint reasoning, alternative design, integration judgments, independent semantic critique, correction diagnosis, and capability evaluation. Terra performs visual/tool execution, implementation iterations and packaging where suitable. Every project retains substantial Astra reasoning even when software assets exist. The expert workflow uses a validated schema, grounded buyer constraints, explicit supported boundaries, independent checks against the original input, counterexample search, fresh tests and bounded correction. Agreement between models is not a certificate of real-world intent.

The initial full project attempt costs **$263.46** before correction, storage and recurring business overhead. This is 8.16M billed Astra input tokens, 1.528M output tokens, 8.2M Terra input tokens and 1.12M output tokens, distributed over many requests, plus search and 187 16GB container sessions. Each request is assumed decomposed below 240,000 input tokens; aggregate quantities are not a single oversized request. Pro subwork and reasoning are already in billed output quantities. A correction costs $37.30 and full independent revalidation costs a further $86.80; bounded tool recovery costs $9.25. None of these is a measured optimized cost.

Resource occupancy is separately exposed: 1,660 critical-workbench minutes and 3,740 billed container minutes per initial attempt, with at most four experimental containers in parallel. Provider token, request, concurrency and budget constraints must be enforced by the simulator. These timings are assumptions, not guaranteed API latency. Tool search content is included in the input-token envelope. The primary model assumes no paid provider-side human review or manual onboarding; automated intake/clarification, support, correction, recovery and packaging are charged. Out-of-scope or repeatedly failed jobs are rejected or abandoned with their costs retained. Buyer effort remains relevant to purchasing and delay.

## Retained capability affects named activities

Capability R can avoid reconstruction of the semantic compiler core and solver interface, worth $55.60 and 340 workbench minutes per compatible use before acquisition, maintenance and uncertainty. It does not eliminate new-task formulation, adapters or integration. Capability V can avoid reconstruction of the certificate checker and adversarial harness, worth $37.00 and 270 minutes; it does not eliminate independent semantic validation or fresh stress execution. These are explicit activity substitutions, not a multiplier on all job performance. A recipient with an equivalent asset gets no incremental acquisition benefit. A failed integration gets no reuse credit.

Complete finite research campaigns cost R $5,959.60, V $8,764.00 and a joint campaign $13,334.00 under the proposed quantities. Each campaign sums candidate construction, independent evaluation, integration, fresh revalidation and automated scoping units, including unsuccessful attempts. The joint plan has fewer duplicated construction and integration units; no discount in model tariffs is assumed. Campaign tokens and resource minutes are recorded so construction can compete with delivery or delay availability. Charges belong to the investment ledger once. Subsequent monthly maintenance, recipient acquisition and periodic revalidation remain separate.

## Commercial calibration boundary

The new human comparison is a complete multidisciplinary engagement, with independently sourced role rates and explicitly assumed hours in the venture-screen register. It is not the previous bounded software-repair price. The novel package has no observed matched fixed-price quote, so human references establish scenario bounds rather than a claimed exact market price. The simulator chooses price under declared buyer-choice, capacity, quality and cash assumptions; no inherited fixed discount or token-cost markup is prescribed.

Indexed evidence storage is charged at the published $0.10/GB-day, using an assumed 1GB retained for 30 days ($3 per project), without taking a free-tier credit. A proposed automated control-plane allowance of $200/month, explored over $50–$1,000, is an assumption. Stripe's published US domestic-card price of 2.9% + $0.30 is an illustrative settlement-cost input, not an account entitlement or a change to AGI Jobs settlement. If another payment mechanism is modeled, its actual tariff and timing must replace it explicitly.

A range of 0.5–4 times the base usage quantities is an exploratory complexity/cost stress, not a frontier capability multiplier. The final frozen simulator configuration is authoritative; this register is an input proposal and its differences from the final configuration must be retained.
