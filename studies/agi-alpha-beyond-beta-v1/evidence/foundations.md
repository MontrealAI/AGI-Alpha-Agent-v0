## 1. The actual foundation and what it establishes

The complete 24-page October 2026 manuscript **Transformational Intelligence: Learning to Change the Game**, Vincent Boucher, MONTREAL.AI, was read, including its mathematical, statistical, experimental, and transformation-record appendices. Original PDF and TEX copies are preserved in `evidence/foundations/`; their bytes match upstream Git objects at Alpha commit `cb1d232668564fec281dfb0ac834ddbb83ab5115`. The PDF SHA-256 is `d7170e8217e3bf6aa1b84868c1c76c5994c6995c5297ed092d7e72a6430c664f`. The provenance record contains the TEX digest, exact source links, last relevant commit and verification method. Any other digest denotes a separate edition.

Source: [fixed-commit TEX](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/cb1d232668564fec281dfb0ac834ddbb83ab5115/docs/Transformational_Intelligence.tex); [fixed-commit PDF](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/cb1d232668564fec281dfb0ac834ddbb83ab5115/docs/Transformational_Intelligence.pdf).

This is an operational foundation, not evidence that a commercially successful autonomous enterprise exists. Its exact example supplies two causal hypotheses, an intervention interface, and a known objective. Passive observations are indistinguishable; an intervention policy reaches 8191/8192 accuracy with at most twelve queries. An unrestricted Bellman planner achieves the same result. The manuscript explicitly disclaims autonomous discovery of the frame, a new causal algorithm, frontier-model performance, and general transformational intelligence.

The transferable discipline is:

- Keep authorized purpose, inherited means, and evaluation instrument distinct.
- Identify a concrete binding restriction and an executable change to representations, information acquisition, dependencies, protocols, or intermediate objectives.
- Construct a bridge back to original outcomes and preserve permissions.
- Include search, failed construction, confirmation, migration, execution, verification, maintenance and retirement.
- Allow complementary changes whose individual deployment value is negative.
- Compare fresh work and full discovery campaigns. A high volume of tasks inside one campaign is not independent evidence of improved discovery.
- Retain unchanged practice, further investigation, waiting and abandonment as legitimate choices.
- Treat a changed evaluator as a separately validated measurement epoch; a candidate cannot approve itself by weakening its judge.
- Separate observed improvement, mechanism evidence and a certified old-frame ceiling. Most commercial tasks have no known ceiling.

The manuscript therefore supports a **conditional theory and test design** for an inventive economy. It supplies no empirical distribution for invention yield, customer demand, validator accuracy, capability transfer, or acceleration.

### Evidence used in the screen

**Algorithm engineering:** [AlphaEvolve original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13131) reports code evolution with evaluators applied to Google scheduling, circuits and training kernels. [Google Cloud's July 2026 account](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/alphaevolve-is-available-for-everyone/) describes a seed algorithm, an explicitly supplied scoring function and a deployment process. These are external reported examples. They support plausibility of useful algorithm engineering; they do not establish autonomous business discovery or transfer their gain percentages to this study.

**Mathematical formulation:** [OptiMUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10172) combines formulation, solver code, debugging and evaluation. [OPT-Engine v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19924v2), revised May 2026, reports that automatically expressing constraints remains a bottleneck as complexity increases. [Microsoft's OptiGuide page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/optiguide-genai-for-supply-chain-optimization/) reports production use for planners' what-if questions; that human-planner workflow does not demonstrate fully autonomous intake.

**Existing algorithms:** [OR-Tools assignment documentation](https://developers.google.com/optimization/assignment/overview) and [capacity-constrained assignment example](https://developers.google.com/optimization/assignment/assignment_cp) establish that existing solver infrastructure already handles relevant problem classes. The new enterprise should reuse it. A solver's success on a generated model does not prove that the model expresses the buyer's original problem.

**Simulation credibility:** [NIST, Credibility Consideration for Digital Twins in Manufacturing](https://www.nist.gov/publications/credibility-consideration-digital-twins-manufacturing) locates V&V and uncertainty assessment throughout the lifecycle. This makes missing data, model discrepancy and drift explicit obstacles rather than free assumptions.

## 3. Recommended primary and its transformation witness

**Primary: Verified Optimization Foundry.** Deliver executable, configurable decision-engine packages for non-safety-critical bounded resource allocation and replanning. A clean initial example is assigning digital batch jobs to heterogeneous computational resources under declared eligibility, capacity and cost constraints. The useful product is the complete supported package, not one answer to a small assignment instance.

**Authorized purpose.** Improve the buyer's stated operational outcome subject to the original mandatory constraints, a response-time limit, a resource budget and supported integration boundary. Do not weaken eligibility, capacity, fairness or safety requirements to improve an internal score. New goals require a new authorized comparison.

**Binding restriction hypothesis.** Each customer or changing operating regime requires repeated interpretation of rules, construction of executable constraints, and reconstruction of evidence that a suggested action is feasible. A final answer without a faithful model and independent checks cannot support reliable automated use. This restriction is a hypothesis about development and validation costs; it is not an assertion that existing unrestricted frontier agents cannot already solve the problem.

**Mechanism.** Build a typed intermediate representation that preserves rule provenance, a compiler to an existing optimization formulation, independent feasibility/optimality checking where available, mutation and counterexample generators, and a replayable integration adapter. On the supported flow/assignment subset, an exact residual certificate can check an optimum relative to the modeled instance. Other problem families require different certificate types or scoped empirical validation.

**Construction and validation.** Charge the initial schema work, discarded schema variants, mathematical and software development, independent test construction, semantic audits, adversarial cases, integration, migrations, monitoring and compatibility maintenance. Independent original-rule tests are essential: two programs generated from the same wrong constraint representation may agree perfectly.

**New feasible action.** The buyer can submit supported changes through a validated interface and receive a bounded-time executable decision with a checkable record, without hiring a service team for each reformulation. The provider may economically serve smaller customized engagements or new constraint combinations that were previously too expensive for its own workflow. Both propositions remain conditional until demonstrated on fresh real work.

**Complementary bundle.** A semantic compiler alone may create outputs that cannot be trusted. A checker alone may have no affordable structured input. A deployment adapter alone does not produce a useful decision. Joint construction can make the whole service usable even where each part has negative standalone commercial value. Investment policy must be able to evaluate and stage the bundle while retaining a stop option.

**Failure conditions.** The natural-language or source-system requirements are underspecified; necessary state is unavailable; the compiler drops a hard constraint; producer and checker share a bad interpretation; a solver times out; generalized constraints invalidate a previously sound certificate; a buyer requires unsupported integration or manual operations; adequate maintenance overwhelms reuse; equivalent capabilities are already available to recipients; demand is too weak at any sustainable price; or construction consumes the available cash.

**Decisive mechanism test.** Freeze the charter, artifact identities, cost accounting, tuning access and confirmation distribution. Compare the retained package with a same-permission unrestricted workflow that can reconstruct the same representation and use the same solver. Include compiler-only, checker-only, combined, corrupt-output, omitted-original-constraint, stale-version and unsupported-domain conditions. For mechanism evidence, measure valid original-purpose outcomes, total construction plus reuse cost, latency and missed material errors. A small-instance exact oracle supplies an independent optimum; it does not validate arbitrary natural-language intake. A shared wrong specification must remain an explicit negative control.

**No overclaim.** A deterministic assignment demonstration establishes properties of its supplied formal problem and implementation. It does not measure a frontier model's ability to invent the representation, elicit requirements, discover customer need, validate semantic equivalence or produce a commercially accepted package. Those transitions belong in the economic model as explicit assumptions until separately observed.

These narrower per-analysis offerings cannot be used as a quote for a full calibrated digital twin.

## 5. Implications for simulation and claims

Prices must be selected within the modeled competitive environment; none of these comparator values prescribes a discount or guarantees a sale. Buyers may choose our service, an equivalent human service, postponement or no purchase. Trust, integration, supported scope, perceived quality, review burden, delivery delay and willingness to pay remain uncertain demand parameters. Report adoption and contribution thresholds rather than calling them calibrated demand.

The primary automated lane includes machine-readable intake, bounded clarification, specification checks, autonomous correction, recovery and revalidation. Every failed intake and unsupported request costs resources. No routine paid human reviewer or manual onboarding employee is embedded in the primary. If a scope requires one, it belongs in a separate intervention-dependent offering, including its full cost and capacity.

Capability value must be evaluated on fresh opportunities and heterogeneous recipients. Some recipients already own an equivalent method; some require adaptation; some cannot use the artifact. Costs of evaluating, adapting, rejecting and maintaining transfers count. A positive score on a reused training instance is not productive transfer.

The controller may learn from collected payments, intake results, validation observations, incidents and completed experiments. It cannot inspect latent true quality, future demand, future collections or counterfactual returns. Full-truth values can exist in the simulator's evaluation layer only.

Investment decisions may construct, retain an unfinished complement, buy information, wait, deploy a bundle, defer, retire or abandon. They require available finite cash and a reserve for known obligations. Positive expected deployment value is distinct from recovery of historical research costs and from causal improvement over a matched no-investment path.

Finally, growing job volume, a larger capability inventory and self-acceleration are different outcomes. A self-acceleration claim requires improvement in the cost, time, success probability or validated quality of **constructing and validating new capabilities**, on fresh matched discovery campaigns. The manuscript supports this distinction; no evidence in this source screen establishes it for the proposed enterprise.
