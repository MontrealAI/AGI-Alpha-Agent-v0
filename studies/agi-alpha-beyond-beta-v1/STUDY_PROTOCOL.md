# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — Learning to Invent Its Economy

Fresh study protocol, initiated 26 October 2026. The executable final freeze and hashes, written before confirmation, supersede draft numeric values. Earlier studies remain historical evidence, with no inherited workload, price, discount or investment-count criterion.

## Question and commercial unit

Under what explicitly supported and assumed conditions can a frontier-powered enterprise create a useful executable offering, operate profitably, and improve its future productive possibilities through optional investment?

Primary venture: Verified Optimization Foundry for bounded, non-safety-critical resource assignment. A sale is a complete new customer/deployment/integration project: original-rule specification, runnable solver/API and adapter, independent checks, reproducible tests, documentation and up to two bounded automated correction/revalidation rounds. Ordinary inputs and solver runs inside an installed package are included; they are not additional full-priced sales. The local assignment widget is an inner mechanism demonstration, not the priced complete product.

## Separate hypotheses and outcomes

1. A posted-price policy can produce positive complete-cost pre-tax cash economics under declared demand, quality and collection assumptions.
2. Retained representation/compiler R, independent evidence workflow V or their bundle can improve fresh project outcomes and net value after full construction and lifecycle cost.
3. Conditional pricing and optional investment can outperform named matched policies under fresh simulated initializations. Restraint and zero investment are valid economic decisions.
4. A configurable original-model representation plus independent certificate check realizes a bounded technical mechanism. This does not measure autonomous discovery, natural-language semantic correctness or demand.

Report useful accepted projects, false passes, losses, collection timing, cash shortfall, prices, queued/declined projects, construction attempts, admitted assets, cross-recipient reuse, operating-funded investment and paired net value separately. No composite success score, graph-size target or mandatory investment count is used. Growth in jobs or retained assets is not called self-acceleration.

## Evidence hierarchy

- **Observed here:** execution of local simulator, deterministic prototype, full ledgers and declared verification procedures.
- **External reported evidence:** official model documentation/prices, service-rate schedules and task-relevant research.
- **Arithmetic calibration:** source tariff × assumed billed activity; source service rate × assumed human effort.
- **Unmeasured assumptions:** real demand, intake reliability, frontier semantic correctness, correlated validation errors, construction yield, transfer, capacity entitlements and useful lifetimes.

No benchmark score is converted into a customer acceptance rate. No generic expert-use uplift is assigned. Frontier reasoning remains charged after asset acquisition. The baseline can reconstruct the same assets using the same public tools; a missing file is not an intrinsic capability ceiling.

## Decision policy and counterfactuals

Buyer best responses consider the enterprise, matched human service, postponement/retention and no purchase. Prices are selected over a bounded grid including discounts, parity and premiums. The model is a seller posted-price game, not a claim of equilibrium among strategic AI competitors. Demand learns only from observable offers and purchase outcomes. True correctness, future collections and realized counterfactual gains remain evaluation-only information.

The resource policy can hold cash, defer, construct R or V, construct a complementary bundle and reject an unproductive candidate. Its approximate finite-horizon cash objective includes complete costs, construction delay, uncertain yield, maintenance and finite collected cash. It does not maximize a proxy count of investments. Seed funding is tracked separately from funds attributable to operating activity.

Primary paired controls use the same exogenous world and project stream: endogenous-price/no-assets, endogenous-price/optional-assets, and a fixed-price diagnostic with optional assets. Structural and economic stresses test mechanism removal, equivalent recipients, reduced reference prices, weak demand, failures, collections, capacity, construction and asset lifetime. The specific final cell list is frozen with the code before confirmation.

## Confirmation discipline

Development examples and economic pilot runs may diagnose implementation errors. They are not confirmatory results. Final source, parameters, policy candidates, seed schedule and experiment definitions are hashed before the independent confirmation runs. If a defect requires revision, retain the failed edition and create an explicit new freeze before rerunning all affected conditions. Do not tune to make a result positive, discard a weak condition or reinterpret an earlier success criterion.

The economic replication unit is a complete independent simulated enterprise campaign, not each descendant project. Distributional uncertainty represents declared model worlds; Monte Carlo intervals describe sampling error conditional on that world model, not empirical confidence in real commercial success.

## Mechanism protocol

`experiment/protocol.json` precedes the 300-case local run. `experiment/foundry.py` uses an established assignment solver and an independently implemented residual optimality checker. Small cases compare against exhaustive enumeration. Corruptions, unsupported scope, infeasibility and shared wrong specifications are explicit negative controls. An unrestricted reconstruction control uses the same tools and should match. Separate QA is not added to the frozen primary sample.
