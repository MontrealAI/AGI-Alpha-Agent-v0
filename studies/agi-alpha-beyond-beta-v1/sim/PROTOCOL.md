# Frozen research protocol — Verified Optimization Foundry

## Scope and evidentiary status

This fresh study evaluates a venture that creates complete executable decision-engine packages for non-safety-critical resource allocation. One commercial arrival is a distinct buyer/deployment or genuinely new integration/schema contract, including a parametric API, optimizer, original-constraint checks, certificate/test evidence, documentation and up to two paid revision rounds. Repeated assignments or changed capacity/cost inputs inside an already sold engine are included in that product and create no extra full-price sale.

The parent mechanism experiment measures a controlled assignment compiler and independent residual-certificate checker, including original-constraint and corruption tests. The public solver algorithm is not claimed as a new invention. Its deterministic correctness within the tested synthetic scope is not an estimate of frontier-AI success on a new customer project. Model semantic accuracy, construction failures, false passes, shared errors, adoption, transfer, pricing response and commercial value remain unmeasured assumptions. The human-service references use published role rates multiplied by assumed hours, not obtained comparable quotes or verified human-only delivery.

## Locked questions and outcomes

The primary question is whether an automatically operated foundry can sell useful new project packages with positive pre-tax fully allocated cash economics under explicit buyer, quality, activity-cost and collection assumptions. Investment is optional. There is no required number of upgrades, no fixed discount and no rule calling every profitable enterprise a self-financing improvement cycle.

Compare three matched policies: optimized posted pricing without paid generalization assets; optimized pricing with optional R/V/bundle research; and optional research with a prespecified 0.65-reference fixed-price diagnostic. Pricing searches fractions 0.20 to 1.20 in increments of 0.05, including parity and premiums. Boundary choices are reported, not expanded after seeing results. The pricing game is sequential posted pricing with buyer best response among our package, a human service, retaining a current method and declining. Incumbent prices are exogenous; no Nash equilibrium or fully strategic rival game is claimed.

Report net cash profit, a separate valuation stress removing remaining paid false-pass revenue, true accepted projects, false passes, pooled costs including zero-output worlds, price distributions, actual paid research and source of funds, admitted/used capabilities, unfinished campaigns, insolvency, and paired incremental value. Per-world quality ratios condition on at least one validated delivery; pooled false passes divided by all validated deliveries is the main error rate. No inherited 1% quality ceiling or mandatory two-investment criterion defines success.

## Information and learning

A finite Bayesian grid represents uncertain buyer value and trust. The controller observes only its own purchase/nonpurchase and arrival counts, plus disclosed incidents and observable backlog. Human/retain/decline labels and latent material correctness are analyst outcomes. The purchase likelihood is a deliberately simplified logit model; actual simulated buyer values vary lognormally, so the controller is not given its demand-generating truth. Known queues and observed incidents enter buyer costs. Buyer review is procurement/acceptance/integration burden, not a hidden paid human QC service.

Research candidates are selected using a receding-horizon expected terminal-cash calculation with a 0.5% monthly financial discount rate, a 10% value hurdle and a deterministic cash/reserve guard. This is a bounded one-campaign lookahead, not a globally optimal POMDP. Its opportunity-cost screen uses average occupied capacity over the estimated build interval; it is a heuristic and may differ from the realized per-month queue and reservation pattern. The reserve covers known refund/recovery obligations, three months of fixed costs, a warranty provision and additional research headroom. It is not a general probabilistic solvency guarantee. Monte Carlo insolvency is separately reported.

R saves named compiler/solver reconstruction activities; V saves named checker/harness reconstruction. Fresh formulation, buyer-specific integration, semantic review, stress execution and packaging remain charged. Existing-equivalent recipients receive no new savings. Ordinary approved work may leave narrow setup metadata; that has no correctness bonus and creates no broad free capability. Adoption of externally available equivalents and transfer to a recipient consume paid acquisition activity. Forecasts use a paid transfer pilot/prior and actually observed transfer attempts, never latent future recipient outcomes.

A research campaign pays for its entire declared construction/evaluation/integration budget, including unsuccessful attempts. It occupies shared tokens, workbench minutes and billed container resources; ongoing campaigns reserve their scheduled share before delivery. New campaigns commit after current-period production and have a conservative extra commitment-month allowance in the forecast. A noisy paid pilot can reject a good asset or admit a bad one, including a shared blind spot. Graph admission checks simulated identity/scope/version/evidence/freshness and carries no quality or authority bonus. The baseline frontier workflow can reconstruct the same compiler/checker; absence of a retained file is not a capability ceiling. Bundle complementarity comes from jointly avoided activities, shared campaign cost and possible capacity effects, not a hard rule making no-asset work impossible.

## Accounting and time

There is one separately paid setup month for the explicit discovery activity budget, followed by 24 operating months and three runoff months. Setup token/minute counts and fixed control costs are recorded. All figures are USD before corporate income tax, excluding transaction-tax pass-through. Standard token tariffs are fixed at the documented rates. Quantity multipliers scale dollars, tokens, workbench occupancy and billed container time consistently. Request inputs are assumed decomposed below 240k tokens; no actual API or account entitlement is demonstrated.

At delivery, payment authorization is observable. Authorized amounts settle after the specified lag; there is no unobserved later default in this model. Failed authorization remains a costful completed attempt. Only cleared cash funds activity. Known defects create refund obligations; deferred recovery waits for paid resources, not instantaneous free repair. No invoice is booked for a queued, expired or rejected output. Available operating funding excludes original seed, protected reserves and unpaid obligations; seed-funded research is permitted and labeled.

## Confirmation and stresses

Development uses seed 731031. After code, cost register, configuration, graph behavior, tests and this protocol are frozen, confirmation uses independent seed 731229, 500 worlds in each of three primary scenarios and all three matched policies. Each policy receives the same keyed buyer/job/error primitives. Final primary parameters are not revised in response to confirmation outcomes.

Prespecified stresses use 200 worlds each with seed 731330: three cheaper global service/value-reference scales; sparse arrivals; excess demand; loose capacity with all usage charges retained; strong common error; equal retained and reconstructed component reliability; every recipient already equivalent; fourfold activity quantities; doubled research campaigns; doubled validation activity; weaker payment authorization; longer settlement lag; disabled retained structure; and narrower supported scope. These stresses test where results fail; they are not alternative primary conclusions chosen after inspection.

The frozen artifacts contain every primary world/policy/month ledger and research receipt. A median-profit world is selected mechanically for illustration. Conditional examples, if used, must be labeled as selected examples and never replace ensemble frequencies or negative cases.
