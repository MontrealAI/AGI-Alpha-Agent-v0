# Paid Work. Retained Alpha. Expanding Possibility. — frozen simulation protocol

## Question, scope and evidentiary status

This version studies paid-job learning. It preserves the earlier fixed-service and dedicated-research development studies without treating them as confirmation for this new question. Primary parameters are fixed before independent confirmation. No live provider inference, real client, external sale, production deployment, actual ENS authentication or financial transaction is claimed.

One commercial arrival is a distinct buyer/deployment or genuinely new integration/schema contract for a complete bounded, non-safety-critical decision-engine package: specified workflow, buyer-specific adapter, parametric optimizer, original-constraint checker, reproducible stress evidence, documentation and up to two bounded paid correction/revalidation rounds. Routine assignments and changed capacities/costs inside an already sold engine are included; they do not create another full-price sale. The model has five declared supported schema classes and one unsupported class. These labels and synthetic instances do not establish measured market diversity.

Published role rates multiplied by assumed multidisciplinary hours construct the USD13,014/22,856/37,175 reference prices. They are not matched quotes or proof of human-only production. Current official Astra/Terra tariffs are externally reported inputs. All task token quantities, tool budgets, frontier success/error rates, demand, emergence, transfer, scope, Beta evolution and benefit mappings remain assumptions. Executed mathematical and graph tests establish their tested local properties; they do not calibrate frontier semantic accuracy or commercial adoption.

## Three disjoint expense ledgers

A pays substantive job production, experimentation, source-component construction, semantic validation, corrections, failures, recovery and evidence storage exactly once. It also contains separately reported A_other operating costs: qualification/intake, ordinary Beta-equivalent acquisition, fixed/setup operations and payment fees. A_direct and A_other are subdivisions of A, not extra expenses.

B pays genuinely additional retention work: candidate scanning (including scans finding nothing), extraction/provenance, bounded sanitization/generalization, fresh component validation, package/dependency integration, authenticated access/distribution, recipient acquisition, new composition checks, maintenance, version reassessment and revocation. Source A work is neither free nor charged again. Primary retention costs are USD290.05 for R, USD307.95 for V and USD569.72 for a joint package before scenario quantity scaling. Each is a single-schema capsule, not the old broad research asset. Further cross-domain expansion is excluded from primary.

C contains the separately identified startup discovery budget and optional dedicated research campaigns. `startup_discovery_in_C` and `separate_optional_campaign_cost` distinguish them. Every expense has one immutable activity ID and exactly one A/B/C allocation. Their sum equals fully allocated expense, including recognized unpaid work. Refunds and refund obligations reverse revenue separately. Reserves are protected cash, not additional expenses. Whole prepaid capability budgets are distinguished from subsequent zero-dollar resource execution records, so financial and physical counts are not added twice.

The source job receipt fixes the already-charged A activity IDs before the B scan. Job records retain approval, authorization, scheduled settlement, actual settlement by the horizon, refund obligation/payment status and analyst-only usefulness. When aggregate refund arrears prevent attribution, cash-refund allocation is explicitly unknown; economic receipts subtract the obligation conservatively. Pending recovery remains unperformed work with its resource/cost obligation until funded execution, or is reported unresolved at the horizon.

## Strong Beta and bounded paid-job emergence

Beta includes competent current frontier models, accessible public solvers/tools, pre-existing compatible capabilities and ordinary local artifacts from prior approved work. The source agent retains useful local source code/context under every policy; that artifact may also be flawed after a false pass. Future jobs still pay fresh semantic judgment, integration, testing and correction. Missing a retained file never means the frontier baseline cannot reconstruct the same component.

A costful scan can nominate an artifact after observable validator approval, without consulting latent truth or future collection. Rights may be unavailable; a candidate can be redundant, unusable, fail generalization or fail fresh validation. Emergence in the paid-job pipeline is distinguished from actually collected useful source work. A source job may have pending or failed authorization. Retention still needs already cleared cash, which may come from other jobs or explicitly labeled seed capital.

Primary R/V capsules bind one source job and one bounded schema class. The source creator already owns its local equivalent, so private retention is often redundant. Network value must arise in compatible recipients lacking equivalent artifacts. Members can decline acquisition; failed transfers are not repeatedly charged as successful acquisition. All shareable admitted capsules are available to authenticated direct-label `*.agent.agi.eth` members subject to scope, version, freshness, composition, prerequisites and revocation checks. The `.club.agi.eth` roles, contracts and settlement authority are unchanged.

Ordinary local learning saturates the benefit: once a recipient has a validated project in that schema, it retains its own local equivalent. Forecasts cap useful transfers by expected distinct recipient/schema first encounters, not unlimited future visits. Acquisition costs use expected distinct adopters, which can differ from first encounters for multi-schema research assets. Copying, growing overlap and moving Beta can erode the advantage. The model does not claim a permanent moat, skill count as value, or acceleration from cloning job records.

## Decisions, observations and Alpha admission

Four matched policies are `optimized_beta`, `optimized_private`, `optimized_network` and `fixed_network`. They share the same exogenous buyer/job/error primitives, recipient population and aggregate quotas. The first excludes paid retention/research assets while preserving ordinary local learning. The second permits private assets. The third permits shared assets. The fourth is the prespecified 0.65-reference price diagnostic. Primary optimized pricing searches 0.20–1.20 in increments of0.05, including parity and premiums. A boundary optimum remains a boundary result; there is no outcome-driven grid expansion.

The controller observes our own purchase/nonpurchase, arrival counts, backlog, approved artifacts, completed transfer attempts, paid component-test evidence and disclosed incidents. Other buyer choices and latent correctness are analyst labels. Buyers choose among the package, a human service, retaining/postponing and declining, with uncertain value, trust, review/integration burden and delivery delay. Quality beliefs are beliefs, not measured human or model acceptance. The logit posterior is deliberately simpler than the simulated lognormal buyer heterogeneity. Incumbent prices are exogenous; this is a bounded sequential posted-price model, not a demonstrated Nash equilibrium.

Investment is optional. The pre-spend controller evaluates the entire still-unpaid incremental B/C budget, future acquisition/composition/upkeep and displaced delivery against a finite-horizon cash forecast. It can retain cash or defer. Already-charged A work is excluded from that incremental comparison. After B/C spending, admission/retention compares supported prospective Alpha with remaining avoidable costs; historical sunk cost and realized recovery remain separately reported. These are heuristic receding-horizon decisions, not a proven optimal POMDP or a general probabilistic solvency bound. Average occupied capacity and Poisson first-encounter/adopter forecasts are declared approximations.

Paid assessment observes modeled bounded compiler/checker/compatibility component tests. Initial evaluation units cover24 cases per component plus8 transfer checks; a USD48 version unit covers16 bounded component cases plus8 transfer checks. A joint reassessment pays per component plus a composition check. Four prespecified component strata provide an uncertainty diagnostic within the narrow schema. There are zero claimed complete-project pilot executions. `MODEL_EXTRAPOLATION_FROM_COMPONENT_TESTS` labels every dollar-valued project effect; semantic accuracy and the mapping remain uncertain scenario assumptions. The skeptical zero-mean prior with eight effective cases is not eight performed tests, and its descriptive interval is not a calibrated confidence guarantee.

The prospective valuation separates existing-project cost/reliability effects from additional constrained starts at a held-fixed price. Capacity credit is net of marginal costs/collection assumptions, bounded by inferred demand and the same first-encounter saturation. It credits no existing-project revenue twice and is zero with slack capacity or zero mapped activity benefit. Actual value is assessed by fresh matched trajectories after all expenses, not by the valuation forecast alone.

Candidate, evidenced, assessed, validated, admitted, stale, superseded and retired states are distinct. Identity/commitment validity is not correctness or economic value. Simulated sanitization keeps client material, restricted content, held-out evaluation cases and scorer internals out of shared capsules. The executable graph has a separate failed-approach memory, but economic simulation does not attribute unmeasured returns to that standalone feature.

## Cash, access, capacity and timing

The model starts with USD20,000 seed cash, pays an explicit discovery/setup period, operates24 months and runs off three more months. Only cleared cash can pay work. Payment authorization is observed at delivery; successful authorizations settle after the stated lag. No unobserved later default is assumed. Known refunds, mandatory recovery, a warranty provision and fixed operating reserves are protected before B/C spending. Seed and operating-generated funding are reported separately; token issuance/appreciation supplies no profit.

All producer, validator, correction, recovery, generalization, evaluator and graph activities pay explicit tokens/tools/container resources. Pro is a reasoning access mode on the documented model, not free subscription infrastructure. Output token totals already include reasoning; they are not charged twice. Assumed request decomposition stays below240k input tokens. Published account tiers are not claimed as verified access. Quantity multipliers scale dollars and resources consistently. Construction/retention occupies shared workbench, token and container capacity; it is not instant. Queues expire without an invoice for an undelivered output. Common failure bursts and shared producer/validator mistakes are modeled, with bounded corrections, abstention and imperfect automated checks.

## Outcomes and confirmation

Report pooled fully allocated cost per true accepted project, including zero-output worlds; conditional per-world ratios are labeled separately. Report false passes, pooled material-error fraction, failed/rejected work, collections and timing, insolvency and unresolved recovery, prices, A/B/C expenses, candidate/admission/retirement counts, attempted transfer/use and true useful fresh recipient outcomes. Reuse counts do not themselves show incremental value.

The principal causal comparisons are network minus Beta, private minus Beta and network minus private, with matched prices/learning trajectories where applicable. The endogenous-versus-fixed comparison is a total-policy effect. A positive policy increment with B and C both active is not an isolated B-asset repayment estimate. The stricter `operating_funded_paid_job_feedback` indicator requires: a useful net-collected source; an operating-funded B capsule used on at least one truly accepted fresh recipient project; positive total and quality-adjusted network-minus-Beta value; and zero optional C campaign expenditure. It is a portfolio-level modeled chain, not proof of realized sales or each asset's individual causal payback.

Development uses seed951043. Frozen confirmation uses independent seed951251,500 worlds per primary scenario and all four policies. All primary world/policy/month records, activities and receipts are retained in gzip level9. Sensitivities use seed951352 and200 worlds per condition, retaining every scalar world and paired comparison plus aggregates; complete histories can be reproduced from the frozen inputs. Earlier pilots and integrity failures remain separately labeled.

The19 prespecified stresses cover a cheaper service/value reference; demand downturn; zero emergence; optional-research-disabled; zero mapping; cost-only effects; already-equivalent recipients; unrestricted free ordinary sharing as a counterfactual; static/rapid Beta;48 recipients at unchanged aggregate compute; specialization; concentrated compatible scope; doubled validation and retention activity; strong shared errors; weaker/slower authorization; loose capacity; and a compound static-Beta/48-recipient/recurring-demand/expanded-capacity condition. The compound case is explicitly exploratory and includes added control cost. No negative condition is removed, no primary parameter is retuned to force retention, and software QA fixtures are not economic evidence.
