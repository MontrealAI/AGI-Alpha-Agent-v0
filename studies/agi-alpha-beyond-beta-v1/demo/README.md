## A job is charged once

The original delivery workflow pays for formulation, construction, experiments, validation, correction, recovery and handoff. It may leave a compiler, solver interface, checker or test harness. Keeping the artifact does not authorize a second charge for its construction.

The cost panel separates:

- **A: delivery and learning already charged.** This includes intake/qualification, failed attempts, correction, revalidation, recovery and evidence storage.
- **B: genuinely additional retention.** Invoked scans, extraction, generalization, independent component evaluation, a retained package, recipient distribution/acquisition, composition, revalidation and upkeep.
- **C: optional dedicated research.** A separate full research campaign with its attempts and construction costs, not a mandatory fee attached to every emergent skill.

The implemented A ledger separates A_direct from A_other; A_other already contains explicitly reported overhead, fees and qualification, and is not added again. A_direct+A_other+B+C equals complete cost. Refunds reverse receipts separately. Reserves restrict available cash and are not another expense. Rejected or unsuccessful candidates retain the costs actually incurred. The monetary quantities are assumptions priced at documented tariffs, not observed production invoices.

Validator approval, payment authorization and cleared collection are separate. A candidate can emerge before payment or after failed authorization. The controller cannot use analyst-only latent correctness or future cash. Additional spending requires cleared funds after obligations. Where available, the replay shows the actual recorded origin, additional activities and receipt IDs rather than a synthesized success sequence. Receipt consistency is a property of simulated accounting, not proof of real transactions.

## Capital decisions, admission and recovery

The **pre-spend capital decision** must price all additional B work and future lifecycle costs. Once expenditure is sunk, **graph admission** tests evidence-conditioned prospective benefit after the remaining costs. Source-job A is excluded from that incremental gate by matched provenance. Already-spent B remains in enterprise profit and a separately reported construction-inclusive residual forecast.

That forecast is not realized historical repayment. Actual modeled paired recovery, useful outcomes, cash, expected Alpha inventory and economic feasibility remain distinct. No prescribed number of investments is required. Waiting, retaining cash or rejecting a candidate can be the appropriate result.

The first component suite is a bounded evaluation unit; it is not a set of complete buyer engagements. Whole-project acceptance, semantic accuracy, demand and economic value remain explicitly conditional model extrapolations. Both failed component tests and successful tests cost resources.

## Controller limitation preserved with the results

The frozen candidate-quality prior is Beta(2,2): even 24/24 successes produce a posterior mean of 0.92857, and 16/16 reassessment successes produce 0.90. Favorable recipient Beta quality is assumed to be at least 0.97. Under the declared price range and maximum activity savings, the recipient rule therefore blocks favorable uptake even for maximally passing candidates. Admission and recipient trust valuation treat quality inconsistently. Zero uptake is a limit of this controller, not evidence of an intrinsic frontier or Alpha ceiling.

The intended `cost_only_alpha` ablation also omits its zero-quality-effect flag in recipient valuation. Its frozen outcome remains visible and is labeled confounded. A separately declared diagnostic may align the candidate prior with existing construction assumptions; any such result remains exploratory and does not replace the primary study or measure improved frontier accuracy.

The primary run uses 500 independent IDs within each condition: 1,500 condition–world evaluations and 6,000 policy histories, with IDs reused across conditions and policies. The stress study reuses 200 IDs across 19 conditions, producing 3,800 case–world evaluations. Neither aggregate is a count of mutually independent worlds.

## Reading the modeled economy

Four matched policies separate competent Beta, private retained capabilities, a shared network and a fixed-price network diagnostic. The display separates human-team reference, chosen prices, buyer alternatives, complete metered cost, collections, available cash, retention, optional research and fresh outcomes. The human reference is published professional rates multiplied by assumed complete-team hours; it is not an observed quote.

The price slider inspects a recorded grid decision. It does not rerun buyers or revise the frozen model. World illustrations are paired across policies. Any selection restricted to funded retention is explicitly labeled and does not change population statistics. All-world pooled cost includes zero-output worlds. False-pass rate pools false passes over delivered passes. Future Beta improvement is an assumption, not a release prediction.

More job volume, transfers or graph membership alone does not establish richer capability. A genuinely new feasible outcome must be identified against the matched competent baseline, with time, cash, quality and capacity constraints explicit.

## Complete sensitivity table

The companion reads all 19 declared v3 conditions from `results/paid_work_sensitivity/results.csv`, their named summary files and the completed `run_manifest.json`. It verifies each configuration and workflow identity against the frozen design and checks the CSV against the case summary. No winner is selected and no adverse row is discarded. The `loose_capacity_cost_only` machine key changes resource allowances while retaining quality effects; its visible label says this explicitly. The separate `cost_only_alpha` case sets realized quality effects to zero, but its recipient valuation omits that flag; the case is preserved as a confounded ablation rather than a clean isolation of cost savings. Reuse aggregates are labeled capability–project pairs when a project can count under more than one retained node. Distinct useful project totals remain separate.
