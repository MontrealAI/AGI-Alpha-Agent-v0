## Question and mechanism

When does customer-paid useful work leave a reusable capability whose additional retention, validation and sharing costs are small enough to create net mission advantage beyond competent frontier Beta? Can network-wide availability improve future useful output and complete economics after cash constraints, failed transfers, maintenance and the advance of Beta?

The proposed mechanism is paid useful work → scoped capabilities learned through delivery → fresh incremental Alpha assessment → network-wide optional reuse → better or economically feasible future work. A separate research investment is an optional action, not a fee imposed on every skill.

The commercial comparison remains equivalent human-service delivery. The incremental capability comparison is internal: the same expert Beta and available recipient capabilities, with and without the proposed retained asset. A large human–AI cost difference does not itself establish Alpha.

## A strong Beta with ordinary local learning

Beta includes expert Astra-powered Pro reasoning, appropriate Terra execution, source grounding, adequate context, decomposition, deterministic tools, critique, correction and independent checks. It also includes the agent's existing ordinary local artifacts and equivalent capabilities. A source agent is not forced to forget useful code it just built. Its installed original-scope product continues to work without paying a second construction charge.

The candidate is therefore **portable incremental advantage**, not the mere existence of a file. A new paid job may produce a locally useful representation, compiler interface, checker or counterexample method. A potentially reusable artifact is scoped to the source job's schema/domain. It is not automatically the earlier globally generalized asset. Broader scope, new dependencies or a composition require additional explicit work and evidence.

Not every difficult job yields a useful candidate. Candidate emergence, rights clearance, sanitation, generalization, transfer and obsolescence are uncertain. A validator-approved source job can still contain a missed error. Candidate generation may use only observable work artifacts and approval, not latent truth. Fresh assessment can reject the candidate. Duplicates and failed approaches remain available as labeled historical/failure records, never as positive admitted Alpha.

## Three disjoint cost records

**A. Job economics.** Charge all resources used for the useful customer deliverable, including within-job learning, experiments, construction, production, validation, correction, failures, packaging, recovery and support. Keep offered price, authorization, settlement lag, refunds and actual simulated collections separate. Customer payment finances work; it does not make the work costless.

**B. Retention and reuse.** Charge only work beyond A: identifying/extracting a reusable candidate, rights and sanitation checks, additional generalization, protected fresh validation, portable packaging, recipient integration, distribution, access checks, composition, revalidation and upkeep. Link the candidate to its source job and exact already-accounted-for activities. Do not charge those activities again in B.

**C. Optional separate investment.** Deliberate work beyond the paid job and incremental retention, such as an independently commissioned broader capability, has a separate research ledger. Its construction, failed attempts and evaluation are paid once. Historical costs remain in enterprise profit; they are not silently moved into a second admission charge.

Each expenditure carries a unique receipt/activity identity and exactly one cost ledger. Startup discovery and operating overhead are separately explicit. Full enterprise profit subtracts A+B+C+overhead from collections. Pairwise policy differences include all changed costs and revenues. No retained-asset gain is counted as extra cash merely because it is recorded as expected value.

For prospective skill admission, compare expected future net mission value with competent Beta and relevant existing alternatives, after **remaining incremental** retention and reuse costs. Do not require repayment of the paid job's already-accounted-for costs again. Prior additional research cost is reported for historical repayment and informs investment before it is spent; it is not a reason to reject useful prospective reuse after it is sunk. Admission, capital allocation, operational authorization and realized profitability remain separate decisions.

The hypotheses are: profitable Beta delivery relative to human references; positive incremental retention value from paid-job learning; extra sharing value over private retention at equal aggregate compute and agent count; survival or retirement under moving Beta; and correct lifecycle/access behavior in the research implementation. None is a compulsory pass/fail hurdle or required investment sequence.

Use matched fresh worlds, buyer/task primitives and resource limits. Report private, shared and no-new-shared-skill policies plus a pricing diagnostic. Separate ordinary local learning, network sharing, extra compute, optional research and broad scope expansion. New feasible work means additional supported jobs completed under the same constraints, not a fabricated task template or a capability lock that prevents competent Beta from trying.

Evidence from bounded component tests may inform an explicitly assumed model of whole-project reliability and value. It does not become measured customer acceptance. Fresh evaluation cases and scorer internals stay outside shared skill contents. Publish exact evidence categories and stress the component-to-project mapping, including zero incremental reliability benefit. The local 300-case mathematical witness remains a limited appendix, not proof of autonomous invention or a completed commercial package.
