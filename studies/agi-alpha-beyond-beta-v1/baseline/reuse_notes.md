## Actual reusable accounting modules

`upstream/shadow-ledger.mjs`, `upstream/automated-costs.mjs`, and `upstream/funding.mjs` are unchanged Apache-2.0 ESM modules from draft research PR4940, commit `daac40ee9e02a1b1bb100d7b9fcfa5caab04c297`. Exact source paths, links and digests are in `upstream/provenance.json`. They were not established as accepted-main production code. The new bridge may invoke their pure calculations; invocation is arithmetic reuse, not production qualification.

Important contracts:

- Canonical observed-revenue fields require observed evidence. Simulated collections must remain explicit scenario values; observed collections are unknown.
- Complete cost allocations include failures. A true-accepted denominator is distinct from validator passes.
- Financing views can expose seed, collections, bills, obligations and reserves. Their historical policy thresholds must not silently become the new investment policy.
- A forecast is not a realized repayment; a positive ordinary operating margin is not causal capability value.
- Every module is read-only arithmetic. No transfer, token issuance, on-chain execution or real settlement occurs.

## Preserved earlier results

`historical/` contains unchanged old freeze/configuration/summary records and the later exploratory selection metadata, without duplicating old raw ledgers. `historical/prior_results_summary.json` is a compact labeled extraction.

The four old standard scenarios made no first or second capability investments. The central old model's mean material-error fraction was approximately 2.89%, above its own 1% threshold. Those results remain intact. They concern the old bounded software-repair model and its decision rules. They do not establish a frontier capability ceiling and do not define success for the new inventive enterprise.

The old two-investment endpoint, USD750 reference, fixed discount and construction settings are historical only. A new policy can choose zero, one, many, staged or complementary investments. The new question concerns productive capability and economic opportunity growth, with self-acceleration tested separately from job-volume growth.
