# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — The Productive Capital Engine

## From valuable work to expanding productive capability

This is a distinct, additive research contribution connecting useful local deliverables, explicit business economics and bounded capability investment. It preserves the existing Economics, Strategy Succession and Software Capability Foundry publications. It does not create a competing Agent release or change deployed contracts.

**Start with the [interactive bilingual demonstration](deliverables/AGI_ALPHA_Productive_Capital_Demo.html), then the [paper](deliverables/AGI_ALPHA_Productive_Capital_Engine.pdf) and [commercial scorecard](COMMERCIAL_SCORECARD.md).** The standalone HTML opens locally without an account, API key, wallet, installation or network connection. Its fresh computations, recorded research and business simulations are labeled separately.

The study establishes scoped local delivery and a cash-conserving modeled financing mechanism. Ordinary retained tooling saves repeated work. The tested parent/child method does not establish an advantage over a strong conventional cache, a larger production frontier, demonstrated self-financing or self-acceleration. The commercial comparison is conditional on sourced human labor rates, equal acceptance and explicit assumptions about human task time and full delivery costs.

## Useful delivery and measured capability result

Six generated projects span SDK release, research publication and operations handoff, each at two complexity strata. Each project produces a source/dependency inventory, a constrained rollout or repair plan, and a bilingual evidence-linked dossier. The source bodies are public, pinned repository files. Project dependency declarations, durations, capacity and changes are synthetic. These are not six sampled customer engagements.

Four cycles and three methods yield **72 joint requests**, all accepted within the frozen domain, and **216 family checks**. Family outputs share a joint production cost; never count three copies of that cost or treat them as independent jobs. The retained parent is actually invoked 24 times and the child 12 times. The child produces a transitive change-impact certificate, preserves unaffected evidence and keeps source, schedule and document revisions consistent.

On fresh-change cycles, the flywheel uses **4.069055 CPU seconds** versus **4.032727 seconds** for ordinary caching, about **0.90% more**. Both rebuild 18 of 132 evidence cards and reuse the other 114. The observed benefit against full reconstruction is conventional reuse; it does not establish a special improvement mechanism. Full authorship, correction, maintenance and human review costs are additional to these local timings.

## Reproduce without changing the archived evidence

The supplied `reproduce.py` never installs dependencies, fetches a network resource, provisions a service or submits a financial transaction. Its default mode verifies immutable identities and reruns the independent recorded-output audits in a temporary copy, preserving the archived reports:

```bash
python3 reproduce.py check
```

For just exact source, input, provenance and output hashes:

```bash
python3 reproduce.py hashes
```

Timing replays are explicitly separate from exact invariant checks. Use a new directory outside the extracted companion:

```bash
python3 reproduce.py primary-replay --output /tmp/productive-capital-primary-replay
python3 reproduce.py corrective-replay --output /tmp/productive-capital-corrective-replay
python3 reproduce.py economic-replay --output /tmp/productive-capital-economic-replay
```

Each command refuses an existing destination. Known-input primary and corrective replays are not new held-out experiments. Timings, solver tie choices and machine behavior can vary; the economic replay is deterministic under the recorded implementation and should reproduce its serialized output bytes. No replay is a test of an authenticated production executor.

The deterministic archive helper `python3 publication/package_study.py` writes a dry-run inventory and compressed-size estimate. Archive creation additionally requires `--build --final-pdf-sha256` followed by the exact approved final PDF hash. It verifies frozen-file inclusion and every ZIP member, fixes archive timestamps, and refuses to replace a different existing archive. Both archives extract beneath `productive-capital/`; the smaller LaTeX archive includes generated fragments, bibliography, figures and an optional flattened TeX entrypoint. Subsequent publication/handoff receipts remain outside these finite source archives.

## Economic interpretation

All business scenarios are **modeled**. Customer savings, invoices, collected cash, receivables, collateral, business costs, obligations, reserves, available earned surplus and capability spending occupy separate accounts. Token issuance and internal transfers are not operating revenue. The initial USD 2,000 is an assumed external seed, not earned surplus.

The empirical adapters jointly produce three outputs; the business model separately prices three assumed service classes. Their shared CPU is allocated once across those classes, which is a modeling convention, not measured marginal service cost or three independently validated customer markets.

The primary comparison uses competent traditional human work with ordinary software. Official wages and an explicit benefits-loading scenario supply rates; they do not establish task duration, matched quality or workers' AI usage. Both internal full-cost break-even time and the customer's price-plus-review threshold are reported. The USD 0.50-versus-USD 200 example and U.S. USD 5–10 trillion digital-labor range remain hypotheses/scenarios, not observed findings or obtainable revenue.

Policies allocating 0%, 25%, 50% or 75% are scenario controls, not authorization or treasury rules. Buying execution capacity, constructing a reusable capability and improving discovery are distinct proposals. The model retains losing cases and can rationally decline an investment. A financed plan can still violate a spending, human-time, provider or elapsed-time limit; financial coverage and execution feasibility are reported separately. A field named `npv_usd` in the raw model denotes an **undiscounted finite-life net benefit**, effectively a zero discount rate, not a separately discounted valuation.

## Operational, source and publication limits

- **NOT RUN:** authenticated browser-to-approved-executor execution, live paid collections, deployed-contract transactions and a matched empirical human panel. Recruitment was not required to complete the conditional market-data comparison.
- Full live Agent cost and actual human attention remain unknown. The simulations supply explicit estimates and assumptions, not zero-filled observations.
- Source inventories do not certify functional code, security, contract behavior or deployed bytecode. Synthetic source-text revisions were not compiled. Declared project dependencies do not discover hidden repository dependencies. A rollout plan does not execute physical work.
- The seven Identify→Out-Evolve records contain useful artifact provenance, but computation precedes their checkpoint records; they are not independently timed or enforced execution stages. The inherited workbench Jobs compilation is generic scaffolding, not the custom deliverables' semantic acceptance charter.
- The original Strategy Laboratory DOCX and its complete accompanying text were unavailable in this retrieval. The historical audit records `202610180921.docx`; the source review separately records the user-facing alias `202610180921(4).docx` without establishing byte identity between those names. Public historical report bytes and cross-linked summary hashes were checked; the original attachments and 62 payloads were not freshly rehashed. See `research/strategy_lab_verification.md` for that distinction.
- The first pre-freeze relative-path interruption has a retained failure summary but lacks its original full timing/output receipt. Later development failures and all primary/corrective confirmation records are retained. Missing development cost remains unknown.
- Source review, a draft PR or this companion does not imply a public software release, live website deployment, new operational authority, or owner approval. Existing release/publication owners and held Harness/Fleet operations remain authoritative.
