## Verdict

The paper's substantive conclusions are consistent with the finite evidence when their stated boundaries are retained. The study implements an inspectable local production history and a conserved modeled financing mechanism. It does **not** demonstrate a complete live Agent cost, accepted paid demand, operational self-financing, same-budget production-frontier expansion, or self-acceleration. Those negatives appear prominently in the abstract, production results and scorecard. Conventional caching remains the strongest production recommendation.

The original 72 joint results remain bound to the original version 1 source. The corrected browser adapter is version 1.1, with separately frozen edge qualification. The paper preserves the original defects, distinguishes development from confirmation, and does not promote version 1 timing into evidence of corrected timing superiority.

## Accounting and resource findings resolved

The first financial implementation charged setup, unsuccessful construction and maintenance in cash but omitted some corresponding human/elapsed time. The correction includes 240 initial minutes, 300 child-construction minutes and 15 active-maintenance minutes where applicable. No original finite scenario breached 3,000 minutes after adding these; the defect concerned completeness and future admission logic. Original source/results are archived, and the correction leaves their production and cash outcomes unchanged.

The first plan-feasibility diagnostic omitted purchased next-cycle provider capacity and its maintenance. The state-aware correction uses a still-live purchased block and charges its maintenance. This changes the affected provider-case diagnostic rather than inventing an experimental capability benefit. The prescribed 20-order plan remains infeasible throughout the finite main scenarios under the service-spending ceiling.

The manuscript cash identity must use **gross collected worker entitlement before conversion/spread and refunds**. The engine books conversion/volatility through paid operating cost. Defining receipts after conversion while retaining that operating-cost item would double-subtract it. The exact disjoint identity is:

`closing liquid cash = opening liquid cash + external financing + gross worker collections + collateral returns − refunds − all operating cash paid − productive investment − collateral deposits`.

Operating cash paid includes prior payable settlement. Collateral return is principal, not revenue; its deposit is a locked asset, not an expense. Receivables and asset depreciation have their own accounts. The root was notified and corrected the displayed equation.

The investment field `npv_usd` is an **undiscounted finite-life net benefit at zero discount rate**. The separately reported 12% collateral opportunity-cost scenario does not discount the investment stream. Paper and interface wording should preserve that definition.

## Sensitivity interpretation and remaining precision notes

The prelaunch remediation replay and the end-period authorship allocation answer different questions. In the replay, assuming 16 additional engineer hours plus four reviewer hours exhausts the USD2,000 seed before serving customers: all three classes have zero accepted outputs, closing cash is zero and closing payables are USD1,176.604567. A retrospective allocation across 362 already-modeled accepted outputs cannot finance that prelaunch gap. The manuscript preserves both results and should state the unpaid balance alongside the zero-output failure.

For numerical customer purchase thresholds, the model includes price plus customer review, while the general equation also permits switching expense. State the numerical assumption of no incremental switching expense and retain its actual value as unmeasured. A nonzero switching expense `S` increases the break-even threshold by `60S/W_H` minutes. This prevents a chart of price and review from silently claiming to encompass every customer's transition cost.

The optional token-route ledger separately reports noncash collateral opportunity cost; the family modeled cost metric includes delivery/business cash categories, capital depreciation and expected conversion costs, but does not allocate that opportunity-cost line. State that boundary if describing token-route family costs as complete economic cost. The direct-sale headline case has no locked collateral, so this distinction does not alter its published cost or break-even values.

Unrecovered original Strategy Laboratory DOCX/accompanying bytes remain unavailable. The public historical audit/provenance and small published results were checked; the 62 original payloads were not all freshly rehashed or reexecuted. Authenticated browser-to-executor operation, deployed-chain readback and financial transactions remain NOT RUN. A valuable deliverable previously impractical under the same constraints was sought but **not established**; this is an explicit negative answer to that objective, not an omitted success claim.

A new versioned PDF/TEX can travel through a separate passive-paper PR with exact catalog hashes and importer-generated README/homepage/service-worker files, committed together or squash-merged as one verified change.

A partial source snapshot that lacks earlier paper originals cannot satisfy full catalog/importer verification. Do not rewrite existing hashes, normalize originals, fabricate missing files, or call a partial check complete. Deliver the exact new PDF/TEX, versioned metadata, hashes and command to the established publication owner with a complete byte-clean checkout. That owner can run the supported importer and validator through the normal process. The finished paper may initially omit optional resources so it need not wait for executable-demo qualification.

Companion metadata requires a real verified download at the repository's canonical research-release URL, with byte count and SHA-256; source/evidence links must use the same research tag. A distinct research release need not replace the latest software release. Existing security findings, held reviews, owner boundaries and publication sequencing remain in force.
