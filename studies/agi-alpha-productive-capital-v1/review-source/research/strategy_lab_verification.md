## The Strategy Laboratory insight, with its limits

The preserved account describes strategy as an executable object: how an agent searches, acquires evidence, schedules, verifies, coordinates and allocates a bounded resource budget. A useful mission can leave a method or a verified counterexample that changes later work. That is the relevant hypothesis for the new productive-capital mechanism.

The DOCX's methodological lead is recorded as **creator → evidence-grounded challenger → independent adjudicator → revision → regression asset**. A challenge must identify original evidence, a consequential discrepancy and a reproducible predicate. Unsupported, trivial or out-of-scope criticism should be refused. Protected confirmation cases cannot be turned into training after exposure. An evaluator change needs its own validation and measurement epoch; a candidate's failure cannot be repaired by silently changing its success criterion. Multiple agreeing models do not establish independent truth.

The original experiment was a restricted arithmetic-AST search over synthetic route, evidence-selection and worker-scheduling tasks. Programs could not contain shell commands, arbitrary Python, authority fields or evaluator changes. Development considered 432 evolutionary and 432 random candidates. The archived independent audit reports 27,648 regenerated confirmation inputs, 193,536 checked policy/control artifacts, three promotions and three holds. Recorded nominal lower-bound decisions are:

| Attempt | Domain | Nominal gain lower bound | Decision |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Route | +0.712524 | Promote |
| 2 | Evidence | −0.001014 | Hold |
| 3 | Schedule | −0.003020 | Hold |
| 4 | Route | +0.062721 | Promote |
| 5 | Evidence | −0.002091 | Hold |
| 6 | Schedule | +0.018489 | Promote |

The first route promotion loses to the fixed weight-2 A* reference; the second route candidate has a descriptive score gain against that reference but higher recorded solver time. Its nominal-plus-shift solver interval is reported as 1.616 times the fixed strong reference. Timers omitted important work such as input/oracle construction and checking. Neither the promotions nor these intervals establish full enterprise cost superiority.

The accessible historical status distinguishes a separate semantic/statistical audit under Python 3.12.14 from a later unchanged original-policy replay under Python 3.13.5. We verified the bytes and cross-linked hashes of those reports/logs. We did not execute that historical code. Their word “fresh” refers to the predecessor study's execution, not this new audit.

## Verified negative findings from Strategy Succession

The new audit separately reads and checks the published `SUMMARY.json`, `ARM_RESULTS.csv` and `DIAGNOSTIC_SUMMARY.json`. All 24 arm/budget rows agree between CSV and JSON on integer counts, work totals, stops and active assets; subset, mean and percentage arithmetic is consistent. At the primary one-million-unit per-stream budget:

| Method | Qualified / offered | Active retained assets |
|---|---:|---:|
| Flywheel | 517 / 864 | 0 |
| Equally enabled learner | 517 / 864 | 0 |
| Fixed portfolio | 591 / 864 | 0 |
| Conventional saved workflow | 465 / 864 | 0 |

The flywheel improves on the saved workflow by 52 outcomes, or 4.333 per stream, but the portfolio delivers 74 more qualified outcomes than the flywheel. At two million units, both qualify 668/864 while flywheel counted work is **16.09945% higher**. In the ancestry diagnostic, inherited treatment qualifies 36/48 using mean work 888,009.5; the parent-free treatment qualifies 37/48 using 225,339.333. Inheritance saves **60.35258%** relative to exact reconstruction, which does not establish superiority over the cheaper conventional alternative.

The public docket also reports zero fresh repairs that both call their parent and qualify. That is preserved as a predecessor report; the present small-file audit does not recompute the full per-output ancestry predicates. Full `trials.jsonl` and large raw shards are intentionally excluded from the source tree and separately handed off. The source manifest labels raw availability `SEPARATE_HANDOFF_NOT_PUBLICLY_RELEASED`, with no public download URL. A direct request for the documented raw `trials.jsonl` path returned 404, consistent with that packaging declaration. We neither reinterpret the absence as failure of the experiment nor pretend that aggregates replace raw verification.

`evidence/prior/verify_prior.py` is the reproducible current check. It imports no producer solver, does not execute historical code and records its limits in `VERIFICATION_CURRENT.json`. Independence is implementation separation within the same commission, not external institutional replication.

## Transformational Intelligence source guidance

The exact source `docs/Transformational_Intelligence.tex` is retained with SHA-256 `9d4a664d951d6b97f5136bf0b44f1ca458e90d585434a6a27a542694bb2ece5f`. Its framework distinguishes task intelligence (particular result), strategy (reusable method), meta-strategy (better discovery across complete fresh campaigns), and transformation (changed formulation). A useful known specialist can deliver economic value without scientific novelty. A win against tested baselines does not establish a mathematical capability ceiling.

The source's lifecycle accounting includes search, build, confirmation, migration, execution, checking and maintenance. A new representation can be worthwhile because it makes verification cheaper, even if production becomes slightly more expensive. Its illustrative reuse condition is `R[(e0+v0)-(e1+v1)] > K`; this is an accounting condition, not evidence that every dollar invested creates a fixed benefit. The new study should add collections, obligations, reserves and working-capital feasibility to this lifecycle logic.

The finite causal example is an exact authored model, not autonomous model discovery or LLM performance. An unrestricted optimal planner matches the transformed policy. The proposed book-understanding application contrasts post-hoc critique with a source-grounded claim/dependency representation, explicitly retains the possibility that graph extraction overhead loses, and requires outcomes that preserve the user's actual charter. Those points support a real dependency-aware capability experiment with strong specialist alternatives and explicit overhead; they do not supply empirical proof for the new study in advance.

## Additive publication route

The inspected authoritative policy is `docs/agent/PAPER_UPLOADS.md` at the same commit, SHA-256 `00729ee5279cb83b0ee9d201685d45c08aedca5c4200063e719574e4662bdfb0`.

1. **Passive paper:** import the original validated PDF and optional TeX with a new versioned ID via `python -m scripts.research_papers add`, then `check`. The importer records byte hashes and regenerates bounded bilingual catalog/homepage/readme/service-worker output. The complete change goes through the existing PR/main process. A verified paper-only upload needs no software version bump. Prior manuscripts remain immutable.
2. **Interactive demonstration:** any new/changed HTML requires full source qualification. Its allowed catalog location is `docs/reports/<versioned-study-name>/<simple-name>.html`, exact SHA-256, maximum25MB. A paper-only overlay cannot introduce executable demo HTML. The software owner must qualify and publish those exact bytes through current release/Pages sequencing.
3. **Companion and evidence:** catalog v2 optionally links a separate research-release ZIP under this repository's canonical release-download route, with exact hash and byte size; a source/evidence link must name the same research release. The publication owner must verify actual downloads before linking. A declared URL is not proof of a public asset.
4. **Preservation:** a distinct research release can distribute the companion without replacing the latest software release. A draft PR, local package or research release does not waive owner qualification or authorize execution of held paths. A new paper should proceed independently of already completed editions.

Thus the concrete publication handoff can contain the passive paper/catalog metadata separately from its study/demo source and research-release assets, with an explicit source-pinned owner request. This is a packaging distinction required by the existing policy; it is not a reason to postpone delivery of the complete user-facing research package or to start a competing software release.
