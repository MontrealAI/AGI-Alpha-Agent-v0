# Commercial scorecard — Productive Capital Engine

**The immediate commercial case is conditional but concrete: sell a scoped accepted deliverable below the customer's equivalent traditional-work cost while retaining enough collected proceeds to cover all business costs and reserves.** This study measures local production and tests that business mechanism in explicit scenarios. It does not observe paid demand, complete live Agent cost or customer willingness to pay.

## What the modeled enterprise retains

Over six assumed 30-day cycles, the base scenario starts with **$2,000.00 external seed capital**. It models **$22,591.25 net external collections**, **$11,621.14 operating cash paid**, **$12,970.11 closing cash** and **$3,202.70 outstanding receivables**. After the scenario's obligation/reserve rules, **$8,776.09** is available operating surplus. Neither seed capital nor uncollected invoices are counted as earned surplus.

The specified next plan—20 target orders per class plus a newly commissioned child and working capital—needs **$3,564.83**. Full-surplus coverage is **2.462×**, and a 50% allocation covers **1.231×**. Yet the plan is **not executable under the unchanged USD 2,000 service-spending cap**: financing coverage is not resource admission. The separate post-result diagnostic finds a smaller 12-order-per-class target within those resource bounds, requiring **$2,396.55** including construction and working capital. Two attempts per order still do not guarantee acceptance.

The model's raw `npv_usd` field means an **undiscounted finite-life net benefit**, equivalent to a zero discount rate. The scope does not justify a token valuation or a forecast of appreciation.

## Token utility and route boundaries

The contract/source audit distinguishes Alpha's $AGIALPHA route, Jobs v0's configurable $AGIALPHA route and Manager's USDC route. The enterprise receives only its verified participant entitlement; customer escrow or volume is not automatically enterprise revenue. Validator allocations, burns, locked collateral, collection delays, gas, conversion and volatility can reduce proceeds or absorb working capital. No automatic 50% reinvestment, general holder profit share, new buyback or guaranteed appreciation is asserted. The study runs no chain transaction and changes no deployed contract.

## Evidence status and decision

The supported commercial proposition is to qualify a narrowly scoped service at a price customers prefer, then keep the strongest available delivery method and invest only where expected benefits justify complete costs. The local study completes useful machine-checked artifacts. The economic advantage remains a market-data-grounded model; paid demand, matched human acceptance, full authenticated Agent billing and actual human attention remain unmeasured.

Primary acceptance is scoped to the frozen generated domain. Four additional input-boundary defects were retained and fixed in a separately frozen v1.1 adapter; its fresh 28-case qualification does not replace the original 72-run timing evidence. The original Strategy Laboratory DOCX was unavailable, and historical report verification is qualified accordingly.
