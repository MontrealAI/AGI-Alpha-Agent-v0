# Productive Capital Engine: prospective financial protocol

Frozen assumptions: `economics_config.json`, SHA-256 recorded in `economics_freeze.json`, before any business-scenario execution. This protocol adds an economics mechanism to the new bounded production study. It does not replicate the existing 42-job Economics writer's experiment.

## Evidence boundary

All customer orders, prices, external proceeds, defaults, refunds, attention budgets, supervision, capability labour costs, maintenance and investment transactions in this protocol are **simulated**. No money moves. Complete authenticated live Agent cost is unknown (`null`), and browser-to-executor execution is not established here. The actual local workbench/repair experiment is a separately hashed measurement bridge. CPU, wall time, work units and local acceptance in that bridge remain recorded observations; they do not become billed operating costs or customer demand.

Human attention assumptions are not timing measurements. The comparator rates use the separately sourced BLS ledger. Salary statistics do not supply task duration. Report traditional-work break-even minutes and a stated sensitivity grid, alongside customer price plus remaining customer review effort. Family output counts remain separate; no arbitrary quality-adjusted aggregate score is used.

## Economic entity and routes

The entity is the worker enterprise. Customer gross prices, worker entitlements, actual/modelled collections and other participants' allocations are distinct. The direct off-chain case is the base scenario. Alpha's large-amount worker share is 94.05%, subject to integer settlement rounding; Manager's validator-participating default is 52%. The enterprise owns neither wallet30 nor wallet10 by assumption. The optional Jobs case explicitly fixes its own fee, validator, burn and multiplier settings; it is not a deployment readback. Alpha collateral at 10% assumes `minimumStake` is nonbinding. Other bond, gas, spread and lock assumptions are labeled. Token appreciation is zero in the base case. No token mint, circular treasury payment, buyer escrow or returned collateral principal is counted as revenue.

## Finite production and cash accounting

Six 30-day cycles use demand for 20, 40, 60, 80, 80 and 80 sets of three complementary deliverables. Demand is an assumption. Each family has independent acceptance checks and an illustrative operational failure probability; each order permits at most two attempts. Failure draws are deterministically keyed by cycle, family, order and attempt so policy comparisons see the same underlying draws. These draws are not empirical estimates of live failure.

Initial contributed cash is $2,000. Each cycle retains the same $2,000 service-cash spending ceiling, 3,000 assumed human minutes, 160 assumed elapsed hours and the stated provider attempt cap. Expected-resource accounting is not a demonstrated real-time schedule. New orders reserve their worst-case two-attempt cash and attention needs before execution. Actual sampled attempts then release unused reservation. No order starts by borrowing against an unpaid invoice.

Existing integration and the unsuccessful construction attempt are charged initially. Per-attempt supervision, verification and model/tool cost; per-order setup and acquisition; per-accepted support, delivery and processing; fixed administration and business costs; maintenance; and paid construction all appear explicitly. Shared measured CPU is allocated once across a three-deliverable joint project. Unknown live usage is not set to zero.

Collections follow the declared cohort weights, with defaults written off separately. Refunds reduce enterprise cash; future receivables are not spendable. Collateral is a locked asset, not an expense, and returned collateral is not revenue. Any modeled capital opportunity cost is shown separately from cash expense. No automatic failure-to-slash inference is made. Fixed costs that cannot be paid become obligations and prevent new production rather than disappearing.

Money uses integer millionths of a USD. Every cycle must reconcile:

`closing_cash = opening_cash + collections + collateral_returns - refunds - paid_operating_costs - investments - collateral_deposits`.

Separately:

`closing_AR = opening_AR + worker_entitlement_invoiced - collections - defaults`.

A positive cumulative cash operating balance must first recover prior losses. Subtract paid investment, outstanding obligations and the illustrative cash-profit obligation reserve when computing earned funds. The available pre-allocation surplus is bounded by both earned funds and unrestricted cash after the contingency floor. The initial contribution is never relabeled operating surplus. The obligation reserve is a scenario buffer, not a jurisdiction-specific tax calculation; a reserve is unavailable cash, not a second cash payment.

## Three investment uses and admission

The 0%, 25%, 50% and 75% fractions provide current-cycle spending ceilings on available earned surplus. An unspent budget is not a transaction and must not be accumulated a second time.

1. **Capacity:** a discrete extra execution block has an assumed price, life and provider-attempt increase. It is admissible only if additional work is otherwise blocked by the provider cap and modeled contribution after other fixed budgets covers construction and maintenance.
2. **Capability:** `dependency-repair-v1` has real local execution evidence but estimated construction labour and maintenance. Value its observed CPU saving against the strong `conventional_cache` alternative, once per joint project. No attention saving, quality premium or constant dollar-to-improvement is invented. Finite reuse and three-cycle depreciation apply. Negative NPV or absent measured benefit gives HOLD.
3. **Discovery:** the discovery-process pilot has no measured productive effect and therefore remains HOLD. Its possible budget is reported without invented success.

An explicitly named compelled-research scenario may finance the actual child construction despite negative compute NPV. It reports that choice and cost as research. It does not relabel it profitable reinvestment. The ordinary conventional-cache option remains available at all times. Actual research construction in the local study was authorized from existing resources and is not evidence that simulated customer receipts paid for it.

## Next-cycle financing test

Specify 20 accepted sets, the actual child construction, one cycle of child maintenance and every modeled operating category. Required working capital covers two attempts per order and new collateral before collection. The plan's complete working capital is counted once; existing contingency/obligation reserves are excluded from its denominator and excluded from spendable numerator. This is a conservative cash requirement, not an assumed successful output guarantee.

`plan_cash_need = child_construction + next_cycle_operating_working_capital + new_collateral + one_cycle_child_maintenance`.

`full_surplus_coverage = available_preallocation_operating_surplus / plan_cash_need`.

`policy_coverage = policy_fraction * available_preallocation_operating_surplus / plan_cash_need`.

Also report construction-only affordability, policy funding eligibility and economic admission separately. A plan may be fundable and economically unattractive. A coverage ratio uses modeled demand, prices and collections and is always labeled modeled.

## Stress cases and reporting

Prespecified cases cover 55% price compression, triple supervision, 30% attempt failure, five useful reuses, slow collections, a 40-attempt provider cap, demand stopping, each currency route and adverse token conversion. Report accepted outputs by family; all attempted and failed work; human/elapsed allocations; cash and AR; costs per accepted output; retained and purchased assets; reserve balances; projected construction/working-capital coverage; and actual investment admission or HOLD. Rising cumulative revenue or output is not self-acceleration.

No result is yet preregistered with an independent institution. The freeze is a local timestamp/hash commitment. Any implementation correction must preserve the frozen assumptions and be disclosed. Independent arithmetic review by another session agent remains limited by shared commission, model family and tools.
