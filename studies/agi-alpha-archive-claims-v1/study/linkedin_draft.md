Some scientific questions can be answered before every ambiguous record is transcribed.

Our new AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL research edition tests that possibility with an executable instrument that preserves alternative readings, shared calibration uncertainty and bounded source errors.

The result: 50 exact comparisons covering 877,728 possible histories. On 36 fresh, research-authored cases, the instrument resolved 12 original questions. Treating calibration independently for each row resolved only six.

The discovery standard matters just as much as the result. Certain-answer databases and related methods already cover the mathematical core. We therefore withheld flagship status. This is a working bounded instrument, not an earned claim of a new scientific breakthrough or beyond-human foresight.

We also tested the same offering commercially, including source validation, failed work, acquisition, refunds, support and financing. At the best tested $800 fee, the baseline modeled campaign loses about $810 over 90 days. A favorable scenario earns about $6,283 before sunk research, but none of the declared robustness cells qualifies. Actual customer collections: $0.

The package includes the code, frozen inputs, independent verifier, ordinary-library comparison, corrected economics and failed explorations. A clean reproduction matched 189 core files and 25 economic outputs. Native Insight, Nova-Seed and FusionPlan components preserve the research record without creating a token, payment or production admission.

The next test is concrete: can agents construct faithful uncertainty models from real, authorized archival sources at a cost that makes valuable questions worth answering?

That is the frontier this edition leaves its successor equipped to investigate.
