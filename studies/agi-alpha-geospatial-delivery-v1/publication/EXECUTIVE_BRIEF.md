### Why the opportunity is useful

Administrative identifiers and geometry sometimes disagree. Silently forcing one answer can turn a tidy map into a misleading data product. Our acceptance contract preserves both evidence channels, confirms supported associations and queues unresolved obligations. It delivers a dependable starting point for integration while leaving source-owner fact correction to a separately agreed engagement.

Both actual source layers are WGS 84 GeoJSON. The demonstrated service does not promise survey accuracy, legal boundaries, field verification or universal mixed-format portability. Its counterpart is a human team producing the same assurance package, including the exception queue.

### What the successor actually inherits

A separate process used the frozen instrument on 100 Anjou facilities, returning 89 confirmed associations and 11 review records. An ordinary library with identical information produced identical artifacts. After review exposed three robustness defect classes, the corrected core passed 48 fresh structural missions; a conventional implementation matched 128 sampled public-source decisions.[3]

Optional CSV/WKT practice changed the executable workflow, but three boundary cases diverged in 32 fresh format/CRS missions. All 256 rows and extra fields were preserved; complete association semantics held in 29 missions. Automatic boundary decisions through that adapter remain on HOLD. Provenance and successful transfer do not establish an exclusive advantage.

The retention arithmetic is equally selective. At an assumed CAD 8 gross saving per use, remaining retention costs require 144 uses to break even, beyond the 96-attempt annual capacity. At CAD 24, positive value begins at 35 uses. Those savings are unmeasured, so optional network investment is held while the source code remains reusable.[4]

### What changes the commercial conclusion

The 80%-of-reference invoice price wins within the tested central policy family; the 50% price also produces positive modeled profit. Under broader uncertain quality, demand and costs, the frozen policy's mean profit falls to **CAD 13,624.01**, and its fifth percentile becomes **−CAD 4,831.75**. A 65% price performs better under that broader prior. Four- and eight-hour competent-human challenges, weak trust and sufficiently high delivery costs can overturn profitability.[5]

The smallest useful next validation is one new source bundle with a matched human effort estimate and metered E/E+ delivery, using the same acceptance contract and a conventional control. That tests the inputs that matter before expansion.
