# Accepted A/B diagnostic — separate exploratory registration

This package leaves the original **166-cell three-arm campaign gated and unexecuted**. On all four registered fresh missions, A and B passed the frozen independent complete customer-handoff checker. C2 passed three and over-included two out-of-scope calls on F03. C2 receives no financial curve or assumed zero-model cost advantage. The original experiment is not reclassified or narrowed to the three tasks C2 passed.

The A/B diagnostic is registered after production and before its market outcomes. It reuses all 24 previously prospective no-benefit stress cells, with paired seeds 3001–3008: 192 paired worlds and 384 enterprise histories. The inherited versioned market core and canonical cash ledger are imported unchanged. The original three-arm gate is not weakened; a separate schema requires every A/B mission and the independent audit rows.

## What this can establish

It is a structural negative control. Equal assumed A/B complete nominal delivery costs imply zero incremental unit saving. The unchanged investment policy therefore has no positive repayment case for the exact B asset, regardless of demand. Running the market tests whether strategic competition, rejected work, delayed cash, reserves and accounting preserve that conclusion. It can also describe the common assumed service market, without interpreting its modeled customers as observed demand.

Zero investment would agree with this algebraic prediction. It would **not** establish that B's true savings equal zero, estimate a treatment effect, validate willingness to pay, or establish superiority over a qualified conventional implementation. Unknown actual recurring and construction costs remain `null` and `IDENTIFICATION_HOLD`. Internal four-task acceptance does not estimate field reliability.

The nominal $5 complete attempt cost and $100 complete reconstruction envelope are transparent counterfactual values, not estimates, lower bounds, upper bounds or inferred bills. Nominal delivery includes model/curator work, labor, tools and routine checking. The retained failure multiplier separately charges failed-work correction. Construction includes discovery, authoring, failed candidates, evaluation, correction, integration and shared allocation. No elapsed agent time becomes a human wage bill.

The frozen acceptance checker covered the complete customer handoff. An auxiliary Workbench `fullTask` reference had one too few parent path components at trial time. Its identical `delivery/raw-task.json` existed and the customer-report references resolved. Any publication navigation aliases are post-outcome presentation repairs; this diagnostic grants no quality or effort premium for them and does not claim every auxiliary trial link was valid.

## Files and browser API

- `protocol.json`: prospective diagnostic protocol and interpretation limits.
- `scenarios.json`: all 24 retained no-benefit cases, with original scenario identifiers.
- `production-accepted-ab.json`: A/B-only projection of the preserved three-arm bridge and independent audit, including the exact B manifest identity and complete four-mission registry.
- `accepted-ab.mjs`: pure browser-compatible `inspectAcceptedAB(production)` and `conditionalAB(production, scenario, seed)`. Inspection must return `COMPLETE_AB_ACCEPTED_WORK` before scenario execution. It still returns `IDENTIFICATION_HOLD` for the actual unknown economic quantities.
- `run-diagnostic.mjs`: refuses absent approval, incomplete source closure, altered original scenarios, mismatched independent acceptance, or an existing output directory. Every world is retained.
- `audit-diagnostic.py`: separate Python reconciliation of all histories, cash, invoices, costs, reserves, decision-time earned funds and exact asset identities.
- `test-guards.mjs`: eight software controls, including acceptance refusal, no narrowing, unknown-cost handling, equal-cost symmetry, rejection revenue and exact B identity. These synthetic checks are not the registered market campaign.
- `candidate-freeze.json`: concrete proposed closure for root approval. The runner will not accept its prospective status.

The GUI may vary only explicit common A/B nominal cost, B construction/upkeep, and common buyer/price/demand/cash/competition assumptions. It cannot assign an unmeasured B advantage or introduce a C2 financial curve. GUI scenarios are exploratory displays and are distinct from the frozen 24 cases.
