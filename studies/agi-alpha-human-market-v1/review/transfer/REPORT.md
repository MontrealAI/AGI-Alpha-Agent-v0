# Six fresh cases: independent recipient and retained donor

**Both routes matched the independent semantic oracle on all six fresh cases. Their canonical outputs were exactly equal.** The donor also passed complete package and native-boundary verification. This result preserves the negative finding on capability scarcity: the recipient had already reconstructed the scoped capability before donor access.

The six inputs contained 8,242 source records. Outputs contained 1,713 clean records, 1,798 exact duplicates, 4,131 quarantined records and 600 superseded records. Every source record has a disposition. These deliberately varied synthetic cases do not estimate real customer defect prevalence.

## Execution and custody

The source, plan, recipient, donor and independent generator/verifier were frozen before fresh entropy and case generation. Twelve one-shot executions used identical source bytes for each pair, with alternating order. No attempt was retried or replaced. The prediction index was sealed before the separate custodian used its withheld expectations. The recipient agent did not read those expectations.

The driver took 1.5768 seconds. Recipient semantic subprocesses totaled 0.3873 seconds wall and 0.3858 seconds CPU. Complete donor subprocesses totaled 1.1270 seconds wall and 1.2297 seconds CPU. **These are different workloads; no speed ratio or speed advantage is inferred.**

Actual complete monetary cost, model usage and human attention remain unmetered. Actual customer collections are zero. The accepted donor packets are synthetic research deliverables; acceptance here means exact independent oracle and package checks, not a customer purchase or professional endorsement.

## Reuse conclusion

There is no observed semantic quality improvement from transfer in these six cases: both implementations pass. Neither reconstructs code during these runs. The earlier 108-second AI-assisted reconstruction is a recorded interval, not human labor or a priced cost. A future reuse scenario can subtract complete adoption and validation effort from that 108-second gross interval; net avoided effort is unknown. An absent donor file was not evidence of an absent capability.
