# AGI ALPHA Flywheel: The Human-Market Advantage

A working bounded CSV cleanup/reconciliation offering, frozen fresh-work evidence, matched human-service price research, complete-category cost scenarios, and a finite-cash buyer/reinvestment simulation. **The commercial result is conditional and simulated; actual customer collections are zero.**

This is an additive research contribution coordinated through [issue #4850](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/issues/4850). It preserves the earlier negative capability, graph and second-investment studies and their ownership. It does not qualify the installed Harness/Fleet, amend AGI Jobs roles or settlement, authorize live ENS use, or claim general intelligence.

## Main results

- Final G2 completes **24/24 fresh primary jobs**; G0 and G1 complete 8/24 and 16/24, with unsupported jobs correctly refused. Six separate stress cases per generation produce 55 completed packets and 35 correct refusals across all 90 evaluations.
- The final primary packets account for every one of **29,152 source records**: 6,018 clean, 15,009 quarantined, 6,263 duplicates and 1,862 superseded. This is a deliberately fault-rich synthetic corpus, not a customer-data distribution.
- Human-service advertisements support a frozen **USD20 / USD25 / USD99** sensitivity panel with **5 / 10 / 15% discounts**. These are posted offers, not transactions, matched custom quotes or a representative market distribution.
- **270/2,808 primary paired worlds** meet the two-investment cycle criterion. All use the USD99 reference. Lower-price initial demand is zero under the registered buyer-friction assumptions; this was predicted algebraically before confirmation.
- A separately registered zero-buyer-integration diagnostic has **80/216 cycles**. One does not recover historical/full project cost. It is not pooled with the primary result.
- The first-seed, middle-cost USD99/10% example charges **USD89.10**, accepts 59 modeled jobs, incurs **USD57.02 fully allocated cost per accepted job**, and ends with **USD2,203.03 available operating-generated funds** after declared reserves and two USD100 investments. Actual all-in money remains unidentified.
- An independently reconstructed recipient and the donor both pass **6/6 separately fresh transfer jobs**. The recipient already had equivalent semantic capability. Scarcity, a quality gain and an exclusive moat are not established.
- Independent event-ledger audit reconciles all **9,072 primary-plus-diagnostic histories**. This checks simulated accounting, not actual demand or costs.

## What the service does

One nonpersonal UTF-8 CSV, five fixed columns (`record_id`, `category`, `date`, `amount`, `updated_at`), explicit delimiter/date/amount/category/missing-value rules, and a declared duplicate/version policy. Maximum 50,000 records, 10 MiB and 256 characters per cell. The full contract is `protocol/DELIVERY_CONTRACT.md`.

The output is a clean-and-exception packet, not a promise that every row is import-ready. No missing value is guessed. G1 adds declared locale normalization; G2 adds explicit latest-version reconciliation, retaining ties as exceptions. Output contains original bytes, exact disposition links, reproducible rules, a scoped revision route, quality/semantic reports, a hash manifest, and an actual portable SUCCESSOR Workbench evidence pack with TRANSFORMATIONAL INTELLIGENCE structural summary.

Native boundary: `HOLD`, authority `[]`, settlement `NOT_CONNECTED`. Runtime delivery has zero model calls and no network activity. Research and authoring model usage is separate and unmetered. Provider/customer attention and professional review are not measured; financial scenarios explicitly charge for review, onboarding, acquisition, failures, revisions, maintenance and other business work.

- One layout failure and one controller indexing error occurred; an additive, separately frozen controller resumed the remaining 89 attempts. The first G0 refusal remains original, with inner timing only because its outer startup measurement was unavailable.

The authoritative executed panel is `[20,25,99]` in `protocol/PRICING_METHODOLOGY.json` and the registered economic protocols. The USD49 listing's 500-row limit did not fit the primary jobs. Discovery history was not rewritten.
- The frozen graph driver failed closed because required root/admission/lifecycle context was omitted. `recovery/skills_graph_context_replay.mjs` is a separately labeled postfreeze fixture diagnostic; it does not replace that primary failure. Live identity enforcement is NOT RUN.
- Initial postoutcome cost classification failed on a missing no-sales delivery category; the failed analysis and correction remain. The analytic new-buyer sensitivity correction affects ten rounded stress thresholds and no campaign outcome.

## Interpretation

Measured runtime is not complete cost. Sourced human-service fees are not verified sales. Modeled buyer choice is not empirical demand. Simulated collections and surplus are not cash earned by this study. Shared research/setup is charged once; accepted deliveries are the denominator; failures and obligations remain charged. Historical research recovery, profitable use of an existing asset and new operating-funded capability development are separately reported.
