\section{A frozen experiment on useful release plans}

\subsection{Mission distribution and protected evaluation}

The confirmatory design has 12 independent streams of 72 offered missions. Each stream contains three 24-mission windows with eight ordinary plans, eight tighter-deadline plans and eight repair tasks. Ordinary instances have 10 or 12 jobs; tighter and repair instances have 14. Durations are integers from 1 to 9, pooled capacity is 2, 3 or 4 workers, and precedence is generated as a directed acyclic graph under a prespecified sparse/dense mixture. The horizon is 128 discrete slots.

Deadlines are drawn from fixed slack multipliers of a registered structural lower bound, not selected from a candidate's output. Ordinary slack is 1.20 or 1.35, tight slack is 1.00 or 1.08, and repair slack is 1.08 or 1.20. A structural bound need not be attainable, so a missed target can be a hard or infeasible request rather than an invalid plan. Each repair uses a separately generated conventional baseline, not the preceding accepted mission's plan; it includes predecessor-closed locks, releases and a changed calendar. The main repair qualification requires feasibility, preserved locks and the deadline. Movement from the baseline is descriptive; optional movement limits are supported and tested separately.

The configuration for each stream is materialized once and reused byte-for-byte across methods and budgets. Discovery sees only six training tasks and four disjoint qualification tasks at each stage. Protected mission inputs are unavailable to candidate search. A global registry checks seed/scope and semantic task hashes across development, browser fixtures, independent-checker probes, load inputs, training, qualification and confirmation. It removes superficial task labels before checking equality; it does not test arbitrary graph isomorphism. The released source, strategy grammar, checker, analysis and resource protocol are frozen before execution. The unexecuted checkpoint and prospective coordination record make that order inspectable.

All delivered schedules receive the separate final checker. Speculative internal portfolio attempts are search computations, not individually accepted deliveries. The conventional learner is intentionally allowed to match the flywheel exactly: strategy learning is not unique to an agent label. Hand-authored specialist rules and the native optimization model are transparent expert-built software references, not a timed trial of independent human experts.

A separate native reference uses SciPy 1.17.0's HiGHS mixed-integer solver \citep{scipy117milp,highs}. Its prospectively selected subset is the first ordinary, tight and repair task in each stream, 36 tasks total. Both deadline-feasibility and makespan modes receive a two-second solve limit and a ten-second process-response ceiling. The makespan mode receives a freshly computed, independently checked portfolio upper bound, whose construction is disclosed. A zero-objective deadline model can prove feasibility without proving optimal makespan. Native model building, solve, startup, checking, CPU and end-to-end elapsed time remain separate from the JavaScript reference-work tariff; equal total native and interpreted computation is not claimed.

\subsection{Resource limits and two kinds of construction}

The primary cumulative budget is 1,000,000 declared work units per stream, with descriptive sensitivity runs at 250,000 and 2,000,000. A delivered output has a 100,000-unit pipeline ceiling. Discovery is offered after completed missions 1, 25 and 49, with at most 500,000 units for a stage. A stage reserves four candidate/reference pairs at 50,000 units per output before spending its exploration remainder. Pair order alternates. Equal \emph{total pipeline} allocations do not guarantee identical solver allowances: the current history and checkpoint overhead must also be paid. Both allocations are logged, and a stage-starved comparison defers admission.

The closed menus offer eight dispatch recipes, six lookahead constructions and three repair constructions. A fixed training-only successive-halving schedule first exposes every surviving method to the same next task, keeps the best half after a completed round, and finishes the last survivor on all six tasks. Eight dispatch methods therefore need at most 17 evaluations instead of 48 full-menu evaluations. An incomplete budget-stopped round cannot decide elimination. All eliminated candidates, partial searches, failures and charges remain in the record. One exact candidate identity is fixed before fresh qualification. A stream may be unable to afford all three opportunities. There is no repeated rescanning of an ever-growing corpus and no extension of demand to manufacture payback.

A separate constructive diagnostic uses 12 fresh streams, four changed missions per stream and four treatments: inherited ancestry, removed ancestry, rebuilt ancestry and an equivalent normalized replacement. Its total cap is 5,000,000 units, stage cap 1,500,000 and qualification allocation 100,000 per output. It may retain a correct method that fails the investment gate so that its descendant can be tested. This intervention is explicitly \emph{correctness-only retention}, not an economic promotion by the selective enterprise. Recorded calls establish whether a parent actually participates in child construction or qualification; exact outputs and replacement/removal effects establish what that dependency changes.

\subsection{Accounting, uncertainty and stopping rules}

Charged work includes named graph, calendar, interpreter and deterministic sorting operations; separate checker operations; source/input and envelope processing; evidence and checkpoint blocks; discovery failures; admission, lookup and maintenance; and retry/recovery work. The source defines the tariff. Wall time and CPU are separately measured. Synthetic fixture construction, including the supplied pre-disruption plan, is measured once as common WORLD-input preparation outside the conditional mission-work endpoint. It is disclosed rather than silently treated as the system's free product. Human and assistant authoring/setup costs have unknown duration and price; the experiment cannot establish all-in monetary profitability.

The primary outcome is qualified coverage of the entire offered stream at the cumulative limit. An early stop leaves the unattempted tasks in the denominator. The registered uncertainty calculation is a paired percentile bootstrap over the 12 independent streams, 10,000 resamples with seed 20261024. It does not treat correlated tasks within a stream as independent observations. There is one primary contrast; additional arms, caps, diagnostic interventions, native comparisons and productivity windows are descriptive. No post-launch exclusions, result-driven retuning or additional runs until a desired win are permitted.

Each stream has a 120-second wall limit and a two-second checker-response ceiling. The controller also enforces scope, authority expiry, finite work and STOP. A candidate cannot change the evaluation distribution, checker, budget or authority. These boundaries establish a bounded local experiment, not a production security proof or a persistent commercial service.
