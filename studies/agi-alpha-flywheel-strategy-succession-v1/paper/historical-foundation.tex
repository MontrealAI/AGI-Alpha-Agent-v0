\subsection{The research lead: make disagreement earn its place}

The DOCX proposes a useful chain: creator, evidence-grounded challenger, independent adjudicator, revision and regression test. The challenger must see the original evidence; a criticism is itself a claim to check. In this edition a challenge identifies a concrete schedule violation, such as overlapping crew demand or a moved locked job. The adjudicator recomputes the disputed predicate. A substantiated counterexample becomes a development regression asset; an unsupported criticism is refused. Protected confirmation cases are never promoted into training during the experiment.

This is executable deterministic checking, not a live multi-model debate. The independently implemented checker shares the same commission and host as the constructor. Its implementation separation reduces one route to correlated error, but does not establish institutional independence or a cryptographic proof of the entire research process. Candidate access is restricted by the AST language; a privileged host administrator remains outside that threat model.
