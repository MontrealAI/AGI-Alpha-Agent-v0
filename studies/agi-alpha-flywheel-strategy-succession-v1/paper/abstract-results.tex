{\sffamily\fontsize{25}{29}\selectfont\bfseries Strategy as a Productive Asset\par}
{\large A bounded experiment in discovering, retaining and building on better methods\par}
\vspace{3mm}
\textbf{Abstract.} Can completed work improve the method of doing the next job? We implement a bounded strategy-succession experiment through the unchanged released AGI ALPHA workbench: worker-constrained release planning, typed dispatch/lookahead/repair methods, separate correctness and investment gates, current result checks, persistent history and explicit stopping. We first recover and audit the original Strategy Laboratory, then freshly replay its six frozen-policy attempts and 27,648 confirmation cases in the recorded runtime. Those benchmark results are not inherited economic proof.

The new source and protocol are frozen before one launch executes 336 stream/treatment trials and 72 native-reference calls. At the primary cumulative budget, the flywheel qualifies 517/864 offered missions versus 465 for a saved workflow (paired mean difference 4.33; 95\% bootstrap interval [3.67, 4.92]). The equally enabled conventional learner matches it exactly, and the stronger fixed portfolio qualifies 591. No new asset is retained in any primary arm: the apparent advantage over the saved workflow cannot be attributed to inheritance. At the larger cap, all arms qualify 668/864 and the flywheel costs 16.10\% more than the portfolio.

A separately budgeted diagnostic constructs 12 typed method lineages. Fresh dispatch-to-lookahead execution and repair qualification reuse are verified, but no fresh repair both invokes its parent and qualifies. Inherited ancestry uses 60.35\% less work than exact reconstruction, and equivalent replacements preserve every fresh schedule. Yet removing ancestry and using the conventional fallback qualifies 37/48 missions versus 36/48 for inheritance at much lower cost. We therefore establish bounded method composition and conditional acquisition reuse, not a qualified three-generation production chain, net delivery advantage, self-acceleration or recovered full research investment. A complete offline browser planner, frozen protocols, raw failures, independent implementation audit, editable figures and reproducible source accompany the paper.

\begin{insight}[The result in one sentence]
A mission can leave a method used by a real successor. Whether that successor earns its cost requires a separate test.
\end{insight}
