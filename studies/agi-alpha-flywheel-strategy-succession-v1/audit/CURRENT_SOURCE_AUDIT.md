## 2. Existing research that this edition must not duplicate

The exact public owner comments are retained in `owner-comments.json`.

Nine cumulative caps span 100,000 through 39,000,000 units. The exploratory pair is charged 18,136,932 units for final-policy profiling. This is a frozen source-reported study, not new evidence obtained by this audit.

**Practical-value integrated paper, source `b72e23a86511fff40d50efbf31258011321d56f19370b57aad16de8655a0bfe5`.** [Comment 6389065165](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/issues/4850#issuecomment-6389065165) reports three public/nonpersonal CSV missions using an already available `smaller_side_hash` specialist and a saved source-bound profile. A changed price-to-cost scope refuses reuse. Conventional saved templates produce identical complete outputs; 65 versus 183 comparison-kernel operations exclude checking overhead. It reports no new algorithm or strategy search and no economic promotion. Its proposed namespace is `studies/agi-alpha-flywheel-compounding-b72e23a8/`; it remains an author handoff, not deployed research.

A new strategy laboratory should therefore establish an actual method-construction dependency and its removal/replacement effects. Merely caching a feasible set, selecting an existing catalogue algorithm or renaming a controller does not distinguish it from these active studies. Candidate discovery is also not unique intelligence when an equally enabled conventional strategy learner matches it.
