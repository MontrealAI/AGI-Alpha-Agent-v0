# Ancestry and executed parent use: scope clarification

**Twelve retained typed lineages are not twelve demonstrated productive deep successors.** This addendum narrows the interpretation of the already verified diagnostic. It changes no frozen source, experiment, raw result, outcome or endpoint. The source-bound artifact audit remains valid; the aggregate parent-call totals require attribution to their actual phases and strategies.

## Decisive result

The retained repair modes are one quality, one selective and ten stable. All 12 fresh lookahead missions call the inherited dispatch method, with 99 rollout calls in total; three missions qualify. Of the 12 fresh repair missions, ten qualify, but only one calls the inherited lookahead method. That one repair does not qualify. Therefore **zero fresh repair missions both qualify and call the inherited lookahead parent**.

Within the selected repair candidates' qualification phase, six outputs call lookahead and four of those outputs qualify. All four qualified outputs also report positive lookahead steps in the returned result. These are meaningful qualification cases, but they do not become successful subsequent fresh repair missions.

## Supported claim and independence limits

The supported result is an executed bounded system that constructs and stores typed method compositions, uses a dispatch parent on fresh lookahead work, and qualifies some repair outputs that invoke lookahead during candidate qualification. The frozen fresh-mission experiment does not show a qualified repair outcome that uses that deeper parent, and does not establish full-investment productive compounding. Equivalent replacement and reconstruction reproduce the retained outputs, but that does not prove a dormant embedded parent was necessary.

`ANCESTRY_EXECUTION_AUDIT.json` contains every paired fresh outcome for all four treatments, all selected-repair qualification cases, the raw compressed-file hashes and one-based raw-record locations. The independent audit rechecked 336 relevant artifacts with the frozen separate checker. It matched all 48 compressed raw-file hashes to the earlier audit. No candidate was rerun and no new experiment was conducted.

Parent-call counters originate in the producer instrumentation. This review independently attributes and sums those counters and independently validates the returned schedules. It does not independently trace CPU instructions. A call alone does not establish necessity, a beneficial effect or economic payback. The review shares the project commission and environment.
