# E — structured expert frontier search

## Judgment and boundary

**No flagship selected.** Four candidates span chemical recovery, construction materials, optical sensing, and quantum communication. Three reduce to identified constructions; the proposed unconditional optical output fails an information test. This is a failed discovery pass, not evidence that the possibilities are exhausted.

The shared exclusion amendment arrived after queries 1–4, before candidate commitment. Passive chemical event recording and unlearning were removed. Five of eight page-open attempts were blocked; chemistry conclusions use retrieved primary abstracts and institutional publication records. No laboratory, buyer, field, or patent validation occurred. All economics below are explicit assumptions or break-even requirements, not observed demand. The JSON preserves all 12 queries, eight opens, evidence states, and executable probes.

## E1. Recovered fluorine as a fluorination feedstock

**Beneficiary/output.** Industrial fluoropolymer reclaimers and fine-chemical manufacturers would jointly obtain destroyed waste plus qualified fluorinating reagent. The proposed transformation treats fluorine in persistent waste as a usable inventory: couple C–F cleavage to fluoride capture and feed the captured material into a specified synthesis.

**Capability and strongest comparator.** Phosphate-enabled mechanochemistry already converts several PFAS classes, including PTFE/PVDF, into KF and K₂PO₃F for fluorination. That 2025 advance supplies the central connection and construction ([Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08698-5); [author-institution abstract](https://chem.web.ox.ac.uk/node/4390421)). Its retrieved abstract does not establish arbitrary mixed-feed purity or every industrial reaction. Those qualification gaps do not establish a new productive capability.

**Smallest witness.** On one characterized concentrated waste, close the fluorine balance across liquid, solid, and captured gas; assay residual organofluorine; use recovered material in one disclosed fluorination beside purchased reagent and the published recovery route. Proposed thresholds: ≥95% fluorine closure, ≥90% useful fluoride recovery, no unexplained organofluorine above the declared analytical limit, and product yield/purity within five percentage points of the purchased-reagent control. None was run. Passing would demonstrate implementation unless the mechanism materially surpasses the primary route.

**Commercial mechanism.** A toll processor accepts specified concentrated waste, earns a destruction fee, and sells qualified salt to a contracted chemical buyer. The same operation bears sorting, reagents, grinding, emissions control, analytics, rejects, transport, and capital costs. Break-even requires gate fees plus accepted-salt revenue exceeding those costs; buyers participate only if qualified delivered cost and reliability beat their alternative. A synthetic upper bound is decisive for dilute feed: one million litres at an assumed 1 μg/L PFAS, 70% fluorine, perfect recovery, and an assumed $100/kg KF yields only **$0.214 gross reagent revenue**. Dilute treatment therefore cannot be justified by that reagent revenue alone.

**Disposition/inheritance.** Reject novelty. Retain the mass-balance protocol, reaction-specific qualification requirement, and concentration-dependent revenue bound. Mixed contaminants, emissions, yields, and actual prices remain unmeasured.

## E2. Electrically rechargeable cement binder

**Beneficiary/output.** A demolition-fines processor supplying a precast plant needs a dry, storable binder that develops new strength with water, using recovered cement constituents. The binding restriction is that hydrated cement has already spent much of its original chemical driving force.

**Connection/construction.** Electrically create chemical disequilibrium: dissolve recovered hydrate through a pH swing, separate calcium-rich and silicon-rich streams, recover Ca(OH)₂ and reactive silica, dry separately, then blend for water-triggered binding. Electrical work pays for regeneration; “ambient processing” cannot mean free reversal of hydration.

**Capability and comparator.** Scalable electrochemical recycling already yields useful calcium/silica products ([RSC 2024](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2024/ee/d4ee03529a)); calcium extraction from waste cement is reported ([Nature Communications 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64339-5)); a 2026 author preprint explicitly claims ambient direct C–S–H regeneration ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7498193)). The accessible text does not settle dry storage followed by structural binding on real mixed fines. However, my construction reduces to recovered lime–silica pozzolanic binding. A different feedstock origin and acceptance specification are insufficient novelty.

**Decisive probe.** Make 100 g batches from three characterized fines, storing precursors dry for 30 days. Compare untreated fines, composition-matched purchased lime/silica, the strongest reproducible published route, and the proposed product. Measure energy, complete element/reagent balances, hydration heat, and 28-day strength. Proposed target: ≥30 MPa standardized mortar, no imported reactive binder, and performance retained after storage. Matching ordinary lime–silica behavior falsifies the claimed new mechanism even if the strength target passes. No chemistry was performed.

**Commercial mechanism.** Sell qualified binder by tonne, accepting specified fines for a gate fee. Yield, impurity rejection, separation reagents, electricity, drying, shipping, capital, and certification belong to this offering. Illustrative assumptions per product tonne: sale $100, yield 0.7, incoming gate fee $20/t, sorting $20/t input, other costs $65/t, electricity $80/MWh. Break-even permits only **0.4375 MWh/t** electricity. These are sensitivity inputs, not market estimates; liability and standards qualification would further constrain adoption.

**Disposition/inheritance.** No substantive advance established. Retain the dry-storage/strength specification and matched-chemistry control. The strongest unresolved experimental gap is whether actual mixed fines can meet that specification economically. Reopening novelty requires a demonstrably different reactive precursor or bonding mechanism, not only a successful recipe.

## E3. Albedo-agnostic clearance behind an opaque corner

**Beneficiary/output.** Warehouse robot operators need a defensible claim that a particular hidden approach corridor is clear, including when a learned detector is uncertain. Proposed construction: infer the set of all hidden radiance/transport states consistent with wall measurements, and declare a cell clear only if every admissible state leaves it unoccupied.

**Capability and comparator.** Passive imaging already reconstructs hidden scenes with unknown occluders ([CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Yedidia_Using_Unknown_Occluders_to_Recover_Hidden_Scenes_CVPR_2019_paper.html)). Intensity-plus-coherence imaging already studies measurement null spaces ([Beckus et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.02444)). Additional measured channels can improve identifiability. Neither retrieved abstract establishes an unconditional safety certificate; replacing reconstruction with feasible-set bounds alone is established inverse-problem reasoning.

**Executed rejection witness.** For the strictly positive transport matrix `A=[[1,2,1],[2,1,1]]`, nonnegative scenes `x=[1,1,0]` and `x′=[0,0,3]` both yield `[3,3]`. The third hidden cell changes from empty to occupied. Seventeen positive temporal scalings preserve exactly zero measurement discrepancy. This is a bounded synthetic irradiance model, not a physical room trial; it defeats the proposed guarantee for its admitted model class. It does not disprove suitably constrained or additionally instrumented NLOS systems.

**Acceptance/falsifier.** A useful successor must specify geometry, radiance bounds, noise, and optical observables; then prove zero false-clear outputs under those assumptions while attaining prespecified nonzero clearance coverage. An ambiguity pair inside the admitted class, or an always-unknown output, fails. A physical room comparator must include the existing multimodal method.

**Commercial mechanism.** A robot maker buys a camera/module and installation service per corner. Facilities provide installation access; the vendor carries calibration and maintenance. Price must exceed hardware, integration, assurance, and support while remaining below measured avoided stoppage/incident costs. No such benefit or price is observed. Universal abstention has no asserted saleable value.

**Disposition/inheritance.** Reject the unconstrained output. Inherit the exact counterexample and admissible-world specification. Reopening requires a new physical observable with an identifiability proof; another reconstruction algorithm is insufficient.

## E4. Heralded qubit transmission through completely depolarizing elements

**Beneficiary/output.** A quantum-network integrator would gain heralded entanglement-preserving transmission from channel elements that individually erase the input state. The proposed physical construction coherently superposes cyclic orders, interferes the order-control modes, and accepts a designated herald branch.

**Capability and comparator.** The full construction exists: N-channel cyclic-order theory gives heralded quantum transmission, with two channels insufficient ([Chiribella–Wilson–Chau, PRL 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00618)). A 2025 silicon-photonic experiment reports four-channel fidelity 0.712±0.013 ([author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07127)). Coherent control without indefinite order is also a relevant resource comparator ([Abbott et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09826)).

**Executed witness.** Enumerating all 256 four-channel Pauli Kraus tuples, coherently summing the four cyclic orders, and projecting the uniform control branch gives success probability **7/16**, six-state fidelity **5/7**, and normalized Choi partial-transpose minimum **−1/14**. Fixed order gives fidelity 1/2. Two and three channels give fidelities 3/5 and 2/3. This reproduces identified prior art exactly; it supplies no novelty.

**Acceptance/falsifier.** A new capability must beat a resource-matched coherent-control comparator on a separately useful channel class, with complete loss, control, herald, and channel-call accounting. Reproducing the four-channel branch fails novelty. Conditional fidelity without accepted throughput cannot establish commercial improvement.

**Commercial mechanism.** Sell an integrated transmission module with coherent routing, control, detectors, and calibration. Under ideal branch probability and extra end-to-end survival η, cost per accepted qubit is `(4×channel-use cost + control + detection)/(0.4375×η)`, before capital/support; there are at least 64/7 channel uses per accepted qubit at η=1. Buyers require delivered entanglement cost/reliability better than their alternative. No field-link or buyer evidence was obtained.

**Disposition/inheritance.** Reject novelty. Preserve the exact simulator and resource ledger. A general-channel criterion remains conjectural in [Wu et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02726), but a mathematical open conjecture is not itself a newly discovered useful offering.

## What would justify another construction round?

None of these four presently clears novelty, technical, usefulness, and commercial gates together. Cement has an exact unmeasured product specification; NLOS has an exact missing observable; quantum communication has a documented general-channel conjecture. Each needs an explicit substantive construction before further implementation is justified. The most useful retained output is the set of counterexamples, matched controls, and economic constraints that prevent those remaining gaps from being mistaken for positive discoveries.
