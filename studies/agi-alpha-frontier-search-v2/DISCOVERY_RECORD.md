## Selection and corrections

No candidate passed novelty, usefulness, feasibility, and commercial plausibility together. `adjudication.json` keeps these dimensions separate and records reviewer authorship. It does not convert a lack of construction into proof that a narrow product cannot exist.

Three essential scope corrections remain explicit: shared DNA-writing hazards do not cover an independent clock; a generic compressed-radiance nullspace does not disprove all snapshot spectroscopy; the hidden-region example does not compare a wholly empty room with an occupied room. Region 3 alone changes clearance status. A sound method may abstain. Native output inspection is not an independent physics experiment.

## Fresh successor search

Reopen a branch only with a substantive mechanism distinct from its strongest comparator and a purpose-preserving decisive experiment. A new source, ordinary library, small parameter variation, or more persuasive narrative does not satisfy that gate. Preserve all unfavorable cases and charge the complete additional investigation once. A research proposal is not a spending authorization.
