\section{One buyer, one need, one complete bounded deliverable}
\subsection{The buyer and recurring decision}
The concrete present user is the maintainer of the public AGI ALPHA native Python agent. Its application lock defines a real FastAPI, Starlette and Pydantic dependency tuple. A substantive change to that tuple or the original consumer definitions creates a maintenance question: do the declared request-boundary and strict mission-input behaviors remain satisfied? The original intent charter identifies this as an internal software-maintenance requirement, not an existing paid customer ticket \cite{usefulwork,coord}.

A mission is one exact consumer revision, one changed dependency tuple, one declared contract and one environment scope. The same request, a new report language, another timing run, a reconnect or a repeated assertion is not a new mission. This distinction is implemented in the product's workload identity and retained in the economic records. Future upstream releases create possible triggers; they do not establish demand, urgency or a buyer's purchase.

The useful deliverable contains the exact before/after locks and source identities, 21 request-boundary and 40 strict input-model cases, observations and execution receipts, a separately authored verification result, a proposed \emph{component} lock patch, English/French explanations and reproducible next steps. It tells the maintainer what can advance to broader qualification and what remains unresolved. It does not claim a completed application migration, security audit or deployment approval.

\subsection{The current delivered decision}
The main product example examines a proposed Starlette 1.7.0 to 1.10.0 and Pydantic 2.13.5 to 2.14.1 refresh, with pydantic-core 2.46.5 to 2.50.1 and FastAPI 0.141.1 unchanged. All 61 declared cases pass before and after in the writer's recorded native component profiles. The original FastAPI import bridge and 15-second body timeout remain intact. The supported action is to \textbf{retain the current native application lock and advance this component evidence to full-application qualification} \cite{usefulwork}.

The current product can import and recheck the registered evidence, prepare its bounded plan through the existing runtime seams and export the complete packet. This continuation successfully used its one-shot inert export. That export does not acquire a new dependency release or execute the consumer probe. The writer's separate bounded workflow produced the primary observations. Joining those two capabilities into an adopted unattended service remains the existing owners' qualification responsibility; it is not implied by local delivery.

\subsection{What competent human work the packet could replace}
At equivalent acceptance scope, conventional work includes identifying the actual consumer and supported requirements, resolving the relevant dependency closure, obtaining exact inputs, running valid before/after checks, investigating discrepancies, verifying the evidence chain, assembling the actionable report and handling exceptions. Existing project tests, package tooling and reusable scripts remain legitimate assets for that worker.

The scope is deliberately narrower than the full release decision. Remaining work includes deciding whether the refresh is useful, checking the complete application lock and supported platforms, and completing authorized authentication, persistence, mission-execution and release checks. Customer effort for those activities is not erased from the comparison merely because this packet passes its finite contract.

\section{The strongest practical alternative is already capable}
Dependabot proposes version-update pull requests and expects tests and review before merge. Renovate offers a free community cloud tier. Griffe compares API versions, while standard Python test tooling, package checks and exact locks support competent conventional delivery \cite{dependabot,renovate,griffe,uv,pip}.

The correct alternative is therefore a maintainer using those tools, existing CI and retained evidence, with comparable scope and reuse. Free software does not make staff effort or compute free; equally, this offering cannot claim value merely because it repeats a free tool's output. Its differentiator must be a complete consumer consequence and justified action with less remaining effort or valuable new coverage.

The primary study includes an independently implemented conventional \texttt{unittest} recorder and package assembler, with the same component, contract, environments, resource permissions, common verifier and Workbench. Both automatic builders may reuse exact prior evidence under the same custody conditions. The retained builder adopted the stronger conventional receipt checker. This reuse is a sensible engineering choice and an attributable investment cost, not evidence of novel algorithmic superiority.

No matched no-AI professional has performed the same accepted work with measured active minutes. Arm A is the actual researcher-assisted first-completion workflow constructed during the study. Its elapsed span includes orchestration and researcher interaction. It cannot serve as a wage-times-duration estimate for a competent human service.

\subsection{Sourced rates, unknown duration, conditional price}
The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports May 2025 median hourly wages of \usd{65.38} for software developers and \usd{50.14} for software QA analysts/testers. These are wages, not contractor rates or fully loaded employer costs \cite{bls}. The official Montreal developer benchmark is CAD45.67/hour, with a different reference period and currency; it is retained separately and not converted into the USD model \cite{jobbank}.

Let $h$ be a sourced hourly wage, $t_H$ the competent alternative's active minutes, $t_C$ remaining customer minutes with the offering, and $d$ a supported difference in other buyer costs. For equivalent accepted outcomes, an equal-cost price ceiling is
\begin{equation}
P_{\max}=h(t_H-t_C)/60+d.
\end{equation}
No observed $t_H$, $t_C$ or $d$ exists for this offering. The transparent comparison therefore reports thresholds rather than an invented saving. With equal other costs, a \usd{99} hypothesis needs 90.85 net developer minutes or 118.47 net QA minutes avoided. Any customer integration/review effort reduces the time saved.

\section{Repayment belongs to the exact improvement}
Selling a service profitably and repaying a capability investment are different claims. The latter requires an equivalent-quality counterfactual: what would those accepted deliveries have cost without the asset, using the strongest practical alternative and fair reuse? The six-mission comparison identifies no complete monetary saving against C.

For full incremental investment $I$, supported gross saving $s$ per equivalent accepted reuse and incremental upkeep $m$, the simplest finite case is
\begin{equation}
N_{\mathrm{break-even}}=\left\lceil\frac{I}{s-m}\right\rceil\quad\text{when }s>m,
\end{equation}
and there is no finite repayment of positive $I$ when $s\leq m$. Failures must remain in the cost numerator; reuse frequency and useful lifetime constrain whether $N$ can be reached.

For the illustrative \usd{600} cost ceiling and \usd{1} upkeep, repayment over 12, 48 or 144 useful reuses requires at least \usd{51.00}, \usd{13.50} or approximately \usd{5.17} gross saving per reuse. Those savings are required conditions, not observed outcomes. If the strong conventional workflow already achieves the same accepted work at equal complete cost, this asset has no positive incremental repayment advantage against it. Ordinary adoption can still be the right choice; it is not evidence of an economic Flywheel.

Useful lifetime ends when the required consumer, import bridge, environment, supported behavior, rights or maintenance economics cease to hold. The capability card defines fallback to the preserved complete manual workflow, explicit hold on incomplete evidence and retirement when need or validity disappears. Actual future recurrence and maintenance cost are unknown, so the delivered actual repayment fields remain null.

\section{Cash, obligations and the next decision}
The commercial extension derives two projections from the same finite item inventory. Economic margin subtracts all attributable paid and accrued current costs once. Cash availability subtracts paid current expenses, current unpaid expenses, distinct opening unpaid obligations and required reserves. An accrued bill cannot also be deducted as an independent cost in the same view. Duplicate supplied item identities across categories are rejected; different IDs for the same real invoice still require source-level review.

The native ledger's assumed-price field carries modeled collections. Observed receipt fields are left unknown in modeled projections, while the preserved actual study ledger records zero collections. Customer savings, token issuance, enterprise valuation, price quotations and projected sales do not enter actual operating cash.

The tested next-allocation sensitivity uses a hypothetical \usd{200} envelope only to test the difference between affordability and justification. No next asset is selected. An initially considered regression-handling gap was corrected by the existing product owner during this continuation; it is no longer presented as an unbuilt future investment. The initial record and this nonnumerical correction are retained. Actual work, useful recurrence, complete cost and avoided customer burden remain unmeasured. The immediate useful action within existing resources is delivering one genuine pending matching maintenance decision while recording active customer effort and attributable bills. No such new request or paid commitment is manufactured here.

The four-repository roles remain as verified in source. Alpha carries mission/evidence and bounded work. Fleet qualification and external execution remain separate. AGIJobManager uses a USDC route; the inspected native pilot has a zero currency cap. AGI Jobs v0 documents the separate \$AGIALPHA ecosystem route. None of these source facts implies a transaction, live settlement, token-holder distribution or permission to activate paid work \cite{manager,jobs,fleet}. This edition performs no financial action.

\section{Accounting conventions and uncertainty}
Every economic quantity carries a scope and basis. \emph{Observed} means a recorded source/command/result or supplied receipt assertion, not independent billing authentication. \emph{Sourced} identifies external rate/tool documentation. \emph{Modeled} means explicitly assumed scenario input. \emph{Unknown} remains null, including complete authoring/inference, residual attention and future demand.

Resource aggregation uses exclusive ownership and an additive parent/nested-evidence distinction. A campaign envelope is not added to its constituent commands. Peak memory is not summed across separate runs. Shared acquisition and both builders' construction remain costs. Existing source-code value is not invented, and repeated replay does not generate revenue or a second reusable asset.

The model uses finite integer USD cents. It refuses negative costs, nonfinite or fractional monetary inputs, unsupported currencies and mismatched asset references. Incomplete categories produce an explicit incomplete-assumptions status and null totals. It does not authenticate the truth of a supplied bill, accepted-outcome flag or reviewer assertion. Human review of source evidence remains necessary before any actual commercial admission.

The actual ledger is kept separate from all hypothetical rows. A positive scenario cannot convert unknown actual margins into profit, and an affordable hypothetical item cannot become authorized spending. No fee, token amount, price or allocation is submitted to a contract.

\section{Evidence needed for a commercial admission}
One real pending matching maintenance request should identify the accountable maintainer, exact current consumer, necessity of the change and accepted scope. Its complete outcome should be delivered with the existing component workflow. Observe preparation, review, integration, exceptions and remaining customer minutes; record the competent alternative's actual work with legitimate existing tools. Every invoice or resource quantity should have a unique underlying identity and a clear allocation.

Before calling the offering profitable, the accepted price and collection conditions must be supported, and full recurring cost must include failures, overhead, maintenance and justified setup allocation. Before calling the asset repaid, the same asset's complete discovery/construction/verification/integration/upkeep cost must be compared with incremental benefits on legitimate subsequent work. Before financing the next asset, existing obligations and reserves must remain covered and the next asset needs its own justified cost-benefit case.

These conditions do not require waiting for self-acceleration to use a valuable service. They prevent a useful technical product from being relabeled as a demonstrated self-financing enterprise before the missing commercial evidence exists.
