The Network That Learns to Invent

Abstract

An engineering team needs a reusable temperature-response calculation; a second team needs fresh diffusion responses; a third needs informative sensors for a finite inverse problem. We connect these useful computational objectives through an inspectable numerical component, then test whether its benefits survive ordinary technical alternatives and complete incremental costs. A researcher-constructed learning system retains an orthonormal basis from twelve founder solves and enriches it through thirty-two residual-directed Gym actions. Four candidates are preserved, one version is frozen, and unseen cases compare the original component, its developed successor and competent numerical baselines. Fresh confirmation comprises 128 paired in-domain cases, 64 shifted cases and 32 inverse-design missions per composition arm. The resulting component reduces mean raw relative field error 26.3-fold on the fresh transfer distribution; this is a measured representation change, not autonomous algorithm invention. Exact correction, assessment, setup and serving remain separately accounted for. All corrected forward methods satisfy the declared numerical requirements. Distribution shift increases the learned solver’s correction cost, while the sensor-design gain is also available to the equally funded competent generalist.

A matched, finite-cash enterprise experiment uses sourced service-rate anchors and explicit assumptions about frontier execution, demand, quality and collections. The reference Beta-only policy produces mean modeled operating profit of $191,886 over 104 weeks; no cash revenue was earned by this study. Optional retention decisions reject the numerical candidates at the declared service scale. The contribution is an executable separation of learning, useful transfer, composition and commercial value, including negative evidence and an existing production admission boundary. It does not establish specialist superintelligence, graph superiority, profitable sharing or self-acceleration.

Begin with the useful work

The immediate work product is a small scientific-software package that computes and checks responses of a heterogeneous diffusion model. Its founder mission represents a thermal-design service: provide reproducible response fields and a numerical envelope over specified material and source variations. A recipient then needs responses across a wider diffusion–reaction regime, and an experimental-design mission needs sensor locations that identify unknown coefficients from noisy observations. The deliverables are executable computations with finite, published acceptance rules. They are not verified physical products, customer engagements or human-expert trials.

This choice makes learned capability tangible. A saved numerical matrix contains information extracted from solved cases. Loading the matrix changes the recipient’s calculation, even though the recipient never observes the original training solutions. A second procedure chooses informative sensor locations. These objects can be independently hashed, transferred, executed, rejected and retired. Their practical value is measurable without assigning an agent a specialist label.

The research question is whether such experience can produce an advantage beyond competent ordinary tools, and whether that advantage justifies the additional resources required to develop and share it. The commercial foundation remains a separate question: can expertly operated frontier AI produce accepted digital outcomes at a cost below a price referenced to equivalent human services? A negative answer about this particular retention program does not refute profitable Beta delivery. Conversely, attractive modeled delivery margins do not establish that learning, a graph or an agent network adds value.

Three distinctions organize the paper. First, a technical result is not a customer outcome. The computation is executed; model-operated engineering and market transactions are simulated. Second, an accuracy improvement is not automatically an improvement in accepted throughput: a competent solver may already meet the requirement cheaply. Third, an accessible capability is not necessarily a capability that every recipient independently possesses. Executable transfer, trained-component transfer and specialist-service access preserve different things.

The work has a transformational aspect when it changes the representation or the experiment: reuse a low-dimensional response space instead of repeatedly starting from an uninformative state, or choose a measurement that resolves uncertainty instead of collecting more uninformative data. These are established scientific methods. The novelty claimed here is their joined, reproducible learning–transfer–composition experiment and its accounting, not the invention of reduced-basis methods or optimal design.

The finite discretization is deliberately reproducible on an ordinary CPU. It is much smaller than many industrial simulations. A positive result cannot be extrapolated to industrial geometry, physical fidelity or expert service quality without another evaluation. This limitation makes the empirical claim narrower; it does not turn the result into an assumed financial uplift.

Why earlier negative retention results matter

Earlier research editions provide distinct counterexamples. The Profitable Work First study reported attractive conditional Beta economics in its reference world while retaining no optional capability in its primary campaigns. A separately frozen diagnostic improved one network policy slightly but still underperformed Beta after complete cost. Other editions recorded assets that were reused without repaying their own incremental costs. The audit preserves their source links and distinguishes reported results from new replications.

Those failures suggest three bottlenecks: screening can cost money without creating an admissible asset; expected reuse can be too small or mismatched to the admission belief; and a capability can improve a task without saving enough of the enterprise’s scarce resource. The present experiment does not repair those findings by assigning guaranteed gains or lowering costs until a win appears. It constructs a real changed artifact, compares against a strong technical baseline, and asks which bottleneck remains.

The inspectable relay

Agent A: the founder mission

The founder solves twelve thermal cases. Each case specifies diffusion coefficients, a localized heterogeneous component, a reaction term and a spatial source. The complete response fields are retained as an orthonormal basis using two-pass orthogonal enrichment. The initial artifact, v1, contains twelve basis vectors. The modeled founder offering includes a reusable numerical engine, so its solves, checks and basis construction are allocated to A once. The executed founder receipt independently checks the twelve fields and their envelope; it is not customer acceptance of the retained basis. Additional packaging and subsequent lifecycle costs remain separate. No customer paid for the research proxy.

“Agent A” names a scripted research role. The surrounding algorithm, task generators and acceptance rules were constructed by the AI-assisted research team. No frontier model independently invented the basis algorithm during a purchased customer job. Nevertheless, the basis entries are determined by executed solutions, rather than written as a desired performance score. This is identifiable numerical learning.

Gym: change a representation through bounded experience

The Gym exposes observations, allowed actions, transitions, budgets and termination. Its state includes the current basis, available training cases and remaining enrichment actions. An observation evaluates residuals for a declared pool of seventy-two practice cases. An action selects an available practice case, solves it, orthogonalizes the new field and records the resulting artifact. The controller selects the largest residual, subject to a cap of thirty-two enrichment actions. A case cannot be selected after removal; an exhausted budget terminates the campaign.

Four candidate checkpoints at ranks 20, 28, 36 and 44 are assessed on development cases. The frozen selection rule prioritizes final accepted count, raw accepted count, then mean raw field error, with rank as a tie-breaker. It therefore selects for numerical fidelity, not secretly for final enterprise profit. All candidates, observations, assessment work and the original development attempt remain available. The initial six-sensor inverse task reached a development ceiling; a two-sensor, higher-noise challenge was specified before confirmation, with the original control retained.

The update changes a stored numerical representation. It does not modify proprietary model weights, learn a general skill-selection policy or demonstrate that an agent learned how to learn. Sensor selection is an executable procedure applied to current mission fields. Keeping these mechanisms separate prevents a larger basis, a higher practice score or more inference from being mislabeled as a general intelligence improvement.

Agent B: use the component on unfamiliar work

Fresh diffusion–reaction cases broaden coefficient and source ranges. The recipient loads the exact frozen artifact and computes a reduced initial solution. A residual-corrected solver then returns a response meeting the same final acceptance criteria as the ordinary methods. The raw approximation is also evaluated separately. Thus the experiment can detect learning even when a correction step makes the final outputs equally accurate.

The transfer domain shares a discretization and operator family with the founder but changes the parameter distribution and job context. It is a meaningful related-domain test, not evidence of unrestricted transfer across physics or software ecosystems. The artifact requires compatible grid indexing, operator parts, units and numerical libraries. Out-of-distribution cases deliberately increase anisotropy, local contrast and source concentration; their failures and overhead remain in the results.

Agent C: compose numerical and experimental expertise

The composition mission evaluates sixteen possible coefficient pairs. A sensor-design procedure derives low-dimensional response features and greedily selects locations using an information-gain criterion. No hidden true coefficient is available to the design procedure. Observations are generated from a held-out true grid member plus the declared noise; reconstruction minimizes the finite observation mismatch.

The comparison includes the numerical component alone with fixed sensors, the design procedure alone with the ordinary forward solver, and their combination. The primary competent generalist receives the same design method and ordinary tools. Fixed sensors are an ablation, not the generalist ceiling. Every arm receives the same candidate grid, sensor count and noise realization. Measured forward, design and reconstruction costs are attributed to each arm even when measurement reuse avoids physically rerunning identical code in the experiment. Unmetered research orchestration and authoring remain unknown; additional enterprise coordination costs are modeled explicitly.

This design can reveal complementarity without assuming a network advantage. If informative sensors improve reconstruction but the competent generalist can use the same procedure at equal resources, the result supports experimental design and does not establish retained Alpha.

Numerical method, acceptance and protection

The discrete system and the learned object

For parameter vector θ\theta, the finite-difference model is A(θ)u=b,A(θ)=kxAx+kyAy+cAc+ρAr,A(\theta)u=b,\qquad A(\theta)=k_xA_x+k_yA_y+cA_c+\rho A_r, \label{eq:operator} on a square with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions. The grid has 44×44=1,93644\times44=1,936 unknowns. Positive diffusion coefficients, a nonnegative local contrast and a nonnegative reaction coefficient make the operator symmetric positive definite. The public generators define the exact distributions. They are assumptions about a computational workload, not empirical industrial demand.

For an orthonormal basis VV, the reduced approximation is û=V(V𝖳A(θ)V)−1V𝖳b.\widehat u = V\bigl(V^{\mathsf T}A(\theta)V\bigr)^{-1}V^{\mathsf T}b. \label{eq:reduced} The projected affine operators are built explicitly. The stored basis preserves learned numerical information; a prose explanation of the method would not reproduce its entries. The construction is a greedy reduced-basis method, not a claim that an arbitrary neural checkpoint has been trained. Established reduced-basis and POD work supplies the methodological context; the sensor procedure uses an SVD of current response fields.(Rozza, Huynh, and Patera 2008; Gräßle, Hinze, and Volkwein 2020; Hinze and Kunkel 2010; Manohar et al. 2018)

Acceptance includes an independent numerical check

Ordinary baselines include sparse LU, incomplete-LU-preconditioned GMRES and a separable spectral preconditioner with conjugate gradients. Incomplete LU is used with GMRES, not assumed to be an SPD preconditioner for CG. The primary ordinary solver is selected on development runtime and frozen. Valid factorization reuse is allowed whenever matrices repeat. An explicitly declared improved-generalist numerical policy tests a stronger ordinary choice on the shifted regime; this is not a newly measured API-model release.(community 2026a, 2026b)

Final residuals are recomputed by a flux stencil separate from the sparse assembly. A direct solve supplies a research reference for field-error assessment. The final tolerances are relative residual 2×10−82\times10^{-8} and relative field error 2×10−62\times10^{-6}. Raw-surrogate tolerances are separately declared and are not substituted for the final requirement.

Protection and uncertainty

Training, development and fresh confirmation use distinct case seeds. Candidate bytes and source identities are frozen before root-supplied confirmation seeds are evaluated. No post-confirmation candidate tuning is admitted into the primary comparison. The shared research team and public generators do not constitute an external, adversarial evaluation service; the protection is procedural and source-auditable. Public release intentionally makes the cases reproducible after the experiment.

Paired cases receive identical equations, reference answers and mission noise. Numerical methods run with one BLAS thread, warmups and rotating execution order. Wall-clock differences at millisecond scale remain vulnerable to host noise; per-case rows and paired uncertainty are reported alongside iteration counts and error. Timing savings are not generalized to another machine by a clock-rate multiplier.

The reported 95% timing intervals are percentile intervals from 5,000 bootstrap resamples of paired cases (seed 81221) within one local run. They do not measure reproducibility across machines or independent sessions, and resampling does not remove host drift shared by several case pairs.

Confirmation estimates finite-distribution performance, not the probability that the assumed workload resembles a real customer portfolio. Repeating seeds reduces Monte Carlo uncertainty conditional on the generator. It cannot remove uncertainty about the generator, the scope of a human service or market demand.

What changed, what transferred, and what did not

The frozen Gym candidate has rank 44, compared with rank 12 for v1. On the 64 fresh transfer cases, mean raw relative field error falls from 0.02869 to 0.00109, a 26.3-fold reduction. However, neither raw component meets the separately declared raw acceptance rule on any of those 64 cases. The corrected methods all pass. Accuracy learning is therefore established within this workload; a standalone approximation meeting the recipient’s requirement is not. On the 64 fresh thermal cases, v2 passes the raw rule on 9 cases.

The meaningful learned change is the basis itself. It supplies a better initial representation of previously unseen response fields. This does not imply a proportional reduction in completed-job cost. The recipient’s competent baseline can solve the original finite system, and the corrected learned method still pays for projection, a linear solve, residual checks and any remaining iterations.

The productive frontier at fixed resources

Define the finite empirical productive set at budget BB and required acceptance qq as ℱ(B,q)={j:Pr(accepted outcome for j)≥q,𝔼[Cj]≤B}.\mathcal F(B,q)=\{j:\Pr(\text{accepted outcome for }j)\geq q, \ \mathbb E[C_j]\leq B\}. The operational definition must fix the beneficiary, original requirements, workload distribution and accounting boundary. A faster approximation that abandons accuracy does not enlarge this set. A composition expands the frontier only if it crosses a required reliability or cost boundary that the equally resourced generalist cannot already cross.

The descriptive time-frontier curves show local differences in verified execution latency. They do not show a new reliable offering beyond the competent generalist: all corrected forward methods meet the numerical requirement, and the composition ties generalist identification. No prospectively specified service-level deadline was crossed uniquely by the learned network. The enterprise model therefore assigns no automatic new-product revenue or quality premium to these objects.

Disjoint costs and cash

The model distinguishes three expense categories:

Frontier use, tools, computation, acceptance, corrections, base delivery overhead and the common startup capacity needed to supply the outcomes. Learning required to finish the paid job belongs here once.

Candidate screening, extra assessment (including every rejected rank), packaging, admission, sharing, serving and upkeep that are not already in A.

Additional Gym experience and discretionary development beyond the paid deliverable. Candidate assessment belongs to B; extra Gym enrichment belongs to C. Capacity expansion is separately identified.

Research labor and subscription/model use during this authoring session were not fully metered. Actual complete research cost is therefore unknown, not zero. Commercial scenario costs are projected operational expenses. They must not be described as invoices paid in this study.

Matched enterprise experiments

Five choices, equal starting conditions

The policies are: competent generalists without retention; private retention; private retention with optional Gym; selective sharing; and sharing with optional Gym and composition. They begin with the same seed, opportunity streams, quality shocks, price menu and resource limits. Candidate admission uses development evidence and declared demand beliefs. The policy cannot inspect future demand, future true quality or its exact date of obsolescence.

No policy is forced to buy training or retain an asset. The primary screen first asks whether even free elimination of all numerical computation could repay minimum retention cost; if that optimistic bound fails, the controller declines without consulting an unpurchased Gym result. When forecast incremental value is negative, declining is the correct policy outcome. Composition uses an equally funded generalist with ordinary access to the experimental-design procedure. Because no graph-specific advantage is claimed, an ordinary library with the same authorized bytes and permissions is an equality control, not a deliberately impoverished search baseline.

The horizon is 104 operating weeks plus collection runoff. Three primary environments vary opportunity arrival, trust, willingness to pay, quality, model cost, default and successor timing. A stronger frontier scenario changes base execution cost and quality for every policy, while an independent numerical comparison tests a stronger ordinary solver. The former is simulated; the latter is executed. The assumptions and all sampled histories are included.

First, resources remain fixed. A separate high-demand experiment allows expansion only after available earned funds exceed the capital and reserve conditions and forecast capacity value exceeds expansion cost. Extra output from purchased capacity is labeled expansion. It is not counted as evidence that retained intelligence compounded.

The admission boundary is an economic inequality

Let Δt\Delta t be the validated time saving per eligible use against the competent recipient; pcp_c the applicable compute price per second; ff additional serving cost; NN eligible uses before retirement; and FF all additional assessment, retention, failed-candidate and Gym costs. With maintenance MM, ΔV=N(pcΔt−f)−F−M.\Delta V=N\bigl(p_c\Delta t-f\bigr)-F-M. \label{eq:break_even} This is a restricted decision model: it values measured runtime only. There is no assigned quality premium, avoided human project or extra demand merely because an artifact exists. If reliable new quality were measured, its customer value would require a separately supported term.

Two necessary conditions follow. First, if pcΔt≤fp_c\Delta t\leq f, more reuse cannot repay a positive fixed investment. Second, if the per-use margin is positive, then N>(F+M)/(pcΔt−f)N>(F+M)/(p_c\Delta t-f) is required. A large number of agents does not change either condition. It helps only insofar as it creates eligible, authorized and valuable uses after coordination, maintenance and congestion.

Limits that determine the next study

The workload is synthetic, low-dimensional in its parameters and restricted to a fixed grid and affine operator family. It lacks human trials, customer purchases, physical validation and measured frontier agent performance. The confirmation is protected procedurally within one AI-assisted research session, rather than administered by an independent laboratory. Timing is local. The enterprise assumes task quality and customer demand. The generic scientific component is outside the frozen production capsule scope.

These limits identify concrete next tests. The first is a customer-relevant scientific-software task with a costly repeated computational bottleneck and independently specified acceptance. The second is a competent frontier generalist that may discover or construct the same ordinary method, with all model and tool costs recorded. The third is an actual authorized service or component transfer with versioned operating permissions and a fresh recipient. Only then should an expanding network be credited with earned economic advantage.
