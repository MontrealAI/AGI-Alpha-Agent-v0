03 / FRESH PERFORMANCE

Does the advantage survive a strong baseline?

All methods solve the same equations and pass the same final accuracy check. Complete scoped time includes assembly, method setup, correction and independent residual verification.

The improved baseline uses the same ordinary solver on familiar inputs and switches to sparse direct solving on the declared shifted regime; timing differences between identical routes reveal measurement variability.
