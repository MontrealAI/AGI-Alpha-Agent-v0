{\large\sffamily\bfseries\color{navy}A learned component is real. Its business value still has to be earned.}

We built and executed a scientific-software relay. Twelve thermal-response solves produced a rank-12 numerical basis. A bounded Gym selected 32 further experiences and produced a rank-44 successor. Frozen artifacts were then tested on 128 fresh in-domain cases, 64 shifted cases, and 32 finite inverse-design missions per arm.

The strongest ordinary solver is essential to the interpretation. The local mean saving against the frozen baseline was only 0.125 milliseconds per fresh transfer solve. An improved ordinary router repeats the same spectral-PCG method in-domain; its paired comparison includes no gain, exposing timing sensitivity. Its substantive routing improvement occurs on shifted cases. A better approximation is not automatically a new reliable offering or a profitable network asset.

\textbf{Provenance.} The research team constructed the algorithms and environments with extensive AI assistance. The numerical basis was learned from executed solutions. This is not autonomous invention, a physical experiment, a frontier-model performance trial or specialist ASI.

\textbf{Implementation boundary.} Existing research lifecycle and native accounting code are reused. The production capsule does not yet support this scientific scope. Synthetic identity, encrypted byte transfer and revocation checks do not constitute live authenticated deployment.

{\Large\sffamily\bfseries\color{navy}Preserve profitable work. Buy learning only when it pays.}\par

The commercial experiment compares five optional policies on identical opportunity streams and starting resources: Beta alone; private retention; private retention with Gym; sharing; and sharing with Gym and composition. It prices software-service scopes against explicit estimated human hours and sourced hourly service rates. Model use, customer demand, trust, quality and collections remain assumptions.

The primary selective policies do not purchase retention or Gym development. Their projected numerical savings do not cover additional assessment, packaging, serving and maintenance. A separate compulsory-retention diagnostic preserves what would happen if that decision were ignored.

\textbf{The break-even condition is simple and unforgiving.} At a \usd{3.52}/hour equipment-price proxy, the measured transfer saving is worth about \usd{0.000000122} per solve. An assumed \usd{0.002} invocation cost already exceeds that benefit before fixed costs. More reuse cannot repair a negative per-use margin. Even at zero serving fee, an illustrative \usd{402} lower bound would require roughly 3.28 billion eligible uses at the same measured saving, excluding screening, initialization, numerical assessment, maintenance and the optional second specialist, with no scale guarantee.

\textbf{What the enterprise result means.} The reference Beta-only policy has modeled mean operating profit of \usd{\ReferenceProfit{}} over 104 weeks. This demonstrates a conditional consequence of the declared cost, quality and demand assumptions. Actual customer revenue in this research is \usd{0}; complete actual research cost is unknown. The adverse scenario is preserved, along with refunds, failed delivery, cash restrictions and obligations.

\textbf{Decision.} Keep the numerical component and its evidence. Do not promote it to an economically validated production capability at this tested scale. The next worthwhile test should target a customer-relevant bottleneck where the transferable artifact changes reliability or a material cost, and compare against a well-operated frontier generalist that can use the same ordinary methods.

\textbf{Delivered.} Executable code, frozen checkpoints, all candidate and fresh-test records, matched enterprise histories, source register, native lifecycle/accounting replay, an evidence companion, LaTeX/PDF and an offline English/French demonstration. Publication remains subject to the repository's existing review and release process.
