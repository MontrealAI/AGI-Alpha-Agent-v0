### Native accounting contract

`Charge` contains `activity_id`, `mission_id`, A/B/C `bucket`, one of nine resource categories, `accrued_micro_usd`, `paid_micro_usd`, `basis`, `evidence_digest` and `note`. Categories are model, tools, compute, storage, network, human, acceptance, correction and overhead. Activity identity is unique across all buckets. Known zero costs need an explicit attestation; unknown cost is `None` with `basis='unknown'` and cannot be silently zeroed.

`CostLedger.apply` verifies a `flywheel-cost-command` signed by an installed accounting principal. It binds the exact journal identity, operation ID, action and record. Native commands distinguish offers/invoices/pending sums from collections and seed capital. Completeness seals are separate for A delivery, B retention/reuse and C additional investment. `report` distinguishes collected revenue, delivery profit, complete-cost profit, cash, known and unknown obligations, reserve and available funds. Tokens are excluded. An incomplete B/C record does not erase independently complete A delivery profit.

The source enforces finite storage and reserves reconciliation headroom; this wrapper does not claim unlimited ledger scalability. Simulation traces may be reconciled through bounded per-world/per-mission groupings only with declared aggregation. `replay_native_costs` reports exactly the command population actually submitted to the native ledger. It does not claim that rows absent from its command list ran there.
