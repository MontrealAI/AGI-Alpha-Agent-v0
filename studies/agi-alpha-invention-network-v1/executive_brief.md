**A learned component is real. Its business value still has to be
earned.**

We built and executed a scientific-software relay. Twelve
thermal-response solves produced a rank-12 numerical basis. A bounded
Gym selected 32 further experiences and produced a rank-44 successor.
Frozen artifacts were then tested on 128 fresh in-domain cases, 64
shifted cases, and 32 finite inverse-design missions per arm.

|                      |                                                                                                                                                         |
|:---------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What changed**     | Fresh recipient raw approximation error fell **26.3-fold**. The numerical artifact changed through executed experience.                                 |
| **What transferred** | Another scripted research role loaded the frozen basis and used it in a broader diffusion problem. Exact correction remained necessary.                 |
| **What failed**      | Neither uncorrected component passed the fresh recipient’s raw acceptance rule on any of 64 cases. Shifted cases made the learned warm start slower.    |
| **What composed**    | Informative sensor placement improved the harder finite reconstruction from 7/16 to 10/16; the equally funded competent generalist also achieved 10/16. |

The strongest ordinary solver is essential to the interpretation. The
local mean saving against the frozen baseline was only 0.125
milliseconds per fresh transfer solve. An improved ordinary router
repeats the same spectral-PCG method in-domain; its paired comparison
includes no gain, exposing timing sensitivity. Its substantive routing
improvement occurs on shifted cases. A better approximation is not
automatically a new reliable offering or a profitable network asset.

**Provenance.** The research team constructed the algorithms and
environments with extensive AI assistance. The numerical basis was
learned from executed solutions. This is not autonomous invention, a
physical experiment, a frontier-model performance trial or specialist
ASI.

**Implementation boundary.** Existing research lifecycle and native
accounting code are reused. The production capsule does not yet support
this scientific scope. Synthetic identity, encrypted byte transfer and
revocation checks do not constitute live authenticated deployment.

**Preserve profitable work. Buy learning only when it pays.**

The commercial experiment compares five optional policies on identical
opportunity streams and starting resources: Beta alone; private
retention; private retention with Gym; sharing; and sharing with Gym and
composition. It prices software-service scopes against explicit
estimated human hours and sourced hourly service rates. Model use,
customer demand, trust, quality and collections remain assumptions.

The primary selective policies do not purchase retention or Gym
development. Their projected numerical savings do not cover additional
assessment, packaging, serving and maintenance. A separate
compulsory-retention diagnostic preserves what would happen if that
decision were ignored.

**The break-even condition is simple and unforgiving.** At a \$3.52/hour
equipment-price proxy, the measured transfer saving is worth about
\$0.000000122 per solve. An assumed \$0.002 invocation cost already
exceeds that benefit before fixed costs. More reuse cannot repair a
negative per-use margin. Even at zero serving fee, an illustrative \$402
lower bound would require roughly 3.28 billion eligible uses at the same
measured saving, excluding screening, initialization, numerical
assessment, maintenance and the optional second specialist, with no
scale guarantee.

**What the enterprise result means.** The reference Beta-only policy has
modeled mean operating profit of \$191,886 over 104 weeks. This
demonstrates a conditional consequence of the declared cost, quality and
demand assumptions. Actual customer revenue in this research is \$0;
complete actual research cost is unknown. The adverse scenario is
preserved, along with refunds, failed delivery, cash restrictions and
obligations.

**Decision.** Keep the numerical component and its evidence. Do not
promote it to an economically validated production capability at this
tested scale. The next worthwhile test should target a customer-relevant
bottleneck where the transferable artifact changes reliability or a
material cost, and compare against a well-operated frontier generalist
that can use the same ordinary methods.

**Delivered.** Executable code, frozen checkpoints, all candidate and
fresh-test records, matched enterprise histories, source register,
native lifecycle/accounting replay, an evidence companion, LaTeX/PDF and
an offline English/French demonstration. Publication remains subject to
the repository’s existing review and release process.
