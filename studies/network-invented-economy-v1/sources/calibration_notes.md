# Beyond Beta calibration memorandum: coordination-mechanism foundry

Evidence retrieved 26 October 2026. This is independent calibration research for **BEYOND BETA: The Network That Invents Its Economy**. It does not import prior study workloads, prices, review assumptions or results. The executable simulator's final protocol must identify which candidate assumptions it adopts. This memorandum defines evidence status and scope; it supplies no economic confirmation conclusion.

## What the evidence supports

The primary venture has a direct research precedent: Liu, Guo and Conitzer's [AMD-LLM paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12203v1) represents auction mechanisms as code, evolves candidate heuristics, and uses manually supplied problem-specific fixes for feasibility and incentive properties. Stronger tested models recovered a familiar theoretical solution within one or two iterations. Consequently, recipients retain general mechanism-design knowledge and ordinary solvers in the no-sharing counterfactual; missing files do not establish missing capability. The paper also reports distribution overfitting. This supports finite-scope invention experiments with independent checking and fresh distributions, not an assumed probability of commercial success.

[SkillsBench v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670v4) reports substantial average gains from curated procedural packages, while [SWE-Skills-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15401) finds that most tested skills do not improve end-to-end pass rates. [SkillLens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23899) finds heterogeneous recipients and negative transfer. These findings justify a mixture of valuable, redundant and harmful transfers. No published percentage is mapped directly to Astra customer acceptance.

The [nine-judge study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29800) reports heavily correlated evaluator errors. Independent sessions and identities therefore must not be modeled as independent correctness evidence. Use executable property checks, counterexample search and distribution shift; retain common-cause failure scenarios. [Co-installed skill conflict evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11647v2) also motivates explicit compatibility and policy checks instead of loading every available skill.

## Beta, Alpha and the commercial reference

**Beta** is competent contemporary frontier assistance with appropriate reasoning, context, tools, standard methods, critique/correction and ordinary accumulated experience. It is versioned and recipient-specific. It includes known mechanism theory, reconstruction ability and already held equivalents. The no-sharing comparator should retain such ordinary learning; a deliberately forgetting arm is only a diagnostic.

**Alpha** means a supported positive complete-cost increment over that versioned Beta and the recipient's current capabilities, for a declared scope and resource constraint. Its evidence may be measured locally, modeled under explicit assumptions, unresolved or nonpositive. The classification must be recorded. Correct code, novelty, graph admission, invocation count and profitability against human-service prices do not independently establish Alpha. A compiled instance of a known method may add value, but only if its complete construction, receiving-agent checking, adaptation and upkeep costs justify the increment.

The human-service reference remains the commercial comparator. Beta is an internal causal control, not a competing AI-market price. The protocol separates **Hbeta** (ordinary modeled service margin), **Halpha** (capability increment above Beta), **Hshare** (network sharing versus isolated retention) and **Hdurability** (persistence as Beta moves). None follows automatically from another. Profitable restraint, no investment and retirement can be appropriate outcomes.

An advance in the baseline makes an earlier Alpha assessment stale and requires reassessment. Correctness, permissions and current economic value remain distinct. The underlying mathematical certificate is not made false merely by better ordinary methods. An asset can become ordinary or duplicative and cease to count as Alpha while its historical evidence remains intact. Assessment records should bind the baseline version, current-capability digest, task scope, cost boundary, evidence and uncertainty.

The already completed exact mechanism witness is local known-method software evidence. Its construction reduction and increased full receiver-checking work are mixed effects in different units; they are not measured dollar savings or an Astra invention success rate. The subsequent economic confirmation needs its own frozen source/configuration and fresh seeds. The full design and venture screen are in `network_invented_economy/evidence/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` and `VENTURE_SCREEN.md`.

The adopted primary simulator billing deliberately charges every fresh input as a 30-minute cache write (Astra USD12.50/M, Terra USD1.25/M), with separately counted cache reads at USD1/.05/M. This is a conservative workload accounting assumption, not an observed cache policy or hit rate. The stage-specific `Work` catalog declares the read quantities. In the no-cache sensitivity, all input including formerly cached quantities is billed at standard USD10/M Astra or USD1/M Terra, with no cache discount. Output remains USD50/M and USD5/M. A fresh write is not also charged as an ordinary input token; read and write quantities remain distinct.

The Build-tier table lists 5,000 requests/minute and 1,000,000 tokens/minute for each model; account eligibility, provider allocation, effective throughput and concurrent jobs remain unverified. Assign a finite network-level quota, not a fresh quota per agent. API usage is billed separately from ChatGPT subscriptions. Do not amortize a Chat subscription as unlimited production API access.

Built-in web search costs USD0.01/call plus retrieved content tokens; hosted-shell/code-interpreter containers list USD0.03, 0.12, 0.48 and 1.92 per 20-minute session for 1, 4, 16 and 64GB, respectively. File-search storage lists USD0.10/GB-day after its free allowance and USD0.0025/tool call. Local compute, storage, network distribution and operational monitoring still need explicit assumptions or measurements. These tool prices do not price all enterprise infrastructure.

## Matched human-service reference

The strongest observed anchors found are US government contract service rates, not wages or project quotations. [RAND's schedule](https://www.gsaadvantage.gov/ref_text/47QRAA24D008Z/110BBS.3WQO6G_47QRAA24D008Z_47QRAA24D008ZCPA19672FNL.PDF) lists economics and quantitative research roles; [Z Federal's schedule](https://www.zfederal.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Z-INC-GSA-MAS-Pricelist-Mod-18.pdf) lists software and management roles. Both give US domestic service conditions. They establish observed hourly service-price anchors, not verified human-only effort or a Canadian market quote. Neither says that its staff avoid AI.

It excludes live operations, legal opinions, deployment and stakeholder negotiation. Assume role-hours `[24,40,24,12]`, `[48,80,48,20]` or `[96,160,96,32]` in the table's order. The resulting derived labor references are USD25,329.00, USD49,857.64 and USD98,114.56. These totals are not observed service prices. The middle case is 196 team-hours, approximately five person-weeks spread across specialties, not five elapsed weeks. Procurement lead time and engineering delivery time are separate assumptions.

The commercial price should be optimized over a declared grid relative to the matched reference. Buyer trust, willingness to pay, substitution to a human provider, integration expense and deferral are unknown; rate cards alone establish none of them. Include a much lower human-reference sensitivity (e.g. half the derived labor estimate) because an expert may solve a bounded familiar mechanism rapidly with established tooling.

For an especially strong challenge to scope equivalence, also test a human-equivalent reference or buyer value at 10–50% of the central estimate and demand near zero. This is a sensitivity recommendation, not an inferred real-world probability. Do not transfer the primary mechanism package's hours or price to the deferred distributed-protocol, interoperability or privacy-protocol alternatives; those need their own matched scopes and evidence.

## Parameters that remain assumptions

`calibration.json` distinguishes documentary prices and limits, external reported findings, arithmetic reference estimates and candidate scenario assumptions. There are no measured Astra/Terra task success, invention success, collection, customer demand or network transfer observations in this evidence screen. Parameters for those quantities must remain explicitly hypothetical, even after a simulator is run.

Key uncertainty bands should cover: no transfer benefit; negative transfer; an ordinary frontier recipient reconstructing the artifact cheaply; overlap approaching one; validator false passes under common-cause faults; insufficient demand; short capability lifetime; leakage or ordinary model progress eliminating reconstruction savings; cash collection after costs; failed construction; costly bundle integration; and no-investment policies. A positive central model is conditional evidence only. Useful skill count must be based on positive marginal effect on fresh work, with duplicated entries and repeated descendant jobs counted separately from independent invention campaigns.

For cost calibration, model actual role calls and aggregate input/output usage, not an undifferentiated expert multiplier. The monetary token formula below makes any assumed workload inspectable:

`cost = sum_model(uncached_M * input_rate + cached_M * cache_rate + write30_M * write30_rate + write12_M * write12_rate + output_M * output_rate) + tools + compute + storage + distribution + maintenance + failed_attempts + support + allocated_setup`.

Use enough context to include the authorized purpose, exact type/constraint specification, current dependencies and reproducible evidence; separate generator and critic contexts while preserving their correlated-error uncertainty. A competent workflow should expose counterexamples and abstention, not guarantee an answer. Economic calibration requires a subsequent prospective pilot measuring complete effort and buyer choices on the same bounded offering.
