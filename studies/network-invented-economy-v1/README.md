# PROFITABLE WORK FIRST. COMPOUNDING CAPABILITY WHEN WORTHWHILE.

**AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL · A modeled commercial core with optional retained Alpha.**

The commercial core delivers demanding, useful work with competent frontier intelligence,
automated intake, validation, correction and recovery, complete costs, and finite cash.
Under the declared scenarios, that **Beta delivery business produces positive operating
surplus**. Retaining capabilities, sharing them, or funding separate research are further
economic decisions. Positive Alpha is not a prerequisite for the modeled delivery business
to earn a margin.

The bounded offering is a **coordination-mechanism foundry**: executable allocation and
procurement protocols, experiments and evidence for complementary inputs under a common
budget. Transformational Intelligence changes representations, dependency handling and
executable intermediate objectives while preserving the authorized purpose. Useful work
may leave reusable learning; the enterprise can retain it when its remaining benefits
justify its additional costs, or continue profitable delivery and hold reserves.

**Evidence status:** this is an executed local research package with conditional modeled
commerce, prepared as a draft under `studies/network-invented-economy-v1/`. No customer
sale, commissioned service, live frontier-role trial, production deployment, ENS
enforcement or on-chain transaction is recorded. Quality, customer acceptance, demand
and task workloads remain assumptions; no empirically qualified service-quality floor
has been established.

## 1. The commercial Beta core

The unchanged experiment evaluates a 104-week operating horizon plus 16 weeks of run-off,
with $60,000 initial seed capital and complete modeled delivery and common costs. The
table reports means across **64 fresh confirmation worlds per scenario**. Prices are
relative to the scope-matched human-service reference, whose observed rates and assumed
team effort are documented in the [calibration register](sources/calibration.json).

| Scenario | Price policy applied to Beta | Modeled collections | Complete accrued costs | Collected operating surplus |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Conservative | Adaptive; mean offer 29.8% of human reference | $677,921 | $266,500 | **$411,421** |
| Central | Fixed 55% of human reference | $4,192,972 | $490,105 | **$3,702,395** |
| Favorable | Fixed 75% of human reference | $7,377,908 | $614,429 | **$6,763,479** |

Surplus excludes seed capital and future receivables. It deducts accrued costs and
unexpensed closing obligations, without charging an already accrued unpaid expense again.
Central Beta closing obligations average $471; the other two means are zero. The modeled
means and Monte Carlo intervals describe these specified worlds, not estimated commercial
returns from a measured customer market. Full distributions and accounting identities
remain in [the primary results](results/summary.json) and retained audit.

A stricter reporting sensitivity counts receipts only from outcomes the simulator marks
correct and on time, while retaining **all** enterprise costs and unexpensed closing
obligations. Its mean surplus is $343,435, $3,522,731 and $6,527,982 respectively. The
conservative minimum is −$30,129, so positive surplus in every conservative world does
not survive this filter. This uses simulated outcome truth; it is not empirical quality
validation. The [commercial-core register](results/commercial_core.json) retains the full
distributions and the receipt-to-outcome reconciliation.

**Policy-selection provenance:** prices were selected using the network policy family in
training, then the same selected policy was applied to Beta and the other confirmation
arms. This is an evaluation of the commercial Beta core under those policies. It is not
an independently optimized Beta pricing result, and no new prices were selected for this
reporting edition.

### Cash available for continued operation and optional allocation

| Scenario | Mean ending available cash | Mean cash above original seed at closing | Mean further-allocation view after the planning buffer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Conservative | $470,952 | $410,952 | **$408,312** |
| Central | $3,761,488 | $3,701,488 | **$3,698,570** |
| Favorable | $6,821,122 | $6,761,122 | **$6,758,482** |

Cash above original seed is the derived view `max(0, available_cash − initial_seed)`.
This is a **closing comparison** that sets aside the original $60,000, not a claim that
the seed was locked during operation. Reported available cash already excludes funded
commitments, warranty reserves and accrued unpaid costs. It is distinct from operating
surplus and may not cover an unreserved portion of future refunds.

The final column applies `max(0, min(profit, available_cash − initial_seed) − operating_floor)`
world by world. The profit cap accounts for unexpensed closing obligations, and the
additional operating floor is $2,640 in each primary Beta scenario. This reporting buffer
is not another expense or an automatic investment allocation. It shows resources that
could remain for continued delivery, additional reserves or a separately justified
capability decision under the declared assumptions. The [commercial-core reporting table](results/commercial_core.json)
separates these quantities and records their derivation from unchanged confirmation data.

All 64 Beta worlds in each primary scenario have positive surplus and no recorded cash
shortfall. Zero shortfalls in 64 worlds still has a 95% Wilson upper endpoint of 5.66%.
False passes remain in the simulation, and profitability under its quality assumptions
does not establish a qualified real offering. The next operational step is a metered,
task-matched Beta engagement and human-service comparison, as specified in the
[operational validation plan](evidence/OPERATIONAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md).

## 2. Retained Alpha is an optional improvement decision

The enterprise may use available resources for more delivery, reserves, incremental
retention, a complementary bundle, or separate R&D. Each capability must justify its
remaining complete costs against competent Beta and the recipient's existing methods.
Waiting, declining acquisition and retaining no capability remain legitimate outcomes.

The shared-capability findings are unchanged:

| Scenario | Beta surplus | Network surplus | Paired network increment, 95% conditional MC interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Conservative | $411,421 | $397,474 | −$13,947 [−$14,104, −$13,790] |

| Favorable | $6,763,479 | $6,754,714 | −$8,766 [−$118,842, $101,310] |

The primary confirmation does not establish a positive enterprise increment from sharing.
Conservative and central selected policies deliver without graph retention; favorable
selection permits retention with hurdle 1.5. No selected primary policy funds separate
R&D. Favorable recipient-use counts describe activity within the simulation and do not
establish additional output caused by sharing.

The separate deterministic recipient panel can have positive remaining retention value.
It is a declared cost surface, not empirical Alpha or an observed reinvestment return.
Actual software checks preserve a mixed mathematical witness: less repeated construction,
more aggregate certificate checking, and no measured net dollar advantage. Negative,
redundant and stale cases remain part of the evidence.

## Paid work, incremental retention, and optional invention

The primary v2 mechanism learns through useful paid work. Its accounting separates three
decisions so that learning is not charged twice:

| Account | What it pays for | Decision boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `A_job`: complete delivery | Intake, reasoning, tools, construction needed by the paid job, validation, correction, recovery and delivery | The full job cost appears once in operating accounts. |
| `B_retention`: incremental sharing | Only additional extraction, sanitization, confirmation for reusable scope, packaging, distribution and maintenance; receiving-job integration remains in A | Retain an already developed asset only when its prospective increment beyond competent Beta justifies these additional costs. |
| `C_R&D`: optional separate invention | A separately chosen search or construction campaign beyond the paid job | Fund only from available cash under the declared investment policy; no required investment count. |

A sanitized failure report can be preserved in separate failure memory without becoming
positive Alpha or an executable skill. This preserves negative learning while keeping the
positive admission rule intact. The graph's generic cost fields bind the supplied
assessment; the ledger and protocol determine which account bears each expense.

## Beta, Alpha, and the network rule

**Beta** includes appropriately configured frontier models, adequate context, useful
tools, source retrieval, decomposition, critique, correction, public methods, accumulated
experience, and the recipient's currently available capabilities. Absence of a particular
file does not establish absence of ability.

**Alpha** is a scoped positive increment over that versioned comparator after complete
attributable costs. An assessment records matched gains, explicit weights where relevant,
planned reuse, uncertainty, acquisition, construction, validation, integration, operating
recovery, risk, maintenance, migration and distribution. The common gate requires a
strictly positive lower net bound. Its uncertainty margin is a declared assumption unless
an experiment separately establishes a statistical interpretation. Novelty, validity,
persuasive labels, cryptographic inclusion and agent agreement do not establish Alpha.

The graph implements:

`candidate → evidence → Alpha assessment → validation → admission → discovery / optional authorized reuse`.

Every admitted shareable capability is discoverable and retrievable by **every currently
authenticated direct-label `*.agent.agi.eth` member**. Access is restricted to this network
at the research API boundary. No preferred recipient subgroup is used. A receiving agent
must still check scope, prerequisites, dependencies, freshness, current Beta, revocation
and the operational permission needed for the intended action.

Complementary components can qualify as a bundle without individually profitable
standalone claims. A changed Beta invalidates older admissions until reassessment and
revalidation; historical evidence remains inspectable. Retired, revoked and superseded
versions cannot regain authority through old history or canonical promotion.

## Reproduce the local components

Run commands from this directory. The recorded Python environment uses **Python 3.12.14**
and **cryptography 50.0.2**. The mechanism witness, simulator and Alpha arithmetic use the
Python standard library; the graph additionally uses `cryptography` for actual local
AES-GCM and Ed25519 operations. No model, wallet or provider credentials are needed.

For a fresh development environment, install the graph dependency using your approved
Python environment, for example:

```sh
python3 -m pip install 'cryptography==50.0.2'
```

This dependency command is setup guidance, not a claim that a clean installation or the
complete upstream release environment was qualified by this study. The unchanged native
accounting modules additionally require Node; their recorded local exercise used
**Node v24.22.1**, separately from the upstream release profile.

### 1. Run the executable checks

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

The test runner reports the current test count. These checks cover mathematical and
protocol counterexamples, lifecycle authority and freshness, Alpha arithmetic, and
specific economic-accounting regressions. A passing test count is not a count of
inventions, customers or independent scientific trials.

### 2. Replay the retained mathematical witness

```sh
python3 src/mechanism_witness.py verify
```

This repeats the deterministic confirmation and checks its retained result identity. The
source/test identities and a recorded wrapper-only replay repair are checked as part of
that path. The evidence and repair receipt remain in `evidence/`; the replay does not
replace the retained result. The CLI also exposes `freeze` and `confirm` for a **separate,
newly specified experiment**, not for overwriting an already confirmed record. See
[mechanism_design.md](evidence/mechanism_design.md) for the frozen scope and seed split.

### 3. Replay graph admission and revocation

```sh
mkdir -p replay
python3 src/skill_graph.py > replay/graph_demo.json
```

The generated JSON contains explicit fixture Alpha assessments, validation and admission,
availability to every fixture member, separation of access from execution, moving-Beta
reassessment, and revocation refusals. It records its simulation and cryptographic
boundaries. The separate replay path preserves [the retained demonstration](evidence/graph_demo.json).

### 4. Run one economic scenario

```sh
python3 -m src.simulator --scenario central --seed 104729 --arm network --out replay/central-network.json
```

`--scenario` accepts `conservative`, `central`, or `favorable`. The simulator emits a
summary and writes the detailed modeled cash ledger, event history, outcomes, retained
capabilities and assessment records to the requested JSON path. Use `--help` to inspect
the actual CLI. A single run is exploratory unless it belongs to an already frozen
protocol and seed schedule; it is not the study's confirmation analysis.

### 5. Economic study harness

Use a fresh output directory to preserve the retained publication evidence:

```sh
python3 -m src.run_study all --out results/replay --workers 4
```

The harness checks that the calibration register and protocol exist, binds the adopted
sources and tests before training, selects policies using the training schedule, then
runs disjoint confirmation and sensitivity schedules. A changed frozen source is refused.
The freeze, training output and confirmed set cannot be silently overwritten. Use a new
directory for an independent replay.

The phases can also be executed sequentially, once each, in the same fresh directory:

```sh
python3 -m src.run_study freeze --out results/replay
python3 -m src.run_study train --out results/replay --workers 4
python3 -m src.run_study confirm --out results/replay --workers 4
python3 -m src.run_study sensitivity --out results/replay --workers 4
```

Choose either `all` or the separate phases for a particular output directory. Outputs
include `protocol_freeze.json`, selected policies, confirmation summaries, complete
confirmation ledgers in `confirmation_ledgers.jsonl.gz`, retained traces under `samples/`,
sensitivity/ablation files and `demo_data.json`. Read their source identities and status
before interpreting a result. Development examples remain separate from confirmation.
Training recovery after a shared-storage interruption preserved the original 1,576-row
prefix and resumed the unchanged frozen functions; see `results/training_interruption.json`.

Full raw evidence, including all 768 compressed confirmation ledgers, is provided as
the separate evidence archive. Its decompressed layout merges into this study root;
see `results/DATA_AVAILABILITY.md`. The source contribution includes the protocols,
summaries and retained failures. You can regenerate the raw evidence with the command
above without live model access, a wallet or an external service.

## Read the results without mixing evidence categories

The mathematical witness is an actual local execution of known methods on synthetic
inputs. It checks a bounded mechanism family and its certificates. It does not show a
frontier model autonomously inventing a new theory or selling the resulting service.

The graph runs real local cryptographic operations with **public fixture keys**. Its
identity directory, roles, validator judgments, clock and economic assessments are
research fixtures. Public fixture keys provide no production secrecy or identity trust.
Authenticated export cannot stop an authorized recipient copying the exported material.

The economic simulator represents heterogeneous ordinary experience, retained assets,
failures, correction, buyer alternatives, pricing, investment, queues, quotas and finite
cash. Its human-service reference, demand and task-quality assumptions must be read with
their calibration provenance. Internal Beta and no-sharing arms answer causal questions;
they are not other AI vendors used as the commercial pricing reference. Read the
[independent review](evidence/simulator_review.md) for the distinction between a direct
paired capability effect and the total effect of an adaptive policy package.

**$AGIALPHA utility remains separate from operating earnings.** The package does not
issue tokens, convert token appreciation into profit, route a payment, or alter existing
settlement. Token issuance, transfers and assumed appreciation contribute **zero** to
modeled operating revenue. Native AGI Jobs/Manager roles and contracts, including
`*.club.agi.eth` responsibilities, remain unchanged.
