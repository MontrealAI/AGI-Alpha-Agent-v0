# Beyond Beta — evidence companion

## How to read this package

This study first tests a commercial opportunity: can competent contemporary
frontier models, tools and ordinary accumulated experience deliver useful bounded
coordination-design work profitably against a matched human-service reference?
This **commercial Beta core is the primary reporting result**. The second
decision is whether the **additional** cost of retaining and sharing learning
already produced during paid jobs improves that enterprise. Separate new research
is another optional decision. A profitable service need not make either additional
investment. The package contains real executable research software and local
tests. Its commercial activity, inventions, customer acceptance and collections
are simulated.

**The complete prespecified experiment is finished:** 2,304 training runs,
768 primary confirmation runs, 2,736 stress-response runs and 240 diagnostic
ablations. Primary ledgers and stress summaries are independently audited.
The commercial Beta service earns positive modeled complete-cost mean profits
in all three primary scenarios: **USD411,420.92 conservative, USD3,702,395.04
central and USD6,763,479.46 favorable**, over 104 selling weeks plus up to 16
runoff weeks. Optional retained Alpha and sharing have **no established positive
enterprise increment** in those primary scenarios. That secondary finding does
not negate the modeled delivery business. A development, training or favorable
local example is not substituted for either confirmation result.

The [27 October reporting amendment](REPORTING_AMENDMENT_20261027.md) records
the user's clarification of this presentation priority. It changes the order and
interpretation of reporting, not the frozen scientific hypotheses, policy search,
source, seeds or economic outcomes. The useful-receipts-only calculation and
additional cash planning buffer below are explicitly postconfirmation descriptive
calculations over retained records. They are not new preregistered endpoints or
fresh economic experiments.

late delivery. A conservative reporting calculation therefore joins each
collection to its completed outcome, retains revenue only for useful on-time
work, and still deducts **every enterprise expense and unexpensed closing

### Cash that remains after the seed and operating planning buffer

Each primary Beta scenario starts with USD60,000, including a charged USD9,500
setup, six producer agents and two validator slots. Available cash deducts
recorded funded commitments, warranty reserves and accrued unpaid expenses.
It is different from total cash and profit. The report first shows cash above
the original seed, `max(0, available_cash − seed)`. That amount does not by
itself impose a forward operating reserve.

The additional reporting buffer is
`max(0, min(profit, available_cash − seed) − operating_floor)`, where
`operating_floor = 6 × (105 + 30 × 2 + 40 × 6 + 35) = USD2,640`.
The minimum with profit prevents unexpensed closing charges from becoming
apparent distributable earnings. This planning buffer is **not another expense**,
an implemented dynamic investment policy, or proof that every future obligation
is predictable.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Conservative | 470,952.16 | 410,952.16 | 408,312.16 | 13,594.70 | 6,382.87 |
| Central | 3,761,488.17 | 3,701,488.17 | 3,698,569.66 | 23,738.42 | 2,634.76 |
| Favorable | 6,821,121.61 | 6,761,121.61 | 6,758,481.61 | 26,438.88 | 2,680.31 |

The observed minimum cash path is not a counterfactual minimum required seed.
The already executed central seed stresses fail in 16/16 Beta worlds at
USD10,000 and in 0/16 at USD25,000 and USD60,000. Those tests constrain the
declared central stress case; they do not establish universal capital sufficiency.
The primary collection delays are seven, four and two weeks in conservative,
central and favorable scenarios. Weekly input/output token quotas are respectively
110/22, 200/40 and 290/58 million, with finite producer and validator capacity.
The capacity stresses later in this companion show that buying more complete
capacity can improve modeled delivery, while finite queues and payment delays
can constrain it. Profitability, liquid funds, capital requirements and optional
capability growth must therefore remain separate measurements.

The commercial result justifies a **conditional service opportunity**, followed
by task-matched quality and buyer validation before operation. Optional retained
Alpha must independently improve that service after its additional complete
costs. The primary comparisons below do not establish such an increment and
provide no reason to force retention or separate R&D into an otherwise
profitable delivery policy.

## 1. The four questions are different

**Beta** is a versioned specification of competent frontier reasoning, appropriate
context and tools, source grounding, critique and correction, established methods
and ordinary accumulated experience. The recipient keeps what it already knows
and any equivalent available capability. A missing skill file does not mean an
agent lacks the ability to solve the task.

**Alpha** is a supported positive complete-cost increment over that Beta and the
recipient's current capabilities, within an explicit scope. A known method's
compiled instance can add value; a novel, correct or graph-admitted asset can
add none. Alpha qualifications bind the baseline version, current capability
snapshot, evidence, costs, intended reuse and uncertainty. A moving Beta requires
fresh assessment. Historical evidence is preserved, but cannot authorize future
use under an obsolete assessment.

| Question | Evidence required | What does not answer it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hbeta, primary commercial reporting result:** can ordinary delivery earn a modeled margin against a matched human service? | Collected operating surplus after complete costs and obligations under the declared buyer model | An impressive prototype, a token price or an offered price with no collection |
| **Halpha:** does a candidate or bundle improve on competent Beta? | Matched recipient/task comparisons and positive increment after additional retention, transfer, checking, upkeep and any separate new R&D | Novelty, a missing file, an admission label or ordinary service profitability |
| **Hshare:** does network sharing improve on isolated retention? | Paired enterprise outcomes with the same initial resources and ordinary capabilities, charging sharing overhead | More members, invocations or graph entries |
| **Hdurability:** does the increment persist as ordinary capability improves? | Reassessment under declared overlap, copying, obsolescence and moving-Beta conditions | Restricted access by itself |

The 27 October reporting priority makes Hbeta the commercial lead without
retrospectively changing the frozen hypotheses or tests. The human service
remains the commercial reference. Beta and isolated retention
are internal causal controls; they are not competing AI suppliers used to set
the market price. Profitable restraint, no invention, deferral and retirement
are legitimate outcomes.

Pro aggregates underlying work in its billed usage. It is not a fixed cost tier

underlying input and output, including reasoning, unsuccessful attempts, critique
and correction. Actual role token quantities are unmeasured.

The frozen primary billing assumption conservatively prices every fresh input
at the 30-minute write rate, with separately enumerated reads. The no-cache
sensitivity instead prices all input, including quantities previously allocated
to cache reads, at ordinary uncached rates. No input is charged simultaneously
as both a fresh write and an ordinary input. These cache quantities are assumed
workload structure, not observed hit rates.

Both model pages list a 1,050,000-token context and 128,000 maximum output. The
documented surcharge above 272,000 input tokens applies to the whole request:
input/cache rates double and output rates increase by 50%. The frozen study plans
requests at no more than 128,000 input and 32,000 output tokens, so campaign totals
span many decomposed requests. The simulator meters aggregates; it does not
execute or independently validate that request decomposition.

Effective weekly network quotas are scenario constraints shared by all agents.
They are not provider entitlements or separate quotas for each agent. Minute-level
provider scheduling is not emulated. API bills are separate from subscriptions,
and no subscription is treated as unlimited production API credit.

The register also records provider search, container and storage rates. The
economic implementation uses explicit stage tool/compute allowances and fixed
infrastructure allocations rather than an observed tool-call bill. Those
allowances cannot be mistaken for measured complete production costs.

## 5. What the mathematical witness actually establishes

[mechanism_design.md](mechanism_design.md) specifies a known-method experiment
on independent finite supplier-cost distributions. One required component belongs
to each supplier; utility is quasilinear; the buyer values only a complete bundle;
reports are bounded integers; the budget and buyer value are public. Collusion,
identity creation, arbitrary dependence, multidimensional types and continuous
extrapolation are outside its guarantee.

The implementation compiles distributions, constructs an exact budget frontier,
checks attainability and Bellman inequalities, and enumerates finite unilateral
deviations and constraints. Its retained
[confirmation record](mechanism_witness.json) reports:

- 48 of 48 small-domain exact-oracle matches and finite protocol passes.
- 22,992 finite type profiles and 440,256 unilateral deviation comparisons.
- Five valid large certificates; the largest declared vector space has
  129,746,337,890,625 vectors, while the corresponding dynamic program records
  14,040 transitions.
- Preserved counterexamples: independent threshold offers and ordinary efficient
  VCG procurement can violate the hard budget even while their incentive
  properties are not the violated condition.

This establishes exactness within the specified mechanism family and assumptions.
It does not establish universal mechanism optimality, an autonomous AI invention
campaign or a previously unknown economic theorem.

## 7. Complete costs, finite cash and unknown research expenditure

[workload_catalog.json](../sources/workload_catalog.json) records assumed token,
tool and latency quantities. The design charges production, validation,
correction, intake, packaging, recovery, failed jobs, additional retention,
optional new capability construction, confirmation, migration, transfer,
maintenance and revalidation. Queues, producer slots, validator capacity and
network token quotas constrain delivery. The three books separate the source of
learning from the later decision to preserve and reuse it:

| Book | What belongs in it | Treatment in a prospective retention decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — paid-job delivery** | Intake, demanding task work, on-job learning and experimentation, reconstruction, task-specific validation, correction, packaging and failure/recovery associated with the commissioned job; later receiving-agent integration is part of that later job's delivery | The original modeled expense remains in enterprise profitability. A source-job direct accrued-cost memo preserves provenance; it is not collected cash and is not charged again to B or prospective Alpha. |
| **B — additional retention and sharing** | Extraction, sanitization, generalization, fresh reusable-skill validation, packaging, access/distribution, maintenance and revalidation beyond ordinary job delivery | Charge the increment once. The Alpha comparison also counts changed receiving-job costs already billed in A. Fixed network overhead is paid once at enterprise level; the prospective skill gate charges marginal per-skill distribution. |
| **C — optional separate R&D** | A genuinely additional invention/search/build/confirmation/migration campaign not already performed in A, including unsuccessful attempts | Charge when undertaken, independently of B. The primary retention panel has C equal to zero; its explicit new-R&D comparison adds C and does not disguise it as an inevitable job-learning cost. |

The original job may be profitable without retention, and retention may add value
even when an unrelated new R&D proposal would not repay its cost. The reverse is
also possible. A favorable incremental B decision does not prove that the full
historical A+B enterprise was profitable. The source job's modeled offered price,
acceptance and collection are different events. An admission labeled as learned
in paid work can originate in a priced job that later defaults; the separate
count of useful source jobs with actual **modeled collections** is stronger
evidence of the modeled paid-work loop.

Retention workloads use separately declared `RETENTION` packets and
`retention_cost_factor`; they do not reuse the larger `CONSTRUCTION` packet
as an automatic charge for work already paid in A. Central retention success
is an explicit 0.90 assumption, with 0.70 conservative and 0.97 favorable, and
with adverse sensitivity cases. These are generalization/packaging outcomes,
not a benchmark-derived frontier ability rate or an intelligence multiplier.

The primary operator accounting allocation includes USD105/week for shared
hosting/orchestration, automated operations and intake support; USD30 per
validator; USD40 per producer; and network distribution/access of USD70 plus USD4 per member.
The standard modeled network has 24 members. Local/isolated retention costs
USD35/week instead. These fixed quantities are assumptions, not invoices.
Active runoff and maintenance are charged; an empty graph does not make the
network's access infrastructure free.

The capacity menu also charges USD500 setup for each producer or validator added
above the baseline. It does not create free extra capacity.

The USD60,000 seed and USD9,500 bootstrap setup are modeled quantities. Setup
remains a historical modeled expense. **The actual bill for producing this
research package is unknown.** The assistant's reasoning and tool use are not
free simply because there were no deliberate role-execution API trials, and
simulated receipts do not pay or recover that unmetered actual bill.

Ledger distinctions matter:

- Offered price is not a collection; receivables are neither spending cash nor
  terminal profit.
- Incurred expense and paid expense can differ; unpaid obligations reduce
  available funds. A reserve is not itself another expense.
- Commitments require available cash. Projected future collections cannot fund
  present retention or construction.
- `investment_cost` summarizes authorized campaign budgets, including bounties
  that may be released. Accrued and paid expenditure must be read from the
  expense ledger, rather than treating the budget as cash already spent.
- Profit subtracts accrued costs and unexpensed closing commitments/refunds.
  An unpaid expense already accrued must not be subtracted twice.
- Historical source-job work and separately incurred R&D remain in enterprise
  profitability even when a later continuation decision properly treats them as
  sunk. Recording either as a provenance memo does not create a new expense.

Creator bounties are payments to independent contributors under the defined
operator boundary. Transfers among agents owned by the same consolidated
enterprise would require a different treatment. $AGIALPHA utility, issuance,
transfer or assumed appreciation contributes zero operating earnings. No terminal
valuation for graph entries is used to manufacture wealth.

The primary provider workflow is fully automated in the model. It contains no
routine human review or manual onboarding hidden in low costs. Automated
clarification, checks, correction and recovery have costs; unsupported cases
defer, abstain or leave the scope. This modeling choice does not establish that
an actual professional service can currently meet the same responsibilities
without human intervention.

## 8. Economic design and what its intervals mean

[FINAL_RUN_DESIGN.md](FINAL_RUN_DESIGN.md) supplies exact choices that complete
the longer [RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md). The controlling v2
manifest binds the following design:

| Phase | Version 2 design | Planned run count |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Policy training | 24 worlds, seeds 760001–760024; 32 policies; three scenarios; network arm | 2,304 |
| Primary confirmation | 64 fresh worlds, seeds 770001–770064; four arms; three scenarios | 768 |
| Sensitivity | 57 cases; 16 fresh worlds, seeds 780001–780016; Beta and network with retention/optional-R&D responses | 2,736 |
| Additional ablations | Five arms; first 16 confirmation seeds; three scenarios | 240 |

These are run counts, not 6,048 independent worlds. The same exogenous world is
intentionally paired across arms and parameter conditions. Jobs descended from
one artifact are not independent invention campaigns. The current campaign
summary counts source jobs among admissions; it must not be read as every
independent attempted campaign.

The policy family has five fixed human-reference ratios (.35, .55, .75, 1 and
1.15), or three offset versions of a bounded adaptive price response. Each is
combined with delivery alone, retention at a hurdle of 1, retention at a hurdle
of 1.5, or retention at a hurdle of 1 plus optional new R&D at a hurdle of 1.35.
Training maximizes mean collected operating surplus subject to observed training
shortfall frequency at most .05. Within USD1, no separate R&D is preferred, then
no retention, then the lexical policy name. The empirical screen is not a
certified risk bound.

Selection uses the network training arm. The selected scenario policy is then
applied to every primary arm. This supports controlled comparisons at that fixed
policy; it does not give each arm its separately optimized profit frontier.
Adaptive pricing uses specified population priors and observable state, not a
buyer's realized private value. Results are bounded policy-family choices against
fixed alternatives, not a solved universal repeated-market equilibrium.

Main selection may choose delivery alone. The stress set therefore forces
retention on and compares optional separate R&D off/on under the selected price;
these conditional responses cannot be mislabeled as the main selected policy.
`network_no_invest` disables C, while still allowing B under the selected
retention rule. If the selected policy already disables C, that arm can coincide
with the network arm; this is a legitimate finding, not another independent test.

A seven-setting initial capacity menu selects a mean-profit winner on stress
worlds 780001–780008, separately for Beta and network, and tests it on
780009–780016 against matched central capacity. It holds the central selected
pricing and retention response without C. The menu includes central capacity,
one/four validators, quarter/double quota, double producers and complete doubled
capacity. This reuses existing stress runs. It tests a one-time initial choice,
not globally optimal dynamic expansion, and eight test worlds provide limited
precision.

Mean and paired-difference intervals use a normal 95% Monte Carlo approximation;
binary frequencies use Wilson intervals. They express finite-world sampling
uncertainty **conditional on the model**, not empirical confidence about real
demand, scope equivalence, frontier quality or willingness to pay. The fifty-seven
sensitivity conditions are assumption stresses; they are not a sample from a
measured probability distribution over actual markets. Correlated or multiple
comparisons must not be advertised as many independent discoveries.

## 9. Recipient cost panel and its relation to enterprise outcomes

[recipient_ablation_design.md](recipient_ablation_design.md) describes the separate
deterministic cost panel in `src/recipient_ablation.py`. It freezes the job,
ordinary experience, held equivalents, resources and current baseline, then
removes only the candidate. Failed and harmful adaptation pay complete attempted
work, reconstruction and recovery. Optional nonuse remains possible.

Its five recipient profiles and 25 declared sensitivity rows produce conditional
mission-cost differences and required reuse/lifetime thresholds. Upfront
affordability is checked separately from projected future savings. Receiver
costs already included in the paired difference are not added again to lifecycle
costs. Shared bundle overhead can make a bundle worthwhile even where individual
components do not repay it; no free synergy bonus is added.

The default capsule route leaves 22% of the corresponding reconstruction
`Work` packet as residual work, then adds integration and checking. That fraction,
the token packets and the recipient-mixture weights are explicit workload
assumptions. They are not inferred from the witness's operation counts or from
an Astra task trial. The witness's known dynamic-programming method is available
to competent Beta; comparing reuse only with exhaustive enumeration would
manufacture an advantage and is not the recipient control used here.

The v2 primary panel uses B's additional retention packets, not a repeated charge
for the source job's invention. It exposes `ledgers.A_job`,
`ledgers.B_additional_retention` and `ledgers.C_optional_new_rd` separately. A is
an optional provenance memo; when no actual simulated source job is linked to
the frozen panel, its cost and collections remain unspecified. Altering a supplied
A memo cannot change prospective Alpha, break-even reuse or affordability. C is
zero in the primary panel and separately positive in the labeled new-R&D
comparison, which includes an explicit hypothetical failed construction attempt.

Failed retention receives no future capability benefit, loses its upfront B
cost and creates no admitted artifact upkeep. Successful retention pays upkeep,
revalidation and moving-Beta reassessment. Their explicitly assumed probability
mixture is an expected economic projection, not a measured success frequency.
`project_net` and `prospective_net` in this panel both refer to the incremental
B/C decision; neither is a claim about full historical enterprise profit.
The panel's success probability applies to the whole declared campaign. The
enterprise separately screens each bundle component and adds defects and false
rejections. Consequently, a panel break-even threshold is not interchangeable
with enterprise expected return or an estimate of how often such a state occurs.

This panel assumes functional fallback, revalidation and paid reassessment succeed
when their modeled resources suffice. Enterprise false passes, retention and
construction failure, queues, collection timing, copying and investment responses are analyzed
by the stochastic simulator. The panel cannot replace that ledger or establish
the frequency of a favorable recipient state. It was development-smoke-tested;
postfreeze execution is deterministic replay, not fresh stochastic confirmation.

Its retained [cost-panel result](../results/recipient_ablation.json) gives a
concrete conditional comparison. With 75% competent reconstructors and 25%
already-equivalent recipients, two uses per week for 40 weeks, and the declared
moving-Beta path, incremental retention requires USD849.84 upfront and USD9,495.70
in expected full lifecycle costs. After receiving costs, its expected delivery
saving is USD434.26 per use and its 80-use incremental net is **USD25,245.09**.
It requires **22 uses** to cover the declared 40-week lifecycle at the path's
mean per-use saving. The cumulative expected balance first becomes positive at
week 2, under continued two-use weekly demand; this is modeled cost repayment,
not observed customer cash repayment.

The separate new-R&D comparison adds USD23,234.20 in C, including one declared
hypothetical failed construction attempt. It requires 97 uses at the same
declared lifetime and returns **−USD5,295.69** at 80 uses, with no positive
lifetime found within that comparison's range. Neither comparison supplies an
unknown A source-job amount. These results explain why low additional retention
and separate invention must be evaluated separately, while remaining a deliberately
specified local opportunity rather than the enterprise experiment's conclusion.

### Sensitivity falsifiers, demand and finite cash

All **57 prespecified cases and 2,736 response runs** are retained in
[sensitivity_runs.jsonl](../results/sensitivity_runs.jsonl). The independent
[sensitivity audit](../results/sensitivity_audit.json), implemented in
[audit_sensitivity.py](../analysis/audit_sensitivity.py), reproduces every
published stress summary and the capacity split: **8,220 checks, zero failures**.
Each stress uses 16 paired worlds, the selected central price and explicitly
enabled retention. It is a conditional alternative to central delivery-only
selection. These are **pointwise 95% Monte Carlo intervals without multiplicity
adjustment** across the 57 cases. They are descriptive; shared seeds do not make
the cases 57 independent market
discoveries. No market parameter is re-estimated from these synthetic results.

Several prespecified conditions decisively limit the network thesis:

| Stress, with retention enabled | Network minus Beta surplus | 95% Monte Carlo interval | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Human-service reference at 10% | −24,721.28 | [−38,251.28, −11,191.28] | The ordinary service can remain profitable while added retention loses value. |
| Token prices three times the primary rate | −281,521.86 | [−451,195.06, −111,848.67] | More expensive production and capability work can overwhelm incremental value. |
| Faster ordinary-Beta improvement | −152,226.20 | [−288,873.40, −15,579.00] | No durable retained advantage is established under this moving baseline. |
| Faster copying/method diffusion | −150,820.89 | [−292,520.12, −9,121.67] | Restricted access does not prevent economic erosion in the modeled diffusion path. |
| Collections delayed to 12 weeks | −307,981.48 | [−483,388.71, −132,574.26] | Collection timing and finite cash matter even when nominal demand remains. |

At 0.05 leads per week, mean Beta surplus is **−USD11,194.24** and retained-network
surplus **−USD13,763.11**; both intervals are below zero. At 0.25 leads per week,
the corresponding means are USD182,645.36 and USD174,544.84, with positive lower
bounds. The tested mean-profit crossing is therefore bracketed between **0.05
and 0.25 weekly leads** under these particular other assumptions. No precise
market break-even rate or interpolated demand curve is established. The joint
commercial downside—10% human reference, 0.5 weekly leads, threefold token
prices, lower trust and delayed collections—also loses money: −USD16,661.09
for Beta and −USD18,757.09 for the retained network.

Initial cash and cost shocks expose risks absent from the primary shortfall
counts. With USD10,000 seed, every world in both arms has a shortfall and no
retention campaign occurs. The network loses USD10,071.00 rather than Beta's
USD10,404.65. This **USD333.65 smaller failure loss is the only stress whose
retention-minus-Beta profit interval is wholly positive**; it is not a capability
gain or a viable enterprise. With USD25,000 seed, network shortfall occurs in
one of 16 worlds. Tenfold token prices produce six shortfalls in 16 network
worlds and five in 16 Beta worlds despite positive mean surplus, showing why
mean profitability cannot replace cash-risk reporting.

Cheap B alone does not establish the flywheel. At one-tenth additional retention
effort, the network-minus-Beta mean is −USD40,317.50 with an interval spanning
zero. Extending useful lifetime to 104 weeks likewise leaves the increment
unresolved. Static Beta also produces a negative increment in its stress, so
the failures cannot all be attributed to frontier improvement. Admission costs,
ordinary experience, maintenance, demand mix and queue responses still matter.
Increasing leads from eight to 16 per week barely changes the retained network's
mean surplus, from USD4.249 million to USD4.252 million, under unchanged capacity.

### Separate R&D and the meaning of enabling an option

Enabling the optional-C decision can incur evaluation costs and change queue,
candidate and routing histories even if it never approves a construction
campaign. In the central-reference stress, its mean C cost is USD817.59 while
the actual construction-campaign count is zero. Two C-enabled policy contrasts
have positive profit intervals—certain ordinary learning and four validators—
but **neither actually builds a separate R&D capability**. They cannot evidence
an invention-driven reinvestment loop.

Only the one-tenth construction-cost stress and its focused joint variant
approve separate R&D campaigns: two and one respectively across 16 worlds.
These are **three campaign events across alternative conditions, not three
independent replications**. The first case has events at seeds 780006 and
780010; the focused variant also uses seed 780010. That repeated seed must not
be counted as fresh independent evidence.
Their optional-C profit intervals both include zero. The frozen legacy label
`cheap-extraction-focused` changes **new construction cost and diversity**;
it must not be misread as a new low-B-retention experiment. These outcomes
support optional evaluation and restraint, not a claim that separately funded
invention improved subsequent enterprise performance.

### Capacity improves the service without proving sharing

Both Beta and the network choose the complete-doubled initial capacity from the
seven-setting menu using the first eight stress worlds. On the fresh eight-world
half, its paired gain over its own central capacity is:

| Arm | Mean gain from selected capacity | 95% Monte Carlo interval |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Beta | USD1,331,416.37 | [685,518.53, 1,977,314.20] |
| Retained network | USD1,448,477.28 | [890,347.72, 2,006,606.83] |

The [capacity record](../results/capacity_policy.json) and independent replay
charge added setup, recurring capacity and usage. This is evidence of a
resource bottleneck within the model, and a positive result for the declared
initial-capacity decision. It is not the isolated effect of sharing. On that
same fresh half, the scaled network-minus-scaled-Beta mean is USD132,375.90,
with a wide interval [−408,781.92, 673,533.72]. It does not establish a retained
network advantage, unlimited scalability or an optimal dynamic expansion plan.

### Diagnostic component ablations and their limits

The [240 additional runs](../results/ablation_runs.json) preserve all five
diagnostic interventions. In favorable conditions, the first 16 matched worlds
give network-minus-removed-representation surplus of USD1,591,249.86
[1,215,509.29, 1,966,990.43] and network-minus-removed-falsifier surplus of
USD607,214.75 [354,032.79, 860,396.71]. Those large differences must be interpreted
with their actual implementation: the eligibility mask prevents use of a component,
while proposed-option appraisal can still value it. The policy can repeatedly
attempt retention that cannot be used, and funding, routing and queues diverge.
These are **total intervention effects including futile retention behavior**,
not clean marginal skill benefits and not evidence overturning the primary
network-versus-Beta result. No postfreeze source repair conceals this limitation.

The duplicate arm declares redundancy and tests the rejection gate; it does not
independently estimate how often real recipients possess equivalents. Deliberate
forgetting removes ordinary learning and is not competent Beta. Its positive
contrast cannot establish the network's advantage over the stronger baseline.
The separate frozen-recipient panel is the controlled cost-removal comparison;
its conditional assumptions and limited external validity remain explicit.

### Final hypothesis disposition

**Hbeta is the primary commercial result:** ordinary delivery earns a positive
complete-cost margin within the primary model under the assumed human reference
and buyer conditions. Mean surplus also remains positive after removing every
collection linked to a non-useful delivery while preserving all costs; one
conservative world becomes negative under that reporting filter. These modeled
results justify investigating the delivery service without requiring retained
Alpha or separate new R&D. **Halpha** and **Hshare** do not receive a positive enterprise
confirmation: central/conservative network overhead is negative, while favorable
retention/sharing effects are unresolved around zero. The separate deterministic
recipient panel can identify conditional cost-saving states, but cannot replace
this enterprise finding. **Hdurability is not established**: fast ordinary
improvement and diffusion have negative conditional retained-network increments
over Beta, and longer life or cheaper retention does not produce a robust positive enterprise
result. The study supports a testable local retention mechanism and a modeled
ordinary-service opportunity, with profitable restraint and resource allocation
often more important than additional graph growth. It does not establish an
economically compounding commercial network or a separately funded invention loop.

## 12. What remains unestablished

The package does not establish current Astra/Terra acceptance rates on matched
customer work, a real buyer-demand curve, verified human-only project effort,
operational Alpha, an authenticated production transfer, live ENS enforcement,
production secrecy, a deployed contract, realized collections or self-acceleration.
Agreement among agents, signatures and Merkle inclusion do not close those gaps.

A prospective pilot would need task-matched frontier execution with metered
usage, complete delivered artifacts and independent task-specific checks,
observed buyer choices, matched human quotations or effort evidence, and the
existing owners' separately authorized operational commissioning. The current
research neither requires those activities to report its modeled findings nor
grants permission to deploy, purchase, transact or change security settings.
