\begin{abstract}
Can demanding paid work leave reusable capabilities whose remaining costs are
small enough to create additional productive value? We study an automated
coordination-mechanism foundry, separating competent frontier-model assistance
(Beta) from scoped net mission advantage due to retained capabilities (Alpha).
An executable simulator maintains three cost books: complete customer-job delivery,
additional retention and reuse, and optional separate research. Historical job
development is never charged a second time to prospective retention. A tested
graph requires a current Alpha assessment before admission and makes eligible
capabilities available to every authenticated network member while keeping action
permissions separate. An exact local mechanism-design witness matches an
exhaustive oracle in 48 fresh small cases and verifies five larger certificates;
its transfer result is mixed, with fewer construction transitions and more
certificate inequalities checked across the creator and recipients. The economic
protocol evaluates 32 policy candidates and 64 fresh confirmation worlds per
scenario and arm. Central Beta produces USD3.70 million modeled surplus over
the horizon, but no primary scenario establishes a positive network increment.
Favorable retention yields a paired mean of USD$-8,766$, with a conditional
95\% Monte Carlo interval spanning loss and gain. No separate research policy
is selected. Official token prices and human
service schedules anchor costs, while workloads, acceptance, transfer and buyer
behavior remain explicit assumptions. No customer sale, live frontier task trial,
ENS enforcement, production admission or financial settlement is demonstrated.
The contribution is a reproducible mechanism, a complete accounting boundary,
and falsifiable conditions for useful accumulation rather than an assumed
self-accelerating economy.
\end{abstract}

\section{The research question: what remains after useful work?}

A customer buys a useful outcome, not a graph node. Completing a difficult job may
require a better representation, a new experiment, an executable protocol, or a
composition of methods. If that work leaves a reusable capability, the next
question is whether retaining it is worthwhile after the additional work needed
to make it safe, applicable and useful to a recipient. This question is different
from asking whether an independently funded research project repays its entire
construction cost. Both are legitimate decisions; they need distinct ledgers.

Our primary question is: \emph{Under what declared conditions does customer-funded
transformational work leave net Alpha that improves subsequent accepted work,
and when does network availability add value beyond isolated retention?} A
second question asks whether competent Beta delivery has attractive economics
against a matched human-service reference. The answer to the second question
cannot establish the first.

We interpret Transformational Intelligence as changing the problem-solving
setup while preserving the authorized purpose. Boucher's supplied manuscript
distinguishes purpose, formulation and evaluation, and requires an executable
construction plus a falsifier \citep{transformational}. The supplied Gym,
Specialist ASI, Navigator and Successor manuscripts reinforce complete mission
architecture, protected evaluation, retained failure and the separation of
historical evidence from present authority \citep{gym,navigator,successor}.
Their illustrative workloads, prices, performance scores and institutional
arrangements are not adopted as calibration.

The study begins from a bounded portfolio screen. It does not treat all useful
inventions as commercially demanded, or all difficult jobs as sources of reusable
knowledge. Retention may be rejected, deferred, superseded or retired. Separate
research and expansion are optional. Waiting is a legitimate outcome.

\section{A demanding paid-job portfolio}

The primary venture is a \textbf{coordination-mechanism foundry}. A consortium,
procurement designer or resource-sharing organization commissions an executable
allocation protocol under a written budget, incentive and delivery contract.
The supported outcome is an offline R\&D package: typed inputs, executable
candidate mechanisms, counterexamples, finite-model checks, integration tests,
documented assumptions and a deployable-design handoff. It is not an operational
market, a legal opinion or a guarantee about unconstrained strategic behavior.

The recurring trigger is a change in participants, resource constraints,
admissible bids, uncertainty or coordination objectives. A beneficiary may pay
because a feasible, checkable design reduces trial-and-error and makes a decision
possible within its development budget. That willingness to pay is modeled,
not observed. A small demonstrator is not assigned the price of a complete
professional engagement.

Three increasing modeled scopes share this venture. The first specifies and
implements one bounded protocol family. The second adds a reusable evaluator
and integration package over changed conditions. The third composes protocols,
dependencies and adversarial tests into a broader design. Alternative ventures
screened in the companion are distributed recovery protocols, compositional
interoperability and privacy-constrained collaboration. They have executable
technical precedents \citep{shardstore,compositional-synthesis,spdz}; their greater
deployment, environment or security obligations make them contrasting cases,
not additional successes claimed here.

Mechanism design is supported by task-relevant prior research. AMD-LLM combines
mechanism-code search with problem-specific repair and reports recovery of known
theory as well as richer designs \citep{amd-llm}. Program synthesis supplies
another executable precedent \citep{program-synthesis}. These studies motivate
the venture and its tests. They do not supply Astra success probabilities,
customer demand or the study's token workloads.

\section{Beta, Alpha and three nonoverlapping cost books}

Let $B_{a,t}$ denote agent $a$'s competent frontier configuration at time $t$,
including ordinary tools, context, experience and available equivalent methods.
Let $K_{a,t}$ be its additional eligible retained capabilities. Beta is not
defined by the absence of a skill file. It can reconstruct methods, use ordinary
dynamic programming, retain experience and possess a functional equivalent.

A candidate $k$ has scoped prospective Alpha when the same mission and resources
produce a positive net increment relative to $B_{a,t}+K_{a,t}$ after all remaining
incremental costs. For a planned reuse count $R$, paired per-mission gross gain
$g_i$, declared nonnegative uncertainty allowance $u$ and remaining cost $C$, the
research gate computes
\begin{equation}
N=R\bar g-C,\qquad N_{\rm lower}=R(\bar g-u)-C.
\label{eq:alpha}
\end{equation}
Admission requires $N_{\rm lower}>0$ and a nonredundant, scoped comparison, followed
by technical validation and the separate admission decision. Explicit weights
represent recipient and task mixtures. The allowance is a declared modeling
margin, not a statistical confidence interval. A numeric gate does not establish
that its inputs are true.

Integration performed as part of a receiving paid job is charged in A. The
counterfactual Alpha calculation includes its incremental effect on that job's
cost; it does not issue another ledger expense in B. Fixed graph service overhead
is charged once to the enterprise's retention book. A marginal admission decision
does not require every new skill to repay the already operating service again.
The full network comparison still has to recover that overhead.

Historical source-job cost is a non-additive memo in each retention record.
Changing that memo from zero to a million dollars leaves the prospective Alpha
decision unchanged; the job's actual ledger remains unchanged and fully charged.
The model distinguishes a priced, delivered source job from a useful source job
whose customer payment was collected. Defaults cannot be turned into funding.

Complementary components can qualify jointly. The bundle bears its complete
incremental costs and pins the components required for the benefit. Components
that lack standalone advantage remain candidate-only dependencies, rather than
being relabeled as independently positive skills. Failed approaches belong in
separate failure memory and never participate as positive graph entries.

\section{An actual executable transformational witness}

Consider buying a complementary bundle of inputs from suppliers with independent
private integer costs. The useful purpose is to obtain the whole bundle within a
hard payment budget. Choosing individually attractive procurement decisions can
violate the shared budget. Truthful incentive labels alone do not repair it.

The executable representation uses posted thresholds $t_i$. Acquire the complete
bundle and pay $t_i$ to each supplier only when every reported cost satisfies its
threshold. Otherwise acquire nothing and pay nothing. With public value $V$,
budget $B$ and known independent cumulative distributions $F_i$, optimize within
this declared family:
\begin{equation}
\max_{\mathbf t:\,\sum_i t_i\le B}
\left(V-\sum_i t_i\right)\prod_i F_i(t_i),
\label{eq:mechanism}
\end{equation}
including the no-purchase option. These are mathematical utility units, not
commercial USD. The finite model excludes collusion, externalities, strategic
distribution reports and continuous unbounded type spaces.

The transformation replaces independent threshold selection with a
budget-indexed dynamic-program frontier and a typed all-or-none protocol.
The construction must preserve budget, allocation and payment semantics, and
the certifier must check individual rationality and finite unilateral deviations.
A Bellman certificate supplies checkable upper-bound conditions. An exhaustive
small-domain oracle is independently informative about the optimizer; a signed
claim about it is not a substitute.

The strongest cold baseline already knows dynamic programming and the relevant
theory. No claim that frontier Beta lacks this algorithm is made. The potentially
retained asset is a compiled frontier for an applicable input family, together
with its protocol and evidence. A recipient with the same artifact gets no
additional Alpha from another copy.
This known-method prototype tests a change in representation; it is neither a
novel mechanism theorem nor autonomous frontier invention.

\section{Automated enterprise and finite-cash simulation}

The primary horizon is 104 weeks plus 16 weeks of run-off, with six producer
agents, two validator slots and USD60,000 seed. An assumed USD9,500 bootstrap
cost is expensed initially. All workflow costs are automated in the primary
configuration. No routine human provider review or manual onboarding is hidden
in the simulation. Governance over real deployment and authority remains outside
routine job execution.

Agents begin with heterogeneous ordinary experience and specialization. Every
competent arm can reconstruct a missing method, retain ordinary experience and
benefit from outside diffusion. A successful observable delivery can yield a
rights-cleared candidate with a declared probability; it can also leave no
reusable learning. A promising but unbuilt direction may enter optional C. A
later job-learned artifact can supersede that direction without being trapped
behind its old research requirement. Full source history remains.

Candidate extraction, generalization and validation consume B resources and can
fail. Admission compares matched recipient-by-depth cost probes with the
cheapest competent reconstruction or currently eligible retained equivalent.
The proposed bundle is optional. These deterministic response surfaces are
model assumptions, not frontier reasoning over actual jobs. Fresh simulated
jobs exercise the admitted asset's modeled consequences; they do not empirically
validate the assumed response surface.

Production has specification errors, implementation errors, component failures
and shared weekly faults. Validation combines assumed executable-error detection
with semantic detection; common faults weaken the latter. False passes, false
rejections, one correction attempt, correction failure, abstention, lateness,
defaults and later refund/recovery are charged. Hidden correctness is used by the
simulated world and the analyst, not as an immediate public trust signal.
Trust responds to observed delivery and subsequent refunds.

Transfer includes integration, failed adaptation, fallback reconstruction,
harmful adaptation and current-status checks. Failed transfer does not retain
the skill's quality or validation shortcut. Redundant or overly expensive
capabilities can be declined. Each work tick checks the applicable skill and
bundle dependencies, so stale or revoked history cannot authorize continued
selection. Revalidation has cost, capacity demand and its own errors.

\subsection{Cash, profit and capacity are different quantities}

Prices are offers, not receipts. Orders reserve worst-case permitted delivery
and correction costs before work starts. Collections arrive after the modeled
delay and can default. Warranty funds and commitments reduce available cash.
No future receipt funds current retention or research. The ledger uses integer
cents and never permits negative cash; unpaid obligations remain visible and
reduce spendable funds.

Let $R$ be collected revenue, $A,B,C,F$ accrued cost books, $K$ closing unspent
commitments and $Q$ future refund obligations not yet expensed. The reported
\emph{collected operating surplus} is
\begin{equation}
\Pi=R-(A+B+C+F)-K-Q.
\end{equation}
Unpaid expenses already in the books are not deducted again. Receivables are
reported but excluded from this conservative terminal measure. Seed is not
earnings. These are constant-price, pretax operating scenarios, not audited
financial statements. The actual historical research-session bill is unknown;
it is not reported as zero or recovered through simulated collections.

Five subweekly work ticks share finite token budgets, producer availability and
validator capacity. Retention and optional construction occupy those same
resources. Maintenance and evaluation consume quota and staff-slot time.
Adding agents alone does not add validators or quota. The scale menu charges
additional worker setup and recurring support. Minute-level API scheduling is
not emulated; a paced production broker and verified account quota would still
be needed.

\begin{equation}
U_{\rm ours}=v q\tau-p/H-\iota-\eta L+\epsilon,

profit-maximizing grid response to the modeled buyer distribution and current
capacity. Thirty-two policy candidates combine fixed or adaptive pricing with
delivery-only, optional retention at two hurdles, or retention plus separate
research. Selection maximizes training mean surplus subject to the declared
sample cash-shortfall constraint. It is optimal only within this finite family,
not a universal market equilibrium or globally optimal dynamic strategy.

Retention and research decisions use remaining value, construction/validation
uncertainty, reserves, available cash and estimated displaced delivery margin.
The enterprise can continue delivery, wait, retain a bundle or abandon a weak
candidate. No mandatory investment count or reinvestment percentage exists.
A separate capacity menu uses one set of simulated worlds to choose and another
to test an initial capacity configuration. This bounded expansion comparison
does not claim optimal hiring or expansion timing over every possible future.

\section{Graph lifecycle, availability and authority}

The research API implements candidate, evidence, Alpha assessment, validation,
admission, discovery and optional authorized reuse. Revalidation repeats the
relevant current checks. Retired, revoked and superseded versions are terminal.
Canonical promotion requires current admission and cannot bypass it. Manifest,
evidence, policy, Beta configuration and dependency identities are bound to the
records. The full current capability-set comparator is checked before validation
and admission. Reuse checks the bound assessment, current Beta, policy, evidence,
scope and dependency state. A later unrelated admission does not automatically
recompute every earlier comparison: detecting new overlap and requesting an
updated assessment remains a controller responsibility.

Every eligible shareable capability is discoverable and retrievable by all
authenticated direct \texttt{*.agent.agi.eth} fixture members. A member may inspect
and export the capsule, including the inputs needed for a composition. Creating
a graph record and executing an action require their separate permissions.
There is no preferred recipient subgroup. Candidate-only bundle components are
retrievable through the admitted bundle, not falsely advertised as independent
Alpha. Receiver checks include scope, dependencies, freshness and revocation.

Identity, record writing, eligible validation, admission and execution are
separate authorities. Nested names and ENS-looking strings do not acquire
direct-label authority. The fixture requires eligible distinct validators and
prevents a producer or attestor from admitting its own record even when that
principal holds multiple roles. Existing AGI Jobs \texttt{*.club.agi.eth} roles,
governance, contracts and settlement are preserved.

Local AES-GCM, Ed25519 and SHA-256 operations execute, but their keys are publicly
derivable research fixtures. Consequently the package provides no production
confidentiality or identity enforcement. It has no live ENS, on-chain registry
or zero-knowledge deployment. Inclusion and signatures bind bytes; they cannot
prove useful capability, truthful measurements or economic Alpha. Authentication
cannot prevent an authorized exporter from copying material.

The implementation is an in-memory Python object with a trusted fixture
directory and harness clock. It checks public-API operations, not arbitrary
access to the Python process. A reuse permit is rechecked against current state
under a lock and consumed once; its receipt explicitly records
\texttt{code\_executed=false}. No capsule program or external action executes
through that API. The documented compact Python-JSON serializer and SHA-256
tree are not an Ethereum, EIP-712 or production protocol compatibility claim.

Separate failure memory preserves sanitized unsuccessful approaches, their
context and evidence. It has a distinct namespace and commitment root, with no
admission, canonical-selection or reuse-permit path. Declarations reject
protected evaluation cases and scorer internals from shared capsules and memory;
the research API does not claim automatic semantic sanitization. Confidential
customer information and restricted third-party material are outside this study.

New model, tool, routing, verifier, data or environment configurations require
versioned Beta and affected requalification. An external controller must detect
and submit that change; the graph does not observe a provider runtime or infer
changed competence automatically. Old assessments remain inspectable
without restoring influence. Admission, acceptance, operating authority and
capital allocation remain distinct decisions. The supplied GoalOS drafts are
design references, not evidence that their dashboards or protocols were already
implemented \citep{merkle,proofloops}.

The evidence-only adapter \texttt{integration/modeled\_admission.py} connects the
deterministic recipient assessment to this graph. It preserves the numeric Alpha
decision while binding its declared Beta path, recipient equivalents, exact
scope, evidence and component identities. The predetermined retention and
optional new-R\&D cases run in separate fixture graphs; positive inputs still
require validation and distinct admission, while rejection remains in failure
memory. All-member retrieval and separate execution permissions are exercised.
The capsules contain public modeled-work descriptors, and their evidence checks

\section{Protocol, counterfactuals and confirmation}

The initial separate-build protocol was frozen and partially trained before the
user clarified that paid-job learning must be primary. Its 18-file source freeze
and 909 completed training records remain archived. No economic confirmation
was run under that protocol. Version 2 is a disclosed design amendment with new
training, confirmation and sensitivity seed ranges. It does not relabel earlier
results as fresh confirmation.

The final protocol and code are hashed before policy selection and confirmation.
Training suffered a storage interruption after 1,576 complete ordered records.
The exact prefix and incomplete tail were preserved; an infrastructure-only
wrapper resumed the remaining tasks through the unchanged frozen functions.
The recovery receipt binds the source, prefix, wrapper and final training hashes.
No seed, model, selection rule or confirmation result was changed or reused.
The three scenarios are conservative, central and favorable joint assumptions.
Twenty-four training worlds per scenario choose among the 32 policies; 64 fresh
worlds per scenario and arm then evaluate the selected policy. Primary arms are
competent Beta, isolated retention, network sharing and network sharing without
separate R\&D. All begin with the same seed capital, agents, quotas, experience
and coupled exogenous market draws. Differences in real modeled sharing or
retention overhead remain charged.

The unit of uncertainty is the independent world. Jobs descended from an artifact
are not independent invention campaigns. Mean and paired-difference intervals
describe Monte Carlo error conditional on the model; Wilson intervals describe
simulated frequencies. They do not quantify uncertainty about real acceptance,
service effort or market demand. Fifty-seven predeclared parameter cases test
retention cost and success, demand, prices, copying, ordinary learning, moving
Beta, transfer, obsolescence, capacity and cash.

Adaptive enterprise comparisons include downstream changes in pricing, queues,
investment and learning. A separate frozen-recipient experiment removes only
the candidate, retaining competent reconstruction and existing equivalents under
the same resource envelope. It charges failed and harmful transfer, adaptation,
recovery, checking and lifecycle upkeep, with separate historical job memos.
This deterministic panel was smoke-tested in development; its replay is an
arithmetic reproducibility check, not an unseen stochastic trial.

\section{Limits and interpretation}

This study provides calibrated billing and service-rate anchors, not a fully
empirically calibrated venture. The largest uncertainties concern matched scope,
actual frontier task performance, willingness to pay, repeatability and transfer.
The high-cost human references and modest assumed machine workloads can produce
large modeled margins; their ratio is an assumption-sensitive result, not a
measured productivity multiple. The sparse finite venture family cannot establish
economy-wide demand or unrestricted invention.

Exact local correctness is conditional on the modeled types and distributions.
The witness's increased aggregate checking counts are retained. Graph arithmetic and
cryptographic integrity do not make an assumed response surface true. Model
improvement can absorb a former advantage; copying can remove rarity; continual
maintenance can consume the remaining value. Access restriction is a policy
boundary, not a demonstrated economic moat.

The simulator uses bounded policy families and supplied population priors.
Capacity expansion is tested as an initial menu, not solved as a full dynamic
control problem. Tax, external financing contracts and jurisdiction-specific
professional liabilities are outside the supported operating scope. The finite
refund model is not a universal liability estimate. Historical actual research
cost is unknown. All of these limits remain visible rather than being filled
with token appreciation or hypothetical sales.

The English/French demonstration has deterministic JavaScript interaction tests
and independently cross-checked small solvers. Browser rendering could not be
qualified because the available runtime lacked Chromium and automatic approval
review rejected installing it under the resource restrictions. The study does
not claim a rendered-browser test. The PDF is separately rendered and inspected.
