# BEYOND BETA: THE NETWORK THAT INVENTS ITS ECONOMY

## Protocol status and scope

**Protocol v2, eligible for freezing by the machine-readable manifest.**
Evidence date: 26 October 2026. `results/protocol_freeze.json` binds
this document, the adopted calibration register, source identities, policy grid,
arm mapping and independent confirmation seeds before confirmation starts.
The executable constants in `src/run_study.py` and `FINAL_RUN_DESIGN.md` resolve the final
run choices; the manifest supplies the timestamp and hashes. Existing development runs and the already executed
mechanism witness retain their original identities and status. Version 1's source,
freeze and 909 completed training rows are preserved under
`evidence/development/protocol-v1`; no v1 economic confirmation was run. Version 2
implements the clarified primary premise: useful capabilities arise during paid
job delivery, and the prospective decision charges only additional retention and
sharing work, with separate new R&D optional. Fresh v2 seeds prevent the partial
v1 training stream from serving as final confirmation.

The research question is whether demanding paid useful work can generate retained,
validated capabilities that create a **positive complete-cost increment beyond
competent contemporary frontier assistance**, and whether making those capabilities
available throughout an authenticated agent network increases that increment.
A second question is when profitable delivery, retention, separate invention,
waiting or retirement
is the economically appropriate response as ordinary capabilities improve.

The commercial reference is a matched human-delivered service. The internal Beta
counterfactual is a causal control for the network claim; it is not an alternative
AI vendor used to set the market price. Profitable ordinary AI-assisted delivery
does not, by itself, establish Alpha, sharing value, compounding or defensibility.

This study combines an executable simulator, a locally tested graph lifecycle,
a known-method mechanism witness, official billing facts, published primary
research and openly hypothetical market parameters. There are no deliberate
frontier-role API trials, customer jobs, receipts, ENS transactions or deployed
settlement contracts in the study. The assistant's research process itself uses
compute; its actual bill is not measured by the simulator.

## 1. Beta, Alpha and the moving reference

**Beta** is an explicit, versioned operational baseline. It includes appropriately
configured frontier models; adequate context; source retrieval; ordinary code,
solvers and formal checks; decomposition, critique and correction; and each
recipient's ordinary accumulated experience and already available capabilities.
It includes public mechanism theory and the ability to reconstruct known
algorithms. A missing filename is not evidence of a missing ability.

This strong reference is motivated by the task-matched
[AMD-LLM study](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12203v1), which generates executable
mechanisms with problem-specific correctness repairs and can recover familiar
theory. Its reported successes do not provide Astra acceptance probabilities.
The model must therefore distinguish inexpensive ordinary reconstruction from
costly reuse of a stored artifact.

For recipient i at time t, let B(i,t,v) denote the ordinary capabilities and
experience under baseline version v, and K(i,t) its eligible retained assets.
An assessed candidate or bundle A is **Alpha-qualified within a declared scope**
only when a documented comparison against B and the recipient's current K
supports positive incremental value after complete attributable costs. The
comparison records the purpose, task family, resource constraints, baseline
version, current-capability digest, evidence, method, horizon and uncertainty.
Alpha is a relational qualification, not a claim of new mathematics or universal
superiority. A familiar method can have a useful compiled instance; an impressive
new method can have no economic value.

Qualification must distinguish:

- **Measured local advantage:** obtained from actual controlled executions within
  the stated domain and units. Operation counts are not automatically dollars.
- **Modeled positive increment:** supported within explicit economic and error
  assumptions, with the relevant sensitivity results attached.
- **Unresolved or nonpositive increment:** no Alpha claim for that comparison;
  retain the evidence rather than relabeling it successful.

A new baseline version can include better ordinary models, public methods,
recipient learning or copied techniques. It makes the previous Alpha assessment
stale until reassessed. This does not logically falsify a mathematical certificate,
erase historical evidence, or mean the bytes ceased to exist. Current correctness,
access rights and current economic increment are separate properties. A duplicate
may remain useful ordinary infrastructure, but must not count as fresh Alpha.

## 2. Bounded primary venture and transformational mechanism

The primary venture is a **coordination-mechanism foundry** for public or synthetic,
non-safety-critical resource-pooling and procurement designs. Its bounded offering
is a requirements contract, executable allocation/payment protocol, scope-specific
checks and counterexamples, a reproducible evidence package, and an integration
guide. It is not a legal opinion, deployed market, managed procurement operation
or guarantee about unmodeled strategic behavior. Alternative opportunities and
selection reasons are in [VENTURE_SCREEN.md](VENTURE_SCREEN.md).

The witness studies all-or-none procurement of complementary inputs under a hard
budget. Useful transformations change the representation, dependency structure
or executable intermediate objective rather than merely drafting a business plan.

| Candidate capability | Binding restriction | Transformation and requirement | Decisive falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typed distribution compiler | Raw inputs hide support, independence and scope assumptions | Compile declared finite distributions into reusable cumulative masses, binding their domain identity | Changed distributions, unsupported dependence or mismatched types are accepted as equivalent |
| Budget-aware frontier composer | Independent component choices can exceed a common cap; full vector enumeration grows rapidly | Construct an exact prefix frontier for the declared mechanism family and record attainability and upper-bound certificates | A small-domain optimum disagrees with the exhaustive oracle, or a Bellman certificate fails |
| Deviation/constraint certifier | A truthful label or agent agreement supplies no task-specific guarantee | Check declared finite profiles and unilateral deviations, individual rationality, complete acquisition and the hard budget | Any admissible profitable deviation, budget breach or malformed allocation escapes rejection |

These components can create joint value. The study does not require each component
to be profitable in isolation. The claim still fails if their complete bundle
cost exceeds the competent Beta solution or if the reusable input family rarely
recurs. A different representation is not economically valuable merely because
it is different.

The ordinary baseline already knows posted-price theory, dynamic programming and
appropriate solvers. The potentially transferable asset is an input-specific,
validated compiled frontier and its executable evidence, or a genuinely useful
method absent from the recipient's current basis. The supplied purpose and
constraints remain fixed when representations and intermediate objectives change.

## 3. Hypotheses and estimands

The economic hypotheses are evaluated separately. A failure of one must not be
hidden by success on another. All dollar results below are conditional simulator
results until supported by a prospective commercial pilot.

| Hypothesis | Primary estimand | Interpretation and falsifying outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hbeta — ordinary commercial margin** | Complete-cost operating surplus of competent Beta delivery under a selected pricing policy, with the matched human service in the buyer choice set | Positive surplus supports a modeled ordinary service opportunity. Nonpositive surplus or unaffordable obligations rejects that scenario; positive margin does not establish Alpha. |
| **Halpha — capability increment above Beta** | Paired incremental complete-cost surplus or a predeclared cost/quality/feasibility gain from a candidate/bundle over the same recipient's versioned Beta and current capabilities | A nonpositive effect, or one that disappears after reconstruction and integration costs, does not qualify as Alpha. Isolated creator retention is also compared with the ordinary Beta arm where implemented. |
| **Hshare — network availability increment** | Paired enterprise surplus and useful on-time outcomes for network sharing versus isolated retention, with equal initial resources and ordinary learning | Improvement must survive distribution, access, adaptation, upkeep and validator congestion costs. More entries or attempted transfers alone do not support Hshare. |
| **Hdurability — moving Beta** | Evolution of Halpha and Hshare effects under predeclared ordinary-capability improvement, overlap, copying and obsolescence paths | If Beta catches up before repayment, the relevant investment fails this condition. Timely retirement or no investment may be the optimal response. |

For a paired horizon, incremental enterprise surplus is

    DeltaPi = (collections_A - accrued_complete_costs_A - unexpensed_closing_liabilities_A)
            - (collections_B - accrued_complete_costs_B - unexpensed_closing_liabilities_B).

Unexpensed closing liabilities include commitments or refunds that are owed but
not yet included in accrued costs. An unpaid expense already accrued is not
subtracted again. Seed capital, future receivables,
token issuance and assumed appreciation are excluded from operating earnings.
Cash, accrued profit, obligations and reserves are reported separately.

### Three accounting books and the retention estimand

**A — paid-job delivery** records the original intake, production, on-job learning,
experimentation, reconstruction, task-specific checking, correction and packaging.
Learning is part of that job's complete delivery cost. Integration and checking
performed by a receiving agent while delivering a later job also remain that
later job's delivery expenses. A commissioned/priced job does not imply its
invoice has been collected. Source-job provenance records direct **accrued cost**,
not collected cash or verified profit; fields retaining a historical `paid_cost`
name must be interpreted from this definition and the transaction ledger.

**B — additional retention** records only work beyond delivery already in A:
extraction, sanitization, generalization, fresh reusable-skill validation,
packaging, admission and incremental distribution/maintenance/revalidation.
The source-job learning cost is preserved as a non-additive memo and is never
charged again in a prospective Alpha assessment. Fixed network access/storage is
paid once at enterprise level; the prospective per-skill gate charges marginal
per-skill distribution. A paired future-delivery comparison includes incremental
receiving integration and failure costs, but does not create a second ledger bill
for integration already charged in A.

**C — optional separate R&D** records additional search, construction, failed
attempts, confirmation and migration genuinely not already done during paid work.
It can be funded, deferred or rejected independently of B. The primary retention
path uses the distinct `RETENTION` workloads; `CONSTRUCTION` belongs to this
separate optional path. No automatic development charge is reintroduced merely
because the retained asset is valuable.

The full enterprise report includes A+B+C, shared fixed costs and obligations
exactly once. The prospective retention estimand compares expected future
delivery changes against additional B and any explicitly authorized C; historical
A is sunk for this decision. Positive prospective Alpha therefore does not, by
itself, establish profitable historical source delivery, positive enterprise
profit or an operating-surplus-funded reinvestment loop. Report useful source
jobs with modeled collections separately from all admissions originating in
commissioned work. Lawful rights to retain and share a sanitized capability are
explicit scope assumptions, not consequences of having completed the job.

A local task comparison records useful on-time completion, material error,
abstention, complete delivery cost, resource use and latency. A claim of newly
feasible work additionally requires a predeclared threshold: the same authorized
job crosses a budget, deadline or accepted-quality constraint with the capability
and fails that constraint under competent Beta. It does not mean that the
underlying mathematics was previously unknown. If no such crossing occurs, report
cost avoidance or reliability improvement instead.

## 4. Controlled experimental arms

The final manifest must map implementation names onto these scientific arms:

| Scientific arm | Ordinary intelligence and learning | Experimental retained assets | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B: competent Beta | Full current baseline, tools, experience and ordinary methods | Existing ordinary assets remain; no extra experimental Alpha capsule is granted | Ordinary commercial opportunity and local Alpha reference |
| I: isolated retention | Same baseline and ordinary learning | Creators retain eligible experimental assets; other agents reconstruct unless they already have equivalent capability | Main no-sharing causal control |
| S: authenticated network sharing | Same baseline and ordinary learning | Every eligible shareable admitted asset is discoverable to all authenticated direct agent members | Primary sharing treatment |
| S0: network, no separate R&D | Same baseline and ordinary learning | Paid-job learning may still be retained and shared under the selected B policy; C is disabled | Increment of optional separate R&D over job-funded learning |
| D: duplicate/cheap reconstruction | Same baseline; higher overlap or inexpensive reconstruction | The same access infrastructure is available, but marginal capability may vanish | Negative control for entry-count compounding |

A deliberately no-retention arm may be reported as a diagnostic. It must not
replace I as the main claim about competent recipients. Per-component removal,
high-transfer-cost and moving-Beta interventions are additional ablations. If a
policy-response arm continues investing in a removed component, its outcome
includes that policy response and must be labeled accordingly. It is distinct
from the frozen-state method-removal comparison below.

Enterprise comparisons use equal seed capital, agent count, model access,
compute ceilings and exogenous market worlds. Sharing's real overhead is charged
only where incurred. Equal resource ceilings do not require equal spending: cost
avoidance is an outcome. Separate capacity-factor interventions test more compute
or more agents without granting the shared asset.

## 5. Frozen-state removal experiment

This small, paired experiment isolates a capability from altered pricing,
investment timing and different job histories. It can operate on modeled states;
its modeled status must remain explicit.

1. **Select without looking at treatment outcomes.** Use the frozen rule to select
   recipient/job states, including duplicates, unsuccessful adaptations and
   recipients with strong prior experience. Do not select only successful uses.
2. **Serialize the state.** Bind job purpose and inputs, recipient identity and
   ordinary experience, Beta version, current-capability digest, current policy,
   graph status, price, deadline, available cash, producer/validator slots and
   remaining shared quota. Keep a content hash.
3. **Clone once.** One branch receives candidate/bundle A; the other removes only
   A. Both retain other skills, equivalent methods already possessed, tools,
   ordinary theory and a competent reconstruction path. No information about the
   realized treatment outcome enters either branch.
4. **Hold external conditions fixed.** Use common exogenous demand/error draws
   and the same resource ceiling. Do not reprice, add compute, grant a new agent
   or perform a new investment between cloning and the paired outcome. A supplied
   response surface is a modeling assumption, not an executed frontier trial.
5. **Execute the full permitted route.** The shared branch pays for discovery,
   applicability, freshness, revocation, import/control, adaptation, checking and
   failure recovery. The Beta branch pays for reconstruction and its own evidence.
   Both can abstain, correct or use existing equivalents. Optional nonuse is allowed.
6. **Report direct and lifecycle views.** The prospective receiving-agent effect
   excludes sunk historical source-job costs. The retained-capability lifecycle
   adds B's incremental extraction, failed retention, confirmation, distribution,
   upkeep and retirement costs, plus C only in an explicitly separate new-R&D
   comparison. A stays visible as a non-additive memo, or unknown when no source
   job is linked. Receiver costs already in the paired difference are not added
   again. Never use future successful reuse counts to make an ex-ante choice
   retrospectively affordable.
7. **Compare jointly.** Record DeltaPi where economic quantities are modeled,
   paired useful outcomes, latency and each resource unit. Attribute a useful
   bundle jointly; report component removals without forcing additive attribution.
8. **Repeat under a new Beta version.** Reassess the same scope under ordinary
   improvement. A stale earlier assessment is not reusable proof of current Alpha.

If the full finite-cash simulation changes subsequent policy and queue states,
that is a separate enterprise-level total effect. The local comparison and total
effect answer different causal questions and should both be reported when
implemented. Any unimplemented comparison is marked NOT RUN.

## 6. Exact witness: antecedent evidence with a mixed outcome

The witness was frozen and locally confirmed before this Beyond Beta economic
protocol. It is an antecedent software experiment, not an unseen economic
confirmation set. Its exact protocol, source hashes and results are retained in
[mechanism_witness.json](mechanism_witness.json); mathematical scope is in
[mechanism_design.md](mechanism_design.md).

The retained record reports 48 of 48 small exact-oracle matches, 48 finite protocol
passes and five large certificate passes. Negative controls preserve the fact
that separately truthful offers and ordinary efficient VCG procurement can
violate the declared hard budget. The result is exact within the specified
posted-price family and finite assumptions, not universal mechanism optimality.

The reuse result is deliberately mixed. Across twelve fresh budget/value queries,
the shared and equally capable cold branches return the same solutions. Shared
construction uses 1,716 transitions versus 13,926 cold transitions, but full
recipient mathematical revalidation raises certificate inequality checks from
13,926 to 22,308 and witness multiplications from 7,022 to 12,103, with 74,496
payload bytes additionally hashed. These distinct operations cannot be summed
as equally priced work or converted into dollar savings without measurement.
The record's trusted-attestation alternative is a separate scenario, not executed
ENS authorization or demonstrated cheap validation. Duplicate construction benefit
is zero. A positive complete-cost Alpha qualification is therefore not inferred
from the lower constructor count alone.

The witness shows how an executable setup transformation and decisive falsifier
can be specified. It does not show Astra reasoning over customer jobs or producing
a novel scientific discovery. Its success rate is not an input to economic
acceptance, demand or invention probability.

## 7. Source calibration and competent frontier use

Each adopted parameter must carry one of these labels: official documentary fact;
published service rate; external reported result; actual local measurement;
derived estimate with assumed effort; or explicit unmeasured scenario assumption.
The source-linked register remains authoritative about evidence status. Numeric
precision in code does not upgrade an assumption into a measurement.

Substantive invention, difficult correction, validation, capability design and
investment evaluation use the proposed `gpt-6-astra`, Pro mode, maximum reasoning
configuration. Qualified visual/computer execution uses `gpt-6.1-terra` at medium
or high effort. These are assumed role configurations, not executed trials.
The official [model guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model),
[Astra page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra),
[Terra page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-terra),
[reasoning guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning) and
[pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) were checked on the
evidence date. No immutable dated snapshot was exposed on the inspected pages;
alias plus retrieval date is recorded rather than a fabricated version.

Standard USD per-million input/cached-input/output rates are Astra 10/1/50 and
Terra 1/.05/5. Output includes billed reasoning. Pro usage aggregates underlying
work; no fixed expert multiplier is added. Workloads represent multiple requests,
with adequate specification, dependency and evidence context. The protocol must
either bound each request below the documented 272,000 input threshold and
128,000 output limit or explicitly charge the long-context surcharge. Both pages
list a 1,050,000-token context. Aggregate campaign tokens can exceed a context.

Cached reads are separate from uncached input and optional explicit cache writes.
Any assumed cache-hit rate needs a declared prior-context reuse route and a
no-cache sensitivity. Current weekly effective quotas are study assumptions, not
verified account entitlements. API charges, subscriptions, local compute and
provider tool fees are accounted separately.

[SkillsBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670v4) supports the possibility of useful
curated procedural packages. [SWE-Skills-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15401v1)
and [SkillLens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23899v1) motivate duplicates, overhead
and heterogeneous or negative transfer. The
[correlated-judge study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29800v1) motivates shared error
causes; [skill-conflict research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11647v2) motivates
compatibility checks. None supplies a direct numerical commercial calibration.

## 9. Pricing, optional investment and finite resources

Before optimization, the final manifest must declare a sustainable-value objective,
candidate policy family, cash-shortfall constraint, tie-break rule and reporting
metrics. A tractable default is to select the policy with the highest training
mean complete-cost surplus among policies satisfying the declared shortfall
bound, then report confirmation surplus, cash, quality and capability effects
separately. A risk penalty can be used instead only if its exact coefficient and
meaning are frozen before selection. Do not choose the objective after seeing
which policy wins.

Price ratios relative to the human reference are decision variables. No fixed
discount or cost-plus rule is presumed. The adaptive policy may use stated
buyer-response models and permitted observations. If it knows true scenario
parameters that a real operator would need to estimate, disclose that information
advantage. The result is optimal only within the evaluated grid or candidate
family. It is not a global dynamic optimum or a universal repeated-game equilibrium.

Choices include continued delivery, reserves, immediate or deferred retention,
separate new R&D, complementary bundles, abandonment and no investment. The forecast
must arise from avoided reconstruction/rework, quality changes, supported
throughput or demonstrated threshold crossings. Unexplained synergy bonuses do
not count as benefits. Charge additional unsuccessful retention, confirmation,
transfer, maintenance and the capacity displaced by that work. Charge search,
construction and migration when separate C is actually undertaken. Keep
historical A and C in enterprise profitability while excluding sunk costs from
the prospective choice to spend again. No minimum retention or investment count
is a success criterion.

Cash must exist before discretionary spending. Offered prices are not receipts;
future collections cannot finance today's retention or construction. The ledger separately
records seed, actual modeled collections, paid costs, unpaid obligations,
commitments, contingency reserves and available funds. All events are simulated.
Attributable fixed infrastructure, storage, access, recovery, failure, packaging,
revalidation and runoff costs remain visible. Reserves are not themselves expenses.
The consolidation boundary distinguishes external contributor compensation from
internal transfers. $AGIALPHA utility and existing native settlement rules remain
separate from USD operating profit.

The frozen 32-policy family combines eight price choices with delivery alone,
retention at hurdle 1 without C, retention at hurdle 1.5 without C, or retention
at hurdle 1 with optional C at hurdle 1.35. Select by mean collected operating
surplus among policies with training shortfall frequency at most .05. Within
USD1, prefer no C, then no retention, then the lexical policy name. The main
selection may legitimately retain nothing. Stress comparisons force retention
on and test C on/off so that an ordinary-delivery winner does not leave the
conditional retention hypothesis unexamined.

Finite producers, validator capacity, network-wide token quotas, work latency,
queues and common-cause faults constrain output. Subweekly work ticks may reduce
time discretization error but cannot create more weekly quota or workers. The
automated configuration has no routine human provider review or manual onboarding;
it charges automated intake, clarification, checks, correction, packaging and
recovery. Unsupported cases defer, abstain or leave the offering. A professional
intervention is separately scoped and cannot be hidden in this configuration.

## 10. Graph lifecycle, availability and Alpha admission evidence

The research lifecycle is Candidate → evidence → Alpha assessment → validation → admission →
discovery → authorized reuse → revalidation, supersession or revocation. Canonical
promotion requires the admission path. Validation attestations are distinct from
display labels and bind exact capability bytes, scope, policy and evidence.
An Alpha claim also binds the baseline and current-capability reference described
in Section 1. Its expiry or baseline change requires reassessment.

Every validated, valuable, reusable shareable capability must be available to all
authenticated direct `*.agent.agi.eth` members. Availability means discoverable
and usable under documented prerequisites, version and current status; use is
optional. A receiving agent checks applicability, dependency versions, freshness
and revocation at execution. Failed or unsuitable transfer is not suppressed.
Different permissions to execute a consequential action do not create a preferred
subgroup entitled to see an otherwise network-shareable skill.

Private capsules and public commitments can support restricted distribution.
Record separately who can inspect, execute, export and compose. Authentication
does not prevent an authorized recipient from copying exported contents.
Creator identity, record writing, validator eligibility, admission and execution
permissions remain distinct; nested names do not inherit direct-label authority
by string suffix. Existing AGI Jobs `*.club.agi.eth` roles, governance and settlement
are not changed. Neither encryption, Merkle inclusion nor identity proves useful
capability or true evidence. Simulated authentication and any mock cryptography
are labeled accordingly.

Required negative tests include unauthorized access, direct-label rejection,
admission bypass, stale evidence, revoked/retired selection, unsupported dependency,
missing or nonpositive Alpha assessment, and baseline-version mismatch. Capability
sanitization excludes private customer information and restricted third-party
material. The public/synthetic study uses neither.

## 11. Robustness and moving-Beta design

Conservative, central and favorable scenarios are declared assumptions, not
confidence intervals. Parameter sweeps or joint stresses examine:

- Demand down to near zero; low trust; human-equivalent scope/value materially
  below the central derived reference; different buyer price sensitivity.
- Production/tool costs, failed delivery, correction failure, default and collection
  delay; long-context/no-cache billing where applicable.
- Invention success, misleading admission, false rejection, correlated semantic
  faults and executable-check coverage.
- Additional retention effort, retention generalization success, useful-learning
  yield from jobs, and strong ordinary learning in every competent arm.
- Recipient specialization, ordinary experience, overlap, transfer failure,
  harmful integration, job diversity, reuse and composition depth.
- Short useful lifetime, stale evidence, revalidation load, copying and ordinary
  methods that make reconstruction progressively cheaper.
- More agents or compute with the same capability set, validator congestion,
  network size and distribution/maintenance overhead.

The moving-Beta path is frozen as a versioned sequence or explicit parameterized
process. It is applied to all arms. The network cannot keep comparing itself to
an obsolete weak baseline while recipients and external methods improve.
Report break-even demand, maximum affordable investment, required useful lifetime,
repayment time, shortfall probability and the binding capacity where identifiable.
If a crossing falls outside the tested range, report the bound rather than an
invented precise threshold. Access restriction is not evidence of a moat.

An initial capacity menu uses seven prespecified settings: central, one or four
validators, one-quarter token quota, doubled token quota, doubled producers, and
complete doubled capacity. For Beta and the network separately, choose the
largest mean surplus on the first eight sensitivity worlds, using the fixed
central selected price and retention response without optional C; lexical case
name resolves an exact tie. Evaluate the selected capacity on the last eight
worlds against matched central capacity. Weekly cost is USD105 plus USD30 per
validator plus USD40 per producer plus the declared retention store/access cost.
Extra producers and validators each incur USD500 initial setup. This is a
split-sample one-time capacity choice; it does not establish globally optimal
dynamic expansion or repeat the experiment with free extra infrastructure.

Development outcomes may inform design, including scheduling corrections and

fresh confirmation, with earlier failures retained.

Version 2 uses 24 training worlds (760001–760024), 64 primary confirmation worlds
(770001–770064), and 16 stress worlds (780001–780016). The final design specifies
32 policies, three scenarios, four primary arms, 57 sensitivity cases and five
additional ablation arms. Primary confirmation retains every complete transaction
ledger. The capacity split reuses the declared stress worlds and creates no
additional independent evidence. Exact case values and source identities are
bound by the manifest before these runs begin.

The independent stochastic world is the replication unit. Jobs, invocations and
descendants of one artifact are not independent invention campaigns. Pair worlds
across arms using shared exogenous draws; report the distribution of paired
differences, mean/median, shortfall rate and an interval based on world-level
resampling or another frozen method. Separately count independent source campaigns.
An interval over seeds describes Monte Carlo uncertainty conditional on the
assumptions, not real-market parameter uncertainty. Robustness ranges address
different assumed environments and must not be pooled into an empirical interval.

Each hypothesis receives its own result: supported within the tested model,
not supported, or unresolved. Lack of evidence is not proof of zero effect.
Scientific support for Halpha/Hshare requires positive incremental effects after
complete costs; graph admission alone is insufficient. Mechanical test success
does not require positive profit, successful invention or reinvestment. No optional
stopping, successful-seed filtering or replacement of unfavorable outcomes is
permitted. Present redesigns and additional tests as exploratory.

## 13. Reporting and path to a prospective pilot

The final report must identify which demanding job yielded the candidate; why its
recipient did not already have an equivalent; which restriction changed; the
construction and validation route; who could retrieve it; what fresh task was
attempted; the matched human scope; the chosen price and investment policy; net
complete-cost effects; and how those effects changed as Beta advanced.

Use separate status labels for actual local implementation, synthetic software
measurement, simulated policy/enforcement, modeled economics and realized activity.
Retain failed transformations, negative transfer, false admissions, no-investment
outcomes and capability retirement. Publication should remain worthwhile if
Beta delivery is profitable but the shared Alpha increment is zero or negative.

A subsequent prospective pilot would need task-matched frontier runs, actual
usage and quota measurements, independently checked delivered artifacts, matched
