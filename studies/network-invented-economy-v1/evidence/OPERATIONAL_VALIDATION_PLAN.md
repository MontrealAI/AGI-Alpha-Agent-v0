# From the research edition to a validated paid offering

This is a proposed validation path, not an authorized deployment, scheduled trial,
customer contract or claim that the research capsules are operational skills.
It follows the current study's supported coordination-design scope. Existing
owners retain deployment, signing, governance and settlement authority.

## 1. Qualify one useful outcome before promising a platform

The initial offering is an offline, executable allocation/procurement design for
public or rights-cleared synthetic inputs. Its contract must state the objective,
finite economic assumptions, budget, permitted mechanism family, deliverable,
acceptance tests, supported integration boundary, exclusions, remedy and payment
timing. It excludes live procurement control, regulated advice and unsupported
guarantees about collusion, externalities or distribution estimation.

Obtain a genuinely matched human-service quotation, including specification,
experiments, implementation, checking, integration and delivery responsibility.
The paper's derived service-rate references are useful prior ranges, not that
quotation. Record whether the human provider uses AI; do not call an advertised
service price verified human-only effort. Measure actual buyer willingness to
pay with predeclared offers and record postponement and refusal as outcomes.

## 2. Measure competent Beta on the substantive job

Pin the available model snapshot or explicitly record the alias and provider
response metadata. Record reasoning mode/effort, context, source pack, tools,
verifier versions, routing, permissions and account quotas. Give the producer,
validator, correction agent and economic evaluator appropriate tools and
adequate budgets. A competent baseline includes known algorithms, ordinary
caching, prior experience and already available equivalents.

Instrument every underlying request, reasoning token, cache read/write, tool
operation, retry, abstention, correction and recovery. Do not infer the cost or
quality of an actual frontier job from the fact that a frontier model helped
author this study's simulator. Keep API usage distinct from subscription access.
If the account cannot expose aggregate Pro usage, record that cost as unresolved
and do not replace it with a guessed single-call bill.

Provider operation is automated. Failed intake may trigger automated clarification;
unresolved or unsupported cases may be rejected. Any exceptional professional
intervention must be identified and costed in a separate offering. Governance
review of a deployment does not become free routine labor inside each job.

## 3. Meter A, B and C once

Use a unique activity ID, job ID, capability/campaign ID, cost book, resource
quantity, billing basis, timestamp and cash status for each activity. A includes
learning and experiments needed for the customer's delivery. A source-cost memo
may follow the resulting capability, but its amount is never another expense.

B begins only for incremental retention: extract an eligible method, sanitize it,
generalize it, validate on protected fresh tasks, package and distribute it, then
maintain and requalify it. Receiving-job integration belongs once in that job's
A ledger. Its incremental cost difference still belongs in the causal Alpha
comparison; this does not create a second payment in B.

C is an optional decision for work beyond the paid outcome. Link it to the
incremental hypothesis, expected benefit, cash source and abandonment rule.
Retaining a reserve or declining C is an allowed decision. Neither sunk A nor
already paid B is charged again to the prospective decision. Enterprise profit
continues to include all historical costs.

Reserve affordable delivery and remedy obligations before work begins. A signed
order, invoice, receivable, token balance increase or internal transfer is not a
collected operating receipt. Do not fund current extraction with future cash.
Reconcile accrued expense, actual payment, commitment and reserve separately.

## 4. Protect a recipient test that can reject the candidate

Define the incremental Alpha hypothesis, benefit unit, task distribution,
dependency conditions, remaining lifecycle costs and decision threshold before
examining held-out recipient outcomes. Use paired tasks under the same resources
with and without the candidate. The receiver may reconstruct a method or use an
existing equivalent. Include overlapping recipients, failed adaptation and
changed distributions. Permit optional nonuse when acquisition is not worthwhile.

Use independent invention campaigns as the replication unit where campaign
effects matter. Many related jobs from one artifact do not create many independent
inventions. Estimate sample size from the relevant task variance and the smallest
economically meaningful effect. Preserve an exploratory development set and a
fresh confirmation set; disclose amendments and do not reuse confirmation to
tune the candidate without another fresh test.

For the witness in this edition, measure wall time, token/compute use and the
complete recipient validation route. Its construction-count reduction cannot
answer whether the full transfer is cheaper. If a trusted-attestation route is
proposed, include its independent evidence, trust policy, shared-failure risk,
revalidation cost and failure remedy in the comparison.

## 5. Separate useful evidence from identity and authority

Use task-specific executable checks and independently informative evidence.
Agreement among related model judges is not independent correctness evidence.
Measure false passes, false rejections, material errors and correlated failures
against protected ground truth or an appropriately scoped external criterion.
No finite sample certifies a zero error rate. For example, under independent
Bernoulli trials, zero failures in 60 cases still gives an exact one-sided 95%
upper bound of approximately 4.87%; dependence makes that example inapplicable.
Choose risk criteria for the supported service before operation.

Operational graph admission needs a nonredundant current comparator, positive
remaining net Alpha, eligible evidence and separate authorized admission. A
customer's acceptance of one job does not automatically admit a general skill.
Attestations identify what was signed; they cannot turn assumptions into measured
truth. The graph's public deterministic keys must be replaced through the existing
owners' authorized security and deployment process, not silently in this study.

## 6. Make access network-wide, then constrain each action correctly

Commission actual authentication of direct-label members and explicit eligibility
for writing, validating and admitting records. Do not infer authority from a
suffix or an ENS-looking string. Integrate through the existing owners' approved
adapters without changing AGI Jobs club governance or settlement.

All admitted shareable capabilities must be discoverable and available throughout
the authenticated agent network. State whether retrieval, inspection, export,
controlled execution and composition are supported. Exported content can be
copied by an authorized recipient; access control alone cannot prevent that.
Operational permission to act is checked separately from access to the skill.

Remove client secrets and restricted third-party material. Keep protected test
cases and scorer internals outside capsules and failure memory. A declaration
field is not a semantic leakage detector. If lawful sanitization cannot be
established, exclude the affected content instead of weakening the sharing rule.

## 7. Requalify change and test the economic loop

Bind the applicable model, tools, data, routing, verifier and environment to the
baseline. Detect material changes outside the current in-memory research API;
invalidate affected authority until the proper requalification completes.
Reassess overlap as new equivalents appear. Retire or supersede a skill whose
advantage disappears, while preserving its historical evidence without restoring
its selection influence.

Report Beta delivery economics, incremental B/Alpha benefit and incremental C
benefit separately. A paid-job retention loop requires actual useful subsequent
work after additional retention costs. An optional research feedback loop also
requires collected surplus funding C and resulting improvement on fresh work.
Increasing effectiveness of capability discovery or validation across independent
campaigns would be needed for a self-acceleration claim. More jobs or graph nodes
are insufficient.

## Authorization required beyond this edition

This package authorizes no production effect. New provider spending or purchases,
credentials, live client material, operational key management or security changes,
real ENS/chain enforcement, deployment, customer contracting, payment collection
and settlement require the relevant existing owners' explicit authorization and
installed qualification procedures. No such action is needed to reproduce the
offline simulator, graph tests, mathematical witness or demonstration.
