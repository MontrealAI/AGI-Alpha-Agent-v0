# Venture screen: demanding work that can change the next job

## Decision and evidence boundary

Evidence date: 26 October 2026. The primary research venture is a **coordination-
mechanism foundry**: executable bounded allocation/payment designs and their
scope-specific evidence. Three adjacent alternatives remain conditional research
options. None is an actual customer pipeline, funded company, completed deployment
or measured market. No investment in the alternatives is required.

The screen asks which demanding useful jobs could produce a reusable capability
that changes the representation, information requirements, dependencies or
executable protocol of later work. An attractive story is insufficient. Each
candidate needs an artifact, a decisive falsifier and a competent Beta baseline
that already has frontier intelligence, established theory, tools and experience.
Any Alpha qualification must survive its full construction, validation, transfer
and upkeep costs relative to that baseline.

The primary lane is distinct from plant scheduling, GPU optimization and a
general numerical-experiment laboratory. It studies incentive and budget-aware
coordination designs on public/synthetic inputs. Existing active work and owners
remain separate; this screen does not commission overlapping implementation.

## 1. Coordination-mechanism foundry — primary

### Useful job and change of setup

A hypothetical consortium needs all members of a complementary input bundle,
has a hard common budget and wants an executable rule for selecting and paying
suppliers. Independent component decisions can satisfy local conditions while
violating the common cap. A general search over every threshold vector becomes
expensive as the number of components and permitted reports grows.

The research artifact changes this setup. A typed compiler binds finite input
distributions and their assumptions. An exact prefix frontier makes budget-aware
composition efficient within a declared posted-price family. An independent
certificate and finite deviation checker specify what was actually established.
Future budget/value queries may reuse the same input-specific frontier if the
distribution family remains applicable. The authorized objective remains a
correct bounded coordination design; the transformation changes how it is solved.

The direct primary precedent is Liu, Guo and Conitzer's
[AMD-LLM framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12203v1), which represents mechanisms
as executable code and combines generation with problem-specific fixing and
evaluation. Its findings support a bounded code-and-evidence offering. They also
show why standard mechanism knowledge and reconstruction must remain in Beta;
the research does not establish Astra-specific customer acceptance or demand.

### Recipient baseline and candidate Alpha

Recipients already have posted-price theory, dynamic programming, ordinary
optimization libraries, source retrieval and testing. They may have related
compiled artifacts from earlier work. The asset is not Alpha merely because a
recipient lacks its filename. It can add value if its validated input-specific
work is expensive to reconstruct and cheap enough to verify or use under a
permitted attestation policy. A duplicate or mismatched distribution fails this
claim. Better ordinary tooling or accumulated recipient experience can erase it.

The three complementary candidates are the distribution compiler, budget frontier
composer and deviation/constraint certifier described in
[mechanism_design.md](mechanism_design.md). Construction attempts can fail. A
correct artifact can have no buyer demand. A useful component can be valuable
only as part of the bundle.

### Decisive experiment and existing result boundary

The actual local witness compares small cases with exhaustive optima, verifies
large frontier certificates, preserves budget-violating negative controls and
tests fresh recipient queries. Its preserved
[record](mechanism_witness.json) reports matching solutions and less construction
work with sharing, but more certificate-check work when every receiver fully
revalidates the larger artifact. This is a mixed computational result. The
trusted-attestation route remains a separate modeled scenario. No measured
dollar Alpha increment is inferred from the witness.

The economic study tests whether some version of this route is worthwhile after
all costs, under heterogeneous agents and a moving Beta baseline. It can conclude
that ordinary delivery is profitable while shared capabilities or reinvestment
are not worthwhile under the tested conditions. That is a valid result.

### Human equivalence and offering boundary

The matched scope is requirements formalization, economics design, executable
implementation, adversarial finite tests, evidence and integration documentation.
The illustrative human team combines an economics researcher, software engineer,
quantitative analyst and project manager. Published US service-rate schedules
provide hourly anchors; role-hours and total scope equivalence are assumptions,
as detailed below. The offering excludes live procurement, legal interpretation,
stakeholder negotiation, deployment and operating responsibility. Those duties
cannot be silently included on the human side and omitted from the automated side.

### Failure and continuation rule

Defer or abandon if validation cannot resolve the declared strategic scope; buyers
need excluded professional responsibility; reusable domains seldom recur; Beta
reconstruction is cheaper; full receiver checking removes the gain; or collection
timing and capacity make the investment unaffordable. Continue profitable delivery
without investment where that is the best modeled action. A fresh investment
requires a positive prospective increment for a specified bundle and useful life.

## 2. Distributed recovery-protocol foundry — deferred

A hypothetical software team needs a protocol that coordinates partial failure,
retries and recovery among a bounded number of components. The reusable output
would be a parameterized executable state machine, explicit invariants, a
failure-schedule generator and a portable counterexample-reduction method. The
setup transformation replaces ad hoc error handling with a representation that
makes interactions and repair obligations inspectable.

Industrial primary evidence supports the relevance of this kind of work, but
not automated commercial success. The authors of
[Using lightweight formal methods to validate a key-value storage node in Amazon S3](https://www.amazon.science/publications/using-lightweight-formal-methods-to-validate-a-key-value-storage-node-in-amazon-s3)
describe applying formal methods to an evolving production storage implementation.
That precedent concerns an engineering organization; it is not evidence that this
study can remove professional responsibilities or reproduce the outcome at the
assumed AI cost.

Beta already includes model checking, established recovery patterns, source
grounding and ordinary code review tools. Possible Alpha is a domain-specific
abstraction or executable recovery method that avoids repeated state-space work
and transfers across genuinely related protocols. The decisive experiment uses
declared bounded schedules, an independent invariant checker and mutation cases
that violate a necessary assumption. A counterexample outside the declared bounds
cannot be dismissed as if the design were globally correct.

The human reference would require distributed-systems engineers, a formal-methods
specialist and integration ownership. The existing general software rate card
does not establish a matched project price or specialist premium. No revenue is
assigned to this alternative. It is deferred because implementation refinement,
real failure models, evolving dependencies and deployment responsibility create
a larger gap between an offline artifact and a buyer-accepted outcome than the
primary bounded mechanism job. A separate funded study would first need a
noncritical scope, an exact implementation/model link and matched human quotations.

## 3. Compositional interoperability foundry — deferred

A hypothetical purchaser needs several independently specified software services
to work together under a global requirement. Locally correct components may make
incompatible assumptions about message order, ownership, completion or recovery.
The proposed output is executable adapters with versioned assume-guarantee
contracts and a composition certificate. Its value would be newly feasible
combinations under a fixed integration budget, rather than another API wrapper.

Finkbeiner and Passing's primary research,
[Compositional Synthesis of Modular Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14783v2),
constructs per-process implementations and interface certificates so that local
requirements can be separated. The reported advantage depends on suitably small
certificates. This motivates a falsifiable dependence on interface complexity;
it supplies no market-demand or frontier-model success probability.

Beta knows conventional interface tooling, protocol specifications, contract
checking and the published synthesis method. Candidate Alpha would be an
input-specific decomposition, reusable compatibility abstraction or verified
adapter family whose full integration cost is lower than competent reconstruction.
The decisive experiment compares the composed system with the declared global
specification, then removes assumptions or changes versions to ensure rejection.
Maintaining old compatibility results across changed dependencies without checking
would invalidate the result.

The human team would include software architecture, integration engineering and
formal-methods expertise. No exact human quotation is available. Reuse could be
high within a stable component ecosystem, but component churn, certificate size,
ordinary code-generation progress and narrow applicability may eliminate the
increment. This candidate is deferred pending a public benchmark family with
known component versions and a measured matched Beta cost. It does not justify
building a general infrastructure product before one family shows an increment.

## 4. Privacy-constrained collaboration compiler — deferred

A hypothetical group wants a joint decision without centrally revealing all
inputs. The research artifact would be a public/synthetic arithmetic circuit,
its stated privacy and adversary assumptions, a costed compilation route using
an established secure-computation protocol, and a functional test harness. The
transformational opportunity is to separate reusable compilation/preprocessing
from task-specific inputs, making a previously impractical collaboration feasible
within a declared resource and information budget.

The primary
[SPDZ research](https://eprint.iacr.org/2011/535) separates preprocessing from a
more efficient online phase under a specific cryptographic model. This supplies
an established method that Beta must possess; it is not a new invention or proof
of this proposed implementation. Old benchmark timings are not used as current
cost calibration. A network identity check or encrypted capsule alone supplies
none of the required privacy guarantee.

Potential Alpha would be a useful application-specific circuit/decomposition,
compatible reuse plan and evidence package that materially reduces complete
cost. Differential functional tests and complete preprocessing/online resource
measurement are necessary. Security claims additionally require the protocol's
actual threat model, correct implementation and corresponding verification;
synthetic functional agreement alone cannot certify them.

The matched human team would include cryptographic engineering, systems and
domain expertise. The supplied rates and hours are not a defensible precise
quote for that scope. This candidate remains outside the present automated
offering because security validation, operational key handling, liability and
real data-access permissions would require a different study and separately
authorized deployment. No private data, security changes or live cryptographic
service are used here. A future narrow prototype can be reconsidered only after
an explicit complete-cost advantage over established Beta tools is testable.

## Commercial reference and decision discipline

The only quantified human reference adopted for this study is the primary
mechanism package. Observed hourly service prices are drawn from
[RAND](https://www.gsaadvantage.gov/ref_text/47QRAA24D008Z/110BBS.3WQO6G_47QRAA24D008Z_47QRAA24D008ZCPA19672FNL.PDF)
and [Z Federal](https://www.zfederal.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Z-INC-GSA-MAS-Pricelist-Mod-18.pdf).
Using assumed low/middle/high role-hours gives USD25,329.00 / USD49,857.64 /
USD98,114.56. These are arithmetic scope references, not actual quotations,
verified human-only costs, market-size estimates or willingness-to-pay observations.
The middle package has 196 team-hours. A skilled team may deliver a familiar
bounded mechanism with substantially less effort.

Price and discount are optimized as modeled decisions against the human-service
choice, postponement and no purchase. Ability to generate a persuasive prototype
does not entitle the enterprise to charge for a completed professional outcome.
For the deferred candidates, price remains unspecified until scope and responsibility
are matched. There is no invented common revenue estimate used to make the primary
venture appear superior.

The primary selection is a bounded methodological decision: it has task-matched
published research, executable purpose-preserving transformations, decisive finite
oracles, a completed known-method witness and a transparent multidisciplinary
reference. The witness's mixed transfer result is preserved rather than screened
away. Further opportunity selection should change only when new evidence improves
the case, not because the primary venture produces an unfavorable result.

## What would justify reopening an alternative?

Reopening requires a specified purchaser problem, a public or otherwise authorized
input family, an executable candidate asset, a strong versioned Beta route,
an independent falsifier, a matched complete-cost comparison and a plausible
collection path. A proposed investment must fit available cash and beat waiting
under declared uncertainty. Neither a larger opportunity count nor more proposed
capabilities constitutes progress. The study can rationally retain reserves and
continue a narrower profitable service while all three alternatives remain deferred.
