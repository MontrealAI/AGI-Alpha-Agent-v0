## Results and limits

24 held-out schemas; 96 unique fresh jobs; 480 repeated evaluations across five methods. Every method delivers 96/96 independently accepted reports. Production examples are rebuild 244, ordinary reuse 61, retained recipe 29, active specialist 29 and current-session LLM-assisted constructor 29. The learned recipe also needs 31 training examples. It does not beat the active incumbent and is not economically promoted. There is no sustained-acceleration result.

Base provider full cost is **modeled** at $1.211784/report, increasing to $15.863884 and $650.040140 under conservative/stress assumptions. Human-work advantage is expressed as break-even durations; actual matched human time and complete cost are unknown. All 30 finance paths are **simulations of a hypothetical future capability**, not sales, earned cash or returns from the rejected recipe.

The companion preserves the primary freeze, independent Python acceptance, failed/refused boundaries, reporting amendments, source licenses, current LLM provenance, complete costs and cash/receivable decisions. Twenty-four boundary probes cover sequential/active/retained strategies; the separate LLM constructor's four ambiguity probes are not run.
