## The result

**The actual second capability works within its registered scope, but this experiment does not demonstrate a worthwhile second investment.** The retained full-lock evidence adapter produced six accepted decisions. A strong ordinary-tool workflow and an independently built conventional wrapper produced the same six decisions. The retained adapter was slower on all six questions, and no active human-effort saving was observed.

The frozen strategic-market continuation separates being able to fund research from repaying its complete cost. It contains **1,920 paired worlds / 5,760 enterprise histories**. There are **114 operating-funded research attempts per treatment across the designed scenario grid and zero qualifying second investments**. Every priced group has negative mean project-inclusive incremental profit. Actual customer receipts are zero; actual all-in research money remains unidentified.

## Finite search and complete economics

The predeclared envelope allowed four choices, 30 minutes of discovery, one selected prototype, one fresh confirmation cohort and a three-hour deadline. It explicitly included maintaining the incumbent, adopting ordinary tools, rejecting an unnecessary custom resolver, and declining broader application qualification without its own bounded closure. The earlier three-choice `NO_GO` search remains preserved and priced as uncertain prior discovery, not removed as an administrative obstacle.

The new economic protocol fixes 52 service weeks, the original $1,000 seed and $200 reserve, and all three branches' same first-asset history. Thirteen conditions cover recurrence including zero, nonzero buyer integration, collection delay, competent cheaper AI, undercutting, price pressure, integration failure, upkeep and quality shock. Buyers can reject, defer, switch and stop purchasing. Competitor behavior is a disclosed simulation mechanism, not a claimed equilibrium.

Three complete one-off scenario allocations are $25.97, $103.85 and $415.40, with upkeep. They include prior discovery, new discovery, rejected candidates/failed attempts, construction, independent verification, integration, other researcher work and model/tools. B and C receive the same cost envelopes. No human-effort, customer-effort, quality, price or demand advantage is invented. Signed measured CPU slowdowns remain in the diagnostic subcent account at an explicitly assumed $1/core-hour tariff.

The adaptive policy pays affordable search and declines construction without justified full-cost repayment. The completed-research replay asks whether the actual built prototype could be financed at week 26; unaffordable research remains an explicit outside contribution, counted once in project results. Later benefits never finance an earlier decision silently.

Among **1,872 priced paired worlds**, 1,870 B−A project differences are negative and two positive. The two favorable realized paths, +$13.24 and +$7.67, remain visible in [the analysis](analysis/market-findings.md). Construction pauses change later simulated buyer choices and feedback; neither case has a direct capability saving or passes the investment gate. No separate pause-only ablation was run. All 78 priced group means are negative. The 48 actual-unknown worlds keep project differences null. Counts over this designed grid are not market probabilities. B/C results tie at cent precision in every priced world.

## What would change the conclusion?

At the preselected 15-use workload, the low scenario needs $1.8313 of additional value per use before downtime; the reference scenario needs $7.34, including 25 weeks' upkeep. The latter is conditional arithmetic: that reference-cost prototype was not financed or used in seed 8001. At the assumed $65.38/hour rate, the low threshold is about 1.681 active provider minutes per use. No such saving was measured. The model assumes only two remaining provider minutes after the first capability; that is a scenario assumption, not observed labor.

The next useful observation is a real pending maintainer decision with measured active attention, strong ordinary-tool comparison, independent acceptance and complete applicable billing. A separately registered bounded study could use that evidence without requiring a paid pilot. This study stops after its declared negative result; it does not change the offering, demand, prices or horizon to force a positive claim.

## Résultat en français

**La deuxième capacité a été construite et testée, mais aucun réinvestissement rentable n'est démontré.** Les trois méthodes produisent les mêmes six décisions acceptées. L'adaptateur conservé est plus lent dans les six cas et aucune économie de travail humain actif n'a été mesurée.

La simulation prolonge une seule histoire d'entreprise, avec capital initial, factures, encaissements, remboursements, dépenses, obligations et réserve. Dans l'exemple choisi avant les résultats, les ressources générées disponibles sont de **82,74 $** à la semaine 26. Le scénario de coût de **25,97 $** est finançable, mais ses 15 utilisations simulées et **1,50 $** d'entretien ne produisent aucun bénéfice direct supplémentaire : la perte différentielle est de **27,47 $**. Le coût de référence de **103,85 $** n'est pas finançable à cette date.

Les 1 920 mondes appariés ne contiennent aucun investissement qualifié. Les 114 tentatives de recherche financées par l'exploitation, par méthode, décrivent la grille de scénarios et non une probabilité commerciale. Les deux trajectoires favorables sont conservées; elles résultent de changements de parcours simulé liés à la pause de construction, sans gain direct de la capacité. Les recettes réelles restent nulles et le coût monétaire réel complet demeure inconnu.

Les dossiers distinguent cohérence des métadonnées, qualification de l'application et autorisation d'exploitation. Ils conservent les résultats négatifs antérieurs, le bénéfice conditionnel du premier déploiement financé par le capital initial, la perte historique et la parité conventionnelle. La démonstration bilingue permet d'inspecter les hypothèses, les preuves et le grand livre sans transformer une hypothèse favorable en résultat confirmé.
