# Research resources and complete-cost boundary

The actual experiment consumed supplied research effort and computer resources. **Its complete actual monetary cost is unknown.** No customer receipts or new paid commitments were observed. Those two zeros do not establish that authoring inference, active researcher attention, infrastructure or future upkeep were free.

This directory indexes preserved resource measurements, construction, rejected directions, failed attempts and review contributions. It does not replace the frozen economic ledger or turn its scenario allocations into actual invoices.

## Files and interpretation

- [research-resource-ledger.json](research-resource-ledger.json) contains the structured activity index, source SHA-256 identities, actual-money unknowns, separate modeled-money fields and explicit overlap rules.
- [research-resource-ledger.csv](research-resource-ledger.csv) is a flat view of the same activity rows. `UNKNOWN` represents a missing observation. Zero is used only where it is observed or explicitly justified.
- [root-reported-publication-failures.json](root-reported-publication-failures.json) preserves publication and browser actions reported by the root researcher when no timed command receipt exists. It is labeled as a researcher report, not an invented instrument trace.
- [build_resource_accounting.py](../tools/build_resource_accounting.py) rebuilds this index from saved local evidence. It does not run production, select packages, simulate markets, download anything or change frozen inputs. Rebuilding creates a new accounting snapshot; retain the delivered archive as the original version.

The source inventory includes detailed resource and review records even where a compact activity row groups them. Its file hashes describe the snapshot time. Later file revisions and closing delivery work need an explicit supplement.

The model's generated-resource test is a financing calculation inside each finite simulated history. It does not identify an actual operating surplus. The same ledger records opening historical expenditure once; prospective deployment views may add it back analytically, but never recreate it as available cash or omit it from full-history profitability. Completed prototype costs outside a modeled enterprise remain an explicit project contribution and are deducted once in project-inclusive results.

All current cost profiles, inherited cost machinery, parameter manifests and calibration sources are hash-bound in `model_money_separate.source_identities`. This accounting work does **not** change their frozen amounts after observing outcomes. The existing scenarios include complete cost categories; they are neither actual bills nor demonstrated finite bounds on the actual research bill.

## What the activity counts mean

The earlier screen considered three possibilities and admitted no second investment. Its **321.809514-second review window** is retained, with unknown active labor, inference billing and full process cost. The new bounded portfolio contains four choices: maintain the incumbent, adopt pip, construct a custom resolver, or broaden to full application qualification. One pip-adoption prototype was selected; two construction directions were rejected; the no-investment alternative remained legitimate.

The actual B and C assets were authored as separate ordinary-tool implementations. B has three explicit recorded development invocations. C has two baseline invocations and two four-case development suites. The original C packet's absolute paths failed independent acceptance; its source, job, results and corrective work remain included. The 259.268458-second final C construction window already contains the earlier 188.085058-second window.

The independent acceptance effort produced one shared verifier and 24 synthetic controls. Seven material prefreeze findings were corrected: B's verified-headline handling, C's input-path contract, three selection/acquisition issues and two campaign-driver issues. These findings are engineering work, not seven new customer outcomes. The disclosed conservative handling of some negative pip failures is a scope limitation, not an additional invented correction.

The primary production experiment executed **six questions and 18 arm evaluations** once. The primary market experiment executed **one campaign, 1,920 paired worlds and 5,760 enterprise histories**. The later relocated reproduction reused the same six questions for 18 evaluations; it created zero new research missions or customer orders. These counts use different units and must not be added into a single sample count.

## Resource measurements and double counting

Ten top-level root-command receipts form a deliberately limited, nonoverlapping subtotal:

| Selected measured subset | Seconds |
|---|---:|
| Sum of command wall times | 73.067375212 |
| Sum of wrapper-self plus child CPU | 72.893025336 |
| Monetary value | Unknown |

This is **a partial computer-resource subtotal, not total research time, labor, expense or an all-in lower estimate priced at a tariff**. It covers baseline acquisition and development commands, registered input selection/acquisition, primary production and the primary market command. It excludes other agents' work, source reading, inference, writing, review, publication, packaging and delivery. Summed command wall time is selected command occupancy, not elapsed team time.

The index keeps other measured executions visible without silently adding them to that subtotal. In particular:

- Complete per-arm service receipts are nested in the outer production command. Their producer and verifier measurements cannot be added to the inclusive service measurement again.
- Market execution/summary/compression timings are nested in the outer market command. The 39.468392-second outer wall receipt differs legitimately from the narrower campaign timing reported in the paper.
- The seven independent synthetic market probes sit inside a separately measured review command. The inner and outer CPU records describe overlapping work.
- The first and corrected full-ledger audit executions are distinct and both retained: approximately 27.618513/27.598205 wall/CPU seconds and 23.685529/23.666268 seconds. The final report repeats the first-pass timing as history; that repeated field is not a third audit.
- Renderer and portable-tooling authoring windows overlap their own executions and other team work. The 338.723352- and 658.292898-second windows are not active human labor and are not summed with nested commands.
- Repeated construction receipts are versioned descriptions, while separately evidenced failed or repeated commands are actual additional work. The index makes that distinction explicit.

Peak memory, embedded per-case measurements and detailed command captures remain in the original referenced receipts. A missing CPU field, service fee or attention measure stays unknown.

## Failures and post-confirmation contributions

The index retains the original unsuccessful C path attempt, the B headline correction, earlier development freezes, wrong-directory market test invocation, driver corrections, the failed audit registration predicate and its corrected review. The audit failure did not change a market outcome: the reviewer initially required the later comment ID inside a protocol URL that correctly pointed to issue #4850. The independent public registration receipt resolved that reviewer error.

Publication work includes two failed LaTeX attempts, subsequent successful builds, a harmless wrong-relative-path patch attempt, final layout work, figures, manuscript review and demonstrations. The moving-argument macro and table-bottomrule failures were corrected without changing scientific outcomes. The root reports the final 15-page, 323,855-byte PDF was rendered and inspected, including selected full-page views. Publication command wall/CPU measurements were unavailable and remain null.

HTML browser checks have a different status. The initial Playwright invocation failed because its Chromium executable was absent; its measured 0.535730-second wall and 0.627962-second child CPU receipt is retained. The root's Chromium151 download then failed with invalid ZIP data after five internal CLI retries, and an archived revision1200 HEAD probe returned 195 bytes of HTML. Those latter command durations are unknown. No successful browser layout inspection is inferred from them. Functional JavaScript/content checks, when delivered, are separate evidence.

Post-confirmation work also includes exact public-wheel provenance, two execution-tool wheels, full/GitHub staging, tamper and no-network checks, the single relocated replay, bilingual packet rendering, economic analysis, the interactive demonstration, and this accounting index. None earns a production saving or creates a market order. The first demonstration build's overwritten original receipt is explicitly a retrospective partial record; later preserved build receipts describe distinct presentation builds. Two early accounting-index invocations themselves failed on receipt-path lookup and were corrected; their unmeasured costs are retained rather than erased.

## What remains outside the snapshot

The activity index is a reference and uncertainty boundary, not a claim to have reconstructed every token, invoice or minute. Unmetered inference, active attention, source-reading/orchestration costs, original research costs, future upkeep and other exposed gaps remain in the JSON's `unmetered_residual`.

Archive construction, final hash checks, file persistence, coordination and other work performed after this accounting snapshot belong in the root researcher's **dated closing delivery supplement outside the immutable archive**. A file cannot include the cost or hash of a later act that verifies and delivers that very file. The supplement must preserve that boundary rather than silently editing completed evidence or assigning zero to the remaining work.
