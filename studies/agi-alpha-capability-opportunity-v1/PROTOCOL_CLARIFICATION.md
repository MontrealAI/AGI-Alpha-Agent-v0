# Primary protocol clarification

The frozen `PROTOCOL.json` remains unchanged so its recorded source identity and
the original experiment can be inspected. Several descriptive fields retain prose
from the earlier exploratory formulation. This clarification does not change any
source, outcomes, thresholds, policies or confirmation task.

## Authoritative primary operating contract

For the point-design service, a plan qualifies only when an **actual simulator
query** records normalized response at least **2.0**, the public plan cost is at
most **1.2**, and its inputs belong to the declared menu. That actual query is
included in the budget. A model policy reserves one query; direct search queries
plans until it obtains a qualifying response or exhausts its budget.

The service does **not** promise the globally cheapest qualifying plan, a maximum
regret, or correctness on unqueried inputs. “Lowest-cost” and “greater than 5%
regret” language remaining in `opportunity_portfolio` describes the initial
research idea and an optional research audit. It is not the final commercial
acceptance contract. The current `acceptance.inverse_design` field, executed
source, raw operational verification response and actual acceptance column record
the final contract. No conclusion may use the stale wording to upgrade the result.

The retained `design_regret` field compares the proposal with a fully evaluated
512-plan menu only as a separately charged research audit. The 384-label prediction
test is also a research audit unless the verified-model service explicitly pays
for all 384 labels. None of these protected labels enters operational acceptance
of the primary point-design service or supplies a free commercial validator.

## Diagnostic prototype

The final diagnostic prototype uses **12 fitting observations plus four fresh
validation observations**, totalling **16** additional calls. Its validation
checks whether a proposed changed-coefficient explanation predicts the observed
faulty response. The benchmark also knows the actual changed coefficient, allowing
a separate correctness audit. No deployed simulator is repaired, and no live
customer acceptance procedure has been demonstrated.

The original simulator remains available, so direct original/faulty differencing
with 32 calls is an explicit ordinary alternative that does not require a learned
nominal model. The diagnostic case must not be presented as a newly established
commercially feasible offering merely because the inherited model solves it.

## Paired comparator label

The original summary's literal comparison string says “best of three Beta
methods.” The executed selector includes **all four** methods whose names begin
with `beta_`: space-filling, full-library volume design, adaptive volume design
and direct actual search. Counts and paired comparisons therefore use the stronger
four-method hindsight portfolio. The text label was not refreshed after the direct
baseline was added. This comparator is deliberately favorable to Beta and is not
an implementable advance selection oracle.

## Research costs and information limits

Repeated counterfactual models share immutable research audit labels in the
executed evaluator. The 896 primary audit values per unique task consist of 384
prediction responses and 512 menu responses. Operational and diagnostic query
costs remain separately recorded. The originating verified-model job uses 128
fitting calls plus 384 verification calls; those verification labels are not
charged again as additional research assessment.

The diagnostic comparison requires a further **32 unique labels per task**: 16
faulty-simulator responses and the corresponding 16 original-simulator responses.
These points are distinct from the 896 prediction/menu audit points. The same
diagnostic labels serve every counterfactual method and budget. An initial ledger
count of 16 omitted the original reference responses; `learning_ledger.json` and
`research_execution_ledger.json` correct that accounting count to 32. No learned
artifact, numerical outcome, operating acceptance or source freeze was changed.

`oracle_support` is an oracle-informed diagnostic comparator supplied with the
true support. It is not deployable, and it is not a proof of a mathematical optimum
over every possible algorithm. No simulation score establishes a frontier-model
capability ceiling or physically calibrated engineering assurance.

## Separately declared stronger offering

The robust-operation supplement has its own fixed protocol, development seeds,
confirmation seeds and source hashes under `technical/robust/`. It asks for one
control setting to meet finite requirements across 16 explicit environmental
contexts. It preserves the original point-design result and the original economic
policy freeze. Its finite-context acceptance does not certify robustness across
an unobserved continuum of environmental conditions.
