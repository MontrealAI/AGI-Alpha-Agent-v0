## Prior experiments inspected at their exact source

### Thermal/scientific relay

Source: [`71d1054dd56a9ef25f19ec5f19e941b1957b02ce`](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/tree/71d1054dd56a9ef25f19ec5f19e941b1957b02ce/studies/agi-alpha-invention-network-v1).
Read `technical.py`, `technical_protocol.json`, `README_technical.md`, the actual confirmation
`results.json`, the lifecycle review summary, and the integration audit. The original evidence was inspected,
not rerun or relabeled as a new confirmation.

The learner orthogonalizes twelve paid-founder response fields and enriches its basis using 32 optional Gym
solves. A rank-44 checkpoint improves raw approximation. It does not eliminate the need for correction:
both raw v1 and v2 fail all 64 fresh recipient B acceptance cases. Both corrected methods can pass because
they invoke the same competent spectral-preconditioned iterative solve. The retained representation improves
an intermediate numerical approximation; customer-level acceptance still depends on ordinary correction.

The correct economic comparator is complete corrected output, including setup, projection and checking.
The all-family recorded mean runtimes are 2.130 ms for spectral PCG, 2.016 ms for the improved ordinary router,
2.042 ms for v1, and 2.004 ms for v2. These tiny local differences are not engineering-service savings.
Under the shifted family, v2 takes 6.702 ms while sparse direct factorization takes 4.308 ms. The equally
equipped conventional sensor-design comparator also removes the supposed composition premium. Neither
the learned artifact's existence nor a graph handoff supplies additional economic Alpha.

Diagnosis: correction dominates useful acceptance; the ordinary mathematical structure was already available;
the local operation is too cheap for a small time saving to repay assessment/retention; shifted inputs cause
negative transfer. Repricing this same computation or multiplying simulated market size would not repair
those causal limitations.

### Manufacturing scheduling relay

Source: [`79948bc255578e8a0222ef9510ce9d8844f5b059`](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/tree/79948bc255578e8a0222ef9510ce9d8844f5b059/studies/agi-alpha-next-advantage-v1).
Read the README, `src/scheduling.py`, primary numerical summary, and actual
`evidence/numerical/beta144_confirmation/summary.json`.

The retained object is seven learned dispatch parameters. Under the primary common 48-candidate search
budget, v1 and v2 improve mean nominal objective by 7.71% and 9.04% over the selected conventional portfolio.
On twelve additional fresh cases, increasing ordinary search to 144 candidates gives a cheap alternative:
learned48 has -1.42% mean nominal and -2.10% mean robust advantage relative to Beta144. All three methods
pass the primary acceptance gate on those twelve cases. Beta144's mean nominal solve is 0.464 s versus
0.149 s for learned48; the extra solver time is small relative to a complete professional deliverable.

Diagnosis: fixed candidate-count comparison exposes a numerical gain but not a convincing economic
bottleneck. Extra ordinary search is inexpensive and broadly available. The new robust offering is also
available to equal-menu Beta. The evidence supports parameter learning and bounded transfer, while leaving
unique product creation, worthwhile retained Alpha and sustained compounding unresolved.

## Publication route

The repository's unchanged paper importer can add a versioned passive PDF/TeX catalog entry through a
normal reviewed PR. A new HTML reader or executable study does not qualify as a passive-paper overlay;
it requires full source qualification and existing owner release sequencing. A separately labeled research
prerelease may distribute a companion without replacing the software release, provided its artifacts,
scope and public downloaded bytes are verified. No source hash, local PDF, draft PR or research release
establishes live website, runtime or authenticated-network qualification.
