## Research resources and operating economics

The paper's new research-resource section separates the experiment's assessment workload from the modeled
enterprise's operating costs. Primary confirmation includes 90,172 method observations and 44,544 common audit
values. The robust supplement records 617,544 query-equivalent units, hypothetically USD 61,754.40 at the declared
USD 0.10 tariff. This burden is excluded from per-attempt operating contribution and is now made explicit.

`technical/analysis/research_resource_reconciliation.json` additionally reports a documented subtotal of 812,928
units across construction, duplicated development, confirmation, the robust supplement and additional recorded
recipient work. The initial constructor's audit count is reconstructed from the routine rather than backed by a
separately preserved initial audit file. Recomputed labels and unmetered fitting, plotting, engineering and review
work are distinguished. The inventory is therefore a documented resource subtotal, not a certified complete cash,
CPU or human-labor cost. It includes the additional 32 diagnostic labels omitted from the integration receipt's
short audit-boundary sentence. The actual customer collections and observed paid API expense remain zero; complete
engineering expense remains unknown.

The paper appropriately describes USD 44.775 versus USD 16.4042 as conditional mean operating contribution per
attempt for a post-confirmation willingness-to-pay sensitivity, before common research expense. Both are positive;
this comparison does not make the baseline business economically impossible. The original enterprise result and
the later robust-offering sensitivity remain separate.
