## Integrity, authority and publication

A SHA256 commitment establishes byte identity. A signature establishes a scoped statement by a configured key. Neither establishes truth, novel discovery, field correctness or commercial value. Public synthetic commitment nonces and the documented Nova research passphrase provide reproducibility, not confidentiality.

The frozen technical file list is `evidence/technical_freeze.json`. Commercial primary and supplemental freezes are separate. `integration/final_summary.json` identifies final-02 and preserves both earlier runs. The final package manifest is self-excluding and hashes all published study files; its role is integrity, not new experimental evidence.

Authority fixture keys are ephemeral and outside the published tree. The package includes public signatures, receipts and journal views only. No minting, token listing, trading, funds movement, private utility connection or permission expansion was performed. The existing `*.agent.agi.eth` research recipient uses a simulated key mapping and the existing worker role, with a new grant for each mission.

This publication uses an additive study-only draft pull request and immutable source links. Canonical Pages, catalog imports, source releases and software qualification remain with the existing owners and their documented gates.
