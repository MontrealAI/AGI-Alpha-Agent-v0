A work roster can be an input to child-care capacity.

Our new AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL study began with an open opportunity search. It led to a concrete possibility: several employers could coordinate consenting workers' shifts, then fund a care opening that fragmented schedules could not support.

We built the arrangement and made its obligations executable.

In the connected synthetic example, two employers and four children need CAD 405/day under the joint arrangement, compared with CAD 705/day under fixed work rosters. Both face the same CAD 450 funding ceiling. Employer staffing and complete care coverage are preserved. Backup, worker compensation and refundable reserves are included.

An independent 48-case audit found 16 arrangements that became feasible only with the joint permissions and commitments. A competent conventional coordinator given the same formulation ties. Existing flexible-care and cooperative services are strong prior art; this is a tested construction, not a claim that no human could have conceived it.

The commercial question stays separate. A CAD 750 coordination package supports modeled mean annual operating profit of about CAD 4,736 under explicit low-provider-cost and demand assumptions. Broader uncertainty produces losses, and central wage-based provider costs exceed the original payer limits. Actual collections are zero.

That evidence tells us what to test next: real provider terms, voluntary roster options, buyer value and an incumbent's equivalent arrangement.

The package includes the paper, executable study, discovery record, independent checks, preserved failures and the native Insight → Nova-Seed → FusionPlan → MARK research artifacts. A fresh recipient can execute the inherited public instrument; identical ordinary-library access performs equally. Paid retention remains on HOLD until it earns its cost.

The opportunity frontier expands when a useful possibility becomes a complete arrangement that people can inspect, fund and choose.
