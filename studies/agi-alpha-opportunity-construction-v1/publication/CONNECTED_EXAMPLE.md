# Connected example — A work roster becomes an input to care capacity

**Evidence class:** executed synthetic construction and simulated accounts. No real worker, child, provider, sale or care event appears in this example.

## 1. An open search finds the interface

The frozen discovery brief asks what valuable possibility capable agents could construct. It supplies no child-care solution. E 1 connects uncertain care demand, independently controlled employer rosters, indivisible opening costs and advance funding. The common search and all twelve candidates remain in `discovery/`; strong flexible-care and cooperative prior art is preserved.

The proposed transformation changes permitted roster choices and commitments while keeping the original useful purpose: complete care and employer staffing. It does not rename an existing solver as a discovery.

## 2. The original day cannot be funded

Two employers each need two workers from 06:00–10:00 and two from 10:00–14:00. Each employer has two parents and two nonparents, with one parent initially on each shift. Every worker in this synthetic origin permits either shift and states a CAD 5 change payment.

| Worker at each employer | Parent? | Original shift | Selected shift | Change payment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| W1 | Yes | Early | Early | CAD 0 |
| W2 | Yes | Late | Early | CAD 5 |
| W3 | No | Early | Late | CAD 5 |
| W4 | No | Late | Late | CAD 0 |

Each child's travel allowance adds thirty minutes before and after work. Fixed rosters require two five-hour care openings. Primary care costs CAD 320; standby costs CAD 40; maximum backup activation needs a CAD 320 reserve; the origin coordination fee is CAD 25. Total commitment: **CAD 705**, above the collective **CAD 450** ceiling.

This is a complete but unfunded reference. It does not become acceptable by omitting backup or asking parents to cover a care gap.

## 3. Consenting changes create a funded opening

The constructor evaluates 36 quota-valid joint rosters. Moving W2 and W3 at each employer places all four children in the 05:30–10:30 opening. Every employer still has two workers on each work shift and 960 worker-minutes, or 1,920 across the cohort.

One primary block costs CAD 170, including the declared CAD 150 fixed opening and CAD 5 per child. Standby costs CAD 20. A CAD 170 reserve covers the complete specified backup activation. Four worker changes cost CAD 20. With the same CAD 25 origin fee, maximum funding is **CAD 405**.

Every child receives 300 care minutes in each of the three declared states: normal, primary provider absent before care starts, or backup provider absent while the primary operates. Simultaneous absence, late-notice substitution and unacceptable locations are refused in separate tests. Declared qualifications and capacities are synthetic inputs, not live licenses.

Changing rosters alone does not make a provider commit. Pooling care alone does not repair the fixed-roster funding gap. The combined arrangement succeeds; a competent conventional coordinator with the same choices ties exactly.

## 4. The priced output is a twenty-day packet

The commercial policy charges **CAD 750** for a complete verified 20-day repeating-cohort package. The native constructor is rerun with a daily equivalent fee of CAD 37.50. Every day receives a case-specific input hash and a stronger-verifier check. Repetition is not counted as twenty independent generalization tests.

A stated allocation makes the economic participation calculation concrete. Each parent pays CAD 40/day toward primary care. Each employer pays its own workers and half the residual common obligations. Its maximum commitment is **CAD 128.75/day**, below CAD 145.

The total commitment is **CAD 8,350**: CAD 750 service fee and CAD 7,600 of segregated pass obligations. The latter includes CAD 4,200 of normal consumption and CAD 3,400 of backup reserve. In the all-normal month, the unused reserve is returned to the employer funders. The coordinator collects CAD 375 after the package is accepted and bears credit risk on CAD 375 due at thirty days. Provider/worker funding never finances the coordinator.

Sixty executed day/state checks verify **funding = consumption + refund**. These are simulated ledger identities. No account statement or actual settlement is implied.

## 5. Individual participation precedes the sale

The model deducts each employer's normal care contribution, expected backup use, reserve financing and coordination time before deciding whether half the service fee is attractive. Under the central low-provider-cost inputs, private monthly cost before the fee is CAD 696.875. After confidence, outside-option and liquidity adjustments, each employer needs about **CAD 1,353 of gross monthly enabled value** to support its CAD 375 share.

The study has not observed that value. Higher prices reduce modeled participation; CAD 1,500 exceeds the exact fee ceiling and fails. The frozen CAD 750 policy produces mean annual operating profit of CAD 4,735.56 in the central fresh simulation, with material losses and early wind-downs. The broad prior produces mean loss of CAD 1,367.74. Actual collections are zero.

Supplier cost can break the arrangement before a service sale is possible. A wage-anchored central opening estimate of CAD 212 raises the selected-priced daily commitment to **CAD 541.50**, above CAD 450. The program refuses. Higher employer ceilings restore technical fundability in a separately executed variant; its economics cannot inherit the original profit.

## 6. The evidence becomes an inspectable research venture

Actual native Insight functions construct a source-linked dossier. The native Nova-Seed draft stays plaintext, unchanged, unminted and unfunded. A separate annex records the witness, rights, uncertainty and evidence. Native FusionPlan functions commit four unsubmitted executable milestone specifications, now pointing to the corrected constructor and reproduction wrapper. MARK's local ABI/qualification checks reject claims of public deployment authority.

Research execution occurs through the supplied software. The native adapter does not execute a venture, submit jobs or move money. Hashes preserve integrity, not truth or an earned foresight claim.

## 7. A successor inherits code, not permission

A new mission renames all actors, changes work times, changes commute requirements and changes prices. The inherited instrument recomputes its meaning and produces a CAD 466 package; an underfunded counterpart refuses. Six further fresh local-recipient missions, generated only after a policy freeze and code handoff, produce identical complete documents after removing only `elapsed_seconds`, through copied authenticated-assessment code and an ordinary library: four offered, two refused.

The access fixture uses existing verifier roles and binds public code, rights, recipient, mission and expiry. Local research execution has its own user authorization; assessment signatures do not grant live execution authority. No live ENS identity or independent frontier-model expertise is claimed.

The illustrative retained-skill economics loses CAD 192 including assessment. Paid admission remains HOLD. What is inherited is a useful public instrument, its limitations, its counterexamples and a stronger checker. Every new customer, provider, worker and parent must supply fresh meaning and authority.
