# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — Executive brief

## The achievement

**A source-grounded opportunity search led to an executable way to turn fragmented work schedules into a funded care cohort.** The constructed package jointly chooses consenting workers' permitted rosters, preserves employer staffing, covers complete care and commute intervals, funds provider and worker obligations, and reserves a finite backup.

In the connected synthetic example, two employers, eight workers and four children need **CAD 405/day** under the joint arrangement, compared with **CAD 705/day** under fixed work rosters. Both face the same CAD 450 payer ceiling. All four children retain full modeled care coverage; employer staffing remains unchanged. The CAD 300 difference is CAD 150 less normal spending plus CAD 150 less refundable reserve. It is not coordinator profit.

An independently implemented oracle found **16 of 48 fresh cases** feasible only with the joint permissions and commitments. A competent conventional coordinator given the same formulation ties. The contribution is an explicit, checked arrangement and its feasible region. Historical novelty, superior discovery and beyond-human foresight are unestablished.

## The commercial opportunity—and its conditions

The priced service is a **verified 20-day coordination and commitment package**, not physical child care. The selected price is **CAD 750**, half collected at acceptance and half due thirty days later. Provider, worker and backup funds are separate: CAD 7,600 of maximum pass funding, including CAD 3,400 of refundable backup reserve.

This is **modeled profitability under the stated low-provider-cost, demand and acquisition assumptions**. The central fifth-to-ninety-fifth world-percentile profit range is **−CAD 1,999 to CAD 11,174**, and **22.3%** wind down early. Under a broad uncertainty prior, mean profit is **−CAD 1,368**. Four leads per month, higher acquisition cost, weak buyer value or low technical eligibility can overturn the case.

Supplier cost is particularly decisive. The original CAD 150 fixed opening is close to a low wage-based estimate. A central wage-anchored estimate of **CAD 212** makes the selected-priced daily package cost **CAD 541.50**, above the original CAD 450 collective ceiling. Higher willing employer commitments can restore technical fundability; the original profit result does not transfer to that case. No supplier quote, actual demand or physical care outcome is claimed.

## What the discovery comparison says

Three isolated runs shared the same broad question, initial evidence, rubric and tool budget. Ordinary frontier-model search, expert AGI ALPHA search and a conventional expert-method **model proxy** each produced four candidates and two provisional survivors. The selected care opportunity was not supplied in the brief. One run per arm, subjective internal scoring and no actual human participant support no superiority claim.

Flexible care reservations, employer cooperatives, roster accommodations and integrated childcare software already exist. The study's narrow construction joins parents' work rosters across independent employers with provider opening, funding and finite fallback obligations. An existing coordinator delivering the equivalent arrangement at comparable cost remains a direct falsifier of market novelty.

## What was actually built

The complete package includes an exact constructor, separate checker, independent exhaustive oracle, adversarial tests, all frozen cases and failures, corrected verification, a 20-day allocation and authority packet, settlement conservation checks, full commercial worlds and a human-led development reference.

The original 42-case suite offered five arrangements and refused thirty-seven, including **all twenty-four broad mixed-constraint cases**. Their care windows, travel, staffing or other restrictions often made the purpose impossible. An independent 48-case design answered a different question and found thirty-nine feasible arrangements, sixteen newly feasible. These samples are not pooled into a market success rate.

The audit found and preserved eight accepted corruptions in the first standalone verifier. After a separate source freeze, the corrected instrument matches 12/12 new independent cases (6 offered, 6 refused) and rejects 20/20 new corruptions. It also rejects all 8 previously accepted corruptions. Software construction is not permission to operate care: each new mission must recheck its meaning and obtain separate employer, worker, parent and provider grants.
