## What the new study should inherit

### 1. Transformational Intelligence

Source: [exact TeX](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/47c17f6aca6b1bb8779dd3e47d5ba5f8af3329f4/docs/Transformational_Intelligence.tex).

Separate authorized purpose, inherited means, and measurement instrument. A transformation consists of a changed formulation, its construction, realization, and a witness describing the removable restriction and falsifier. Changing a convenient proxy cannot erase the original useful purpose. Report discovery, execution, and technical attribution separately.

The actual illustrative result is exact under a supplied two-world causal model: passive accuracy is at most 50%, while a capped intervention policy attains 8191/8192 with at most twelve queries. A conventional unrestricted optimal planner matches the policy. No frontier-model trial, new causal algorithm, autonomous frame discovery, or universally superior architecture is established.

The useful reusable method is the purpose bridge plus strong same-permission controls, bounded complementary construction, cost accounting, and fresh confirmation. Its minimum lifecycle denominator includes search, construction, confirmation, migration, execution, verification, and maintenance. A faster inner loop alone is insufficient. Hundreds of cases within one supplied campaign are not hundreds of independent discoveries.

### 2. Invent the Economy

Source: [exact TeX](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/47c17f6aca6b1bb8779dd3e47d5ba5f8af3329f4/docs/Invent_the_Economy.tex).

The object is a capability plus counterparties, arrangement, financing schedule, rights, and evidence. Technical possibility, modeled viability, accepted arrangement, realized exchange, and repeatable market are distinct evidential stages. The supplied model combines shared construction with coordinated commitments; each alone is unprofitable under the restricted starting arrangement. An unrestricted conventional entrepreneur given the same choices ties.

The four hypothetical buyers each pay $300k after success, the core costs $600k, adaptations cost $75k each, success probability is 0.8, and opening cash is $900k. Successful contribution is $300k; expected contribution is $120k. A $40k per-delivery route burden reverses the expected gain. These are illustrative monetary interpretations of exact supplied parameters, not quotes or actual sales. The independent event checker agrees across 2,240 parameter cases.

Use the distinction between profitability and every-prefix cash feasibility. Refundable principal, seed capital, and pass-through funds are not earned revenue. A positive joint surplus does not prove individual participation or truthful revelation. Shared capabilities can create correlated failure: four beneficiaries do not diversify a common failure into four independent risks. Token settlement activity is not token valuation or productive surplus.

The implementation reuses generic, content-bound module checks while recomputing every mission input/output and granting authority separately. Its compiler reuses graph structure but changes algebra from weighted bill-of-materials demand to Boolean remediation impact. Generic tests fall from 1,600 to 64; all counted reference steps fall 88.85%. The competent direct specialist has fewer candidate events, lower pooled CPU and better final median wall time. Preserve the practical inheritance rule: reuse stable structure, recheck mission meaning, regrant authority. Current authority is not inherited from a historical receipt. Local instrument tests do not qualify production-host execution, human service parity, or new monetary evidence.

## Geospatial opportunity lineage and distinct contribution

Operational Alpha2.1.0 screened a geospatial elevation-reuse candidate (ENG1) but did not select it. It downloaded twelve NOAA metadata packages totaling2,084,023 bytes and two NcML headers, finding conflicts between titles, abstracts and CRS labels. One source describes clamping elevations below0.5m to0.5m above MHW, a noninvertible step. That study did not inspect every raster cell, validate flood decisions, or establish paying demand. Its choice was scientific-model reuse instead.

A new complete mixed-format/mixed-CRS delivery is therefore best described as an explicit continuation of that deferred opportunity direction with a new scoped deliverable and fresh executable evidence. Distinguish horizontal point/feature interoperability from vertical-datum/elevation assurance; do not let coordinate transformation prove physical accuracy, regulatory suitability or flood safety. Existing GIS pipelines such as GDAL/PROJ/QGIS/FME remain strong prior art and controls. Novelty, if any, is the joined service design, purpose-preserving acceptance package and economics, not reprojection.

## Suggested framing for the new paper

Lead with the strongest conditional commercial result of the newly matched service. Put modeled price, equivalent human scope, quality assumptions, complete costs, acquisition, collection risk and capacity beside the headline. Report executed core acceptance independently from simulated mission success. Report E-U and E+-E separately, including zero or negative operational gains and ordinary-library parity. Retention may be profitable only above an explicit reuse threshold, or rejected entirely, without erasing a viable E baseline. The next validation should measure the most profit-sensitive unknown with the smallest paired scoped trial; no mandatory live sale or supplier quote is needed to complete this research edition.
