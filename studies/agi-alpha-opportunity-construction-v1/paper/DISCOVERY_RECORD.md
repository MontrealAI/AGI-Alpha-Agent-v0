# Discovery record

## Question and evidence boundary

What useful possibility could AGI ALPHA construct that is not supplied as the answer in the brief?
The initial packet described consequential needs, stranded resources, coordination barriers and changes in
agent capability. It excluded the already explored battery, microscopy and manufacturing examples from the
flagship selection. It did not name private reserve-pool feasibility, multiparty computation or a target profit.

The comparison consists of four actual hosted frontier-model search completions: ordinary use (U) and expert
operation (E) in each of two rounds. Each completion had its own context. The inherited hosted profile was the
same; its exact backend revision, token consumption and monetary billing were not exposed. This is a small
exploratory comparison, not an estimate of a population effect or a human-ceiling result.

## Frozen inputs and budgets

The first common packet and protocol were committed locally before either search output. Each arm could make
six primary-source web queries, use roughly six minutes, and return at most 1,800 words. Both received the same
criteria: customer value (25%), unresolved gap (20%), plausibility (15%), testability (15%), commercial feasibility
(15%) and reusable capability (10%). These ordinal judgments are aids to selection; they are not calibrated
probabilities or valuations. The ordinary prompt asked for competent opportunity search and a small probe.
The expert prompt added explicit restriction/resource decomposition, recombination, outside options and
adversarial prior-art checking. Both could use tools and reject ideas.

The first review removed arm labels and self-scores and randomized candidate IDs. The result was **label masked**,
not fully blind: prose and source clusters could reveal an arm. Four additional primary-source queries checked
the most consequential collisions. All six first-round ideas were rejected as flagships in their proposed form.

A second common packet supplied that same feedback to both new contexts, raised the query cap to eight and the
word cap to 2,000, and asked for a missing representation, instrument or capability. The intended final application
remained absent. This is adaptive exploration with a newly frozen packet, not a replay of an untouched first test.

The exact packets, prompts, source logs, output files, commitments and review records accompany this document.
Local timestamps and hashes bind a recorded sequence and bytes; they do not independently prove temporal
priority, truth or the absence of prior human invention.

## Probes and what they actually established

The ordinary first-round reserve probe used invented compatibility and ten single/double events. It illustrated
how two hypothetical spares cover only eight events; an invented universal spare covered ten. It did not establish
physical transformer compatibility, procurement demand or an uncovered market.

The expert relocation probe enumerated 24,576 hypothetical combinations. Three met the supplied constraints;
its selected eight-household example required a $2 million bridge and about $248,603 in sponsor cost, together
with full principal-loss protection. A tighter timing or withdrawal condition broke feasibility. These are synthetic
construction facts, not a funded transaction or a legal conclusion.

The second-round junction probe evaluated 36,864 synthetic junctions over 1,024 reference pairs. It found no
false merges or splits under its supplied kernel. A small worked case showed six false combinations when a
coupled ambiguity was represented by independent intervals. The relevant methods are known; this did not
establish biological performance or a novel genomic instrument.

The second-round semantic probe reproduced four known finite-field alias patterns and round-tripped 10,008
values through an established two-limb representation. It justified preserving application meaning across an
interface, not claiming a new cryptographic primitive.

## The selected connection

The researcher-agent then combined the ordinary search's reserve-coordination need with the second round's
emphasis on preserving meaning under a representation boundary. Primary-source checks supplied a third
component: established secure multiparty computation can evaluate a coupled allocation function without
centralizing raw inputs. The selected application became **confidential pre-emergency reserve-pool feasibility**.

The proposed information product asks whether every declared simultaneous-demand scenario can be met using
owner-asserted eligibility, scenario availability and asset-specific withholding. Its output is one agreed bit per
independent input epoch, accompanied by public scope and execution evidence. It does not grant a reserve,
identify an asset to dispatch, certify equipment, or solve a physical shortage.

This narrower output differs from SPAREConnect's documented decentralized contact and emergency-request
function. Grid Assurance already offers dedicated reserve access and logistics. Those distinctions justify testing
an application; they do not prove that either organization cannot build it or that buyers need it. GAO's evidence
supports the wider reserve problem and barriers facing smaller utilities. It does not establish privacy as the
binding barrier. The latter is an explicit market hypothesis.

The independent cross-round review issued a conditional go for construction and identified a strong prior-art
collision: Beaude and colleagues already study private, coupled resource allocation using secure computation
(SIAM Journal on Optimization, DOI 10.1137/19M127879X). Prime Match also demonstrates production private
inventory matching, with a stronger malicious-security model. Consequently the contribution is a scoped
application construction and evidence package. Confidential allocation itself is not new.

## Attribution and discovery result

The selected application was not in the initial brief or any raw arm's final recommendation. It is a disclosed
cross-round synthesis. It therefore cannot be credited as a win by E over U. Both arms supplied useful material,
and expert search's higher structure did not establish superior foresight in this sample. No human-expert search
trial was run; a separate human-led development estimate is not a substitute for one.

The researcher-agent supplied the final three-party, six-asset, six-demand scope, Hall-condition formulation,
independent matching oracle, generator, seed schedule, acceptance criteria and commercial policy family.
Implementation agents constructed and checked that supplied design. Their correct execution is evidence for
the instrument, not independent rediscovery of the opportunity or low-level algorithm.

This record preserves a genuine discovery process and a testable application hypothesis. It does not establish
"beyond human foresight," "pinpoint accuracy," a world-first invention, or an enormous market valuation.
