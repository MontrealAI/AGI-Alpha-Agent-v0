# Executive brief

## A collective answer without a central raw inventory

AGI ALPHA constructed a confidential reserve-planning instrument. Three owners can jointly test whether their
declared assets cover every agreed simultaneous-demand scenario while keeping raw eligibility, availability and
withholding inputs at their respective computation roles. The output is a scoped assessment for a funding decision.

**The frozen instrument matched an independent allocation oracle on 240/240 fresh synthetic worlds**, including
120 separately executed successor cases with the harder 41-scenario question. An ordinary library with identical
information tied exactly. The full business simulation selects **no launch** at the tested demand and readiness
assumptions: a $12,000 assessment has positive variable contribution, but the complete venture loses money.

### Why a customer might care

A group considering shared reserve arrangements may need to know whether further engineering and contracting
are worth financing. Separate bilateral availability checks can miss conflicting claims on the same asset. A
centralized analysis is a good alternative when owners accept disclosure. The proposed service targets only groups
that accept a narrowly defined collective answer while retaining their raw inputs.

GAO documents reserve-transformer challenges and participation barriers for smaller utilities. SPAREConnect
already offers decentralized contacts and voluntary sharing, while Grid Assurance offers dedicated reserves and
logistics. The scoped pre-emergency information product is distinct from those documented services. A
privacy-sensitive paying segment remains a hypothesis. [1–3]

### What was discovered and constructed

Two ordinary and two expert frontier-model searches generated twelve possibilities from frozen common inputs.
The first six were rejected after prior-art checks; the second round sharpened representation and instrument
questions. A disclosed researcher-agent synthesis combined the reserve need with established private computation.
The study does not award expert search a victory or claim that humans could not find the opportunity.

The executable package contains three actual MPyC processes, an independent allocation oracle, complete
single/pair and single/pair/triple scenario families, adversarial examples, source/input commitments and local
one-use authorization using existing AGI ALPHA trust and Journal components. The Nova-Seed and FusionPlan
preserve the opportunity, work specifications and evidence. MARK checks remain local and unfunded.

### Economics for the same offering

The sponsor buys one complete assessment for three owners. A correctly computed negative answer is accepted
delivery; it may prevent premature planning expenditure. Prices of $6,000/$12,000/$18,000, payment terms, direct
launch, conditional commitments and no launch are compared using the same finite prospective pools.

The central repeat-delivery budget is $1,200 plus modeled retries and payment effects. Startup readiness is
separate: $9,547/$25,908/$74,526 low/central/high, including independent security review and owner-controlled
deployment. A competent human-led version of the same research fixture is estimated at $4,016/$10,948/$29,135;
live-pilot readiness is a different, larger scope. Neither estimate is a market selling price or a measured AI saving.

Across 1,000 fresh 180-day modeled worlds, the best tested direct offer from development—$12,000 with a fully
refundable deposit—produces **$9,324 contribution per accepted-equivalent**, including failed-job variable costs.
Its **mean full-venture loss is $30,147**, with 5th–95th percentiles of −$41,176 to −$8,816. Mean accepted volume
is only 0.218 assessments; 31.8% reach readiness. Even after successful startup is treated as sunk, ready-world
service operation averages a $6,932 loss after acquisition, business and insurance costs.

A separately frozen follow-up of all nine conditional-commitment policies also launches zero ventures on another
1,000 worlds. Timing and the limited pipeline dominate: at $18,000, four sufficiently valuable timely commitments
can satisfy the controlled admission test, but the simulated pipeline never assembles them. Screening still costs
about $3,510. There is no profitable launch region in these tested scenario averages.

At day 180 the direct policy retains $29,889.53 of modeled cash from a $60,000 seed, but only $28,581.12 is
available after support and reserves. That is depleted financing, not earned surplus. Actual collections are **$0**.
The useful commercial result is a concrete admission threshold and a reason to validate a coalition before
committing startup capital.

### What a successor receives

It receives an executable instrument, its exact meaning, licenses, dependencies, counterexamples and evidence.
The successor's triple-demand mission requires a new check and new authority. The ordinary identical-library
control receives the same knowledge. No incremental retained Alpha, graph moat, closed-model training or
superhuman specialization is established; optional Gym spending is zero in the primary policy.

### The next decision

For one explicitly consenting three-owner group, obtain its actual next-decision budget and outside option,
agree the one-bit query, time input preparation and seek an independent deployment/review quote plus a
conditional paid-assessment commitment. Stop if the information cannot change a funded decision or a trusted
broker adequately serves the group. This small validation calibrates the unresolved economic condition.

**Evidence boundary.** Actual local computation; public synthetic inputs; modeled commercial conditions. Existing
MPC and matching methods are prior art. No independent utility deployment, collected revenue, proven market,
live ENS grant or token transaction is claimed. Complete actual research expenditure is unknown.
