# Public synthetic data

Every input in this directory is generated by `src/study.py` from the frozen protocol. The three `private/party_*.json` files represent owner-private inputs to separate MPC processes, but are deliberately public research fixtures. The research supervisor has all of them for the independent exhaustive allocation oracle.

There are three owners, two distinct padded asset slots per owner and six unit-demand locations. Eligibility, scenario-specific availability and asset-specific withholding are binary declarations. Their probabilities are stress-generator assumptions, not measured utility populations or equipment failure rates. The public scenario family is complete for the chosen maximum demand count. The original and successor seeds and cohort sizes are in `config/technical_protocol.json`.

No customer inventory, personal data, protected evaluation answer or live organizational identity is used. Expected answers and held-out fixtures became public only after the recorded source freeze and execution. Once published, these seeds are reproduction fixtures; new claims of prospective learning require a new protocol and fresh independent work.

These newly generated synthetic fixtures are distributed with the study under Apache-2.0. This does not grant rights to use the MONTREAL.AI mark or to operate equipment, connect to private systems or commit another organization's resources.
