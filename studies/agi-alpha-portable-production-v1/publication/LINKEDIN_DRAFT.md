🌌♾️ **AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — When Capability Creates Opportunity**

The question behind AGI ALPHA is demanding:

What important productive capability can we construct that makes previously inaccessible work possible—and who benefits from its first useful instance?

Our latest bounded study investigated five directions, from obsolete-component replacement to manufacturing interoperability.

We built an executable nominal-CAD artifact. After preserving and correcting an initial failure, it reproduced three public source geometries and passed three fresh geometric requests. The code, source identities, failed evidence and economic scenarios are available for inspection.

The decisive finding came from the comparison: established tools and the original free source already generated the central nominal geometry. The new recipe also lost some original dimensional relationships.

That means the engineering result is useful supporting work. It does not establish a transformational enterprise.

The financial model reaches the same boundary. A favorable scenario becomes profitable through selective follow-on work and shared preparation, but a conventional provider with the same resources has the identical modeled result. Actual customer collections remain zero.

The next milestone must begin with a real beneficiary, a binding production constraint, and an accepted outcome that the strongest existing alternative cannot already deliver within the required cost or time.

AGI ALPHA's ambition remains to expand what humanity can discover, construct and economically accomplish. Its evidence standard must be strong enough to stop an attractive claim when the comparison fails.
