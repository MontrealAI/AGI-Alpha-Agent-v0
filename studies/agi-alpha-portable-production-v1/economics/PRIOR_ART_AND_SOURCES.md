# Adversarial prior-art and claim boundary

The strongest challenger for the executed public examples is their original CadQuery program. It is already open, editable and parameterized. A new representation which reproduces one solid while dropping source dependencies is not an advance in recovered design intent.

Formally, agreement P(theta0) = R(phi0) for a nominal design does not establish P(theta) = R(g(theta)) for edits. Original dependencies such as bolt coordinates computed from plate length must either survive explicitly or the claimed capability must be narrowed to supplied variants. The current study adopts the narrower nominal-conformance scope.

## Primary sources reviewed

- **CadQuery introduction:** https://cadquery.readthedocs.io/en/latest/intro.html . CadQuery directly supports scripted parametric models, STEP output, source-level editing and feature-relative modeling. It supplies the strongest free alternative for these examples. Retrieved 2026-10-28 UTC.
- **STEP-NC AP238:** https://www.steptools.com/stds/stepnc/ . The published representation connects CAD design requirements with CAM working steps and device-independent information. It defeats a broad claim that explicit portable manufacturing semantics are newly invented here. STEP Tools' performance/promotional claims are not independently reproduced in this study.
- **CAD-Recode (ICCV 2025):** https://cad-recode.github.io/ and https://github.com/filaPro/cad-recode . Prior art reconstructs executable CadQuery scripts from point clouds and supports subsequent editing. Its reported benchmark results do not establish exact functional equality for this study, and its model was not run here.
- **CADFit (ICML 2026):** https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/nehme26a.html . Prior art reconstructs editable programs through geometric feedback with extrusions, revolutions, fillets and chamfers. It demonstrates that reconstruction of richer editable operations is an active, already developed capability. No superiority over CADFit is measured.
- **NIST digital thread:** https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/digital-thread-manufacturing . Established STEP AP242 semantic PMI, conformance work and manufacturing information integration further constrain novelty. The existing repository's #4980 already covers NIST/MTC manufacturing inspection evidence.

## Correct comparison

Provide every arm the same original source, nominal STEP, feature definitions, explicit edit requests, frozen tests and permissions. Native source and a competent conventional shared library are mandatory controls. Withholding native source to manufacture a source-recovery gap would invalidate the comparison.

The proposed deliverable may remain a conventional conformance/test artifact. Passing the finite geometry checks alone supports neither manufacturing release, general editability, recovered semantic intent, nor a newly feasible production process. No independent vendor tool comparison or patent-priority survey has been executed.

## Economic anchors

- **BLS Mechanical Engineers OOH:** https://www.bls.gov/OOH/architecture-and-engineering/mechanical-engineers.htm . Retrieved official page reports May 2025 median annual wage USD104,110 and hourly wage USD50.05. The modeled USD100 loaded rate is an explicit assumption; it is not a quote.
- **2022 Economic Census, EC2200BASIC, NAICS 332710:** https://data.census.gov/table/ECNBASIC2022.EC2200BASIC?q=332710 . Primary-source indexed table reports 16,876 firms and 17,163 establishments. Direct text open shows a JavaScript-only shell; the retrieved primary-source table text supplies the counts. Exact table vintage is retained.
- **2023 County Business Patterns profile:** https://data.census.gov/profile/332710_-_Machine_shops?codeset=naics~332710 . Primary-source indexed profile reports 17,156 establishments. This different vintage is disclosed and not mixed into the frozen 2022 denominator.

The establishment count grounds a scale threshold only. It does not establish which establishments use these software tools, need this artifact, can pay, or will adopt. No current market share, observed willingness to pay or enterprise valuation is derived from it.
