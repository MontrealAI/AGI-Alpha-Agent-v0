# Conditional economics and staged decisions

**Decision: no commercial or flagship admission.** The constructed examples have freely available native parameterized CadQuery source. A nominal reconstruction does not create a missing source-access capability, recover parameter dependencies, or establish buyer value. The proposed USD1,200 digital conformance/test outcome is an untested custom-work offer, not the price of these tutorial examples.

Actual customers, commitments and collections are zero. Actual full study cost and actual profit are unknown. All amounts below are USD. The model is a reproducible conditional calculation, not observed demand.

## Reproduce

From this directory, use a new output directory each time:

```sh
python model.py --output replay
python audit.py --output replay
```

Only the standard library is needed. The run refuses changes to the frozen inputs, protocol, model and audit. `freeze.json` records the pre-result seal at 2026-10-28T01:42:30Z. Current executed output is `results-r1/`; it contains exact-rational summaries, every finite weighted history in compressed CSV, financing calculations and a separate arithmetic reconstruction.

The main study has 36 arrangement/policy/scenario cells and 7,122 finite histories. The additional frozen grid has 48 price/capital cells. The audit passes 143,017 arithmetic and scope assertions and all 12 matched conventional/proposed shared cells. These are accounting checks, not customer missions.

Source dependency loss remains disclosed. One correction and bounded digital support are included. There is no inference of tolerances or original design intent, G-code release, physical part, manufacturing qualification or general service deployment.

The USD100 loaded hourly labor rate is an assumption anchored to the BLS May 2025 mechanical-engineer median wage of USD50.05/hour, not a supplier quote. Human-service examples of 6, 16 and 28 hours are assumptions for hypothetical custom work; they do not describe effort needed for the public tutorials. They include an engineering/integration outcome and responsibility, not a price for free source code.

Loaded labor is assumed to cover ordinary employment and facilities overhead. The finite model explicitly counts acquisition, failed attempts, corrections, permissions, compute, support, qualification, contracted refunds and unpaid balances. Unknown insurance/license obligations or additional engineering requirements remain blockers to a real offer; the calculation is complete only within its stated obligation assumptions. The USD2,400 historical build is illustrative, not a measurement of this study. Actual historical development is not silently priced at zero or charged again as fictional research.

## Three separate accounts

The provider account distinguishes nominal accepted revenue, collections, bad-debt expense, resource costs, prospective profit, historical build cost and unrestricted cash. A customer's written-off balance is not assumed forgiven.

The customer account uses the strongest feasible conventional outside option and subtracts the contracted price and buyer integration effort; failed attempts incur buyer effort without benefit. Current tutorial examples retain the free original source and doing nothing as outside options.

The venture-scale account is a separate population-based threshold. It never enters expected values as a speculative terminal valuation.

## Profitability and financeability

The first booked attempt requires at least **USD1,542 unrestricted initial capital** under the frozen worst-case reservation. That includes acquisition, preparation, full execution, a correction, nonrecoverable deposit fees, refund administration and a USD150 minimum cash reserve.

The 50% deposit remains refundable and restricted until final digital acceptance. It cannot reduce the initial funding requirement. Accepted work earns the fee at day 21, the balance is due at day 51, and funded support closes at day 66. The three prospect slots are 90 days apart. Final balances can default; their write-off remains a cost.

No credit facility is assumed available. Financing sensitivities show USD45.63 or USD91.25 for 90 days of interest on USD1,542 at assumed 12% or 24% annual rates. Those are arithmetic sensitivities, not financing offers. Equity opportunity cost is not measured. A separately accepted preparation milestone could change cash timing only through a new useful scope and buyer agreement.

## Optional retention and specialists

An ordinary library already holds the identical reusable asset. No additional retained-agent saving has been demonstrated. Additional extraction/validation of USD600 plus three months of USD50 maintenance costs USD750; the incremental retained result is therefore **-USD750**, and retention is rejected.

At ten fresh reuses, 80% usability and the assumed USD100 loaded hourly rate, the extra asset would need to save more than 15/16 hour per usable reuse **beyond the same-asset ordinary library**. This is a break-even threshold, not an observed saving. Gym training and live ENS authentication are not run.

## Billion-plus threshold, not a market claim

The 2022 U.S. Economic Census reports 17,163 employer establishments and 16,876 firms in NAICS 332710 Machine shops. Establishments are not necessarily eligible buyers. The 2023 County Business Patterns figure of 17,156 is a different vintage and is not substituted into this model.

At an assumed USD250 net customer benefit after price and integration, USD1 billion over ten years requires four million accepted outcomes. The frozen adoption ramp from 0.5% to 30% yields 1.145 cumulative adoption-years per establishment. It would require **203.55 accepted outcomes per active shop per year**. Twenty outcomes per active shop-year yields **USD98.26 million**, about one tenth of the threshold. Even immediate 100% adoption for all ten years requires 23.31 outcomes per shop-year.

At four million outcomes the hypothetical provider fees total USD4.8 billion at USD1,200 each. That is conditional revenue at the threshold volume, not obtainable revenue, profit or valuation. The finite cohort, not the establishment population, supplies the only modeled obtainable revenue. There is no grounded adoption path from the current tutorial artifacts to this scale, so billion-plus enterprise admission is rejected.

## Staged decision

1. **Preserve the engineering result:** nominal equivalence and explicit supplied variants within the frozen finite scope; preserve dependency-loss failures.
2. **Stop flagship admission:** native parameterized source already provides the stronger capability, and the conventional shared control ties.
3. **Hold a paid pilot:** require a real buyer-defined unmet conformance/test need, input rights, acceptance criteria, correction terms, adequate responsibility/insurance and qualified delivery scope. None is inferred from public examples.
4. **Require funding before a booked attempt:** apply the unrestricted-capital reservation; a refundable deposit is not available working capital.
5. **After an accepted pilot, decide again:** compare stopping with a finite next block using actual customer and cost evidence, then check cash separately.
6. **Retain only incremental improvements:** fresh recipient benefit must exceed extraction/upkeep versus an ordinary library holding the identical asset. Historical evidence grants no new authority.

No customer contact, contract, payment, spending, production deployment, authentication or social posting occurs in this model.
