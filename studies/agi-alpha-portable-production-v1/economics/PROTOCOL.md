# Frozen prospective economics protocol — PP-ECO-20261027-R1

This is a conditional commercial model for a conventional **digital engineering package**. No flagship is admitted. No physical part, tolerances, manufacturing release or customer acceptance is claimed. Actual customers, commitments and collections are zero; complete actual study cost and profit are unknown.

## Scope and three accounts

The proposed hypothetical fee is USD1,200 per finally accepted source-bound nominal CAD conformance/test artifact with explicitly supplied variants. Current public tutorial source is already freely parameterized; the reconstruction loses parameter dependencies and does not recover design intent. This is supporting engineering, not a newly admitted productive offering. The acceptance scope, 50% refundable restricted deposit, correction obligation, payment dates and data permissions are fixed in inputs.json. A geometry fit alone does not establish recovered design intent or manufacturing fitness. All costs and scenario probabilities except the stated external wage and population anchors are analyst assumptions.

Provider results distinguish nominal accepted service revenue, cash collections, written-off final balances, complete modeled prospective cost, prospective profit, illustrative full-history profit after one USD2,400 build charge, cash and unearned deposits/reserves. The illustrative build charge does not estimate actual historical work. Customer results use the strongest feasible conventional alternative, deduct the fee and integration effort, charge failed-attempt effort, and retain uncertainty about value. Future venture scale is a separate threshold, never a terminal EV term.

## Matched arrangements

Separate preparation repeats USD600 prospective commercial preparation per booked order. Conventional shared and proposed shared use **the identical generic asset**, pay preparation once, receive identical information and execute identical technical work. They must tie exactly. This is a test of ordinary shared preparation, not an exclusive AI advantage. A reusable public library is an explicit free software outside option; the reference cost is integration/engineering responsibility for the matched accepted outcome, not a fee for otherwise free software.

## Event model and buyer behavior

A zero-incremental-value control represents the actual public-source outside option. The other three value distributions refer only to hypothetical future commissioned work, not the tutorial examples. Three prospective buyer slots are separated by 90 days; this deliberately avoids assuming unlimited parallel delivery. Each candidate first incurs acquisition cost. A buyer's reference outside-option value is one of three frozen values. Purchase eligibility requires nonnegative expected net benefit using the disclosed prior probability of acceptance: p*(value-price-integration) - (1-p)*failed-attempt-loss >= 0. Procurement may still refuse. The latent common state persists across all orders, inducing correlated procurement and qualification failure.

For a booked order, outcomes are clean acceptance, acceptance after the single correction, or failure after correction. Accepted outcomes independently either pay the final balance or default on it. A write-off is a provider expense, not proof the customer's obligation or benefit changed. All fees, failed delivery, acquisition, administration, support and correction costs remain charged. No conventional provider is denied the revised formulation or reusable asset.

## Cash and authority

Capital is not revenue. Deposits remain restricted until final digital acceptance. Failed deposits are refunded and never recognized as revenue. Accepted support is fully paid from a dedicated reserve. Before taking a deposit, a fixed worst-case reservation includes prospective preparation, complete execution, one correction, nonrecoverable deposit fee, refund administration and the minimum unrestricted reserve. An undercapitalized mission is declined. The model uses owner equity only; debt-cost thresholds are separate sensitivities, not available funding.

## Executable policies

1. Stop now: no incremental action or spending.
2. Deliver first only: acquire and, if buyer participation and cash allow, attempt the first outcome; then stop.
3. Deliver selectively: execute the same first slot. Update the common-state probabilities from the actual observable event. Expand to the remaining two slots only if their exact posterior expected incremental profit, including coordination, is positive under the same cash gate. Historical build and already paid preparation are not charged a second time.

The policy cannot inspect the hidden common state or later outcomes. Rejected prospects and failed pilots provide information but no revenue. The first three slots are the full finite horizon, not a proxy for a hypothetical billion-dollar outcome.
