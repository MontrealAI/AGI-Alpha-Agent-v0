This protocol prepares the next decision without authorizing customer contact, expenditure or machine action. The current study stops the portable-production formulation as a flagship and holds commercial admission. No probabilities assigned to a speculative major enterprise rescue that decision.

## Gate 1 — Establish the unavailable outcome

A willing beneficiary must specify one useful outcome, what cannot currently be obtained, the affected productive activity, its deadline or cost ceiling, and the value of doing nothing. Record actual outside options, including an existing tool, conventional service, internal work and refusal to buy. Obtain the exact authorized source files, their revision, data rights and named acceptance role. Do not infer authority from a previous mission or public example.

**Stop:** no consequential beneficiary; no permission; the needed outcome is already available within the stated bound; or the remaining task is ordinary assistance without a newly enabled productive activity.

**Deliverable from this gate:** an inspectable case statement and conventional-challenger brief. No drawing, source code or design leaves the authorized scope merely to find demand.

## Gate 2 — Make the contract and cash reviewable

Include unsuccessful delivery, acquisition, permissions, integration, support, insurance/licensing and actual human work. Unknown obligations block admission rather than becoming zeros.

**Stop:** required funds are unavailable; the deposit is restricted and cannot fund obligations; acceptance cannot be specified; or necessary cost/responsibility remains unresolved.

## Gate 3 — Test the indispensable transformation

Give the strongest conventional challenger the identical objective, revised formulation, information, tools and resource budget. Distinguish a new arrangement from an algorithm or model-operation advance. Preserve the constraint that makes the outcome newly feasible. Narrow breadth to one meaningful instance, not to a toy that removes the hard part.

Before executing, fix discovery cases and hold out fresh confirmation cases. Preserve every failure and amendment. A changed input or criterion creates a new freeze. Independently reconstruct acceptance from detailed evidence; summary counts alone do not pass.

**Advance only if both tests pass:** the buyer receives a useful accepted outcome even if the venture stops; and the outcome establishes an indispensable capability that ordinary service delivery would not establish. Positive geometric checks alone failed the second test in Edition01.

A viable conventional business may remain, but that does not reinstate an exclusive transformational claim.
