# Legal Work Advantage — scope and offering assessment

Checked 26 October 2026. This is research design, not independent licensed legal assessment. The source ledger is `evidence.json`.

## Recommendation

Evaluate **NDA change reconciliation and versioned-playbook exception packets for an Ontario commercial lawyer** first. Keep full initial-document playbook review as the serious higher-value candidate, but require a separate measure of its verification burden. A useful recurring trigger is a counterparty's revised NDA or amendment arriving after counsel has established the intended baseline and business playbook. This buyer and workload remain a market hypothesis; no named customer or recurring paid demand has been established.

The intended outcome is a reviewable packet enabling counsel to determine which changes require attention and whether the draft is ready for a professional decision. It is not a legal opinion, negotiation, execution recommendation or complete client legal service. Each packet should include immutable input identifiers, exact source locations, change inventory, in-scope obligations, playbook deviations, preapproved-language candidates, unresolved cross-references, missing-context questions, and a professional review record. A case with unresolved material issues is escalated, not accepted as a completed substantive review.

## Supported research scope

Use English, text-native, bilateral business NDAs with explicit Ontario governing-law text, identifiable sections, and zero real client material. Use original synthetic text or attributed license-compatible public templates. State that playbook requirements are **declared business preferences for the experiment**, not statements of Ontario law or lawyer-approved defaults.

Cover confidential-information definitions, permitted use, permitted recipients, protective standard, enumerated exclusions, compelled disclosure, duration/survival, return/destruction and retained copies, governing-law/forum deviations, and amendment precedence. Unknown or ambiguous provisions receive an explicit unresolved status. Employment, consumer, personal-information transfers, IP licensing, securities, tax, restrictive covenants, litigation strategy, regulatory reporting and legal enforceability opinions are outside the supported scope.

Source grounding, exact spans, cross-reference existence, version identities and checksum preservation can be tested mechanically. Whether a clause creates a legally important obligation, whether an exception is appropriate, whether a proposed revision preserves intended legal meaning, and whether a contract is acceptable require qualified judgment. Separate **engineering packet acceptance** from **professional legal acceptance** in every ledger and display.

## Freshness and failure design

A capability carries jurisdiction, document family, playbook digest, input format, dependency versions, evidence references, assessment type and expiry/revalidation state. A historical cryptographic attestation proves what was attested; it does not prove that the interpretation remains applicable. Tests should change the playbook, create new amendments, remove a referenced provision, alter numbering, introduce contradictory survival text, insert an out-of-scope request, and make the declared law-scope metadata stale. Unsupported combinations must suspend the affected conclusion.

Severity should be declared before fresh evaluation. A high-severity missed obligation or unsupported substantive revision prevents a clean result regardless of aggregate extraction accuracy. False-positive burden matters because it consumes the lawyer's time. Escalation counts as safe handling when appropriate, but not as accepted full review or zero-cost delivery. Rejected and corrected attempts remain in cost and acceptance denominators.

## Interpretation of external productivity evidence

The cited lawyering study supports investigating human-assisted productivity. Its NDA task does not supply a positive speed or quality calibration for this offering. Current contract-review products already retain playbooks, so retained instructions alone cannot establish a unique AGI ALPHA method advantage. If commercial-product access or a qualified matched review is unavailable, record those arms as unexecuted; an internal approximation cannot be relabeled the strongest practical comparator.

The experiment can still establish reproducible source integrity, scoped automation, transfer and lifecycle behavior. Commercial viability remains conditional on equivalent professional outcomes, the actual residual review burden, realistic demand, and pricing against alternatives. This boundary should guide the scope of the claim rather than stop engineering work.
