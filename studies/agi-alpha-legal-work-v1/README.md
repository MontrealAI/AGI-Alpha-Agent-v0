# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL: The Legal Work Advantage

**Executed research result: the commercial flywheel is not established.** The first contract-playbook prototype failed whole-packet reliability. A bounded adaptation to exact plain-text reconciliation passed fresh technical checks, but human effort, professional acceptance, demand and full actual costs remain unmeasured. No actual customer collection or earned capability investment occurred.

This companion contains executable **source-linked NDA change-control and mechanical playbook support**, the complete original outputs, failures, independent factual evaluations, conditional economics, paper and bilingual demonstration. It is designed for qualified counsel reviewing original synthetic Ontario commercial mutual NDAs. The eight-rule research playbook is an authored policy, not a statement of Ontario law or a counsel-approved standard.

## What the professional user receives

The first-prototype packet contains the source documents and their SHA-256 identities, exact text changes, obligation passages, eight explicit playbook results with source offsets, unresolved references and amendments, and a review queue. Its insufficient reliability blocks a commercial claim. The narrower packet contains both complete source versions, an exact operation sequence, source hashes, uninterpreted amendment text and explicit review responsibilities. Neither automatically revises, negotiates, files or executes anything. Every professional-acceptance field remains `unknown`; no independent qualified professional is provisioned in this study.

The buyer hypothesis is an Ontario-qualified in-house commercial lawyer at a B2B software company receiving repeated counterparty NDA revisions. The recurring workload in this companion is an independently authored synthetic research cohort. No buyer interview, order, client recurrence or sale is represented as observed demand.

## Reproduce the production workflow

The production code uses Python and Node standard libraries. The actual local research runtime was Python 3.12 and Node 24.22.1; other versions and the repository's pinned Node 22.17.1 release environment were not qualified by this study.

From this directory:

```bash
python src/run_production.py --cohort primary --output replay/primary
node src/native_packaging.mjs replay/primary/packaging-manifest.json replay/primary/native-records.json
python reconciliation/reconcile.py replay/reconciliation
node reconciliation/package.mjs replay/reconciliation/packaging-manifest.json replay/reconciliation/native-records.json
```

Use a new output directory for each reproduction. The runner refuses to overwrite a campaign. The frozen primary campaign and its raw outputs are retained under `evidence/primary/`; replays do not create new scientific observations or customers.

The local Python analysis stage prepares the packet. The unchanged portable SUCCESSOR Workbench then verifies source hashes and exact source spans, invokes the existing Transformational Intelligence source-coverage instrument and produces the existing unfunded seven-job compilation. It retains `HOLD`, empty active authority and `NOT_CONNECTED` settlement. This two-stage arrangement is explicit: an attempted callback subprocess failed, and its source and failure are preserved under `history/failures/`. No execution restriction was bypassed. The signed-operator Harness and Fleet were not provisioned or invoked.

## Evidence boundaries

`protocol/production-freeze.json` binds the production source before fresh cases were generated. The case author did not read the implementations. Private factual annotations remained under `evaluation/private-keys/` until all production predictions were sealed. The final companion retains these annotations for independent reproduction; they are never read by production, skills or retrieval. The fixed evaluator measures an authored synthetic playbook and source integrity. It does not confer professional legal correctness.

Retained source defects and rejected outputs remain in the archive. Mechanical acceptance, professional acceptance, customer acceptance, simulated financial viability and actual cash collection are distinct fields. Monetary scenarios do not convert unknown authoring, inference, review or maintenance costs into measured zero cost. Actual collections and token financing are zero.

## Economics

The CAD sensitivity panel imports the existing canonical ledger and buyer-choice primitives. It distinguishes human replacement cost, capacity savings, avoidable cash expenditure, price, simulated collections, costs, unpaid obligations, reserves and available earned surplus. The reference does not assume human-time savings. Alternative scenarios are explicit conditions, not measured legal-service economics. No paid commitments, financial transactions or token proceeds are used.

The reference 26-week simulated cash net result is a mean loss of CAD 428.92, with zero collections. More permissive bundled scenarios still lose cash. The narrower offering's CAD 5/10/20 prices require approximately 3.54/7.08/14.16 minutes of saved professional time before integration at the declared wage-and-burden assumption; none of those savings was observed. Actual all-in research expenditure cannot be reconstructed from absent metering and invoices.

See the paper and evidence index for rejected attempts, missing measurements, cost categories, selection history, rounding errata and publication status. Historical research cost stays separate from prospective investment decisions; an unknown historical construction cost is not charged twice or treated as zero.
