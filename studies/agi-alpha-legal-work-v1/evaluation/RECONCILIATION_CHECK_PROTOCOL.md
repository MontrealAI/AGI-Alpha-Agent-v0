# Independent reconciliation confirmation

The second and final discovery prototype supplies exact plain-text version reconciliation for original synthetic research documents. It does not supply a playbook review, legal interpretation, a consolidated authoritative agreement, professional acceptance or a client decision. The first prototype, its failures and its frozen scorer remain unchanged.

## Frozen acceptance

The independent checker is constructed before new confirmation inputs and private keys are authored. It imports neither the producer nor a diff library. It binds the packet to the original case, checks the complete document and hash inventories, validates every source span using Python Unicode codepoints, and reconstructs both entire texts from ordered contiguous spans. Operation labels must match the actual strings: equal, replacement, insertion or deletion. Boolean offsets, gaps, overlaps, source substitution, omitted operations, duplicate amendments and interpreted amendment effects are rejected. Every amendment must remain separately and exactly preserved. Legal conclusions and proposed revisions must remain empty, and professional acceptance must remain unknown.

The fresh author may record construction edits but those edits do not define a unique permitted alignment. Different valid alignments of repeated or moved text are allowed. A whole-document replacement can pass conservation; the acceptance endpoint therefore does not prove useful change localization, minimal edit distance, semantic equivalence or reduced professional review time.

## Descriptive measurements

Before fresh generation, the checker declares operation counts, changed-operation counts, highlighted original and revised codepoints, unchanged codepoints, and the preserved-context ratio for each version. The ratio is unchanged codepoints divided by that version's total codepoints, with an empty version reported as 1. These measures describe the produced alignment. They impose no added acceptance threshold and do not establish redline usability. No method will be selected using private answers.

Production measurements remain separate from subsequent independent scoring and unmeasured professional review, correction, onboarding, support and maintenance. Both implementation methods use ordinary standard-library SequenceMatcher. Neither is a new retained legal capability, an observed Microsoft Word workflow, or an independent B/C replication.

## Controls and release order

Thirteen hand-authored positive and negative controls include Unicode with a variation selector, an empty insertion span, alternative complete segmentation, a false equal label, missing content and source substitution. The six previously public development cases are checked under both methods. Ruff and strict mypy verify the independent checker. Scope and corpus-plan hashes are bound in the checker's preconfirmation source freeze; the root subsequently binds this freeze into the global second-prototype freeze.

After the new corpus is authored, all 24 outputs from 12 cases and two methods must be sealed before private-key release. Original case identities and all frozen hashes must still match at scoring. Independent exact-text acceptance and unknown professional acceptance must be reported separately. Same-session independent authorship is not external professional evaluation.
