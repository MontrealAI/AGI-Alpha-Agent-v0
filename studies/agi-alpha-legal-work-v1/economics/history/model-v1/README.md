# Legal-work economics companion

This is an additive CAD scenario adapter for the existing AGI ALPHA shadow ledger. It imports the unchanged canonical `planMission`, `replayLedger` and `calculateInvestment`, and the unchanged inherited market's `chooseBuyer` and `randomDraw`. `inherited-manifest.json` identifies every reused byte. The inherited general market simulator is not invoked because it names unrelated software-package assets and USD.

## Evidence boundaries

The actual ledger records zero observed collections, unknown professional acceptance and unknown complete costs. Synthetic technical tests do not become legal acceptance. No model result is revenue, a customer, a paid pilot, or a financial action. No token issuance or appreciation enters the model.

Only executed stage runtimes, attempt counts and deterministic pass/failure observations enter from production. A technical failure affects a cost scenario only through the explicitly assumed correction minutes; the default is zero. Technical pass rates are **never** used as legal-accuracy probabilities.

Stages are B (newly constructed ordinary baseline), C1 (first retained definition/reference capability), C (C1 plus amendment capability), and D (independently constructed conventional alternative). A is the human-only economic workflow, using ordinary non-AI tools. Human task time, professional review, demand and commercial-software effectiveness were not observed. D is an executed conventional method; the separate commercially available AI-software alternative is an unexecuted generic proxy.

`sources.json` provides primary-source context and limits. Ontario's published lawyer wage anchors only an employee opportunity-cost sensitivity. It is not a billing rate. The vendor's older five-NDA issue-spotting duration is contextual and is not transferred to the complete Ontario review packet. Microsoft Word already has comparison; Draftable and Spellbook are practical alternatives. A commercially attractive offering must beat the buyer's relevant alternative after review and integration.

## What is charged

Provider marginal cost includes operator handling, explicit assumed corrections, compute allocation, tools and support. Provider recurring cost also includes acquisition, onboarding, weekly business expense and maintenance. Construction includes declared unsuccessful attempts. Fully allocated historical profit stays null unless a complete historical research-cost assumption is explicitly supplied; no unknown cost is replaced with zero.

Customer professional review and integration are separate from the provider's costs and included in total buyer burden. A failed simulated attempt earns no invoice, keeps its provider cost and incurs professional review plus a complete human fallback. Time released from a salaried professional is opportunity value; avoidable payroll cash is zero by default. Compute is rounded to accounting cents at the assumed rate; small measured execution times do not establish a free service.

The canonical ledger represents each simulated week as an accounting period, not a legally accepted case. Its `price` assumption contains simulated collections. `collected` is always zero. The opening seed stays outside revenue. A fixed reliability reserve remains unavailable for the entire horizon. All modeled expenses are paid immediately; payment delay and noncollection affect receipts. No debt is silently created to fund adoption.

## Investment and change

The reinvestment strategy starts with B. It compares C1 with D under the same declared first-construction cost, chooses the larger positive modeled benefit, and can spend only earned surplus after costs and reserve. It forecasts reuse from past simulated accepted work, charges a service pause, and applies a finite useful lifetime. Second investment in C requires subsequent productive use of C1 and direct modeled repayment of its construction and per-use upkeep; possession of excess seed is insufficient. A new skill cannot extend its dependency's expiry. Once the dependency expires, production returns to B.

The campaign pairs worlds using deterministic keyed draws and reports downside distributions. Its intervals summarize hypothetical worlds, not statistical confidence in market profitability. `investmentSensitivity` reports break-even reuse over correction-time, upkeep and reuse assumptions. Those direct-cost thresholds exclude business/demand effects, which are included in the paired market scenario. Comparing complete scenario profit with the baseline captures the cost of paused delivery; direct-cost repayment alone is a narrower result.

The predefined reference assumes no unmeasured professional-time improvement and no assumed technical-correction savings. Other scenarios explicitly vary those missing quantities. Favorable sensitivity conditions must not be promoted to measured productivity, calibrated demand, professional acceptance, or recovered historical research cost.
