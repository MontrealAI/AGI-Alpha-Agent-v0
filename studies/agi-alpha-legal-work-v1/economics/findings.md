# Legal-work economic findings

**The broad playbook-review offering did not establish an economically attractive accepted-delivery service or a financeable capability succession.** B, C1 and C each passed the complete technical packet standard on the same 2 of 24 fresh cases; D passed 0 of 24. No professional legal acceptance or human review-time benefit was measured. The repaired economic campaign produced no positive cash result and no earned first or second investment in the declared scenario set.

## 1. What the numerical results mean

All money below is hypothetical CAD unless explicitly described as observed. “Cash net result” means simulated collections minus all modeled delivery, business, maintenance and newly paid capability-construction costs. It is not measured profit or a financial-statement accounting result. A complete historical research cost was unavailable and remains null. The actual ledger has observed collections of CAD0, while actual full cost, operating margin, reserves, obligations and available surplus remain unknown/null.

The campaign contains 9 predefined scenarios, 24 paired seeds per scenario and 5 strategies: 1,080 stored strategy runs. Scenarios are sensitivity cases, not an estimated distribution of the legal market. Their equal-weight aggregate is not a forecast. Percentiles summarize simulated worlds within a stated scenario; they are not confidence intervals for real profitability.

B is the newly constructed ordinary implementation. C1 adds the retained reference/definition resolver. C adds the amendment resolver as a second asset. D is the independently reconstructed conventional implementation. These are distinct from the generic unexecuted commercial-software alternative available to modeled buyers. Seeded strategies pay assumed construction at the opening and revert to B when the assumed 13-week asset lifetime expires. Consequently, later accepted invoices in the D-seeded strategy can come from B after expiry; they do not imply that D passed a fresh primary packet.

## 2. Validity repair and reporting errata

The original frozen v1 model could invoice a technically rejected packet after unobserved assumed correction. Its unchanged predefined no-buyer-onboarding scenario generated 115 such invoices in seed 1. That was an accounting-validity defect, not a scientific commercial result. The original source, input and diagnostic are retained under `history/model-v1`. V2 requires the actual deterministic acceptance flag before any modeled professional acceptance or invoice; failure costs remain charged. All scenario parameters, seeds and production records are unchanged. The v2 source was frozen before the valid campaign. Twenty-one local accounting and input-integrity checks pass.

Two disclosure limits remain explicitly attached to the frozen v2 output. First, its emitted text saying technical acceptance affects “only” correction burden is stale: it also controls acceptance and invoices. Second, buyer offer quality is an assumed 0.95 perceived professional acceptance value, while realized acceptance is separately limited by the observed technical gate. Buyers are therefore modeled with optimistic perceived quality; demand is not calibrated to measured overall reliability. The negative outcomes survive this optimistic assumption. The frozen source/results were not silently amended or tuned.

At the reference wage and burden assumptions, a 45-minute human-work coordinate costs CAD63.58 in opportunity terms. A CAD10 packet plus 45-minute review and allocated buyer onboarding costs CAD80.64. The generic alternative coordinate costs CAD34.79. These illustrate the need to reduce verified complete workload; they are not observed matched legal-service prices. For a CAD10 packet at those assumptions, residual review would have to fall below about 32.93minutes to beat the human coordinate and 12.55minutes to beat the generic alternative. Neither reduction was measured.

Employee capacity released is distinct from avoided cash expenditure. The default payroll-cash realization fraction is zero. A buyer can gain useful capacity while continuing to pay the same salary; the model does not turn that into cash available for the supplier. The quoted public wage is not a lawyer’s invoice, and compute time is not the cost of a professionally accepted deliverable.

## 6. Reuse, repayment, lifetime and maintenance

B, C1 and C have the same complete accepted-case set. Measured compute differences round below a CAD cent at the declared shadow rate, and no human correction/review saving was measured. Under the no-unmeasured-benefit reference, B→C1 and C1→C have zero modeled gross marginal saving. Positive construction and maintenance therefore have no finite break-even reuse and no demonstrated repayment time. Increasing reuse cannot repay an asset with nonpositive net saving per reuse.

The separate sensitivity grid evaluates 12/50/100/250 reuses, assumed correction durations 0/2/5/10/20/45minutes and upkeep 0/25/100 CAD cents per use. B, C1 and C have the same failure rate and accepted set, so these correction-cost sensitivities do not create incremental retained-skill value. The declared 13-week useful life is a model limit, not a measured legal capability lifetime. Changed law, playbook, format, source, dependency or authority must trigger revalidation or withdrawal. The first dependency’s expiry limits the second asset; adding a new asset cannot extend its ancestor’s freshness.

First construction can be considered only after ordinary delivery has created earned surplus beyond expenses and reserves and its forecast direct benefit exceeds construction, failure and upkeep costs plus expected contribution lost during the pause. Second construction also requires subsequent use and repayment of the first direct cost and its estimated paused contribution. These are tested code/control properties; no campaign path exercised successful first or second investment. The high-maintenance sensitivity increases common weekly upkeep from CAD2 to CAD20, adding CAD468 over the 26-week reference and worsening its mean result by exactly CAD468.

## 7. Independent arithmetic and invoice reconciliation

The same-session independent evaluator reviewed 1,080 stored strategy summaries and the exact source/input freezes, with 13,403 arithmetic/invariant assertions and no reported discrepancy. This is an independent software/accounting review within the research session, not a licensed professional legal or financial audit. It also reproduced one high-attempt scenario from the frozen source and matched its stored summary exactly.

For every stored strategy summary it checked provider cost-category sums, collections minus costs, initial seed plus cash result, reserve/obligation deductions, earned-surplus bounds, construction charged once and null historical-recovery fields. In the high-attempt replay it reconstructed collections from invoice dates, the frozen keyed payment draws and the collection delay. The older vendor-described LawGeex study’s 92minutes for five NDAs is an issue-spotting result from a different experiment, not a complete Ontario packet estimate. It does not calibrate the 45-minute reference coordinate. All human task/review durations, professional acceptance probability, buyer perceived quality, recurrence, willingness to pay, support, acquisition, integration, construction, upkeep and payment behavior here are explicitly assumed. No measured human-work advantage, competitive superiority, commercially viable accepted legal support, unique-method advantage, historical-cost recovery or self-financing Flywheel follows from this campaign.

The allowed narrower second prototype is assessed separately as exact source-bound reconciliation with two actual comparison methods. It cannot erase or relabel the negative broad-review result. Its separately frozen analysis uses ordinary-tool review-time sensitivity and zero measured review benefit; no new retained asset or duplicated efficacy arms are invented.
