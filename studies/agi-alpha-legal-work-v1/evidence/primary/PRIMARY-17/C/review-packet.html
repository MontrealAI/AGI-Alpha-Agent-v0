SYNTHETIC RESEARCH DOCUMENT — NOT AN EXECUTABLE AGREEMENT
Company Alder (synthetic) and Company Boreal (synthetic) are evaluating a proposed software supply and integration relationship. Each is a Receiver when receiving the other company's Protected Material. These bilateral duties apply equally to both companies.
1. Permitted activity. A Receiver may use Protected Material solely to evaluate whether the two companies should enter the proposed software supply and integration relationship. No other use is permitted.
2. Secrecy period. Each Receiver shall preserve the confidentiality of Protected Material for 24 months following each disclosure.
3. Material at end of discussions. Within 10 calendar days after a written request from the disclosing company, the Receiver shall return or destroy all Protected Material.
4. Public authority demands. Where disclosure is compelled by law, the Receiver shall notify the disclosing company in writing before disclosure to the extent legally permitted.
5. Unaided recollections. A Receiver must not use residual information retained in unaided memory.
6. Transfer of this agreement. A party may assign this Agreement only after receiving the other party's prior written consent.
7. Applicable law. The laws of Ontario, Canada govern this Agreement.
8. Access by personnel. Disclosure to advisers and other representatives is allowed only where they need to know the material for the permitted evaluation. The Receiver remains responsible for breaches by those representatives.
