ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC RESEARCH TEXT — NOT FOR EXECUTION
Mutual information-exchange agreement between Research Company Cedar and Research Company Dune, fictional organizations.
1. Marking. The supplying company will mark the files listed in the delivery register as restricted project material. Oral presentations will be identified in the meeting record.
2. Access. Access is limited to personnel assigned to the proposed integration project. A personnel list will be updated at each scheduled review.
2A. Public statements. Neither company may use the other company's project name in an external announcement without a separate written approval identifying that announcement.
3. Technical inspection. The receiving company may inspect the sample output using the supplied test interface. Source code is not included in the sample output.
4. End of evaluation. The project coordinators will record the date on which evaluation access ends and identify the copies held by their respective teams.
