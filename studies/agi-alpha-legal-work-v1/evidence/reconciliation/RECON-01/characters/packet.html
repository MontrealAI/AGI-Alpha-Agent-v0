ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC RESEARCH TEXT — NOT FOR EXECUTION
Mutual information-exchange agreement between Research Company Cedar and Research Company Dune, fictional organizations.
Project: Harbour Connector.
1. Evaluation. The exchanged technical records may be used only to evaluate whether the companies should enter a software interoperability project and to build an internal proof-of-concept using the supplied records. A demonstration supplied with those records remains part of the evaluation material.
2. Safeguarding. Each receiving company will limit copies to those reasonably needed for that evaluation and will maintain an inventory of copies supplied to its project staff.
3. Discussions. Neither company promises to purchase, supply or license any product merely by taking part in the discussions. Any later commercial arrangement will be recorded separately.
4. Records. Each company will identify the person responsible for its project records before the first demonstration session.
