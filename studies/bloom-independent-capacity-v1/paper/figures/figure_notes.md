# Publication figure notes

All four figures are 6 inches wide. PDF and SVG retain vector geometry; PNG previews are 200 dpi.
The plotting script reads frozen analysis and raw evidence and imports no scientific kernel.
The source and all input/output SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `plot_provenance.json`.
Raw science files are never changed. The independent G1 receipt is recorded as an overlay, preserving the original pending field.

## fig01_effects

Frozen preregistered ratio and CI transformed by saving = 100*(1-ratio); CI endpoints reverse. No recomputation or pooling.

Neither competitive superiority nor the receiver benefit threshold passed in confirmation or fresh reproduction.

## fig02_cpu_phases

For each method, treatment and world select the middle of three total-CPU cold replicates. Sum phases from that same record; residual is total minus all recorded phases. Plot unweighted means over 48 worlds. Stable totals exactly reconcile with frozen primary means.

Operational CPU is measured; these bars do not establish complete monetary cost. All predecessor work is included; symmetric external scientific audit is separate.

## fig03_robustness

Confirmation only. Group by method, treatment, verification and world; repeated outcomes must agree. A cell denominator is 48 worlds, not independent queries or the number of logical replicates. Unsafe bypass was run only for BLOOM.

Qualified plans, correct infeasibility and correct refusal are distinct. The missing-edge arm is correct only relative to supplied incomplete input; every world omits some complete-graph impact. Unsafe bypass accepts corrupt artifacts and produces wrong outputs.

## fig04_operator_choices

Confirmation only. One final operator choice per method, treatment and world; choices must agree across repeated stable trials. Show counts and fractions, without inferential claims.

The stable result is a comparison of selection and surrounding costs with a shared SCC capability. Under storage or constructor constraints conventional memoization and walking remain useful fallbacks.
