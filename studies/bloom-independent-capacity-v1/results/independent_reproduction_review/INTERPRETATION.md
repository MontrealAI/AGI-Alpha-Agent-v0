## Costs and usefulness

The fresh reproduction used 8.109308537 CPU seconds and 8.114178006 wall seconds; the confirmation replay used 21.231306559 CPU seconds and 21.237746829 wall seconds. These campaign totals include external research audit and one measured conventional replacement production per world. They are not sums of per-query pseudo-cost units. Human/model/service authoring, hardware ownership, integration maintenance and demand remain unknown or unestablished; there are no observed cash receipts, firm offers or commercial transactions. G5 remains incomplete.

The successful engineering result is an exact, inspectable local dependency-impact and finite-coverage system with independently checkable portable operators, explicit rejection/reconstruction boundaries, and conventional alternatives that retain equal construction access. Exactness is relative to the supplied graph and coverage catalogue. It does not establish a complete real dependency graph, preserved regression fault detection, autonomous industry creation, or profitable enterprise operation.

The most valuable next study is a real release-audit workload with complete dependency and test-coverage evidence, independently observed acceptance criteria, and measured integration/human costs. Preserve the current negative competitive result and use an effective conventional implementation as the default.
