The numerical mission is to screen disturbance energy in stable coupled dynamical systems. Each accepted artifact includes a computed covariance and a residual-based error certificate. A reduced basis learned from solved instances can make repeated screening cheaper.

The beneficiary is a scientific-software or control-engineering team comparing many designs. Buyer demand, service price and contractual deadlines remain explicit hypotheses.

06 / An operating policy that can stop

Offer useful work. Require the next investment to earn its place.

Start with a bounded numerical screening packet: exact inputs, covariance results, residual certificates, declared exclusions and reproducible code. Qualify the customer's model, outcome, deadline and verification burden before quoting a price.

Retain a component only when applicable demand and complete incremental economics support it. Recheck after distribution shift or a stronger frontier baseline. Preserve failed candidates, and stop additional Gym work when its marginal return is unsupported.

Before live operation

Buyer evidence and willingness to pay; complete operating costs; representative and independently governed validation; qualified scientific capsule support; current real identity and rights checks; service capacity, monitoring and maintained fallbacks.
