# Separate-agent economics and policy review

This is a static research-code review by a separate AI-assisted analysis lane. It is not independent human validation, a financial audit, customer evidence, or an endorsement of the assumed market. `economics_review_snapshot.json` records the exact files reviewed. Confirmation outputs had not been generated at the time of this snapshot.

Executable accounting and edge-case checks are tracked separately in `economic_review.md` and `study/test_economics.py`. Their reported tested source hashes govern any claim about a later corrected version; this static memo is not a substitute for those tests.

## Findings resolved during review

1. **Fallback accounting:** commercial inputs now use measured complete policy-chain timings, including rejected attempts, rather than adding component measurements that each included assembly. Initial reconstruction remains in the loader but is overwritten by the required measured-chain CSV before use.
2. **Maintenance:** selective admission values expected future upkeep when making the investment decision, while cash costs are charged only when upkeep occurs. The same future upkeep is no longer charged upfront and periodically.
3. **Refund cash:** full refunds after failed customer acceptance are paid immediately at collection; failed receipts no longer temporarily inflate spendable cash through the general supplier-payment delay.
4. **Failed prototypes and displaced production:** commercial packaging has an explicit, assumed failure probability. Failed attempts retain their costs. Setup, retention, Gym, and packaging consume declared capacity in addition to their monetary costs.
5. **Unpaid origin examples:** the six executed originating examples create no actual or base-scenario simulated collections. Their research costs are mapped once to C. A paid-origin implementation would charge outcome-required learning once to A; it is a different provenance claim.
6. **No future-outcome resource decision:** the revised delivery decision reserves the contract's declared processing budget before evaluating sampled technical and customer acceptance. The policy does not select work using the realized hidden success value.
7. **Repeated admission:** selective admission is now evaluated only while the enterprise remains in ordinary mode. A previously completed investment is not repeatedly logged as another new action.
8. **Execution status:** immutable pre-run freeze information is preserved, while the result artifact separately identifies development or confirmation execution.

## Fair comparison and accounting assessment

The compared policies receive the same capital, total daily processing capacity, demand draws, product catalogue, ordinary tools, posted-price grid, and originating records. Failed commercial packaging draws are common across policies. Private duplication and shared admission consume their own money and capacity. The ordinary-library treatment has identical contents and permissions to shared reuse; the code asserts identical operating surplus. This construction cannot establish a graph-specific advantage, and none should be claimed.

Collections, A/B/C incurred costs, cost payments, obligations, reserves, available funds, and seed capital are separated. Gross collected receipts from a failed delivery are offset by an explicit refund cost. Receivables outside the horizon are excluded from collected-basis operating surplus. Opportunity cost is represented by displaced production, without a second subtraction from cash. Hypothetical $AGIALPHA or asset appreciation is absent from earned revenue.

The policy uses only past acquired lead counts when updating the intensity estimate; its declared utility distributions and cost assumptions are available equally to all treatments. Finite posted-price search and myopic productization/admission rules are inspectable. They do not constitute a globally optimal enterprise policy.

## Boundaries that remain material

- **Demand and prices are assumptions.** Eight posted-price candidates are evaluated against a declared buyer-choice distribution. Estimated human-team hours and an assumed billing rate are not a matched customer quote. Simulation confidence intervals cannot validate those inputs.
- **The opportunity catalogue is researcher supplied.** Numerical selection prototyping and commercial packaging do not demonstrate autonomous invention by matched frontier-model enterprises, independently assessed buyer usefulness, or an advantage from learned opportunity judgment.
- **Selective capability valuation is optimistic and incomplete.** It uses in-domain 32-case savings and a simplified acceptance estimate. It does not fully optimize across product mix, shift frequency, transfer failure, capacity, and future baseline progress. The explicit search-limit disclosure is necessary.
- **Shift routing remains a better ordinary alternative.** Schur is faster on the measured shifted population than attempting failed specialist/adaptive chains. Recommendation D is a scope guard that routes known out-of-domain inputs to that ordinary method. Guard detection cost and false decisions require their own validation; the present forced-use ablation is not an optimized live routing policy.
- **The upgrade scenario is a common hypothetical improvement.** Scaling every complete numerical chain and every frontier-token bill equally does not measure a successor frontier model, nor establish that relative specialist advantage survives one. A real matched model-upgrade comparison remains open.
- **Fixed operating commitments matter.** Daily overhead continues through the 60-day horizon even after lead acquisition is declined. The existing policy does not optimize shutdown or release of that commitment. A best-alternative claim must retain this constraint.
- **Origin timing rounding:** at this review snapshot, the commercial code used a rounded 0.04 seconds per originating full solve. Exact observed stage times are retained in the technical data. The rounded value must remain an assumption or be replaced by those measurements.
- **Scope and cost completeness:** the model covers the declared offline numerical report. Physical model validation, controller commissioning, professional sign-off, actual customer acquisition, and full live operating costs have not been measured. Local signature fixtures and hash-verified file transfer do not establish production ENS admission.

## Claim discipline

The executable evidence can support measured technical reuse, rejected raw certificates, guarded correction, distribution-shift failure, and a later researcher-constructed prototype. The commercial simulation can support conditional comparisons and a policy that declines additional investment under its assumptions. It does not, by itself, establish a newly feasible real market, autonomous enterprise discovery, earned revenue, graph-specific superiority, or sustained profitable compounding.
