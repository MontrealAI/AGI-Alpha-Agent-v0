The corrected low-willingness-to-pay policy stops both lead acquisition and optional prototypes. It still loses USD 874.26 because the fixed 60-day operating commitment and common setup/origin research costs remain. The preserved initial policy lost USD 984.26 in that scenario, including USD 110 of avoidable prototypes. One declared amendment conditioned productization on the marginal contribution of an acquisition-viable portfolio; a new 256-seed cohort confirmed the correction without changing technical criteria, prices or demand assumptions. This is a researcher-corrected policy, not evidence of autonomously learned commercial judgment. The growth arm can earn much more under its assumed high demand because every policy may purchase extra capacity. That result concerns scalable ordinary delivery under the assumed market, not a learned expansion of what is technically possible. Quality-adjusted units use declared weights 1/3/6 for accepted output, and a separate metric divides them by processing hours. These are accounting weights, not measured buyer value.
