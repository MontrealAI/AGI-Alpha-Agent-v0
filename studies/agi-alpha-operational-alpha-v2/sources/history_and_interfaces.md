## Consequence for the new edition

The new U/E/E+ experiment addresses a real gap. The previous numerical studies compare competent mathematical procedures; their “Beta” often includes what the new vocabulary calls expert operation. Preserve each old definition. Their modeled commercial profits cannot be counted once as Beta profit and then enlarged by the same expert-operation benefit.

The strongest defensible new contribution would freeze a compact expert operating workflow and give it to a separate recipient on unfamiliar work. Same model, tools, public customer requirements, acceptance, budget and stopping rule must apply. Workflow construction and incremental packaging/assessment must be visible costs. A synthetic response generator can test the mechanism, but its assumed E−U effect cannot establish frontier-model Operational Alpha.

### Admission feasibility before buying an assessment

The corrected old gate forecasts maximum direct gross benefit `Gmax = U × t × cap`, reuse expense `U × f`, remaining fixed expense `K`, and hurdle `η = 1.25`. An assay cannot possibly permit admission when either `Gmax < η(Uf + K)` or `Gmax ≤ Uf + K`. Both inequalities matter because admission requires strictly positive net value even at zero fixed cost.

The old cheap screen compared optimistic gross benefit with `0.6K`, while final admission used `1.25(Uf+K)`. It could therefore buy evidence that could not change the immediate admission decision. All 93 reference and 475 favorable assessments were unreachable; maximum hurdle ratios were 0.730158 and 0.789708. Mean assessment cost remained $261.5625 and $1,335.9375 per 64-world profile. The total $102,240 remains charged. A corrected prescreen must receive a prospective fresh evaluation because cash, capacity, cooldowns and later choices can change.

The test bounds that old policy's nominal direct-savings forecast. It does not bound all enterprise value or rule out quality improvement, different offerings, future states or different decision rules.

Each signed command binds journal identity, operation ID, action and exact record. Repeated identical operations are idempotent; changed terms at an old identity fail. Amounts are integer USD micro-units. Categories are model/tools/compute/storage/network/human/acceptance/correction/overhead.

Known protected-content strings and reserved evaluator fields are excluded by the research lifecycle. This is not proof against arbitrary covert encoding. ## Publication route and live prerequisites

The unchanged passive-paper importer can add PDF/TeX originals through the existing reviewed process. New HTML or executable study content requires full source qualification and owner release sequencing; it is not a passive overlay. A separately labeled immutable research prerelease can distribute an evidence package, with source/asset verification, without asserting software release or canonical Pages deployment.

Live operation still requires approved model/account access, quota and tariffs, hard budget/liability bounds, billing reconciliation, qualified tools/sandbox, HTTPS and durable storage/key/checkpoint custody, two approved mainnet RPC readers/code pins, eligible owner-controlled identities, rights-cleared work, fresh independent acceptance, and qualified production capability scope. A live service handoff is not independent possession of a specialist's intelligence.

## Design review questions for the new experiment

1. Does U have ordinary tools, reasonable correction and the same customer acceptance contract? Make no claim of representative ordinary humans without actual sampling or honest modeled assumptions.
2. Is E a frozen method, not extra hidden compute or a different model? Report matched budget and accepted-quality cost separately.
3. Is E+ genuinely new after E, with prior E construction cost preserved and only additional improvement charged as C/B?
4. Does a recipient receive only transferable method bytes, without hidden scorer/test knowledge, previous solutions or developer assistance?
5. Are quality failures, retries, refusal, resource reservations and uncertainty recorded without changing the task after failure?
6. Is low-cost evaluation proportionate? A small method may justify a small assay, but a fixed statistical gate must have an attainable positive path under its declared bounds.
7. Does sharing receive equal-content ordinary-library and reconstructible-method controls? Method usefulness and graph distribution are different effects.
8. Is a frontier crossing fixed before comparison and positive on complete cost? If both arms qualify, call it better economics.
9. Are simulated response advantages, buyer demand and prices clearly assumptions? Simulation intervals establish conditional behavior only.
10. Does the report separate observed execution, simulated operating cash, unknown engineering cost, token utility and deployment authority?
