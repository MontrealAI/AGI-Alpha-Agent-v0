    06
    The next useful offering must qualify
    A campaign recovery audit composes journal, scheduling, artifact and settlement evidence into a reproducible release/hold decision. Every configuration gets the same verified components and permission to propose alternatives.
    Supplementary experiment
    A source-bound decision packet
    A successful component replay is insufficient. Ownership, completed execution, committed artifacts and exact per-job settlement must agree, and the packet must bind the full input evidence.
    Fixed technical envelope: 1,200 seconds and 36 logged operations. The $250 price, two-business-day turnaround and 30-minute customer effort are hypotheses, not an observed contract.
    What would count as a crossing?
    The enhanced configuration must satisfy the frozen usefulness, quality, deadline and customer-effort conditions at positive complete-cost margin while the equally equipped baseline does not. If both qualify, report parity or improved economics.
    Unknown actual costs and demand prevent an observed commercial crossing here. An executable audit is not remote self-healing or a universal exactly-once guarantee.

    07
    An operating blueprint with a stopping rule
    Profitable accepted delivery is the foundation. Retention, sharing and training are optional decisions supported by expected additional value.
    1. Sell a bounded useful outcomeName the source, deliverable, rights, acceptance, deadline and customer effort. Compare credible human and in-house alternatives.
    2. Deliver and verify expertlyAutomate preparation, execute in a qualified environment, preserve failed attempts and record complete costs.
    3. Check whether retention can payScreen an assessment’s reachability before buying it. A reachable rule is not evidence of a valuable capability.
    4. Share rights-cleared executable expertiseTransfer dependencies and provenance, exclude secrets and protected tests, and check actual recipient outcomes.
    5. Train, expand, hold or stopUse only information available at the time. Requalify after model or task changes. Do not force Gym investment or book hypothetical opportunities as revenue.
    What remains for live operation
    Metered provider access, qualified execution isolation, real customer acceptance and payment, durable custody, appropriate identities and the existing production and settlement gates. The research changes no validator role, governance or wallet authority.
    Keep token utility separate
    The existing Jobs route uses $AGIALPHA; Manager uses USDC. This study performs neither settlement nor currency conversion. Implemented utility, legal classification, speculative valuation and enterprise cash profit are separate questions.
    Preserve what the evidence already taught
    Earlier thermal, scheduling, control and capability studies retain stronger-baseline ties, negative transfer, rejected admission and corrected accounting. No old profit is relabeled as new learning.
