## 6. Remaining risks and publication constraints

| Severity | Remaining limitation | Required interpretation or next step |
|---|---|---|
| High for commercial claims | Actual tokens, complete costs, paid demand and collections are unavailable | Do not claim observed profitability, accepted-output dollar savings, net learned Alpha or a positive-margin feasibility crossing |
| Medium | Four constructed primary task families and one supplied composition task; one batch per arm | Treat equality and elapsed times descriptively; use protected fresh tasks and repeated, metered execution for broader conclusions |
| Medium | Hosted model identity/access is not an independently verified API snapshot; U is a competent tool-using proxy | Do not generalize to sampled ordinary human usage or attribute model upgrades to workflow learning |
| Medium | Shared-host protocol isolation and no original exact-prompt precommit | Preserve the limitations; a new trial should commit complete prompts and enforce stronger execution boundaries |
| Medium | Synthetic authority and positive monetary control fixture | Keep technical byte transfer distinct from useful transfer, live membership and production admission |
| Medium | Four-task posterior extrapolation, constructed demand, H scope estimates and nominal assay interval | Treat profits and confidence intervals as conditional simulation outputs; measured buyer/cost/evidence acquisition is needed |
| Medium | No sampled Gym campaign was selected in the headline scenario | Implementation support for training is not demonstrated specialist improvement or sustained superiority |
| Low, interpretive | `false_admissions` in the main simulator detects nonpositive latent quality gain | It does not count every admission whose small positive gain fails to repay full lifecycle costs |

The human-service scope assumptions were narrowed before economic/C5 confirmation. They remain explicit estimates,
not human trials or matched customer quotes. The source-based human rate reference does not validate the assumed
hours, demand or buyer willingness to pay. The nominal six-pair monetary lower-bound heuristic is not calibrated
coverage under correlated validation error. Broad-market extrapolation would not remove these evidentiary limits.

The appropriate supporting-study conclusion is that the workflow, transfer and accounting software can be
reproduced, while this small pilot establishes neither an accepted-output advantage nor net monetary benefit from
retaining the helper. The additive economic controller demonstrates a conditional benefit from declining an
unjustified assessment. Those findings should remain visible behind any later, separately supported opportunity-led
result.
