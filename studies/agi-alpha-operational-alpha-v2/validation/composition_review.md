## Offering review independent of the proposing context

All three agents selected the supplied `bounded-campaign-audit` hypothesis and delivered working code. Each `OFFERING.md` identifies an engineering release-review beneficiary, the same fixed contract, missing live evidence, and practical and commercial falsifiers. The proposals do not claim customers or earnings. They correctly decline the unsupported live-self-healing and universal-exactly-once alternatives.

The accepted artifact is a read-only synthetic campaign auditor. Its independent checks bind per-job ownership and consumption, completed scheduling, committed tenant artifacts, complete input hashes, and final reconciliation. It grants no release or payment authority and establishes neither the authenticity nor completeness of external exports. A true production offering would additionally need representative authorized exports, input provenance, a supported schema mapping, a decision owner, workload limits, measured preparation/review cost, and customer acceptance.

The prospective protocol contains hypothetical $250 price, two business days' turnaround, 30 minutes of customer effort, and a four-hour human-service estimate. These were not observed buyer commitments or tested customer effort. Actual token counts and complete costs remain unknown. The study therefore reports no observed positive-margin feasibility crossing.

All three configurations satisfy the same technical requirements. E−U and E+−E acceptance increments are both zero on this one task; fourteen assertions are not fourteen independent opportunities. The elapsed times are descriptive hosted-session observations, with no randomization or repeated-run latency inference. The result does not prove equivalence in a broader task population.

The components, contract and plausible offering menu were researcher-supplied before the agents began. Selection and repair demonstrate bounded composition, not autonomous opportunity discovery. This control should be preserved behind the newly opportunity-led flagship rather than used as its discovery evidence.
