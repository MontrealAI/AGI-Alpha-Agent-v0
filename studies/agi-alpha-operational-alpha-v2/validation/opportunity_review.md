## 4. Review of explanatory claims

The automated evaluator checks text presence and minimum lengths for some explanations. This reviewer therefore
read all three frozen `REPORT.md` files and checked their substantive interpretations against the public contract,
source-derived calculations and packet outputs.

All three reports correctly preserve these distinctions:

- The stationary vector and mean acetylation are conditional on the supplied model and nominal parameter point.
  They do not establish fitted-parameter uncertainty, biological generalization or full structural identifiability.
- Sixteen normalized states provide at most fifteen independent equilibrium mean coordinates. The 33-parameter
  superset therefore has a necessary rank deficiency of at least 18. This does not declare the selected eight-parameter
  model unidentifiable or invalidate the original fitted stationary use. The fixed `da_b=1` normalization remains
  legitimate, and observation-noise parameters are excluded from kinetic counts.
- Positive clock scaling preserves `Qp=0` while generally changing a finite trajectory to `x(ct)`. The two supplied
  scales are hypothetical physical embeddings of the normalized model, not newly fitted or observed biological rates.
- The relaxation result propagates an explicitly assumed positive calibration interval by inverse scaling. It is
  neither an observed calibration, confidence interval, biological half-life nor finite-time settling guarantee.
- Boehm's finite observation times do not justify inheriting H4's equilibrium-only equivalence. Its reported contrasts
  are read from the supplied reference simulations, not from new experiments, a new nonlinear simulation or refitting.
  Its unit declaration does not prove every temporal interpretation calibrated.
- Source hashes establish byte identity, not authenticated origin or scientific truth. Neither packet nor capability
  creates execution authority, endorsement, customers, revenue or physical validation.

No substantive overstatement was found in these explanations within the stated finite contract. The reports' detailed
test statements are local participant verification, not external replication. Their pre-report resource checkpoints
are explicitly distinguished from authoritative final runner totals.
