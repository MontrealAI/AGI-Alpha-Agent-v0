Why the bounded S3 follow-up was selected
PEtab Select already supplies model-space specification, model-selection support and integrations. Its 2026 paper
describes comprehensive identifiability analysis across large model spaces as costly and selective automation as
future work. That documented gap supports a selective reuse-qualification instrument: bind a new scientific
request to source evidence, identify a concrete unsupported interpretation, and specify the missing evidence. It
does not justify claiming a novel identifiability algorithm or an empty market. [S2]
The beneficiary is a team deciding whether a published model can safely support a particular next prediction.
The costly dependency is expert source interpretation and requalification across data, equations, units, requested
meanings and missing observations. The smallest test preserves a supported use while refusing an unsupported
one, then transfers to a changed source without carrying over the old conclusion. It is falsified if competent
conventional work already produces the same accepted packet at no greater complete burden, or if prospective
buyers do not value the decision enough to fund delivery. [S1; S3]

Declared exploration and construction limits
Selection was recorded at 16:16:24 UTC on 27 October 2026 before instrument construction. The bounded
construction allocated 30 minutes to the developer, 30 minutes to internal independent review, and a 1,200-
second/48-operation envelope to each fresh hosted arm. No large simulator or full model-selection search was
built to rescue an unconvincing opportunity. These are protocol limits, not a priced research budget or complete
labor measurement. [S1; S5]
What was discovered versus supplied. The reuse gap was sharpened through source investigation. Researchers
supplied the eventual requests, clock-scale examples, calibration interval and benchmark composition. All
confirmatory arms received the discovered formulation. The observed experiment evaluates execution and transfer
after that discovery; it does not compare autonomous discovery policies.

3. Preserve the science; expose the missing clock
Original purpose. The H4 source models stationary combinatorial acetylation patterns. The recovered public

model-space declaration, not the selected fit. No physical time-unit declaration or independent clock calibration
is supplied. [S3–S4]
New requested purpose. A recipient asks for physical relaxation time. Equilibrium data alone leave
the common physical clock unspecified. Removing that information restriction requires additional calibration;
computing harder cannot manufacture it. The instrument turns an ambiguous request into a preserved equilibrium
result, an explicit counterexample and a bounded evidence obligation.

witness is elementary:

Scope that keeps the witness honest
The instrument covers closed, irreducible, first-order one-to-one conversion networks with one constant unit-
volume compartment, a normalized initial total of one, constant parameters and no condition overrides. It is not
a general SBML/PEtab engine or complete structural-identifiability analysis. Its spectral time is the inverse
slowest nonzero decay rate, not a half-life, settling guarantee or biological confidence interval. The fixed 10−9
zero threshold and ordinary floating-point intervals limit very slow or poorly separated modes. Boehm contrasts
come from supplied reference simulations; no new nonlinear fit or biological experiment occurred. [S4; S6]

OPERATING BLUEPRINT


9. A bounded path to a live decision
 1. Specify the useful decision. With a prospective beneficiary, freeze the requested prediction, scientific
    acceptance, turnaround, required inputs, customer effort and outside options. Obtain a matched human-service
    quote and explicit willingness-to-pay hypothesis. A rate card alone is insufficient.
 2. Qualify the source and authority. Ingest licensed public or authorized data, check provenance and scope,
    and separate the right to read from the right to act. Unsupported physics, nonlinear dynamics or calibration
    triggers an explicit specialist or deferral route.
 3. Deliver through the cheapest qualifying method. Permit conventional tools and competent ordinary
    operation. Use the expert workflow when it measurably helps. Record retries, failures, correction, model/tool
    charges, latency, customer burden and actual residual human obligations.
 4. Measure the candidate’s incremental benefit. Compare E+ with E on fresh model families at fixed
    resources and at matched accepted quality. Separate technical construction, coordinated commitments and
    their combination. Include negative transfer, shifted requirements and improving model defaults.
 5. Choose retention or HOLD. Check admission feasibility before costly assessment. Estimate reusable
    demand only from information available then. Charge extra rights review, retention, distribution and
    maintenance once. Use optional Gym practice only with a stated artifact, environment, feedback, update
    rule, budget and stopping condition.
 6. Fund dated obligations. Verify real commitments, acceptance and refund terms, milestone usefulness,
    support reserves and each cash prefix. Do not spend refundable principal as earned surplus. Preserve
    conventional delivery, no coalition and stopping as choices.

Define a frontier crossing before comparison
An offering crosses the economic frontier only when the enhanced configuration alone meets the frozen useful-
quality, deadline, customer-effort and selling-price conditions at positive complete margin within the same
operating envelope. If both qualify, report improved economics. S3 currently has zero observed exclusive
technical crossings and zero observed commercial crossings. No unlimited demand or physical product
follows from a computational packet. [S5; S9]

Verified model information versus this run
The official documentation snapshot dated 27 October 2026 lists gpt-6-astra, gpt-6.1-sol and gpt-6.1-terra.
Standard short-context uncached input/output prices per million tokens are respectively $10/$50, $2/$10 and
$1/$5. The manifest records tool support, reasoning settings, context limits, cache/long-context charges and
published limits. These sourced aliases are not evidence of account entitlement or the identity of hosted calls.
ChatGPT Pro is a plan, not an API model. No API billing, API-key entitlement or proprietary weight training
was established in this study. [S12]

Live stop conditions
Stop or change scope if buyers decline, conventional delivery is already sufficient at lower complete burden,
unsupported models dominate demand, correlated verification failures emerge, current admission cannot authorize
the service, or payment timing exceeds available funds. A changed model default can make a previously valuable
method ordinary Beta. Requalification and retirement are productive decisions, not research failures.
