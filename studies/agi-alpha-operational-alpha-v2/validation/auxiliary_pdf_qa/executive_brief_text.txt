  A consequential opportunity: qualify a scientific model for its next use. A published model
  may support an equilibrium result while lacking the evidence needed for a physical-time prediction.
  This edition constructs an executable qualification instrument, transfers it, and tests whether expert
  operation changes accepted outcomes. All four routes pass: ordinary use, expert operation, the enhanced
  recipient and a conventional construction. The result is useful scoped capability, with commercial
  advantage still unestablished.


Explore before constructing
Six materially different candidates were investigated: biological mechanism discovery, laboratory protocol
portability, elevation/datum qualification, legacy PLC migration, security qualification and physical
measurement qualification. Their prior art, missing evidence and smallest decisive tests were retained. A
bounded follow-up selected S3: scientific-model reuse qualification. The 2026 PEtab Select paper
identifies the cost of comprehensive identifiability analysis and selective automation as an unresolved
direction. This motivates selective qualification; it does not establish novelty or paying demand. [S1–S3]

The proposed service and its decision
Research software teams, core facilities and modeling services would receive a source-bound packet answering:
Which requested predictions are supported, what assumptions remain, and which measurement or analysis
is needed next? The packet includes executable calculations, source and request fingerprints, a specific
unsupported-use witness, a calibration obligation and a fresh-scope decision. It preserves valid uses of the
original model. It grants no execution authority and makes no biological validation claim. [S4–S6]

Decision for the enterprise
Retain this edition as a reproducible research instrument and a customer-discovery proposition. Do not

COMMERCIAL BLUEPRINT


Sell an accepted decision; earn the next step
Offer. Begin with a tightly scoped, reproducible model-reuse qualification packet. Agree on the prediction,
evidentiary standard, turnaround, customer effort and acceptable deferral before work. Obtain a comparable
human quote and a buyer’s alternative; price useful accepted work rather than marking up compute. Harvard
Chan’s published FY26 consulting rates of $193/hour for external academic clients and $265/hour for
industry are a sequencing-analysis analogue, not an S3 quotation. No H4 price is inferred from them. [S8]
Deliver and account. Automate source preparation, supported calculations, evidence binding and
packaging. Charge each activity once: A covers complete contracted delivery including learning required
for that outcome; B covers additional assessment, retention, sharing and upkeep; C covers optional further
research or Gym work. This edition’s research was unpaid. Actual collections are $0; complete actual cost
and profit remain unknown. [S5; S9–S10]
