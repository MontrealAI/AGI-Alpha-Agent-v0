[full_edition_review.sources.json](full_edition_review.sources.json).

All copied source and result files were SHA-256 compared before and after the complete replay. **Every byte was
unchanged.** Report-producing original validators/auditors ran in temporary copies. Fresh native keys, journal
identifiers and timings were judged by their declared semantic controls rather than required to repeat random bytes.

After successful replay, the reviewer appended a harmless comment to the temporary copied frozen instrument.
The opportunity replayer rejected the changed source **before development, participant acceptance or native
execution**. This is an integrity negative control, not a mutation of any archived scientific artifact.

The complete top-level replay took approximately 42.05 seconds in the reviewed environment; standalone copy/hash/
negative-control bookkeeping took approximately 42.95 seconds total. Those timings are observed local execution,
not a portable speed promise or hosted frontier-model cost.
