# Prospective supplement: choose whether assessment is worth buying

The original primary result is retained. Its optional policy screened for **possible admission** and could then
purchase an assessment even when continuing established expert operation had greater expected value. An attainable
admission gate is necessary for an informative assessment; it is not sufficient to justify buying it.

This supplement was designed **after** inspecting that original result. It is a disclosed post-primary research
extension, followed by a separate prospective economic confirmation on fresh worlds. It neither erases the earlier
loss nor establishes new Operational Alpha, real customer profit or recovered historical assessment expense.

## Exact alternatives

For private retention, shared retention and the identical-content ordinary library, the full development search
now chooses between two explicit modes:

- `hold`: continue the unchanged `expert_E` implementation, with all existing delivery, cost, reserve, prototype
  and resource rules. No new assessment or Gym expenditure is bought by this mode.
- `assess`: run the unchanged original optional policy, including its reachability screen and optional Gym rule.

The selection jointly searches mode, price and reserve. It uses the original32development seeds and unchanged
objective, costs, customers, priors, quality extrapolation, human reference and resource envelope. Control policies
U, E and equipped E+ retain their original implementations and grid. Ties prefer holding cash. There is no
lowered admission threshold, free resource, invented method improvement or altered production-cost configuration.

## Meaning and limits

The test asks whether an explicit economic option avoids buying evidence with insufficient expected benefit
under the retained assumptions. It is a bounded policy-selection test, not an estimate of the true value of
all prompting, learning or sharing. Its Monte Carlo intervals describe the modeled worlds only. Actual customer
collections remain zero and actual complete research cost/profit remain unknown. The original four-task pilot
still lacks native six-cluster monetary qualification. Sustained compounding and a real commercial feasibility
crossing remain unestablished.
