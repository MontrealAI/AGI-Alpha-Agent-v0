# Opportunity discovery — commercial coordination track

Recorded before global flagship selection, 27 October 2026. These are research candidates, not customer commitments. Eleven targeted queries and at most sixteen primary-source openings were budgeted; two candidates were investigated. No implementation or market simulator was constructed for either before this dossier. The completed operational-repair studies are not selection evidence for either candidate.

## M1. Maintainer-approved shared qualification of dependency repairs

**Beneficiary and useful outcome.** Maintainers and downstream organizations need a defensible decision to accept a repair of a dependency they actually use, with the supported release behavior preserved. The proposed service would deliver a reproducible defect witness, a scoped patch, regression and compatibility evidence, and an explicit unresolved-risk statement. A count of generated bug reports is not the outcome.

**Evidence of need and existing supply.** Alpha-Omega explicitly funds maintainers and security work across critical open-source projects, including small essential components [M1]. GitHub already combines funding, security tools, model access, education, incident support and maintainer participation [M2–M3]. Its program specifies $10,000 per selected project, staged over the program and later check-ins; that is program funding, not a market price for our proposed service. Curl's own audit history records recent AI-assisted professional security work [M4]. These observations establish an important problem with active investment and strong existing alternatives. They do not establish an underserved buyer willing to purchase another agent service.

**Specific residual hypothesis.** There may be a gap between discovering a valid defect and producing a maintainer-usable qualification package spanning the particular versions and downstream configurations used by several organizations. The proposed restriction to remove is repeated, isolated reproduction and regression work. An instrument could factor reusable environment descriptions, failure witnesses and compatibility contracts from customer-specific confidential configuration. Whether this duplication is material, and whether recipients would trust the output, remain unmeasured.

**Why intelligence might matter.** Expert agents could translate disparate build systems, construct reproductions, distinguish relevant from irrelevant environments, and adapt checks. Available scanners, fuzzers, CI, CodeQL, dependency alerts, AI remediation and professional auditors must be included in the baseline. Neither AI patching nor joint funding is new. The common core must emerge from inspected cases; it cannot be supplied along with conveniently matching buyers and called invention.

**Construction and smallest decisive experiment.** With one consenting maintainer, select three already-disclosed defects in supported versions before assigning methods. Compare ordinary tool-equipped frontier use, a frozen expert qualification workflow, and the same workflow receiving a prior case's executable instrument. An independent maintainer defines acceptance and reviews packages without arm labels. Record accepted patch coverage, false claims, review minutes, full compute and environment costs, and rights. Give the discovered representation to a conventional engineer to distinguish structure from discovery. Do not send unrequested reports or touch external systems.

**Commercial package and complementarity test.** Hypothesize a milestone-funded qualification campaign jointly purchased by downstream users. Their outside options include upstream waiting, internal engineering, professional audit, supported distributions and existing grant programs. Compare independently commissioned work, coordinated demand alone, reusable qualification alone and their combination, with identical access to conventional tooling. Upfront funds must cover environment construction and refund obligations before any success fee. Withdrawal, unresolved defects, incompatible version requirements, maintainer nonparticipation and support obligations may defeat the package. No price is inferred from a sponsor's grant amount.

**Falsifiers.** Reject if maintainers find the packages redundant, review burden rises, existing tools produce the same accepted evidence at lower complete cost, downstream configurations share too little structure, rights prevent reuse, or no participants prefer the offer to their real outside options. A patch that passes a self-authored test but changes supported behavior fails.

**Selection recommendation: defer.** Consequence is high and an experiment is possible, but current evidence does not isolate a sufficiently distinctive unserved gap. Existing commercial and community offerings are substantial. A stakeholder-led pilot is more decisive than another synthetic patch benchmark. Generic agentic patching is rejected as this edition's flagship.

## M2. Shared qualification of custom measurement workflows

**Beneficiary and useful outcome.** Small laboratories and manufacturers need to know whether a particular measurement result is accurate enough for a particular production or scientific decision. A usable deliverable would connect the measurand, calibration chain, uncertainty model, operating conditions and decision tolerance, with explicit reasons to collect another reference or abstain.

**Evidence of need and existing supply.** NIST distinguishes traceability from fitness for purpose and assigns providers responsibility for supporting their traceability claims [M5]. Its proficiency-testing program already organizes interlaboratory comparisons for named participants and scopes [M6]. NIST also provides uncertainty and consensus software [M7–M8]. PTB provides a Digital Calibration Certificate schema, documentation and tooling [M9–M10]. A certificate parser, ordinary uncertainty propagation or a generic shared calibration consortium would duplicate established building blocks.

**Specific residual hypothesis.** A custom measurement spanning several instruments may lack a reusable, machine-checkable mapping from certificate evidence to the new mission's required decision. The removeable restriction could be treating each qualification dossier as an indivisible bespoke service. The reusable part would be uncertainty propagation and instrument-scope checks; the mission-specific measurand, current calibration state and physical premises must be rechecked. Our historical detector Harness already investigates a related narrow mechanism, so repeating its offset/drift example would not advance this edition.

**What must be constructed.** The proposed instrument would ingest real calibration evidence and an authorized mission definition, establish which components are applicable, generate a traceable uncertainty budget, and identify unresolved physical premises and missing observations. The difficult work is defensible semantics and independently checked measurement premises. An XML schema pass cannot supply those facts.

**Smallest decisive experiment.** Work with an accredited laboratory on three distinct, retrospective measurement chains. Freeze tolerance and measurand. Compare existing NIST/PTB tools with an experienced metrologist, ordinary frontier assistance, and the retained instrument. Blindly replay held measurement chains and deliberate scope changes. Use the laboratory's independent results and uncertainty evaluation as the acceptance reference. A computational simulator alone is insufficient to validate the physical service.

**Commercial package.** Hypothesize a pooled instrument-qualification subscription or staged joint project among laboratories sharing standards and permitted method structure. Participants retain responsibility for their results; shared generic software is licensed separately from certificates and customer records. Outside options include established calibration laboratories, internal uncertainty analysis and existing proficiency-testing schemes. Test technical reuse and coordinated purchasing independently, then jointly. Fees and opening cash require real quotations; no human service price is available for this exact hypothetical package.

**Falsifiers.** Reject if ordinary expert practice already handles the chains efficiently, certificate semantics cannot be safely normalized, physical model errors dominate, laboratory liabilities prohibit participation, or support and requalification costs exceed reuse. A lower software cost with unchanged unvalidated premises is not a measurement breakthrough.

**Selection recommendation: defer.** The unresolved mission-qualification question is important but this environment cannot provide the physical calibration evidence or independent laboratory acceptance needed for the proposed claim. Existing tools and the prior Harness substantially cover the easy parts. It remains a possible partner-led extension, not a favorable numerical flagship.

All candidate commercial mechanisms, buyer values, latent duplication, and expected AI benefits are **conjectures**. The tool availability and program descriptions above are **sourced observations**. No sales, adoption, new algorithm, accepted physical measurement or superior discovery process was observed in this track.
