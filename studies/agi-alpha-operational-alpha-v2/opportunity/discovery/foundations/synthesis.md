## 2. Transformational Intelligence: exact result and empirical obligations

**Sources:** supplied PDF pp. 1–12, 13–17 and 19–24; repository `docs/Transformational_Intelligence.tex`.

The purpose charter fixes authorized outcomes, material constraints, permissions, and budgets. A formulation can change representations, assumptions, information acquisition, policy language, and intermediate targets. The evaluator is a distinct scientific instrument. Replacing a weak proxy requires a separate validated measurement change and a common comparison of incumbent and challenger; it cannot privately lower the acceptance bar (pp. 3, 7–12).

The exact causal example has two supplied worlds with identical passive-observation distributions. Every purely passive procedure has at most 50% identification accuracy under that model. An authorized intervention policy reaches `8191/8192` accuracy at a maximum of twelve interventions. A standard unrestricted Bellman planner matches it exactly. Thus the example establishes a formulation-dependent information limit; it does not establish autonomous discovery, a new causal algorithm, or a frontier-model performance advantage (pp. 4–6, 19–20).

The witness must preserve purpose, identify the restriction and why it may be removable, specify executable construction and a purpose bridge, predict a falsifier, name strong comparisons, bind fresh confirmation, and state reuse/retirement conditions (pp. 23–24). Classification and value are separate: a transformation can be genuine and fail; a known method can be commercially valuable without scientific novelty.

Several requirements directly constrain this edition:

- **Give the discovered formulation to the original solver.** If that solver then matches the result, the structure may be useful without proving superior execution or discovery (pp. 10, 14).
- **Allow complementary exploration.** Two edits with negative standalone value can have positive combined value. Preserve bounded incomplete hypotheses separately from admitted methods (p. 9).
- **Distinguish natural tasks from supplied mechanisms.** A researcher-generated task can reveal its intended solution. Fresh parameter draws do not make the opportunity independently discovered (p. 13).
- **Use campaign-level replication for learning to invent.** A better inherited instrument does not prove a better discovery policy. Cross old/new discovery policies with old/new starting artifacts (pp. 14–15).
- **Count the whole transformation.** Search, construction, confirmation, migration, execution, verification, and upkeep belong in lifecycle comparisons (p. 11).

The supplied statistical appendix is conditional on fresh independent paired episodes, bounded scores, fixed choices, and valid measurement. Its width-two Hoeffding correction and any calibration penalty are not free certificates of real usefulness (pp. 20–22).

These values appear in pp. 7–8. The 2,240-case exact grid produces 540 admitted and 1,700 rejected cases; this is coverage of a selected synthetic grid, not the prevalence of real businesses. A conventional unrestricted enumerator obtains the same joint result (pp. 7–9).

The four-way comparison separates shared construction from coordinated commitments. Neither individually supports investment under the restricted reference rules; the combination does. A conventional entrepreneur with the same coordination and reuse options is allowed to match it. The additional route-cost controls matter: $10,000 per successful delivery leaves $88,000 expected contribution; $40,000 makes it −$8,000 (pp. 8, 13). These are assumed total route burdens, not measured protocol fees.

The successor example is equally conditional. After first-stage success, $300,000 is earned above a protected $900,000 reserve. A second setup of $300,000 produces positive expected contribution of $60,000 but needs $450,000 before receipts, so that reserve policy cannot finance it. A supplied setup reduction to $150,000 lowers prefunding to $300,000 and yields $210,000 expected contribution. The full three-leaf expectation above original opening cash is $288,000. Neither cost reduction was technically measured, and no perpetual compounding follows (p. 9).

A natural extension must therefore include nonparticipation, withdrawals, partial delivery, common failure, default, refunds, support, rights, and funding timing where material. Buyer caps and probabilities remain hypotheses until independently elicited. A positive net-present forecast does not permit use of refundable customer principal as working capital. Any advance funding must state permitted uses and obligations (pp. 5–6, 11, 17, 20–21).

Token operation, enterprise receipts, and customer benefit are three separate ledgers. The paper neither grants equity nor infers token price from successful service delivery. It explicitly allows a useful native route to fail against a cheaper conventional one (pp. 12–14, 16).
