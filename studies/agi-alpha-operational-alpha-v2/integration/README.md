## Assessment before spending

`admission.research_gate_envelope(spec)` checks whether any allowed assay could pass the unchanged research gate. That gate requires at least six independent paired clusters, a positive lower per-use advantage, and at least one cent of lower lifetime net value after lifecycle and assessment costs. The theoretical upper envelope is:

`floor(maximum_per_use_delta_cents × forecast_eligible_uses − lifecycle_cost_cents − evaluation_cost_cents)`.
