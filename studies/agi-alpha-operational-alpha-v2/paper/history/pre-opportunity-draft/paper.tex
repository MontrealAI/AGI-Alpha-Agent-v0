\section*{Abstract}
Frontier intelligence is widely available, while problem formulation, context preparation, tool orchestration, error detection and verification remain possible sources of productive advantage. We distinguish a matched human-service reference H, ordinary tool-equipped model use U, an established expert workflow E, and E augmented with a specific newly retained capability \Eplus. Operational Alpha is the effect of E relative to U; additional learned Alpha is the effect of \Eplus\ relative to E. These differences must not be added again to earlier profits that already assumed expert operation.

We conduct a small prospective repair study using the same inherited hosted model and ordinary programming tools. One development job yields three executable contract probes. Four unfamiliar stateful-service missions evaluate each configuration; a separately frozen composition task investigates a next useful offering. An independently authored finite evaluator checks each complete public contract. Exact workflow bytes pass through existing source-verified lifecycle interfaces, with synthetic authority clearly distinguished from live authentication. \PilotFinding\ \CompositionFinding

A structural enterprise simulation uses common opportunity tapes, disjoint A/B/C costs, uncertain quality, customer alternatives, delayed payment, optional retention and development, and an ordinary library with identical contents. Its intervals quantify Monte Carlo uncertainty under assumptions, not the truth of those assumptions. A separate prospective experiment checks admission reachability before purchasing an assessment. The four-job unmetered pilot does not meet the existing native six-cluster monetary gate. The study supports a concrete operating blueprint and an inspectable test of reusable expertise; it does not establish production qualification, ordinary-human representativeness, earned revenue, specialist ASI or sustained compounding.

\textbf{Keywords:} operational expertise; agent workflows; capability transfer; program repair; productive frontier; complete-cost economics; prospective evaluation.

\section{Start with a useful outcome}
A software team has a workflow service that reserves resources, schedules dependent work, receives artifacts and settles internal accounting. A happy-path test passes. Retries can nevertheless repeat an effect, a changed request can inherit an old receipt, a rejected operation can leave partial state, and a caller can mutate an apparently returned snapshot. The buyer needs an executable repair and evidence that the declared behavior now holds. A fluent explanation alone is insufficient.

This is a recognizable engineering problem. AWS's account of idempotent APIs explains the ambiguity of retries and the importance of binding an identity to the intended operation; Temporal's workflow documentation describes durable execution and replay requirements \cite{aws,temporal}. These sources establish technical need and available approaches. They do not establish demand for this specific service. A buyer can fix the service internally, hire a developer, use a mature platform, defer the work or decline.

Our first offering is deliberately bounded: repair a specified Python component, preserve its public contract, deliver executable regressions, and state the limitations. Its usefulness is a corrected behavior that another engineer can inspect and run. The experiment covers sequential in-memory semantics. It does not claim operating-system crash durability, distributed concurrency, real identity verification, real money settlement or a production warranty.

The strategic motivation is the opportunity-discovery vision published at \url{https://agialphaagent.com/}. It suggests a productive cycle: identify valuable work, formulate a useful offering, operate frontier intelligence well, deliver an accepted outcome, preserve surplus and selectively improve reusable expertise. The site's \$15 quadrillion projection and intellectual-property superlatives are promotional claims without a derivation or independent validation on the inspected page \cite{vision}. They are not inputs to our demand model, revenue forecasts or results.

Expert operation can be a productive asset without a new algorithm or weight update. A reusable way to formulate a problem, choose context, stage tool calls, find failures or verify results can reduce waste or improve quality. Prior prompt-program optimization research also treats instructions and workflow structure as optimizable components \cite{dspy,textgrad}. That possibility does not establish this method's benefit; the fresh comparison must do so.

For a frozen task distribution $D$, shared model access $M$, acceptance function $a$ and resource envelope $R$, define descriptive operational effects
\begin{align}
\Delta_{\mathrm{op}} &= \mathbb{E}_{D}[a(E;M,R)-a(U;M,R)],\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{learn}} &= \mathbb{E}_{D}[a(E^{+};M,R)-a(E;M,R)].
\end{align}
These are quality effects at a declared envelope. Economic value additionally depends on complete costs and demand. A recipient's counterfactual is its own state without the received method. A different recipient in another condition is an imperfect proxy for that counterfactual, not direct observation of it.

\subsection{A productive frontier that can be falsified}
Represent offering $o$ by beneficiary, deliverable, acceptance, deadline $d_o$, maximum customer effort $e_o$, price $p_o$ and operating envelope $R_o$. Let $q_o$ be a fixed reliability requirement and $C_k(o)$ the complete cost of configuration $k$, including unsuccessful work attributable to delivering accepted outputs. The economically deliverable set is
\[
\mathcal F_k=\{o:Q_k(o)\geq q_o,\ T_k(o)\leq d_o,\ E^{\rm customer}_k(o)\leq e_o,\ p_o-C_k(o)>0\}.
\]
An exclusive crossing requires the enhanced configuration to meet every frozen condition while the matched baseline does not within the same envelope. If both qualify, report improved economics or parity. If complete cost or customer willingness to pay is unknown, a technical pass cannot become an observed commercial crossing.

This formulation allows transformational changes to problem representation, information acquisition, experiment design or capability composition. It forbids changing the purpose, dropping a requirement or weakening the evaluator and then calling the task solved. A computational artifact also does not validate a physical product.

\section{Experiment before interpretation}
\subsection{A compact expert method}
E receives a small versioned method, constructed before the development job. It maps public operations to state transitions, identity keys, authorization and failure semantics; writes the invariants that must survive successful and rejected operations; reproduces a failure; stages validation before mutation; and checks ordinary use, exact retry, changed intent, partial failure and returned-object isolation where the contract requires them. It then packages code, tests and limits.

U receives the full contract and a reasonable engineering request, the same language runtime, editor and public tests, and permission to use any ordinary programming technique or construct reusable helpers. U is not restricted to a single weak prompt. E's additional method is information, not extra model access or private acceptance data. Both may correct their code within the common budget.

\subsection{Development-origin expertise}
The development reservation ledger exposed four interacting defects: changed reservation terms were accepted as retries, the wrong owner could settle a request, changed settlement amounts were ignored, and snapshots aliased internal state. The solver repaired the service and added 15 regression methods, including 3,200 deterministic operations checked against a record-derived accounting model. The separately authored development acceptance evaluator passed all ten categories.

During that work, the solver extracted three ordinary but reusable assertions: rejection must preserve observable state; an immediate exact retry must preserve result and state; and mutating a returned object must not change service state. The helper deep-copies the baseline before testing. Without that copy, an aliased observation can change with the service and conceal a failure. The helper contains no task-specific solver, customer data or protected answers.

This is researcher-directed artifact construction during delivery. It is neither an autonomous scientific discovery nor a proprietary weight update. The development task was not purchased by a real customer. The distinction matters: it demonstrates an executable route by which useful delivery can produce transferable method bytes, while paid-origin learning and commercial reuse remain hypotheses for live operation.

\section{Do not purchase an impossible assessment}
The earlier commercial correction exposed a logically separate issue: a candidate can be rejected because evidence is poor, or because no evidence allowed by the declared assessment could ever clear its own rule. The latter cannot meaningfully test whether useful skills exist.

For the historical capped direct-savings rule, let $U$ be forecast uses, $t$ transfer effectiveness, $b$ the maximum permitted per-use saving, $f$ the per-use expense, $K$ the remaining fixed expense and $\eta\geq1$ the hurdle. Admission is unreachable if
\[
Utb < \eta(Uf+K)\quad\text{or}\quad Utb\leq Uf+K.
\]
The second condition preserves strict positive net value, including the zero-cost boundary. This is a necessary-condition screen for that rule, not a universal bound on quality, new-offering value or all future enterprise states.

For the unchanged research gate used here, the implementation separately checks the declared maximum independent-cluster count, the allowed per-use advantage support and a one-cent lifetime net threshold. A reachable envelope grants neither admission nor execution authority. The assessor still must produce valid fresh evidence. If an assessment fails after purchase, its expense remains charged. A prescreen is valid only when its claimed bound is justified; a posterior mean is not an upper bound on possible observations.

\section{Gym development and obsolescence}
A Gym campaign is optional. It must specify its starting artifact or policy, environment, observations, actions, feedback, update rule, resource budget and resulting version. Economical prompt, test-selection or orchestration improvements can be tried before supported adapters or reinforcement learning. A training receipt is not a competence certificate.

The retained helper in this edition originates in delivery; no proprietary model weights change. Additional Gym development is not required before recognizing useful delivery. Its enterprise value depends on future eligible reuse, the strength of ordinary alternatives, assessment costs, distribution shift and the time until model defaults make the technique redundant. If a new default already performs the verification reliably, the old helper may become pure overhead. A versioned method must therefore be requalified after material changes in model, tool, mission or distribution.

Reward exploitation is addressed by preserving the original contract and withholding acceptance cases. Overfitting remains possible because the public tasks and helper are small. Negative transfer can occur when a supposed retry legitimately returns time-varying status, when observations cannot be safely copied or compared, or when concurrency changes the state between actions. Complementary specialists need composition tests because individually useful methods can add latency or introduce incompatible assumptions. Sustained superiority on unfamiliar problems would be required for a defined-domain ``specialist ASI'' claim; this study does not make one.

\section{A usable operating blueprint}
\subsection{What to sell first}
Offer a bounded repair-and-regression package with a named source revision, complete contract, reproducible failing example, explicit acceptance and a short limitations note. Quote against credible human alternatives and buyer value, not a token-cost markup. Scope residual review and customer effort before agreement. A larger campaign audit can be offered only after its integrated acceptance, complete costs and customer need qualify.

\subsection{How to deliver it expertly}
Automate intake of a minimal reproduction, source rights and dependencies; create an isolated execution environment; map the contract to transitions and failure semantics; reproduce; patch; test; independently verify; package the result and record cost evidence. Give the same competent tools to the baseline. Record failures and refusal as outcomes. Do not publish a deployment or act on customer systems merely because the local patch passes.

\subsection{What to retain and share}
Retain a method only when its scope, prerequisites, provenance, rights, complete additional expense and fresh recipient benefit are sufficiently supported. Keep existing expertise, newly learned method, newly received method and later improvement as distinct lineage events. Exclude customer secrets, restricted IP, protected cases and scorer internals. Package executable dependencies with the manifest; a prose description alone may lose the useful operation.

Use a proportionate assay. A cheap reversible helper can begin with a small informative experiment; a safety-critical specialist needs much stronger independent evidence. Before paying for either, verify that its own admission rule is attainable within the allowed observations. Include uncertainty about reuse, false admissions, upkeep and obsolescence. Holding cash or declining training is a productive decision when the expected increment is negative.

\subsection{What remains for live operation}
The next live test needs an approved provider account and quotas, metered model/tool bills, qualified execution isolation, durable custody, fresh protected customer-relevant tasks, rights-cleared production source and actual acceptance. It needs independently meaningful reviewer roles, liability bounds, approved resource and identity configuration, and the existing publication and settlement gates where applicable. These are concrete missing operating inputs, not proof that the concept is impossible.

No universal manual-onboarding allowance is imposed. Automate routine preparation and verification where suitable, record the cost of the automation, and measure any residual attention. A small measured gain may justify a cheap workflow even when it cannot support a large specialist-development program. The simulation's false-admission counter identifies nonpositive latent quality increments; a positive quality increment may still be uneconomic and is not exhausted by that counter.

\section{Limits, falsifiers and the next decisive test}
The most immediate falsifier is a strong U configuration that meets the same contract and budget with equal or lower complete cost. Another is recipient failure after apparently successful transfer. A third is insufficient reuse to amortize packaging, assessment and maintenance. Better model defaults, cheaper human competition, correlated validators and hidden customer effort can also erase a nominal advantage. Each is allowed to overturn an attractive story.

This pilot has four researcher-constructed primary missions, one supplemental composition task and one run per configuration. Its evaluators and references are authored within the same team. Case order and hosted service conditions can affect latency. Protocol-level withholding is not secure multi-tenant isolation. Actual bills and complete research expense are missing. The economic environment is assumed, including the mapping from finite acceptance to future quality. No interval over that environment establishes real demand or causal prompting effects outside the sampled tasks.

The next decisive test is a larger, source-frozen, metered paired trial on genuinely unfamiliar customer-relevant repositories. Sample competent ordinary use honestly, preserve available alternatives, record all usage and residual effort, and test recipient transfer without sending solutions. Then seek a small real purchase with the same scope and acceptance. Expand the offering set only after a capability changes a predeclared feasible outcome or produces a measured complete-cost improvement. Neither favorable market size nor a new label substitutes for that evidence.

\section*{AI assistance, provenance and rights}
AI assistants contributed source research, benchmark and workflow construction, code, execution, analysis, internal review and writing. The named author attribution is retained exactly as requested. Independent acceptance means a separate role and separately written checks within this assisted research team, not external replication. Researcher-constructed artifacts are labeled; deterministic sequence generation is not autonomous invention. Hosted task execution is observed; unavailable frontier usage and markets are modeled or unknown as stated. New source is Apache-2.0 and upstream notices remain intact. Shared capability content contains synthetic researcher-controlled material. No proprietary model training rights or customer IP are claimed.
