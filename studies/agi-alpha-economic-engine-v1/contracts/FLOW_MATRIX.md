# Economic flow matrix

Read-only source inspection, 25 October 2026. Exact formulas are in `ROUTE_FORMULAS.md`; machine-readable claims and source ranges are in `FLOW_MATRIX.json`. Source implementation is separate from tests, historical deployment attestations and current commissioning.

| Flow | Asset and payer | Recipient / economic treatment | Authority and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary invoice | chosen fiat currency; Customer | Named supplier and separately contracted collection/tax counterparties. Proposed enterprise policy only; no automatic token demand or 50% reinvestment. | A named enterprise decision-maker would approve surplus appropriations after costs, reserves and liabilities. |
| manager escrow | USDC / 6 decimals; Job employer | Reserved for job settlement/refund. Escrow is an obligation, not supplier profit or free treasury cash. | Constructor selects explicit governor; intake begins paused; role and policy controls govern admission. |
| manager success | USDC / 6 decimals; Employer escrow | Correct-side validators; wallet30; wallet10; assigned agent. Only a legally/economically identified recipient's actual receipt can enter its revenue account; wallet names do not prove ownership. | Posting-time validator rate; wallet rotation only owner with paused intake and zero live escrow/bond reserves; deferred claims retain original beneficiary. |
| manager failure | USDC / 6 decimals; Escrow and independently posted bonds | Employer refund, appropriate bond owners and correct reviewers. Refund liabilities and beneficiary claims are unavailable for discretionary investment. | Source-defined cancellation, deadline, dispute and settlement-pause conditions. |
| alpha access and seed | No payment in these two contracts; ERC721 seed ownership; No token transfer in inspected Access or NovaSeed functions | Operational identity and seed owner. A seed is not evidence of corporate equity or legal IP rights. It constrains a downstream plan; it is not spendable cash. | Access owner controls admission; seed ownership and admitted business role govern downstream operations. |
| alpha oracle stake | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Admitted validator | Same validator on permitted withdrawal. Stake remains committed working capital, not revenue or a cost per successful job absent a loss/opportunity-cost model. | Governor binds marketplace once; admitted validator publishes availability; eligible seed owner requests bounded risk review. |
| alpha market success | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Business directly or MARK reserve on its behalf | Worker and two approving validators; token supply burn; original-client remainder. Worker net token receipts may be revenue to its identified operator; usable USD requires separate realized conversion, liquidity and spending authority. | Admitted ENS business/agent/validators; selected three-person committee; two eligible approvals; one-time MARK binding. |
| alpha market failure | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Escrow and worker bond on slashable failure | Original client refund; burn. Slash receipts are penalties, not stable customer revenue. Refunded client capital cannot be counted as earned profit. | Bounded deadlines and source-state transitions; original client claims once; MARK can collect its own refund. |
| alpha mark funding | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; campaign accounting lots; Admitted campaign funder | Campaign reserve; funder on permitted sell. Reserve is purpose-restricted contributed capital, not free retained earnings. | Opening requires eligible seed owner and green review; selling unused funding remains possible despite revoked admission or expired risk status. |
| alpha mark spend and close | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Pre-funded campaign reserve | JobMarket workers/validators on accepted jobs; remaining reserve to lot holders after close. Unused-capital redemption is not a claim on external profits; plan-authorized construction is possible only within separately funded/approved scope. | Named original business must remain seed owner with same admitted ENS identity and green review; plan proof/index prevents arbitrary spend or replay. |
| jobs success | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Employer reward R plus separately calculated fee F | Worker; weighted validators; FeePool; burn. A payout boost consumes prefunded capital and does not create revenue. Ship defaults are templates, not live configuration. | Configured registry/modules, role identity and tax acknowledgements, sufficient stake, pause and blacklist conditions; governance-only fund/withdraw operator reward pool. |
| jobs failure and nonreveal | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Failed-job escrow and independently slashed role stakes | Configured employer / treasury / operator / validator shares and burn. A full-buyer-refund assumption is invalid for the general failed route. Numerical failed-route economics NOT MODELED unless every relevant parameter and branch is frozen. | Owner-adjustable review parameters, governance-defined distributions, eligible/locked stake and source phase/deadline conditions. |
| jobs fee pool | AGIALPHA / 18 decimals; Job fees transferred by StakeManager; separate voluntary contributors | Burn, configured boosted-stake role claimants, limited treasury dust. Eligible staker fee rights are not an entitlement for all passive tokenholders, fixed yield, or enterprise-profit share. Permissioned treasury withdrawal is not automatic appropriation. | Owner sets role, burn, allowed treasury and rewarders; governance-only withdrawal target is configured treasury or burn address. |
| fleet boundary | USDC or AGIALPHA retained as explicit planning context; No payment created by the inspected offline project journey | Requested report and internal check; no automatic contract settlement. Useful execution evidence is not revenue, current deployment authorization or an enterprise treasury bridge. | Explicit scoped local authorization and separate funded-operation commissioning; productionQualified remains false in the source preview. |
