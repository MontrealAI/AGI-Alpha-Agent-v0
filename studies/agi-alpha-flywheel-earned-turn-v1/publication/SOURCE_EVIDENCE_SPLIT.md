## Included sources

`prepare_source_selection.py` records each selected relative path, byte count and SHA-256. It always checks and
includes the 32-file scientific freeze and the complete unchanged vendor manifest/closure. It selects:

- Root research README, reproduction/package guide, requirements and license when supplied.
- `companion/` Python source; `runtime/` Python/Node source, documentation and small component QA records;
  every exact upstream file under `runtime/vendor/`.
- Browser executable and presentation source, single-file HTML, recorded result panel, locks/manifests and
  textual checker/journey/recovery records. Browser screenshots are excluded.
- Paper TeX, bibliography, build/figure code, author PDF and editable PDF/SVG figures. Renderer screenshots,
  logs and intermediate TeX products are excluded.
- Complete protocol source, development charter and intervention ledger; declared development source,
  calibration, variant and failed-attempt summaries.
- Compact confirmation analysis, derived trace, execution/seed/summary records and independent review outputs.
  Per-trial raw cases, arrays, packs and journals remain in the separately indexed archive.
- Public source/policy/foundation identity records and exact inspected interfaces needed to substantiate the
  integration mapping; existing source PDFs and extracted full paper texts are not copied into the source PR.
- Exact final process-recovery summary/verification, publication provenance and final textual PDF QA records.

## Explicit exclusions

The source selection excludes `helpers/`, all private transfer machinery, provider download locators,
signed transport URLs, the full checkpoint directory, raw archive bodies, compiled bytecode, virtual
environments, `node_modules`, Git state, duplicate deliverables, raster screenshots and intermediate render
products. It excludes the full 1.5 GB native trial directory from loose Git blobs. Existing public historical
papers are linked and hashed, not reissued as new results.

The selector scans selected bytes for private provider locators and fails rather than silently rewriting
scientific or frozen files. If a non-scientific custody receipt requires a public projection, that projection
must identify its omitted transport fields and preserve the original artifact identity. The original remains
preserved in its identified custody or prior-study archive, with availability explicit in the docket. A public
projection cannot replace a frozen executable or measurement. The historical Strategy Laboratory audit has
the distinctly named `HISTORICAL_STRATEGY_LAB_AUDIT.public.md` projection; the original filename is excluded
from this public selection. `PUBLIC_PROJECTIONS.json` records both byte identities and the two omitted
provider transport identifiers by field count, without exposing their values.

## Reproduction and availability

The source-only browser computes its four fresh missions immediately. Source freeze verification and
small component tests do not require the large archive. Independent reanalysis of original trial arrays,
portable factors, receipts and journals requires the matching raw archive restored at its original relative
paths. A source checkout without those paths is not a complete reproduction of the original audit.

Do not use unbounded or overwrite-capable extraction. Validate the archive's exact bytes and every indexed
member, reject absolute paths, parent traversal, links and non-ordinary entries, and refuse a destination
whose existing bytes differ. The archive contains data and inert source; extraction does not execute it.
