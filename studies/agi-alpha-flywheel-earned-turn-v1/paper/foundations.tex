\section{Use the foundations as engineering obligations}

The foundational AGI ALPHA paper supplies an organizational substrate: bounded work, explicit evaluation,
reusable means and attributable evidence \citep{foundation_agialphaunifiedpublicationfinal}.
\TI{} contributes a sharper experimental demand: change the means of satisfying a purpose while leaving
the external acceptance contract fixed, then remove the proposed change to test its contribution
\citep{foundation_transformationalintelligence}. Here the purpose remains a checked network response.
The executable method can change from solving a fresh linear system to invoking a retained factorization,
then to applying a scoped update instrument. A conventional implementation receives the same options.
The symbol $\infty$ names the research programme; it grants neither unbounded resources nor a presumed result.

\subsection{Every Out-stage has an observable job}

\phase{Identify} chooses a declared worthwhile request or a repeated bottleneck and checks its scope and
remaining accounts. \phase{Out-Learn} measures the construction, checking and reuse costs on bounded
development cases. \phase{Out-Think} challenges a reuse claim with a fresh load, changed operator or removed
parent. These stages preserve failures and counterexamples rather than revising the final evaluator
\citep{foundation_outlearn,foundation_outthink}.

\phase{Out-Design} constructs an executable factor or update package with a current dependency record.
\phase{Out-Strategise} compares the available specialist, conventional reuse, prospective adoption cost and
remaining recurrence before allocating more work \citep{foundation_outdesign,foundation_outstrategise}.
\phase{Out-Execute} dispatches the fixed adapter through the existing Alpha workbench and obtains a new
balance/energy check. \phase{Out-Evolve} retains, declines or invalidates the asset and tests whether it
contributes to a distinct construction \citep{foundation_outexecute,foundation_outevolve}.

The source papers' physical calibration, finite-code theorems, bit-parallel machine model, institutional proof
network and commercial programme remain outside this experiment. Their specific role here is to demand
purpose preservation, useful construction, full accounting and separately checked succession. Familiar
numerical methods remain familiar numerical methods; assigning them an Out-stage does not create a novel algorithm.
