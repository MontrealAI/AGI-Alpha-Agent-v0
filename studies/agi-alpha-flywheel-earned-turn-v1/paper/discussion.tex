\section{What this flywheel has earned}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{A stronger mechanism, with a narrower economic conclusion}

The new experiment resolves a concrete weakness in Strategy Succession: the retained object now participates
in constructing a distinct capability, and that capability is used on fresh, qualified work. C is causally
downstream of the actual accepted B08 response. Removing the parent while permitting exact reconstruction
changes the measured work required to obtain the same successor. An independently constructed equivalent
is also allowed, and conventional reuse receives the same algorithm vocabulary, information and resources.
These interventions distinguish productive inheritance from a decorative ancestry label.

The scientific advance is this complete, inspectable construction path inside the existing workbench, coupled
to an honest admission decision. It is not a new sparse-solver theorem, an autonomous discovery of the
Woodbury identity, or evidence that a general-purpose agent beats equally enabled conventional learning.
The numerical methods are established; their combination, inherited state, decision context and full costs
are what this experiment tests. No language model generates algorithms during confirmation.

The distinction matters because the strongest conventional controls already solve every registered request.
The flywheel has useful reuse and construction improvement within its declared scope, but no measured
capability expansion in qualified coverage. It does not pass the whole-system productivity gate. Accumulating
18 correct instruments cannot reverse that decision. A system that preserves the incumbent and reports
\textsf{HOLD} is performing a necessary part of selective improvement.

\begin{insight}[Three claims that must remain separate]
\textbf{Benchmark qualification:} a method satisfies the frozen mathematical and scope checks.\par
\textbf{Product adoption:} the complete method clears the required benefit against the best available
alternative. This experiment does not clear that gate.\par
\textbf{Investment recovery:} cumulative attributable benefit repays discovery, failed variants, development,
integration, maintenance and human effort. Unknown human/model costs and unestablished incremental benefit
leave recovery unresolved.
\end{insight}

\subsection{The bottleneck moved outside the algebra}

The development screen correctly identified shared structure: repeated loads and small positive operator
changes admit economical reuse. It did not adequately establish how much of the complete accountable
workflow that structure could save. Native factorization was already cheap at these problem sizes, while
input binding, workbench execution, verification, custody and persistence remained substantial.

A post-confirmation headroom calculation makes the design error tangible. In the recurring regime, the
native C action averages 66.53\,ms against 1,642.07\,ms for the entire native stream, about 4.05\%. Even
eliminating that entire action would not alone establish a 5\% stream improvement; other savings would also
be needed. This is a retrospective diagnosis, not a preregistered impossibility bound. It explains why a
visibly better construction step was an insufficient basis for an optimistic whole-system forecast.

The local benefit is nevertheless measurable. Across the same eighteen scoped streams, the inherited
algorithm/solve/persistence phase averages 1.879\,ms versus 11.570\,ms after exact parent reconstruction.
The corresponding complete C actions average 57.447 and 69.434\,ms, with inheritance lower in seventeen
streams. The independently constructed equivalent averages 10.462\,ms and 69.466\,ms. These post-run
attribution summaries support a local mechanism; they do not replace the registered whole-stream analysis
or supply a multiplicity-corrected new efficacy claim. The validation-path asymmetry described in
Section~\ref{sec:results} further limits interpretation of the native-constructor contrast.

\subsection{Retention has a physical and operational price}

The flywheel and conventional-reuse arms each retain 49,545,144 bytes in 48 unique physical capability files
across 24 streams: about 2.06\,MB per stream. Native refactorization retains 79,861,440 bytes in 48 files;
exact rebuilding retains 88,767,528 bytes in 72; independently equivalent construction retains 72,626,520
bytes in 72. The direct specialist retains no factor NPZ files, although it still uses ordinary matrix and
response state. These counts deduplicate logical aliases that refer to the same immutable path. They are
capability storage, not the substantially larger raw research-evidence archive.

A smaller retained footprint is one useful property, not sufficient proof of value. The factor must remain
loadable, applicable and bound to its dependencies. Maintenance pays for those obligations, and the matched
maintenance regime exposes a case where direct CG is cheaper. The implementation bounds the number of
missions and candidate assets; it does not demonstrate an indefinite archive with free retention or a
long-horizon optimal retirement policy. Immutable historical evidence and a currently admissible executable
are different objects. Neither automatically inherits fresh proof or operational authority.

\subsection{What the feedback account actually proves}

The resource ledger proves conservation and permitted reassignment: accepted service work can release
unused externally reserved CPU allowance, and a subsequent bounded action can spend it without borrowing
the separate research account. It does not prove that the method caused the released headroom. Controls
receive the same generous reservations, and the aggregate point difference against native refactorization
is only 280.073\,ms across all streams, with uncertainty including no advantage. Calling the 9,600\,ms
reassigned allowance ``earned savings'' would overstate the evidence by both origin and amount.

Actual CPU accounting is closer to usable capacity than an invented operation currency, but one host's
unused reservation is still not cash, an operator's recovered hour, or a market margin. There is no paid
demand in this study. A prospective commercial decision would require measured arrival rates, service
commitments, utilization, deployment costs and an actual price for the capacity or labor saved. Token supply
and price are irrelevant to whether this numerical instrument creates productive value.

The complete research investment is also larger than the participant totals. The preserved record includes
screening, six numerical variants, three bounded campaign attempts, two incomplete development failures,
independent verification and publication work. The final raw audit alone adds 98.522\,s of CPU. Human task
definition, source inspection, debugging, review and setup occurred before or outside the automated run;
their durations and model charges were not reliably measured. They are unknown costs, not zero costs.

\subsection{Limits and the next falsifiable hypothesis}

These are synthetic grounded networks on one host, with 24 independent seed streams and four prescribed
regimes. Loads within a stream share an operator and are not independent replications. The tested finite
foreground loop does not establish unattended production uptime, distributed scalability or persistent
operation after its process exits. The source-bound checker and second aggregator improve inspectability,
but the commissioned team and host are shared. Instrumented parent calls are not an external CPU trace.
The process-recovery probe and conservative pending-action quarantine establish specific failure behavior,
not a general durable distributed transaction protocol.

The next hypothesis is specific and remains unexecuted: \emph{when sparse factorization occupies a material
share of whole-workflow cost, a retained response instrument can make small operator changes cheaper even
after matched verification, persistence and maintenance.} A bounded three-dimensional network family is one
candidate because its fill-in can make factorization more costly. The first experiment should be a small
end-to-end headroom screen with the same cheap competent builder, native reuse and conventional learning.
Reject the family if the maximum plausible removable cost cannot clear the required margin. Compact
incremental provenance is another candidate improvement, but it must preserve the same guarantees and be
available to all relevant comparators.

Any such test needs a new identity, new development cap, newly frozen analysis and untouched confirmation
seeds. The present negative gate does not authorize another search on these 24 streams. Execution improvement
and construction improvement should be established before a further claim that the discovery process itself
improves. Increasing the rate of improvement would then require its own repeated, comparable measurements.
A useful system need not accelerate forever; this one has not yet demonstrated acceleration.

\begin{insight}[Build a flywheel that earns its next turn]
A mission can leave behind an executable means of doing later work. This experiment demonstrates that path
and its verification. Earning the next turn requires the additional whole-system benefit that this campaign
did not establish. The appropriate successor decision is to retain the evidence, keep competent service,
and leave product admission on \textsf{HOLD}.
\end{insight}
