\section{A frozen experiment with strong alternatives}
\label{sec:experiment}

\subsection{Development, freeze and protected confirmation}

The development charter fixes three domains, four screen seeds, two sizes per domain and twelve repeat queries.
The screening results are preserved, including a very cheap scheduling baseline and effective ordinary shortest-path
reuse. Sparse-network analysis is selected for its inspectable construction mechanism: an accepted response can
select a different operator and its parent solve can construct that operator's response instrument. The screen is
small and purposeful, not evidence that this domain is commercially optimal or uniquely suitable.

Six numerical variants are investigated on four declared development seeds: two native sparse orderings,
cold and warm iterative solves, and two low-rank sizes. Development calibrates a fixed cost forecast. It also
exposes the substantial checking, hashing, dispatch and persistence burden omitted by an isolated kernel timing.
Three full campaign attempts and a bounded remainder are retained. The third campaign stops on a restore taking
154.044\,ms against a 150\,ms reservation. Its remainder stops on 274.58\,ms against 250\,ms. Final conservative
resource limits are set before confirmation; those unsuccessful attempts are not overwritten or counted as final passes.

Independent review adds consequential counterexamples. Integer grounding values initially caused diagonal
assembly truncation; malformed updates and incoherent restored identities needed refusal; an existing-output
failure handler could overwrite prior evidence; mutable factor filenames could overwrite the incumbent. The
corrections, pre-fix examples, narrow regression checks and reviewer authorship overlap are preserved. No fourth
efficacy campaign is run to seek a preferred outcome.

The 32-file freeze is created at 01:09:39 UTC on 25 October 2026, corresponding to the evening of 24 October in
Montr\'eal. The frozen protocol archive is saved before a prospective notice in issue \#4850
\citep{earnedFreezeNotice}. Fresh network seeds are generated only afterward. The single campaign uses this
closure unchanged, including both aggregation implementations, the checker, calibration, dependencies and
browser execution modules. The browser's small arithmetic demonstration is a distinct experiment.

\subsection{Tasks, regimes and treatments}

Twenty-four independent network streams are assigned evenly to four fixed regimes. Conductances are sampled
uniformly from $[0.5,2]$ on the declared grid, grounding is $0.1$, and successive loads are fresh normal draws.
Each stream delivers A, eight B requests, C, fresh D requests and a changed-grounding request. A negative
conductance request is a separate prospectively unsupported case. It must be explicitly refused.

C's edge choice uses the actual accepted B08 response in each arm. All resulting task identities are compared
across arms. A divergence would be a reported comparability defect. The fixed offered denominator includes
unattempted missions after failure. A failed trial writes its partial inputs, solutions, receipts and summary;
it cannot disappear because its loop ended early. Each trial directory is created atomically and cannot be
silently reused to overwrite accepted history.

\subsection{Three admissions and one primary endpoint}

\textbf{Correctness} concerns the exact output and operator scope. \textbf{Provisional research use} additionally
requires a development kernel forecast: candidate construction plus remaining use, with a 20\% surcharge, must
beat native construction plus use. This forecast does not estimate every lifecycle cost; it is explicitly not
product admission. The reconstruction ablations may execute outside that forecast to identify construction dependence.

\textbf{Product admission} requires fresh whole-participant evidence. Define, for paired stream costs $T_i$,
\begin{equation}
 R=\frac{\frac{1}{24}\sum_i T_i^{\mathrm{flywheel}}}
          {\frac{1}{24}\sum_i T_i^{\mathrm{native\ refactor}}}.
\end{equation}
The primary gate requires the upper bound of a two-sided 95\% confidence interval for $R$ below $0.95$,
all offered flywheel outcomes qualified, and no trial/resource violation. A conventional-reuse guard requires
its corresponding upper CPU-ratio bound below $1.10$, with full matched comparator qualification. Failure
leaves product status on HOLD. Even a pass would not recover unknown human and monetary research investment.

The registered analysis resamples complete paired network streams within their four strata, six draws per
stratum, for 10,000 bootstrap replicates using seed 20261025. Arm order is shuffled with seed 20261024. Other
contrasts, regime-specific results and construction timings are descriptive. There is one confirmation campaign,
no optional exclusion and no post-outcome retuning or rerun. Protected tasks and authority rules are outside
the candidate's closed data-construction interface; this is an integrity-bound same-host design, not a separately
administered evaluation enclave.

\subsection{Cost and resource boundaries}

The primary cost is measured Python process CPU plus actual reaped workbench-child CPU. It includes stream
setup, matrix assembly, hashing, checking, dispatch, retention, failed work, restoration and writing raw artifacts.
Wall time is measured separately. Kernel CPU, checker CPU and action CPU are nested components, not quantities
to add again to the total. Final report serialization and package production are research overhead outside the
participant measurement. Import, development, review and independent audit costs remain separately attributable.
Unknown human duration, model fees and monetary cost are represented as unknown.

Per stream, the final dispatch accounts contain 10,000\,ms of service allowance and 1,000\,ms of externally
supplied improvement allowance. Released quota starts at zero and is capped at 500\,ms. Each accepted B may
transfer up to 50\,ms of its unused 1,000\,ms service reservation; eight B requests can therefore transfer
400\,ms. C reserves at most 400\,ms from that account. Restore reserves 600\,ms from improvement. Shared startup,
custody settlement and aggregate artifact writing have a separately measured 2,000\,ms external control bound.
Every treatment receives the same opportunity except the explicit no-reinvestment ablation.

An observed native-call overrun is charged and refused; these accounting controls do not physically preempt
an indivisible BLAS call. Most critically, unused quota is externally supplied capacity. Its transfer and debit
show operational resource feedback. Only the paired timing comparison can establish an incremental saving,
and neither observation creates cash or proves a self-financing enterprise.
