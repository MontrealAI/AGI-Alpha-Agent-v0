# Recovered Strategy Laboratory: provenance, verification, and scientific limits

## Finding

The original **SUCCESSOR Ω — Strategy Laboratory v0.1.0** archive and the complete **202610180921.docx** were recovered. Historical results need not be recreated. The archive contains source, protocols, development populations, frozen candidates, confirmation seeds, per-case artifacts, statistical summaries, hash-linked history, registry, original test/replay logs, and the earlier pilot experiment.

The preserved experiment supports a narrow but substantive finding: a bounded local search process proposed arithmetic priority programs, tested exact candidates on fresh generated tasks, promoted two successive route priorities and one scheduling priority, and retained incumbents when its registered evidence threshold was not met. ## 3. Executed mechanism

`omega/programs.py` defines a restricted JSON arithmetic AST with domain, version and expression only. Allowed operations are addition, subtraction, multiplication, protected division, minimum and maximum. Constants are finite and bounded by 8 in absolute value; depth is at most 6 and size at most 47 nodes. Evaluation clamps arithmetic values. Candidates cannot contain shell commands, arbitrary Python, authority fields, or evaluator changes.

Each domain/round uses 40 training and 40 validation tasks. Evolutionary development considers 72 distinct candidates. It seeds search with the incumbent, original reference, naive strategy and up to three prior nominees; then uses elite selection, subtree mutation/crossover and random restarts. The top six candidates and the incumbent receive validation evaluation. An equal-count random-search control receives the same training/validation cases and the same number of candidate evaluations. It includes the incumbent and otherwise draws random expressions. The selected candidate and all controls are frozen before confirmation seeds are sampled.

Confirmation executes seven policies on each task: candidate, incumbent, original reference, strong reference, selected random-search control, unchanged sham and empty-artifact control. The registered promotion gate compares the candidate with its **incumbent**. The strong and random comparisons are visible, but have no separately allocated superiority test in the protocol.

The local history retains all nominees for development search, including held nominees. Only candidates passing the gate become incumbents. A held candidate's reuse as a future proposal seed is permitted development reuse; it is not readmission or inherited proof. The route incumbent in attempt 4 is exactly the accepted attempt-1 candidate.

The runtime supplies cooperative STOP, bounded rounds/attempts, persistent registry, hash-linked journal, source/configuration commitments and resume checks. A registered interrupted attempt consumes its testing allowance. These are local process features. There is no persistent service after process exit, autonomous customer acquisition, or live production authority. A hard crash can require manual stale-lock removal after establishing that no original process remains.

### Strong specialist comparison

`omega/engine.py` defines the route strong reference as fixed weighted A* with weight 2. Evidence and scheduling use the same program for their original and strong references; these are not separately optimized specialist competitors.

| Route attempt | Discovered strategy | Fixed weight-2 A* | Random-search nominee | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| First | 0.824725 | **0.890632** | 0.694144 | Promotion over weak budget-constrained ordinary A* loses to the stronger specialist |
| Second | **0.917588** | 0.892152 | 0.849101 | Descriptive score advantage over this fixed specialist; superiority was not its registered admission hypothesis |

The ordinary A* reference scores about 0.08 under the deliberately tight expansion budget. The source explicitly discloses that poor match. It is unsuitable as the sole comparator for a general improvement claim.

The final route program is interpretable. It maximizes

`0.7132 × (0.3998 × g − max(2.203, terrain)) − (g + h)`.

On these bounded inputs, where arithmetic clamping does not bind, this is equivalent to minimizing

`g + 1.398870138 × h + 0.997674183 × max(2.203, terrain)`.

It is a terrain-augmented weighted-A* priority. The experiment does not compare it with a similarly budgeted search over conventional A* weights and terrain penalties. There is no evidence of a new general pathfinding state of the art.

## 5. Repeated testing and the scope of the uncertainty claim

The registered score is bounded in [0,1]. For paired differences D in [−1,1], the code computes the lower bound

`L = max(−1, mean(D) − sqrt(2 s² log(2/alpha)/n) − 14 log(2/alpha)/(3(n−1)))`,

where s² is the unbiased sample variance. The protocol derives this from the cited empirical-Bernstein bound via a bounded-variable transform. This audit independently recomputes the formula; it is not an external proof of the theorem or the data-generating assumptions.

For global attempt t, nominal and shift tests each receive `total_alpha / (2t(t+1))`. Promotion requires a changed candidate, nominal lower bound greater than 0.005 and shifted lower bound at least −0.08. That shifted tolerance permits some degradation; it must not be described as proof of no regression.

The original six-attempt pilot uses total_alpha=0.05 and spends 3/70 ≈ 0.04285714. The final workspace reserves total_alpha=1/140 ≈ 0.00714286 for all of its future attempts. Its first six attempts spend 3/490 ≈ 0.00612245. The two preserved six-attempt runs therefore spend 12/245 ≈ 0.04897959 combined; the full reserved final allowance plus the six pilot attempts is at most 0.05. This accounting does not permit silently resuming the pilot beyond six, opening arbitrary further workspaces with fresh 0.05 budgets, or treating the strong/random descriptive comparisons as covered confirmatory superiority tests.

The pilot contains 13,824 confirmation seeds; the final run contains 27,648. Their intersection is empty. Final development seeds are also disjoint from final confirmation seeds. Pilot/final candidate development is not claimed to be independent: the source modules match except report rendering, and the deterministic development seed scheme is reused.

## 6. Full cost is the key missing gate

Equal exploration budgets were implemented. Equal total computation, elapsed time, monetary cost and human burden were not established.

The source reveals exact timing boundaries:

- `engine.search` constructs training/validation tasks and route Dijkstra oracles **before** its development timer starts.
- `engine.evaluate_frozen` generates each confirmation task and computes its route oracle **before** the per-policy timer starts.
- The per-policy timer stops immediately after candidate execution and **before** artifact judging, statistical analysis, serialization or journal work.

The reported confirmation execution time is therefore solver time only. The total recorded development interval across six attempts is 10.617974 seconds and the sum of recorded solver intervals is 61.328411 seconds; adding them still does not produce a complete end-to-end experiment cost. No complete authoring, setup, debugging, human review, maintenance, retrieval, storage lifecycle, CPU-energy or monetary ledger is present.

The final route comparison illustrates why this matters:

Across nominal and shifted cases, the final candidate's recorded solver time is 1.723292 seconds, compared with 1.066404 seconds for weight-2 A*: a ratio of 1.616. This is an original recorded interval comparison without timing confidence intervals, not a controlled current performance benchmark. It establishes that the preserved quality gain does not automatically imply lower computational cost.

The gate rewards benchmark score, not enterprise net value. It has no quantified benefit threshold after discovery, failed proposals, independent checking, maintenance or human intervention. The record does not establish break-even, paid demand, revenue, profit or liquidity.

## 7. Independence and authority

The original checker is separate code from candidate strategy search; route quality uses its own Dijkstra implementation. Candidates inhabit a grammar that cannot edit the checker. The code commits source, candidate, development and seed files and validates lineage. This is a useful local proof boundary.

All components nevertheless share local administration. There is no separate institution, protected evaluation service, external timestamping authority, or proof that a hostile host cannot rewrite an entire evidence package. Candidate search cannot express such changes, but local integrity checks are not a security boundary against the administrator. The source explicitly records `independent_external_review:false` and authority `BENCHMARK_ONLY`.

The original tests and this audit do not confer production authority. No live provider calls, credentials, token transactions, real contract settlement, or production deployment were part of the original experiment. The optional model proposer has mocked transport/failure tests; live operation remains unverified by this archive.

## 8. What transfers to the new flywheel

The strongest reusable idea is an executable strategy with a declared scope, a fixed evaluator, fresh evaluation, measured cost and revocable admission. The original produces two successive accepted route priorities and seeds later search from earlier nominees. It does not isolate whether that inheritance caused the second improvement; there is no memory-removal/replacement test and no equally enabled conventional strategy-learning control.

The new edition should therefore treat the archive as a verified foundation, with explicit extensions:

1. Begin with competitive specialists and allow conventional adaptation the same tools and search opportunities.
2. Freeze mission streams, opportunity assumptions, resource accounting, exclusion and stopping rules before confirmation.
3. Split correctness/applicability from net-benefit admission. Count the cost of discovering, rejecting, checking, retrieving, maintaining and retiring assets.
4. Demonstrate an inherited method helping construct or qualify a distinct later capability, with removal and replacement tests.
5. Retain an incumbent, reject a descendant, defer discovery when expected reuse cannot cover cost, and retire stale assets.
6. Run the bounded mission selector through the supported existing AGI ALPHA path; do not rename this standalone lab an installed production runtime.
7. Make every source, checker, scope and authority boundary visible in the browser and evidence docket.

## 9. DOCX lead: disagreement as a productive asset

The recovered DOCX is a conversational research lead. Its central constructive proposal is:

**Creator → evidence-grounded challenger → independent adjudication → revision → regression asset.**

The challenger must have the original evidence. It must identify a consequential and reproducible defect, cite the supporting evidence, and explain why the defect matters for the intended mission. A critique is a candidate claim requiring verification. False, trivial, out-of-scope or unsupported criticisms should not compel revision. Resolved counterexamples become reusable regression assets, with protected confirmation cases kept separate.

The document also proposes strategy as an object of improvement: how a system searches, investigates, verifies, coordinates and allocates a fixed amount of resources. A mission can produce a method rather than only an answer; a method can help discover or qualify subsequent methods. This is a hypothesis for the new experiment, not something established by the historical scores alone.

Its proposal to challenge evaluators must be separated from the candidate's admission test. An evaluator revision can have its own development and validation process, but cannot redefine success after observing a candidate's confirmation failures. Correlated model agreement is not independent proof. A separately implemented checker still has common-mode design risks, which need disclosure and adversarial regression tests.

The DOCX mentions external research and historical anecdotes. This audit uses their methodological role only. It does not independently verify the Leclerc account, the described Apple study, or other external claims. Those should receive fresh primary-source verification if used in the new paper. The complete original remains available rather than selectively quoting the persuasive sections.
