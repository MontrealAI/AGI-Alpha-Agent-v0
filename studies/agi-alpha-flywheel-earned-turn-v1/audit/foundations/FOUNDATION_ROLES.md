## Claim ladder to implement

1. **Validity:** output satisfies the frozen mission and numerical/residual contract.
2. **Applicability:** retained method’s matrix, dependencies, update class and tolerance remain valid.
3. **Marginal product adoption:** prospective future cost/quality beats the strongest available alternative, conditional on costs already sunk.
4. **Investment recovery:** accumulated realized benefit covers all attributable development, failed candidates, qualification, integration and maintenance costs. Unknown human/money costs remain unknown.
5. **Construction succession:** the actual accepted prior state and retained executable method materially assist construction or qualification of a distinct capability. An ancestry pointer is insufficient.
6. **Feedback:** a resource that service actually releases is allocated and consumed by the next construction under explicit accounts. Externally supplied research funds are separate.
7. **Acceleration:** improvement rate increases under comparable conditions. Cumulative work alone does not qualify.

The proposed new study should implement the first six as separate recorded propositions and leave the seventh unclaimed unless a predeclared repeated-rate experiment supports it.
