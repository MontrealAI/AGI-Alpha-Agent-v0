## What transfers

The numerical mission is a response analysis on a grounded conductance network. Its outputs are modeled node responses, checked by a separately implemented edge-balance and energy verifier. This is software computation on synthetic inputs, not physical construction or validation of real infrastructure.

The native finite stream is:

1. **A:** deliver a useful initial response with a competent native specialist. Its factorization is an executable byproduct; the system need not pay for an artificial second builder.
2. **B01–B08:** answer fresh loads on the same network, rechecking input/operator bindings and every result.
3. **C:** select positive edge changes from the largest absolute flows in the actual accepted **B08** result. Compare a scoped update instrument with the available native alternative. If constructed, its provenance binds that preceding receipt and output.
4. **D:** evaluate fresh loads on the changed network. A successful inheritance claim requires both actual parent use and a qualified result.
5. **SHIFT and refusal:** a changed grounding invalidates the inherited scope and invokes the specialist; nonpositive conductance is explicitly refused.

The sparse factorization and Woodbury update are established numerical methods. The contribution under test is bounded, causal, full-cost succession through an existing work system. Conventional implementations receive the same mathematical options. A lineage label, a smaller program or a valid output is not enough to establish productive advantage.

## The comparisons are part of the product

The fixed design has **24 independent network streams**, six in each of four regimes, and **seven arms**. It varies recurrence, update rank and maintenance. Paired comparisons use complete streams as the independent unit; correlated loads within a stream are not counted as independent experiments.

## Keep three decisions separate

- **Correctness and applicability:** the output satisfies the frozen numerical contract and the retained method applies to these exact inputs and dependencies.
- **Prospective product admission:** using the method is worthwhile against the best available alternative after the costs relevant to that decision.
- **Investment recovery:** all attributable development, rejected candidates, construction, checking, integration, maintenance and human effort have been repaid.

A provisional cost forecast is not final product admission. A sensible adoption after sunk costs is not proof that the complete research investment has been recovered.

## What resources mean here

Service and improvement have separate enforceable reservations. A third account starts at zero and can receive a bounded transfer of unused **already supplied service allowance** after an accepted B mission. The ledger records both its origin and any subsequent construction debit. The transfer conserves externally supplied quota; it creates no cash and does not, by itself, show capability-caused savings. Independent whole-workflow comparisons must establish that additional claim.

Native accounting separates Python and child-process CPU, elapsed time, action reservations, control overhead, storage and outside research work. Indivisible native calls can overrun a reservation: their actual cost is charged and acceptance is held. This is observed-cost enforcement, not an operating-system guarantee of instantaneous preemption. Full operator authoring duration, model charges and monetary investment remain unknown where not measured. Unknown is not zero; CPU and work units are not converted into revenue or profit.

These exclude inter-participant gaps and outside research, and actions within a stream are dependent. The larger network regime tests computational burden; one serial foreground campaign does not establish concurrent service scalability or a production SLA.
