# Design review before confirmation

**Status: pre-confirmation recommendations; this is not an outcome report.** The proposed family is grounded sparse-network analysis. A worthwhile mission solves a declared network for a load vector. Accepted work may retain a factorization; fresh loads reuse it. A changed-operator successor uses low-rank positive edge additions derived from the actual preceding accepted response and can construct a Woodbury update instrument using the retained solves.

## What would count as a stronger contribution

The contribution can be an executed, honest mechanism for retaining and selectively reinvesting in useful numerical capabilities. Sparse factorization reuse and Woodbury identities are established conventional methods. Their inclusion makes the incumbent stronger; rediscovering their names is not a novel numerical algorithm. The experiment should distinguish integration novelty, causal evidence of transfer and any measured advantage beyond equally enabled conventional implementation.

A conventional reuse system given the same factors, builder, checks, budgets and update history may tie the flywheel. Such a tie is informative: it establishes that useful behavior is implemented by ordinary reusable computation, while preventing an unsupported AGI-specific superiority claim.

The domain screen should be reported as prospectively bounded development, not an exhaustive search for the best domain. Its exact finite alternatives and all results remain visible. Cached batch solves in the screen may have a different API/BLAS advantage from sequential queries; confirmatory comparator access must be equal.

## Causal mission journey

- A: deliver a checked response and make the exact native factorization a candidate retained asset. Count factor construction once in the service that actually required it; charge retention, serialization, compatibility inspection and any extra qualification separately.
- B: change the load vector, preserve the operator, recheck operator identity and scope, execute the retained solve, and obtain a fresh independently checked response. Log actual parent solve calls rather than only an ancestry field.
- C: derive the positive update from the actual accepted B response. Record B's receipt and task identity, the update selection rule and the changed operator. Construct the low-rank instrument through calls to the parent solver. Check the child on fresh loads before scoped admission.
- Shift: demonstrate a changed dependency, unsupported weight or invalid factor identity that causes rejection/retirement/refactorization. A parent pointer must never bypass applicability checks.

The parent-removed treatment must be allowed to reconstruct using the cheapest competent builder, and a strong direct solver must be allowed to solve the new operator without rebuilding the exact historical search. An equivalent independently built asset should be constructed by its actual method with its cost recorded; a renamed parent is not independent construction.

## Comparator fairness

For each unchanged and changed operator, consider direct native sparse factorization, a justified iterative specialist such as warm-start preconditioned CG, conventional factor retention and an equally enabled low-rank update workflow. Freeze any policy choosing between them before confirmation. Do not run every solver on a confirmation task and retroactively call the fastest one a cost-free incumbent. If an ensemble runs all solvers, charge all executed work.

The strongest justification can come from the bounded development set and stable algorithmic structure, not from hidden held-out tuning. If the changed operator or update rank favors ordinary refactorization, preserve that result and retain the specialist. At small network sizes, lookup, checking, persistence or IPC may dominate factor savings; all belong in the applicable service cost.

Supply all comparators the same graph, load, accepted-state history and available libraries. Distinguish information equality from compute equality. Common corpus construction and evaluation are research costs even when excluded from per-arm conditional service measurements. Record that boundary.

## Three different economic decisions

Correctness admission requires a checked exact candidate within its defined scope. Product adoption compares future execution and maintenance burden against the best currently available competent alternative. Full-investment assessment includes domain screening, unsuccessful variants, original construction, qualification, review, integration, discovery, retries and attributable human work.

Already spent construction does not belong in a marginal decision to use an otherwise worthwhile existing factor. It still belongs in the investment record. The earlier study conflated these questions, so the new records should have separate fields and decisions.

If native factors cannot be serialized and must be rebuilt after a restart, that lifecycle cost is material. A recipe that survives is not an in-memory factor that survived. Charge rebuilding and report the difference. If a conventional process keeps the same factor in memory, give it the same persistence opportunity.

Savings against a stateless direct reference are not savings against conventional reuse. Show both. A reduction in numerical operations is not a monetary margin. Human time and commercial outcomes remain unresolved unless independently measured.

## A defensible earned-resource account

A successful mission must release a resource that can actually be reused for the next authorized task. A bookkeeping number does not itself create CPU capacity.

Define the original service commitment, its protected execution capacity and the rule that attributes released capacity to a valid mission. State how the reference cost is obtained. If a shadow baseline is executed to estimate savings, its compute is real experiment cost and cannot also be counted as free released capacity. If a calibrated reference is used, retain estimation uncertainty and prevent it from retroactively changing after confirmation results.

A useful bounded demonstration can reserve an existing resource envelope, preserve the service commitment, measure the actual completed execution, and allow a child construction only from conservatively credited reusable capacity. The ledger must distinguish:

- externally supplied service endowment;
- externally supplied research endowment;
- unused endowment that is not attributable improvement;
- a measured or explicitly model-dependent savings credit;
- the actual charged successor construction;
- any unspent credit and expiry.

Debit the actual construction work/time; stop or defer when it cannot be funded. Do not silently borrow from promised service resources. If credits are merely abstract interpreter work units, label the result a quota-accounting demonstration rather than real processor savings. If the resource is CPU time, report wall time separately and account for all worker processes. A faster numerical kernel may not improve end-to-end throughput when orchestration or checking dominates.

The same credit rule and opportunity to reinvest should be available to an equally enabled conventional system. A no-reinvestment ablation can retain useful execution methods while leaving credits unused, isolating whether the further construction actually causes a benefit.

## Fresh confirmation and uncertainty

Freeze the final executor, the exact candidate grammar, dependency versions, task generator, scope changes, load frequencies, maintenance events, budgets, primary outcomes and aggregation code before generating new confirmation identities. The candidate receives a task to execute but cannot rewrite the checker, admission rules or authority controls.

Randomize or alternate benchmark execution order prospectively to reduce warm-up and thermal bias. If measurement replication is needed, define the repetition count and aggregation in advance. Do not keep repeating noisy timing trials until the preferred method wins. A small finite pilot cannot justify precise single-millisecond claims across hardware.

The independent statistical unit is a network/stream, not hundreds of correlated RHS columns on the same factor. Pair methods within each stream and aggregate uncertainty across streams. Report validity/quality first, then measured execution, construction and total costs. Preserve invalid outputs, timeouts, refusal, failed adoption, negative transfer and rank/recurrence crossovers.

The result may show a one-time speedup and amortization without acceleration. Acceleration requires an increasing improvement rate under comparable workload and accounting, with a separately declared endpoint. Do not infer it from cumulative output or growing credit balances.

## Checker and independence limits

The separate checker directly sums edge flows and grounding balances with Python `math.fsum`; it does not use the executor's SciPy matrix or solve routines. Its tests contain analytically calculated cases and deliberately corrupted outputs. The residual and energy conditions are transparent, but their agreement is not two institutionally independent reviews. Both implementations share the commission and hardware.

The checker establishes consistency of the modeled numerical problem. It does not validate physical conductances, real engineering safety, customer demand or economic savings. Grounding keeps the declared family positive definite; a negative-weight shifted case is outside scope and should be refused rather than made to pass by silently changing the model.
