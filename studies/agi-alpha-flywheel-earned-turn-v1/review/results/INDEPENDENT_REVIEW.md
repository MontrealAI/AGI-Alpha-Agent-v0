# Independent confirmation review — Earned Turn v1

## Decision

**The frozen raw-artifact audit passes. The registered product-benefit gate remains HOLD.** A retained executable asset was used to construct a distinct, verified successor in 18 scoped streams, and 156 fresh successor outputs qualified while using the parent. That is a stronger construction mechanism than the preceding study demonstrated. It does not establish whole-system productivity superiority, recovery of the research investment, discovery improvement, acceleration, or commercial growth.

This review analyzes the first and only confirmation under study `AGI-ALPHA-EARNED-TURN-20261024-v1`. It does not rerun missions, repair frozen source, change an admission threshold, or substitute a new confirmation set. Its numerical auditor and checker were frozen before confirmation. The separate construction-phase attribution and storage summaries are explicitly descriptive analyses written after outcomes.

## What the constructive succession evidence establishes

Mission A produced a checked network response and a native sparse factorization as a useful byproduct. Across the flywheel's 24 streams, all 192 fresh B outputs used the retained factor and passed the independent checker. Mission C took its changed requirements from the actual accepted B08 output, rather than from a separately generated hypothetical predecessor.

For the 18 low-rank scoped streams—six recurring, six rare and six maintenance—the retained parent was invoked to solve the two-column direction system used to build a changed-operator Woodbury method. All 18 C outputs qualified. The construction trace records 18 parent block solves and 36 direction right-hand-side columns. The resulting asset served 156 fresh D outputs: 72 recurring, 12 rare and 72 maintenance. All 156 qualified with recorded actual parent use.

All matched C input digests were identical across the seven arms. The causal comparisons preserve reconstruction, independently constructed equivalent factors, direct specialist access and conventional reuse. The six wide-change flywheel streams correctly retain native service instead of constructing the low-rank candidate under the frozen provisional forecast. Rebuilt/equivalent wide-change construction is a forced causal ablation and must not be described as product admission.

None of these secondary phase comparisons overturns the whole-system HOLD.

## What the resource feedback does and does not establish

The flywheel released exactly 400 ms of unused, externally supplied service allowance per stream after eight accepted B actions, totaling 9,600 ms. Its successor account debited 1,034.057 ms for C and retained 8,565.943 ms. C debits averaged 57.448 ms on the 18 construction streams. Six wide-change streams released quota but did not construct the low-rank successor. Every arm except the explicit no-reinvestment ablation had the same release opportunity.

This is an executed account transfer followed by actual successor construction. It establishes that the released allocation was reusable in this bounded runner. It is **not 9.6 seconds of independently established performance savings**: it originated from an external reservation, and it greatly exceeds the uncertain 280.073 ms observed whole-study difference from native refactor. Unspent reserved quota is neither money nor resource creation.

Flywheel account debits were 32,462.910 ms for service, 1,034.057 ms for successor construction and 912.143 ms for externally funded maintenance. The separate control CPU was 7,128.718 ms. Account debits round each action upward to a whole microsecond; their sum therefore exceeds the unrounded CPU total by approximately 0.259 ms. The frozen auditor checks both the rounding and conservation rules. Maintenance and control costs were not funded entirely from the transferred successor quota.

Independent audit CPU was another 98,521.918 ms, with 100,936.013 ms wall time. This is additional research cost, outside the already measured service trials. The descriptive phase analysis reports a further 162.280 ms CPU. Development, review, human effort and monetary costs remain separate; incomplete measurements are unknown, not zero. Complete research-investment recovery is unresolved.

Wide-change differences must not be attributed to low-rank succession because the flywheel did not construct it there.

Storage also has a real boundary. Logical asset labels can reference the same immutable NPZ, so summing alias metadata double-counts physical bytes. Across 24 streams, the flywheel and conventional reuse each retain 49,545,144 unique NPZ bytes in 48 files; native refactor and no-reinvestment each retain 79,861,440 bytes in 48 files; rebuilt retains 88,767,528 bytes in 72 files; equivalent retains 72,626,520 bytes in 72 files. Direct specialist retains no NPZ factors. These are retained executable asset bytes, not total raw evidence storage; trial `storage_bytes` measures the latter boundary. No claim of cost-free indefinite retention follows.

## Independence and scope limits

The checker accumulates balances directly from edges without importing the producer's SciPy matrix multiplication or solver. Aggregation is independently implemented and does not import the producer analyzer. However, both operate on the same host under the same commission. The reviewer also corrected executor validation and integer-grounding assembly during development, so authorship is not wholly disjoint. See `review/INDEPENDENCE_DISCLOSURE.md`.

The reviewer received preliminary producer aggregate results before executing this exact frozen audit; this was not a blinded review. The audit code and its criteria were already frozen and remained unchanged. Parent-call counters are producer instrumentation independently attributed to checked outputs, not a separate instruction-level CPU trace. Same-host hashes establish content consistency given trusted receipts, not authentication against an adversary able to replace both evidence and its authority root.

The numerical assets are a native sparse factorization and a Woodbury update with declared applicability. The experiment demonstrates inspectable construction and reuse within a fixed algorithm vocabulary. It does not demonstrate an open-ended method-discovery process improving its own search policy. Changes in later requirements, preserved parent calls, construction work avoided and fresh verification are substantive; they are not evidence for untested acceleration.

The prior Strategy Succession study remains frozen. Its 517/864 flywheel outcomes, zero primary capability admissions and absent qualified fresh parent-using repair output are not overwritten by this result. The earlier acquisition audit attributes the missing primary nominee to reserve/search allocation preventing completion of the required search schedule; the new study changes the acquisition representation and service/improvement boundary under a new identity. It does not retroactively validate or repair the old confirmation.

## Supported publication statement

> Under one frozen 24-stream experiment, an accepted native factorization supported construction of a distinct low-rank successor in 18 scoped streams, followed by 156 independently checked fresh parent-using outputs. Removing the parent increased the matched successor-construction cost, while an equally enabled conventional workflow achieved the same useful mechanism. The registered whole-system benefit criterion was not met, so product admission remains on hold. Resource transfers demonstrate bounded reuse of an external allowance; full investment recovery, acceleration and commercial value remain unestablished.

A specific next hypothesis is that shared immutable-input validation and persistence can be amortized across accepted missions without weakening fresh output checks, making the verified construction saving material at the full service boundary. That requires a new prospectively bounded protocol, equal optimization for conventional alternatives, and new confirmation inputs. It is a recommendation, not another search started on this confirmation set.
