## Purpose and comparison

The employer's decision is the unit: obtain a complete usable digital deliverable at an explicit acceptance standard, turnaround, total cost and remaining attention. Computer-mediated work means work performed through digital interfaces, using a keyboard, mouse or equivalent input, whose result or action is delivered through software. Tested work is exact tabular reconciliation and descriptive exception reporting. Selecting authoritative sources, resolving business truth, authorizing updates and stakeholder management remain human responsibilities.

The primary benchmark is competent traditional human work using ordinary non-AI software, including efficient spreadsheets, SQL, macros, scripts and reusable templates. Methods need not be identical. This edition is a market-data-grounded comparison using official occupational descriptions, wages, employer costs and published historical human evidence; a newly recruited panel is NOT RUN and is not a prerequisite. Adjacent-task timings will not be transferred as observed times for this portfolio. Sources that do not establish AI usage will say so. Salary data alone never supplies task duration or quality.

## Acceptance and fairness

Accept a job only if all exact matching pairs, unmatched rows and unequal common non-key fields are correct and complete, including duplicate Cartesian pairs and exact source strings. Every exception has data-row references; input hashes match; all original values remain in exact JSON; CSV formula-like cells are escaped; grouped actions preserve all occurrences; summaries and local links match files. No guessed truth correction, silent truncation or unsupported operation. Material error tolerance is zero. The independent-role oracle uses Python CSV parsing and SQLite BINARY joins, imports no producer modules and is frozen before confirmation. Its role independence is not external organizational independence, human acceptance or blinding. Usability checks concern technical artifacts only.

The common employer deadline is one eight-hour business day. The system has a separate 30-second worker safety cap and 35-second outer-process timeout; these are not limits imposed on the traditional human workflow. Failures, timeouts and abandoned work remain in the ledger, including missing resource measurements. No confirmation rerun may replace an original result; a correction requires a separately labeled later edition. Development attempts and failures remain preserved. The initial generator's small-development-fixture IndexError and two independent-review accounting defects (outer-timeout persistence and unknown CPU aggregation) were corrected before freeze; no confirmation input existed then.

The market benchmark permits ordinary human automation and learning; it does not pretend a human manually scans every cell. Future optional matched trials must use the same brief, permitted actions, acceptance and deadline and retain both human templates and system tools. No participant recruitment/payment/publication is authorized or required by this study.

## Measurement and analysis

Primary confirmation unit is one of the 36 jobs, not a row, algorithm, assertion or timing repeat. Report acceptance count and Wilson 95% interval descriptively; this purposive finite set is not a random sample of world work. Report complete process-turnaround medians, ranges, p95 and deterministic 10000-resample bootstrap median intervals (seed 20261025), plus family/difficulty distributions. Include input loading, original methods, export, supplementary evidence, independent verification, hashing and ZIP construction. Process startup is included in outer turnaround; scheduler queue time is separate. CPU seconds include worker process startup and completed child processes through packaging; the final receipt-serialization tail is excluded. Stage CPU counters exclude work outside their individual window. RSS is per-worker/maximum-child, not a sum or peak host measurement. Source acquisition, research authoring and browser download duration are outside per-job execution and are separately marked unknown investment costs.

Do not equate zero recorded unplanned intervention in a scripted batch with zero human attention needed to adopt the product. Human setup, source preparation, review, correction, maintenance and oversight have no stopwatch observations here. Provider calls are exactly zero for these local task executions, but complete monetary cost is UNKNOWN without host pricing and those allocations. Record missing values as null, never zero-fill them.

Economic analysis uses within-currency source rates: U.S. employer costs, Quebec occupational wages with explicit employer-load assumptions, and European employer costs with occupation-scope limits. External published timing and accuracy evidence remain in their original task/population/measurement scope. Conditional model: C_H=(w_H t_H/60+v_H)/q_H and C_A=(w_A h_A/60+p_CPU u/3600+k+A/N)/q_A. Times include all attributable work/correction/review per attempted job; q is eventual acceptance fraction, not an assumed universal reliability. Fully allocated investment A/N and maintenance k are visible. The break-even human duration is derived from C_H=C_A, with human timing unknown where no matched source exists.

Predeclared sensitivity: traditional effort 5/15/30/60 minutes; residual system-related human effort 1/5/15 minutes; nonlabor plus allocated investment 0.10/1/5/20 units of each local currency per attempt; CPU price 0.1/1/5 local units per CPU-hour; acceptance assumptions 0.8/0.95/1. These are scenarios, not observations. No empirical human/AI speed or price ratio may be claimed. Freed employee capacity, avoided overtime/outside expenditure, realized cash savings, provider fees and profit remain separate.

## Market scale and claims

The $40T worldwide annual computer-mediated labor expenditure is a motivating hypothesis, not an empirical input. Investigate ILOSTAT, national agencies, BLS/O*NET, Job Bank/Statistics Canada and Eurostat, recording date/year, geography, occupation, currency, coverage and method. Separate total compensation, digital-task share, demonstrated eligibility, adoption, savings and provider revenue. Conservative/central/ambitious scenarios expose every assumed fraction. GDP, wages, savings and provider revenue are not interchangeable. No selected task portfolio establishes automation of most jobs.

Permitted conclusions are actual deliverables, finite machine acceptance, measured local resource use, byte-identical executable reuse, known-capability composition, observed finite concurrency and source-grounded conditional economics. Broader capability, complete net productivity gains, causal learning gains, increasing improvement rate, customer willingness to pay and self-acceleration require additional evidence and are not assumed.
