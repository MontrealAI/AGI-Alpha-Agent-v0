## What the foundations contribute

the formulation used to pursue it, and the instrument used to measure success. A proposed
transformation carries construction, realization, a purpose bridge and a falsifier.
Its two-world causal example has identical passive observation laws, hence a 50% ceiling
under the supplied prior. Permitted interventions reach 8191/8192 accuracy with a maximum
of twelve queries. An ordinary unrestricted optimal planner ties the result. This is an
exact information-structure example, not a novel causal algorithm, model trial or autonomous discovery.

from improving the process that discovers artifacts.

new theory of market coordination. Ordinary unrestricted enumeration finds the same arrangement.

## Admission feasibility before additional assessment

The unchanged research gate requires 6–24 paired independent mission clusters, a one-sided 95% t
lower bound above zero, and positive lower-bound lifetime net benefit after **both** assessment
and other lifecycle expenses. It binds candidate, comparator, policy, scope, environment, comparison
universe, times and prerequisites. Approximate t intervals do not control systematic semantic error,
invalid forecasts or simulator assumptions.

The prospective diagnostic `gate_reachability.assess_bound` checks a necessary condition:
if every paired delta is at most M cents, forecast eligible uses is R, risk reserve is S cents/use,
remaining lifecycle L and assessment A, then any lower lifetime net bound is at most
(M − S)R − L − A. If M − S is nonpositive or this maximum is below the existing hurdle,
assessment cannot yield admission under those stated bounds.

A reachable result is **not** approval or evidence that the candidate works. Source correctness,
rights, current identity, mission semantics and fresh evaluation remain mandatory.
The 12 local boundary controls include a reachable positive case, impossible costs, exact one-cent
threshold, fractional horizon and risk removing all benefit. This is an analytic/control result,
not a re-estimate of historical profit or a confirmed policy benefit.
A changed prescreen must be frozen and tested prospectively because later cash/capacity/cooldowns can change.
