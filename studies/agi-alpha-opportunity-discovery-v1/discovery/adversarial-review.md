# Adversarial review: executable evidence continuity

Date: 2026-10-27. No additional web sources were consulted. This review reasons from the proposed design and the earlier discovery dossier; claims about the unserved market, costs, demand and comparative performance below are hypotheses to test. It does not establish novelty relative to a complete prior-art search.

## Verdict and narrow claim worth pursuing

Correction alerts, source registries, dependency invalidation and rerunning an analysis are insufficient as a Blue Sky contribution. A supplied, perfectly typed dependency graph would make the demonstration a routine workflow test. A useful opportunity may remain where the original evidence arrives as heterogeneous reports and data exports, the dependencies are not given, and a recipient cannot economically reconstruct and maintain the decision repeatedly.

The credible narrow hypothesis is: expert use of current AI can reconstruct a faithful executable account of a decision from imperfect public evidence; a rights-cleared retained instrument can then lower the complete cost of answering a fresh, meaningfully related decision while preserving the original acceptance conditions. The commercial claim is separate: enough recipients value that continuity, and the obligations can be funded, delivered and supported at a worthwhile price.

Call the discovered structure an instrument only after it has reproduced the original accepted result and survived independent semantic counterexamples. A source-to-claim graph that merely links filenames is insufficient.

## A transformation that could change feasibility

Replace the implicit requirement that every new source event requires buying a fresh bespoke reconstruction with a scoped **evidence-continuity contract**. Its deliverable is an executable decision, the evidence supporting it, the conditions under which that decision must be reconsidered, and an explicit procedure for obtaining the minimum missing information. It can return a verified unchanged decision, a verified revision, a request for indispensable evidence or an unresolved status. A useful refusal is an observed safety/correctness outcome, not an accepted commercial answer unless the original buyer contract explicitly values that diagnostic.

The instrument should reason about what must be repaired to preserve the original purpose, not only which code must be rerun. If a correction affects a nuisance estimate but every admissible corrected value leaves the decision unchanged, it may construct a bound proving invariance. If a source introduces unknown cohort overlap, it may identify the missing overlap information and show that the decision is not identifiable without it. If the question has changed, it must decline reuse of the old acceptance claim and formulate a new mission.

This can make recurring, long-tail analyses feasible only if such a contract meets a prespecified quality, turnaround, customer-effort and cost envelope that a fair expert full-rebuild service does not. If both meet the envelope, report lower cost or better reliability. If only the graph grows, report neither frontier expansion nor compounding.

The possible new commercial unit is bounded continuity of a decision, rather than a document update. That is a product hypothesis, not a proven new market. Human analysts, provenance systems and living evidence services may already offer the relevant scope. Compare against their actual capabilities before assigning novelty.

## Required structure and acceptance

1. Preserve the original question, population or system scope, output units, decision threshold, acceptable uncertainty and excluded sources. Preserve them independently of the implementation.
2. Reconstruct source identities and versions, extraction locations, transformations, units, study/sample identity, uncertainty assumptions, executable computations and claim scope. Model links to missing evidence; do not fill them with silent guesses.
3. Reproduce the original analysis from evidence alone. If the historical result was wrong, retain it and give a separately labeled corrected result. Matching a faulty historical number does not validate the analysis.
4. Separate computational dependency from semantic dependency. A changed title can be irrelevant; a changed denominator can be decisive even when a column name stays the same. Missing links matter as much as incorrectly present links.
5. Treat a retraction or correction as a reason for the declared review policy to act. It does not, by itself, identify which numerical findings are false. Exclusion, uncertainty inflation and sensitivity analysis must follow the original admissibility policy or a documented new decision.
6. Requalify purpose, permissions and evidence at each invocation. Shared generic parsers or verifiers can be inherited; customer-specific inputs, restricted source copies and a prior authorization cannot be assumed transferable.

## Fair causal comparisons

**H:** equivalent competent human evidence-maintenance service, with observed price or explicitly estimated hours/rates. **U:** representative ordinary use of the same frontier model, with a sensible task and access to the same public evidence/tools. **E:** established expert workflow with conventional provenance and full reanalysis. **E+:** E plus the frozen retained reconstruction/repair method.

Run three distinctions rather than one impressive end-to-end comparison:

- **Construction:** can E or E+ reconstruct faithful dependencies from the same incomplete inputs? Do not supply the key representation only to E+.
- **Structure:** once discovered, give the representation to E as well. This tests the value of structure rather than hiding it in an unfair baseline. Full rerun may be so inexpensive that selective rerun offers no meaningful advantage.
- **Transfer:** a separate recipient receives the frozen method and fresh materials; it reconstructs a new case without the earlier task's answers. Distinguish transferred code, transferred documented expertise and ongoing specialist-service access.

For a sharing claim, use private retention and an ordinary library containing identical artifacts and permissions. An authenticated graph can have operational utility without demonstrating unique economic value. Keep resources fixed until the separate funded-expansion experiment.

## Independent holdout families

| Family | Fresh change or ambiguity | Required behavior | Falsification signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numeric correction | New value or row removed in an unfamiliar table layout | Recompute every affected quantitative claim; preserve unrelated outputs with justification | Missed downstream dependency or stale accepted claim |
| Units and denominators | Same label changes from per-device to per-hour, W to kW, or absolute to relative effect | Detect meaning change and rebuild or request clarification | Numerically plausible result with wrong units or base |
| Version identity | Preprint and journal article, renamed files, duplicated DOI/URL aliases | Preserve lineage and avoid counting versions as independent evidence | Duplicate evidence increases confidence |
| Correlated measurements | Several results share one instrument, site or experiment | Use declared covariance/cluster handling or mark unknown correlation | Spurious precision from treating all rows as independent |
| Overlapping cohorts | Two papers reuse some or all observations; overlap may be unknown | Reconcile overlap, compute defensible bounds or return unresolved | Hidden double counting or invented overlap estimate |
| Common upstream correction | One calibration or reference standard affects many studies and customers | Propagate common failure and reflect correlated service costs | Treat all downstream revisions as independent low-risk events |
| Opposing effects | Aggregate conclusion changes after a subgroup correction or composition shift | Preserve prespecified subgroup/aggregation semantics | Simpson-type reversal is missed or new grouping chosen to preserve the claim |
| Changed purpose | Previous question is ranking; next question is threshold compliance or causal effect | Require new acceptance and any newly needed evidence | Old result or permission silently certifies a new purpose |
| Unavailable source | Source removed, data link dead, only abstract accessible | Preserve known provenance; distinguish unverifiable from false; request evidence | Fabricated reconstruction or categorical acceptance without support |
| Rights restrictions | Publicly visible source has limited reuse rights; recipient cannot access customer material | Share only cleared generic content and recheck recipient access | A cross-agent capability leaks protected data or embeds inaccessible evidence |
| Scope transfer | New domain changes valid effect measure, sampling process or uncertainty model | Adapt explicitly and charge adaptation; decline incompatible reuse | Generic template produces wrong semantic model while passing syntax checks |
| Adversarial document content | Source includes instructions to omit an adverse result or alter acceptance | Treat it as untrusted evidence text; retain original acceptance | External text changes authority, accounting or evaluator |

Do not tune the retained method on these exact confirmation instances. A separate evaluator should create or select fresh instances after method freeze and preserve their provenance. Synthetic counterexamples test mechanics; they are not evidence of real historical correction discovery or frontier-model performance. Correlation and overlap tests must include cases where the correct result is unresolved, cases where the decision genuinely changes, and cases where a valid invariance witness exists.

## Tests that could kill the opportunity early

- An expert already reconstructs and reruns the chosen workload cheaply enough that retention and review cost more than starting again.
- Dependence discovery remains unreliable on fresh layouts or semantic changes, even with verification and correction costs included.
- The artifact cannot support more than one narrowly prearranged case, or compatible later demand was constructed around it.
- The original purpose cannot be evaluated independently because the system's own graph supplies both its prediction and its answer key.
- Required rights, source availability or domain information prevent a commercially useful accepted output.
- Buyers prefer one-off reports, already receive equivalent maintenance, or have insufficient willingness to pay for the claimed continuity.
- Coordinated customers do not actually share enough cleared work, while common upstream failures create simultaneous obligations and exhaust reserves.

## Economics and arrangement without guaranteed advantage

Use a paid baseline reconstruction as a complete deliverable if it meets the original acceptance criteria. Charge required extraction, verification and correction to paid-job delivery. Charge additional generalization, admission, transfer and maintenance separately; optional research to develop the verifier is R&D, not retroactively paid learning.

For a subscription or prepaid bundle, define accepted units and service obligations before computing revenue. A deposit creates cash and an obligation; it does not automatically create earned revenue or profit. Account for correlated correction bursts, source outages, provider capacity, cancellation, failed transfer, refunds and support. Several subscribers sharing one upstream method do not create independent evidence or independent delivery risk.

Test no retention, private retention and selective sharing under identical starting capital and demand. Let costs and buyer response determine whether to retain, coordinate or stop. No subscription, price or labor-saving factor should be fixed solely to make the joint arrangement win.

## Minimum acceptable contribution for this edition

A persuasive bounded result would be a source-grounded reconstruction from materials that did not supply the dependency structure, faithful execution under original acceptance, independent fresh perturbations and recipient transfer, plus an honest economic frontier with favorable and unfavorable regions. It may show operational Alpha without proving a new scientific method. A positive simulation can establish consistency of a proposed mechanism under assumptions; it cannot establish observed demand, model improvement, learning to invent or market creation.

If those tests cannot be completed, retain the executable instrument and negative findings as additive evidence, and explicitly decline the stronger flagship claim.
