# Supplemental discovery sources

This directory preserves sources obtained during opportunity investigation. It was added after the frozen recipient assays. It does not alter their inputs, outputs, scores or claims.

## What was available, and to whom

The crop workbook **was retrieved during discovery** and existed in the research workspace. It was **excluded from every U/E/E+ permitted recipient packet**. The three recipients were asked to perform bounded source mapping and identify semantic conflicts using the same eight original source files. Their task was not a complete crop-data reconstruction or network meta-analysis.

The crop notebook and source notes **were included equally in those recipient packets**. Their copies here preserve discovery provenance; they are not newly introduced evidence for the assay. The MetaLab about/changelog document was prior-art evidence and was excluded from every recipient packet.

Accordingly, a missing-data statement must say that numerical crop inputs were missing **from the permitted recipient packet**. It must not claim that no public crop data existed or that the research team could not retrieve them. Refusal of a physical-unit conclusion remains a limited semantic-control result, not a full-corpus crop-analysis comparison. Equal access to the bounded packets does not establish performance on a complete evidence corpus.

No crop workbook analysis, network-model reproduction or source-conflict resolution is performed by this preservation step. No frozen assay is rerun or amended.

## Byte verification and working-copy differences

`SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json` records source URLs, immutable commits, SHA-256 digests, byte counts and verified Git blob identifiers for all five files. The crop workbook has **321,691 bytes** and Git blob `dc88fe405433e738976d2c8ee0658bebc1664833`.

The initial scratch copies of the crop notes, crop notebook and MetaLab document each contained one extra trailing line feed. This formatting difference is disclosed in the receipt. The canonical files preserved here match the exact upstream Git blobs; original scratch copies and their hashes remain recorded. The numerical workbook was already byte-exact.

The original investigation chronology and four-document follow-up are preserved in [systems-additional-challenge.md](../systems-additional-challenge.md). This source-preservation action does not create additional discovery findings or change the prior-art budget count.

## Recipient disclosure limits

The frozen assay protocol defines the tasks as fresh teacher-expectancy mapping and crop semantic-conflict disposition. It permits the supplied source files and notes that the filesystem separation is procedural. It does not explicitly disclose that the discovery researcher had retrieved the workbook elsewhere. Recipient U's saved worklog explicitly states that the crop RDS was outside its permitted inputs and that absence elsewhere was not asserted. E and E+ likewise restrict their conclusions to supplied or permitted material.

This directory makes the wider research-team access explicit after the assay, without retrospectively expanding recipient access or treating restricted source availability as evidence of frontier-model incapability.
