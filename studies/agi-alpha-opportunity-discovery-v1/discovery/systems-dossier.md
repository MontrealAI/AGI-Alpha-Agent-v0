# Systems and knowledge-market opportunity dossier

Prepared 2026-10-27 for the additive AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL edition. This is opportunity investigation, not a claim that either candidate has been validated.

## Research budget and decision

Budget: at most 12 substantive primary-source reads and approximately 15 minutes. Completed 9 substantive primary-source reads, with several failed retrievals recorded separately. Search snippets were used for triage. No live customer interviews, sales, proprietary tool trials, or model comparison were performed. Existing solutions substantially narrowed both initial hypotheses.

**Provisional ranking:** Candidate B has the stronger documented unresolved burden and the cleaner independently checkable mechanism. Candidate A has a consequential buyer outcome but a more crowded market and a less-established unmet niche. Neither should be selected simply because a small simulator is easy. If another discovery stream establishes a more consequential, testable gap, these belong in the rejected/deferred dossier.

## Candidate A: An operational exit option that preserves the customer's obligations

### Beneficiary and useful purpose

An organization using a small or specialized SaaS provider wants to keep delivering a defined service when that provider disappears, becomes unaffordable, or cannot remain an acceptable supplier. The useful outcome is an operational alternative that continues the organization's own commitments: the right person can perform each permitted action, prohibited actions remain prohibited, records retain their meaning and history, deadlines still trigger the right work, and output can be reconciled with the original obligations. A successful file export is only one ingredient.

The opportunity is specifically **replacement-vendor semantic continuity when the original implementation cannot be licensed or redeployed**. It is not generic cloud migration, a backup service, or source-code escrow.

### What exists and what it changes about the hypothesis

The European Commission's Data Act explainer establishes switching and interoperability as explicit policy concerns, and distinguishes functional equivalence for infrastructure services from other service obligations [A1]. This motivates investigation; it does not establish a customer's contractual rights in any particular situation.

Commercial escrow providers already offer sophisticated recovery. Escode explicitly includes deployment scripts, configuration, API documentation and recovery materials, and verifies whether an application can be rebuilt or redeployed. The Escrow Company describes SaaS continuity for major cloud environments [A2, A3]. Those services must be strong conventional comparators, not caricatured as storing an inert code archive.

CloudM's vendor-authored marketplace listing advertises migration of mail, files, contacts and calendars across numerous platforms [A4, triage only]. We did not inspect a product trial or validate its limitations. A file-format mapping alone cannot establish an unmet opportunity.

### Remaining gap — a hypothesis, not established market evidence

Can an independently executable, purpose-scoped acceptance package make replacement of a vendor-specific system affordable for organizations whose obligations are too particular for a standard migration but too small to justify a bespoke continuity project? Existing escrow assumes some recovery access to the original software assets. A replacement option instead reconstructs the minimum service semantics against another implementation, without unauthorized copying, and records which original requirements it cannot preserve.

The hard asset would be a **mission contract and transformation witness**: source records, meaning-preserving mapping rules, an executable acceptance suite, coverage and uncertainty, an authorized replay mechanism, and a verified rollback or remediation plan. It must include negative requirements, such as access denials, as well as successful outputs.

### Why now; what needs construction

Frontier intelligence might reduce the cost of extracting business semantics from mixed documentation, configuration and observed behavior; constructing adapters; finding counterexamples; and packaging auditable evidence. This is a hypothesis about expert operation, not a demonstrated model capability or a new migration algorithm. Existing migration, property testing and differential-testing techniques are building blocks.

The technical invention under investigation is a reusable method for deriving and testing mission invariants across heterogeneous systems. The commercial invention is making a verified exit option affordable through shared adapter work plus organization-specific acceptance contracts. A shared adapter never supplies a new customer's meaning or authority automatically.

### Smallest decisive experiment

1. Obtain two independently maintained, rights-compatible implementations of the same service class and a real public export example; freeze versions before analysis. Do not author both source and destination to be conveniently compatible.
2. Define the actual service obligations before choosing the transformation: identity boundaries, state transitions, audit history, time rules, refunds/cancellations where relevant, and a small set of blinded acceptance tasks.
3. Have an expert construct one exit package using established tools. Freeze the reusable method. Transfer it to an independent recipient operating on a fresh source version or a second service class, with the same tool access as its comparator.
4. Execute source and replacement tasks; include deliberate semantic adversaries such as timezone changes, withdrawn permissions, stale state, duplicate records and a missing destination feature. Require rejection when the purpose cannot be preserved.
5. Compare E+ with E under matched construction budgets and costs at matched accepted scope. U is ordinary competent use of the same model with normal tools, not an intentionally underspecified prompt. H is an experienced migration/continuity team, whose quoted or explicitly estimated time is the commercial reference.

The experiment can establish adapter correctness and bounded transfer. Without live model trials it cannot establish Operational Alpha. Without observed customer choices it cannot establish market demand. A deterministic toy with hand-supplied invariants is a useful fixture but cannot establish discovery.

### Falsification and stopping

Reject or narrow the opportunity if an available product or established expert workflow already supplies the same acceptance scope at comparable complete cost; if preserved exports cannot support required behavior; if critical requirements cannot be independently specified; if negative permissions fail; or if adaptation and verification consume the shared savings. Stop before a commercial simulator if no specific underserved customer segment is evidenced.

### Commercial arrangement and liquidity

Hypothesis: a cohort pays conditional deposits for common, rights-cleared adapters, then each organization separately buys its own purpose-specific exit assessment. Construction starts only after both a funding threshold and sufficient technical compatibility are met. Deposits remain obligations until the agreed milestone; they are not profit. Model nonparticipation, withdrawal, partial delivery, common adapter failure, refund exposure, serving capacity and requalification after updates. Compare isolated custom delivery, shared technology alone, pooled demand alone, and their combination. No prices or willingness-to-pay have been sourced for this narrow service. Obtain competing quotes or disclose human-hour estimates before asserting commercial advantage.

### Successor opportunity

A later recipient could test a different service obligation using a retained converter, replay harness or counterexample generator. The new offering becomes feasible only if it crosses predeclared cost, correctness, authority and turnaround thresholds that the baseline misses. A cheaper second migration is better economics unless the baseline was infeasible.

## Candidate B: Executable revalidation of conclusions after research evidence changes

### Beneficiary and useful purpose

Evidence-synthesis teams, research publishers and research-intensive organizations need to know **which conclusions remain supported after an included source is corrected or retracted**, and what work must be repeated. Their useful outcome is an auditable change-impact package tied to the original question and methods, not an alert that a DOI has changed.

The initial target should be research maintenance, not automatic clinical advice. A quantitative summary and an editorial decision have different acceptance requirements. The instrument must not make an automatic medical or publication verdict from a statistical threshold.

### Evidence of the burden and existing solutions

Crossref makes Retraction Watch data openly available through its API and a daily-updated CSV; corrections and expressions of concern are less comprehensively covered than retractions [B1]. This is usable infrastructure, not something the enterprise has invented.

The 2025 VITALITY retrospective cohort study investigates the incorporation of retracted trials into evidence syntheses and the persistence of uncorrected reviews [B2]. Its indexed summary supports the problem's existence. Full result extraction was blocked on rereading, so numerical results must be verified before use in the paper.

Scite already classifies citation context, monitors collections and flags retractions or editorial concerns. RetractoBot alerts authors when cited papers are retracted [B3, B4]. Detection, alerts and citation graphs therefore are not sufficient novelty claims.

In June 2026 Cochrane described a new impact framework and emphasized that removing included studies can require reanalysis, changes to certainty assessments and updates throughout a review. It explicitly says no automatic threshold determines whether a review should be retracted [B5]. This both supports a real maintenance burden and supplies a demanding expert baseline. The new experiment must build on that workflow, not claim to invent impact assessment.

### Remaining gap and restriction

Static articles and spreadsheets often do not expose a machine-executable link from a source's specific contribution to each derived quantity and downstream claim. A DOI-level citation edge does not distinguish a background citation, an included data source, a duplicate report or a contested methodological dependency. An alert can therefore create a large manual reconstruction task.

Hypothesis: frontier intelligence can help reconstruct an **evidence dependency instrument** with traceable source-to-extraction-to-calculation-to-claim links, so later amendments trigger only the necessary computations and questions. The claim is reduced revalidation cost with preserved research purpose and less unnecessary alarm. It is not that a graph settles scientific truth.

### What must be built

A versioned evidence manifest, extraction provenance, executable analysis, source-state events, an impact propagator, and a recipient-facing revalidation packet. Changes must distinguish retraction, correction, expression of concern, reinstatement and uncertainty. The instrument carries rights and provenance; proprietary articles cannot automatically be redistributed. Validity of a derived artifact must expire or be reassessed when the relevant source or mission changes.

Known statistical software and existing guidance supply the conventional calculations and adjudication framework. AI assistance would be tested in locating the relevant evidence, reconstructing dependencies and identifying counterexamples. The scientific method itself is not a new meta-analysis algorithm.

### Smallest decisive experiment

1. Select a small public quantitative review with open extraction data and a documented later amendment, plus a second, independent held-out review. Freeze source versions and original purposes before constructing the instrument. Separate genuine amendment events from injected stress fixtures.
2. Reproduce its original calculation using standard statistical tooling. If reproduction fails, retain that failure rather than rewriting the target.
3. Build the dependency instrument from the original public material. Freeze it before revealing held-out amendment events and transfer the reusable method to a different recipient.
4. Evaluate corrections to included estimates, duplicate reports, a background-only citation, a retraction, and a reinstatement. Ground truth must be independently checked; include cases where the conclusion is unchanged.
5. Measure exactness of impacted outcomes, missed dependencies, unnecessary invalidations, reproduced effect estimates and intervals, complete cost and recipient effort. No universal rule such as p<0.05 should stand in for the review's original scientific purpose.
6. Give E the same papers, software and Cochrane framework. Give E+ the frozen retained instrument. Compare to an ordinary library containing identical evidence and permissions before claiming graph-specific value. Record what is actually executed and label unavailable model behavior separately.

A simple executable demonstration can test dependency propagation and accounting. It cannot establish automated extraction accuracy if the dependencies were manually supplied. An LLM judged only by its own extracted answers supplies no independent acceptance evidence.

### Falsification

Reject the proposed mechanism if expert use of existing tools already regenerates the required evidence at equal complete cost; if reconstruction is too ambiguous for reliable independent checking; if missed dependencies outweigh reduced work; if the shared artifact leaks protected source material; or if correction frequency and reusable overlap are too low to repay maintenance. A finding that only alerts are useful narrows the offering rather than proving a transformational instrument.

### Commercial mechanism and unknowns

Possible service: a publisher or consortium purchases a reproducible evidence baseline once, then a maintenance retainer covers monitoring and a capped number of impact assessments. Each subscriber retains responsibility for its own editorial or research decision. A common public extraction or analysis component can be co-funded while confidential project decisions remain private. This is a participation hypothesis, not observed willingness to pay. Authors, publishers, funders and downstream users may have misaligned incentives; an alert recipient is not necessarily a buyer.

Test technical instrument alone, coordinated commissioning alone and their joint package. A consortium that dissolves or fails to pay must not be assumed to fund the build. Stage deposits or a commissioning institution may finance opening work; deposits and future retainers are obligations, not earned surplus. Recheck whether the cross-client work is actually permitted to be shared.

### Successor opportunity

The same provenance and selective re-execution instrument could support fresh evidence updates, differently framed questions, or another domain's quantitative synthesis. Every new mission still requires its own inclusion criteria, outcome meanings, evaluation and authority. Inherited execution structure may lower setup cost; it does not establish that the enterprise has learned to discover better scientific questions.

## Shared research design requirements

The actual experiment should preregister acceptance conditions, resource limits and three distinct questions: commercial delivery against H, E versus U Operational Alpha, and E+ versus E retained capability value. Do not add those advantages together as separate revenue. If no live models are tested, mark E/U quality and cost as assumed or simulated and present the actual artifact results separately.

For any pooled build, measure the four cells of capability absent/present and coordination absent/present. All configurations get the same demand information and starting cash. Neither compatible demand nor a useful common core may be supplied only to the treatment. Price selection needs buyer choices and outside options, not a fixed discount.

Cost A is complete paid delivery. Cost B is extra assessment, admission, transfer and upkeep. Cost C is additional R&D or Gym practice. A failed candidate, human reconstruction, rejected share and remediation all consume resources once. Preserve cash expenses separately from the opportunity cost of displaced work.
