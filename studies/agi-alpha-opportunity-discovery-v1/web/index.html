Abstract

What useful work becomes possible when an enterprise combines expert frontier-model operation, reusable capabilities and a viable commercial arrangement? We investigate six materially different opportunities before selecting a bounded experiment on evidence continuity: reconstructing the source, meaning and computation behind a quantitative conclusion so that a later change can be revalidated without silently changing the original purpose. Existing retraction alerts, living reviews, statistical packages and expert impact frameworks are strong prior art. The proposed gap is the reconstruction and maintenance of imperfect legacy evidence, not faster meta-analysis or a new alert system.

We execute an additive hybrid study: frozen open secondary data, a conventional REML adapter, purpose and rights checks, 64 injected snapshot events, three separate same-model recipient sessions, unchanged native lifecycle/accounting controls, and exact conditional economic calculations. All 64 declared v1 dispositions conform; only 24 yield numerical outputs, while 40 correctly hold or reject. The independent semantic rubric gives U, E and E+ each 18/18. A fresh recipient executes the authenticated inherited bytes on a different source mapping. A later diagnostic exposes ignored event fields and noncumulative semantics; the first version remains intact and a prospective correction is evaluated separately. Full conventional reanalysis takes roughly 1.6–1.7 ms on these small tables, eliminating arithmetic speed as a credible commercial rationale.

No buyer, sale, complete job-cost advantage, production identity or economic frontier crossing is established. A hypothetical four-prospect package permits at most USD 1,209.40 in fixed construction and additional retention costs under its stated assumptions; separate cash paths expose default, common failure and prefunding constraints. The contribution is a reproducible instrument-transfer pilot, a preserved negative comparative result, and an operating blueprint whose admission conditions can fail. The next decisive experiment must measure reconstruction cost, source coverage and buyer acceptance on unfamiliar legacy work.

Opportunity is the research object

The AGI Alpha vision motivates an enterprise that discovers useful work, delivers it profitably, and leaves the successor better equipped. It does not validate a market size, an intellectual-property valuation or token economics. This edition asks a narrower empirical question: can a capability change the deliverable and its commercial arrangement enough to make a consequential service viable under the recipient’s actual alternatives?

An evidence-synthesis team may receive a correction to a paper on which a published conclusion depends. The practical questions extend beyond whether the paper has a flag: which extracted number changed, what it measured, which other estimates share its participants, which computation used it, and whether the original conclusion still follows. A static report often does not carry a complete executable account of these dependencies. Rebuilding that account can dominate the computational work. A service that supplies and maintains such an account could therefore be valuable even when every statistical operation is conventional.

This is a proposed customer problem, not an observed purchase. Cochrane’s June 2026 framework already combines matching of retracted studies with impact assessment and editorial action; it explicitly treats the assessment of changed conclusions as substantive work. Crossref provides Retraction Watch metadata, with coverage qualifications. Existing living-analysis services further limit any novelty claim. We take these as building blocks and counterfactuals, not competitors to omit.(Cochrane 2026; Crossref 2026; Natural History Museum 2026)

The study distinguishes four questions. First, is there a consequential gap? Second, can an executable instrument preserve the useful purpose? Third, does it improve outcomes or complete cost against an available expert? Fourth, do enough counterparties accept a fundable arrangement? Passing one question does not imply the next. The present evidence passes bounded execution and transfer checks; it does not pass commercial admission.

The selected opportunity dossier

Beneficiary and outcome. A publisher, evidence-synthesis unit or research group would receive a source-qualified reconstruction, an executable conditional analysis, unresolved scientific questions, and an explicit rule for accepting a later revalidation. A useful refusal or missing-evidence diagnosis counts as a purchased deliverable only if the buyer contracted for it.

What remains difficult. A legacy bundle may mix a report, extraction table and code with inconsistent effect definitions, row counts, study identities or units. An alert does not determine how these inconsistencies affect the original question. A clean secondary dataset avoids much of that problem; it can test the adapter, but cannot by itself validate automated legacy reconstruction.

Why now. Frontier tool use can inspect prose and code together, construct small adapters, execute checks and challenge a tempting interpretation. This is an operational possibility to test. It supplies no guaranteed gain over an expert using the same model and conventional tools.

What must be built. The proposed service needs a purpose contract, versioned extraction provenance, a compatible numerical model, source-event qualification, an acceptance witness and a separate authority check. The present construction supplies a bounded snapshot adapter and transfer package. It does not supply autonomous notice detection, arbitrary document extraction, chronological event maintenance or editorial judgment.

Smallest decisive experiment. Reproduce public conditional results; inject independently specified perturbations; require semantic refusal for a real incompatible case; transfer frozen generic bytes to a recipient with fresh source-mapping work; compare U/E/E+ with the same tools and budget caps; then measure the full reconstruction cost against H and E before commercial admission.

Falsifiers. A stale or wrong-unit result passes; an unresolved dependence is treated as known independence; transfer changes the question; expert reconstruction already meets the same envelope at equal complete cost; buyers value only existing alerts; or retention and coordination consume all savings. The first version’s contradictory-field diagnostic is a material partial falsification of robust event ingestion, preserved below.

Commercial hypothesis. Sell a bounded baseline reconstruction before offering optional continuity. Where several parties genuinely need the same cleared generic work, contingent co-commissioning may fund it. No coalition, price, buyer value or subscription demand has been observed. Both the technical capability and arrangement are available to an unrestricted expert comparator.

Three foundations, one mechanism

Transformational Intelligence: preserve the purpose

The first foundation separates the authorized purpose, the chosen formulation and the evaluator. Its intervention example demonstrates the value of a permitted information change under a supplied causal model; an unrestricted conventional planner ties it. We preserve that distinction.(Boucher 2026c) Here the proposed restriction is the implicit need to repurchase a bespoke document reconstruction after every relevant source change. The alternative is an executable account that carries its meaning and qualification conditions. The original purpose remains a defensible answer to the same question, not a lower numerical tolerance or a newly chosen favorable endpoint.

This edition constructs part of the alternative and tests its transfer. It does not show that the enterprise autonomously discovered a new dependency representation from arbitrary literature. The source tables and much of their preprocessing semantics were supplied. Calling those ingredients an invention would erase the main unresolved task.

Invent the Economy: construct the whole offer

A capability alone is not a transaction. The joint package specifies outcomes, participants, outside options, price, responsibilities, timing, rights, financing and acceptance. Shared code with no paying need has no earned commercial surplus. Compatible needs with no reliable common deliverable have no qualified coalition.(Boucher 2026a)

For this opportunity, the arrangement separates a completed reconstruction from an optional future continuity obligation. Participants may decline, defer, withdraw or choose established services. A source failure can affect many customers together. Refunds, collection delay, support, requalification and capacity must therefore be priced and funded as parts of the offer, rather than appended after a favorable expected-profit calculation.

SUCCESSOR Ω: inherit structure, earn acceptance

The third foundation carries stable executable structure into a new mission while rechecking meaning and permission. Its conventional numerical instrument is valuable only under the declared reuse conditions; the strong expert ties it and first use can lose.(Boucher 2026b) The current instrument similarly transfers generic code, not a scientific conclusion or a perpetual authorization.

The mechanism connecting the foundations is sequential but allows complementary exploration: identify a restrictive delivery assumption; construct a purpose-preserving instrument and a candidate contract together; execute acceptance; determine complete incremental value; then retain only the cleared capability whose expected reuse exceeds its additional costs. A promising intermediate hypothesis need not be profitable in isolation. The completed package must pay for all necessary work before its benefit is admitted.

Executable content and supplied ingredients

The v1 package contains a Python adapter, a small mapping validator, a frozen expert-operation checklist and a manifest. It uses ordinary independent-effects intercept-only restricted maximum likelihood, supplied sampling variances and a normal 95% interval. It checks source bytes and declared purpose, supports scoped changes and removals, and returns a hold for explicitly declared overlap, scope or evidence uncertainty. It does not implement multilevel, covariance-aware or network meta-analysis.

Its archive SHA-256 is daa27a2f32cd959757300c9df18be518166027729d549d46f1c422bed999223f. This archive excludes source datasets and protected evaluation materials. The package has 5555 bytes; small size is a delivery property, not proof of learning or value. The original manifest honestly records evidence as pending at its freeze; subsequent receipts supply evidence without rewriting the frozen object.

The data come from the independently maintained metadat repository at commit 0cb96ce4ff16586a9dbc8a9b0f9bc617538d2738. Original text, build scripts, documentation, package metadata and license notices are retained. Normalized CSV files change serialization only. Experimental design and fair counterfactuals

Frozen numerical confirmation

Construction hashes were frozen before confirmation, followed by two seeded event suites using seeds 77319 and 77320. There are 32 cases for each dataset: corrected estimates/removals, unchanged snapshots, unresolved scope/dependence/rights and invalid inputs. Expectations are fixed before execution. All events are injected fixtures; no actual correction or retraction is alleged for these source studies.

An independent implementation solves the analytic REML score equation with a bracketed root solver, rather than reusing the adapter’s profile-likelihood minimization. Numerical-update agreement tolerance is 2×10−62\times 10^{-6}. Two rendered metadat reference results are checked at their published rounding with tolerance 5.1×10−55.1\times 10^{-5}. R/metafor was not executed in this environment; a prepared R reference is explicitly pending. Later reviewer checks include an analytic equal-variance solution and a zero-heterogeneity boundary. These tests validate bounded calculations, not the scientific assumptions of the datasets.

The commercial arrangement and its failure conditions

Define the offer before its price

The proposed initial offer is a bounded reconstruction of one openly accessible quantitative evidence packet. The buyer receives a source/version inventory, effect and variance mapping, executable reproduction, uncertainty and missing-evidence register, and an acceptance record. Editorial decisions, inaccessible primary materials, causality claims and production monitoring are excluded unless separately specified and priced. Automation may do intake, hashing, parsing, calculations, routine verification and packaging. Residual expert work follows actual unresolved obligations; no arbitrary daily review allowance is inserted to predetermine margins.

A comparable human service is the primary commercial reference. This edition obtains no quote. Its explicit planning sensitivity is 4–12 hours at USD 100–200/hour, or USD 400–2400, for the narrow reconstruction/checking scope. These are assumed hours and rates, not market observations or a claim that a complete systematic review costs this amount. A genuinely new continuity contract needs a buyer-value hypothesis, an outside option and actual willingness-to-pay evidence; it cannot borrow a market price merely by naming a new product.

Price is not fixed near token cost. A buyer can accept, decline, defer or use an alternative. The price grid USD 500/800/1200 explores possible quotes, not an optimized price; its demand curve is unknown. A live pricing decision would maximize expected collected contribution subject to participation, acceptance, capacity and cash constraints. Discounts and milestone payments must be justified by changed buyer response, financing or risk, rather than prescribed as 10% or a fixed reinvestment fraction.

Technology and coordination are separate interventions

The mechanism runner can evaluate no shared capability/no coordination, capability only, coordination only, and both. The study-specific template remains unpriced rather than inserting a guaranteed savings factor. The original supplied Invent-the-Economy example is replayed only as a historical calibration of the ledger: its constructed shared core and buyers are not rediscovered as this opportunity.

For the new opportunity, let K0K_0 and K1K_1 denote fully measured setup costs without and with the retained capability. With nn otherwise identical served buyers, coordination costs G0,G1G_0,G_1, and constant delivery terms, the interaction between a shareable setup reduction and pooled demand can have magnitude (n−1)(K0−K1)(n-1)(K_0-K_1) before extra coordination/retention risk. This is algebra conditional on a real shared cost and compatible demand. If K0=K1K_0=K_1, or customer-specific verification dominates, the technical complement disappears. If participants withdraw, a favorable setup amortization can disappear with it.

No value for K0−K1K_0-K_1 is estimated from the millisecond numerical timings or the tied recipient scores. Thus no causal positive technology-by-coordination interaction is claimed here. Giving an expert the same representation, code and contract is required; restricting the expert to one buyer would manufacture the flagship result. The historical restricted 2×2 remains clearly labeled, while an unrestricted expert selects the same feasible package.

Selective inheritance, optional practice and authority

Capability lineage

The lineage has four distinct kinds of contribution. Established statistical methods and source datasets are supplied ingredients. The source-aware adapter and checklist are assistant-constructed generic artifacts. The authenticated capsule and native accounting are inherited repository mechanisms. The recipient constructs its own fresh mapping and checks current semantics. None of these is evidence that the enterprise has learned to invent better.

The v1 correction feedback is recorded as software refinement: original artifact, fixture environment, adverse diagnostic, edit, new version and fresh checks are identifiable. No proprietary weights are modified, no fine-tuning occurs, and no reinforcement-learning score is reported. An optional future Gym campaign could optimize mapping/verification policies across unrelated layouts, incompatible models and source omissions, but only if expected reuse exceeds its cost. Its evaluation must include prompt injection in evidence, false confidence, leakage, negative transfer and obsolete defaults. Correctly declining a campaign can coexist with profitable delivery.

The present candidate may be preserved as research evidence without being admitted as economic Alpha. A useful operational method can later become ordinary Beta as model defaults improve. The baseline and eligible scope must then be updated prospectively; past benefit cannot be counted again under a new name. “Specialist ASI” is an ambition, not a description justified by this small pilot.

Admission must be possible, then earned

An unchanged admission policy requires 6–24 paired independent mission clusters, a positive one-sided 95% lower bound, and positive lower-bound lifetime net benefit after assessment and lifecycle costs. Candidate identity, comparator, policy, scope, environment, times and prerequisites must match. This edition supplies no such empirical monetary evidence, so no positive admission follows from control conformance.

A necessary prescreen can avoid structurally impossible assessments. If the maximum benefit per eligible reuse is MM, risk reserve per use is SS, eligible reuse forecast is RR, assessment expense is AA and additional lifecycle expense is LL, then an upper bound on admissible lifetime value is U=(M−S)R−A−L.U=(M-S)R-A-L. If even UU cannot clear the policy hurdle, no favorable sample can do so under those bounds. Passing this prescreen merely means assessment could be informative. It is not evidence of realized benefit, valid reuse forecasts, correctness or permission. Twelve local boundary controls validate the necessary-bound computation. Nine additional economic cases explore reachable and impossible assumptions without assigning a positive measured lower bound.

The earlier corrected-study notice reports 568 assessment states whose capped benefits cannot reach their final hurdles, with maximum ratios 73.016% in the reference profile and 78.971% in a favorable profile. Its assessment expenses remain USD 261.56 and USD 1335.94 per network world. Those are preserved post-confirmation diagnostics from the notice, not rerun measurements or recovered profit. The complete private paper and ledgers were not retrieved.(MONTREAL.AI coordination thread 2026) Any changed prescreen must be frozen and tested prospectively; different timing can change later cash, workload and opportunities.

Protected sharing without inherited permission

The intended network makes admitted, rights-cleared generic capabilities available to currently authorized *.agent.agi.eth members through existing protected interfaces. Current membership, revocation, Jobs validator roles, execution authority and settlement remain separate checks. Wildcard naming is not proof of identity. A prior success, graph node or surviving local copy cannot revive expired permission.

Source secrets, restricted IP and protected evaluation answers must remain outside shared capsules. Here original sources stay under their own licenses and the generic archive excludes them. Revocation can stop later mediated retrieval; it cannot erase an already delivered copy. Requalification follows changes in the model, source, purpose, schema, rights or relevant economic baseline. The present fixture proves neither live ENS authentication nor admission into a production scientific service.

What the successor can do next

A productive frontier with an explicit null result

Define a feasible offering before comparing configurations: the original useful output is accepted, complete expected contribution is positive, obligations and reserves are fundable at every payment date, serving capacity is sufficient, rights and authority are current, and agreed turnaround/customer-effort limits are met. If both configurations satisfy this envelope, a difference is better economics or reliability, not newly feasible work.

The present evidence establishes no offering that passes this envelope only under E+. Conditional numerical reproduction and semantic diagnosis are available to U, E and E+. A complete crop-yield answer passes for none. Live cumulative monitoring, validated editorial decisions and positive commercial continuity contracts remain unqualified. No measured customer set expands and cumulative incremental commercial net value remains unknown; observed customer revenue is zero.

What the successor does inherit is concrete: executable generic calculations, a small operation checklist, provenance and rights boundaries, transferable bytes, the fresh teacher mapping, adverse cases, a stricter snapshot candidate, exact cash accounting and a necessary admission-feasibility test. Those assets make additional questions executable. Their preservation is justified as research provenance in this edition, not by an invented positive return on future reuse.

Operating blueprint

Offer. Begin with the narrow source-qualified reconstruction or a clearly priced missing-evidence assessment. Identify a specific synthesis owner and its outside option. Obtain an equivalent human quote or a defensible activity estimate for the same scope. A diagnostic refusal must have been valuable under the original contract; it is not relabeled as success afterward.

Deliver expertly. Freeze the question, units, study identity, admissibility policy, uncertainty model, evidence access and acceptance. Automate inventory and reproducible calculations. Use current suitable frontier models to inspect and reconcile meaning, execute conventional tools, challenge the mapping and correct failures. Record retries, model/tool usage, operator and customer effort, source delay and actual support. Independent checks must not reuse the same mistaken assumption as the producer.

Construct. Build only the generic structure needed to serve a real task family: extraction adapters where rights allow, source/effect/model contracts, missing-information requests and executable checks. A live continuity extension must bind ordered events to updated packet hashes, handle idempotency and reinstatement, verify source coverage, and refuse incompatible changes. These are engineering requirements, not already executed production features.

Retain. Separate paid-delivery work from extra qualification/generalization. Apply the necessary feasibility bound before expensive assessment. Preserve rejected candidates and their expense. Admit only after fresh semantic, rights, authority and incremental-value evidence; otherwise retain research receipts without broadcasting a qualified specialist. Use an ordinary library as the same-content counterfactual.

Coordinate and finance. Seek co-commissioning only where customers actually share cleared work. Make the funding threshold, milestones, ownership/licenses, responsibilities, withdrawal consequences, refunds and common-failure response explicit. Do not spend refundable funds or invoices. Model collection timing and capacity during correlated correction bursts. Compare production, exploration, assessment, optional practice, specialist invocation, sharing, preserving cash and stopping without requiring a fixed number of investments.

Expand or stop. Expansion requires earned surplus after liabilities/reserves, accepted quality on unfamiliar work and positive complete incremental value. Stop when participation is too small, adaptation destroys the setup saving, the human/expert alternative already fits the envelope, source rights or evidence are unavailable, defaults exhaust liquidity, or improved model defaults erase the operational advantage. More agents or jobs at larger budgets would not establish compounding.

Limitations, publication and conclusion

The most important limitation is the distance between a source-qualified snapshot calculation and an economically viable continuity service. Supplied secondary data simplify reconstruction. Both numerical datasets were known to the constructor. Explicit event kinds simplify interpretation. Public-source ambiguities remain unresolved. Reviewer and recipients are AI agents with procedural separation. The sample cannot estimate representative user behavior, rare semantic failure or service reliability. No customer effort, full research cost, human equivalent quote or willingness to pay is observed. Conditional cash scenarios and any future simulation intervals cannot validate those assumptions.

The study nevertheless contributes an inspectable experiment and a disciplined rejection of stronger claims. Source meanings survive one actual recipient transfer; incompatible reuse is refused; conventional experts remain strong; a failed diagnostic remains visible; monetary unknowns do not become profit; production authority does not follow from an artifact. These outcomes improve the enterprise’s evidence and test apparatus without proving learning-to-invent or a commercial flywheel.

Publication remains additive. Immutable source versions, all failed/adverse observations, original v1 bytes, prospective corrections, raw result files and licensed source notices are delivered with the LaTeX/PDF, evidence companion, executive brief and English/French browser demonstration. The existing repository paper process and issue 4850 owners govern public deployment. New executable HTML requires full source qualification; a draft source handoff or asset hash is not a verified live paper-reader release. No alternate hosting path, production scope or reserved software version is appropriated.(MONTREAL.AI 2026b)

The practical next step is small: one unfamiliar, rights-cleared legacy packet whose owner values a defined outcome, a fair human/expert alternative, complete activity metering and fresh acceptance. If that test fails, stop or change the opportunity. If it succeeds, evaluate compatible recurrence and financing before sharing or expanding. The successor should inherit useful structure and the reasons it can fail, while earning each new acceptance and authority.
