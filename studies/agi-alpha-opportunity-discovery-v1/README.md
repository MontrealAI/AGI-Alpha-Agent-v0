## What this edition establishes

The selected opportunity is **evidence continuity contracts**: reconstruct a legacy numerical evidence packet
into a portable, purpose-bound instrument that can revalidate a declared snapshot when a source changes,
and stop when interpretation, dependence, rights or authority require fresh qualification.
Six materially different opportunities were investigated before selection. The dossier identifies existing
alerting, living-review and meta-analysis services; it does not claim those fields or methods are new.
See [selection](discovery/selection.json), [science dossier](discovery/science-dossier.md),
[systems dossier](discovery/systems-dossier.md) and [adversarial review](discovery/adversarial-review.md).

This repository executes conditional numerical reproduction, source-semantic controls, a small recipient
pilot, byte transfer, ordinary-library parity, and complete hypothetical cash paths. It does **not** establish
commercial demand, complete delivery costs, a representative ordinary-user effect, sustained specialist
superiority, live protected-network admission, earned revenue or commercial compounding.
Actual complete research and delivery costs are unknown. Actual customer revenue is zero.

The instrument computes an intercept-only REML fit with a normal interval for supplied, conditionally
independent effects. It is not a new meta-analysis algorithm. It does not extract full papers, detect actual
notices, infer covariance, perform network meta-analysis, adjudicate retractions, or operate a cumulative
event history. Reproduction of a secondary extraction does not validate the underlying causal claim.
The crop example is a useful refusal to endorse unresolved semantics; it is not a completed crop analysis.

## Correction and commercial decision discipline

The original v1 confirmation suite passed, but subsequent adversarial review found that additional,
contradictory event fields could be ignored. A list of events was also a collection of independent
snapshots, not a persistent event log. The [preserved review](validation/review/completed_study_independent_review.md)
records these limitations. The additive `continuity_v1_1.py` rejects unsupported event semantics and
explicit cumulative/sequential batch modes. Its [freeze](protocol/correction-freeze-v1_1.json) binds the
candidate, requirements and checker before generation of 46 fresh instances. Its passing results do not
retroactively repair the transferred v1 artifact or establish a fresh v1.1 recipient transfer.

[Economic implementation](economics/README.md) keeps A paid outcome delivery, B additional assessment,
retention/admission/sharing/upkeep, and C optional R&D disjoint. It records deposits as refundable
obligations, accepted milestones, collections, support, withdrawal, failure, delay/default, capacity,
reserves and opening cash separately. Seed capital is never revenue. Expected contribution does not
establish the ability to finance work before payment. Hypothetical counterparties are not customers.

The original *Invent the Economy* reference remains exactly distinguishable: successful contribution
$300,000; expected contribution $120,000; required opening cash $900,000; added route burden produces
negative $8,000 expectation. A successor's $300,000 surplus cannot finance a $450,000 prefunding need;
the assumed setup reduction to $150,000 makes the $300,000 need financeable. These are original synthetic
assumptions and mechanical regression controls, not this offering's observed prices or learning gains.

The 2×2 technical reuse/coordinated demand decomposition remains explicit. Its original single-binding-buyer
restriction is only a historical mechanism fixture; an unrestricted expert may choose the same package.
Private retention and sharing must use measured adaptation/serving costs and independently justified
reuse. A same-content library receives no artificial penalty. No speculative future benefit is earned
surplus. The present evidence supports continued bounded measurement, not monetary Alpha admission or
expansion financed from unmeasured savings.
