# Claim-to-evidence map

Research cut: **28 October 2026**. Paths below are relative to this study directory. The complete paper is `publication/PAPER.md`; its concise companion is `publication/EXECUTIVE_BRIEF.md`. `SOURCES.json` records primary URLs, exact predecessor-paper versions and the scope of source inspection.

**Evidence scope.** The construction, finite checks and circuit-software executions are actual computational work. Buyers, contracts, commercial acceptance, payments and enterprise outcomes are simulated. A separate same-session checking role is used; this is not external peer review. None of the records establishes physical commissioning, independent demand, a new theorem, an exclusive algorithm, a trained specialist or an autonomous compounding advantage.

## Publication and authority boundary

The scientific freeze binds the experiment, not permission to release software. Repository source publication, canonical paper replacement and runtime release are separate actions governed by the existing project policy. This map does not claim that a local test pass, generated PDF or browser edition has been published or has met full release qualification. Any publication/readback receipts belong to the downstream handoff and must identify their exact source version.
