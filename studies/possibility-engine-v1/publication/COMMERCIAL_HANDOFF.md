# Commercial handoff: gates for reopening the offer

## Current decision

**Stop the tested narrow paid offering. Retain the constructor and evidence.** In the frozen reference simulation, the $4,500 quoted offer loses $53,187.20 over the full project, compared with $36,950 for stopping after the completed research and archival exit. Continuing adds $16,237.20 of mean loss. An equally useful free alternative yields zero sales for the paid offering. These conditional results justify a stop decision under the stated assumptions; they do not identify real demand. [Results](../economics/FINDINGS.md)

The useful asset is an executable way to construct a jointly informative intervention and measurement for a narrow supplied network family. The current delivery scope is labeled sixteen-node strongly regular networks with declared uniform-parameter assumptions, finite access choices and bounded error. A computational acceptance does not establish a physical fixture or arbitrary thermal-system identification. [Scientific acceptance](../protocol/ACCEPTANCE.md)

## Evidence required before reopening

| Gate | Concrete evidence to obtain | Decision if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| A beneficiary decision | A team supplies a question, permitted access and the action that changes when the alternatives are distinguished. Compare the value of learning with an action that works across all remaining models. | Preserve the example; do not sell a distinction the beneficiary does not need. |
| A qualified construction | The supplied problem fits the current acceptance contract, or a separately versioned extension survives independent tests of its new assumptions. Physical work needs independently specified timing, calibration, component variation, initialization and missing-hypothesis challenges. | Refuse the unsupported scope or define a capped research question. |
| A useful alternative comparison | Give an unrestricted specialist the same hypotheses, access rights, AI, free source and acceptance criteria. Compare the actual deliverable and the total extraction, validation, integration, correction and support effort. | Do not claim an exclusive or retained-capability advantage; assess whether ordinary dependable delivery still has value. |
| A credible purchase case | Obtain external evidence of willingness to pay for the specific computational outcome and its delivery terms, with self-delivery, free tools, competitors, waiting and declining available. | Keep demand at zero or a supported range; do not convert model-generated enthusiasm into sales. |
| Complete future economics | Measure delivery and qualification effort on externally supplied tasks. Reestimate acquisition, verification, corrections, support, maintenance, collection risk, financing and exit. Distinguish incremental return from the full-project return retaining the original estimated research cost. | Stop if a revised commitment is unattractive across plausible assumptions. |
| A feasible operating arrangement | Identify who owns access, provides models, accepts results and bears correction costs. Establish reuse rights, confidentiality, scope boundaries, deposit release and refund conditions. | Decline the commitment until these dependencies are concrete. |

The original campaign allocated 304 startup hours and $36,200, including failed discovery, development, independent validation, documentation and computing allowances. Those are complete economic estimates, not actual session invoices. Future measurements must include the work surrounding inference. The reference simulated campaign required 512.75 total human hours, including 7.48 hours of operational verification, and still averaged only 1.62 accepted packs. ## Price, capacity and shared commissioning

The tested policy quotes $4,500 before bounded negotiation, takes a 50% restricted deposit and caps intake at two contracts monthly. Its optimistic full-year calculation needs about 39.5 signed contracts to cover full project cost, above its 24-contract capacity. Even after sunk research, the corresponding favorable operating threshold is about 21.5 contracts. The $5,890.08 full-capacity price floor is a necessary arithmetic condition under favorable exclusions, not a demonstrated market price. Raising the price alone does not answer the demand problem. [Break-even calculation](../economics/break_even_analysis.json)

A shared commission may spread fixed work, but the conventional provider must receive the same arrangement. The simplified $4,000-per-sponsor schedule reaches shared research, integration, coordination and validation recovery at twelve committed sponsors; acquisition, financing, support, warranty and exit would add costs. No such sponsors are established. Reuse consent and a conditional commitment deadline would be prerequisites. Deposits remain restricted until the stated conditions are satisfied, and unsuccessful formation requires refunds. Open availability makes waiting and free riding plausible. ## Separate research investment from reopening sales

The modeled $4,000 successor experiment remains unfunded. Its conservative assumed incremental benefit is $1,600, and none of the simulated worlds clears the operating gates. A future pilot should buy evidence capable of changing an explicit decision; it should not book a large imagined terminal value. The possible $20,000 integration stage is a later, separate decision.

If external evidence supports a pilot, define its cost cap, acceptance, alternative, information value and stop condition before committing. Preserve this study's negative outcomes. Any revised offer or extension needs a new version, a bounded development plan, an explicit policy freeze, fresh independently issued confirmation and complete accounting through exit. Do not retune this frozen result.

The ten-year $1 billion beneficiary-value threshold remains an aspiration requiring evidence: at an assumed $2,500 net incremental value per pack, it requires 400,000 useful deliveries and approximately 10,000 active beneficiaries ordering four annually. Neither that reachable population nor that value has been established. The next commercial task is one defensible beneficiary decision, with an economically credible delivery—not a scale forecast.

Customer outreach, purchases, paid commitments, transfers, credential changes and physical deployment require their own authorization. This handoff does not make those commitments. Source publication and software production qualification follow the repository's separate permissions and acceptance process.
