## The possibility

A laboratory should be able to ask what instrument it needs to construct to answer a question that its existing measurements cannot resolve. An enterprise built around that ability would expand what its beneficiaries can investigate, rather than only make an existing procedure faster.

This campaign constructs a precise computational example. Two different hidden diffusion networks produce the same response at the existing port for every input waveform. Adding actuators alone does not help. Adding sensors alone does not help. A joint actuator–sensor addition makes the distinction robustly answerable.

**Decision: retain the instrument; stop the tested narrow paid offering and redesign its application bridge.** The capability change is demonstrated within the declared mathematical family. A commercially consequential physical flagship is not established.

## Commercial delivery and complete economics

**Simulation scope:** all buyers, contracts, commercial acceptance, collections and enterprise results are simulated. Technical construction and circuit-software checks are executed. Costs and demand are disclosed estimates; they are not invoices or observed market behavior.

The candidate offering is a fixed-scope Computational Experiment Construction Pack for a prototype team that can still modify access. Its current implementation supports the tested regular-graph family, not arbitrary thermal systems. A buyer who only needs an equivalent thermal model can choose existing tools or self-delivery.

Two bounded price grids tested 24 policy entries, representing 20 unique combinations. The selected attempt quotes **USD4,500**, takes a **50% restricted deposit**, and allows **two new contracts per month**. Fresh confirmation executes 128 histories in each of ten scenarios, plus conventional and stop counterparts. Independent reconstruction reconciles **1,536 trajectories, 385,682 journal events and 1,828 contracts**, with no terminal deposits, liabilities or receivables left open.

## What the successor should attempt

The next useful construction would connect the exact instrument to a beneficiary decision and a permitted physical fixture. Require a question whose answer changes an action; independently specified nonuniform-component, timing and calibration limits; an incomplete-hypothesis challenge; and a competent unrestricted specialist comparison. Then establish willingness to pay and complete delivery effort.

Expansion is staged. The modeled USD4,000 successor experiment does not pass the conservative incremental-value gate and receives no funding. The possible later USD20,000 integration stage remains separate. Shared commissioning can reduce duplicated work, but the same conventional arrangement receives that benefit; the simplified twelve-sponsor funding threshold is not evidence that twelve willing sponsors exist.

USD1 billion of ten-year incremental beneficiary value would require, at an assumed USD2,500 net value per accepted pack, **400,000 useful packs** and roughly **10,000 active beneficiaries** buying four annually. At current assumed capacity that implies about **1,667 delivery teams**. Reachable demand and that net value have not been established. Revenue, profit and valuation remain distinct.
