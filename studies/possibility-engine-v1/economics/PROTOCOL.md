# Enterprise simulation protocol (pre-confirmation)
Version 1; USD; 12 selling months plus 6 runoff months. Calendar labels start November 2026 and are hypothetical.

**Scope.** Buyers, demand, contracts, operational acceptance, payment and finances are simulated. Technical evidence is produced separately. This first offer is restricted to the validated 16-vertex strongly regular graph family and supplied labeled linear networks. It does not certify arbitrary thermal systems, construct physical equipment, or promise field identification. A delivery is an executable ambiguity witness, jointly selected actuator/sensor access, forcing/measurement protocol, RC netlist and explicit model-uncertainty envelope. No customers were contacted.

## Commercial sequence and incentives
A prospective lab or circuit research team provides admissible labeled networks, bounds, access rights and a clear research question. The seller first qualifies the finite scope. Quotes have a negotiated discount capped at 10%; refusal and capacity limits remain available. Deposits are restricted and remain liabilities until software acceptance. Failed delivery after one correction receives its deposit back. Accepted deliveries receive three months of modeled support/warranty; collection, enforcement, writeoffs and all refunds finish during runoff. Renewal requires a fresh need and a fresh choice against all alternatives.

Each buyer maximizes or noisily maximizes discounted expected research value less price, internal effort and integration cost, subject to budget. Choices are enterprise, same-AI competitor, competent internal self-delivery, free tools, waiting and declining. Enterprise, competitor and competent self-delivery share the same assumed 0.90 usefulness probability. The reference free-tool assumption uses16integration hours and0.82quality. The strongest-free scenario instead assigns the same0.90quality and the same delivery delay as the enterprise, with4hours extraction/validation; low-labor sensitivity uses8hours and reduces posted operating labor rates30%. This stronger alternative is included in policy selection. These probabilities and values are hypotheses, not technical test rates or independently elicited preferences. Logit choice is a bounded-rationality model; a deterministic best-response scenario challenges it.

The competitor chooses match/cut/premium-bundle prices to maximize modeled contribution over 24 public archetypes, without access to actual buyers' private values. A separate imitation scenario cuts price 30% from month four while retaining equal technical ability. Supplier stress triples inference cost and restricts throughput. Correlated quality stress affects deliveries together. Buyers can withhold invoices when modeled immediate gain exceeds expected principal recovery and future-relationship value. The enterprise pursues only positive-expected-recovery invoices. Enforcement probabilities and relationship values are hypothetical behavioral parameters, not legal claims.

The conventional arrangement receives exactly the same information, same AI, same artifacts, same efficiency and same rights. Its economics tie by construction. Any retained-instrument efficiency claim requires a separate empirical result and is not inserted into this model.

## Full cost boundary
The model expenses research, failed discovery, development, independent validation, documentation, startup inference/compute/tools, acquisition, qualification, negotiation, delivery, verification, correction, support, warranty refunds, maintenance, collection, finance and exit. Shared development is charged once. Each hourly amount is an estimated economic cost, not measured session labor or an invoice. Owner labor is not free. Neither retained code nor speculative future value is capitalized.

Startup is 304 hours: research 96h at $125, failed discovery 40h at $125, development120h at $100, validation32h at $125 and documentation16h at $100, plus $1,600 inference/compute/tools: $36,200. Rates are loaded expert-service assumptions; typical operating roles range $80–$140/h. BLS May2025 median pay provides context only: software developers $135,980/year; electrical engineers $120,630/year. These imply roughly $65.38/h and $58.00/h at 2,080h/year, before benefits, nonbillable time, overhead and specialist margin. They do not establish consulting quotes.

## Accounts and solvency
Journal amounts are integer cents with equal debit and credit legs. Cash, restricted cash and receivables are assets; customer deposits, refund/support payables and debt are liabilities. Revenue is recognized only on software acceptance. Support and expected warranty obligations are accrued and later settled or reversed. Cash collections, restricted deposits, revenue and profit are reported separately. Opening equity $80,000 is a journaled financing inflow, not revenue. A $20,000 finite facility can fund shortages at12% APR; opening fee$500 and monthly availability fees$50 are charged even when undrawn. An insolvent world raises an error; it cannot silently erase debt. Exit assigns zero terminal asset value and charges$750. Required cash is the greatest realized unrestricted funding deficit, excluding restricted deposits; this is a path quantity under the model, not a real financing quotation.

Every final world must have zero restricted cash, receivables, customer deposits, support/refund payables and debt; all contracts close. Final unrestricted cash minus opening equity must equal full-project profit. Independent checking must reconstruct these from the journal, not trust supplied balances.

## Development, freeze and confirmation
Two bounded grids of12policies each are compared: initial prices$6k/$8k/$10k and one redesign$3k/$4.5k/$6k, each ×2deposits(30%/50%) ×2monthly capacities(2/4). These24entries contain20unique parameter combinations because$6koverlaps. All are scored under the corrected common model on the same12development seeds and six design-weighted scenarios. Original12-policy source/output remains archive-v1; an intermediate correction run remains archive-v2-pre-free-delay. The only economic redesign lowers prices toward explicitly modeled free/self alternatives; no demand/value input is increased. Weights are decision-design weights, not market-probability estimates. The greatest mean full-project-return score selects the attempted offer. No demand/value retuning is permitted after selection. The no-launch decision after research retains the complete $36,200 research cost and $750 archive/exit cost; it avoids future operating losses. Before any research existed, doing nothing would cost zero. Those are different decision dates.

Freeze includes model.py, assumptions.json, selected_policy.json, protocol and arrangement code. Challenger issues at least128 unique seeds only after hash freeze; none may overlap development. Confirmation executes all10 scenarios, conventional parity and stop-after-research. Numerical standard errors describe Monte Carlo sampling under fixed assumptions; they are not confidence in demand or causal usefulness. Every failed or corrected run is retained.

## Successor stage
Only a campaign with at least12 accepted deliveries,2 renewals, $12k operating contribution and sufficient cash can consider spending$4k on an informative successor experiment from month9. The lower-bound expected incremental benefit must also exceed its cost. Current conservative inputs0.2success probability ×$8k conditional value=$1.6k do not clear the$4k gate, so expansion remains on hold even if operating milestones pass. No benefit is booked. A possible later$20k integration stage stays on hold pending external evidence. The prototype pilot should buy evidence about transfer to a different dynamics family and whether external users value it; uncertain enormous future payoff never enters current returns. Negative commercial results do not make this optional information purchase profitable.


## Joint commissioning and scale
The optional arrangement shares one generic instrument among three consenting sponsors, with private model data separate. At assumed$4k/sponsor, shared integration12h×$125, coordination6h×$125 and validation6h×$125/sponsor cost$4.5k for the cohort. Full$36.2k shared research is charged once, not once per sponsor. Twelve committed sponsors would cover that development and the simple variable-cost schedule; that count is a threshold, not identified buyers. The conventional same-AI arrangement receives identical costs and rights and ties exactly. With independent70%reuse consent, unanimous three-sponsor participation is34.3%; a correlated refusal factor reduces this further. Conditional restricted pledges refund everyone when the cohort fails. Generic open-source availability creates free riding and a waiting equilibrium; no mechanism guarantees cooperation. Coordination expense remains even when commitments fail. See arrangements.py for arithmetic and sensitivity.

Billion-plus significance is defined as$1billion of incremental net beneficiary value relative to the best available alternative, in USD over10years. At an assumed$2,500net benefit per accepted pack, this requires400,000packs,40,000/year and10,000active beneficiaries each commissioning4/year. No reachable pool, adoption or net-benefit estimate is established. Revenue, profit and valuation are not inferred from this threshold.
