# Pre-build candidate selection: a question the instrument cannot yet answer

Recorded 28 October 2026. This is a selection and acceptance proposal before the candidate implementation is evaluated. A final freeze must bind the actual source, costs, tests, hypotheses and uncertainty policy before fresh confirmation.

## Selection

Selected exploratory construction: **construct an added permitted actuation or measurement port that makes exact rooted diffusion hypotheses distinguishable**. Current-port forcing alone is the inherited restriction. The constructor must build an executable computational instrument and experiment specification; a physical port is not considered manufactured merely because it appears in a simulation.

The alternatives were explored as possible future activities before commercial entry was chosen. Availability of a convenient dataset was not the selection criterion. Conventional algorithmic parity is allowed. Commercial and flagship admission still require their own evidence.

## Seven transformation statements for the selected construction

1. **If this succeeds, beneficiaries will be able to** distinguish specified hidden diffusion or transport mechanisms using an executable redesigned experiment, **which current alternatives do not adequately enable** when confined to the existing port and its declared observations. This is a scoped claim about the current instrument, not a claim that the field lacks experimental-design methods.
2. **The constraint we are changing is** the assumed immutability of the actuation/readout interface. Identical transfer behavior under every allowed current-port forcing is a genuine information limit; allowing a justified new port changes the accessible evidence.
3. **The new means we will construct are** a finite-model obstruction detector, a constructor for an affordable allowed port/experiment, and an executable discrimination instrument with a separate exact or independently numerical checker and an explicit uncertainty envelope.
4. **The recent frontier change that makes this plausible is** accessible reasoning and coding systems that can combine algebraic investigation, executable scientific tool construction, adversarial testing and reusable interfaces within one bounded campaign. Public scientific claims and accessible tools are distinguished in the frontier matrix; no result depends on access to an unavailable internal model.
5. **The first useful commercial outcome is** a fixed-scope computational experiment-design package for an instrument developer or thermal-modeling team: supplied candidate models, permitted port constraints, a runnable redesign, predicted distinguishing observations, stated error limits and commissioning instructions.
6. **That first outcome exercises the larger transformation because** it must turn a previously unanswerable model distinction into a checkable experiment under the same scientific purpose and a declared construction budget, rather than only improve the fit of existing data.
7. **The next productive possibility it could open is** a successor that receives a meaningfully different multi-hypothesis or restricted-port task and uses the retained constructor to design a new experiment or demonstrate that more apparatus is necessary; comparison includes both a conventional fresh expert and an ordinary library of the same artifacts.

## Commercial scope and acceptance boundary

The initial buyer is an instrument-development or thermal-modeling engineering team whose prototype design still permits sensor or actuator access decisions. Its purchase is a **computational design commission**, not a diagnosis of an installed physical device. The scoped deliverable accepts supplied finite candidate networks and permitted ports. Acceptance requires reproducible current-interface indistinguishability where claimed, a valid new-interface distinction or an honest impossibility/refusal result, independent recalculation, robustness within the declared uncertainty class, complete source and a handoff the buyer can assess. A refusal report by itself is not the transformational achievement; at least one accepted enabling construction is required.

The construction is a seed because every delivery exercises model-to-experiment synthesis and records the limits under which a new observation is useful. Those are directly relevant to a future physical instrument commissioning service and to different model-discrimination families. Proposed hardware, calibration, access negotiation, installation and physical acceptance remain future costs rather than hidden completed capabilities.

## Independent prior-art and free-substitute check

### Existing scientific theory

H. J. van Waarde, P. Tesi and M. K. Camlibel, *Topology identification of heterogeneous networks: Identifiability and reconstruction*, Automatica 123, 109331 (January 2021), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2020.109331 ; open preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11054v2 . The paper already formalizes topology indistinguishability for every input and characterizes identifiability through transfer functions. It provides identification methods for heterogeneous linear networks. Our declared finite-family port construction must be compared with established experimental design, not described as inventing topology identification or transfer-function reasoning. Finite candidate-family discrimination is weaker than identifying an arbitrary unknown network.

N. Chatzikiriakos, K. Jamieson and A. Iannelli, *High Effort, Low Gain: Fundamental Limits of Active Learning for Linear Dynamical Systems*, AISTATS 2026, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/chatzikiriakos26a.html . It studies finite-hypothesis identification and derives conditions governing the value of excitation design. Its lesson for this campaign is to test whether the added freedom actually matters and preserve cases where active design buys little. It does not validate this implementation or establish a commercial opportunity.

### Existing practical need and conventional methods

N. J. Ziegeler, P. W. Nolte and S. Schweizer, *Optimization-Based Network Identification for Thermal Transient Measurements*, Energies 14(22), 7648, published 16 November 2021, DOI https://doi.org/10.3390/en14227648 . This primary study treats thermal structure reconstruction from step responses as established practice and develops inverse optimization to improve noise robustness. It supports the relevance of compact thermal-model inference, but it also rules out presenting thermal transient analysis as a newly discovered activity. Its one-dimensional thermal-equivalent problem differs from the proposed finite topological hypothesis problem.

E. Schulz, C. Strössner and C. Grötzbach, *Identifiability of Cauer thermal networks*, author primary listing https://erickschulz.dev/publications/src/data/publications/identifiability-cauer-thermal-networks/ , marked accepted in IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics (2026). The authors report a profile-likelihood test for how many layers can be resolved, implementation in PLECS and tests on synthetic and measured data. This is a strong existing approach to practical identifiability. Only the author abstract was available here; do not claim a complete independent reproduction or infer a release/version from that listing.

### Free substitute

**PyRth**, primary repository https://github.com/nizie002/PyRth , MIT-licensed according to its repository. It evaluates thermal transient data and includes deconvolution, thermal structure functions, an optimization module, bootstrap analysis, theoretical models and temperature prediction. It is an existing free alternative to parts of a thermal-analysis service. It is not described as an arbitrary-topology added-port constructor, so merely failing to use PyRth to solve a different task is not evidence of superiority. Buyers who only need an equivalent thermal model may rationally use it and decline the proposed commission.

### Commercial substitute

Siemens' primary product material for Simcenter Micred T3STER describes controlled power steps, thermal impedance and structure functions, including thermal package comparison and model calibration: https://www.siemens.com/en-gb/products/simcenter/simulation-test/thermal-testing/ . The recent official article https://blogs.sw.siemens.com/simcenter/extend-thermal-characterization-to-high-power-ics-and-gan-devices/ describes an extended sensing-current architecture. These sources establish available alternatives and the relevance of measurement architecture, not price, buyer willingness to pay, or direct applicability to the exact proposed finite graphs.

## Claims permitted if the tests pass

- An executable construction changes which supplied finite hypotheses can be distinguished under a named interface restriction and uncertainty model.
- Its design can be independently assessed and used as a computational commissioning artifact.
- A conventional method may achieve the same answer, and retained-capability advantage may fail even when the artifact is useful.

## Claims not established by such a pass

- A new actuator-placement theorem or exclusive algorithm.
- A physically validated thermal instrument or actual damage diagnosis.
- A new market category, independent buyer demand, profitable operations or realized revenue.
- General mechanism discovery outside the supplied model family.
- Learned specialist improvement or sustained skill compounding merely from reusing code.
