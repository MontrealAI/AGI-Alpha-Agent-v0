# Independent challenge memorandum

Prepared for the AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL campaign, 2026-10-28. This is a same-session independent work role, not external peer review. No fresh confirmation seeds have been generated.

## The claim that needs to survive

An instrument may establish a useful change in *what can be attempted* without establishing a new scientific algorithm, a monopoly, a market, or compounding intelligence. These are four separate propositions. A campaign that conflates them should fail even when its software passes.

The strongest locally testable achievement would expose an activity that is impossible under an explicitly specified initial observation/action grammar, construct a lawful additional instrument or representation, and show the desired outcome becomes attainable. The impossible-before statement must be mathematical or precisely resource-bounded; it must not be shorthand for “we did not try a competent alternative.”

## Independently explored possibilities (not ranked by available datasets)

1. **Turn an unanswerable scientific question into a runnable distinguishing experiment.** A researcher supplies competing mechanistic explanations; the system can construct a new intervention, composite readout, or measurement protocol that resolves an ambiguity none of the original experiments can resolve. Missing means: an expandable experiment grammar with semantics, instrument synthesis, and a checker that preserves the scientific question. First useful delivery: a reproducible discrimination protocol for a defined mechanism family. Risk: ordinary optimal experimental design is mature; novelty requires an expansion of the available experimental means, not merely choosing the next standard assay. Physical use remains conditional until the new protocol is actually performed.

2. **Design a material from an intended behavior that no catalog part can perform.** An engineer asks for a passive structure whose force–displacement or spectral response implements a useful switch, memory, or computation. Missing means: search over architectures plus faithful mechanics and manufacturable output. First delivery: a fabrication-ready structure and simulation certificate. Risk: inverse metamaterial design has extensive prior art and a simulation-only result is not a validated physical device. A finite-element reference and fabrication tolerances are essential.

3. **Create a joint productive activity across organizations that cannot sustain it individually.** Complementary participants jointly support a shared instrument, experiment, or production process; the arrangement makes a previously infeasible output possible while each participant can rationally prefer participation. Missing means: executable technical composition, explicit reuse rights, conditional commitments, and robust allocation. First delivery: one coalition-backed artifact with a conventional joint-delivery comparison. Risk: assigning large invented buyer values manufactures apparent transformation; coalition feasibility and demand must remain separate.

4. **Let a successor search a different space by retaining a discovered representation.** A predecessor creates a computational instrument that converts an intractable family of tasks into a tractable one; a successor uses it on a fresh task with different goals. Missing means: extraction, semantic validation, transfer, integration, and upkeep. First useful delivery: an exact executable representation and a demonstrated before/after search. Risk: a cached answer or library of examples is not a learned method; equal-information ordinary-library and expert-AI baselines are mandatory.

5. **Create interoperable knowledge without forcing owners to reveal all private inputs.** Organizations jointly discover a process or model from complementary confidential capabilities. Missing means: compositional semantics plus a privacy-preserving computation and a participation arrangement. First delivery: a specific joint outcome unavailable to any member independently. Risk: secure multiparty computation and federated optimization are established; merely using them does not establish a new productive possibility, and privacy claims require an explicit threat model.

## Baseline warning from primary literature

The following primary work makes “AI selects better experiments” or “AI inverse-designs a mechanical structure” inadequate as standalone novelty claims:

- von Kügelgen et al., *Nonparametric Identifiability of Causal Representations from Unknown Interventions* (2023), https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~blei/papers/K%C3%BCgelgenBesserveLiangGreseleKeki%C4%87BareinboimBleiSch%C3%B6lkopf2023.pdf. Identifiability depends on explicit assumptions and intervention information.
- Tigas et al., *Interventions, Where and How? Experimental Design for Causal Models at Scale* (NeurIPS 2022), https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/98a5c0470e57d518ade4e56c6ee0b363-Abstract-Conference.html. Bayesian active design already chooses intervention targets and values for nonlinear causal models.
- *Identifiability and experimental design in perturbation studies* (2020), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7355299/. Explicit network identifiability and experimental-design analyses predate the current campaign.
- Ronellenfitsch et al., *Inverse Design of Discrete Mechanical Metamaterials* (2019), https://math.mit.edu/~dunkel/Papers/2019RoEtAl_PRM.pdf and https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.07214. Inverse design, switchable spectral gaps, and finite-element verification already exist.
- *Rapid inverse design of metamaterials based on prescribed mechanical behavior through machine learning* (2023), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10505607/. Includes open source code (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8210037).

These sources were retrieved as prior-art cautions, not exhaustively replicated. Root should open any cited source before including a web citation in the final response.

## Mandatory comparative conditions

### Construction

Freeze beneficiary purpose, task distribution, safety limits, allowed input, resource budget, and acceptance metric before the confirmation task exists. The developer may make bounded changes on development tasks; original attempts and failure logs remain immutable.

Compare at least:

1. Current means: a competent conventional method restricted only by the *real* constraint the proposed construction changes.
2. Competent alternative: expert current AI plus free software, with the same facts, implementation time and permitted action space as the proposed system.
3. Ordinary library: all predecessor artifacts and documentation, with no privileged structured extraction or compiled instrument.
4. Retained instrument: the same artifacts plus the purported executable method, charging extraction, validation, integration and upkeep.

If alternatives 2 or 3 tie, report the construction’s usefulness while rejecting exclusive or retained-capability advantage. Never handicap the baseline by forbidding the obvious representation change while allowing it to the invention.

### Fresh successor

The successor must change at least one meaningful axis: objective, action grammar, topology, mechanism class, or beneficiary requirement. Fresh random coefficients in the same task are a generalization check, not a materially different mission. The new task and its seeds are generated only after the method and policy are frozen. Record all oracle calls, human interventions, failures, verification work, and exception queues.

### Independent check

Use an independently implemented checker or trusted exact solver to establish semantics. Tests copied from implementation internals are insufficient. Reproduce a known impossible case, a boundary feasible case, and an adversarial nearly feasible case. Verify that failure reports and unsupported states are preserved rather than silently coerced into accepted outputs.

## Enterprise simulation acceptance

The scope should be prominently stated once: strategic buyers, transactions, and enterprise accounts are simulated; executed software outcomes have separate evidence. Unknown demand is modeled as an assumption, not inferred from a model’s enthusiasm.

Keep these quantities distinct:

- Revenue recognized for accepted work.
- Collections actually received in the simulated world.
- Restricted deposits owed back until release conditions hold.
- Deferred obligations, refunds, correction and warranty liabilities.
- Total project profit after discovery and development.
- Operating contribution before shared one-time development.
- Unrestricted cash, external financing, financing cost and final investor recovery.

Each world must run acquisition → qualification → negotiation → contract → delivery → acceptance/correction → collection/refund → support → renewal/exit → final liability runoff. No horizon truncation may make unfinished obligations disappear. Charge common research and development once per enterprise world; do not allocate them again to each customer while also expensing the global total.

Buyer alternatives must include self-delivery using the same accessible AI, competitor service, free substitutes, waiting and declining. Introduce imitation and price cuts; do not represent a powerful competitor as permanently inert. Payment default should be a strategic option wherever the assumed contract makes default privately beneficial. Conventional delivery under the same coalition or pooled arrangement is a separate baseline.

Record policy selection on development scenarios. Freeze selected offer, acceptance terms, work-selection rule and financing rule before fresh confirmation. Use common random numbers for paired policy comparisons, but not to retune the selected policy after confirmation.

The confirmation report should include expected full-project return, median and lower-tail return, operating contribution, maximum cash deficit, probability of loss, expected unpaid receivables, refunds, obligations at closure (must be zero or explicitly funded), and break-even price/volume/acceptance/collection thresholds. A Monte Carlo standard error quantifies simulation sampling precision; it does not quantify confidence in demand or cost assumptions.

## Accounting reconciliation identities

For a closed unlevered enterprise with no residual assets or liabilities, final cash minus owner capital equals project profit. With financing, explicitly account for net financing principal and finance costs. During operation:

`cash + accounts_receivable + restricted_cash + other_assets = debt + deposits_liability + accrued_obligations + contributed_equity + retained_profit`.

Check the identity after every event, not merely the final aggregate. A separate verifier should independently reconstruct totals from the event journal rather than trust a summary object computed by the simulator.

## Billion-plus significance

Specify metric, currency, horizon and reachable market mechanics. Beneficiary value, enterprise revenue, profit and valuation are not interchangeable. A model with 100 customers at USD 10,000 cannot establish a billion-dollar enterprise. A prospective scale route can be bounded by sales throughput, implementation capacity, support load, market adoption and repeatable margin; absent evidence should remain a conditional threshold table, not an expected valuation.

## Decision rule

Expand only when technical acceptance survives the competent alternative, actual delivery advantage is measured, and staged investment has a bounded attractive decision threshold under conservative assumptions. Redesign when useful construction survives but the proposed commercial form or retained advantage fails. Stop the offering when its contribution reduces to routine work without the consequential activity that justified it, or when honest full costs defeat the modeled opportunity across plausible conditions.

No amount of polished publication should turn a bounded negative campaign into a claimed flagship.

## Additional candidate challenge: controllers learned from actual subprocesses

The challenger proposed a directly executable variant: learn an opaque stateful subprocess through interventions, construct a finite-state model, and synthesize a controller that achieves a beneficiary's purpose. This would create an operational means, rather than just report a fault. However, a subsequent primary-source check found direct prior art, so the candidate is **not endorsed as a new activity on this basis alone**:

- Zhang, Feng and Li, *Control of Black-Box Embedded Systems by Integrating Automaton Learning and Supervisory Control Theory of Discrete-Event Systems* (2019), https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1343552/FULLTEXT01.pdf. The research explicitly combines learning, testing, and controller synthesis into an online software patch.
- LearnLib, https://learnlib.de/ and https://github.com/learnlib/learnlib, supplies active and passive learning, multiple automaton classes, abstraction/refinement support, and equivalence approximations.
- Smeenk et al., *Applying Automata Learning to Embedded Control Software* (2015), https://www.mbsd.cs.ru.nl/publications/papers/fvaan/ESM/, reports an industrial printer/copier software application. This establishes that black-box learning is not merely a new synthetic possibility created by the present campaign.

A distinct application-level construction could still be valuable. The paper must explain what that construction makes practical that existing arrangements do not adequately enable, and compare fairly with the above methods.
