    04 / SEPARATE ACTORS · EXPLICIT FUNDING

    Earn review. Fund the committed work.

    Independent review, opening a campaign, approving spend and purchasing lots are separate actions. Resume after each confirmed transition.

    Independent risk review

    The eligible owner opens a review. Genuine validators check the evidence and sign their own decisions. A round can expire or lose validity; current green status is read from the contract.

    I am an independent validator

    Connect your own eligible validator wallet. Owner and creator exclusions, stake and actual review obligations are checked on-chain. Different addresses alone do not establish independence.

    Validator stake and availability in shared MARK workspace →

    Campaign terms

    Set a reachable funding target and a work window that includes auction, delivery and review. Funding a partial target does not fund every job. An existing campaign is resumed, never silently replaced.

    Fund with an exact, bounded amount

    Lots are campaign funding units, not company shares or a promise of returns. Approval permits spending; it does not buy anything. Purchase is a separate wallet action. Never send tokens directly to MARK.

      Transfer NFT ownership deliberately

      Transferring the NFT does not transfer the capsule passphrase, existing campaign authority or legal rights. It can interrupt review and activation. No blanket operator approval is needed.

      Create a successor, preserve the parent

      A successor is a new seed and plan. Current parent ownership is checked, and the parent remains unchanged. Approval, campaign authority, capsule access and legal rights are separate; none are inherited automatically.

      Convert an older, unminted capsule

      Keep the original file. Define and compile new jobs in Plan the work, add public evidence in Protect & publish, then decrypt the older capsule here. Conversion produces a new encrypted package with the exact plan; it cannot replace the commitments of an existing NFT.
