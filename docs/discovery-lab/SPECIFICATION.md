## Foundation and six connected stages

### Foundation: Transformational Intelligence

Freeze the intended outcomes, constraints, permissions, evaluator and construction grammar. Each transformation needs an executable realization, a purpose-preservation bridge and evidence against competent alternatives. A successor receives a versioned charter; never silently relax the task.

### Out-Learn: construct the measurement interface

Search sampling times, polarity schedules, correction weights and reference calibration jointly with the expected use horizon. Produce raw calibration evidence, a gain interval, executable measurement program, certified error, valid scope and costs.

Pass the resulting observation/context relation to Out-Think. Preserve interval ambiguity: a downstream stage cannot turn uncertain measurements into exact facts by assertion. Changed drift scope or insufficient calibration must cause rejection or new work.

### Out-Think: construct an executable representation

Derive distinctions required for answers and close them under permitted operations. Construct checked encoder, contextual decoder and update functions. Allow retirement of obsolete internal labels while retaining required behavior.

The actual decoder/updater programs and their resource footprint become inputs to Out-Design. Reject codes that answer now but cannot update. Adding an operation may require a larger representation or erase the advantage.

### Out-Design: construct usable capacity

Compile joint realizations of required services and independently check full finite behavior, worker limits and continuous service availability. Produce a release certificate and a valid transition route.

The released worker must be in the right location for the next installation. Out-Strategise receives only genuinely reachable placements and construction actions. Include a case where equivalent computational savings at the wrong location are useless, and compare extra staging when allowed.

### Out-Strategise: construct a completion that needs less information

Jointly select observations and robust construction. A coarse diagnosis plus a bypass may succeed in every remaining world without identifying the exact fault.

Produce a conditional policy, residual beliefs, common completions, mandatory checkpoint checks and resource reservations. These define Out-Execute's actual constraints and coupling. Include cases where full diagnosis or no-observation construction is better. A stricter checkpoint must invalidate an obsolete route.

### Out-Execute: retain progress through change

Construct complete finite boundary value functions from explicit factor scopes. Retain compatible components, rebuild affected ones and stop propagation only when complete profiles and relevant bindings permit it.

Always materialize current witnesses and perform current acceptance. Pass a reusable accepted capability, not merely an old answer, to Out-Evolve. Reject hidden cross-component interactions, changed interfaces and scalar-only reuse claims. Keep stronger fixed-certificate competitors.

### Out-Evolve: build the next means of discovery

Construct a platform whose useful response has a simpler qualified structure. Search an explicit grammar of isolation, reference and modularization operations; qualify the claimed structure from semantics and independent finite checks, not just a favorable curve fit.

Freeze the platform before the supplied downstream query evaluations. Introduce a new setting or operation and construct a stronger successor, preserving old obligations and explicitly requalifying changed ones. Compare against scratch and record which parent assets survived.

The existing Out-Evolve nuisance-channel runtime does not automatically implement the revised paper's broader interaction-structure theory. Add a bounded constructor/checker where needed and label the distinction. A conditional theorem is not a measured physical result.
