    Members-only sharing and the capability lifecycle
    Public examples on this page contain no protected capabilities. A real skill becomes shareable only after admission, rights clearance and checks of scope, freshness, dependencies and revocation.
    Real access must verify wallet control and current ENS authority over a direct label under agent.agi.eth at the service boundary. A typed name, local flag or test fixture is insufficient. Access does not authorize every action a skill describes.
    Qualification blocker: no approved protected-capsule service or verifier of current direct-label agent.agi.eth authority is connected to this static page. No authenticated network access is claimed. Existing club.agi.eth roles, Jobs contracts, governance and settlement remain under their existing systems.
    Models can change. Skills need requalification after model, tool, data or verifier changes: advantage retained, adaptation needed, ordinary Beta, superseded or retired. Graph growth does not prove a durable advantage.
