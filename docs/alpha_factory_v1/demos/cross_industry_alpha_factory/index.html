CROSS-INDUSTRY / SUPPLIED ASSESSMENTS

Compare priorities. Keep the evidence attached.

Bring your own opportunities, ratings and evidence notes. Adjust review thresholds and priorities, then inspect a reproducible ranking and the trade-offs it leaves open.

The example is synthetic. Ratings are supplied assessments, not verified facts. Scores do not forecast revenue, returns or success. No provider, market source or evidence URL is contacted.

1. Supply your comparison

Priority weights · whole points from 0 to 100

Eligibility thresholds

Higher benefit and confidence increase the score; higher effort and risk reduce it. Weights are normalized. At least one weight must be positive.

Supplied opportunities

Keep evidence as notes or references. Each line is preserved as supplied text and is never fetched. Replace synthetic assumptions with your own measured references before decisions.

2. Inspect ranking and trade-offs

A non-dominated option has no other eligible option at least as good on every rating and strictly better on one. It can still rank below another option under your selected weights.

Review the evidence and uncertainty yourself. Eligibility is a supplied threshold check, not permission to act. Equal scores use ASCII identifier order; excluded candidates have no rank.
