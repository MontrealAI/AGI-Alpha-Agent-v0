\hypertarget{abstract}{%
\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}}

We connect versioned offerings and bounded enterprise decisions to an
existing reasoning/tool executor, optional workflow-learning Gym,
protected capability service and disjoint cost ledger. An internal
engineering artifact is executed and independently checked. The frozen
workflow is tested on 12 fresh finite problems. The system preserves the
economically defensible negative result: admission is rejected,
additional investment is declined, and an adjacent offering remains a
hypothesis. A separate fictional-authority episode tests actual
protected loading and invocation. Neither episode establishes live
frontier-model advantage, actual customer demand, complete-cost profit
or sustained compounding.

\hypertarget{implementation-and-decision-method}{%
\subsection{Implementation and decision
method}\label{implementation-and-decision-method}}

\texttt{flywheel-offering/v1} records a beneficiary, useful outcome,
tangible deliverable, scope, exact acceptance criteria, internal need
evidence, demand uncertainty, matched human-service reference, required
capabilities, permitted tools, candidate prices, capacity, turnaround,
complete-cost ceiling, risks and limits. Consecutive revisions reset
qualification. They cannot inherit a previous version's success.

The separate stages are hypothesis, executed prototype, technical
qualification, commercial assessment and authorized offering. A
completed model response cannot advance an offering. Prototype
qualification requires its own actual enterprise launch and exact
terminal executor record. Technical qualification uses the existing
independently signed, rights-bound general acceptance and complete A
accounting. Its current implemented adapter remains finite service
placement. Commercial forecasts remain signed assumptions. A negative
assessment stays recorded and cannot authorize production. Even a
positive fictional assessment does not establish a live customer or
grant permission to make external commitments.

The bounded decision policy considers production, assessment, Gym,
specialist invocation, retention, sharing, prototyping, holding,
deferring and stopping. It maximizes conservative forecast benefit minus
the cash-cost cap and separately declared opportunity cost over the
supplied feasible alternatives. Ties choose lower cash cost, then a
stable identifier. This is a transparent finite policy, not a universal
optimum or calibrated demand predictor. Alternative prices can compete
in the same menu; no discount or reinvestment fraction is fixed.

Current journal evidence, source/configuration, current capability
eligibility, reserves, unknown liabilities, occupied capacity, time and
monetary bounds feed each decision. Unknown costs block additional
funded work. The decision is advice until a separate signed exact permit
authorizes the existing executor. Production and prototype missions must
preserve the offering's beneficiary, purpose, deliverable, acceptance
criteria, mode, tools and budgets. One decision permits one attempt. All
alternative evidence and offering need records are rechecked in the
dispatch transaction. An old decision refuses after relevant source,
capability, control, offering, capacity or ledger changes.

\texttt{mission-run\ -\/-enterprise-decision} connects the policy to the
existing executor. Other lifecycle actions need their existing signed
commands. Capability-dependent production composition remains
unsupported; a requirement never invokes a capsule. Protected invocation
retains the existing ENS/wallet and mission-permission boundaries.

\hypertarget{executed-comparisons-and-economics}{%
\subsection{Executed comparisons and
economics}\label{executed-comparisons-and-economics}}

The recorder executes one internal research-funded service-placement
deliverable, checks it with the existing exhaustive oracle, records its
offering qualifications, and runs the same origin into the existing Gym
and protected capability lifecycle. Training searches retained
executable workflows; proprietary model weights do not change. The
frozen v2 is evaluated on 12 materially changed finite placement
instances. Separate ordering, pruning, composition, strong conventional,
private, shared and identical-content ordinary-library controls execute
on four cases.

The technical path rejects admission: competent current frontier Beta
and complete actual monetary costs are unmeasured. The controller
selects holding funds and the adjacent disruption-aware rollout offering
remains a hypothesis. Technical transfer is not a commercial
qualification. The strong conventional procedure matches v2,
identical-content access controls have the same algorithmic result, and
composition adds coordination work. These observations do not
demonstrate a graph advantage, commercially newly feasible work or
sustained compounding.

A separate positive \textbf{fictional protocol episode} starts with its
own connected offering/execution origin and invokes a pinned capsule
through real signatures, encryption, loading and computation. Model, ENS
and billing attestations are fictional. This tests the protected relay
mechanism; it is not actual authenticated mainnet Agent B, real model
work or net Alpha. The rejected technical episode is never relabeled as
admitted.

Actual provider calls and customer collections are zero. Complete actual
research cost and profit remain unknown. The illustrative equivalent
human-service reference is 40 team hours at USD 100/hour (USD 4,000),
disclosed as an estimate, not observed market pricing. USD
1,000/2,000/3,000 prices are hypotheses, not accepted quotations.
Conformance seed capital and category amounts are fictional. USDC
operating economics, accounting USD units and applicable \$AGIALPHA
utility remain distinct.

Required development for the internal deliverable is charged once in A.
Additional Gym development is C, and incremental
assessment/retention/serving is B. No new controller fee is added to the
ledger, and no paid-delivery work is billed again as research. Actual
accounting still requires independent attribution of every resource
category, corrections, failed attempts, refunds and upkeep. Opportunity
cost is a decision input, not a second cash charge. Profitable Beta-only
delivery is a valid policy without any retained capability.

\hypertarget{failure-boundaries-and-remaining-qualification}{%
\subsection{Failure boundaries and remaining
qualification}\label{failure-boundaries-and-remaining-qualification}}

Attempts reserve capacity before I/O and consume their decision
permanently. Failed/interrupted executions retain uncertain liabilities
and capacity. No automatic retry or restoration of authority occurs. To
release a terminal failed attempt, first stop the enterprise, reconcile
and seal all A liabilities with the existing accounting authority, then
submit a signed \texttt{reconcile} command naming the exact launch head
and current evidence. Terminal receipts must match the reserved permit,
mission and profile. A lost launch finalization can be reconciled from
that bound terminal failure; the consumed decision stays consumed. An
attempt with no executor receipt or with an in-flight receipt requires
separate operator investigation; this version deliberately cannot clear
it automatically.

Directed regression tests exercise delivery, negative/positive fictional
assessment, no-learning production, permission widening, stale evidence,
roles, expiry, replay, version reset, unknown costs and interrupted
recovery. The isolated full setup also reported dependency conflicts; a
clean bootstrap is not established. Exact-source hosted review, required
CI, full release coverage and installed/public qualification remain
separate gates. Preserve the v1.46 main publication reservation; this
child does not dispatch another publisher or replace existing papers.

Live qualification requires the existing minimum approved resources:
model account with quota, liability caps, hard budget and billing;
qualified sandbox/browser; protected HTTPS/storage/key/checkpoint
custody; two approved mainnet RPC readers with checked code pins; two
distinct owner-controlled eligible agent wallets; fresh rights-cleared
missions, actual competent frontier comparisons and independent
acceptance. No credentials, paid infrastructure, persistent access,
financial transactions or external sales were provisioned. General
capability-dependent composition also needs further engineering, not
just credentials.
