\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
An enterprise can complete a task without improving its capacity to perform the next one. We study a stronger, bounded mechanism: an accepted achievement becomes an executable asset with explicit applicability, dependencies, evidence and cost; a successor can reuse that asset while earning its own acceptance. We implement and evaluate this mechanism in the existing AGI ALPHA workbench and mathematical enterprise controller. Six checked exact-rational programs support later transform, convolution and inverse tasks; a changed-kernel successor becomes a seventh retained artifact. At 48 tasks, retention uses 63,196 declared arithmetic-work units versus 915,136 for repeated construction. The supplied expert uses 5,760, and full inherited successor qualification loses 7,918 versus 2,030 for direct qualification. These losses are integral results.

A second construction addresses a thermal-metrology counterexample: a 4.9477\% prediction--observation relative residual coexists with 8.6964\% error against a numerical heat reference. A separate simulated volume witness and bounded, volume-weighted temperature difference yield a conditional 1.8862\% error bound. The adapter executes through the native worker and durable journal and supports three measurement families. Physical validity remains unestablished. We separately exercise source-defined Ascension contracts in a local EVM with simulated balances; the complete independently accepted thermal enterprise-to-settlement journey remains unfinished.

The contribution is an inspectable construction and evaluation of capability inheritance: retained executable lineage, full accounting within a declared metric, informative negative results, and explicit distinctions among numerical, institutional and physical acceptance. We derive conditional amortization and error-enclosure relations, identify validation replay as a measured bottleneck, and specify falsifiable transitions toward physical instrumentation and productive infrastructure. No monetary advantage, achieved general intelligence or unlimited growth follows from these finite demonstrations.

\section{From delivered work to expanded capability}\label{sec:introduction}
The useful question for a productive organization is not exhausted by whether it finished a task. A delivery can leave behind an instrument, executable method, representation, process or manufacturing asset that changes the feasible work available to its successors. Such an achievement has two outputs: the immediate result and a reusable means of producing further results. Their value must be assessed separately.

\TI\ names the project's discipline of changing the means by which a worthwhile objective is pursued: improve the observations, representation, instrument or organization while preserving the objective and its acceptance boundaries \cite{boucher2026transformational}. Here that discipline is made operational through a deliberately finite system. The software has supplied objectives, fixed checkers, bounded resources, persistent evidence and explicit refusal states. It cannot increase its authority or redefine success to manufacture progress.

We investigate four questions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can accepted executable structure survive its original mission and support a distinct successor?
\item Does reuse lower complete counted work as later workload grows, and how does it compare with a supplied expert?
\item Can a constructive measurement interface expose an error that a prediction--observation residual misses?
\item Which execution, acceptance, economic and physical boundaries remain before these achievements can support useful enterprises?
\end{enumerate}

The answer is mixed in an informative way. Retention amortizes construction, but the expert wins the workload comparison. A successor extends the retained inventory, but inherited qualification is more expensive than direct qualification. A conditional thermal enclosure addresses the specified numerical mismatch, but does not establish physical calibration. Native execution and local-contract settlement both run, but the thermal enterprise handoff actually refuses the unadmitted family. These outcomes constrain the mechanism rather than weaken its description.

\subsection{Contribution and relation to prior work}
Reusable temporal structure has substantial antecedents. Options formalize temporally extended policies within reinforcement learning \cite{sutton1999options}; our exact-rational program inventory is a different, fixed-interface construction, not an options-learning benchmark. Automated physical science also predates this project: Robot Scientist Adam combined hypotheses with laboratory experiments \cite{king2009automation}, and a mobile robotic chemist combined physical laboratory operation with constrained search \cite{burger2020robotic}. The present thermal experiment does not reproduce those physical achievements.

Our contribution is the particular executed combination of:
\begin{itemize}
\item a retained capability graph with explicit applicability and a changed-requirement successor;
\item a complete ledger within a declared arithmetic metric, including failed search, admission and replay;
\item a constructive response to the preserved Foundry measurement failure;
\item source-bound execution, recovery and contract evidence whose unconnected boundaries remain visible.
\end{itemize}
We do not claim a new transform algorithm, the first reusable skill, the first autonomous laboratory, a generally superior enterprise, or open-ended recursive improvement. The distinction between scope-preserving software reuse and institutionally accepted, physically useful production is the organizing contribution \cite{alphasource,compoundingdata,thermalguide}.

\section{A precise model of capability inheritance}\label{sec:model}
\subsection{Enterprise, asset and evidence}
For this study, an \emph{enterprise} is a software operating record, not an incorporated entity. Write
\begin{equation}
E_t=(O,A_t,B_t,K_t,J_t,\mathcal{Q}_t), \label{eq:enterprise}
\end{equation}
where \(O\) is the objective, \(A_t\) permitted authority, \(B_t\) resource allowances, \(K_t\) retained capabilities, \(J_t\) history and \(\mathcal{Q}_t\) obligations. A capability is represented conceptually by
\begin{equation}
k=(f,D,\operatorname{dep},h,e,v,c), \label{eq:capability}
\end{equation}
with executable program \(f\), applicability domain \(D\), dependencies, content identity \(h\), evidence \(e\), validation policy \(v\) and attributed cost \(c\). The notation summarizes the implemented records; it is not a new universal interchange standard.

An accepted artifact may be reused only where its domain and relevant dependencies remain applicable. A digest identifies bytes. It does not by itself establish semantic correctness, measurement accuracy, rights, reviewer independence or permission to act. Acceptance of an ancestor does not accept an arbitrary descendant.

\subsection{Two kinds of progress}
For a resource allowance \(b\), define the set of \emph{witnessed feasible missions}
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{F}(K,b)=
\left\{m:\ \exists\,\pi\in\Pi(K,m),\
\operatorname{valid}(\pi,m)\ \land\ C(\pi,m)\leq b\right\}, \label{eq:feasible}
\end{equation}
where \(\Pi\) contains the supplied, evaluated paths. This set is intentionally relative to tested paths; the implementation does not prove that every possible construction has been searched.

Two distinct changes can matter:
\begin{align}
\Delta\mathcal{F}&=\mathcal{F}(K',b)\setminus\mathcal{F}(K,b), \label{eq:breadth}\\
\Delta C(m)&=C_{\mathrm{old}}(m)-C_{\mathrm{new}}(m). \label{eq:efficiency}
\end{align}
The first measures newly supported work within the evaluated inventory and allowance. The second measures efficiency on common work. The changed-kernel experiment expands the retained executable inventory; it does not by itself prove strict expansion of \(\mathcal{F}\), because direct construction remains available. Inherited qualification also loses on full cost. Neither quantity establishes demand or a monetary surplus.

Retaining old assets does not guarantee that operational feasibility grows monotonically. Review policy, validity conditions, budgets, available operators and physical resources can change. An older artifact can remain historically authentic while becoming inapplicable to a new mission. The runtime preserves that distinction by separating completed-history recovery from unfinished-work continuation.

\subsection{Conditional amortization}
Let \(B\) denote complete construction work, \(V\) current admission work and \(W(R)\) checked execution for \(R\) rounds. If identical assets remain applicable and both paths face the same checked workloads, then
\begin{align}
C_{\mathrm{retain}}(R)&=B+V+W(R), \label{eq:retain}\\
C_{\mathrm{rebuild}}(R)&=R(B+V)+W(R). \label{eq:rebuild}
\end{align}
\begin{proposition}[Conditional reuse advantage]\label{prop:reuse}
Under the assumptions of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:retain}--\eqref{eq:rebuild}, retention avoids \((R-1)(B+V)\) counted work for \(R\geq1\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Subtract Eq.~\eqref{eq:retain} from Eq.~\eqref{eq:rebuild}. The shared execution term cancels.
\end{proof}
This elementary accounting identity is useful because its assumptions can be tested. A changed dependency, new admission burden, storage cost or more expensive execution changes the comparison. If retention adds burden \(H(R)\), the difference becomes \((R-1)(B+V)-H(R)\); reuse helps only when that quantity is positive. Neither identity proves that generated assets beat an expert alternative.

\subsection{Cost is a vector before it is a price}
The relevant cost record includes arithmetic work, processing time, human attention, energy, money by currency, maintenance and failures. These quantities cannot be added without a justified conversion model. In this experiment, only a declared arithmetic projection is comprehensively charged:
\[
\mathbf{c}=(c_{\mathrm{arith}},t_{\mathrm{human}},e_{\mathrm{energy}},
c_{\mathrm{cash}},c_{\mathrm{maintenance}},\ldots).
\]
Unmeasured fields are recorded as unknown, not zero. A monetary return would require actual beneficiaries, accepted deliveries, prices and measured resource costs. Repeated program execution and token settlement are neither willingness to pay nor profit.

\subsection{Why the three directions are different}
We compared three promising directions. A \emph{self-checking experimental interface} changes what an instrument observes and can therefore address the concrete Foundry mismatch. A \emph{discoverability compiler} constructs experiments that distinguish hypotheses; it is a separately owned research implementation. A \emph{shared productive-capability foundry} organizes qualified assets and capacity across voluntarily supported investigations. The third needs evidence of demand and operating capacity in addition to sound software.

We select the first for the thermal flagship and pair it with an executed capability-inheritance experiment. This pairing studies complementary mechanisms without pretending that the thermal instrument is already an accepted mathematical asset. The long-term foundry is a development direction, not an observed market.

\section{Experiment I: a software enterprise that retains its means}\label{sec:mathmethods}
\subsection{Task, grammar and checkers}
The implementation uses the existing finite exact-rational construction grammar and separate checker at dimension \(n=8\). The initial inventory contains identity-1, Walsh-2, Walsh-4, Walsh-8, XOR-convolution-8 and an inverse for kernel \((9,1,1,1,1,1,1,1)\). A transform, a convolution and an inverse request form one round.

The evaluation compares retained assets, rebuilding every round, and a supplied static specialist on identical public inputs at \(R\in\{1,4,16\}\). Component workloads use the retained LCG protocol and seed \(20261022\); the separate controller journey uses \(20261021\). We do not describe this public finite case set as a blind discovery benchmark. The controller freezes the assets before its later task generation, which prevents those task values from silently changing the accepted construction.

The checker uses exact rational calculations, so equality is not a tolerance-adjusted numerical success. Exactness within these authored interfaces does not make the grammar complete or the specification adequate for an unrelated task. The system is not allowed to modify the objective or its checker during this evaluation.

\subsection{Prospective protocol and historical correction}
The native criteria were frozen at 23:40:39.270 UTC on 22 October 2026, after earlier post-implementation component observations. The initial native run began at 23:43:29.649 UTC. A later source/data inspection found a provenance-label defect: specialist execution had been attributed to a generated artifact digest. A recorded amendment at 23:46:02.115740 UTC corrected the attribution to the actual specialist source, with artifact digest null. The corrected native run began at 23:46:02.359 UTC.

The objective, dispatch, criteria and work definition were unchanged. Initial source, protocol and results remain retained. Different invocation and mission identifiers yield different workspace hashes, so we verify each history rather than demanding identity across distinct executions. The separately performed audit checked the 53-source closure, raw receipt hashes and accounting; it did not constitute an institutional or owner approval \cite{compoundingdata}.

\subsection{Measurement scope}
The metric \metric\ charges applicable search, construction, current admission, productive arithmetic, reference arithmetic and comparisons. It does not count CPU instructions, bit complexity, hashing, framing, serialization or all development effort. Timing fields are observational and are not the decisive cost metric.

A complete build costs \(B=28{,}398\); current admission costs \(V=28{,}398\). Checked execution of the generated three-task round costs 400, so \(W(R)=400R\) in the measured path. The expert round costs 360. Its exposed arithmetic preparation is zero; the human authorship, proof and institutional qualification of that supplied implementation remain unknown.

The controller's scoped allowances are 2,000,000 construction units, 50,000,000 admission units and 2,000,000 per task. These are work allowances, not cash. The configured execution is synthetic-local, with empty authority and obligations, currency none, collected cash zero and external submission absent.

\section{Results II: constructing a measurement that can witness the error}\label{sec:thermal}
\subsection{The inherited limitation}
The preserved Foundry case is a counterexample to treating model--measurement agreement as measurement accuracy \cite{boucher2026foundry}. Its predicted receiver heat is 5,175.864695 J. A command-based calorimeter reports 5,445.281559 J, while the simulated heat integral is 5,009.624458 J. The runtime residual is
\begin{equation}
r=\frac{|Q_{\mathrm{command}}-Q_{\mathrm{prediction}}|}
{|Q_{\mathrm{command}}|}=4.9477\%,
\end{equation}
which passes a 5\% agreement rule. Yet the calorimeter's error relative to the numerical heat integral is
\begin{equation}
\epsilon_{\mathrm{ref}}=
\frac{|Q_{\mathrm{command}}-Q_{\mathrm{reference}}|}
{|Q_{\mathrm{reference}}|}=8.6964\%.
\end{equation}
The measured quantity and the prediction can share a misleading premise. Here a flow command is not the actual delivered flow. The relevant intervention is a different observation channel and a complete heat-accounting interface.

\subsection{A conditional volume-witness construction}
The adapter takes witnessed volume and the mean temperature difference weighted by that volume over each interval. The target is receiver-fluid heat:
\begin{equation}
Q=\int \rho c_p q(t)\,[T_{\mathrm{out}}(t)-T_{\mathrm{in}}(t)]\,dt
 =\sum_j \rho_j c_{p,j} V_j\overline{\Delta T}_{V,j}.
\label{eq:heat}
\end{equation}
The second equality assumes constant fluid properties within each interval. For varying properties, or a temperature statistic that does not cover the whole volume window, this representation needs additional information. A point temperature and an unrelated volume total are insufficient.

Each input has a declared interval. The implementation evaluates the 16 endpoint combinations of density, specific heat, volume and signed temperature difference, then sums interval extrema with outward integer rounding. In its fixed integer representation,
\begin{equation}
Q_{j,\mu\mathrm{J}}=
\frac{\rho_{j,\mathrm{kg/m^3}}\ c_{p,j,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}}\
 V_{j,\mathrm{nL}}\ \overline{\Delta T}_{j,\mu\mathrm{K}}}{10^{12}}.
\label{eq:microheat}
\end{equation}
Arbitrary-precision integers avoid floating-point inward rounding in this production calculation. The result is a point estimate \(E\), an interval \([L,U]\) and a conditional relative-error bound.

\begin{proposition}[Conditional heat enclosure]\label{prop:enclosure}
Suppose each interval covers its actual whole-window inputs, the property assumptions of Eq.~\eqref{eq:heat} hold, and all measurement windows are included. Then the constructed sum contains the target \(Q\). If \(0\notin[L,U]\), a valid bound for the reported point estimate is
\begin{equation}
\frac{|E-Q|}{|Q|}
\leq
\frac{\max\{|E-L|,\ |E-U|\}}
{\min_{q\in[L,U]}|q|}.
\label{eq:bound}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The heat product is multi-affine in its four bounded inputs, so its extrema on a rectangular domain occur at vertices. Summing outward enclosures preserves containment. The numerator in Eq.~\eqref{eq:bound} bounds absolute error over the interval; the denominator is a positive lower bound on the target's magnitude.
\end{proof}
The adapter rounds Eq.~\eqref{eq:bound} upward to integer parts per million. An interval containing zero cannot supply this finite relative bound. Missing interval coverage, unsupported variable-property semantics and unsuitable denominators remain refusals. A small numerical residual cannot override them.

This is deterministic interval reasoning under declared premises. It is not a standard uncertainty, a 95\% confidence interval or an independently certified measurement procedure. The GUM and NIST uncertainty guidance provide the broader measurement framework against which a physical implementation must be evaluated \cite{jcgm2008gum,taylor1994uncertainty}. A content hash can preserve a calibration record; it cannot create calibration.

\subsection{Numerical experiment and results}
A separately implemented evaluator integrates four thermal states and a fifth accumulated-heat state using fixed-step RK4 at \(h=1/32\) s. The receiver reference is the integrated heat state, not a value supplied to the worker. A 180 s reset precedes the 720 s mission: 28,800 steps and 115,200 right-hand-side evaluations. Both inlet temperatures are 20~\(^{\circ}\)C; density and specific heat are fixed synthetic constants, 998 kg/m\(^3\) and 4,180 J/(kg\,K). The mismatch multiplies the two commanded flows by 1.08 and 0.92 and heater power by 1.05. Appendix~\ref{app:thermal} records the schedule and the measurement premises.

The simulated volume channel has a \(+0.3\%\) bias inside a declared 1\% band. The differential-temperature channel has a \(+0.002\) K bias inside a declared \(\pm0.005\) K calibration band. A supplied curvature bound, trapezoidal-integration allowance and upward quantization increase the reported temperature allowance to 7,606 \(\mu\)K. These are simulation premises, not observed sensor certificates.

\section{Economic usefulness and the missing bill}\label{sec:economics}
\subsection{An asset must change a beneficiary's alternatives}
The mathematical inventory changes what this software can execute from retained programs. The thermal construction changes what its declared observations can support. These are mechanisms for productive value, not observations of revenue. No paying customer, market-clearing price, actual willingness to participate or monetary surplus is measured here.

A credible economic test compares accepted useful output against a matched alternative and counts the resources needed to deliver it. Let \(y\) be the same accepted service, \(R(y)\) realized revenue and \(C_{\mathrm{full}}(y)\) the measured cost of construction, qualification, operation, failed attempts, oversight, maintenance and capital use. Then
\begin{equation}
\Pi(y)=R(y)-C_{\mathrm{full}}(y)
\label{eq:profit}
\end{equation}
is a proposed accounting definition, not an estimated profit in this study. Avoided cost requires an observed counterfactual: the relevant expert or conventional provider must deliver equivalent quality under equivalent conditions.

Our strongest observed relative result is lower declared work than rebuilding the same assets repeatedly. The expert workload loss, the successor qualification reversal and the conventional thermal tie remain economically relevant. An arrangement that makes a new job possible may still be inferior to buying an existing service.

\subsection{Oversight and scaling must be measured}
The study supplied objectives, program grammar, reference implementations, checkers, protocol criteria, authority limits and the thermal model. The automated facade probe recorded local operator-API reviews; owners retain institutional admission and merge decisions. These actions are not evidence of independent human review. Automated execution reduces no measured number of oversight hours in this evidence package because active human time was not recorded.

A proposed deployment should record at least beneficiary acceptance, operator and independent-reviewer minutes, rejected work, requalification, resource occupancy, failure recovery and fully attributed cost. The same budget cannot be spent independently by multiple descendants. Shared reviewers and instruments are finite resources.

For one class of accepted jobs, a simple planning upper bound is
\begin{equation}
\lambda_{\mathrm{accepted}}\leq
\min\!\left(
\lambda_{\mathrm{demand}},
\frac{N_e}{\tau_e},
\frac{N_v}{\tau_v},
\frac{N_h}{\tau_h},
\frac{\dot B}{c_{\mathrm{job}}}
\right),
\label{eq:capacity}
\end{equation}
where execution, review and hardware each have \(N\) available units and mean occupancy \(\tau\); \(\dot B\) is the spending allowance per time. All terms must use the same job definition. Rework and failures increase effective occupancy and cost. This is a planning relation under stated assumptions, not a fitted capacity model.

The workload experiment increases repeated numerical jobs through 48 tasks. It does not test simultaneous tenants, queueing, reviewer scarcity, demand saturation, hardware utilization or changing prices. The changed-kernel test shows a validity boundary and a cost reversal; it does not establish robustness to general distribution shift.

\claim{The next economic claim must be earned}{A voluntarily supported mission should deliver an accepted service, retain a reusable capability, and then deliver a distinct successor service. Its report must show the beneficiary's alternative, all observed costs, the human work and whether reuse survives changed conditions. Unknown prices or hours remain unknown.}

The first deployable software step is legitimate thermal-family integration into the existing Studio and Ascension path. The next scientific construction is a calibrated liquid-loop bench. Thermal storage and environmental losses must be identified for the chosen useful objective. \section{Limits, failure modes and scientific interpretation}\label{sec:limits}
\textbf{Finite and supplied search.} The program grammar, workload and expert are designed by the study. Basis checking supports exact claims within that restricted grammar, not arbitrary software verification. Reuse is evaluated against repeated construction and a supplied expert, not all possible implementations.

\textbf{Post-implementation protocol.} Earlier component observations informed the frozen mathematical native evaluation. The thermal native evaluation repeats public cases. Neither experiment is a blind prospective discovery study. No population-level generalization or statistical confidence interval is claimed.

\textbf{Metric incompleteness.} The ledger is complete within its declared arithmetic projection and identified invocation scope. It is not complete economic cost. Unknown human effort, money, energy and development overhead prevent a profitability or labor-saving conclusion.

\textbf{Reference dependence.} Separate code for the thermal reference helps expose software mistakes but integrates the same declared physical model. It is neither an independent physical experiment nor a certified numerical enclosure of the differential equation. The deterministic measurement enclosure is conditional on its premises.

\textbf{Authority and independence.} Different fixture addresses and authenticated local reviews do not establish organizational independence. Physical acceptance, governance admission and owner-reserved decisions remain separate. No autonomous worker is granted governor privileges by this study.

\textbf{Incomplete composition.} The mathematical controller, thermal native worker and local-contract lifecycle are distinct exercised paths. Their evidence cannot be concatenated into a nonexistent complete thermal enterprise journey. The actual Studio refusal and unresolved review restriction remain part of the result.

\textbf{No open-ended growth proof.} Seven retained artifacts, three simulated measurement families and finite workloads do not prove general intelligence, unlimited improvement, universal autonomy or civilization-scale outcomes. The contribution is a mechanism that can be challenged, measured and extended with explicit next tests.

\section{Conclusion}
The productive unit is more than the completed job. It can be the checked means that survives the job: an executable method, an instrument interface or a validated process with an applicability domain and a cost history. The reported software demonstrates this inheritance, a distinct changed-requirement successor and useful negative results. It also constructs a conditional measurement interface that addresses the preserved numerical thermal failure.

The evidence supports neither automatic economic advantage nor completed physical autonomy. The expert wins, inherited qualification can lose, and the thermal enterprise handoff remains blocked. Those boundaries make the next construction concrete: complete the legitimate integration, reduce measured verification overhead without weakening it, and qualify one physical witness for one useful service.

\claim{Achievements that become capabilities}{The credible path to broader usefulness is a sequence of accepted constructions. Each should leave its successor better equipped, while carrying forward the evidence, costs and limits that make that inheritance trustworthy.}
