\section{The enterprise builds the means to ask better questions}
A small organization may have a valuable thermal question and no economical way to ask it. A cooling team needs a reliable temperature response for a proposed device. A heat-recovery team needs to know whether the receiving circuit obtains heat at a useful temperature. A research group later needs a time-resolved calorimeter for programmable heat pulses. Each can commission an isolated study; each also needs fixtures, calibrated sensors, controlled boundary conditions, a repeatable starting state and evidence that the answer survives reuse.

The foundry asks a different production question: \emph{can we construct an instrument that makes an expanding class of investigations possible?} Its product is an enabling experimental interface. Replaceable thermal cartridges share known mechanical and fluid ports, two hydraulically isolated water loops, independent control channels, an attachable calibration reference and a finite conditioning procedure. A qualified response model makes later protocols executable. The initial instrument does not predetermine a catalogue of successful businesses: requirements, physical scope, individual budgets and accepted terms screen candidate opportunities.

The central transformation preserves the useful objective. The temperature and accuracy requirements do not become easier when a candidate fails. A request at $65^{\circ}\mathrm C$ is refused, retained in the record and excluded from the success count; it is not silently renamed a low-temperature success. Similarly, a more capable calibration procedure receives no additional spending authority. This intent-preserving discipline follows the distinction between a purpose charter and a changeable formulation in Transformational Intelligence, \S\S2.1--2.3 \cite{transformational}.

\begin{claimbox}
\textbf{What exists now.} Downloadable Python source, numerical experiments, generated construction manifests, raw evidence, separate checking code, an offline five-step experience, a compiled manuscript, and a local demonstration of the candidate harness's maintained report adapter.\\[3pt]
\textbf{What has not been demonstrated.} A physical thermal-fluid instrument, installation in a customer laboratory, certified calibration, warranties, paid customers, an external validator council, deployed thermal execution, or any transaction. The concrete proposed launch is a reproducible instrument-design and qualified-software service; real-world commissioning is a subsequent experiment.
\end{claimbox}

AGI ALPHA organizes agents, tools, memory, bounded jobs and validation. TRANSFORMATIONAL INTELLIGENCE asks which restrictive conditions can change while the beneficiary's objective stays fixed. The six Out mechanisms construct specific observation, representation, resource, policy, composition and successor operations. This changes the object retained by the harness. A result record says that one temperature trace passed. A capability record retains the command interface, fitted dynamics, calibration method, reset conditions, dependency identities, checker, scope and cumulative costs needed to obtain future results. In this companion, a new request is checked against those retained conditions before continuation; missing evidence or unsupported inputs invalidate reuse. The finite constructor and hand-written calorimeter composition remain bounded supplied methods. Their reusable consequence, rather than an open-ended invention claim, is the contribution.

Second, does the frozen interface support a genuinely different downstream measurement requirement? \section{The constructed foundry and its digital twin}
\label{sec:world}
\subsection{Physical interface and conserved quantities}
The twin contains two isolated through-flow thermal circuits, a shared solid cartridge and a slow thermal reservoir representing housing memory. A thermostat supplies each inlet; reservoir regulation is an external boundary service, not a free closed-system energy source. The controlled flows are $q_1,q_2$, inlet temperatures $T_{i1},T_{i2}$ and electrical heater power $P$. The observable channels are inlet/outlet temperatures, wall temperature, flow and pressure; the latent reservoir temperature is withheld from the constructor's fitting input.

Let $T_1,T_2$ be outlet lump temperatures, $T_w$ the cartridge wall and $T_h$ the memory state. With $a_j=\rho c_pq_j$ and $K_j=G_j(q_j/q_*)^{0.7}$,
\begin{align}
C_f\dot T_j &= a_j(T_{ij}-T_j)+K_j(T_w-T_j)+P_{\mathrm{pump},j},\quad j=1,2,\\
C_w\dot T_w &=P-\sum_jK_j(T_w-T_j)-U_a(T_w-T_a)\notag\\
&\quad-K_h(T_w-T_h)+P_{cw},\\
C_h\dot T_h &=K_h(T_w-T_h)+P_{ch}.
\label{eq:thermal}
\end{align}
Controls use L/min, W, s and $^{\circ}$C; $q_j$ converts to $\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}$ in the balances. Temperature differences are K. Commanded flow, pressure/flow readout and heater input are treated as exact; only temperature readout error and electronic calibration drift are stochastic. \subsection{Hydraulics and the limits of a convenient reduction}

\begin{equation}

Pump dissipation $\Delta p_jq_j$ enters the fluid energy equation. The viscosity model is
\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
Reynolds number uses a $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ bore. This phenomenological viscosity law and fractional flow exponent are synthetic constitutive assumptions within the stated range, not a universal fluid correlation.

\subsection{Sampling, polarity, weights and observed gain}
Each measurement first captures the differential signal into a modeled sample-and-hold. Electronic input reversal changes its sign without reversing a fluid process. The frozen signal assumption is necessary: the thermal state can change while the held electrical sample is read. A physical implementation must establish hold droop, settling, sensor linearity and reference traceability. Here hold droop at most $0.001$ K is an explicit unverified interface assumption.

For one block,
\begin{equation}
y_i=g\sigma_i\theta+b_0+b_1t_i+b_2t_i^2+\epsilon_i.
\label{eq:calibration}
\end{equation}
The simulator draws noise with standard deviation $0.002$ K. Qualification uses a finite Cartesian set of positive gains $0.96,1.04$, offsets $-0.3,0.4$ K, slopes $\pm0.09$ K/s, quadratic coefficients $\pm0.002$ K/s$^2$, and known differences 2,5,11 K. These electronic-nuisance stresses are supplied scenarios. Gaussian noise is not bounded, so finite maximum errors are not universal confidence bounds.

A program constructs $\bm t$, $\bm\sigma$ and $\bm w$ subject to
\begin{equation}
\bm w^\top\bm\sigma=1,\qquad
\bm w^\top\bm t^{\,k}=0\quad(k=0,\ldots,d).
\end{equation}
An observed 8 K reference estimates $\widehat g=\bm w^\top\bm y_{\mathrm{ref}}/8$, and $\widehat\theta=\bm w^\top\bm y/\widehat g$. References are per channel and block; unrelated sensors inherit no gain. Readings outside the admissible gain interval are rejected. For affine drift, ABBA uses times $(0,1,2,3)$ s, polarities $(+,-,-,+)$ and weights $(1,-1,-1,1)/4$. The initial qualifier accepts maximum observed error at most $0.025$ K.

This is an adaptation of Out-Learn's observation-program and unknown-gain mechanisms \cite[Theorems3.1/4.1 and \S5]{outlearn}, with conventional reversal/drift cancellation explicitly credited \cite{evans1996,nistdrift}. The same policy executes across the nuisance worlds; hidden gain or drift never selects a different program. That uniform policy implements the separate-or-neutralize discipline of Out-Strategise within the supplied nuisance class \cite[p.4]{outstrategise}.

\Delta Q_j=\frac{\rho c_p q_j(t_b-t_a)}{2}

\subsection{Exact allocation, burning and refunds}
All amounts use token base units, with 18 decimals. For settled price $p$, each approving validator's gross share is
\begin{equation}
v=\lfloor p/40\rfloor,\qquad w=p-2v.
\end{equation}
For each gross allocation $g\in\{w,v,v\}$, burning is $\lfloor g/100\rfloor$ and the recipient obtains $g-\lfloor g/100\rfloor$. This is nominally 95/5 allocation and one-percent \emph{payout} burning, with exact integer floors. It is not burning on every transfer. Unused bounty $B-p$ is a separate refund and is not a validator fee or a return on investment.

For the retained synthetic example, $p=100000000000000000103$ and $B=120000000000000000123$. Each validator gross is $2500000000000000002$; worker gross is $95000000000000000099$; total burning is $1000000000000000000$; unused bounty refund is $20000000000000000020$. These are arithmetic/model outputs only. No token appreciation, passive return or fiat exchange rate is assumed. The Manager USDC route remains separate from this AGIALPHA path.

\section{Limits and a practical deployment path}
\label{sec:limits}
The digital twin is deliberately small. It assumes a single-phase fluid, known flow exponent, lumped thermal states, externally regulated inlets, fixed heat capacities, ideal control-setpoint tracking and a specified calibration/noise family. It excludes leakage, fouling, cavitation, boiling, distributed transport delay, changing contact geometry, environmental disturbances beyond those represented and common-cause reference corruption. Conservation consistency is necessary but cannot validate those constitutive choices. The production plant, fitter, predictor and specialist share one forward implementation. The independently implemented high-order reference reduces that implementation coupling for numerical checks, while the declared discrepancy cases expose selected departures. All still depend on synthetic constitutive assumptions; physical validation is a distinct task.

The search does not autonomously invent its grammar or perform unrestricted scientific discovery. The held-out requirement is unknown to the constructor until freeze, but the evaluator knows the available primitives and model class. The three realizations share one plant parameter instance; the later module-commissioning experiment changes the plant separately. No large-population inference follows from either. Scope hashes and local signatures bind records, not truth or legal ownership. The separately written checks share this project and its assumptions. The fresh challenge was selected and committed after the parent capability freeze and before its new outcomes. Local timestamps and agent separation document chronology; they do not prove external blinding.

Economic viability is conditional on hypothetical prices, labor, useful heat thresholds, budgets, reservations and utilization. No customer interview, invoice, supplier quotation, tax analysis, certification or purchase commitment occurred. Conventional shared laboratories may already be the best implementation. Any physical offer needs actual pricing, validated calibration, maintenance capacity, terms allocating responsibility and evidence of willingness to commission. Funding and token transfers cannot supply missing demand or scientific evidence.

The next useful deployment unit is small: deliver one reproducible design/qualification study for a specified beneficiary question, with its command envelope, candidate bill, uncertainty assumptions, response and checker. Free local work needs no fundraising. A bench pilot then measures one two-loop module: calibrated inlet/outlet and wall temperatures, independently measured flow and electrical power, pressure, reset carryover, isolation and repeated pulse response. Obtain component quotations and participant acceptance before any physical commissioning commitment. Compare measured fluxes and stored energy with a predeclared uncertainty budget; renew the scope when a criterion fails.

Only after that pilot should the fixed thermal worker be reviewed and installed in the existing harness, followed by a bounded commissioning arrangement and qualified remote execution. A second bench or venture family repeats the relevant qualification; it does not inherit unlimited capacity by copying software. The declared serial schedule already fails beyond four beneficiaries. Broader adoption must price model diversity, calibration renewal, shared-fixture contention, failed launches and marginal demand. Certification, warranties, customer contracts and live-chain readiness are future evidence-bearing milestones.
