\sect{Abstract}
We investigate whether the existing AGI ALPHA system exposes a recurring burden that justifies constructing a retained productive asset. We first audit the complete preceding \emph{The Advantage That Funds the Next} companion and preserve its adverse result. We then profile the actual, frozen source-assurance and catalogue/native-continuity workflows, keeping their existing specialists, verification, caches, artifacts and limits intact. Five real committed source scopes and four public source-inventory cohorts produce nine distinct internal discovery requests, each evaluated twice. The repetitions, files, work drafts and assertions are not additional jobs or customers.

All ten source-assurance deliveries and their reconnects verify successfully. Three catalogue cohorts complete native delivery and history reopening in both repetitions. A fourth, valid 1,216-row catalogue request produces all nine local artifacts but is refused by the native 200,000-code-unit artifact policy. The largest successful native catalogue path takes a median 16.857 CPU seconds; the largest real source-assurance scope takes 3.504 CPU seconds. These are local resource measurements, not monetary costs or human time.

No screened opportunity establishes a positive lower bound on incremental value at a credible reuse horizon after complete construction and operating costs. Authoring inference, integration, maintenance, customer attention and demand remain unpriced or unmeasured. The canonical cost and shadow-ledger contracts propagate those unknowns. We therefore select no productive investment, start no confirmation trial and claim no repayment. The contribution is a reproducible measurement and admission record, with useful actual reports and a precise native delivery limit, rather than another assumed-benefit financing example.

\begin{finding}
\textbf{Decision: discovery \hold.} This is not a demonstrated economic improvement. It does establish where the current supported paths spend resources, what they deliver, why the larger native output is refused, and which missing observations prevent a defensible investment decision.
\end{finding}

\sect{1. Separate the claims before measuring}
A useful service, a worthwhile retained asset, a distinctive improvement method and sustained acceleration are separate hypotheses. A conventional integration can be a good investment without being a new learning algorithm. Conversely, an attractive human-work comparison does not prove that constructing another component improves an already automated incumbent.

The present decision concerns the next asset. We require an observed burden or newly enabled useful outcome, a credible recurrence basis, comparable accepted quality, and complete attributable cost bounds. We do not infer savings from a smaller internal counter, an elapsed builder interval, a nominal price, a growing archive or a scheduled second run. The available local implementations are the experimental subjects; production authorization and commercial acceptance are separate.

\sect{9. Keep the human-work opportunity visible}
The absence of an admitted improvement does not settle whether these services can be commercially useful. A buyer may value an accurate report, portable artifacts, clear exceptions and verifiable history. That value requires a comparison with competent traditional work using ordinary Git, spreadsheets and scripts, not an artificially slow cell-by-cell operator.

No equivalent competent-human duration was observed for these exact requests. Current primary BLS sources provide compensation context: June 2026 private-industry total compensation is \$73.69 per hour worked for professional and related occupations and \$36.73 for office and administrative support. The May 2025 median QA/tester annual wage is \$104,300; that wage-only statistic is not a loaded hourly quotation. None measures these task durations or a contractor's selling price.\cite{bls,ecec}

For accepted quality held equal, let \(C_A\) denote the complete Agent cost and \(w_H\) the appropriate human hourly cost. The provider's duration threshold is
\[
t_H^{*}=\frac{60C_A}{w_H}\quad\text{minutes}.
\]
For the buyer, substitute \(P+C_{\mathrm{customer\ attention}}+C_{\mathrm{switching}}\) for \(C_A\). Each dollar of total buyer cost requires more than approximately 0.814 minutes of equivalent professional work or 1.634 minutes of equivalent office/support work at those broad rates. These are conversion thresholds, not measured savings. Actual Agent cost, price, human duration, remaining customer effort and acceptance rates remain unknown.

The task-to-occupation match must be justified before choosing a rate. QA/source-assurance work is closer to professional technical work than generic office work, while neither broad category is an exact quotation for this offering. Low utilization, maintenance shocks and failed deliveries increase fully loaded cost; they cannot be neutralized by a price assumption. The delivered record therefore offers thresholds and unknowns rather than another favorable scenario sweep.

\sect{10. What must change before construction can be selected}
The smallest missing evidence is a \emph{recurring useful request with a material burden}, not another title or adapter chosen for easy testing. At least one screened direction needs the following through the existing owner and profile:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Validation reuse:} an actual repeated task population, recorded full-path cost and quality, an immutable-cache or other conventional alternative, and a bounded integration/upkeep plan that preserves exact checking.
\item \textbf{Larger native outputs:} a demonstrated need for the native result beyond the already available local catalogue, with lossless whole-request acceptance under the current per-artifact and transport limits. Partitioning must preserve cross-key and duplicate semantics, rather than simply splitting rows.
\item \textbf{Richer repository or semantic work:} a legitimate recurring failure population and measured whole-workflow baseline. The existing Repo-Healer already implements bounded triage, patch safety and validation and must remain an incumbent alternative. It was source-inspected, not exercised in this screen; its dry-run/report-only modes are not evidence of verified repaired deliveries, but neither are they shown unavailable or unauthorized. The separate canonical coding route executes bounded \code{solve(*args)} tasks. Extending that route to complete repository work needs the existing owners' reviewed source, patch, dependency, test and delivery integration. Its mandatory container boundary has no host fallback. Provider/attempt hooks must also capture returned usage, failed calls and bill mapping.\cite{boundaries}
\end{enumerate}
These are reopening conditions, not authorizations to bypass the remaining Security, operator, Harness or Fleet decisions. Fresh untouched confirmation would be reserved only after an opportunity passes, then both builders would be instrumented before construction. A second cycle requires a first qualified asset and its actual measured benefit; none exists from this screen.
