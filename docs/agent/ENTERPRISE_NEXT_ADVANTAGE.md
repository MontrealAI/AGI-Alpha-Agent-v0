## What was implemented

`flywheel-offering/v1` records a beneficiary, useful outcome, tangible deliverable,
scope, exact acceptance criteria, internal need evidence, demand uncertainty,
matched human-service reference, required capabilities, permitted tools, candidate
prices, capacity, turnaround, complete-cost ceiling, risks and limits. Consecutive
revisions reset qualification. They cannot inherit a previous version's success.

The separate stages are hypothesis, executed prototype, technical qualification,
commercial assessment and authorized offering. A completed model response cannot
advance an offering. Prototype qualification requires its own actual enterprise
launch and exact terminal executor record. Technical qualification uses the
existing independently signed, rights-bound general acceptance and complete A
accounting. Its current implemented adapter remains finite service placement.
Commercial forecasts remain signed assumptions. Their evidence is checked again
when considering and dispatching production, including after authorization. A negative assessment stays
recorded and cannot authorize production. Even a positive fictional assessment
does not establish a live customer or grant permission to make external commitments.

The bounded decision policy considers production, assessment, Gym, specialist
invocation, retention, sharing, prototyping, holding, deferring and stopping. It
maximizes conservative forecast benefit minus the cash-cost cap and separately
declared opportunity cost over the supplied feasible alternatives. Ties choose
lower cash cost, then a stable identifier. This is a transparent finite policy,
not a universal optimum or calibrated demand predictor. Alternative prices can
compete in the same menu; no discount or reinvestment fraction is fixed.

Current journal evidence, source/configuration, current capability eligibility,
reserves, unknown liabilities, occupied capacity, time and monetary bounds feed
each decision. Offering reservations are scoped to their own offering while the
global slot ceiling remains separate. Legacy unscoped reservations stay
conservative until reconciliation. Unknown costs block additional funded work. The decision is advice
until a separate signed exact permit authorizes the existing executor. Production
and prototype missions must preserve the offering's beneficiary, purpose,
deliverable, acceptance criteria, mode, tools and budgets. One decision permits
one attempt. All alternative evidence and offering need records are rechecked in the dispatch
transaction. An old decision refuses after relevant source, capability, control,
offering, capacity or ledger changes.

The native `mission-run --enterprise-decision` path executes that connection.
The other actions continue through their existing separately signed lifecycle
commands; this contribution does not autonomously dispatch Gym, admission or
protected invocation from a recommendation. Capability-dependent production
composition remains explicitly unqualified; declaring a requirement does not
load or invoke it. Protected invocation still requires live ENS/wallet boundary
checks in a real deployment, independently of mission permission.

## Failure recovery and release boundary

Attempts reserve capacity before I/O and consume their decision permanently.
Failed/interrupted executions retain uncertain liabilities and capacity. No
automatic retry or restoration of authority occurs. To release a terminal failed
attempt, first stop the enterprise, reconcile and seal all A liabilities with the
existing accounting authority, then submit a signed `reconcile` command naming
the exact launch head and current evidence. Terminal receipts must match the
reserved permit, mission and profile. A lost launch finalization can be reconciled
from that bound terminal failure; the consumed decision stays consumed.
An attempt with no executor receipt or with an in-flight receipt requires separate
operator investigation; this version deliberately cannot clear it automatically.

Directed regression tests exercise
delivery, negative/positive fictional assessment, no-learning production,
permission widening, stale evidence, roles, expiry, replay, version reset, unknown
costs and interrupted recovery. The isolated full setup also reported dependency
conflicts; a clean bootstrap is not established. Exact-source hosted review,
required CI, full release coverage and installed/public qualification remain
separate gates. Preserve the v1.46 main publication reservation; this child does
not dispatch another publisher or replace existing papers.

Live qualification requires the existing minimum approved resources: model account
with quota, liability caps, hard budget and billing; qualified sandbox/browser;
protected HTTPS/storage/key/checkpoint custody; two approved mainnet RPC readers
with checked code pins; two distinct owner-controlled eligible agent wallets;
fresh rights-cleared missions, actual competent frontier comparisons and independent
acceptance. No credentials, paid infrastructure, persistent access, financial
transactions or external sales were provisioned. General capability-dependent
composition also needs further engineering, not just credentials.
