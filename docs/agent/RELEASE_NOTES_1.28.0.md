Open the [bilingual workshop](../successor/build/index.html), name your Ω, run a computing experiment,

## What is included

- Alpha's actual fixed candidate engine compares four aggregation configurations and freezes the chosen
  program before six further public comparisons with the incumbent and deterministic baseline.
- An unchanged, source-pinned copy of the AGI Jobs compiler produces 21 bounded draft work orders with
  dependencies, roles, acceptance criteria, budget limits, expiry, rollback and unsigned commitments.
- Visitors can keep the working HTML, full JSON package, readable job briefs and a native Alpha request.
  French runs retain French in the native request. Reopened results remain historical notes until rerun.
- The companion AGI Jobs repository verifies and recompiles exported packages, rejecting modified terms,
  forged authority and live-settlement claims. Its website mirrors the workshop with a pinned checksum.
- Both recorded deployed systems prominently share Ethereum mainnet **$AGIALPHA**:
  **`0xA61a3B3a130a9c20768EEBF97E21515A6046a1fA`**. All five Alpha and 24 Jobs instances have full
  selectable addresses, explorer links, readable roles and dated source records.

## Evidence and operational scope

This is a synthetic local rehearsal. Device timings can favor either baseline. Public comparisons do not
establish independent proof, full economics, mission dominance or Specialist ASI. Work orders stay DRAFT;
admission stays HOLD; authority and transferred funds remain zero. A planning allowance is not a quote,
balance or funding instruction. Hashes establish consistency, not authenticated authorship.

The shared token does not connect the markets automatically. Alpha MARK targets its own immutable market;
the documented deployed/current-source and AGI Jobs commissioning distinctions remain explicit. No smart
contracts are deployed or changed by this release. Fresh independent evaluation, complete costs and a
separate scoped, expiring, revocable admission remain necessary before operational use.

## Acceptance and upgrade

Focused checks cover real computation, compiler consistency, forged claims, native request language,
keyboard controls, escaped input, offline download/reopen/rerun, mobile reflow and accessibility. The new
workshop is also exercised by the existing complete gallery and published-Pages acceptance workflow.
Publication requires all existing gates on the exact integrated commit; the release manifest records the
actual accepted results. Existing demonstrations, research, flowcharts and personal labs are retained.

No journal migration is introduced. Follow the existing [backup and recovery process](OPERATIONS.md).
