### OUT-EXECUTE in the existing capability workspace

The [capability workspace](../successor/capabilities/index.html) now executes bounded finite nonnegative
integer min-sum models. It constructs complete boundary profiles, independently verifies each retained
contract and its evidence, distinguishes preserved from invalidated work, and assembles and accepts a current
result. Pointwise equality of the complete profile can stop coordinator re-optimization; an equal scalar optimum
or a self-declared hash is not a proof of behavioral equivalence. Current witnesses are still reconstructed and checked.

The existing project history retains source bindings, attempts, lineage, failures, unknowns, budgets and actual
logical-work counters. Interrupted checkpoints and ordinary/unsaved recovery exports can be reopened without
automatically running work. Stale evidence, source/evaluator/schema changes, non-equivalent profiles, forged
terminal states and divergent histories are rejected or require new work. The pane shows the contract, change,
reuse/invalidation decisions, acceptance and costs without creating a separate product or history store.

The implementation guide ([repository source](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/main/examples/successor-capability/out-execute/README.md))
specifies the typed interface,
finite bounds, trust assumptions and accounting exclusions. USDC6, AGIALPHA18 and Fleet handoffs are inert,
source-bound review drafts, not submitted jobs or executable authorizations. Existing Jobs/Fleet runtimes do not
install this new adapter. Fleet qualification remains unprovisioned; no denied review is represented as cleared.
