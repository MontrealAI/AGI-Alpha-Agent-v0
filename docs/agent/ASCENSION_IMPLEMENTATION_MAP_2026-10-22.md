## Contract facts that adapters must preserve

All five Alpha suite contracts plus AscensionToken have identical source at historical deployed revision `c0fc5217e364a39a4cd123d17a8317c06b80f820` and the inspected Alpha main. This is not verification of current chain bytecode or roles.

- Business: `LABEL.alpha.agi.eth`; agent: `LABEL.alpha.agent.agi.eth`; validator: `LABEL.alpha.club.agi.eth`. Current ownership and governor admission are required. Token holding alone grants no role.
- Alpha auction selection considers bounded price, duration and bid-time reputation, then rechecks eligibility. It does not guarantee cheapest/fastest or assess delivery quality at award.
- For price `p`, each of two affirmative validators receives gross `floor(p/40)`; worker gross is `p - 2*floor(p/40)`. Each recipient burns `floor(gross/100)` separately. This is not a tax on every transfer.
- At price 100 base units: worker gross/net 96, validator gross/net 2 each, burn 0. At price 3999: worker gross 3801/net3763; validators99 each; total burn38. At price4000: worker gross3800/net3762; validators100/net99; burn40.
- MARK verifies the Merkle leaf using the actual plan index. The posted job stores `hashSpec(0,spec)`. Preserve both commitments for nonzero indices.
- Missing reviewer quorum after delivery is not proof of worker failure. Refund/collateral outcomes follow source.
- MARK's funding-phase withdrawal remains available despite revoked admission or expired risk. Bloom creates a plan-restricted campaign treasury, not legal sovereignty.
- The current-main deployment document records historical chain-1 observations and explicit unapproved activation/public funding. Fresh ABI/bytecode/proxy/dependency/role reads, dated readiness and the actual local-EVM lifecycle remain outstanding.
