## Scope and limits

The Gym develops capabilities; it does not certify Specialist ASI. A frozen candidate must
separately beat Current and the best credible alternative on agreed protected work and constraints.
Accountable authorization remains required for succession or operational authority. The local
public synthetic checks establish neither independent evaluation nor a novel algorithm, model
improvement, general intelligence or realized economic advantage. Optional value estimates use
the user's supplied assumptions and do not establish full costs or actual savings.

The deployment and scoped graph verification are recorded separately from activation,
external dependency qualification, security assurance and off-chain commissioning.
No public funding or activation is approved by this release. There is no global pause.
Existing wallet profiles and historical contract addresses are not automatically migrated.
The five October 18 records are separate from the older 29-address ecosystem snapshots;
historical archives and pinned wallet profiles retain their original identities.
External authority closeout completed at 2026-10-18 15:24:15.848 UTC, releasing both
original actor leases without signing or broadcasting transactions. The original
deployment session remains historically incomplete at sequence 20 with its journal unchanged.
Actual manual binding calls were verified post hoc; that evidence does not recreate missing
pre-sign intent. See the record for deviations.

The release source includes publication changes and therefore has a different commit
from the immutable deployed source. Use the deployment record's exact commit/tree when
reproducing deployed contracts. The compiler workaround is narrow, not a guarantee
against every compiler defect or a claim of production readiness.
