## Long-term ambition: construction at every transition

Qualified digital methods could support a physical instrument; that instrument
could make a useful material or process experiment identifiable; a reproducible
process could justify production tooling; accepted production could support a
site-specific installation and useful energy delivery. Each transition requires
new construction, resources and validation. Software copying supplies none of
the sensors, samples, tooling, materials, electricity, installation labor,
maintenance or operating capacity by itself.

Manufacturing needs yield and batch-variation evidence, inspection, supplier
capacity and accounting for rejected material. Infrastructure needs engineering,
commissioning, maintainability, availability, service and a real site demand.
Useful heat needs an actual destination at the required temperature, independent
delivery meters, pumping/control inputs, losses and degradation included. An
apparent gross energy benefit is insufficient if net inputs, outages and complete
costs remove the advantage. The detailed [development path](COMPOUNDING_CAPABILITIES.md#layer-3--proposed-physical-progression)
states the resources, validation and economic question at every step.
