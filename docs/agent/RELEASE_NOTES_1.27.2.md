## Deployment and integration scope

The shared token is an existing dependency of both suites. This publication does not deploy contracts
or alter their balances, permissions, admission settings or pause state. The recorded Alpha MARK contract
routes to its own immutable AscensionJobMarket; automatic routing to AGIJobsv0's separate JobRegistry is
not established. Identity compatibility in source requires its own configuration and validation.

Alpha's October 11 deployed-source archive predates later settlement hardening. AGIJobsv0's October 12
deployment retains its rc.4 source identity. The guide identifies the dated public records and links to
their operating boundaries; a source-verification record is not a fresh live-state observation.
