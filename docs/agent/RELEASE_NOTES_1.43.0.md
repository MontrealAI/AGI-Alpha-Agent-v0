# Alpha Agent 1.43.0 — Finite recurring source studies

The existing `alpha-agent successor-study` operator can execute a finite sequence of approved source-study
arrivals after one explicit start. Its operating envelope binds exact source, charter, inputs, operator
manifests, budgets, recurrence limits and deadline. Status and export report the selected policy, all
comparison costs and pending work. Stop is permanent for that operation; no background service is
installed by default. See [the complete operating guide](SUCCESSOR_SOURCE_OPERATION.md) and
[release installation and first run](START_HERE.md).

The opt-in `shared-closed-artifacts` profile retains one executable, validated SelectionProgram from
completed training and tests it on fresh changed inputs. Successor and conventional reuse receive the
same artifact, tools and budgets. The direct-construction control may ignore the artifact. Each later
mission retains fresh observations and checks; inherited history carries no production authority.

The recorded source-71e5963 installed campaign completed twelve main comparisons after SIGKILL and an
unchanged CLI restart, canceled an active Stop handoff, and completed the declared 12/24/30 comparison
load cases. The one-second deadline reached Hold after two comparisons. Six fresh inherited trials
selected the retained program four times and kept simpler incumbents twice. Equally enabled
conventional reuse tied it; capability growth, self-acceleration, customer demand and profit are not
established. Human engineering/operation effort and unclean-exit CPU remain unknown. Original cold
campaigns and corrective failures retain their separate source and evidence identities.

This release candidate composes that reviewed operating lineage with published 1.42 and the additive
preferred Flywheel Edition 02 PDF. Its new version avoids overwriting any published tag or asset.
Previous configured source review and recorded development installation are not final combined-source
acceptance: current-source review, PR checks, full resulting-main qualification, public package download
and installation remain required. No successful report is inferred from this release note.

The free `/try/` catalogue experience remains browser-local. It is not connected to this native CLI
operator and closing the tab stops its local computation. This edition does not add a general desktop
executor, qualified public-repository repair mission, new model-provider connection or four-product
installed tuple. The original Harness #4863/#4865 and Fleet restrictions remain unchanged. All five
held source pins, original research evidence, prior releases and historical URLs remain preserved.
USDC and AGIALPHA routes stay separate and unfunded; no transaction, contract or security-setting change
or new persistent access is included.

The dated [compatibility matrix](FLYWHEEL_COMPATIBILITY.json) and [capability matrix](FLYWHEEL_CAPABILITIES.json)
record exact source identities, schemas, operations, evidence and unavailable combinations. An absent
or unknown tuple remains unqualified. A source candidate row never establishes public installation or
operational authorization.
