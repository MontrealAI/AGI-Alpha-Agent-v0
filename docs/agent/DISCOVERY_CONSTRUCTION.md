## Discovery and purpose-preserving construction

`flywheel-opportunity/v1` records useful purpose, exact deliverable and acceptance,
input/output meanings, independently motivated needs, outside options, evidence,
limiting assumptions, materially different formulations and unresolved questions.
Ingredients disclose supplied, established, discovered or constructed provenance.
Incomplete exploratory hypotheses are allowed. A signed claim is not proof that
the system autonomously discovered a need, method or coalition.

Purpose stays identical across revisions. A different purpose or easier acceptance
requires a new identity; historical evidence remains intact. A transformation
witness pins that purpose and opportunity version, a removable restriction,
replacement, realization in the original setting and falsifying observation.
It supports representation, information, experiments, dependencies, workflow,
task boundaries and coordination. Text alone establishes no technical advantage.

Exploration bundles allow complementary steps that may be unattractive alone.
Dependencies must be ordered and acyclic; total cash/time bounds cover every step.
Checkpoints and falsification pause recording until a separately signed decision
continues or stops. Plans grant no execution or spending authority. Actual work
still uses the existing scoped mission permits and budget reservations.

Executed observations bind the frozen mission, exact artifact-bearing terminal
receipt, evidence mode, actual permit bucket, elapsed-time lower bound and original
requirements. Accepted placement outputs use the existing independent acceptance
service. Other interpretations remain explicitly attested and unqualified.
Research artifacts can be recorded without inventing execution receipts.

An actual ledger mission/bucket can be allocated once across all bundles. For an
executed observation, the mission ID comes from the exact execution receipt;
the operation ID remains the execution identity and is not a ledger alias. This
reads the existing ledger rather than charging it again. Unknown costs pause the
package. Every signed continuation rechecks the original execution identity and
current accounting, preserves old incomplete records, recomputes totals once and
resumes or stops on the resolved budget. Typed version-two allocation records
keep mission identity distinct from an operation ID with identical text. Before
any new allocation or continuation, every legacy allocation is resolved through
its original observation, bundle and exact execution receipt, then reserved in
the typed index. Both earlier untyped formats and duplicate aliases for the same
observation are supported. Old allocations and observations remain historical.
Conflicting legacy claims or inconsistent provenance require explicit correction;
processing a newer bundle first cannot steal an older claim. No ledger charge
is added. It never
replays an action or restores consumed permission. Stale heads/evidence, replay,
wrong roles, expired signatures, stopped control and changed source refuse.

The enterprise child also repairs caught executor preflight failures that leave
no execution record after reserving capacity. A signed reconciliation requires a
paused enterprise, the exact failed launch and complete A accounting for its
original mission. New launches retain that ledger identity; legacy launches
without it require the full original mission matching the frozen request digest.
An absent receipt alone cannot clear a merely attempting launch. Terminal failure
receipts retain the existing exact permit/profile/mission checks. Reconciliation
releases the reservation, records its failure kind and leaves the decision
consumed; it grants no retry authority. This correction is offered for adoption
by the existing enterprise owner rather than changing its branch or release.

Offering links preserve exact purpose, deliverable, criteria, scope and evidence
mode. They cannot qualify or authorize an offering. Discovery dispositions are
recommendations; production permits, acceptance, admission and current ENS/wallet
resource access retain separate original roles.

## Operational Alpha and arrangements

Frozen plans represent H as human-service reference, U as reasonable ordinary use,
E as expert operation and E+ as the same expert workflow plus one explicit retained
capability. U/E/E+ share model profile, access, tools, resources and purpose.
Starting capabilities, fresh-task policy and complete-cost policy are recorded.
E versus U and E+ versus E remain separate contrasts, never double-counted profit.
The included configuration is a prospective example, **not a frontier-model trial**.
Its scripted profile is literal. Ordinary-use representativeness, actual tokens,
customer effort, monetary cost and human-service market prices remain unmeasured.

Arrangements link existing offerings, capability dependencies, participants,
outside options, prices, responsibilities, commitments, rights and schedules.
Joint scenarios preserve shared technical risk, withdrawal, conditional delivery,
defaulted receivables, refunds, coordination, support and maintenance. Each A/B/C
activity has one identity; cash and opportunity costs are distinct.

Assessments are projections, including in a live context. They cannot post a
payment, change funds, admit Alpha or authorize a contract. Signed linkage checks
current offering, need, participant and dependency evidence. Revisions reassess.
Supplied financing is shown beside a separate actual ledger snapshot rather than
asserted as an available bank balance or earned revenue.

Cash timelines order debits before equal-time credits. They separate contribution,
collections, receivables, refunds, obligations, reserves, available funds, branch
liquidity and worst required prefunding. Exact rational numerators use strings to
avoid unsafe JavaScript integers. Positive expectation cannot fund a cash trough
or cure missing commitments or rights.

The *Invent the Economy* regression preserves four hypothetical buyers, each
price 300,000/value 360,000, fixed construction 600,000, conditional delivery 75,000
each and one common 80% qualification event. Success contribution is 300,000,
expected contribution 120,000 and prefunding 900,000. Failure pays construction
only, with no delivery or receipts. Amounts are supplied thousands of a common
numeraire, interpreted as USD for this fixture. It is **not a market forecast**.
Technical-only, coordination-only, three-buyer, fee, cash-shortage, partial/default,
refund and obligation controls preserve losing outcomes.

## Discovery selection and the information instrument

The starting experience is an open need or possibility. It now also retains
existing approaches, the remaining gap and why the question is worth revisiting
in the existing Alpha export. These fields survive duplicate exports, language
changes and reopening; populated fields do not establish their truth.

Serious runtime candidates can attach an `OpportunityCase`: prior approaches and
recipient availability, current gap evidence, why now, proposed construction,
decisive experiment, commercial mechanism, falsifier, missing evidence and novelty
scope. Old opportunity records remain readable without this optional case. Both
registration and subsequent witness/bundle decisions recheck case evidence on the
same Journal. No separate registry or source of authority is introduced.

The optional investigation compares three materially different possibilities:
uncertain-effect diagnosis, a scientific response instrument already owned by
#4970, and pooled resilience planning. The latter is retained as a historical
fixture because no independently supported unresolved gap was established. The
scientific lane remains with its owner. The selected internal experiment targets
a concrete local gap: the general executor preserves an uncertain attempt and
refuses replay, but does not identify what additional observation could resolve
the original effect. This is a recipient-relative integration opportunity. It is
not a claim that distributed recovery, idempotency or experiment design is new.

`flywheel_probe.py` constructs a bounded information request from explicitly
supplied effect hypotheses and read contracts. It never executes imported probe
text, reads a service, retries a tool or changes the executor's authority. With
three identical missing-ack logs it cannot classify the effect. A supplied,
operation-bound audit distinguishes the synthetic not-applied, applied and partial
states; unavailable audits remain unresolved and unexpected observations invalidate
the finite model. A direct audit lookup matches the classification. The same
content runs in a second local process on a different mission; this is not live
ENS authentication or an authenticated production peer experiment.

The exact source, dossier and conformance protocol are journaled before the new
replay. A second artifact holds its actual local results, then an observation
links them to the existing C cost ledger. Unknown actual monetary costs leave the
bundle `paused-uncertain`. A separately signed recommendation stops the commercial
claim. The instrument's classification is explicitly conditional on the declared
finite world set; neither a digest nor a resolved model state establishes real
coverage, actual acceptance or retry permission. Released cases are reproducibility
and refusal controls, not fresh blinded frontier-model observations.

Primary references challenge the scope rather than certify this application:

- [Temporal's MCP integration](https://docs.temporal.io/develop/python/integrations/mcp)
  already supports durable activity histories and requires attention to side
  effects and idempotency. Where it solves the recipient's problem, use it.
- [MCP Tasks extension design, SEP-2663](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/seps/2663-tasks-extension)
  separates read observation and cancellation signaling. Its design is not a
  guarantee about a particular deployed provider or irreversible effect.
- [UndoBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05622) already studies enterprise-agent
  recovery with effect-history/state oracles. The local instrument is not claimed
  to supersede that benchmark or its methods.

These sources were checked on 27 October 2026. Their existence does not establish
external demand for this diagnosis service. An authorized natural pilot still
needs operation-specific read access, independently justified coverage, actual
complete costs, recipient usefulness and accepted commercial terms. Production
capsule support for arbitrary information instruments remains unimplemented; the
existing closed selection and placement paths keep their original scope.
