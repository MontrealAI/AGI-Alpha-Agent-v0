## Discovery and purpose-preserving construction

`flywheel-opportunity/v1` records useful purpose, exact deliverable and acceptance,
input/output meanings, independently motivated needs, outside options, evidence,
limiting assumptions, materially different formulations and unresolved questions.
Ingredients disclose supplied, established, discovered or constructed provenance.
Incomplete exploratory hypotheses are allowed. A signed claim is not proof that
the system autonomously discovered a need, method or coalition.

Purpose stays identical across revisions. A different purpose or easier acceptance
requires a new identity; historical evidence remains intact. A transformation
witness pins that purpose and opportunity version, a removable restriction,
replacement, realization in the original setting and falsifying observation.
It supports representation, information, experiments, dependencies, workflow,
task boundaries and coordination. Text alone establishes no technical advantage.

Exploration bundles allow complementary steps that may be unattractive alone.
Dependencies must be ordered and acyclic; total cash/time bounds cover every step.
Checkpoints and falsification pause recording until a separately signed decision
continues or stops. Plans grant no execution or spending authority. Actual work
still uses the existing scoped mission permits and budget reservations.

Executed observations bind the frozen mission, exact artifact-bearing terminal
receipt, evidence mode, actual permit bucket, elapsed-time lower bound and original
requirements. Accepted placement outputs use the existing independent acceptance
service. Other interpretations remain explicitly attested and unqualified.
Research artifacts can be recorded without inventing execution receipts only when
the planned step has no frozen execution request. A step that pins a mission must
bind its actual execution receipt even for an inconclusive or falsifying observation.
Refusal leaves the bundle, command identity, cost allocation and checkpoint unchanged;
a corrected observation can then use the actual receipt without replaying execution.

The receipt-binding repair and a fresh source-bound local replay are retained in
`evidence/discovery-frozen-execution-20261027/`. The original embedded dossier and
earlier qualification packet remain historical evidence for their own source.

An operation/bucket can be allocated once across all bundles. This reads the
existing ledger rather than charging it again. Unknown costs pause the package.
A signed checkpoint rechecks reconciled reports, preserves old incomplete records,
recomputes totals once and resumes or stops on the resolved budget. It never
replays an action or restores consumed permission. Stale heads/evidence, replay,
wrong roles, expired signatures, stopped control and changed source refuse.

Offering links preserve exact purpose, deliverable, criteria, scope and evidence
mode. They cannot qualify or authorize an offering. Discovery dispositions are
recommendations; production permits, acceptance, admission and current ENS/wallet
resource access retain separate original roles.

Arrangements link existing offerings, capability dependencies, participants,
outside options, prices, responsibilities, commitments, rights and schedules.
Joint scenarios preserve shared technical risk, withdrawal, conditional delivery,
defaulted receivables, refunds, coordination, support and maintenance. Each A/B/C

Assessments are projections, including in a live context. They cannot post a
payment, change funds, admit Alpha or authorize a contract. Signed linkage checks
current offering, need, participant and dependency evidence. Revisions reassess.
Supplied financing is shown beside a separate actual ledger snapshot rather than
asserted as an available bank balance or earned revenue.

Historical `flywheel-offering/v1` records and their required human references retain
the same fields and meaning. Explicit `flywheel-offering/v2` records can instead
carry a buyer-value/willingness-to-pay hypothesis for a new outcome without a
credible human equivalent. Paired ranges may stay unknown, candidate prices may
stay empty, and evidence/uncertainty/outside options remain mandatory. Unknown
prices cannot pass commercial price selection or production authorization. The
selected workflow candidate uses this hypothesis path without fabricated team

at existing offering gates.

Cash timelines order debits before equal-time credits. They separate contribution,
collections, receivables, refunds, obligations, reserves, available funds, branch
liquidity and worst required prefunding. Exact rational numerators use strings to
avoid unsafe JavaScript integers. Positive expectation cannot fund a cash trough
or cure missing commitments or rights.
