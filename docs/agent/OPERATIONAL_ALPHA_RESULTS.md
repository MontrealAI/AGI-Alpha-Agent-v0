## The useful construction

The unmet integration need is evidence-linked attribution. An attractive prompt
or frozen comparison plan cannot establish that the planned work was executed,
that its original criteria passed, or that its complete costs were reconciled.
Unlinked score uploads fail this requirement; a second evaluation registry would
duplicate existing custody. The selected construction derives a read-only result
from the existing Journal, exact operation identities and original mission costs.

The transformation is researcher-selected integration work. No autonomous
invention, representative ordinary-user population or commercial premium is
established by the protocol regression.

## Prospective binding

`ComparisonPlan.cases` optionally freezes up to sixteen cases, each with exactly
one U, E and E+ trial. Each trial contains its complete `ExecutionMission` and
reserved observation operation ID. Freeze it through the existing signed
`comparison` action **before any of those operations has an execution record**.
Older comparison records remain readable as plans without measured results.

The three arms preserve the same original purpose, criteria, common inputs,
beneficiary, modes, rights, stopping conditions and mission limits. Inputs may
differ only in the frozen `comparison_workflow` and `comparison_retained` fields.
E+ uses E's exact workflow plus one disclosed retained input. These fields record
the declared condition; they do not prove that a retained payload was invoked.

Case input digests must be distinct. A fresh-recipient case also requires a
different declared recipient from the primary cases. These are prospective
declarations, not proof of training exclusion, organizational independence or
live ENS authentication. The original protected lifecycle remains responsible
for actual authorized sharing and technical transfer evidence.

Typed comparison-operation and comparison-mission indexes use the same Journal.
They prevent reuse as another independent sample; they grant no permit, reserve
no funds and post no charge. Failed registration rolls back all index writes.

### Model I/O evidence

A bound execution and its turn counter do not prove a model request. The executor
increments the counter before checking the context cap. Each cell therefore exposes
`model_io` separately: the declared component, a lower bound on durable request
boundaries, recorded model-response events, processed usage records and unresolved
request state. A persisted `pending=model/N` boundary without its response stays
unresolved; a positive reservation without a response is also unresolved. Missing or
inconsistent boundary data remains explicitly insufficient. The absence of a durable
boundary means only that none was recorded, not that a provider independently confirms
zero calls. `provider_call_count` stays unknown and `provider_provenance_verified`
stays false in this report.

`model_evidence` distinguishes no bound executions, bound executions without model-I/O
evidence, unresolved requests, recorded scripted responses and recorded responses with
unverified provider provenance. The `live` component is a declared transport condition;
an observed local response does not independently authenticate its provider or establish
frontier performance. Historical `actual-model-records-with-unqualified-attribution`
exports remain readable as historical bound records whose I/O is unverified. Unknown
future values render as unverified evidence, never as actual live operation. These
labels change no acceptance, cost, denominator, commercial or operating authority.

All present A/B/C charges under each original trial mission are read once.
`additional_costs` declares disjoint B/C scopes that were not already charged to
delivery. Duplicate scopes and overlaps with trial missions refuse. No second
research bill is created. Cost differences require all relevant accounting seals;
the retained cost difference additionally requires explicit B accounting, including
an attested zero where justified. Failed assessments remain costs. Decimal-string
totals preserve exact amounts beyond JavaScript's safe integer range.

Completeness remains the configured accounting signer's attestation. The result
does not infer that undeclared resources were free, treat tokens as invoices, or
turn cash, customer savings, deposits or seed capital into earned revenue. The
existing commercial and payment interfaces retain those responsibilities.

The result carries the exact plan, ledger and source identities. Its export is
a current read-only snapshot; old downloaded results remain historical and are
not rewritten when subsequent accounting is resolved.
