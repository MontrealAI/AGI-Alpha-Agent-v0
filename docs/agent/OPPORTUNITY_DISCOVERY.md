## Opportunity and falsifier

The [dossier](https://github.com/MontrealAI/AGI-Alpha-Agent-v0/blob/codex/opportunity-observations-20261027/examples/opportunity-discovery/dossier.json)
compares native idempotency, trace-to-outcome qualification, durable workflow replay
and mission-specific observation design. Broad retry/replay novelty is rejected:
Stripe and ECS provide idempotency mechanisms, Temporal documents lost
acknowledgements and recommends idempotent activities, and LangGraph already
persists workflow state. These are serious available alternatives.

The bounded pilot asks whether constructing an authorized outcome observation can
remove an information restriction: an ambiguous acknowledgement cannot distinguish
completed and uncommitted effects. The instrument searches permitted reads that
separate satisfied from unsatisfied states while preserving the exact required
artifact. It retains HOLD for indistinguishable or unexpected observations and
emits no permission to retry, mutate, accept a customer delivery or pay.

The world table, read semantics, code and candidate menu are researcher supplied.
The exact distinguishing-set enumeration is established. Only the probe subset is
constructed by the search. The generic scaffold and compiler were drafted during
investigation; the local pilot protocol was fixed before end-to-end effects. This
is not a blind study of autonomous frontier invention.

Falsifiers include incorrect bytes classified as satisfied, erased ambiguity,
inherited authority, a gain erased by complete costs, and a competent ordinary
method matching the proposed contribution. An inaccessible outcome is not made
observable by stronger reasoning alone.

## Explicit economics without fabricated customers

The existing arrangement engine evaluates 48 exact sensitivity cases plus one
fully inspectable illustration. Prices, order counts, willingness to pay, costs
and branch probabilities are **assumptions**, not calibrated demand or observed
market prices. The scripted model is a protocol fixture, not a calibrated
frontier-performance forecast. These limitations affect those claims; they do not
stop local engineering.

| Assumption | Values or branches |
| --- | --- |
| Price per hypothetical job | USD100 / USD500 |
| Potential jobs | 1 / 4 / 12 |
| Optional C construction | USD100 / USD1,000 |
| Complete A delivery and support | USD50 + USD5 per job |
| Additional B assessment | USD10 once per cohort |
| Willingness to pay per job | USD50 / USD1,000 |
| Seed cash | USD0 / USD5,000; never revenue |
| Required reserve | USD50; never an expense |
| Mutually exclusive outcomes | Acceptance 70%; payment default 10%; common technical failure 10%; withdrawal 5%; full refund 5% |

A price above the stated willingness assumption produces nonparticipation, no
construction, no delivery and no sales. Technical failure has no conditional
delivery or receipts. Default leaves a receivable and all incurred costs; refunds
reverse collections once. A/B/C activities are disjoint and appear once per branch.
All modeled obligations, timing and cash paths use the existing engine.

For the illustrative USD500 × four-job case with USD100 construction, expected
hypothetical economic contribution is USD1,103 and required prefunding is USD380.
A zero-cash operator cannot finance it. Even with seed cash, the model returns
HOLD because no participant has committed. No actual collections or authority are
posted. Conventional parity supplies no measured incremental Alpha. Actual
research costs, available funds and profit remain unknown, and actual customer
collections are zero.

There is no credible sourced price for a scope-equivalent human service in this
pilot. The sensitivity therefore exposes buyer-value hypotheses rather than
inventing human hours, wages or a market quote. No modeled gain is earned capital.
