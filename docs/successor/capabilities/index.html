    01 · DESIGN THE POSSIBILITY
    What should become possible?
    Unsaved proposal
    
            
    Use public, non-personal examples only. Complete every field, naming unknowns honestly. The title is limited to 160 characters; each other brief field to 2,000.
    
            
    
            
    The construction
    Describe the new thing itself, the useful outcome it preserves, and what would count as a real test.
    
            
    A family, not a one-off
    Explain what another person or project could reuse, and where the idea may stop working.
    
            
    Economic assumptions, not commitments
    These are hypotheses to investigate, not customers, financing, revenue, savings or adoption established by this page. Write “unknown” where there is no evidence.
    
            
    Optional: a bounded synthetic witness
    This fixed, bounded instrument is an optional test bed, not a translation or execution of your blue-sky description. Unrelated instruments, representations and architectures are not constructed here. The brief remains a proposal even when this separate witness passes.
    Only the model’s supported configuration is accepted. No code, URLs, plugins or arbitrary expressions are executed.
    
            
    Name the lesson, failed assumption or deliberate change. Earlier evidence stays attached to the version that produced it.
