What will my Ω actually do?

It tries four small programs, chooses one, then checks it on six new sets of sample events. You see whether its answers match and how long it took. These are real calculations on your device.

This is a focused computing experiment, not a general AI assistant. It does not complete jobs, make money, or take actions for you.

    SOMETHING REAL TO KEEP
    Your Ω. On your device.
    Save one small file with your name, experiments and the working lab inside. Open it in your browser to play again—even without internet.
    Choose “Save my Ω”, then look in Downloads. Open the HTML file in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Save a new copy after your next experiments; an open file cannot update itself.
    You can save it before or after your first experiment.
    

    A few reassuring answers.
    

    What do I own?
    You keep your named lab and your experiment files on your device. The included software is open source under Apache 2.0: you may use, copy and modify it under that license. This is not a token, an NFT, company ownership or exclusive intellectual property.
    

    Where does my information go?
    The lab does not send your name or experiments anywhere. It does not use accounts, analytics or automatic browser storage. Save your copy before closing or reloading. Downloaded files are not encrypted; share only what you intend to share.
    

    Can I use my phone or tablet?
    Yes, you can experiment here. Some phones preview downloaded HTML without running it. To use your saved interactive copy, open it in a desktop browser. Keep the original download if your device only shows a preview.
    

    What can I explore next?
    The full SUCCESSOR Ω workspace offers step-by-step evidence review, generation-two comparisons and a path to an installed runtime.
