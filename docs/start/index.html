                            What is AGI ALPHA?
                            

                                An open-source project for planning,
                                experimenting with AI agents, and checking their
                                results. Start with small, bounded tasks. The
                                research vision is broader than the capabilities
                                demonstrated here.
                            

                        

                        

                            
                                Do I need to pay or connect a wallet?
                            
                            

                                No. This first plan runs in your browser without
                                an account, payment, wallet or AI service key.
                                Installing an agent or choosing external
                                services later may have separate requirements
                                and costs.
                            

                        

                        

                            
                                Can I change my mind?
                            
                            

                                Yes. Use “Change my choices” to go back. Nothing
                                is purchased, published or sent on your behalf.
                                Check your result before relying on it.
                            

                        

                        

                            
                                Need larger text or help using the page?
                            
                            

                                Use “Larger text” at the top, or your browser’s
                                zoom. You can use Tab to move between controls,
                                Enter to choose a button, and Space to tick a
                                checkbox. There is no timer.
