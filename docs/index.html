A little reassurance

A few things you may be wondering.

Open any question to read the answer.

Is the Alpha flagship free to try?

Opportunity planning, mission preparation and workspace downloads are free, without an account or wallet. Native Computer Use execution requires an operator’s configured runner and model, with separate provider and operating costs. Maintained local engineering examples remain available.

Do I need to install anything?

Nothing to install for browser planning, the catalog example, personal lab or simple planner. Native Computer Use execution requires a configured model and container runner. Your saved workspace remains an HTML file you can open offline.

Where do my inputs and results go?

Browser planning and catalog processing stay in your browser. Native Computer Use sends the approved work surface and screenshots to the operator-selected model. Use public, non-personal inputs. Save your complete workspace before closing and after changes; exports include your supplied inputs.

What advantage has this actually proved?

A proposed capability, generated patch or mathematical argument is a candidate. Inspect its actual work, test the important claims and measure full costs before claiming advantage. Independent proof, Specialist status, income and operational permission require their own evidence.

Can I make it easier to read?

Yes. Choose Larger text at the top or use your browser zoom. English and French are available on the homepage, in the Alpha flagship, in your personal lab and in the simple planner.
