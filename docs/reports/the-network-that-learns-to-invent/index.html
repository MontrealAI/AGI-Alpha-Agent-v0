Abstract
An engineering team needs a reusable temperature-response calculation; a second team needs
fresh diffusion responses; a third needs informative sensors for a finite inverse problem. We connect
these useful computational objectives through an inspectable numerical component, then test
whether its benefits survive ordinary technical alternatives and complete incremental costs. A
researcher-constructed learning system retains an orthonormal basis from twelve founder solves
and enriches it through thirty-two residual-directed Gym actions. Four candidates are preserved,
one version is frozen, and unseen cases compare the original component, its developed successor
and competent numerical baselines. Fresh confirmation comprises 128 paired in-domain cases,
64 shifted cases and 32 inverse-design missions per composition arm. The resulting component
reduces mean raw relative field error 26.3-fold on the fresh transfer distribution; this is a measured
representation change, not autonomous algorithm invention. Exact correction, assessment, setup
and serving remain separately accounted for. All corrected forward methods satisfy the declared
numerical requirements. Distribution shift increases the learned solver’s correction cost, while the
sensor-design gain is also available to the equally funded competent generalist.
A matched, finite-cash enterprise experiment uses sourced service-rate anchors and explicit
assumptions about frontier execution, demand, quality and collections. The reference Beta-only
policy produces mean modeled operating profit of $191,886 over 104 weeks; no cash revenue was
earned by this study. Optional retention decisions reject the numerical candidates at the declared
service scale. The contribution is an executable separation of learning, useful transfer, composi-
tion and commercial value, including negative evidence and an existing production admission
boundary. It does not establish specialist superintelligence, graph superiority, profitable sharing or
self-acceleration.
The evidence in one sentence
Experience changed a reusable numerical representation and improved its fresh approximation;
complete-cost admission rejected the reference retention program. Complementary experimental
design helped, but did not establish a unique network advantage.
1 Begin with the useful work
The immediate work product is a small scientific-software package that computes and checks responses
of a heterogeneous diffusion model. Its founder mission represents a thermal-design service: provide
reproducible response fields and a numerical envelope over specified material and source variations. A
recipient then needs responses across a wider diffusion–reaction regime, and an experimental-design
mission needs sensor locations that identify unknown coefficients from noisy observations. The
deliverables are executable computations with finite, published acceptance rules. They are not verified
physical products, customer engagements or human-expert trials.
This choice makes learned capability tangible. A saved numerical matrix contains information extracted
from solved cases. Loading the matrix changes the recipient’s calculation, even though the recipient
never observes the original training solutions. A second procedure chooses informative sensor locations.
These objects can be independently hashed, transferred, executed, rejected and retired. Their practical
value is measurable without assigning an agent a specialist label.
The research question is whether such experience can produce an advantage beyond competent
ordinary tools, and whether that advantage justifies the additional resources required to develop and
share it. The commercial foundation remains a separate question: can expertly operated frontier AI
produce accepted digital outcomes at a cost below a price referenced to equivalent human services?

The work has a transformational aspect when it changes the representation or the experiment: reuse a
low-dimensional response space instead of repeatedly starting from an uninformative state, or choose
a measurement that resolves uncertainty instead of collecting more uninformative data. These are
established scientific methods. The novelty claimed here is their joined, reproducible learning–transfer–
composition experiment and its accounting, not the invention of reduced-basis methods or optimal
design.
The finite discretization is deliberately reproducible on an ordinary CPU. It is much smaller than
many industrial simulations. A positive result cannot be extrapolated to industrial geometry, physical
fidelity or expert service quality without another evaluation. This limitation makes the empirical claim
narrower; it does not turn the result into an assumed financial uplift.

2.1 Why earlier negative retention results matter
Earlier research editions provide distinct counterexamples. The Profitable Work First study reported
attractive conditional Beta economics in its reference world while retaining no optional capability in
its primary campaigns. A separately frozen diagnostic improved one network policy slightly but still
underperformed Beta after complete cost. Other editions recorded assets that were reused without
repaying their own incremental costs. The audit preserves their source links and distinguishes reported
results from new replications.
Those failures suggest three bottlenecks: screening can cost money without creating an admissible
asset; expected reuse can be too small or mismatched to the admission belief; and a capability can
improve a task without saving enough of the enterprise’s scarce resource. The present experiment
does not repair those findings by assigning guaranteed gains or lowering costs until a win appears.
It constructs a real changed artifact, compares against a strong technical baseline, and asks which
bottleneck remains.

3.2 Gym: change a representation through bounded experience
The Gym exposes observations, allowed actions, transitions, budgets and termination. Its state includes
the current basis, available training cases and remaining enrichment actions. An observation evaluates
residuals for a declared pool of seventy-two practice cases. An action selects an available practice
case, solves it, orthogonalizes the new field and records the resulting artifact. The controller selects
the largest residual, subject to a cap of thirty-two enrichment actions. A case cannot be selected after
removal; an exhausted budget terminates the campaign.
Four candidate checkpoints at ranks 20, 28, 36 and 44 are assessed on development cases. The frozen
selection rule prioritizes final accepted count, raw accepted count, then mean raw field error, with rank
as a tie-breaker. It therefore selects for numerical fidelity, not secretly for final enterprise profit. All
candidates, observations, assessment work and the original development attempt remain available.
The initial six-sensor inverse task reached a development ceiling; a two-sensor, higher-noise challenge
was specified before confirmation, with the original control retained.
The update changes a stored numerical representation. It does not modify proprietary model weights,
learn a general skill-selection policy or demonstrate that an agent learned how to learn. Sensor selection
is an executable procedure applied to current mission fields. Keeping these mechanisms separate
prevents a larger basis, a higher practice score or more inference from being mislabeled as a general

3.3 Agent B: use the component on unfamiliar work
Fresh diffusion–reaction cases broaden coefficient and source ranges. The recipient loads the exact
frozen artifact and computes a reduced initial solution. A residual-corrected solver then returns a
response meeting the same final acceptance criteria as the ordinary methods. The raw approximation is
also evaluated separately. Thus the experiment can detect learning even when a correction step makes
the final outputs equally accurate.
The transfer domain shares a discretization and operator family with the founder but changes the
parameter distribution and job context. It is a meaningful related-domain test, not evidence of
unrestricted transfer across physics or software ecosystems. The artifact requires compatible grid
indexing, operator parts, units and numerical libraries. Out-of-distribution cases deliberately increase
anisotropy, local contrast and source concentration; their failures and overhead remain in the results.
3.4 Agent C: compose numerical and experimental expertise
The composition mission evaluates sixteen possible coefficient pairs. A sensor-design procedure derives
low-dimensional response features and greedily selects locations using an information-gain criterion.
No hidden true coefficient is available to the design procedure. Observations are generated from a
held-out true grid member plus the declared noise; reconstruction minimizes the finite observation
mismatch.
The comparison includes the numerical component alone with fixed sensors, the design procedure
alone with the ordinary forward solver, and their combination. The primary competent generalist
receives the same design method and ordinary tools. Fixed sensors are an ablation, not the generalist
ceiling. Every arm receives the same candidate grid, sensor count and noise realization. Measured
forward, design and reconstruction costs are attributed to each arm even when measurement reuse
avoids physically rerunning identical code in the experiment. Unmetered research orchestration and
authoring remain unknown; additional enterprise coordination costs are modeled explicitly.
This design can reveal complementarity without assuming a network advantage. If informative sensors
improve reconstruction but the competent generalist can use the same procedure at equal resources,
the result supports experimental design and does not establish retained Alpha.

4.2 Acceptance includes an independent numerical check
Ordinary baselines include sparse LU, incomplete-LU-preconditioned GMRES and a separable spectral
preconditioner with conjugate gradients. Incomplete LU is used with GMRES, not assumed to be
an SPD preconditioner for CG. The primary ordinary solver is selected on development runtime
and frozen. Valid factorization reuse is allowed whenever matrices repeat. An explicitly declared
improved-generalist numerical policy tests a stronger ordinary choice on the shifted regime; this is not
a newly measured API-model release.[5, 6]
Final residuals are recomputed by a flux stencil separate from the sparse assembly. A direct solve
supplies a research reference for field-error assessment. The final tolerances are relative residual
2 × 10−8 and relative field error 2 × 10−6. Raw-surrogate tolerances are separately declared and are
not substituted for the final requirement.

4.3 Protection and uncertainty
Training, development and fresh confirmation use distinct case seeds. Candidate bytes and source
identities are frozen before root-supplied confirmation seeds are evaluated. No post-confirmation
candidate tuning is admitted into the primary comparison. The shared research team and public
generators do not constitute an external, adversarial evaluation service; the protection is procedural
and source-auditable. Public release intentionally makes the cases reproducible after the experiment.
Paired cases receive identical equations, reference answers and mission noise. Numerical methods run
with one BLAS thread, warmups and rotating execution order. Wall-clock differences at millisecond
scale remain vulnerable to host noise; per-case rows and paired uncertainty are reported alongside
iteration counts and error. Timing savings are not generalized to another machine by a clock-rate
multiplier.
The reported 95% timing intervals are percentile intervals from 5,000 bootstrap resamples of paired
cases (seed 81221) within one local run. They do not measure reproducibility across machines or
independent sessions, and resampling does not remove host drift shared by several case pairs.

5 What changed, what transferred, and what did not
The frozen Gym candidate has rank 44, compared with rank 12 for v1. On the 64 fresh transfer cases,
mean raw relative field error falls from 0.02869 to 0.00109, a 26.3-fold reduction. However, neither raw
component meets the separately declared raw acceptance rule on any of those 64 cases. The corrected
methods all pass. Accuracy learning is therefore established within this workload; a standalone
approximation meeting the recipient’s requirement is not. On the 64 fresh thermal cases, v2 passes the
raw rule on 9 cases.
The meaningful learned change is the basis itself. It supplies a better initial representation of previously
unseen response fields. This does not imply a proportional reduction in completed-job cost. The
recipient’s competent baseline can solve the original finite system, and the corrected learned method
still pays for projection, a linear solve, residual checks and any remaining iterations.

5.2 The productive frontier at fixed resources
Define the finite empirical productive set at budget B and required acceptance q as
F (B, q) = {j : Pr(accepted outcome for j) ≥ q, E[Cj] ≤ B}. (4)
The operational definition must fix the beneficiary, original requirements, workload distribution and
accounting boundary. A faster approximation that abandons accuracy does not enlarge this set. A
composition expands the frontier only if it crosses a required reliability or cost boundary that the
equally resourced generalist cannot already cross.
The descriptive time-frontier curves show local differences in verified execution latency. They do not
show a new reliable offering beyond the competent generalist: all corrected forward methods meet the
numerical requirement, and the composition ties generalist identification. No prospectively specified
service-level deadline was crossed uniquely by the learned network. The enterprise model therefore
assigns no automatic new-product revenue or quality premium to these objects.

6.2 The human-service reference and the pricing decision
SDSU lists an external scientific-computing service rate of $80/hour; Vanderbilt research software
engineering starts at $84/hour; UTEP lists an external analyst rate of $190.31/hour for services
including study design. These are service-rate anchors, not quotes for our artifacts.[12–14]
The central scope estimate uses 72, 96 and 120 hours at $84/hour for the three offerings: $6,048,
$8,064 and $10,080. The effort includes formulation, implementation, independent numerical checks,
correction, documentation and delivery. Every effort estimate remains an assumption; rates for more
complex specialist work can differ. Demand and price acceptance are modeled separately.
A frozen finite grid compares price fractions 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.75, 0.90 and 1.05 of the human-service

same selected prices apply across matched policies. The comparison therefore does not give shared

7.1 Five choices, equal starting conditions
The policies are: competent generalists without retention; private retention; private retention with
optional Gym; selective sharing; and sharing with optional Gym and composition. They begin with the
same seed, opportunity streams, quality shocks, price menu and resource limits. Candidate admission
uses development evidence and declared demand beliefs. The policy cannot inspect future demand,
future true quality or its exact date of obsolescence.
No policy is forced to buy training or retain an asset. The primary screen first asks whether even
free elimination of all numerical computation could repay minimum retention cost; if that optimistic
bound fails, the controller declines without consulting an unpurchased Gym result. When forecast
incremental value is negative, declining is the correct policy outcome. Composition uses an equally
funded generalist with ordinary access to the experimental-design procedure. Because no graph-
specific advantage is claimed, an ordinary library with the same authorized bytes and permissions is
an equality control, not a deliberately impoverished search baseline.
The horizon is 104 operating weeks plus collection runoff. Three primary environments vary oppor-
tunity arrival, trust, willingness to pay, quality, model cost, default and successor timing. A stronger
frontier scenario changes base execution cost and quality for every policy, while an independent
numerical comparison tests a stronger ordinary solver. The former is simulated; the latter is executed.
The assumptions and all sampled histories are included.
First, resources remain fixed. A separate high-demand experiment allows expansion only after available
earned funds exceed the capital and reserve conditions and forecast capacity value exceeds expansion
cost. Extra output from purchased capacity is labeled expansion. It is not counted as evidence that
retained intelligence compounded.

do not transfer to a new system by analogy.[17, 18]

10.1 Limits that determine the next study
The workload is synthetic, low-dimensional in its parameters and restricted to a fixed grid and affine
operator family. It lacks human trials, customer purchases, physical validation and measured frontier
agent performance. The confirmation is protected procedurally within one AI-assisted research session,
rather than administered by an independent laboratory. Timing is local. The enterprise assumes task
quality and customer demand. The generic scientific component is outside the frozen production
capsule scope.
These limits identify concrete next tests. The first is a customer-relevant scientific-software task with a
costly repeated computational bottleneck and independently specified acceptance. The second is a
competent frontier generalist that may discover or construct the same ordinary method, with all model
and tool costs recorded. The third is an actual authorized service or component transfer with versioned
operating permissions and a fresh recipient. Only then should an expanding network be credited with
earned economic advantage.
