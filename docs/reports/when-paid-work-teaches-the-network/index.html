Abstract
Can an automated enterprise earn useful operating profit from competent current tools, and
then obtain additional value by retaining what paid work taught it? We implement a certified
numerical-reformulation foundry around an existing, byte-pinned skill lifecycle. Candidate con-
struction records, evidence gates, current-state authorization, graph transitions, resource constraints,
finite-cash accounting and bounded policy selection execute as software; frontier-model outcomes,
customers, transfer and payments are modeled. Development selects one common commercial
controller and optional private/network retention policies before fresh confirmation. Across three
64-world primary cohorts, with 52 selling and 20 runoff weeks, Beta mean profit is $121,004, $919,696
and $1,810,306. Only 5, 44 and 39 worlds, respectively, meet the complete service-quality gate.
All selected retention policies choose off, making primary incremental effects exactly zero. A
prespecified enabled-assessor challenger loses money relative to Beta in each profile. A separate
cheaper-retention mechanism cohort achieves 123 useful reuses, including 86 by peers, yet its mean
direct retention balance is $-1,020 and its enterprise increment is $-139,215. Twenty-one individual
admitted skills have positive directly linked balances before shared discovery costs. A separate
executed numerical witness ties a retained template with competent structured Beta. All 5,082
histories reconcile, and nine complete records replay byte-for-byte. These results support profitable
simplicity under stated assumptions and selective evidence requirements; they do not establish
empirically profitable autonomy or a positive network flywheel.
Three questions, three answers. Can Beta earn money? Yes, conditionally in the primary
simulation; quality qualification is incomplete. Can paid work leave a useful reusable method?
Yes in a mechanism diagnostic, and the ordinary-tool witness exhibits a concrete representation.
Does the selected network policy add net enterprise value? No positive increment is established.
Retention off is selected; enabled alternatives incur losses.
1 Question, value and evidence
The commercial foundation is useful delivery. An enterprise does not need an accumulating skill
graph to justify profitable use of existing frontier models and ordinary tools. Conversely, a profitable
enterprise does not establish that its retained skills, network distribution or reinvestment create value.
We separate these claims before choosing a venture, a price policy or a statistical analysis.
Beta is competent use of capabilities already available to the recipient: current frontier reasoning,
ordinary numerical libraries, grounded decomposition, executable checking, critique, correction and
accumulated ordinary task familiarity. Alpha is additional net mission advantage over that baseline
and the recipient’s existing inventory, within a declared scope. Familiar mathematics may support
a useful transformation; familiarity also makes it available to Beta. A stored wrapper, graph edge,
download or authorization does not by itself establish Alpha.

3 A venture with an executable transformation
The venture is a certified numerical-reformulation foundry. A customer supplies a bounded public
or synthetic resource-allocation specification. The service returns an executable representation or
experiment, a purpose bridge to the customer’s original requirement, explicit numerical assumptions
and a checkable result. The primary family changes sparse or low-rank representations. Limited
contrasts construct discriminating certificate experiments and complementary scenario/recourse
protocols. This is demanding algorithm and engineering work, not clerical output or an idea list.
The term “certified” refers to the specified acceptance contract. Neither an uncertain simulated validator
nor the floating-point witness proves every semantic property of a real customer’s mission. Mature
ordinary tools already solve many such problems: HiGHS supports LP , MIP and QP; SCIP exposes
exact-solving and certificate facilities under documented qualifications; independent optimization
certificates have a substantial literature [10, 11, 12]. Beta is allowed those capabilities.
Jobs have known scope multipliers 0.65, 1.00 or 1.45, continuous difficulty from 0.25 to 0.95, declared
acceptance requirements and one-week or two-week deadlines. The family mixture is 52% representa-
tion, 33% certificate and 15% recourse. Scope scales project costs, team references and requirements
consistently; a per-project saving is never multiplied by an unrelated weekly workload. All three
families are already offerable by Beta. Additional accepted recourse work therefore measures capacity

5 Causal world and information boundaries
Four agents— atlas , lyra , orion and vega under the modeled agent.agi.eth namespace—begin
with heterogeneous ordinary experience. Their recipient overlap increases through completed work
in every arm and through common Beta improvement. Private graphs are separate per creator: an
inaccessible peer capability cannot be counted as already available to a private recipient. The network
graph provides common availability to every current authenticated fixture member, without requiring
invocation.
Stateless keyed randomness identifies exogenous events by world, job, week and semantic event. A
different number of candidate assessments never shifts the next unrelated market draw. Markets,
scopes, preferences and relevant production shocks are matched across policies. Endogenous queues,
agent assignments and available capabilities can differ; those differences are part of the policy effect,
not exogenous privileges. All arms share price options, reserve choices, task ceilings, resource budgets
and initial capital.
5.1 Learning has causes and can fail
An accepted paid artifact may produce no candidate. Otherwise, novelty and a modularity draw
generate duplicate, narrow, transferable, complementary or misleading candidates. The configured
conditional mixture is 20% duplicate, 25% narrow, 29% transferable, 16% component and 10% mislead-
ing; incorrect source jobs yield misleading candidates. Components outside a usable recourse bundle
have no standalone saving. Only a rights-cleared artifact can enter assessment. A candidate is not
made useful because the enterprise has cash to invest.
Hidden world state includes actual correctness, technical transfer, systematic assay bias, unexpected
obsolescence and future shocks. Observed state includes current task scope, past started-job counts,

5.2 Automated delivery and finite resources
Routine intake, clarification, production, validation, up to two correction attempts and recovery are
automated in the model. Each consumes costs and relevant resources. Four workers provide 32
effective production hours each per week; validation has 40 hours; the shared token ceiling is 480
million per week. These effective budgets are model assumptions below advertised ceilings, not
verified provider throughput. Intake and retention compete with delivery for the same budgets.
Queues and deadlines can make an otherwise correct result commercially unacceptable.
Defect probability depends on known difficulty, recourse scope, ordinary experience and common
Beta progress. Weekly failure bursts affect producers and validators; a job-level shared blind spot
can raise the false-pass probability across correction attempts. Validators can falsely reject or abstain.
Corrections consume additional production, validation and tokens; they may fail or introduce a defect.
Similar-model agreement never becomes independent proof.
Beta advances every 13 weeks in the baseline, lowering modeled defects and increasing overlap.
Exogenous family/pattern absorption can make a retained capability redundant. The same absorption
schedule applies to every arm and persists in current baseline keys, so a new equivalent candidate
cannot evade redundancy by receiving a new ID. Faster improvement, changing scope and surprise
obsolescence are tested separately.
The runner reserves retention resources before delivery. This is consequential: a campaign can displace
high-value work even when its fee is small. The finite policy class does not optimize all possible
scheduling orders, asynchronous screening or queue-aware deferral rules. We report this limitation
rather than infer that every possible retention architecture is uneconomic.

7 Commercial decisions and disjoint accounting
7.1 Buyer choice and bounded price optimization
A buyer chooses AI, human service, postponement or decline. Given reference price H, latent value V
and delay cost d, the implemented utilities are
UAI =bqV − P −bτd − ρV + ϵAI , (10)
UH = 0.985V − H − 3d + ϵH, (11)
Upostpone = 0.08V − 0.06H + ϵP, Udecline = 0. (12)
The buyer takes its best response. Value ranges from 1.05 to 2.30 times the human reference, urgency
and tastes vary, and ρ is a profile-specific trust penalty. The seller observes declared scope and its
past reliability signal, not latent buyer value. The reliability signal incorporates observed lateness and
delayed refund accruals; undetected hidden correctness is unavailable. These response and reputation
dynamics are uncalibrated assumptions.
The seller selects a posted-price ratio from 0.55, 0.75 or 0.95 of the scope-matched human reference,
rather than imposing a universal discount or cost-plus markup. It also chooses no entry or entry,
four/eight weeks of overhead reserve, and a known difficulty ceiling of 0.75/0.95. This is a bounded
leader-price/buyer-response model, not a proof of equilibrium in a changing competitive market. The
best common commercial controller is selected on Beta and applied to all architectures. We thereby
isolate retention and sharing at common freedoms; we do not estimate every architecture’s globally
optimized maximum.
7.2 A, B and C pay once
The ledger has three disjoint expense categories:
• A: delivery. Intake, clarification, paid-job learning and development, production, tools, checking,
corrections, hosting and operational governance.
• B: retention and reuse. Additional screening, extraction, generalization and fresh assessment;
packaging, integration/distribution, catalog discovery, every paid invocation attempt, revalidation
and upkeep.
• C: optional extra investment. Independent formulation experiments beyond the paid job, only
when observed evidence and funds justify them.
Every activity has a unique identity. The paid artifact is not charged a second development bill in
B. Customer payment does not erase A. Rejected candidates, failed assays, failed transfer and paid
invocation attempts remain expenses. A demonstration’s source-job quote less directly attributed A is
a direct contribution before shared overhead, not fully allocated job profit.
Seed capital is $30,000 financing. Reservations constrain spendable resources before commitments.
Expense accrual creates vendor obligations; invoices create receivables; collections and payments affect
cash. Cash cannot go negative. Obligations survive closure. Weekly settlement nets due collections
before due payments: this time-resolution assumption may understate intraperiod liquidity needs. The
20-week runoff and maximum six-week job age resolve modeled receivables and refund obligations;
none are hidden at the terminal date.

11 Mechanism, durability and uncertainty
The primary model assumes no retained quality increment and no demand expansion. Removing
either is therefore an exact negative control, not proof that those channels have been empirically
ruled out. Turnaround benefit is derived from avoided construction work, rather than assigned as an
additional expert multiplier. Removing that time saving retains the scoped execution-cost channel
while eliminating its resource-release channel.
In the fixed mechanism panel, no-speed and cost-savings-only conditions leave approximately the
same small useful-reuse count but reduce the network’s mean enterprise increment to about $-170,031.
Zero transfer produces no admissions or useful reuse and a mean increment of $-170,013. Faster Beta
leaves one admission across 16 worlds and no useful reuse. Changed recourse scope remains negative.
Adding an assumed 2.5-percentage-point quality benefit produces the same realized aggregate result
in that small base sample; this is low realized exposure, not validation of a quality gain.
The difference between complete enterprise increment and direct retention balance includes displaced
work, queueing, corrections, pricing responses, assignment, quality and collections. We do not label
that residual a separately identified “capacity effect.” The ablations identify interventions within this
world model, not additive structural components that can be summed without interaction. Usable
capacity, turnaround, recourse acceptance and composition counts are retained in every result record.
Nineteen composed uses occur in the cheaper fixed-challenger network. The witness confirms that
its complementary equality component is necessary on its declared fixtures. Neither observation
establishes an additional offering unavailable to competent Beta. The no-composition selected-policy
stress is correctly null when the selected policy uses no skills; it is not a powered empirical estimate of
bundle value.

11.2 Stochastic and parameter uncertainty are separate
The 24-point parameter design changes assumptions jointly and uses two matched shock replicates per
point. All 48 Beta histories are profitable, but only 21/48 meet quality. Sixteen points have at least one
passing replicate, and only five pass both. These statements describe chosen design coverage. They
are not probabilities that a real enterprise will succeed, and two shocks per point are insufficient to
estimate each point’s tail risk precisely.

14 What the evidence supports
Profitable Beta survives as a conditional commercial result. The source-priced reference model
can deliver substantial correct, on-time output at modeled cost below human-team references. A
no-retention enterprise is a legitimate outcome. Quality failures and stress losses materially limit the
result: product viability requires reliable service, not only positive accounting.
Paid work can produce something useful to another agent in the model. The cheaper-retention panel
records fresh peer reuse with explicit scope, fees, recipient overlap and lifecycle transitions. Some
individual capabilities have positive directly linked balances. The executed numerical witness makes
the transformation concrete while showing that ordinary knowledge may already supply the same
capability. Neither demonstration is a live model-learning trial.
The tested acquisition program does not earn its additional cost. Candidate search, rejected as-
sessments, upkeep and resource competition matter. A graph can contain useful methods while the
enterprise does better without its retention program. A policy that chooses off protects profitable
simplicity; it does not erase those useful methods or establish their universal worthlessness.
Network value remains unproven. Sharing creates availability, not demand. In the cheaper diagnostic
the network-private mean is positive but uncertain, and both retention architectures lose against Beta.
Ownership, inclusion proofs and authenticated availability are necessary boundaries, not economic
mechanisms by themselves. There is no evidence here of permanent scarcity, automatic exponential
compounding or self-acceleration from greater volume.
The most consequential limitations are empirical. Model success, token work, transfer, recurrence,
customer value, trust and reference project hours remain assumptions. Provider aliases and limits
are documented, but account throughput and immutable model snapshots are not measured. The
six-seed development search is deliberately small and the scheduling class restricted. The simple buyer

claim that such a pilot has occurred.

experiment, profitable Beta is preserved, unnecessary retention is declined, useful peer reuse is
visible, and the complete added cost remains visible with it. The demonstrated flywheel is a
disciplined decision process: deliver, test, keep only what earns its place, and retire it when the
baseline or mission changes. A positive, durable economic network flywheel is not established.
