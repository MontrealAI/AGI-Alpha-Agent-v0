CSV format and a short example

Prices and shipping use decimal amounts. Shipping is one fixed charge per supplier in USD. fx_to_usd is the explicit conversion ratio from one unit of the source currency to USD; use 1 for USD. Repeated item quantities, supplier shipping and currency ratios must agree.

item_id,item,quantity,vendor,pack_units,pack_price,currency,fx_to_usd,shipping,delivery_days
