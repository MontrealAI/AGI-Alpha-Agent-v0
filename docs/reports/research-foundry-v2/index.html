Foundry v2

SUCCESSOR Ω Harness · Powered by TRANSFORMATIONAL INTELLIGENCE ∞. · A Foundry of Discovery Instruments

A simulated thermal-fluid foundry constructs fixtures, a calibration station and a qualified research instrument, then a new calorimetry method and a distinct simulated successor. A retained failure: the 4.9477% predicted/observed heat difference passes the 5% runtime rule, while the calorimeter has 8.6964% error against reference-model true heat. Conventional engineering ties useful accuracy with 384 versus 672 readings; a centralized lab is CAD 460 cheaper. Participant withdrawal and underutilization fail in the tested economic cases. Finite search, modeled economics and Python contract checks; physical construction and deployment NOT RUN. Results and limitsRésultats et limites

The 4.9477% runtime prediction/observation difference passes its 5% rule while true-heat error is 8.6964%. Conventional engineering ties useful accuracy with 384 rather than 672 readings; the centralized lab is CAD 460 cheaper.
