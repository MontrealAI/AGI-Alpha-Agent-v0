## Evidence and limitations

- **Foundry:** modeled thermal physics/economics and source-derived Python contract demonstration. The calorimeter passes 4.9477% prediction/observation comparison yet has 8.6964% true-heat error. Conventional engineering uses fewer readings; a centralized lab is cheaper. No physical deployment, EVM execution, mainnet settlement or commercial success is established by that study.
- **Capability Inheritance:** retained/rebuilt/expert arithmetic work is 63,196 / 915,136 / 5,760. The expert wins. Inherited successor qualification costs 7,918 versus 2,030 directly. Thermal bounds are conditional simulations. Local EVM/worker evidence remains separate from unresolved end-to-end thermal integration. Real money, human effort and energy costs remain unknown.
- **Discoverability:** conventional baselines tie and centralized direct execution can win. The ≤$100 total-cost to ≥$10,000 genuinely realizable incremental-value challenge remains **UNPROVEN**. No customer, executable offer, settlement receipt or complete cost ledger is invented.
