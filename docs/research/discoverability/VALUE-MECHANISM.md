## The deliverable and why it could be useful

The commercial accounting unit remains **quantity 1: one versioned,
nonexclusive software-and-evidence delivery bundle**. Its current contents are
the executable finite compiler, generated digital diagnostic and synthetic
assay programs, decoders, input/model assumptions, rejection evidence,
acceptance checks and reproduction instructions. The held-out network routing
requirement supplies successor evidence within the same research package; it
does not create another sale or an extra counted asset valuation.

The compiler constructs integer-weighted measurement combinations that cancel a
declared nuisance class and distinguish only those modeled worlds requiring
different decisions. Its output is an executable measurement program with a
bounded decision procedure and an acquisition-cost certificate for the admitted
finite grammar. It can explicitly report ambiguity, insufficient acquisition
budget or exhausted search. Those refusals are useful because they prevent a
program from presenting an unresolved inverse problem as an established answer.
The grammar and qualified construction templates can be reused when their
validity boundary still applies; a changed boundary requires refusal or new
construction. This is the productive asset under study. It is software for a
finite model class, not a physical instrument or proof of the forward model.
See [the paper](paper.tex), [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCTION.md) and
[held-out evidence](evidence/heldout-first-results.json).

A plausible beneficiary category is an **independent engineering or research
organization that already has a bounded digital diagnostic decision, lawful
access to its measurement inputs and an operational consequence for an accurate
answer**. No organization, buyer, demand, purchase budget or willingness to pay
has been identified in this record. A possible service is configuring and
qualifying one measurement program against that organization's agreed decision
contract. Any price would have to be justified by that scoped work and delivery;
the availability of open-source compiler code does not establish a sale price.

## A concrete customer acceptance and independent pricing protocol

The following is a **prepared next-step protocol**, not a completed customer
engagement or a commitment. It is deliberately one bundle and one decision.

1. **Freeze one digital decision contract.** An accountable acceptance owner,
   separate from the producer, specifies the required action, permitted
   measurements, world catalogue, nuisance/error assumptions, lawful data access,
   scope boundaries, delivery format and immutable pass/refusal criteria.
   Customer-supplied held-out data and an independently checked forward model
   would test external validity. A model mismatch is a failure of that proposed
   application, not permission to loosen its acceptance criterion.
2. **Price the matched alternative independently.** Before claiming savings,
   retain an actual conventional service quote or supported internal delivery
   cost for the same decision, rights, quality, quantity and realization window.
   Include a competent centralized method and let it win. A quotation for a
   different scope, an arbitrary replacement price or the research team's own
   appraisal cannot establish the counterfactual.
3. **Obtain a separately authorized, independently set executable offer.** Only
   after authorization for customer engagement, seek one arm's-length buyer's
   executable bid or purchase commitment for quantity 1. Record counterparty
   independence, acceptance owner, price/currency, exact conditions, expiry,
   payment timing, delivery capacity and unavoidable acquisition, validation,
   delivery, support and settlement costs. A buyer's interest, a grant, an
   internal transfer or an unsigned price suggestion is insufficient.
4. **Execute and accept against the frozen contract.** Preserve producer outputs
   and a distinct acceptor's complete decision, including rejection. For the
   enterprise claim, the native owner must first admit the reviewed adapter
   through the existing integration seam and commission its actual typed job.
   Passing the current synthetic model or the existing Alpha contract suite
   cannot replace that acceptance.
5. **Close one reconciled ledger.** Count the offer only once and only up to its
   committed quantity and available delivery capacity. Establish incremental net
   realizable proceeds relative to the matched alternative after unavoidable
   costs; record settled cash separately when it actually arrives. Count each
   cost once. Do not add a capability valuation to its downstream revenue.
   Claim the challenge only if total attributable production cost is known and
   at most US$100, independently realizable incremental net value exceeds
   US$10,000, and the complete rights, conditions and fully loaded sensitivity
   are documented. An offer above US$10,000 alone does not pass those gates.
