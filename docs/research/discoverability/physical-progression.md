# Conditional progression to useful physical capacity

The **≤US$100 total attributable production cost → >US$10,000 independently realizable incremental value** challenge remains **UNPROVEN**. It first concerns the digital deliverable and a documented executable realization mechanism. It does not budget an industrial facility at US$100.

**Demonstrated:** finite synthetic measurement construction, scoped inheritance and recorded software evidence.  
**Testable next:** a separately authorized low-voltage switched resistance/contact fixture, tested against independently calibrated references and blinded faults.  
**Long-term ambition:** the physical succession below, with every new construction, resource requirement and commercial condition validated separately.

| Transition | Required construction and resources | Decisive acceptance and economic condition |
|---|---|---|
| Digital capability | Compiler, scope, data rights, compute and review | Correct decisions versus competent alternatives; actual independent executable offer; complete costs |
| Scientific instrumentation | Physical fixture, reference loads, acquisition hardware, firmware, bench labor and calibration access | Blind fault decisions within a measured uncertainty envelope; compare full cost with existing instruments |
| Manufacturing | Tooling, specified materials, suppliers, assembly process, inspection and batch records | Repeatable yield, interchangeability, aging and rework; orders cover materials, scrap, maintenance, delivery and warranty |
| Infrastructure | Qualified modules plus power equipment, site rights, installation, protection, commissioning, operators and spares | System-level outage/fallback/recovery tests; funded owner acceptance and service obligations |
| Useful energy | Real generation/conversion/storage/efficiency equipment and an external energy source | Independently metered delivery or adjusted-baseline savings; actual beneficiary acceptance and net receipts |

Material, tooling, labor, validation and delivery capacity bound physical output; independent executable demand separately bounds commercial realization. Available cash and authority bound reinvestment. No assumed unlimited growth or automatic transfer of physical validity follows from a software certificate.

Primary context: [NIST metrological traceability](https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability), [DOE supply-chain readiness](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/manufacturing/articles/department-energys-office-manufacturing-and-energy-supply-chains), and [DOE measurement and verification](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/measurement-and-verification-options-federal-energy-and-water-saving-projects). These support the validation requirements; the proposed progression is our conditional research plan, not a reported result.
