# One exact confidential assessment: useful execution and a decisive stop

**AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL · Engineering decision brief · 27 October 2026**

**Decision: STOP this candidate as the first flagship breakthrough.** Preserve the small executable preflight and
its counterexamples as ordinary research tools. Do not launch the modeled venture or pay for extra retention on
this evidence. The sprint supplies useful construction and a sharper acceptance boundary; substantive novelty and
robust positive complete economics do not pass.

**The useful purpose and the proposed change.** Research #4991 selected a confidential pre-emergency reserve
assessment for three independent owners. The original useful outcome is an exact answer to whether every declared
simultaneous-demand scenario admits an eligible, available, non-withheld injective allocation. Negative answers are
useful because they can prevent reliance on an infeasible pool. Physical equipment, truthful declarations and real
emergency execution remain outside that assessment. This engineering sprint takes that selected application rather
than repeating the research team's broad opportunity search.

The original worker uses established MPyC secure computation and opens its authorized final predicate. Its native
grant already restricts the exact source, inputs, scope and epoch, and refuses adaptive same-epoch replay. The
question tested here is an additional application requirement: can the authorized answer identify a declared private
state, particularly when correlated outputs are batched or the recipient already knows part of the state? This is
not a break of MPC, a bypass of the one-use grant, or a finding that the original paper promised zero inference.
Output disclosure is an established distinction.

The constructed change is an offline preflight over a finite public table of hypothetical private states, recipient
information and exact output vectors. It requires at least two distinct secret alternatives in every possible
observer/output class. Its decision is computed over all declared possible answers before actual-state selection.
Thus a revealing denial cannot itself be selected by the real private answer. This is a checkable, deliberately weak
non-identification property: substantial partial disclosure and unequal posterior probabilities remain possible.
The table is authored synthetic input, not a verified model of all real organizational knowledge.

**What was constructed and actually executed.** Eight small Python source files reuse the native canonical codec,
Journal, signatures, trust and A/B/C CostLedger, plus the unchanged research worker and one-use authority helper.
They connect the opportunity and mission to source-bound packets, preflight assessments, real subprocess outputs,
separate acceptance checks, explicit nonexecution outcomes and an unknown-cost native accounting record. No
parallel registry, executor, payment store, broad platform or new UI was built.

Development covered 48 actual MPC worlds. After source/policy freezing, confirmation generated 24 new authored
missions in six families: one required property, two properties, three properties, composed single-property queries,
correlated unavailability and recipient side information. Every mission includes eight hypothetical secret values.
The full characterization workload has 256 worlds because composed-query missions contain several output columns.
Fresh cases exercise changed asset/client mappings, unavailable scenarios, composed outputs and known state bits.
They are synthetic protocol-held-out work, not protected external cases or evidence of broad generality.

All **256/256** unchanged MPC outputs matched the separate exhaustive assignment checker: **85 positive** and
**171 negative** answers. Each worker opened only its own public synthetic owner file. Actual loopback execution
took **24.49 seconds** and sent **28,162,266 application-stream bytes** across the three workers. The transport
counters exclude TCP/IP headers. This was an explicit public characterization experiment; it is not a live service
that intentionally disclosed real private states.

The preflight and separate relational checker agreed. Under the added rule, **11/24** missions were eligible and
then actually executed with fresh one-use grants; **8** of them had informative output variation within an observer
class. Three eligible cases were invariant negative results. The remaining **13** missions stayed explicitly
unexecuted in the guarded delivery. The guarded subprocess workload took **0.91 seconds** and sent **1,211,280
application-stream bytes**. These observations establish a bounded working preflight plus useful authorized
execution. They do not turn 13 refusals into accepted deliveries of the original exact-answer product.

**The decisive comparison.** Technical construction, authorization and disclosure protection solve different parts
of the job. The controls below use the same public hypotheses and original feasibility predicate.

| Configuration | Useful exact answers | Added non-identification rule | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing cleartext assignment reference | Exact reference answers | Supervisor possesses all synthetic inputs | Strong scientific control, no confidential-host claim |
| Unchanged MPC alone | 256/256 agree | Authorized final answers can identify a declared secret state | Correct cryptographic execution is insufficient for the added requirement |
| Existing one-use authorization | Restricts exact input/source/scope/epoch | Does not alter information inherent in a permitted output vector | Existing replay controls remain necessary and pass |
| Preflight plus authorization plus MPC | 11/24 missions execute correctly | All admitted declared output classes retain at least two alternatives | 13 original exact-answer missions are refused |
| Ordinary library with identical information | Same preflight decisions and feasibility results | Same finite guarantee | No extra retained or operational advantage demonstrated |

In particular, three one-bit questions may each leave four alternatives while their combined vector identifies the
entire three-bit state. Checking columns independently is insufficient. Recipient side information can similarly
turn a previously non-identifying answer into an identifying answer. Conversely, the guard must not count a
recipient-already-known output as informative. The code includes directed controls for those distinctions.

For a singleton output class, exact correctness and the added two-alternative rule are incompatible in this declared
domain. The minimum remaining technical decision is explicit consent to that disclosure, a justified change of
question, or refusal. Changing the answer or claiming that a refusal delivered the original result would weaken
the acceptance contract. This experiment chooses honest refusal and reports the resulting utility loss.

**Novelty result.** This is a new application probe in the repository, not a new query-auditing method. The scoped
primary-source check identified Nabar, Marthi, Kenthapadi, Mishra and Motwani, *Towards Robustness in Query Auditing*
(VLDB 2006). It already addresses inference from aggregate answers and the danger of denials selected by a hidden
answer. The implementation here enumerates a tiny supplied domain rather than advancing that theory. MPyC's own
documentation explicitly distinguishes secret computation from opening outputs. No world-first offering,
cryptographic discovery or discovery superiority is supported. The research application is credited to #4991;
this engineering agent supplied the hypothesis table and output-audit formulation.

**Same-offering complete economics.** The commercial experiment preserves #4991's original offering, buyer model,
startup estimates, financing constraints, fixed policy choices and complete event accounting. It does not use
profit from another service. Forward startup cost assumptions remain USD9,547.31 / 25,907.73 / 74,525.86 for
low/central/high cases; seed capital is USD60,000. The original startup allowance already includes privacy review.
No new arbitrary recurring manual-review charge is imposed on the automated preflight.

After freezing, **256 new economic worlds** were drawn from a new seed range. The inherited direct policy charges
USD12,000 with full deposit; its mean complete settled venture profit is **−USD29,971.90**, with fifth/ninety-fifth
world percentiles **−USD42,908.25 / −USD9,327.72**. The conditional policy charges USD6,000 with a 25% deposit;
its mean is **−USD3,515.93**, with percentiles **−USD5,625.29 / −USD1,529.78**, and no accepted assessments.
Both policies lose money in every one of these 256 primary worlds. No launch has zero modeled operating profit
and preserves seed capital. That zero excludes the already-incurred, unmetered research effort; it is an outside
option for prospective venture spending, not a claim that this sprint was free.

Twenty-four frozen sensitivities combine assumed refusal probabilities of 0/25/50/75% and automatic per-job costs
of USD0/0.01/1 across both policies. All mean profits remain negative, ranging from **−USD31,777.50** to
**−USD3,515.93**. Refusals are conservatively represented through the original post-booking nonacceptance/refund
path. The authored technical cohort is not used as an estimate of real buyer refusal rates. These are conditional
scenarios, not confidence intervals on market truth or observed demand. A separately implemented Decimal replay
reconciles all primary cost-category sums and profits in **1,536 checks**; the run also checks every sensitivity's
profit arithmetic and acceptance/cash bounds.

The underlying commercial bottleneck remains startup/readiness and a sparse, uncertain paying pipeline. Positive
variable contribution per accepted assessment does not finance the complete venture. Customer benefit, provider
profit, deposits, collection cash, seed capital, unpaid obligations and reserves remain distinct in retained records.
Actual collections are **USD0**. The existing CostLedger records this additional engineering assessment once in B,
with unknown complete actual cost and no computed actual profit; development, failures, recovery, verification and
handoff are included in that unknown aggregate. Original production/readiness A is not charged twice. No Gym or
extra retention campaign was undertaken.

**Qualification, handoff and the smallest next test.** The bounded checks include 270 new tests over all 256
three-input Boolean truth functions and directed boundary cases, 37 existing protocol tests, 21 unchanged native
authority controls, and strict type/lint checks. Separately implemented references are same-session algorithmic
checks, not organizational independence. Broad protected-runtime regression encountered an incomplete sparse
checkout and then filesystem exhaustion; its failures are retained and it is not reported as passing. Required
full repository, clean installation, platform, release and public browser gates remain open. Existing owner branches,
the 1.46.1 publication sequence, validator roles and all historical content are preserved.

Advance only the low-cost ordinary instrument if an owner has a concrete use. Reopen the enterprise candidate only
with a funded, consenting three-owner brief that fixes the exact permissible disclosure and outside option, plus
commitments sufficient to cover original readiness/acquisition/cash requirements. Compare a competent confidential
broker and established query-auditing methods. That is the smallest meaningful remaining experiment. The current
evidence does not justify an elaborate UI, platform expansion, paid retention or a full paper.
