# AGI ALPHA FLYWHEEL — From rival explanations to decisive experiments

Design recorded before confirmation, 28 October 2026. This is an executable development campaign, not a released paper or a claim of scientific discovery.

## The hypothesis and scope

A beneficiary should know which affordable observation would change a consequential decision, and when the supplied models do not permit a justified decision. The constructed instrument compiles a finite table of model predictions, interval uncertainties, action costs and decision labels into a minimum-cost separating set. It attaches independently checked pair witnesses and, when applicable, a structural non-separability certificate. It executes selected reduced-model investigations with separate numerical methods and updates the set of surviving explanations.

This is established model-discrimination design and weighted set cover, not a new theory. It is neither a research crawler nor a generative mechanism discoverer. It accepts candidate hypotheses, action sets, source links and uncertainty bounds supplied by the operator. Supplying those inputs remains the main scientific labor. The instrument's name cannot count as discovery of an opportunity. Independent code is written in the same author session; no independent person or organization has endorsed the result.

The claim under test is that this construction improves the cost or correctness of decision-resolving investigation beyond competent existing tools. Exact subset enumeration and SciPy/HiGHS binary optimization are strong controls with the same inputs. Greedy selection and one-cheapest-measurement are diagnostic ablations. A fresh separately elicited frontier generalist and prompt-only arm cannot be executed here; deterministic methods must not be relabeled as those arms. A tie against exact conventional controls is preserved and does not itself veto a commercial service.

## Causal diagnosis of earlier work

Pinned source and inspected reports are inventoried in `source_inspection.json`. The source audit of the earlier transformational-enterprise foundation records finite experiment selection, nuisance partitions and conventional ties already in the series. That evidence rules out presenting ordinary finite selection as a new discovery mechanism. The historical thermal studies' missing physical bridge is a legitimate HOLD, not proof that no useful thermal opportunity exists. Repeating a test with different arbitrary constants would not repair it.

Unmerged PR 5010 reports corrected exact-table comparisons and lifecycle losses; a cheaper familiar solver did not create the missing productive means. Unmerged PR 5011 reports a conditionally profitable reversible catalogue but unprotected payment-after-delivery and parity-market failures. These are owner-reported/inspected artifacts, not reruns performed in this campaign. They are also contrary evidence to an automatic technical-tie-to-economic-veto rule: an ordinary implementation can still sell a completed service under appropriate conditions.

The mechanism selected here addresses experiment choice explicitly and tests the complete protected delivery of its finite certificate. It does not solve the harder gaps: identifying a previously inaccessible activity, discovering adequate rival models from evidence, calibrating uncertainty, or establishing real intervention validity. Those limitations remain alternative explanations for failure, rather than being hidden by a synthetic success score.

## Selected translation card: membrane investigation service

Exact result: a minimum-cost set separating all cross-decision pairs under declared rational intervals. Binding constraint in the reduced model: four candidate diffusion/solubility pairs share steady permeability. Mechanism: choose a transient action when it separates those explanations. Artifact: task, certified plan, independent finite check, separately solved observations and retained-model decision. Acceptance: exact budget/optimality/source and witness checks plus numerical observation consistency; acceptance never certifies physics. Beneficiary/buyer: a hypothetical research operator supplying an already justified finite model and action table. Price: selected only from $1/$2/$4 synthetic offers on training scenarios. Complete real delivery cost, actual customer value and market benchmark H are unavailable, so H−P and H−C are not asserted. The model prices a narrowly defined automated local certificate, not a scientific consultancy.

Falsifier: a competent exact control matches the outcome at comparable total resources, a measured observation falls outside the candidate set, or no beneficiary has a material unmet activity. Wider scale bottleneck: model elicitation, calibration and native authorized execution. The actual numerical experiment tests only the first reduced-model step. It does not establish an externally unresolved scientific result. The commercial probe is separately capped at 18 training and 192 confirmation worlds; it cannot consume the protected final verification allocation.

## Budget, corrections and freeze

The initial budget was 100 minutes from 22:45 UTC, zero external spend, with at most two training repair rounds and a protected final 15 minutes. Diagnosis/construction consumed about 40 minutes; 15 minutes are available for evaluation, 15 for integration/installation, 15 for report/handoff, and 15 protected for reproduction. Evaluation is computationally small; a large benchmark would not repair missing physical or generalist evidence. Root storage exhaustion required verified partial snapshots and a durable checkpoint. No other task's directory or dependency was modified. Current storage is measured, not treated as unlimited.

Training includes 36 technical cases, 18 market worlds and scoped regressions. Earlier implementation attempts and failures are preserved. Repairs include missing snapshot imports, exhausted-disk truncation, typing/style issues, and stricter certificate/time checks. These were training changes before confirmation; the two declared post-evaluation repair rounds have not been consumed. Confirmation uses 48 newly seeded technical cases and 192 paired market worlds. Code, tests, comparator, evaluator, auditor, design and policy are frozen before the seed is generated. No failed final case may be dropped or silently replaced. Changes after a final failure require an explicit version and fresh confirmation; reused results become exploratory.
