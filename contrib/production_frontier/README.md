## What was constructed

`engine.construct(ProductionBrief, ConstructionPolicy)` joins backward output/dependency relevance
with forward quantitative construction. It generates combinations from the declared recipe library;
the caller does not supply a successful route. Scarce stock is consumed, complementary capabilities
are required, and every original obligation must be preserved. Permission denials, cost/time caps,
cancellation and incomplete search remain explicit. The search covers at most twelve single-use
sequential recipes and 4,096 subsets. There is no arbitrary code or tool execution from a recipe.

`verify.verify` revalidates its inputs, checks a strict result envelope and independently replays offered
witnesses. It certifies neither global optimality nor an impossible-world claim. A separate conventional
permutation oracle uses the **same full grammar, resources and information**, without relevance pruning
or the subset cache. It is limited to seven recipes. Known operations and conventional success receive
full credit; no algorithmic novelty is claimed.

`discovery.discover` attaches the generated construction, ablations, outside option, five inspectable
causal relationships, evidence/counters, falsifiers, scale arithmetic and next experiment to each
research hypothesis. It preserves rejected causal cores rather than rediscovering them unchanged.
Hypothesis meanings and recipe libraries were curated by this coding agent from the public sources;
this is not an autonomous field discovery experiment or a claim about what humans could imagine.

`native.proposal` validates an **unsaved** record in the existing `Opportunity` schema. Its unresolved
custody references convey no acceptance or authority. The live adapter delegates only to the existing
`FrontierConnector`; missing access raises an error and never becomes simulated success. The separate
simulation adapter labels every construction as execution of a declared model. No calibrated physical
performance or simulated frontier-model competence is claimed.

## Discovery and first commercial decision

Three public-requirement directions were investigated: distribution-transformer production,
additive-manufacturing metrology and dynamic grid hosting capacity. The last was not reduced to a
discrete stock fixture: that would omit voltage, time-series reliability and real control acceptance.
DOE's existing configuration/interchangeability work and National Grid's engineering-led SKU work
are substantive conventional constructions. NIST's metrology work already combines relevant methods.

The two quantitative hypotheses both remain **HOLD**. Local conformance cannot validate the original
engineering translation, physical recipe performance, buyer acceptance or complete outside-option
economics. The capacity-capped ten-year threshold scenario serves 985,000 units and yields conditional
USD985 million of customer net benefit at an assumed USD1,000 per unit. USD1,015.228427 per unit would
arithmetically reach USD1 billion. Adoption, unit benefit, production capacity and USD20 million of
uncommitted capital are unverified assumptions. This is a threshold audit, **not a credible scale claim,
company revenue, provider profit, market-size estimate or valuation**.

The first proposed offer is a two-owner engineering decision pack: eight requirement cards, at most
twelve recipes, a conditional route or bounded refusal, an independent discrepancy list and correction
obligations. It might help a buyer decide whether to fund engineering qualification if actual input and
acceptance conditions can be established. That value has not been purchased or observed.

The existing `Offering` and `Arrangement` models assess six price/scope policies, four correlated
worlds each, including failed work, corrections, verification, acquisition, loaded human work,
infrastructure, collections, defaulted receivables, refunds, reserves and funding. Development is
counted once; no incremental retention/Gym investment is admitted. The human-service reference is an
explicit estimate of forty team hours at USD150/hour, not a quote or an impossible human deliverable.
All six modeled expected profits are negative, from **−USD10,900 to −USD4,275**. Stopping is the best
tested future investment. All commercial policies HOLD. Actual collections and earnings are USD0;
complete actual costs, profit, customer net benefit and unrestricted funds remain unknown.
