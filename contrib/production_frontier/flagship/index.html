The discovery–construction loop

Keep the relationships that make an outcome useful.

The instrument searches backward from required outputs and forward through possible constructions. Its inputs include materials, complementary capabilities, permission conditions, costs and acceptance obligations.

01 / NEED

Equipment that can actually be delivered

DOE documents transformer supply constraints and existing standardization work. A public problem is evidence of need, not an order from a buyer.

02 / CONSTRAINT

A shared input has one balance

Two buyers cannot each receive the same unit of material. A metrology plan must leave fresh specimens after destructive tests.

03 / CONSTRUCTION

Search for the complete arrangement

A finite compiler combines declared recipes, checks complementary dependencies and independently replays its witnesses.

04 / OUTCOME

A conditional, inspectable production path

The code produces a route or a bounded refusal. Removing a component tests necessity. Conventional success receives full credit.

05 / ECONOMICS

A modeled offer remains on hold

Six price/scope policies include development, failures, corrections, verification, acquisition, support, refunds and reserves.

06 / NEXT

Validate the translation into reality

Real requirement cards, independent engineers and a supplier commitment are needed before a physical or commercial claim.
