# Compounding discovery: registered bounded experiment

This protocol is frozen with the runner and candidate implementation before new
confirmation seeds are generated. It evaluates declared fixed-route electric
dial-a-ride schedules using a public 16-passenger, two-vehicle instance. The
generated work changes travel, charging, energy use and appointment windows. It
does not sample new cities, validate battery physics, optimize route orders, or
establish willingness to pay. A mathematical infeasibility certificate is useful
diagnostic output; it does not transport passengers.

## Questions and endpoints

The primary endpoint is the proportion of fresh tasks with independently
accepted, task-bound FEASIBLE or INFEASIBLE certificates. The independent Decimal
checker is identical in every arm. Secondary endpoints are accepted feasible
passenger capacity, unresolved outcomes, rejected unsupported conclusions,
independently contradicted conclusions, objective
for paired accepted feasible tasks, resource use and modeled complete execution
cost. A certificate makes an activity newly feasible only when it supplies a
valid feasible schedule that the paired comparator did not supply. Correctly
rejecting an impossible schedule is reported separately.

The practical minimum gain is five percentage points in accepted certificates;
the secondary cost criterion is at least 10 percent lower marginal execution
cost at no more than one percentage point lower acceptance. These thresholds
are research decision rules, not validated beneficiary utilities. No positive
finding may erase inferiority to the strong conventional reference.

All eight A/S/C configurations use the same observable task, generic solving
methods, total resource cap, authority and final checker. Disabled transfer may
independently discover the same structure. Disabled S may use the unchanged
initial strategy. Disabled C removes extra targeted search; it cannot omit the
final checker or redefine correctness. The event ledger charges failed trials,
strategy proposals, adaptations, criticism, retries and verification. It records
unspent budget rather than forcing useless expenditure.

The direct specialist is the existing conventional compiler/LP path. A second
reference may be the separately authored, source-pinned frontier-model program
from the preceding campaign. That is a frontier-authored deterministic program
reference, not a new frontier model call or an independently staffed enterprise.
If absent, the second reference is UNAVAILABLE and no replacement is silently
relabeled. All actual API spending is zero.

The frozen frontier program uses an older wire representation for infeasibility
witnesses. The runner preserves its exact raw artifact and translates only the
declared fields into the current format: positive bounded gap equals its
declared residual lower bound minus its declared weighted right-hand side,
serialized to 24 decimal places within the current input precision bound.
The original solver source and common checker remain unchanged; translation
has a charged stage. The development refusal from the unadapted format remains.

The implemented S grammar is a bounded portfolio of LP algorithms and presolve
settings, rather than open-ended program synthesis. C searches two seeded
moderate travel, energy-use or appointment-window perturbations and independently
checks any claimed feasibility boundary. A separately checked corrupted
certificate is an additional integrity probe. These bounds limit the inference
about general strategy invention and adversarial discovery.

## Allocation and analysis

The default allocation is four regimes × four tasks × three independent run
seeds for each of eight arms, paired on exact task bytes. Replicates and budget
can be reduced only by creating a new protocol/run identity before execution.
Regimes are nominal, charging/energy stress, appointment-window stress, and a
combined shift. Tasks perturb the same public raw instance and published route
order; this is a controlled parameter panel, not cross-domain generalization.

Analysis reports every cell mean, the full-versus-none paired effect, three
average main effects, three pairwise differences-in-differences averaged over
the remaining factor, and the three-way alternating contrast. The response
scale is additive accepted-certificate proportion. The label synergy requires
a positive interaction whose paired 95 percent bootstrap interval excludes
zero. Multiplication is not tested or claimed. Confidence intervals resample
whole task clusters, retaining arms and repeated run seeds together. They
describe this generated panel and do not turn it into a representative market
sample. All contrasts are reported; no cherry-picked multiple-comparison claim
is permitted. A supportable positive overall claim also requires the practical
minimum gain and no false-acceptance increase.

## Chronological experience

There are three declared training cycles and four checkpoints, including the
initial state. The cumulative system retains its own training outcomes and
structured assets. The frozen-initial system never updates its initial state.
The reset system starts each current task from the initial state. The ordinary
retrieval system receives the same historical training records as the full
learner but cannot use the structured-transfer index. History is chronological;
no task or label from a later cycle is available earlier. Evaluation probes are
read-only and cannot update any learner.

Every checkpoint is evaluated on the same difficulty-controlled regime design
with fresh task instances and three run seeds. All systems see the same
current-task source and history availability, with budget parity. Learning and
retention costs are separate from marginal deployment costs and added for the
lifecycle view. Individual tasks record the exact memory version, used asset
identities and historical record hashes. Improvement requires a positive
paired gain against frozen, reset and retrieval controls, not just a rising
unpaired curve. A frozen/reset tie is expected when both use the same initial
state and deterministic execution; their names do not create distinct evidence.

A final panel seed is generated and committed after source freeze but its
instances are not executed by the initial run. The supported final command
evaluates the already frozen final candidate and controls once into a separate
directory. No repair based on confirmation can reuse the label fresh. Post-run
changes require a new version and new confirmation; old evidence remains.

## Integrity, failure and accounting

Runner, runtime, checker, base input, reference source, protocol and configuration
hashes are committed before seeds are sampled. Input hashes bind every artifact.
Only a fixed installed runtime is imported. A reference path is an explicit
operator-owned program, never a task-supplied module or candidate instruction.
Evaluator source hashes are rechecked after execution. The candidate's data
contract cannot carry expected statuses, hidden labels or evaluator code.

Outputs use exclusive new directories. Interrupted runs retain completed
records; resume validates configuration/source hashes and skips only exact
completed record identities. Final evaluation and reproduction use separate
directories. Failed promotions keep measured CPU and a failure receipt before
the exception propagates. Resume reconstruction costs have separate receipts
and cannot rewrite the original chronological lifecycle expenses.
Timings are measured on the current host; quantity-times-rate
currency entries are explicitly modeled equivalents, not invoices. Shared
development costs are incurred once and separately allocated, never repeated as
actual spending across counterfactual worlds. All event IDs must be unique.
Observations use lossless `.json.gz` records with deterministic gzip headers;
`gzip -dc FILE.json.gz` produces their complete inspectable JSON. Inputs,
configuration, memories and summaries remain ordinary JSON. No historical
field or raw reference artifact is removed to obtain the storage reduction.
Execution CPU is priced at the declared simulated USD 0.10 per CPU-hour, with
a tenfold rate sensitivity. One month of retained memory is priced at the
inherited modeled USD 0.03 per decimal GB-month. These are explicit assumptions,
not measured provider invoices. Per-execution costs, promotion costs, historical
record generation and retention are shown separately. Historical record origin
cost is allocated to each history-consuming counterfactual for lifecycle
comparison; actual expenditure records charge its generation once. Authoring,
original source work and commercial mechanism costs need their separate ledger.
Unknown historical cost and actual buyer demand remain unknown. The experiment
does not itself prove profit, earned funding or additional capability construction.

The system is a bounded deterministic construction and strategy experiment. A
null, tie, negative transfer, cost disadvantage or unavailable reference remains
a result. The broader mission remains NOT COMPLETE until its missing scientific,
commercial and source-qualified native links are independently established.

## Resource infrastructure revision

Confirmation v1 retained its complete endpoints but did not qualify against the
declared 200,000,000-byte free-disk reserve. Its negative learning outcomes and
all failed certificates remain evidence. The infrastructure revision leaves the
candidate implementation, acceptance rules, primary metrics, strategy options,
task generator, sample allocation and monetary assumptions unchanged. It adds a
prospective filesystem check before every observation and checked promotion,
records each measured free-space value, and pauses the dependent edge before
candidate execution when the reserve is unavailable. It does not lower the
reserve to obtain completion. Concurrent external disk use can still intervene
between checks. A new source freeze and fresh confirmation seed distinguish the
revised run; v1 is never relabeled qualified. An already completed resume returns
the preserved summary after exact source/input checks and performs no candidate
work. Partial resumes preserve original events and separately price reconstruction.

## Exact producer representation revision

V3 follows the separately recorded `V3_PROSPECTIVE_AMENDMENT.json` after the
complete executed v2 checkpoint was persistently saved and verified. It strips
only insignificant fractional zeros from the producer's Farkas contradiction
string. Exact values, genuine precision restrictions, independent checking,
strategies, metrics, allocations, thresholds and resource caps are unchanged.
All component configurations share the repair. The evaluator also receives
typing-only annotations, local variable names and typed closure helpers; exact
parity fixtures retain its task generation and estimators. The actual new
regressions, amendment and seed allocation are included in the next source
snapshot before fresh seeds. V2 outcomes and diagnostic replays remain
historical evidence and cannot count as fresh v3 confirmation. The v3 final
panel has its own untouched reserve. Encoding qualification cannot establish
algorithmic superiority, commercial demand or an earned-funded successor.
