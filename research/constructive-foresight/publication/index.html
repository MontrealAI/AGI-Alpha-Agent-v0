01 / THE QUESTION

From anticipating change
to qualifying an advantage.

Transformational intelligence (TI∞) motivates the inquiry. Defined tasks, credible alternatives and measured evidence determine the answer.

When does calibrated foresight help an agent identify a mission bottleneck, build an enabling capability, and deliver greater full-cost mission value before the opportunity changes?

Mission Alpha is the useful value delivered by a chosen policy, after full costs, above the best credible alternative under comparable constraints. Better forecasts can be useless. Early construction can be costly. Waiting can be the right decision.

02 / THE VERDICT

Useful readiness.
No unique full-cost advantage established.

The instrument worked and local forecasts improved. Conventional factorization reuse explains the speed benefit. In the declared decision model, constructive foresight did not establish an advantage over the calibrated fixed rule and chose the same actions as an adaptive policy with the same information and lower measured controller cost.

Observed · local computation

A capability that can be inspected.

All 432 held-out batches—3,024 right-hand sides—passed. At 16 RHS, the instrument was about 7.0–13.6× faster than repeated cold factorization, yet took 1.01–1.44× the time of the native cached solver. Conventional reuse accounts for the benefit. Read the comparison.

Observed · local forecast

A forecast that can be scored.

At the preregistered primary deadline (multiplier 1), frozen forecasts scored 0.0721 Brier versus a calibration-only pooled constant of 0.2600 across 720 correlated outcomes; lower is better. Across all three deadlines, the secondary score was 0.0315 versus 0.2424 across 2,160 outcomes. These are local timing events, not AGI-arrival or adoption forecasts. Read the forecast analysis.

Controlled simulation

A decision that pays its full cost.

Against the calibrated fixed rule, selected using calibration only, nominal incremental value was +0.0257 assumed credits per episode (95% interval: −0.0390 to +0.1273). Under distribution shift, it was −0.0628 (−0.1216 to −0.0064). An adaptive policy with the same information chose identical actions in all 4,608 simulated episodes at lower controller cost. Read the model and uncertainty analysis.

Observed · engineering

A record that carries no permission.

Both fixed numerical runs passed, including actual refactorization after knowledge-only restore. Eight native authority/proof refusals and six receiver refusals behaved as required. Complete process cost: 6.552 seconds wall time. Inspect the retained evidence.

Scope: local computational delivery and a controlled decision model. Model credits are assumed utility units. Intervals use simulation seed blocks; episodes are not beneficiaries. External acceptance, paid demand, sector forecasts and investor returns are not established.
